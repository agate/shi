新年作

刘长卿Liu Changqing · Tang唐

Written at New Year

 

xiāng乡xīn心xīn新suì岁qiè切

tiān天pàn畔dú独shān潸rán然

lǎo老zhì至jū居rén人xià下

chūn春guī归zài在kè客xiān先

lǐng岭yuán猿tóng同dàn旦mù暮

jiāng江liǔ柳gòng共fēng风yān烟

yǐ已shì是cháng长shā沙fù傅

cóng从jīn今yòu又jǐ几nián年

This collection writes the word for ape with an old variant character. The common text uses the standard form; the meaning is the same.

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 乡心新岁切The new year has come, and my longing for home grows sharper;新年到了，想家的心更加急切，
  2. 天畔独潸然alone at the edge of the sky, I cannot hold back my tears.我一个人在天边，忍不住流下眼泪。
  3. 老至居人下I am old now, yet I still rank below others;人已经老了，官职却还在别人下面，
  4. 春归在客先spring has come back, and it got here before me, the stranger.春天回来了，却比我这个异乡人先回到这里。
  5. 岭猿同旦暮Morning and evening, only the apes of the mountains keep me company;早早晚晚，陪着我的只有山里的猿猴，
  6. 江柳共风烟the willows by the river share the wind and mist with me.江边的柳树，和我一起经受风雨烟雾。
  7. 已是长沙傅I am already like Jia Yi, sent away to Changsha long ago;我已经像当年被贬到长沙的贾谊一样，
  8. 从今又几年from now on, how many more years must I wait?从今往后，还要再过多少年才能回去？
02

Words字词注释

乡心xiāng xīn
xiāng xīn: a heart that longs for home.乡心：想家的心。
新岁xīn suì
xīn suì: the new year.新岁：新年。
切qiè
qiè: urgent, very strong.切：急切，很强烈。
天畔tiān pàn
tiān pàn: the edge of the sky, a place very far from home.天畔：天边，指离家很远的地方。
潸然shān rán
shān rán: with tears running down.潸然：流眼泪的样子。
老至lǎo zhì
lǎo zhì: old age has come.老至：人老了。
居人下jū rén xià
jū rén xià: to rank below others, holding only a small post.居人下：地位在别人下面，官做得很小。
在客先zài kè xiān
zài kè xiān: before me, the stranger. Kè is a person far from home, here the poet himself.在客先：比我这个异乡人先到。客，离家在外的人，这里指诗人自己。
岭猿lǐng yuán
lǐng yuán: apes in the southern mountains. Lǐng means the Five Ranges, the mountains north of today's Guangdong and Guangxi.岭猿：南方山岭里的猿猴。岭，指五岭，今天广东、广西北边的山。
同旦暮tóng dàn mù
tóng dàn mù: spending morning and evening with me. Dàn is morning, mù is evening.同旦暮：和我一起度过早晚。旦，早上；暮，傍晚。
共风烟gòng fēng yān
gòng fēng yān: sharing the wind and mist with me.共风烟：和我一起经受风雨烟雾。
长沙傅cháng shā fù
cháng shā fù: Jia Yi of the Han dynasty. He was very gifted but was sent away to Changsha as tutor to the prince there. The poet compares himself to him.长沙傅：指汉代的贾谊。他很有才，却被贬到长沙，当长沙王的太傅（老师）。诗人用他比自己。
从今又几年cóng jīn yòu jǐ nián
cóng jīn yòu jǐ nián: how many more years from now (before I can go back)?从今又几年：从现在起，还要过多少年（才能回去）。
03

Background创作背景

Liu Changqing was blunt and upright, offended people, and was sent away to a small post far in the south. It is usually thought that he wrote this poem at New Year after being sent to Nanba, around today's Maoming in Guangdong. Another view says he wrote it later, when he was sent to Muzhou in today's Zhejiang. New Year is a time for the whole family to be together, but he was alone, far from home, so he wrote this sad poem of homesickness.

刘长卿性格刚直，得罪了人，被贬到很远的南方做小官。一般认为，这首诗是他被贬到南巴（在今天广东茂名一带）时，过新年写的。一说是他后来被贬到睦州（在今天浙江）时写的。新年本来是全家团圆的时候，他却一个人在离家很远的地方，所以写下了这首思乡又伤心的诗。

04

The poet作者

Liu Changqing, courtesy name Wenfang, was a Tang poet. Sources differ on the years of his birth and death; he lived in the eighth century. He was best at five-character poems and was called the "Great Wall of five-character verse", meaning his poems in that form were as strong and great as the Great Wall. He was sent away twice in his life, and many of his poems tell of the loneliness and unfairness he felt after that.

刘长卿，字文房，唐代诗人，生卒年说法不一，大约生活在8世纪。他最擅长写五言诗，被人称为“五言长城”，意思是他的五言诗像长城一样坚固、了不起。他一生两次被贬，很多诗写的都是被贬后的孤独和不平。

05

The mood意境

The first line says what is on his mind: with the new year, his longing for home grows sharper. The second shows him alone at the edge of the sky, quietly crying. Lines three and four are clever. He is old, but his post is still so low. Spring has come back, but he cannot go back, so spring got there before him. He is comparing himself with spring, and the more he compares, the sadder he feels. Lines five and six show what is around him. Only apes and the willows by the river keep him company, the same every morning and evening, the same in wind and mist. This shows how alone he is. The last two lines use the story of Jia Yi. Jia Yi was sent away to Changsha, and the poet too has been sent far away, not knowing how many years he must wait. The poem turns the cheer of New Year inside out and shows coldness and hurt instead.

第一句就说出心事：新年一到，想家的心更急了。第二句画出一个人在天边偷偷流泪的样子。三、四句很巧：人都老了，官还这么小；春天都回来了，我却回不去，春天反而比我先到。这是在跟春天比，越比越难过。五、六句写身边的东西：只有猿猴和江边的柳树陪着他，早晚一样，风烟一样，写出了孤单。最后两句用贾谊的故事比自己：贾谊被贬到长沙，我也一样被贬到远方，不知道还要等多少年。整首诗把新年的热闹反过来写，写出了冷清和委屈。
