Da You: The Ninth Day

 

xì戏mǎ马tái台qián前

cǎi采huā花lí篱xià下

wèn问suì岁huá华hái还shì是chóng重jiǔ九

qià恰guī归lái来nán南shān山cuì翠sè色yī依jiù旧

lián帘lóng栊zuó昨yè夜tīng听fēng风yǔ雨

dōu都bú不sì似dēng登lín临shí时hòu候

yí一piàn片sòng宋yù玉qíng情huái怀

shí十fēn分wèi卫láng郎qīng清shòu瘦

hóng红yú萸pèi佩

kōng空duì对jiǔ酒

zhēn砧chǔ杵dòng动wēi微hán寒

àn暗qī欺luó罗xiù袖

qiū秋yǐ已wú无duō多

zǎo早shì是bài败hé荷shuāi衰liǔ柳

qiǎng强zhěng整mào帽yán檐qī敧cè侧

céng曾jīng经xiàng向tiān天yá涯sāo搔shǒu首

jǐ几huí回yì忆gù故guó国chún莼lú鲈

shuāng霜qián前yàn雁hòu后

Common editions, such as the Three Hundred Song Lyrics, read deng lin (climbing up to look) and zhen chu (the washing stone and its pounding stick). The source text here has deng gui and zhen gan, which are copying or conversion slips.

通行本（如《宋词三百首》）作“都不似、登临时候”“砧杵动微寒”。这里的底本作“登归”“砧杆”，是传抄或转换时的错字。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 戏马台前Climbing at the Horse-Racing Terrace,在戏马台前登高，
  2. 采花篱下picking chrysanthemums below the fence,在篱笆下采菊花，
  3. 问岁华还是重九I ask what time of year it is: Double Ninth again.问问时节，原来又到了重阳节。
  4. 恰归来南山翠色依旧I have just come home, and the southern hills are as green as before.刚好回到家里，南山还是那样青翠。
  5. 帘栊昨夜听风雨Last night behind the curtained window I listened to wind and rain,昨夜在窗帘后听了一夜风雨，
  6. 都不似登临时候and none of it felt like the old days of climbing up to look far.都不像从前登高望远时的心情。
  7. 一片宋玉情怀My heart is full of Song Yu's autumn sorrow,满怀像宋玉那样悲秋的心事，
  8. 十分卫郎清瘦and I am as thin as young Wei Jie.人也像卫玠那样十分清瘦。
  9. 红萸佩I wear the red dogwood berries,佩着红红的茱萸，
  10. 空对酒but only sit before the wine for nothing.却只是白白地对着酒杯。
  11. 砧杵动微寒The pounding of washing stones stirs a faint chill,捣衣声响起，带来淡淡的寒意，
  12. 暗欺罗袖which slips quietly into my silk sleeves.悄悄地钻进丝绸衣袖。
  13. 秋已无多Little of autumn is left,秋天已经剩下不多了，
  14. 早是败荷衰柳the lotus has already withered and the willows faded.早已是荷叶枯败、柳树凋零。
  15. 强整帽檐敧侧I force myself to straighten my wind-tilted hat.勉强把被风吹歪的帽子扶正，
  16. 曾经向天涯搔首Once, at the far ends of the earth, I scratched my head in worry.曾经在天涯海角愁得直挠头。
  17. 几回忆故国莼鲈How many times have I remembered the water-shield and perch of home,多少回想起故乡的莼菜和鲈鱼，
  18. 霜前雁后just before the frost, just after the geese flew south.正是在下霜以前、大雁南飞以后。
02

Words字词注释

戏马台xì mǎ tái
xì mǎ tái: the Horse-Racing Terrace in today's Xuzhou, Jiangsu. Legend says Xiang Yu watched his horsemen train there. Liu Yu, an emperor of the Southern Dynasties, held a great feast with poems there on the ninth day of the ninth month.戏马台：在今天江苏徐州，传说是项羽看人骑马练兵的高台。南朝宋武帝刘裕曾在九月九日到这里大宴宾客、作诗。
采花篱下cǎi huā lí xià
cǎi huā lí xià: picking flowers below the fence. It echoes Tao Yuanming's famous line about picking chrysanthemums by the eastern fence.采花篱下：化用陶渊明的“采菊东篱下”。这里的花指菊花。
岁华suì huá
suì huá: the time of year, the passing seasons.岁华：时光，一年的光景。
重九chóng jiǔ
chóng jiǔ: the ninth day of the ninth lunar month, the Double Ninth Festival. People climb hills, wear dogwood and drink chrysanthemum wine.重九：农历九月初九，重阳节。这一天人们登高、插茱萸、喝菊花酒。
南山nán shān
nán shān: the southern hills. Tao Yuanming wrote of calmly seeing the southern hills. Here the poet comes home at Double Ninth and finds the hills as green as ever.南山：陶渊明有“悠然见南山”的诗句。这里写重阳回到家，看见南山还是那样青翠。
帘栊lián lóng
lián lóng: a window with a hanging curtain.帘栊：挂着帘子的窗户。
登临dēng lín
dēng lín: to climb up high and look into the distance.登临：登上高处，看远处的风景。
宋玉情怀sòng yù qíng huái
sòng yù qíng huái: the feelings of Song Yu, a poet of the state of Chu who wrote, "How sad is the air of autumn!" It means the sadness autumn brings.宋玉情怀：宋玉是战国时楚国的文人，写过“悲哉，秋之为气也”。宋玉情怀就是对着秋天伤心的心情。
卫郎清瘦wèi láng qīng shòu
wèi láng qīng shòu: Wei Jie, a handsome young man of the Western Jin who was thin and often ill. The poet says he is just as thin.卫郎：西晋的卫玠，长得很美，但身体瘦弱多病。清瘦：瘦。
红萸hóng yú
hóng yú: the red berries of the dogwood. At Double Ninth people wore them to keep bad luck away.红萸：红色的茱萸果。古人重阳节把茱萸佩在身上，说能避邪。
空对酒kōng duì jiǔ
kōng duì jiǔ: facing the wine for nothing, with no heart to drink.空对酒：白白对着酒，没有心思喝。
砧杵zhēn chǔ
zhēn chǔ: the stone and wooden stick used to pound clothes clean. In autumn the sound of pounding meant cold weather was coming.砧杵：捶洗衣服用的石板和木棒。秋天家家捶衣，声音让人觉得天冷了。
暗欺罗袖àn qī luó xiù
àn qī luó xiù: the cold quietly creeps into the silk sleeves. Qī means to bully or invade.暗欺罗袖：寒气悄悄地钻进丝绸的衣袖里。欺，侵犯。
早是zǎo shì
zǎo shì: already is.早是：已经是。
败荷衰柳bài hé shuāi liǔ
bài hé shuāi liǔ: withered lotus leaves and fading willows.败荷衰柳：枯了的荷叶，凋零的柳树。
帽檐敧侧mào yán qī cè
mào yán qī cè: the hat brim blown crooked. At a Double Ninth feast in the Eastern Jin, the wind blew off Meng Jia's hat, a famous story.帽檐敧侧：帽子被风吹歪了。东晋孟嘉在重阳宴会上被风吹落帽子，成了有名的故事。
搔首sāo shǒu
sāo shǒu: scratching one's head, a sign of worry.搔首：用手挠头，是发愁、着急的样子。
天涯tiān yá
tiān yá: the edge of the sky, a place far from home.天涯：天边，指离家很远的地方。
故国gù guó
gù guó: the old home. Some readers think it also hints at longing for the Song state itself.故国：故乡。一说也暗含对故国（宋朝）的怀念。
莼鲈chún lú
chún lú: water-shield soup and perch. Zhang Han of the Western Jin, serving in Luoyang, felt the autumn wind, longed for these dishes of his home in Wu, and quit his post to go home. It came to mean homesickness.莼鲈：莼菜汤和鲈鱼。西晋张翰在洛阳做官，秋风一起，想起家乡吴地的莼菜和鲈鱼，就辞官回家了。后来用它指思乡。
霜前雁后shuāng qián yàn hòu
shuāng qián yàn hòu: before the frost and after the wild geese fly south, that is, late autumn.霜前雁后：下霜以前，大雁南飞以后，正是深秋。
03

How to read it读音

重chóng
In chong jiu, this character is read chóng, meaning double: the ninth day of the ninth month has two nines.“重九”的“重”读 chóng，意思是重复、两个。九月九日有两个九，所以叫重九。
强qiǎng
Here it is read qiǎng, meaning to force oneself to do something.这里读 qiǎng，意思是勉强、硬撑着。
敧qī
This character is read qī and means tilted or leaning.“敧”读 qī，意思是歪、斜，同“欹”。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about the Double Ninth Festival, the ninth day of the ninth lunar month. The poet comes home for the festival. He thinks of the old customs of climbing hills and picking chrysanthemums, but his heart is full of autumn sorrow and homesickness. The lyric uses many old stories tied to the festival and to autumn: the Horse-Racing Terrace, Tao Yuanming picking chrysanthemums, Meng Jia losing his hat, and Zhang Han longing for the food of home. We no longer know the year it was written.

这首词写的是重阳节（农历九月初九）。词人回到家乡过节，想起登高、采菊这些重阳的老习俗，心里却满是秋天的愁苦和思乡之情。词里用了很多和重阳、秋天有关的典故，比如戏马台、陶渊明采菊、孟嘉落帽、张翰思念莼鲈。具体写在哪一年，已经说不清了。

05

The poet作者

Pan Xibai lived at the end of the Southern Song. He came from Yongjia, today's Wenzhou in Zhejiang. He is usually said to have passed the top state exam in the Baoyou years under Emperor Lizong. Very few of his lyrics survive. This one is known because it was chosen for the Three Hundred Song Lyrics.

潘希白，南宋末年人，字怀古，号渔庄，永嘉（今浙江温州）人。一般说他在宋理宗宝祐年间考中进士。他留下的词很少，这首《大有》因为被选进《宋词三百首》而广为人知。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza opens with the bright customs of the festival: climbing high, picking chrysanthemums, the southern hills still green. But last night's wind and rain have changed his mood. Like Song Yu he grieves for autumn, and like Wei Jie he has grown thin. The second stanza is colder still. He wears the dogwood but has no heart for wine. With the sound of pounding washing stones, the cold creeps into his sleeves. The lotus has withered and the willows have yellowed. He forces himself to straighten his hat and remembers worrying at the ends of the earth, longing for home again and again in late autumn. The lyric is like a painting of autumn whose colours fade as you go, while the sadness grows deeper.

上阕从重阳的热闹写起：登高、采菊，南山依旧青翠。可是昨夜的风雨让心情完全不同，词人像宋玉一样悲秋，像卫玠一样消瘦。下阕更冷清：佩着茱萸却没心思喝酒，捣衣声里寒气钻进衣袖，荷叶枯了，柳树也黄了。他硬撑着扶正帽子，想起自己曾在天涯发愁，多少次在深秋里想念家乡。整首词像一幅秋天的画，越往后颜色越淡，愁也越深。
