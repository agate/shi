No. 54 · Song lyric · ci lyric to the tune Ta Suo Xing, in two stanzas第 54 首 · 宋词 · 词·踏莎行（双调）

tà踏suō莎xíng行·yàn燕yàn燕qīng轻yíng盈

jiāng姜kuí夔Jiang Kui · Song宋

To the Tune of Ta Suo Xing: Light as a Swallow

 

yàn燕yàn燕qīng轻yíng盈

yīng莺yīng莺jiāo娇ruǎn软

fēn分míng明yòu又xiàng向huá华xū胥jiàn见

yè夜cháng长zhēng争dé得bó薄qíng情zhī知

chūn春chū初zǎo早bèi被xiāng相sī思rǎn染

bié别hòu后shū书cí辞

bié别shí时zhēn针xiàn线

lí离hún魂àn暗zhú逐láng郎háng行yuǎn远

huái淮nán南hào皓yuè月lěng冷qiān千shān山

míng冥míng冥guī归qù去wú无rén人guǎn管

The lyric has a long original heading: "Coming from east of the Mian River, I reached Jinling on New Year's Day of the year Dingwei, and wrote this on the river after a dream." The corpus gives only the tune name, so the first line is used as the title, as is common.

这首词原有一个很长的题目：“自沔东来，丁未元日至金陵，江上感梦而作”。资料库只有词牌，这里按通行做法用首句作题。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 燕燕轻盈She is light as a swallow,她像燕子一样轻盈，
  2. 莺莺娇软soft-voiced as an oriole.像黄莺一样声音娇柔。
  3. 分明又向华胥见Clearly I saw her again in a dream.我清清楚楚地又在梦里见到了她。
  4. 夜长争得薄情知She said: how could you, cold one, know how long the nights are?她说：长夜难熬，你这薄情人怎么会知道？
  5. 春初早被相思染Only early spring, and I am already soaked in longing.才是早春，我心里早已满是相思。
  6. 别后书辞The letters she wrote after we parted,分别后她写来的信，
  7. 别时针线the clothes she sewed as we said goodbye,分别时她亲手缝的衣裳，
  8. 离魂暗逐郎行远her soul slipped away in secret and followed me far.她的魂魄悄悄离开身体，一路远远跟着我。
  9. 淮南皓月冷千山The bright moon over Huainan turns a thousand hills cold.淮南的明月照着千山，一片清冷，
  10. 冥冥归去无人管In the dark, her soul goes home alone, and no one looks after it.她的魂在昏暗中独自回去，没有人照看。
02

Words字词注释

燕燕轻盈yàn yàn qīng yíng
yàn yàn, yīng yīng: swallows and orioles, used here to stand for the woman he loves. qīng yíng: light and graceful.燕燕、莺莺：燕子和黄莺，这里借来比喻词人的心上人。轻盈：身子轻巧，动作好看。
娇软jiāo ruǎn
jiāo ruǎn: soft and sweet, like her voice.娇软：声音娇柔、温软。
分明fēn míng
fēn míng: clearly.分明：清清楚楚。
华胥huá xū
huá xū: a dream. Legend says the Yellow Emperor once dreamed he visited the land of Huaxu, so the name came to mean dreamland.华胥：梦。传说黄帝白天睡觉，梦见去了华胥国，后来“华胥”就指梦境。
争得zhēng dé
zhēng dé: how could.争得：怎么能。
薄情bó qíng
bó qíng: a cold-hearted one. Here it is what the woman calls her lover, half in love and half in blame.薄情：负心、冷淡的人。这里是女子对情郎又爱又怨的称呼。
相思染xiāng sī rǎn
xiāng sī rǎn: soaked through with longing, the way cloth takes in dye.相思染：被思念浸透了，像布被染上颜色一样。
书辞shū cí
shū cí: the words in her letters.书辞：信里写的话。
针线zhēn xiàn
zhēn xiàn: needle and thread, the clothes she sewed for him.针线：她亲手缝的衣物。
离魂lí hún
lí hún: a soul that has left the body. There are old tales of a girl whose soul left her body to follow the man she loved.离魂：离开身体的魂魄。古代有女子魂魄离开身体、追随心上人的传说。
暗逐àn zhú
àn zhú: to follow in secret.暗逐：悄悄地追随。
郎行láng háng
láng háng: where her lover is.郎行：情郎那里。
淮南huái nán
huái nán: south of the Huai River, here the area around Hefei, where the woman lived.淮南：淮河以南，这里指合肥一带，是那位女子住的地方。
皓月hào yuè
hào yuè: a bright, white moon.皓月：明亮洁白的月亮。
冥冥míng míng
míng míng: dark and dim.冥冥：昏暗的样子。
03

How to read it读音

燕yàn
Yàn yàn means swallows, and here also the beloved. Read yàn, not yān.“燕燕”指燕子，也借指心上人，读 yàn。
争zhēng
Zhēng dé is Song-era speech for "how could". Zhēng is read as usual.“争得”是宋代口语，意思是“怎么能、怎么会”，“争”读 zhēng。
薄bó
In bó qíng the first syllable is read bó, not báo.“薄情”读 bó。
行háng
In láng háng, háng is Song-era speech meaning "where he is". It is usually read háng. Some take it as "walking" and read it xíng.“郎行”的“行”是宋代口语，意思是“那里、身边”，一般读 háng。也有人理解为“行走”，读 xíng。
04

Background创作背景

On New Year's Day in 1187, Jiang Kui was traveling east by boat from Hanyang to Huzhou. As he passed Jinling, today's Nanjing, he dreamed of the woman he loved, and wrote this lyric when he woke. Scholars believe she was a singer he had met in Hefei when he was younger. He wrote many lyrics remembering her throughout his life. Her name and story were never recorded.

宋孝宗淳熙十四年（1187年）正月初一，姜夔从汉阳往东去湖州，坐船经过金陵（今江苏南京），夜里梦见了心上人，醒来写下这首词。研究者认为，这位女子是他早年在合肥认识的一位歌女，他一生写了不少怀念她的词。她的名字和身世没有留下记载。

05

The poet作者

Jiang Kui (about 1155 to about 1221), also called Yaozhang and known as the Daoist of White Stone, was a Southern Song lyric poet and a fine musician who wrote his own tunes. He never held office. He lived on the help of friends and moved from place to place. His lyrics are cool, clear and airy, like moonlight. The scholar Wang Guowei wrote that of all Jiang Kui's lyrics, the lines he loved most were the last two of this one.

姜夔（约1155—约1221），字尧章，号白石道人，南宋词人，也精通音乐，自己能作曲。他一辈子没做官，靠朋友接济，四处漂泊。他的词清冷空灵，像月光一样。王国维在《人间词话》里说，姜夔的词他最喜欢的就是这首的最后两句。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is the dream. His beloved, light as a swallow and soft as an oriole, appears clearly before him. She even scolds him: the nights are so long, how could you know? Spring has barely come, and I am soaked in longing. The second stanza thinks of her deep love. Her letters and the clothes she sewed are still with him. Her soul has even left her body to follow him all this way. The last two lines are the loveliest. The bright moon over Huainan turns a thousand hills cold. Her soul goes home alone in the dark, with no one to look after it. It is her loneliness, and also the poet's own ache for her.

上片写梦：心上人像燕子一样轻，像黄莺一样柔，清清楚楚地出现在梦里。她还埋怨他：长夜这么难熬，你怎么会知道？早春刚到，我已经被相思染透了。下片想她的痴情：信、亲手缝的衣服，都还在身边；她的魂儿甚至离开身体，一路追着他来。最后两句最美：淮南的明月照着千山，冷冷清清；她的魂在黑夜里独自回去，没人照看。写的是她的孤单，也是词人自己的心疼。
