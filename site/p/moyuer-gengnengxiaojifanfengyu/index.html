摸鱼儿·更能消几番风雨

辛弃疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

To the Tune of Mo Yu Er: How Many More Storms

 

gèng更néng能xiāo消jǐ几fān番fēng风yǔ雨

cōng匆cōng匆chūn春yòu又guī归qù去

This page uses the common edition (for example, Deng Guangming's annotated edition of Xin Qiji). It differs from the corpus in a few places. The common text has cháng pà ("always afraid") where the corpus has cháng hèn ("always regret"). It has wú guī lù ("no road back") where the corpus has mí guī lù ("lost the road back"). It has zhū wǎng ("spider's web") where the corpus has a look-alike word meaning "pearl web". It has wēi lán ("high railing") where the corpus has wēi lóu ("tall tower"). Old editions really do differ here. The lyric also has a short preface that the corpus leaves out; see the background.

本文用通行本（如邓广铭《稼轩词编年笺注》）。和语料库的文本有几处不同：通行本“惜春长怕花开早”，语料库作“长恨”；通行本“天涯芳草无归路”，语料库作“迷归路”；通行本“画檐蛛网”，语料库作“珠网”；通行本“休去倚危栏”，语料库作“危楼”。各本文字本来就有出入。另外，这首词原有一段小序，语料库没有收，内容见“背景”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 更能消几番风雨How many more storms of wind and rain can it bear?还能再经得起几番风雨呢？
  2. 匆匆春又归去Once again spring is hurrying away.春天又匆匆忙忙地要走了。
  3. 惜春长怕花开早I love the spring, so I always fear the flowers open too soon,我爱惜春天，总怕花开得太早，
  4. 何况落红无数and now fallen petals lie everywhere.更何况现在已经落花满地。
  5. 春且住Spring, please stay a while!春天啊，请你先留一留！
  6. 见说道天涯芳草无归路I hear sweet grass has grown to the sky's edge and blocked your road home.听说芳草已经长到天边，挡住了你回去的路。
  7. 怨春不语But spring says nothing, and I resent its silence.可春天不说话，真让人怨它。
  8. 算只有殷勤When I count it up, only one thing tries hard:算起来，只有那殷勤的
  9. 画檐蛛网the spider's web under the painted eaves,画檐下的蜘蛛网，
  10. 尽日惹飞絮catching the drifting willow fluff all day, as if to hold spring back.整天粘住飘飞的柳絮，想把春天留住。
  11. 长门事Think of the story of Changmen Palace:想起汉代长门宫的故事：
  12. 准拟佳期又误Empress Chen was sure the happy day would come, but it was missed again.陈皇后满心以为能重新得宠，好日子却一再落空。
  13. 蛾眉曾有人妒Because she was beautiful, others were once jealous of her.只因为她美丽，曾经遭人嫉妒。
  14. 千金纵买相如赋Even if she bought a fine piece by Sima Xiangru with a thousand gold,就算花千金买来司马相如的好文章，
  15. 脉脉此情谁诉to whom could she pour out the feelings in her heart?满心的深情又能向谁诉说？
  16. 君莫舞You who are pleased with yourselves, don't dance!那些得意的人，你们别高兴得跳舞！
  17. 君不见玉环飞燕皆尘土Don't you see? Even Yang Yuhuan and Zhao Feiyan, loved as they were, all turned to dust.你们没看见吗？杨玉环、赵飞燕那样得宠的人，最后都化成了尘土。
  18. 闲愁最苦A sorrow with no name hurts the most.说不出的闲愁最让人痛苦。
  19. 休去倚危栏Do not go and lean on the railing of the tall tower,不要去高楼上倚着栏杆远望，
  20. 斜阳正在for the setting sun is shining just那落日正照着
  21. 烟柳断肠处on the misty willows, a sight that breaks your heart.烟雾笼罩、让人伤心断肠的柳树。
02

Words字词注释

更能消gèng néng xiāo
gèng néng xiāo: how much more can it bear? Xiāo means to stand, to endure.更能消：还能经得起。消，经受、禁得住。
几番jǐ fān
jǐ fān: a few more times.几番：几次。
匆匆cōng cōng
cōng cōng: in a great hurry.匆匆：急急忙忙的样子。
长怕cháng pà
cháng pà: always afraid. Cháng here means "often, always".长怕：常常担心。长，同“常”。
落红luò hóng
luò hóng: fallen red petals, fallen flowers.落红：落下来的花瓣。
且住qiě zhù
qiě zhù: stay a while, please don't go yet.且住：暂且停下，先别走。
见说道jiàn shuō dào
jiàn shuō dào: I hear people say.见说道：听人说。
天涯tiān yá
tiān yá: the edge of the sky, the far ends of the earth.天涯：天边，极远的地方。
芳草无归路fāng cǎo wú guī lù
fāng cǎo wú guī lù: sweet grass has covered the land to the sky's edge, so spring has no road back home.芳草无归路：芳草长满天涯，把春天回去的路都遮没了。
算只有suàn zhǐ yǒu
suàn zhǐ yǒu: when you count it up, there is only.算只有：算起来只有。
殷勤yīn qín
yīn qín: eager and attentive, trying hard.殷勤：热心、尽心尽力。
画檐huà yán
huà yán: painted eaves, the edge of a roof decorated with colors.画檐：画着彩画的屋檐。
蛛网zhū wǎng
zhū wǎng: a spider's web.蛛网：蜘蛛网。
尽日jìn rì
jìn rì: all day long.尽日：整天。
惹飞絮rě fēi xù
rě fēi xù: catches the drifting willow fluff. Willow fluff is the white down on willow seeds that floats like cotton.惹飞絮：粘住飘飞的柳絮。柳絮是柳树种子上白色的绒毛，像棉花一样飘。
长门事cháng mén shì
cháng mén shì: the story of Changmen Palace. Empress Chen of Emperor Wu of Han lost his favor and lived alone in the lonely Changmen Palace. Legend says she paid a fortune to Sima Xiangru to write a piece called the Changmen Rhapsody, hoping the emperor would come back to her.长门事：汉武帝的陈皇后失宠后，住在冷清的长门宫。相传她花重金请司马相如写了《长门赋》，想让汉武帝回心转意。
准拟zhǔn nǐ
zhǔn nǐ: had fully expected.准拟：本来料定、满心以为。
佳期jiā qī
jiā qī: a happy meeting; here, the day she would be loved again.佳期：美好的约会，这里指重新得到宠爱的日子。
蛾眉é méi
é méi: eyebrows long and curved like a moth's feelers; it stands for a beautiful woman.蛾眉：像蚕蛾触角一样细长弯弯的眉毛，代指美丽的女子。
有人妒yǒu rén dù
yǒu rén dù: someone was jealous of her.有人妒：有人嫉妒。
千金qiān jīn
qiān jīn: a thousand pieces of gold, a huge sum.千金：很多很多钱。
纵zòng
zòng: even if.纵：即使。
相如赋xiàng rú fù
xiàng rú fù: a rhapsody by Sima Xiangru, a famous Han dynasty writer. A rhapsody (fù) is a kind of long, ornate writing.相如赋：司马相如写的赋。司马相如是汉代有名的文学家，赋是一种文体。
脉脉mò mò
mò mò: full of feeling but silent.脉脉：心里有很多话，却默默无言。
谁诉shuí sù
shuí sù: to whom can I tell it?谁诉：向谁诉说。
君jūn
jūn: you; here, those jealous people who are pleased with themselves.君：你们，这里指那些嫉妒别人、得意的人。
莫舞mò wǔ
mò wǔ: don't dance for joy.莫舞：不要得意地跳舞。
玉环yù huán
yù huán: Yang Yuhuan, Lady Yang, the favorite consort of Emperor Xuanzong of Tang.玉环：杨玉环，唐玄宗的宠妃杨贵妃。
飞燕fēi yàn
fēi yàn: Zhao Feiyan, the much-loved empress of Emperor Cheng of Han. Both she and Yang Yuhuan came to bad ends.飞燕：赵飞燕，汉成帝的皇后，很得宠。她和杨玉环最后都没有好结局。
皆尘土jiē chén tǔ
jiē chén tǔ: have all turned to dust.皆尘土：都化成了尘土。
闲愁xián chóu
xián chóu: a vague sorrow you cannot name or put down.闲愁：说不出来、放不下的忧愁。
危栏wēi lán
wēi lán: the railing of a tall tower. Wēi here means high.危栏：高楼上的栏杆。危，高。
斜阳xié yáng
xié yáng: the slanting sun, the sun about to set.斜阳：快要落山的太阳。
烟柳yān liǔ
yān liǔ: willows wrapped in mist.烟柳：笼罩在雾气里的柳树。
断肠处duàn cháng chù
duàn cháng chù: a place that breaks your heart.断肠处：让人伤心到极点的地方。
03

How to read it读音

更gèng
Read gèng here, meaning "still, any more", not gēng.这里读 gèng，意思是“再、还”，不读 gēng。
相xiàng
In the name Sima Xiangru, this character is read xiàng.司马相如的“相”读 xiàng。
脉mò
Read mòmò, meaning gazing silently with deep feeling; not mài.“脉脉”读 mòmò，是含情不语、默默望着的样子；不读 mài。
谁shuí
This character is often said shéi in speech today; in poems textbooks usually mark shuí. Both are accepted.“谁”现在口语多读 shéi，读诗时课本常标 shuí，两种都对。
04

Background创作背景

A short preface says this lyric was written in 1179. That year Xin Qiji was moved from a post in Hubei to one in Hunan, managing taxes and grain. A colleague, Wang Zhengzhi, gave him a farewell feast at the Xiaoshan Pavilion, and Xin wrote this lyric there. Xin had come south more than ten years before. He longed to lead an army to win back the north, but the court kept moving him from post to post and never gave him real power. When the lyric talks of spring slipping away and a beauty envied by others, it is really talking about his own worries. A Song writer, Luo Dajing, recorded that Emperor Xiaozong was said to be displeased when he read it.

词前有一段小序，说这首词写于淳熙六年（1179年）。那年辛弃疾从湖北调到湖南做管钱粮的官，同事王正之在小山亭摆酒为他送行，他就写了这首词。辛弃疾南归已经十几年，一心想带兵收复北方，朝廷却总把他调来调去，不给他重用。词里写春天留不住、美人被人嫉妒，其实是借这些说自己的心事。宋人罗大经的《鹤林玉露》记载，据说宋孝宗读了这首词，很不高兴。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207), also called Jiaxuan, was a great lyric poet of the Southern Song. He was born in the north, which was ruled by the Jin state. In his early twenties he led men to fight the Jin, then came south to the Song. All his life he wanted to win back the lost land, but he was often pushed aside or dismissed. Most of his lyrics are bold and strong. This one is soft and indirect: he hides his anger inside a story of spring and of a lonely beauty.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），号稼轩，南宋大词人。他生在被金国占领的北方，二十出头就带人起兵抗金，后来回到南宋。他一辈子想收复失地，却多次被冷落、罢官。他的词大多豪迈有力，这首却写得柔婉含蓄，把满腔的不平藏在惜春和美人的故事里。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is about spring leaving. The poet asks: how many more storms can it bear? Then he begs spring to stay, saying the grass has already blocked its road home. Spring pays no attention. Only the spider's web under the eaves catches willow fluff all day, as if it too wants to keep spring. This tiny picture is sweet and sad at once. The second stanza turns to the story of Empress Chen. A beauty is envied, and even a fine piece of writing bought with gold cannot carry her feelings to anyone. Then the poet tells the proud ones: don't be glad too soon; Yang Yuhuan and Zhao Feiyan are dust now. At the end he does not name his sorrow. He only says: don't climb the tower, for the setting sun is on the misty willows. The sorrow stays in that evening light. Every line seems to be about spring or a beauty, but really it is about his own pain at having no way to serve his country.

上阕写春天要走了。词人先问：还经得起几番风雨？再求春天留一留，说芳草已经挡住了你回去的路。春天不理他，只有屋檐下的蜘蛛网，整天粘着柳絮，好像也想留住春天。这个小小的画面又可爱又心酸。下阕换成了陈皇后的故事：美人被人嫉妒，花千金买来好文章也没人听她诉说。接着词人对那些得意的人说：别高兴得太早，杨玉环、赵飞燕都成了尘土。最后不直接说愁，只说别去高楼远望，夕阳正照着烟雾里的柳树。愁就留在这片暮色里。整首词句句写春、写美人，其实句句写自己报国无门的苦闷。
