Song lyric · ci lyric to the tune Di Tai Chun, two stanzas宋词 · 词·帝台春（双调）

dì帝tái台chūn春·fāng芳cǎo草bì碧sè色

lǐ李jiǎ甲Li Jia · Song宋

Di Tai Chun: The Fragrant Grass Is Green

 

fāng芳cǎo草bì碧sè色

qī萋qī萋biàn遍nán南mò陌

nuǎn暖xù絮luàn乱hóng红

yě也zhī知rén人

chūn春chóu愁wú无lì力

yì忆dé得yíng盈yíng盈shí拾cuì翠lǚ侣

gòng共xié携shǎng赏

fèng凤chéng城hán寒shí食

dào到jīn今lái来

hǎi海jiǎo角féng逢chūn春

tiān天yá涯wéi为kè客

chóu愁xuán旋shì释

hái还sì似zhī织

lèi泪àn暗shì拭

yòu又tōu偷dī滴

màn谩zhù伫lì立

biàn遍yǐ倚wēi危lán阑

jìn尽huáng黄hūn昏

yě也zhǐ只shì是

mù暮yún云níng凝bì碧

pàn拚zé则ér而jīn今yǐ已pàn拚le了

wàng忘zé则zěn怎shēng生biàn便wàng忘dé得

yòu又hái还wèn问lín鳞hóng鸿

shì试chóng重xún寻xiāo消xī息

In the database text, the line "pan ze er jin yi pan le" has its characters jumbled and uses a different character for pàn. This follows the common text.

资料库原文第二十一句作“拼则而已今拼了”，字序错乱，这里按通行本作“拚则而今已拚了”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 芳草碧色The fragrant grass is green,芳草一片青绿，
  2. 萋萋遍南陌growing thick along every southern path.茂茂盛盛地长满了南边的小路。
  3. 暖絮乱红Willow fluff in the warm air and petals blown about,暖风里的柳絮和乱飞的落花，
  4. 也知人they too seem to know好像也懂得人的心思，
  5. 春愁无力how spring sorrow leaves me without strength.知道人被春愁压得没有一点力气。
  6. 忆得盈盈拾翠侣I remember the lovely girl who came with me on spring outings,还记得那位美丽的女伴，
  7. 共携赏how we went out hand in hand和我手拉着手，一起游赏
  8. 凤城寒食to enjoy the Cold Food Festival in the capital.京城寒食节的春光。
  9. 到今来But now,可到了今天，
  10. 海角逢春I meet the spring at the corner of the sea,我在海角遇到春天，
  11. 天涯为客a wanderer at the edge of the sky.在天涯做一个漂泊的客人。
  12. 愁旋释No sooner is my sorrow loosened愁绪刚刚解开，
  13. 还似织than it is woven thick again.又像织布一样密密地缠上来。
  14. 泪暗拭I secretly wipe away my tears,眼泪偷偷擦干，
  15. 又偷滴and secretly they fall again.又偷偷地流下来。
  16. 谩伫立I stand a long time, in vain,白白地久久站着，
  17. 遍倚危阑leaning on every part of the high railing,把高楼的栏杆都靠遍了，
  18. 尽黄昏all through the dusk,一直站到黄昏过去，
  19. 也只是and all I see看到的也只是
  20. 暮云凝碧are evening clouds gathered in the blue-green sky.傍晚的云停在青碧的天空里。
  21. 拚则而今已拚了If I am to let go, then now I have let go;说放下吧，现在我已经豁出去放下了；
  22. 忘则怎生便忘得if I am to forget, how can I forget just like that?说忘掉吧，可又怎么能说忘就忘呢？
  23. 又还问鳞鸿So again I ask the fish and the geese that carry letters,于是我又去问问送信的鱼和雁，
  24. 试重寻消息and try once more to find news of her.试着再一次打听她的消息。
02

Words字词注释

萋萋qī qī
qī qī: lush and thick, of grass.萋萋：草长得很茂盛的样子。
南陌nán mò
nán mò: the southern paths. Mò is a path between fields.南陌：南边的小路。陌，田间小路。
暖絮nuǎn xù
nuǎn xù: willow fluff drifting in the warm air.暖絮：暖风里飘的柳絮。
乱红luàn hóng
luàn hóng: flower petals falling every which way.乱红：纷纷飘落的花瓣。
春愁无力chūn chóu wú lì
chūn chóu wú lì: so weighed down by spring sorrow that one has no strength.春愁无力：因为春天的愁绪，人没有一点力气。
盈盈yíng yíng
yíng yíng: graceful, here meaning a lovely young woman.盈盈：姿态美好的样子，这里指美丽的女子。
拾翠侣shí cuì lǚ
shí cuì lǚ: a companion on a spring outing. Shí cuì means picking up kingfisher feathers, and came to mean women's spring outings.拾翠侣：一起春游的女伴。拾翠，捡拾翠鸟的羽毛，后来指女子春天出游。
携赏xié shǎng
xié shǎng: to go out together hand in hand to enjoy the sights.携赏：手拉着手一起游玩观赏。
凤城fèng chéng
fèng chéng: Phoenix City, a fine name for the capital.凤城：京城的美称。
寒食hán shí
hán shí: the Cold Food Festival, a day or two before Qingming. People did not light fires to cook, and often went out to enjoy the spring.寒食：寒食节，在清明节前一两天，这一天不生火做饭，人们常出门踏青。
海角hǎi jiǎo
hǎi jiǎo: the farthest corner by the sea.海角：海边极远的地方。
天涯为客tiān yá wéi kè
tiān yá wéi kè: to be a stranger at the edge of the sky, that is, to wander far from home.天涯为客：在天边一样远的地方做客，就是漂泊在外。
旋释xuán shì
xuán shì: just let go. Xuán means "soon, just"; shì means to loosen.旋释：刚刚解开。旋，随即、刚刚；释，放下、解开。
还似织hái sì zhī
hái sì zhī: again woven thick, like cloth on a loom.还似织：又像织布一样密密地交织起来。
暗拭àn shì
àn shì: to wipe secretly.暗拭：偷偷地擦。
谩伫立màn zhù lì
màn zhù lì: to stand a long time, in vain. Màn means "in vain"; zhù lì means to stand still for long.谩伫立：白白地久久站着。谩，同“漫”，徒然；伫立，长时间站着。
危阑wēi lán
wēi lán: the railing of a tall building. Wēi means high.危阑：高楼上的栏杆。危，高。
尽黄昏jìn huáng hūn
jìn huáng hūn: all through dusk.尽黄昏：整个黄昏，一直到黄昏结束。
暮云凝碧mù yún níng bì
mù yún níng bì: evening clouds gathered still in the blue-green sky.暮云凝碧：傍晚的云凝聚在青碧的天空里。
拚pàn
pàn: to give up, to let go no matter what.拚：舍弃，豁出去不管了。
而今ér jīn
ér jīn: now.而今：现在。
怎生zěn shēng
zěn shēng: how.怎生：怎么。
鳞鸿lín hóng
lín hóng: fish and wild geese. In old stories they carried letters, so the words stand for letters or messengers.鳞鸿：鱼和大雁。古人传说鱼和雁能传书信，所以用来指书信、送信的人。
消息xiāo xī
xiāo xī: news.消息：音信。
03

How to read it读音

谩màn
Here this character is used for màn (in vain) and is read màn.“谩”在这里同“漫”，意思是徒然、白白地，读 màn。
拚pàn
This character means to give up regardless and is read pàn. The database text writes it with the character now read pīn.“拚”是舍弃不顾的意思，读 pàn。资料库原文写作“拼”，今天也有人读 pīn。
为wéi
Here it means "to be, to act as" and is read wéi.这里是“做、当”的意思，读 wéi。
旋xuán
Here it means "soon, just" and is read xuán.这里是“随即、刚刚”的意思，读 xuán。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about a man far from home who misses a former love in spring. He remembers a Cold Food Festival long ago, when he and his sweetheart enjoyed the spring together in the capital. Now he is at the edge of the world, spending another spring alone. The lyric does not say when or where it was written, and very little is known about the poet's life, so the setting can no longer be told.

这首词写的是一个漂泊在外的人，在春天里想念从前的恋人。他想起当年寒食节，和心上人一起在京城游春；如今自己却远在天涯，独自过着又一个春天。词里没有写明是哪一年、在哪里写的，作者的生平也知道得很少，所以写作背景已经说不清了。

05

The poet作者

Li Jia, courtesy name Jingyuan, was a Northern Song ci poet. Very little is known about his life. Records say he came from Huating, around today's Songjiang in Shanghai, held minor local posts, and was a skilled painter. Few of his lyrics survive; this one was chosen for Three Hundred Song Lyrics.

李甲，字景元，北宋词人，生平事迹留下来的很少。据记载他是华亭（今上海松江一带）人，做过地方小官，还擅长画画。他的词留下来的不多，这首《帝台春》被收进了《宋词三百首》。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza begins with spring. Fragrant grass fills the paths, and willow fluff and petals fly in the warm wind, as if they knew how weak he feels. Then he remembers the past, when he spent the Cold Food Festival in the capital with his sweetheart, so happy. Now he is a stranger at the edge of the world. It is spring again, but his heart is completely different. The second stanza opens with four short lines of three characters each: the sorrow is let go and woven up again; the tears are wiped away and fall again. The rhythm is short and quick, like sob after sob. Then he leans on every part of the railing, and sees only evening clouds. The next two lines are in plain speech: if I am to let go, I have let go; but forget her? How could I! They show the struggle inside a person very truly. In the end he still cannot help asking for news of her.

上片从春景写起：芳草长满小路，柳絮和落花在暖风里乱飞，好像也知道人没有力气。然后回忆从前，和心上人在京城过寒食节，多么快乐；再看现在，自己在天涯海角做客，同样是春天，心情完全不同。下片连用四个三字短句：“愁旋释，还似织，泪暗拭，又偷滴。”愁刚放下又缠上来，泪刚擦干又流下来，节奏又短又急，像一声声抽泣。接着他靠遍栏杆，看到的只有暮云。“拚则而今已拚了，忘则怎生便忘得”用的是大白话：说放下，已经放下了；说忘掉，哪里忘得了！这两句把人心里的矛盾说得非常真切。最后他还是忍不住去打听她的消息。
