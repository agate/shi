清平调·其三

李白Li Bai · Tang唐

Qing Ping Tune, No. 3

 

míng名huā花qīng倾guó国liǎng两xiāng相huān欢

cháng长dé得jūn君wáng王dài带xiào笑kān看

jiě解shì释chūn春fēng风wú无xiàn限hèn恨

chén沉xiāng香tíng亭běi北yǐ倚lán阑gān干

Tap a character for its pinyin. Underlined words have notes. 一 and 不 are marked with the tone you actually say.点一个字看拼音。字下有虚线的，点开有注释。“一”“不”标的是实际读出来的变调。

The corpus writes the pavilion's name with an old form of the character chén. Common editions use the modern form. The meaning is the same.

资料库原文写作“沈香亭”。“沈”是“沉”的古字，通行本写作“沉香亭”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 名花倾国两相欢The famous peonies and the peerless beauty set each other off, both a delight,名贵的牡丹和倾国的美人互相映衬，都让人欢喜，
  2. 长得君王带笑看so the emperor always gazes at them with a smile.惹得君王总是笑着观赏。
  3. 解释春风无限恨North of Chenxiang Pavilion, she leans on the railing,在沉香亭北边，美人靠着栏杆，
  4. 沉香亭北倚阑干and all the endless worries the spring wind brings melt away.君王心里有再多的烦恼，也都被消除了。
02

Words字词注释

名花míng huā
míng huā: a famous flower; here the peony.名花：有名的花，这里指牡丹。
倾国qīng guó
qīng guó: so beautiful that a whole country falls for her; here Lady Yang, the emperor's favorite consort.倾国：美得能让一国的人都为她倾倒，这里指杨贵妃。
两相欢liǎng xiāng huān
liǎng xiāng huān: the flower and the beauty set each other off, and both bring joy.两相欢：花和美人互相映衬，都让人喜欢。
长得cháng dé
cháng dé: always able to make.长得：常常得到，总能让。
君王jūn wáng
jūn wáng: the ruler; here Emperor Xuanzong.君王：皇帝，这里指唐玄宗。
解释jiě shì
jiě shì: to melt away, to ease. Not today's meaning of to explain.解释：消除、化解。和今天“解释”的意思不同。
春风chūn fēng
chūn fēng: the spring wind; here it stands for the emperor.春风：这里代指君王。
无限恨wú xiàn hèn
wú xiàn hèn: endless worries and gloom.无限恨：说不完的烦恼和愁闷。
沉香亭chén xiāng tíng
Chenxiang Pavilion: a pavilion in the Xingqing Palace, built of precious aloeswood.沉香亭：皇宫兴庆宫里的一座亭子，用名贵的沉香木做成。
阑干lán gān
lán gān: a railing.阑干：栏杆。
03

How to read it读音

长cháng
Here this character is read cháng and means always.长在这里读 cháng，意思是“常常、总是”。
看kān
Here this character is read kān, in the level tone, so it rhymes with huān and gān. Some read it kàn.看在这里读 kān，是平声，和“欢、干”押韵。也有人读 kàn。
04

Background创作背景

There are three Qing Ping Tunes, and this is the third. They are usually dated to a spring in the early Tianbao years, one view says 743. Li Bai was then a court writer in Chang'an, someone who stayed near the emperor to write poems. The peonies by Chenxiang Pavilion were in bloom. Emperor Xuanzong and Lady Yang came to enjoy them, and the emperor sent for Li Bai to write new songs. Li Bai quickly wrote these three, and the court musicians sang them.

《清平调》共三首，这是第三首。一般认为写于唐玄宗天宝初年（一说天宝二年，743年）的春天。当时李白在长安做翰林供奉，就是陪在皇帝身边写诗文的人。宫中沉香亭前的牡丹开了，唐玄宗和杨贵妃一起赏花，让人把李白叫来写新词。李白很快写成了这三首，由乐师配曲演唱。

05

The poet作者

Li Bai (701–762) was a great Tang poet, called the Immortal of Poetry. In his forties he was called to Chang'an to write poems for the emperor and became very famous, but he left after less than three years. These three Qing Ping Tunes are his best-known works from the palace, rich in color and light in step.

李白（701—762），唐代大诗人，被称为“诗仙”。他四十多岁时被召到长安，在皇帝身边写诗，名气很大，但不到三年就离开了。这三首《清平调》是他在宫中最有名的作品，写得华丽又轻快。

06

The mood意境

The first two songs say the flower is like the lady and the lady is like the flower. This one puts the flower, the lady and the emperor together. The first line says the peony and the beauty both bring joy; you cannot tell which is lovelier. The second line shows the emperor watching them, a smile always on his face. The third line says that with such flowers and such a lady, any worry of spring melts away. The last line stops on one picture: north of the pavilion, the lady leans lightly on the railing. The poem ends there and says no more. It leaves a calm, bright scene of flower viewing in the palace for us to imagine.

前两首写花像人、人像花，这一首把花、美人和君王放在一起。第一句说牡丹和美人“两相欢”，分不清哪个更美。第二句写君王看着她们，脸上一直带着笑。第三句说，有了这样的花和人，春天里再多的烦恼也能化开。最后一句收在一个画面上：沉香亭北边，美人轻轻靠着栏杆。诗到这里就停了，不再多说，留下一幅安静、明丽的宫中赏花图，让人自己去想。
