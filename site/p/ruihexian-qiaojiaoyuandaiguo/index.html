Song lyric · ci lyric in two stanzas宋词 · 词·双调

ruì瑞hè鹤xiān仙·qiǎo悄jiāo郊yuán原dài带guō郭

zhōu周bāng邦yàn彦Zhou Bangyan · Song宋

Rui He Xian: The Quiet Countryside Around the City Wall

 

qiǎo悄jiāo郊yuán原dài带guō郭

xíng行lù路yǒng永

kè客qù去chē车chén尘mò漠mò漠

xié斜yáng阳yìng映shān山luò落

liǎn敛yú余hóng红yóu犹liàn恋gū孤chéng城lán阑jiǎo角

líng凌bō波bù步ruò弱

guò过duǎn短tíng亭hé何yòng用sù素yuē约

yǒu有liú流yīng莺quàn劝wǒ我

chóng重jiě解xiù绣ān鞍

huǎn缓yǐn引chūn春zhuó酌

bú不jì记guī归shí时zǎo早mù暮

shàng上mǎ马shuí谁fú扶

xǐng醒mián眠zhū朱gé阁

jīng惊biāo飙dòng动mù幕

fú扶cán残zuì醉

rào绕hóng红yào药

tàn叹xī西yuán园yǐ已shì是huā花shēn深wú无dì地

dōng东fēng风hé何shì事yòu又è恶

rèn任liú流guāng光guò过què却

yóu犹xǐ喜dòng洞tiān天zì自lè乐

The common text writes the character for "railing" in line 5 with an older form; the source uses the modern form. The meaning is the same. The source also keeps an old form of yú in that line, written in its usual simplified form here.

通行本作“犹恋孤城阑角”，底本作“栏角”，意思相同。底本的“馀”是繁体转换留下的写法，今写作“余”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 悄郊原带郭Quiet fields wrap around the city wall.静悄悄的郊野环绕着城墙，
  2. 行路永The road ahead is long.要走的路还很长，
  3. 客去车尘漠漠The traveler leaves, and the dust behind his cart hangs thick.行人走了，车后的尘土一片迷蒙。
  4. 斜阳映山落The slanting sun lights the hills as it sinks,斜阳映着山，慢慢落下，
  5. 敛余红犹恋孤城阑角and, gathering its last red light, still lingers on a corner of the lonely city's railings.收起最后一点红光，还恋恋不舍地停在孤城的栏杆角上。
  6. 凌波步弱A woman walks by with light, delicate steps.一位女子迈着轻盈柔弱的步子，
  7. 过短亭何用素约We meet at the roadside pavilion; no plan was needed.在短亭相遇，哪里用得着事先约好。
  8. 有流莺劝我A girl who sings like an oriole urges me to stay,有像黄莺一样会唱歌的人劝我留下，
  9. 重解绣鞍so once more I take off the embroidered saddle我又解下了绣花马鞍，
  10. 缓引春酌and slowly drink the spring wine.慢慢喝着春酒。
  11. 不记归时早暮I do not remember if I went back early or late,不记得是早还是晚回去的，
  12. 上马谁扶or who helped me onto my horse;上马时是谁扶我上去的，
  13. 醒眠朱阁I woke up asleep in a red chamber.醒来时已经睡在红色的楼阁里。
  14. 惊飙动幕A sudden gust shakes the curtains.突然一阵大风吹动了帘幕，
  15. 扶残醉Still half drunk,我带着还没醒透的醉意，
  16. 绕红药I wander around the red peonies.绕着红芍药走来走去。
  17. 叹西园已是花深无地Alas, in the west garden fallen flowers lie so deep the ground is gone,可叹西园里落花已经堆得看不见地面，
  18. 东风何事又恶so why is the east wind blowing so hard again?东风为什么又刮得这么猛？
  19. 任流光过却Let time flow past as it will;就任凭时光这样流走吧，
  20. 犹喜洞天自乐I am still glad to find my own joy in my little world.还好我在自己的小天地里，自得其乐。
02

Words字词注释

悄qiǎo
qiǎo: quiet, hushed.悄：静悄悄的。
郊原带郭jiāo yuán dài guō
jiāo yuán dài guō: the fields outside town wrap around the city wall like a belt. Guō is the outer city wall.郊原带郭：城外的原野像带子一样围着城墙。郭，外城墙。
行路永xíng lù yǒng
xíng lù yǒng: the road ahead is long.行路永：要走的路很长。
漠漠mò mò
mò mò: thick and hazy with dust.漠漠：尘土弥漫、看不清的样子。
敛余红liǎn yú hóng
liǎn yú hóng: the sun gathers in the last of its red light. Liǎn means to draw in.敛余红：夕阳收起最后一点红光。敛，收起。
阑角lán jiǎo
lán jiǎo: the corner of a railing, here on the city tower.阑角：栏杆的一角，这里指城楼上的栏杆。
凌波步弱líng bō bù ruò
líng bō bù ruò: a woman's light, delicate steps. The phrase comes from Cao Zhi's poem on the River Luo goddess, who walked as lightly as if on the waves.凌波步弱：女子走路轻盈柔弱。“凌波”出自曹植《洛神赋》，形容美人脚步轻得像在水波上走。
短亭duǎn tíng
duǎn tíng: a roadside shelter for travelers. In old times there was a short pavilion every five li and a long one every ten.短亭：路边供行人休息的亭子。古时五里一短亭，十里一长亭。
何用素约hé yòng sù yuē
hé yòng sù yuē: no need to have planned it beforehand. Sù means in advance.何用素约：哪里用得着事先约好。素，平素、事先。
流莺liú yīng
liú yīng: an oriole flitting about and singing. Here it stands for a singing girl who urges him to drink.流莺：飞来飞去、叫声婉转的黄莺。这里比喻劝酒的歌女。
绣鞍xiù ān
xiù ān: an embroidered saddle. Taking it off again means getting off the horse to stay.绣鞍：绣着花纹的马鞍。重解绣鞍，就是又下马留下来。
缓引春酌huǎn yǐn chūn zhuó
huǎn yǐn chūn zhuó: slowly drinking spring wine. Yǐn is to lift the cup; zhuó is wine.缓引春酌：慢慢地喝着春酒。引，举杯；酌，酒。
醒眠朱阁xǐng mián zhū gé
xǐng mián zhū gé: waking to find himself asleep in a red-painted chamber.醒眠朱阁：醒来时发现自己睡在红色的楼阁里。
惊飙jīng biāo
jīng biāo: a sudden gust. Biāo is a storm wind.惊飙：突然刮起的大风。飙，暴风。
动幕dòng mù
dòng mù: stirring the curtains.动幕：吹动帘幕。
残醉cán zuì
cán zuì: drunkenness that has not quite worn off.残醉：还没完全醒的醉意。
红药hóng yào
hóng yào: red herbaceous peonies.红药：红色的芍药花。
花深无地huā shēn wú dì
huā shēn wú dì: fallen flowers lie so deep that no ground shows.花深无地：落花堆得厚厚的，看不见地面了。
恶è
è: fierce, harsh.恶：猛烈，凶。
任流光过却rèn liú guāng guò què
rèn liú guāng guò què: let time flow past as it will. Liú guāng is time slipping by like water.任流光过却：任凭时光就这样流走。流光，像水一样流走的时光。
洞天dòng tiān
dòng tiān: in Daoist belief, a cave world where immortals live. Here it means his own peaceful little world.洞天：道家说的神仙住的地方，这里指自己安闲的小天地。
03

How to read it读音

悄qiǎo
Here it means quiet and still, read qiǎo.“悄”在这里是静悄悄的意思，读 qiǎo。
重chóng
Here it means "once again", so it is read chóng.“重解”是再一次解下，“重”读 chóng。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric shows a drunken outing after a farewell, and the feelings that come on waking. Its date can no longer be fixed. A Song dynasty notebook tells a story: in old age Zhou Bangyan lived in Qiantang and wrote this lyric in a dream. Later, during the Fang La uprising, what happened to him as he fled matched the lyric line by line. The story sounds like a legend and may not be true, but it shows the lyric was already famous in the Song.

这首词写的是一次送别后的醉游和醒来后的感慨，写作时间已经不能确定。宋人笔记里有个说法：周邦彦晚年住在钱塘，梦中写成这首词，后来遇到方腊起事，他逃难途中的经历竟和词里写的一一对上。这个故事带有传奇色彩，不一定可靠，但可见这首词在宋代就很有名。

05

The poet作者

Zhou Bangyan (1056–1121) was a Northern Song poet from Qiantang, today's Hangzhou. He knew music deeply and once ran the court's music office. He was strict about the rules of rhythm and sound. He liked to lay out a story from start to finish, with many turns along the way. Later readers saw him as the great master of the gentle, graceful style of ci.

周邦彦（1056—1121），字美成，号清真居士，钱塘（今浙江杭州）人，北宋词人。他精通音乐，主管过朝廷的音乐机构大晟府。他写词讲究格律，善于把一件事从头到尾细细铺开，层层转折，被后人看作婉约词的集大成者。

06

The mood意境

This lyric reads like a diary from one evening to the next day. It opens at dusk outside the city. The road is long, the dust from the carts hangs in the air, and the setting sun seems unwilling to go, still caught on a corner of the city railings. Then he meets a lovely woman by chance. Someone urges him to drink, so he simply gets off his horse and stays, sipping slowly. The second stanza is fun. He got so drunk that he cannot remember how he went home or who helped him onto his horse. He wakes up already asleep in a chamber. A gust of wind rouses him. Still tipsy, he goes to look at the peonies, but finds the garden deep in fallen petals, and the east wind still blowing hard. Spring is leaving, and he feels sad. Yet in the end he says: let it go, I can be happy in my own little world. Inside this letting go there is a quiet sigh.

这首词像一段从傍晚到第二天的日记。开头是城外的黄昏：路长长的，车尘迷蒙，夕阳舍不得落下，还挂在城楼的栏杆角上。接着偶遇佳人，有人劝酒，他干脆下马留下，慢慢喝起来。下片很有意思：他喝得太醉，连怎么回去、谁扶他上马都不记得，醒来已经睡在楼里。一阵风把他吹醒，他扶着醉意去看芍药，却看到满园落花，东风还在使劲吹。春天要走了，他心里难过，但最后说：随它去吧，我在自己的小天地里也能快乐。这份“放下”里，其实藏着一点无奈。
