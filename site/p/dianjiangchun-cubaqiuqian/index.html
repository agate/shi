点绛唇·蹴罢秋千

无名氏Anonymous · Song宋

Dian Jiang Chun: Done with the Swing

 

cù蹴bà罢qiū秋qiān千

qǐ起lái来yōng慵zhěng整xiān纤xiān纤shǒu手

lù露nóng浓huā花shòu瘦

bó薄hàn汗qīng轻yī衣tòu透

jiàn见kè客rù入lái来

wà袜chǎn刬jīn金chāi钗liū溜

hé和xiū羞zǒu走

yǐ倚mén门huí回shǒu首

què却bǎ把qīng青méi梅xiù嗅

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 蹴罢秋千Done with the swing,荡完了秋千，
  2. 起来慵整纤纤手she gets up and lazily rubs her slender hands.她起身，懒懒地揉一揉细嫩的手。
  3. 露浓花瘦In the garden the dew is heavy and the flowers look thin.园子里露水很重，花儿细细瘦瘦的。
  4. 薄汗轻衣透A light sweat has soaked through her thin clothes.细细的汗水湿透了薄薄的衣裳。
  5. 见客入来Suddenly she sees a guest come in.忽然看见有客人进来，
  6. 袜刬金钗溜She runs in her socks, with no time for shoes, and her gold hairpin slips out.她来不及穿鞋，只穿着袜子就跑，头上的金钗也滑落了。
  7. 和羞走Shy and flustered, she hurries away.她又害羞又着急地跑开。
  8. 倚门回首At the door she leans on it and turns to look back,跑到门边，又靠着门回过头来，
  9. 却把青梅嗅pretending to sniff a green plum.却装作在闻青梅的香味。
02

Words字词注释

蹴cù
cù: to kick or push off with the foot, as when swinging.蹴：踩，蹬。这里指荡秋千时用脚蹬。
罢bà
bà: finished, done.罢：完了，结束。
慵整yōng zhěng
yōng zhěng: to tidy lazily. Yōng means lazy, languid.慵整：懒懒地整理。慵，懒洋洋的。
纤纤手xiān xiān shǒu
xiān xiān shǒu: slender, delicate hands.纤纤手：又细又嫩的手。
露浓花瘦lù nóng huā shòu
lù nóng huā shòu: the dew is heavy and the flowers look thin and small.露浓花瘦：露水很重，花儿显得又小又细。
薄汗轻衣透bó hàn qīng yī tòu
bó hàn qīng yī tòu: a light sweat soaks through her thin clothes.薄汗轻衣透：细细的汗把薄薄的衣服都湿透了。
袜刬wà chǎn
wà chǎn: walking in socks only, with no time to put on shoes.袜刬：只穿着袜子走，鞋都来不及穿。
金钗溜jīn chāi liū
jīn chāi liū: her gold hairpin slips out. A hairpin is an ornament women wore in their hair.金钗溜：头上的金钗滑落下来。金钗，女子插在头发上的首饰。
和羞走hé xiū zǒu
hé xiū zǒu: she runs off, full of shyness. Hé here means with.和羞走：带着害羞跑开。和，带着。
倚门回首yǐ mén huí shǒu
yǐ mén huí shǒu: leaning on the door, she turns her head to look back.倚门回首：靠着门回过头来看。
却把青梅嗅què bǎ qīng méi xiù
què bǎ qīng méi xiù: but she pretends to sniff a green plum. A green plum is an unripe plum.却把青梅嗅：却假装去闻青梅。青梅，还没熟的梅子。
03

How to read it读音

薄bó
In bó hàn (a light sweat) the character is read bó. In everyday speech, meaning thin, it is often read báo.“薄汗”的“薄”读 bó，指一层细细的汗。口语里说“很薄”时读 báo。
刬chǎn
Read chǎn. Wà chǎn means walking in socks only, without shoes.读 chǎn。“袜刬”是只穿着袜子走路，没穿鞋。
04

Background创作背景

This poem shows one small moment: a young girl in a garden at the end of spring has just finished swinging when a guest suddenly arrives. Who wrote it is debated. Some books say Li Qingzhao, some say the author is unknown, and others name Su Shi or Zhou Bangyan. Today many people read it as a work of the young Li Qingzhao, but this cannot be proven.

这首词写的是一个少女在春末夏初的园子里荡秋千，忽然有客人来了的一个小片段。作者是谁有争议：有的书说是李清照写的，有的说是无名氏，也有人说是苏轼或周邦彦写的。现在很多人把它当作李清照少女时期的作品来读，但这一点并不能确定。

05

The poet作者

The author is unknown. The most common view is Li Qingzhao (1084–about 1155), from Zhangqiu near Jinan, the most famous woman writer of song lyrics in the Song dynasty. The lyrics of her girlhood are lively and bright, mostly about life at home. This poem feels much like her early work, so many people think she wrote it.

作者不详。最常见的说法是李清照（1084—约1155），号易安居士，济南章丘人，宋代最有名的女词人。她少女时期的词活泼、明快，写的多是闺中生活，这首词的风格和她早期的作品很像，所以很多人认为是她写的。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a short film. The first half is a quiet picture: the girl has just finished swinging, lazily rubs her hands, and has a light sweat on her skin. The heavy dew and thin flowers describe the garden, and also set off how young and tender she is. In the second half the picture suddenly moves: a guest is coming! She is so flustered that she runs off in her socks, and her gold hairpin falls out. Red in the face, she hurries away. But at the door she cannot help looking back, and she pretends to sniff a green plum to hide her curiosity. Shy, flustered and curious: a few small actions bring the girl's feelings to life.

这首词像一段小电影。上阕是安静的画面：少女刚荡完秋千，懒懒地揉着手，身上出了一层细汗。“露浓花瘦”写园子里的花，也映衬出少女的娇嫩。下阕画面忽然动起来：有客人来了！她慌得鞋都没穿，金钗也掉了，红着脸跑开。可是跑到门口，她又忍不住回头看，还假装在闻青梅，好掩饰自己的好奇。害羞、慌张、好奇，几个小动作就把一个少女的心思写活了。
