贺新郎·篆缕销金鼎

李玉Li Yu · Song宋

To the Tune He Xin Lang: Incense Smoke Curls Away in the Golden Burner

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 篆缕销金鼎In the golden burner the curling incense smoke burns away.金色香炉里，弯弯的香烟慢慢烧完了。
  2. 醉沉沉庭阴转午I am heavy with wine; the courtyard shade has moved round to noon.我醉得昏昏沉沉，院子里的树影已经移到了中午，
  3. 画堂人静The painted hall is quiet, no one stirring.华美的屋子里静悄悄的，没有一点人声。
  4. 芳草王孙知何处Where is he now, the wanderer who never comes home?像王孙一样远游的他，不知道在哪里？
  5. 惟有杨花糁径Only willow down lies scattered over the path.只有杨花，撒满了小路。
  6. 渐玉枕腾腾春醒On the jade pillow I slowly wake, hazy, from a spring drunken sleep.靠着玉枕，我迷迷糊糊地从春天的醉梦里醒来。
  7. 帘外残红春已透Outside the curtain the flowers have fallen; spring is nearly gone.帘子外面落花满地，春天已经快过完了，
  8. 镇无聊殢酒厌厌病All day I am weary, lost in wine, listless as if ill.我整天无聊，被酒缠着，没精打采像生了病。
  9. 云鬓乱My cloud of hair is tangled,浓密的头发乱了，
  10. 未忺整and I do not feel like fixing it.也不想去梳理。
  11. 江南旧事休重省Do not think again of those old days south of the river.江南的往事，不要再去回想了。
  12. 遍天涯寻消问息I have asked for news of him to the ends of the earth,我走遍天涯打听他的消息，
  13. 断鸿难倩but not even a lone wild goose will carry my letter.连离群的孤雁也没法托它带信。
  14. 月满西楼凭栏久Moonlight fills the west tower; I lean on the railing a long time.月光洒满西楼，我靠着栏杆站了很久，
  15. 依旧归期未定Still the day of his return is not set.他回来的日子还是没有定下。
  16. 又只恐瓶沉金井And I fear our love will sink like a jar to the bottom of the well.我又怕，我们的感情会像沉进井底的瓶子一样断了。
  17. 嘶骑不来银烛暗No neighing horse comes, and the silver candle grows dim.听不到他骑马回来的马叫声，蜡烛也快烧完暗下去了，
  18. 枉教人立尽梧桐影All for nothing I stand until the paulownia's shadow is gone.白白让我站到梧桐树影都移走了。
  19. 谁伴我Who will keep me company有谁来陪我？
  20. 对鸾镜as I face the lonely luan-bird mirror?只能对着那面孤单的鸾镜。
02

Words字词注释

篆缕zhuàn lǚ
zhuàn lǚ: thin threads of incense smoke curling like seal script. Zhuàn is an old style of writing with curving strokes.篆缕：香炉里升起的细烟，弯弯曲曲像篆字。篆，一种古老的字体，笔画弯曲。
销金鼎xiāo jīn dǐng
xiāo jīn dǐng: burning away in the golden burner. Jīn dǐng is a bronze incense burner.销金鼎：在金色的香炉里慢慢烧完。金鼎，铜做的香炉。
醉沉沉zuì chén chén
zuì chén chén: heavy and drowsy with wine.醉沉沉：喝醉了，昏昏沉沉。
庭阴转午tíng yīn zhuǎn wǔ
tíng yīn zhuǎn wǔ: the shade in the courtyard has moved round; it is already noon.庭阴转午：院子里树的影子移动，已经到了中午。
画堂huà táng
huà táng: a beautifully decorated hall.画堂：装饰华美的屋子。
芳草王孙fāng cǎo wáng sūn
fāng cǎo wáng sūn: an old poem says "The young lord travels and does not come home, while the spring grass grows thick." After that, wáng sūn came to mean someone far away who does not return. Here it is the man she misses.芳草王孙：《楚辞》里说“王孙游兮不归，春草生兮萋萋”，后来用“王孙”指在外远游、迟迟不回的人。这里指她想念的那个人。
惟有wéi yǒu
wéi yǒu: only.惟有：只有。
糁径sǎn jìng
sǎn jìng: scattered all over the path. Sǎn means to scatter.糁径：撒满了小路。糁，散落。
腾腾téng téng
téng téng: hazy, half awake.腾腾：迷迷糊糊的样子。
春醒chūn xǐng
chūn xǐng: waking up from wine and dreams in spring.春醒：春天里从醉中、梦中醒来。
残红cán hóng
cán hóng: fallen, fading flowers.残红：凋落的花。
春已透chūn yǐ tòu
chūn yǐ tòu: spring is already deep, nearly over.春已透：春天已经很深，快要过去了。
镇zhèn
zhèn: all day long, always.镇：整天，总是。
殢酒tì jiǔ
tì jiǔ: lost in wine, drinking too much.殢酒：被酒缠住，喝得太多。
厌厌病yān yān bìng
yān yān bìng: listless, as if ill.厌厌病：没精打采，像生了病一样。
云鬓yún bìn
yún bìn: hair as thick and soft as clouds.云鬓：像云一样浓密的头发。
未忺整wèi xiān zhěng
wèi xiān zhěng: not in the mood to tidy it. Xiān means to feel like doing.未忺整：不想梳理。忺，想要，乐意。
休重省xiū chóng xǐng
xiū chóng xǐng: do not think back on it again. Xiū means don't.休重省：不要再去回想。休，不要。
寻消问息xún xiāo wèn xī
xún xiāo wèn xī: asking everywhere for news.寻消问息：到处打听消息。
断鸿难倩duàn hóng nán qiàn
duàn hóng nán qiàn: even a lone wild goose cannot be asked to carry a letter. People once believed wild geese could carry letters. Qiàn means to ask someone's help.断鸿难倩：离群的孤雁也很难托它带信。古人传说大雁能传书信。倩，请人帮忙。
凭栏píng lán
píng lán: leaning on the railing.凭栏：靠着栏杆。
归期guī qī
guī qī: the day of return.归期：回来的日子。
瓶沉金井píng chén jīn jǐng
píng chén jīn jǐng: the water jar sinks to the bottom of the well when the rope breaks. Bai Juyi used this picture for lovers parted for good. Jīn jǐng is a well with a fine railing.瓶沉金井：打水的瓶子沉到井底，绳子断了。白居易的诗里用它比喻男女分离、感情断绝。金井，井栏装饰华美的井。
嘶骑sī qí
sī qí: a neighing horse, meaning him riding home.嘶骑：嘶叫的马，指他骑马回来。
银烛yín zhú
yín zhú: a bright candle.银烛：明亮的蜡烛。
枉教人wǎng jiào rén
wǎng jiào rén: making me, all for nothing,枉教人：白白让人。
立尽梧桐影lì jìn wú tóng yǐng
lì jìn wú tóng yǐng: stand until the shadow of the paulownia tree has moved away, that is, stand for a very long time.立尽梧桐影：站到梧桐树的影子都移走了，指站了很久很久。
鸾镜luán jìng
luán jìng: a mirror with a luan bird carved on the back. Legend says a luan bird saw itself in a mirror, took it for a mate, and cried until it died. So the mirror suggests loneliness.鸾镜：背面刻着鸾鸟的镜子。传说鸾鸟看见镜中自己的影子，以为是同伴，悲鸣而死，所以鸾镜常带孤单的意思。
03

How to read it读音

殢tì
This rare character is read tì and means to be stuck in, to wallow in.“殢”读 tì，意思是沉溺、被缠住。
厌yān
Here the doubled character means listless, the same as another word read yān yān, so it is read yān, not yàn.“厌厌”这里同“恹恹”，是没精神的样子，读 yān yān。
重chóng
In "think back again", this character means "again" and is read chóng.“重省”是再一次回想，“重”读 chóng。
省xǐng
Here this character means to reflect on, so it is read xǐng, not shěng.“省”这里是回想、反省，读 xǐng。
骑qí
This character here means a horse being ridden. It is read qí today; in old usage the noun was read jì.“骑”指骑的马，现在统一读 qí；古代作名词时读 jì。
教jiào
Here this character means "to make (someone do)", read jiào.“教”这里是让、使的意思，读 jiào。
04

Background创作背景

This is a boudoir lament: a lyric about a woman at home missing her lover far away. The first stanza shows a late spring day, from drowsy noon to waking, full of idleness and gloom. The second shows her anxious search for news and her loneliness as he fails to return. Almost nothing is known about the poet Li Yu. The lyric became widely known because it was chosen for the anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics. When and why it was written is not recorded.

这是一首闺怨词，就是写女子在家中思念远方爱人的词。上片写春末一天从中午到醒来的慵懒愁闷，下片写打听不到消息、盼人不归的焦急和孤单。作者李玉生平事迹不详，这首词因为被选进《宋词三百首》才广为人知。写作时间和背景都没有记载。

05

The poet作者

Li Yu was a Song dynasty lyric writer. Almost nothing is recorded about his life; we cannot even say exactly when he lived. This is usually thought to be his only surviving lyric. He is not the same person as Li Yu, the last ruler of the Southern Tang, whose name sounds similar.

李玉是宋代词人，生平事迹几乎没有留下记载，连他生活在哪个年代都说不清。一般认为他传下来的词只有这一首。他和南唐后主李煜不是一个人，名字读音相近，不要弄混。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is like a short film in slow motion. The camera starts on threads of incense smoke. She is drunk, the courtyard is still, and willow down covers the path. She wakes, sees the fallen flowers outside the curtain, knows spring is leaving, and cannot be bothered to comb her messy hair. In the second stanza the camera pulls back. She asks for news everywhere, but not even a wild goose will carry her letter. At night the moon fills the west tower. She leans on the railing and waits, until the candle dims and the tree's shadow moves away, and still the horse does not come. The last question, who will keep me company, leaves her alone with a mirror. The word "miss" never appears, yet every picture is full of missing him.

这首词像一部慢镜头的短片。镜头从香炉里一缕缕的烟开始，人醉着，院子很静，只有杨花铺满小路。她醒来，看见帘外落花，知道春天要走了，头发乱了也懒得梳。下片镜头拉远：她到处打听，连大雁都不肯帮她带信。夜里月亮照满西楼，她靠着栏杆一直等，等到蜡烛暗了、树影移走，那匹马还是没来。最后一问“谁伴我”，只剩她对着一面镜子。全词没有一个字说“想”，可每个画面里都是想念。
