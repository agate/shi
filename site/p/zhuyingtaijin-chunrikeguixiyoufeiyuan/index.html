Zhu Ying Tai Jin: Visiting a Ruined Garden at Guixi in Spring

 

cǎi采yōu幽xiāng香

xún巡gǔ古yuàn苑

zhú竹lěng冷cuì翠wēi微lù路

dòu斗cǎo草xī溪gēn根

shā沙yìn印xiǎo小lián莲bù步

zì自lián怜liǎng两bìn鬓qīng清shuāng霜

yì一nián年hán寒shí食

yòu又shēn身zài在yún云shān山shēn深chù处

zhòu昼xián闲dù度

yīn因shén甚tiān天yě也qiān悭chūn春

qīng轻yīn阴biàn便chéng成yǔ雨

lǜ绿àn暗cháng长tíng亭

guī归mèng梦chèn趁fēng风xù絮

yǒu有qíng情huā花yǐng影lán阑gān干

yīng莺shēng声mén门jìng径

jiě解liú留wǒ我shà霎shí时níng凝zhù伫

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 采幽香I go to gather faint, wild scents,我去采集野花的幽香，
  2. 巡古苑And wander through the old garden,在古老的废园里四处走走，
  3. 竹冷翠微路Past cold bamboo, along a path into the green hills.竹林清冷，小路通向青翠的山间。
  4. 斗草溪根By the stream, girls once played the grass game;溪边曾有人在玩斗草，
  5. 沙印小莲步The sand still shows their small footprints.沙地上还印着小小的脚印。
  6. 自怜两鬓清霜I pity myself, my temples white as frost;可怜我两鬓已经白得像霜，
  7. 一年寒食Another year's Cold Food Festival has come,又到了一年一度的寒食节，
  8. 又身在云山深处And once again I am deep among clouds and hills, far from home.我却又一次身在云山深处，远离家乡。
  9. 昼闲度The day passes idly.白天就这样闲闲地过去了。
  10. 因甚天也悭春Why is Heaven so stingy with spring,为什么老天对春天这么吝啬，
  11. 轻阴便成雨That a little cloud turns at once to rain?天色刚有一点阴，就下起雨来？
  12. 绿暗长亭Thick green shade darkens the roadside pavilion;绿树浓密，遮暗了路边的长亭，
  13. 归梦趁风絮My dream of going home drifts off with the willow fluff on the wind.我回家的梦，跟着风中的柳絮飘走了。
  14. 有情花影阑干The flower shadows on the railing seem to have feelings,栏杆上的花影好像懂得感情，
  15. 莺声门径And so do the orioles singing by the gate path;门前小路上的黄莺也在啼叫，
  16. 解留我霎时凝伫They know how to hold me here, standing still a while, lost in thought.它们懂得把我留住，让我站着出神一会儿。
02

Words字词注释

幽香yōu xiāng
yōu xiāng: a faint, delicate scent; here, wild flowers.幽香：淡淡的清香，这里指野花。
巡古苑xún gǔ yuàn
xún gǔ yuàn: wandering around an old garden. Yuàn means a park or garden.巡古苑：在古老的园子里四处走走。苑，园林。
翠微cuì wēi
cuì wēi: the soft green of the hills; here, hillside paths.翠微：青翠的山色，这里指山间。
斗草dòu cǎo
dòu cǎo: a spring game played by girls long ago, competing over who picked more plants or whose stem was stronger.斗草：古代女孩子们春天玩的游戏，比谁采的花草多、谁的草茎更结实。
溪根xī gēn
xī gēn: the edge of the stream.溪根：溪水边。
小莲步xiǎo lián bù
xiǎo lián bù: small footprints of girls. People of old called a woman's steps "lotus steps".小莲步：女子小小的脚印。古人常用“莲步”形容女子的脚步。
自怜zì lián
zì lián: to pity oneself.自怜：可怜自己。
两鬓清霜liǎng bìn qīng shuāng
liǎng bìn qīng shuāng: the hair at both temples has turned white as frost.两鬓清霜：两边鬓角的头发都白了，像霜一样。
寒食hán shí
hán shí: the Cold Food Festival, a day or two before Qingming. No fires were lit and people ate cold food. It was also a time for spring outings and visiting graves.寒食：寒食节，在清明节前一两天，这一天不生火，吃冷的食物，也是春游扫墓的时候。
昼闲度zhòu xián dù
zhòu xián dù: the day passes idly by.昼闲度：白天就这样闲闲地过去了。
因甚yīn shén
yīn shén: why, for what reason.因甚：为什么。
悭春qiān chūn
qiān chūn: stingy with spring, unwilling to give fine spring days. Qiān means stingy.悭春：对春天很吝啬，舍不得给人好的春光。悭，小气。
轻阴qīng yīn
qīng yīn: a light cloud cover, slightly overcast.轻阴：天上有薄薄的云，有点阴。
长亭cháng tíng
cháng tíng: a roadside pavilion where travelers rested and friends said goodbye.长亭：古代路边供行人休息、送别的亭子。
趁风絮chèn fēng xù
chèn fēng xù: following the willow fluff on the wind. Chèn means to go along with.趁风絮：跟着随风飘的柳絮。趁，追随。
阑干lán gān
lán gān: a railing.阑干：栏杆。
门径mén jìng
mén jìng: the path to the gate.门径：门前的小路。
解jiě
jiě: to know how to.解：懂得，知道。
霎时shà shí
shà shí: for a moment.霎时：一会儿，很短的时间。
凝伫níng zhù
níng zhù: to stand still, lost in thought.凝伫：站着不动，出神地看。
03

How to read it读音

斗dòu
Dòu cǎo is a game, so this character is read dòu, meaning to compete.“斗草”是一种游戏，“斗”读 dòu，意思是比赛、争斗。
甚shén
Yīn shén means for what reason. Here this character is read shén, as in the word for what.“因甚”就是“因为什么”，这里的“甚”读 shén，同“什么”的“什”。
04

Background创作背景

Guixi is in today's Deqing area of Zhejiang. The title says Wu Wenying wrote this in spring while staying at Guixi as a guest, on a visit to a ruined garden. It was the Cold Food Festival, and he was alone far from home. The garden's scenes made him think of growing old and of his drifting life, so he wrote this lyric. The exact year is not known.

龟溪在今天浙江德清一带。题目说，这是春天词人客居龟溪时，去游一座荒废的园子写的。正赶上寒食节，他一个人在外地，看到园中景物，想到自己年纪渐老、漂泊不定，写下了这首词。具体是哪一年写的，已经无法确定。

05

The poet作者

Wu Wenying (about 1200 – about 1260), known by his pen name Mengchuang, came from Siming, today's Ningbo in Zhejiang. He never held office and spent many years as a guest adviser to officials around Suzhou and Hangzhou, often living away from home. His lyrics choose words with great care and paint fine, detailed pictures. He often weaves the scene before him together with the past and with dreams, which gives his work a hazy beauty.

吴文英（约1200—约1260），号梦窗，四明（今浙江宁波）人。他一生没有做官，长年在苏州、杭州一带给官员做门客，常常客居他乡。他的词用字讲究，画面细密，常把眼前景物和往事、梦境交织在一起，读起来有一种朦胧的美。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is the walk through the garden. He picks wild flowers, passes through the old garden, by cold bamboo, along a path into the hills. In the sand by the stream there are still small footprints from girls playing the grass game. This place was once lively, but now only he is here. He touches his temples and finds them white. Another Cold Food Festival has come, and he is still a guest deep in the hills. The second stanza is about weather and mood. The day passes idly, and Heaven is stingy: a few clouds and it rains. Green shade hides the pavilion, and his dream of home can only drift away with the willow fluff. At last the flower shadows and the orioles seem to understand him, holding him still for a long moment. The whole lyric is quiet and light, yet full of a loneliness and homesickness that is hard to put into words.

上片写游园。词人采野花，走过古园，竹林清冷，小路伸进山里。溪边沙地上还留着女孩子玩斗草时的小脚印，可见这里曾经很热闹，如今却只剩他一个人。他摸摸两鬓，已经白了；又是一个寒食节，他却还在深山里做客。下片写天气和心情。白天闲闲地过去，老天偏偏小气，天一阴就下雨。绿荫遮住长亭，想回家的梦只能跟着柳絮飘走。最后，花影和莺声好像懂他的心，把他留住，让他在原地站了好一会儿。整首词淡淡的，却透着一种说不出的孤单和思乡之情。
