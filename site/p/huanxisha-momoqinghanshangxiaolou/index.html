Huan Xi Sha: A Faint Chill Climbs the Little Tower

 

mò漠mò漠qīng轻hán寒shàng上xiǎo小lóu楼

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 漠漠轻寒上小楼A faint chill quietly spreads up into the little tower.淡淡的寒意静静地漫上小楼。
  2. 晓阴无赖似穷秋The cloudy morning is dreary, as if it were late autumn.早晨天阴着，让人烦闷，好像到了深秋。
  3. 淡烟流水画屏幽The painted screen shows pale mist and flowing water, quiet and still.画屏上画着淡淡的烟和流水，显得幽静。
  4. 自在飞花轻似梦Petals drift freely, as light as a dream.自由飘飞的花瓣，轻得像梦一样。
  5. 无边丝雨细如愁The endless fine rain is as thin and soft as sorrow.无边无际的细雨，细得像心里的忧愁。
  6. 宝帘闲挂小银钩The fine curtain hangs idly on a little silver hook.精美的帘子随意地挂在小银钩上。

03

Background创作背景

This ci describes a cold, cloudy spring morning and the light sorrow someone feels up in a small tower. We do not know when it was written, and the ci never says where the sorrow comes from. Some readers think it shows a young woman's spring sadness in her room. Others think the poet is using the scene to share his own feelings.

这首词写春天里一个阴冷的早晨，一个人在小楼上感到的淡淡忧愁。写作时间不详，词中也没有说出忧愁从哪里来。有人认为写的是一位女子在闺房里的春愁，也有人认为是词人借景抒发自己的心情。

04

The poet作者

Qin Guan (1049–1100), also called Shaoyou, was a Northern Song ci poet. He was a student of Su Shi and one of the "Four Scholars of Su's School." His ci are delicate and soft. He was good at turning feelings you cannot see into scenes you can see, and he is a leading poet of the graceful, gentle style. His career went badly and he was sent away many times, so his ci often carry sadness.

秦观（1049—1100），字少游，北宋词人，是苏轼的学生，“苏门四学士”之一。他的词细腻柔美，善于把看不见的心情写成看得见的景物，是婉约派的代表。他一生仕途不顺，多次被贬，词里常带着忧伤。

05

The mood意境

The whole ci never complains out loud, yet sorrow is everywhere. The light chill, the cloudy sky, the pale mist and water on the screen are all faint and quiet. The most famous lines say the drifting petals are as light as a dream and the fine rain is as thin as sorrow. Most people would say sorrow is like rain. The poet turns it around and says rain is like sorrow. He compares flowers and rain, which we can see, to dreams and sorrow, which we cannot. All of them are light and fine, hard to name and hard to chase away. The last line only shows a curtain on a hook. The person seems to do nothing but watch quietly, and the sorrow lives in that stillness.

整首词没有大声诉苦，却处处是愁。轻寒、阴天、画屏上的淡烟流水，都是淡淡的、静静的。最有名的是“自在飞花轻似梦，无边丝雨细如愁”：平常人会说“愁像雨”，词人却反过来说“雨像愁”，把看得见的花和雨，比成看不见的梦和愁，又轻又细，说不清，赶不走。最后一句只写帘子挂在钩上，人好像什么也没做，只是静静看着，愁就在这份安静里。
