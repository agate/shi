Song lyric · ci lyric in two stanzas宋词 · 词（双调）

què鹊qiáo桥xiān仙·huá华dēng灯zòng纵bó博

lù陆yóu游Lu You · Song宋

To the Tune Que Qiao Xian: Gambling Under Bright Lamps

 

huá华dēng灯zòng纵bó博

diāo雕ān鞍chí驰shè射

shuí谁jì记dāng当nián年háo豪jǔ举

jiǔ酒tú徒yī一yì一qǔ取fēng封hóu侯

dú独qù去zuò作jiāng江biān边yú渔fǔ父

qīng轻zhōu舟bā八chǐ尺

dī低péng篷sān三shàn扇

zhàn占duàn断pín蘋zhōu洲yān烟yǔ雨

jìng镜hú湖yuán元zì自shǔ属xián闲rén人

yòu又hé何bì必guān官jiā家cì赐yǔ与

This follows the common text. The source's last line has "the emperor's favor"; the common text has "the emperor" (a Song word for the ruler). The meaning is close. The source also writes the water plant in the fourth-to-last line with the simplified character used for apple, a slip of machine conversion; the plant is meant.

这里用通行本。原始资料末句作“又何必、君恩赐与”，通行本作“又何必、官家赐与”，意思相近，“官家”是宋代对皇帝的称呼。“蘋洲”原始资料作“苹洲”，是繁简转换的结果，这里的“蘋”指一种水草，不是苹果。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 华灯纵博Gambling to our hearts' content under bright lamps,在灯火辉煌下尽情赌博，
  2. 雕鞍驰射galloping on carved saddles and shooting arrows:骑着骏马奔驰射箭，
  3. 谁记当年豪举who still remembers the bold deeds of those years?当年那些豪迈的事，还有谁记得？
  4. 酒徒一一取封侯My drinking friends, one by one, have become great lords,一起喝酒的伙伴一个个都做了大官，
  5. 独去作江边渔父while I alone have gone off to be an old fisherman by the river.只有我独自离开，做了江边打鱼的老头。
  6. 轻舟八尺A light boat, eight feet long,一只八尺长的小船，
  7. 低篷三扇a low canopy of three panels,三扇低矮的船篷，
  8. 占断蘋洲烟雨and all the mist and rain over the islets of water clover belong to me.长满蘋草的小洲上的烟雨，全都归我一个人。
  9. 镜湖元自属闲人Mirror Lake has always belonged to those at leisure.镜湖本来就属于清闲的人，
  10. 又何必官家赐与Why should it need to be a gift from the emperor?又何必要皇帝来赏赐呢！
02

Words字词注释

华灯huá dēng
huá dēng: bright, splendid lamps.华灯：灯火辉煌的彩灯。
纵博zòng bó
zòng bó: gambling and playing games to one's heart's content.纵博：尽情地赌博游戏。
雕鞍diāo ān
diāo ān: a carved saddle, standing for a fine horse.雕鞍：雕着花纹的马鞍，代指骏马。
驰射chí shè
chí shè: shooting arrows while galloping.驰射：骑马奔跑着射箭。
豪举háo jǔ
háo jǔ: bold, heroic deeds.豪举：豪迈的举动。
酒徒jiǔ tú
jiǔ tú: drinking companions. Here it is a little scornful: ordinary fellows.酒徒：一起喝酒的人。这里带点看不起的意思，指那些平庸的同伴。
一一取封侯yī yì qǔ fēng hóu
yī yī qǔ fēng hóu: one by one they became great lords. Fēng hóu means to be made a marquis, a high title.一一取封侯：一个个都当了大官。封侯，封为侯爵，指做大官。
渔父yú fǔ
yú fǔ: an old fisherman.渔父：打鱼的老人。
低篷dī péng
dī péng: a low canopy. Péng is the cover on a boat that keeps off wind and rain.低篷：低矮的船篷。篷，船上挡风雨的棚子。
占断zhàn duàn
zhàn duàn: to have all of it.占断：全部占有。
蘋洲pín zhōu
pín zhōu: an islet covered in water clover. Pín is a water plant.蘋洲：长满蘋草的水中小洲。蘋，一种水草。
镜湖jìng hú
jìng hú: Mirror Lake, near Shaoxing in Zhejiang, close to Lu You's home.镜湖：在今浙江绍兴，陆游的家乡附近。
元自yuán zì
yuán zì: by nature, from the start.元自：本来就。元，同“原”。
闲人xián rén
xián rén: a person with no office, free and at leisure.闲人：没有官职、清闲的人。
官家赐与guān jiā cì yǔ
guān jiā cì yǔ: given by the emperor. When the Tang poet He Zhizhang retired, Emperor Xuanzong gave him a part of Mirror Lake. Lu You turns this story around.官家赐与：皇帝赏赐。唐代贺知章告老还乡，唐玄宗把镜湖的一角赐给他。陆游反用这个故事。
03

How to read it读音

父fǔ
In "old fisherman" this character means an old man, read fǔ, not fù as in father.渔父的“父”指老年男子，读 fǔ，不读父亲的 fù。
扇shàn
Read shàn, as a counting word for panels.扇，量词，一扇两扇，读 shàn。
占zhàn
Read zhàn, meaning to take all for oneself.占断，全部占有，读 zhàn。
属shǔ
Read shǔ, meaning to belong to.属，归于，读 shǔ。
04

Background创作背景

In his late forties Lu You served in the army at Nanzheng, today's Hanzhong in Shaanxi, near the front line against the Jin. There he lived a bold life of riding and shooting. Later he was dismissed from office more than once. This lyric is usually dated to the Chunxi years (1174–1189), when, out of office, he was living by Mirror Lake in his home town, Shanyin, today's Shaoxing. Thinking back on those days and looking at his life now, he wrote this lyric.

陆游四十多岁时曾在南郑（今陕西汉中）的军队里做事，那里靠近抗金前线，他过了一段骑马射箭、热血豪迈的日子。后来他多次被罢官。一般认为这首词写于淳熙年间，他被罢官后住在家乡山阴（今浙江绍兴）镜湖边的时候。回想当年，再看眼前，他写下了这首词。

05

The poet作者

Lu You (1125–1210), who called himself Fangweng, was a great poet of the Southern Song. All his life he wanted to win back the north, which the Jin had taken, but he was never given an important role and was sent home several times. His poems often carry the anger of a man who cannot serve his country. On the surface this lyric is about a fisherman's easy life, but underneath lies that same sense of unfairness.

陆游（1125—1210），号放翁，南宋大诗人。他一生都想收复被金国占领的北方，却总是得不到重用，几次被罢官回乡。他的诗词里常有报国无门的悲愤，这首词表面上写渔父的闲适，底下藏着的正是这种不平。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza looks back. Games under bright lamps, galloping and shooting: how bold it was! Then two words, "who remembers", break the scene: no one does. His old drinking friends have all become great lords, and he alone is a fisherman by the river. The contrast is sharp. The second stanza shows the fisherman's life: a boat eight feet long, a canopy of three panels, and all the mist and rain on the lake his own. It looks free and easy. The last two lines are the most striking. He Zhizhang's piece of Mirror Lake was a gift from the emperor. But Lu You says the lake has always belonged to people at leisure, and needs no one's gift. It sounds carefree, but it is stubborn and bitter too. On the surface is an easy life; beneath it is the sadness of a hero with nothing to do.

上片先回忆：灯火下尽情游戏，骑马奔驰射箭，多么豪迈。可“谁记”两个字一下子把热闹打碎了：没人记得了。当年喝酒的伙伴都做了大官，只有自己成了江边的渔父，对比很强烈。下片写渔父的生活：小船八尺，船篷三扇，满湖烟雨都归自己，看起来很自在。最后两句最有意思：贺知章的镜湖是皇帝赏的，而我说，镜湖本来就属于闲人，用不着谁赏。这话听起来洒脱，其实带着倔强和不平。表面写闲适，心里却是英雄无用武之地的悲凉。
