xún寻lù陆hóng鸿jiàn渐bú不yù遇

jiǎo皎rán然Jiaoran · Tang唐

Looking for Lu Hongjian and Not Finding Him

 

yí移jiā家suī虽dài带guō郭

yě野jìng径rù入sāng桑má麻

jìn近zhòng种lí篱biān边jú菊

qiū秋lái来wèi未zhuó著huā花

kòu扣mén门wú无quǎn犬fèi吠

yù欲qù去wèn问xī西jiā家

bào报dào道shān山zhōng中qù去

guī归shí时měi每rì日xié斜

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 移家虽带郭You moved your home here, close to the city wall,你把家搬到这里，虽然离城墙不远，
  2. 野径入桑麻yet the way in is a country path through mulberry and hemp.却要走一条乡间小路，穿过桑麻地才到。
  3. 近种篱边菊By the fence you have just planted chrysanthemums.篱笆边新种了菊花，
  4. 秋来未著花Autumn has come, but they have not bloomed yet.秋天到了，还没有开花。
  5. 扣门无犬吠I knock at the gate, and not even a dog barks.我敲门，连一声狗叫都没有，
  6. 欲去问西家About to leave, I go and ask the neighbor to the west.正要走，就去问问西边的邻居。
  7. 报道山中去The neighbor says: he has gone into the hills,邻居说：他到山里去了，
  8. 归时每日斜and every day he comes back only when the sun is low.每天都要到太阳偏西才回来。
02

Words字词注释

移家yí jiā
yí jiā: to move house.移家：搬家。
带郭dài guō
dài guō: close to the city wall. Guō is the outer city wall.带郭：靠近城墙。郭，外城墙。
野径yě jìng
yě jìng: a country path.野径：乡间小路。
桑麻sāng má
sāng má: mulberry trees and hemp; farm fields in general.桑麻：桑树和麻，泛指庄稼地。
篱lí
lí: a fence woven from bamboo or branches.篱：篱笆，用竹子、树枝编成的矮墙。
著花zhuó huā
zhuó huā: to blossom.著花：开花。
扣门kòu mén
kòu mén: to knock on a door.扣门：敲门。
西家xī jiā
xī jiā: the neighbor to the west.西家：西边的邻居。
报道bào dào
bào dào: answered, saying.报道：回答说。
日斜rì xié
rì xié: the sun slants west; late afternoon.日斜：太阳偏西，傍晚。
03

How to read it读音

种zhòng
Here it is a verb, to plant, read zhòng.“种”是栽种的意思，是动词，读 zhòng。
著zhuó
Zhuó huā means to bloom, so the character is read zhuó.“著花”就是开花，“著”读 zhuó。
斜xié
Today it is read xié. In old times it was read xiá and rhymed with the other line endings.现在读 xié。古音读 xiá，和“麻、花、家”押韵。
04

Background创作背景

Lu Hongjian is Lu Yu; Hongjian was his courtesy name, a second name used by educated men. He wrote The Classic of Tea, the world's first book about tea, and is honored as the Sage of Tea. Jiaoran was his close friend. Lu Yu later settled around Huzhou. Jiaoran went to visit him but did not find him at home, and wrote this poem. It is usually dated after Lu Yu moved to Huzhou; the exact year is unknown.

陆鸿渐就是陆羽，“鸿渐”是他的字（古人在名字以外取的称呼）。他写了世界上第一部讲茶的书《茶经》，被尊为“茶圣”。皎然是他的好朋友。陆羽后来在湖州一带住下，皎然去拜访他，却没见到人，就写了这首诗。一般认为写于陆羽搬到湖州以后，具体年份不详。

05

The poet作者

Jiaoran was a Tang poet-monk, a Buddhist monk who wrote poems. His family name was Xie, and he lived for many years in a temple in Huzhou. He knew both poetry and tea, and wrote a book on how to write poems. He and Lu Yu were lifelong friends who often drank tea and wrote poems together.

皎然，唐代的诗僧，也就是会写诗的和尚，俗家姓谢，长期住在湖州的寺庙里。他懂诗也懂茶，写过讲作诗方法的书《诗式》。他和陆羽是一辈子的好朋友，常常一起喝茶写诗。

06

The mood意境

This poem is about a visit where the friend was not home, yet it does not feel disappointing at all. The poet first shows the house: near the town, but hidden among fields, with chrysanthemums by the fence not yet in bloom, quiet and a little wild. He knocks, and not even a dog barks, so the owner must often be away. He asks a neighbor and learns that his friend goes into the hills every day and comes back only at dusk. The poem never praises Lu Yu directly, yet a hermit who loves nature and does not care about worldly things stands right before us. Not meeting him only makes us want to meet him more.

这首诗写的是一次扑空的拜访，读来却一点也不扫兴。诗人先写朋友的家：离城不远，却藏在桑麻地里，篱笆边种着还没开的菊花，安静又有野趣。他敲门，连狗都不叫，可见主人常常不在家。问邻居，才知道他每天进山，傍晚才回。诗里一句没有夸陆羽，可一个爱自然、不在乎俗事的隐士已经站在我们眼前了。没见到人，反而更让人想见他。
