sòng送rén人dōng东yóu游

wēn温tíng庭yún筠Wen Tingyun · Tang唐

Seeing a Friend Off on a Journey East

 

huāng荒shù戍luò落huáng黄yè叶

hào浩rán然lí离gù故guān关

gāo高fēng风hàn汉yáng阳dù渡

chū初rì日yǐng郢mén门shān山

jiāng江shàng上jǐ几rén人zài在

tiān天yá涯gū孤zhào棹huán还

hé何dāng当chóng重xiāng相jiàn见

zūn樽jiǔ酒wèi慰lí离yán颜

The corpus uses an old variant form of zhào (oar), and an older form of zūn (wine cup). Common editions use the standard forms; the meanings are the same.

语料库写作“孤櫂”“尊酒”。“櫂”是“棹”的异体字；“尊”是“樽”的古字，都指酒杯。通行本写作“孤棹”“樽酒”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 荒戍落黄叶By a deserted fort the yellow leaves fall;荒凉的营垒边，黄叶纷纷飘落，
  2. 浩然离故关with a bold heart you leave the old pass.你心意坚定，离开了这座老关口。
  3. 高风汉阳渡Strong autumn winds blow at the Hanyang ferry;汉阳渡口吹着强劲的秋风，
  4. 初日郢门山the morning sun lights up Yingmen Mountain.初升的太阳照着郢门山。
  5. 江上几人在By the river where you go, how many old friends remain?你去的江边，还有几位老朋友在呢？
  6. 天涯孤棹还Alone in one boat, you sail toward the end of the world.你独自驾着一条小船，漂向天涯。
  7. 何当重相见When shall we meet again,我们什么时候才能再见面，
  8. 樽酒慰离颜and raise our cups to comfort our sad parting faces?举起酒杯，安慰离别的愁颜？
02

Words字词注释

荒戍huāng shù
huāng shù: a deserted border fort.荒戍：荒凉的边防营垒。
浩然hào rán
hào rán: firm-minded and bold.浩然：心意坚定、气概豪迈的样子。
故关gù guān
gù guān: the old pass, the place he sets out from.故关：原来的关口，指出发的地方。
高风gāo fēng
gāo fēng: the strong winds of autumn.高风：秋天强劲的风。
汉阳渡hàn yáng dù
hàn yáng dù: the ferry at Hanyang, in today's Wuhan, Hubei.汉阳渡：汉阳的渡口，在今天湖北武汉。
初日chū rì
chū rì: the newly risen sun.初日：刚升起的太阳。
郢门山yǐng mén shān
yǐng mén shān: Jingmen Mountain, on the Yangtze northwest of today's Yidu, Hubei.郢门山：荆门山，在今天湖北宜都西北的长江边。
江上几人在jiāng shàng jǐ rén zài
jiāng shàng jǐ rén zài: how many old friends are still there by the river where you are going?江上几人在：你要去的江东一带，还有几位老朋友在呢？
孤棹gū zhào
gū zhào: a lone boat. A zhào is an oar, standing for the boat.孤棹：一只船。棹是划船的桨，这里代指船。

03

How to read it读音

还huán
Read huán, meaning "to return", not hái.读 huán，意思是“回来、回去”，不读 hái。
重chóng
Read chóng, meaning "again".读 chóng，意思是“再一次”。
04

Background创作背景

Wen Tingyun wrote this poem to see off a friend travelling far to the east. The places named, the Hanyang ferry and Yingmen Mountain, show that the parting was in today's Hubei, and the friend would sail east down the Yangtze. We no longer know who the friend was or what year it was written. The falling yellow leaves and strong winds show it was autumn.

这是温庭筠送朋友向东远行时写的诗。从诗里的“汉阳渡”“郢门山”看，送别的地方在今天湖北一带，朋友要坐船顺长江往东去。朋友是谁、具体写于哪一年，都已不清楚。从“落黄叶”“高风”看，当时是秋天。

05

The poet作者

Wen Tingyun (about 812–about 866) was a late Tang poet and lyricist. He was very quick-witted; legend says he could finish a long rhymed piece after folding his hands just eight times, so he was nicknamed "Eight Folds Wen". He is seen as a founder of the Huajian school of lyrics, and as a poet he was paired with Li Shangyin. This poem is open and bright, unlike his richly decorated lyrics.

温庭筠（约812—约866），本名岐，字飞卿，晚唐诗人、词人。他才思敏捷，传说叉手八次就能写成一首八韵的赋，人称“温八叉”。他的词写得很好，被看作“花间派”的开创者；诗和李商隐齐名，合称“温李”。这首诗写得开阔明朗，和他华丽的词风不太一样。

06

The mood意境

Most farewell poems dwell on sadness, but this one opens with energy. Yellow leaves fall by a deserted fort, yet the friend sets out without hesitation, in high spirits. Lines three and four show places on his way: strong autumn winds at the Hanyang ferry, the morning sun rising over Yingmen Mountain. The picture is wide and bright. Only in lines five and six does worry come in: how many old friends are there where you are going? Alone in one boat, sailing to the end of the world, won't you be lonely? At the end the poet hopes they will meet again and drink together to comfort each other. The poem is bold first and tender later, so it does not feel too sad.

一般的送别诗多写伤心，这首诗开头却很有气势：黄叶在荒凉的营垒边飘落，朋友却毫不犹豫，意气昂扬地出发了。第三、四句写朋友一路要经过的地方：汉阳渡口秋风强劲，郢门山上旭日初升，画面辽阔明亮。第五、六句才转入担心：你去的地方还有几个老朋友？你一个人一条船漂到天涯，会不会孤单？最后盼望将来重逢，再一起喝酒，安慰彼此。全诗先豪迈后深情，读来不会太悲伤。
