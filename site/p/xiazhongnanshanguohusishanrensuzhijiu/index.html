Tang poem · five-character old-style poem唐诗 · 五言古诗

xià下zhōng终nán南shān山guò过hú斛sī斯shān山rén人sù宿zhì置jiǔ酒

lǐ李bái白Li Bai · Tang唐

Coming Down Zhongnan Mountain, I Stay the Night with the Hermit Husi, Who Sets Out Wine

 

mù暮cóng从bì碧shān山xià下

shān山yuè月suí随rén人guī归

què却gù顾suǒ所lái来jìng径

cāng苍cāng苍héng横cuì翠wēi微

xiāng相xié携jí及tián田jiā家

tóng童zhì稚kāi开jīng荆fēi扉

lǜ绿zhú竹rù入yōu幽jìng径

qīng青luó萝fú拂xíng行yī衣

huān欢yán言dé得suǒ所qì憩

měi美jiǔ酒liáo聊gòng共huī挥

cháng长gē歌yín吟sōng松fēng风

qǔ曲jìn尽hé河xīng星xī稀

wǒ我zuì醉jūn君fù复lè乐

táo陶rán然gòng共wàng忘jī机

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 暮从碧山下At dusk I come down from the green mountain,傍晚我从青翠的终南山上下来，
  2. 山月随人归and the mountain moon follows me home.山上的月亮一路跟着我回来。
  3. 却顾所来径I look back at the path I came by:回头看看走过的小路，
  4. 苍苍横翠微dark green dusk lies across the slopes.只见青苍苍的暮色笼罩着山腰。
  5. 相携及田家The hermit takes my hand and leads me to his farmhouse,斛斯山人拉着我的手来到他家，
  6. 童稚开荆扉and the children run to open the wooden gate.小孩子跑来打开柴门。
  7. 绿竹入幽径Green bamboo lines the quiet path,绿竹夹着幽静的小路，
  8. 青萝拂行衣and green vines brush my clothes.青萝轻轻擦过我的衣裳。
  9. 欢言得所憩I say happily that I have found a good place to rest,我高兴地说，找到了歇脚的好地方，
  10. 美酒聊共挥and we raise our cups of fine wine together.我们一起举杯，畅快地喝美酒。
  11. 长歌吟松风We sing out loud the song of wind in the pines,放声唱着《风入松》的歌，
  12. 曲尽河星稀and when the song ends, the stars of the Milky Way are few.歌唱完了，银河里的星星已经稀稀落落。
  13. 我醉君复乐I am drunk, and you are happy too;我喝醉了，你也很快乐，
  14. 陶然共忘机in our joy we both forget the schemes of the world.我们开开心心，把世上的心机全忘了。
02

Words字词注释

碧山bì shān
bì shān: the green mountain, meaning Zhongnan Mountain, south of Chang'an.碧山：青绿色的山，指终南山。终南山在长安南边。
却顾què gù
què gù: to look back.却顾：回头看。
径jìng
jìng: a path.径：小路。
苍苍cāng cāng
cāng cāng: dark green, dim in the evening light.苍苍：深青色，暮色中模模糊糊的样子。
翠微cuì wēi
cuì wēi: the green slopes of a mountain.翠微：青翠的山腰，也指青山。
相携xiāng xié
xiāng xié: hand in hand. The hermit Husi meets him and they walk together. Husi is a surname; a "mountain man" is a hermit living in the hills.相携：手拉着手。指斛斯山人来接他，两人一起走。斛斯是姓，山人是住在山里的隐士。
及jí
jí: to reach.及：到。
童稚tóng zhì
tóng zhì: young children.童稚：小孩子。
荆扉jīng fēi
jīng fēi: a gate woven from thorny branches, a simple farm gate.荆扉：用荆条编的柴门。
幽径yōu jìng
yōu jìng: a quiet, shady path.幽径：幽静的小路。
青萝qīng luó
qīng luó: a green vine that hangs down from trees.青萝：一种攀在树上垂下来的绿色植物。
拂fú
fú: to brush lightly against.拂：轻轻擦过。
得所憩dé suǒ qì
dé suǒ qì: to find a place to rest.得所憩：找到了休息的地方。憩，休息。
聊共挥liáo gòng huī
liáo gòng huī: to drink freely together. Huī here means raising the cup.聊共挥：一起畅快地喝。聊，姑且；挥，举杯喝酒。
松风sōng fēng
sōng fēng: one view is that it is the zither tune "Wind in the Pines"; another, that it is the sound of wind in the pines.松风：一说指古琴曲《风入松》，一说指松林里的风声。
河星稀hé xīng xī
hé xīng xī: the stars of the Milky Way grow few, so it is very late and nearly dawn.河星稀：天上银河里的星星变少了，说明夜已经很深，天快亮了。
复fù
fù: also.复：也。
陶然táo rán
táo rán: happy and at ease.陶然：快乐、舒畅的样子。
忘机wàng jī
wàng jī: to forget all scheming and stop worrying about gain and loss.忘机：忘掉心机，不再算计世上的得失。
03

How to read it读音

行xíng
Xíng yī means the clothes one walks in, so it is read xíng.“行衣”是走路时穿的衣服，读 xíng。
曲qǔ
Here it means a song, so it is read qǔ.这里是“歌曲”的意思，读 qǔ。
乐lè
Here it means happy, so it is read lè.这里是“快乐”的意思，读 lè。
04

Background创作背景

Zhongnan Mountain lies south of Chang'an, and many hermits lived there. In this poem Li Bai comes down the mountain one evening and spends the night at the home of a hermit surnamed Husi. They drink and sing late into the night. Nothing is recorded about the hermit's full name or life. One view is that the poem dates from Li Bai's years in Chang'an.

终南山在长安南边，山里住着不少隐士。这首诗写李白有一天傍晚从终南山下来，到一位姓斛斯的隐士家里住了一夜，两人喝酒唱歌到深夜。斛斯山人的名字和生平都没有记载。一说这首诗写于李白在长安的那几年。

05

The poet作者

Li Bai (701–762) was a great Tang poet, called the Immortal of Poetry. He loved travelling in the hills, loved wine, and liked making friends with hermits. Most of his poems are bold and sweeping, but this one is quiet and warm. It feels a little like Tao Yuanming's poems about country life.

李白（701—762），唐代大诗人，人称“诗仙”。他喜欢游山玩水，爱喝酒，也爱和隐士交朋友。他的诗多半奔放豪迈，这首却写得很安静、很亲切，有点像陶渊明写田园生活的味道。

06

The mood意境

The poem is like a little travel diary. It starts with coming down the mountain. The moon follows him, as if it does not want to leave either. Looking back, he sees the mountain turned dark green by the dusk. At his friend's house the children open the gate, bamboo lines the path, and vines brush his clothes. Everything feels warm and welcoming. Then they drink and sing until the stars grow few. The last two lines touch the heart most: I am drunk, you are happy, and we both forget the scheming of the world. Reading it, you can almost smell the bamboo, hear the wind in the pines, and feel the simple joy between two friends.

诗像一段小小的旅行日记。开头写下山：月亮跟着人走，好像也舍不得离开。回头看，山已经被暮色染成一片青苍。到了朋友家，孩子开门，竹林夹路，藤萝碰着衣服，一切都那么亲切。然后就是喝酒、唱歌，一直到星星都稀了。最后两句最动人：我醉了，你也乐了，我们都忘了世上那些算计。读这首诗，好像能闻到竹林的清香，听到松风的声音，感到朋友之间那份简单的快乐。
