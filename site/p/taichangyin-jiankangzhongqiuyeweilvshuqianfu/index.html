太常引·建康中秋夜为吕叔潜赋

辛弃疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

Tai Chang Yin: Written for Lü Shuqian on Mid-Autumn Night in Jiankang

 

yī一lún轮qiū秋yǐng影zhuǎn转jīn金bō波

fēi飞jìng镜yòu又chóng重mó磨

bǎ把jiǔ酒wèn问héng姮é娥

bèi被bái白fà发qī欺rén人nài奈hé何

chéng乘fēng风hǎo好qù去

cháng长kōng空wàn万lǐ里

zhí直xià下kàn看shān山hé河

zhuó斫qù去guì桂pó婆suō娑

rén人dào道shì是qīng清guāng光gèng更duō多

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 一轮秋影转金波The round autumn moon moves on, pouring out golden waves of light.一轮秋月慢慢移动，洒下金色的光波。
  2. 飞镜又重磨It is like a flying mirror, freshly polished again.它像一面飞在天上的镜子，又被重新磨亮了。
  3. 把酒问姮娥I raise my cup and ask Chang'e on the moon:我端起酒杯问月中的嫦娥：
  4. 被白发欺人奈何White hair keeps bullying me. What can I do?白发一天天欺负我，我该怎么办？
  5. 乘风好去I want to ride the wind up into the sky,我想乘着风痛快地飞上天去，
  6. 长空万里across ten thousand miles of open air,穿过万里长空，
  7. 直下看山河and look straight down on the mountains and rivers below.笔直往下看人间的山河。
  8. 斫去桂婆娑Then I would chop down the swaying cassia tree on the moon,再砍掉月中那棵枝叶摇摆的桂树，
  9. 人道是清光更多for people say the moonlight would be even brighter.人们说，这样月光会更加明亮。
02

Words字词注释

秋影qiū yǐng
qiū yǐng: the autumn moon.秋影：秋天的月亮。
金波jīn bō
jīn bō: golden waves, the moonlight that seems to flow like water.金波：月亮发出的金色光芒，像流动的波浪。
飞镜fēi jìng
fēi jìng: a flying mirror, meaning the round moon.飞镜：飞在天上的镜子，指圆圆的月亮。
重磨chóng mó
chóng mó: polished again. Old mirrors were made of bronze and had to be polished to shine.重磨：又磨了一遍。古代的镜子是铜做的，要常常磨才亮。
把酒bǎ jiǔ
bǎ jiǔ: holding up a cup of wine.把酒：端着酒杯。
姮娥héng é
héng é: Chang'e, the goddess who lives on the moon in the old story.姮娥：就是嫦娥，传说住在月亮上的仙女。
被白发欺人bèi bái fà qī rén
bèi bái fà qī rén: bullied by white hair, meaning one grows old before the great task is done.被白发欺人：被白头发欺负，意思是人还没做成大事，头发却白了。
奈何nài hé
nài hé: what can I do?奈何：怎么办。
乘风chéng fēng
chéng fēng: riding the wind up into the sky.乘风：借着风飞上天。
好去hǎo qù
hǎo qù: go off freely and happily.好去：痛痛快快地去。
直下zhí xià
zhí xià: straight down.直下：笔直往下。
斫zhuó
zhuó: to chop with an axe.斫：用刀斧砍。
桂婆娑guì pó suō
guì pó suō: the swaying, leafy cassia tree. Legend says a cassia tree grows on the moon.桂婆娑：枝叶摇摆、长得很茂盛的桂树。传说月亮里有一棵桂树。
人道是rén dào shì
rén dào shì: people say.人道是：人们说。
清光qīng guāng
qīng guāng: clear moonlight.清光：清亮的月光。
03

How to read it读音

重chóng
Here the character means 'again', so it is read chóng, not zhòng.重：这里是“再一次”的意思，读 chóng，不读 zhòng。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually dated to 1174. Xin Qiji was then an official in Jiankang, today's Nanjing. Much of northern China was held by the Jin state. He longed to win it back but was not given real power. On Mid-Autumn night he watched the moon with his friend Lü Shuqian and wrote this for him. The line about chopping down the cassia tree borrows an idea from Du Fu, who wrote that cutting the moon's cassia would make its light brighter.

这首词一般认为写于宋孝宗淳熙元年（1174年）。那时辛弃疾在建康（今江苏南京）做官。北方的大片土地被金国占着，他一心想收复，却得不到重用。中秋夜，他和朋友吕叔潜一起赏月，写下这首词送给朋友。“斫去桂婆娑”一句，借用了杜甫诗里“斫却月中桂，清光应更多”的意思。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) was a great Southern Song lyric poet. As a young man he raised troops in the north to fight the Jin, then came south to join the Song. All his life he hoped to win back the north, but he was often pushed aside. His lyrics are bold and strong, and he is named together with Su Shi. The wild fancy in this lyric also hides his wish to serve the country.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），号稼轩，南宋大词人。他年轻时在北方起兵抗金，后来南归宋朝。他一生想收复中原，却常常被冷落。他的词豪放有力，和苏轼并称“苏辛”。这首词里的浪漫想象，也藏着他的报国心。

06

The mood意境

The first half paints the moon. It is like golden waves, and like a mirror just polished, round and bright. Then the poet raises his cup and asks Chang'e: white hair is bullying me, what can I do? The mood turns from beauty to worry. He sighs that he is growing old before his great task is done. The second half is full of wild imagination. He wants to ride the wind into the sky, look down on the land from far above, and chop down the cassia tree on the moon so the light shines brighter. The tree that blocks the light is like the people at court who stood in the way of fighting the Jin. Cut it down, and the world below grows brighter. The lyric reads like a fairy tale, but behind it beats a heart that longs to serve the country.

上片先写月：秋月像金色的波浪，又像一面刚磨亮的镜子，又圆又亮。接着词人端起酒杯问嫦娥：白发欺负人，怎么办？一下子从美景转到心里的烦恼，他是在叹自己老了，大事还没做成。下片想象奇特：他要乘风飞上天，从万里高空往下看山河，再砍掉月中的桂树，让月光照得更亮。桂树挡住月光，就像朝廷里阻碍抗金的人。砍去它，人间就会更亮。整首词像一个童话，背后却是一颗想为国家出力的心。
