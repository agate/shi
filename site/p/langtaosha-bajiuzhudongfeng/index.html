làng浪táo淘shā沙·bǎ把jiǔ酒zhù祝dōng东fēng风

ōu欧yáng阳xiū修Ouyang Xiu · Song宋

Waves Washing the Sand: Raising My Cup to the East Wind

 

bǎ把jiǔ酒zhù祝dōng东fēng风

qiě且gòng共cóng从róng容

chuí垂yáng杨zǐ紫mò陌luò洛chéng城dōng东

zǒng总shì是dāng当shí时xié携shǒu手chù处

yóu游biàn遍fāng芳cóng丛

jù聚sàn散kǔ苦cōng匆cōng匆

cǐ此hèn恨wú无qióng穷

jīn今nián年huā花shèng胜qù去nián年hóng红

kě可xī惜míng明nián年huā花gèng更hǎo好

zhī知yǔ与shuí谁tóng同

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 把酒祝东风I raise my cup and make a wish to the east wind:端起酒杯，向东风许个愿：
  2. 且共从容please stay with us a while, and let us take our time.请你也留下来，和我们一起慢慢游玩。
  3. 垂杨紫陌洛城东East of Luoyang, willows hang over the roads outside the city.洛阳城东，垂柳拂着城外的大路。
  4. 总是当时携手处These are all the places where we once walked hand in hand,这里全是当年我们手拉手走过的地方，
  5. 游遍芳丛and we wandered through every bed of flowers.那时我们走遍了一丛丛花。
  6. 聚散苦匆匆Meeting and parting always come in such a rush,相聚和分别，总是这样匆匆忙忙，
  7. 此恨无穷and this regret never ends.这份遗憾没有尽头。
  8. 今年花胜去年红This year the flowers are redder than last year.今年的花比去年开得更红，
  9. 可惜明年花更好Sadly, next year they will be even lovelier,可惜明年的花会更好，
  10. 知与谁同but who knows who will be here to see them with me?却不知道到时候会和谁一起来看。
02

Words字词注释

把酒bǎ jiǔ
bǎ jiǔ: to hold up a cup of wine. Bǎ means to hold.把酒：端起酒杯。把，拿着。
祝东风zhù dōng fēng
zhù dōng fēng: to make a wish to the east wind. The east wind is the wind of spring.祝东风：向春风许愿、祷告。东风，春天的风。
且共从容qiě gòng cóng róng
qiě gòng cóng róng: please stay with us a while and do not hurry off. Cóng róng means calm and unhurried.且共从容：请你暂且和我们一起，慢一点，别急着走。从容，不慌不忙。
垂杨chuí yáng
chuí yáng: willows with hanging branches.垂杨：枝条垂下来的杨柳。
紫陌zǐ mò
zǐ mò: the roads outside the city. Old poets used this phrase for roads around a capital or a great city.紫陌：城外的大路。古人常用“紫陌”称京城或大城市郊外的道路。
洛城luò chéng
luò chéng: the city of Luoyang, in today's Henan.洛城：洛阳城，在今天的河南。
总是zǒng shì
zǒng shì: all of these were.总是：都是，全是。
携手处xié shǒu chù
xié shǒu chù: the places where we walked hand in hand.携手处：手拉着手一起走过的地方。
芳丛fāng cóng
fāng cóng: clusters of sweet-smelling flowers.芳丛：一丛丛香花。
聚散jù sàn
jù sàn: meeting and parting.聚散：相聚和分别。
苦匆匆kǔ cōng cōng
kǔ cōng cōng: so hurried that it hurts. Kǔ here means it is painful that.苦匆匆：太匆忙了，让人难受。苦，苦于、让人痛苦的是。
此恨无穷cǐ hèn wú qióng
cǐ hèn wú qióng: this regret has no end. Hèn here means regret, not hate.此恨无穷：这种遗憾没有尽头。恨，遗憾。
胜shèng
shèng: better than.胜：比……更好。
知与谁同zhī yǔ shuí tóng
zhī yǔ shuí tóng: who knows who I will be with then. Zhī here means who can tell.知与谁同：不知道那时会和谁一起来看。知，这里是“哪知道、不知道”的意思。
03

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually dated to the spring of 1032. Ouyang Xiu was then an official in Luoyang. He and an old friend came back to the east side of the city, where they had enjoyed the flowers together the year before. One account says the friend was the poet Mei Yaochen. They had only just met again and would soon have to part, so Ouyang Xiu wrote this lyric.

一般认为，这首词是宋仁宗明道元年（1032年）春天写的。那时欧阳修在洛阳做官，和一位老朋友重游洛阳城东，想起去年曾一起在这里赏花。一说这位朋友是诗人梅尧臣。朋友相聚不久又要分开，欧阳修就写下了这首词。

04

The poet作者

Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072) was a great writer of the Northern Song. He called himself the Old Drunkard, and he is one of the Eight Great Prose Masters of Tang and Song. As a young official in Luoyang, he often went out with friends such as Mei Yaochen to enjoy the hills, write poems and drink. He cared deeply about friendship, and this lyric is about meeting a friend and having to part again.

欧阳修（1007—1072），北宋文学家，号醉翁，是“唐宋八大家”之一。他年轻时在洛阳做官，和梅尧臣等朋友常常一起游山玩水、写诗喝酒。他很看重友情，这首词写的就是和朋友相聚又分别的心情。

05

The mood意境

At the start, the poet raises his cup and talks to the spring wind, asking it not to leave so fast. What he really wants to keep is not the wind, but the happy time with his friend. Then he sees the willows and roads east of Luoyang, and remembers walking hand in hand through the flowers last year. The second half says what hurts: meetings are always too short, and partings come too soon. The last three lines are the best. This year's flowers are redder than last year's, and next year's will be even better. But who will come with me to see them? The flowers grow more beautiful each year, while friends may drift apart. The lovelier the flowers, the sadder the parting feels.

词一开头，诗人就端起酒杯对春风说话，求它别走得太快。其实他想留住的不是风，而是和朋友在一起的好时光。接着他看到洛阳城东的垂柳和大路，想起去年两个人手拉手走遍花丛。下半首说出心里的苦：见面总是太短，分别总是太快。最后三句最妙：今年的花比去年红，明年的花会更好，可是明年谁陪我来看呢？花越开越美，人却说不定散了。越是写花好，越让人觉得分别难过。
