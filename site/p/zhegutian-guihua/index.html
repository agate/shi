zhè鹧gū鸪tiān天·guì桂huā花

lǐ李qīng清zhào照Li Qingzhao · Song宋

Partridge Sky: Osmanthus

 

àn暗dàn淡qīng轻huáng黄tǐ体xìng性róu柔

qíng情shū疏jì迹yuǎn远zhǐ只xiāng香liú留

hé何xū须qiǎn浅bì碧shēn深hóng红sè色

zì自shì是huā花zhōng中dì第yī一liú流

méi梅dìng定dù妒

jú菊yīng应xiū羞

huà画lán阑kāi开chù处guàn冠zhōng中qiū秋

sāo骚rén人kě可shà煞wú无qíng情sī思

hé何shì事dāng当nián年bú不jiàn见shōu收

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 暗淡轻黄体性柔The osmanthus is a pale, soft yellow, gentle in form and nature.桂花颜色淡淡的，浅浅的黄，样子和性情都很温柔。
  2. 情疏迹远只香留It keeps apart and grows far from people, leaving only its fragrance behind.它性情淡泊，长在远离人的地方，只把香气留给人间。
  3. 何须浅碧深红色Why should it need bright colors like pale green or deep red?何必要有浅绿深红那样鲜艳的颜色？
  4. 自是花中第一流By its nature it is already the finest of all flowers.它本来就是花中最好的一等。
  5. 梅定妒The plum blossom must be jealous,梅花一定会嫉妒，
  6. 菊应羞and the chrysanthemum must feel ashamed.菊花想必也会害羞。
  7. 画阑开处冠中秋Blooming by the painted railing, it is the best flower of mid-autumn.它在画栏边开放，是中秋时节最美的花。
  8. 骚人可煞无情思The poet of Li Sao truly had no eye for it.那位写《离骚》的诗人，实在是太没有眼光了，
  9. 何事当年不见收Why did he not put the osmanthus in his poem back then?为什么当年不把桂花写进他的诗里呢？
02

Words字词注释

暗淡àn dàn
àn dàn: dull, not bright in color.暗淡：颜色不鲜艳。
轻黄qīng huáng
qīng huáng: a pale yellow.轻黄：淡淡的黄色。
体性柔tǐ xìng róu
tǐ xìng róu: gentle in form and in nature.体性柔：样子和性情都很温柔。
情疏迹远qíng shū jì yuǎn
qíng shū jì yuǎn: aloof by nature and growing in far-off places, keeping apart from people.情疏迹远：性情淡泊，长在深山远处，不和人亲近。
只香留zhǐ xiāng liú
zhǐ xiāng liú: only its fragrance is left for people.只香留：只把香气留给人间。
何须hé xū
hé xū: why should it need?何须：何必、哪里需要。
浅碧深红qiǎn bì shēn hóng
qiǎn bì shēn hóng: pale green and deep red, the bright colors of other flowers.浅碧深红：浅绿、深红，指别的花鲜艳的颜色。
自是zì shì
zì shì: is already, by its very nature.自是：本来就是。
第一流dì yī liú
dì yī liú: the very first rank, the best.第一流：最好的一等。
定妒dìng dù
dìng dù: will surely be jealous.定妒：一定会嫉妒。
应羞yīng xiū
yīng xiū: must feel shy and ashamed, knowing it cannot compare.应羞：想必会害羞，自愧不如。
画阑huà lán
huà lán: a painted railing.画阑：有彩绘的栏杆。
冠中秋guàn zhōng qiū
guàn zhōng qiū: first among all the flowers of mid-autumn.冠中秋：在中秋时节的花里位居第一。
骚人sāo rén
sāo rén: a poet; here it means Qu Yuan, who wrote Li Sao.骚人：诗人，这里指写《离骚》的屈原。
可煞kě shà
kě shà: truly, really.可煞：真是、实在是。
无情思wú qíng sī
wú qíng sī: lacking feeling, lacking a good eye.无情思：没有情意、缺少眼光。

hé shì: why?何事：为什么。

bù jiàn shōu: was not included.不见收：没有被收进去。
03

How to read it读音

应yīng
Here the character means surely, must be, and is read yīng.“菊应羞”的“应”是“应该、想必”的意思，读 yīng。
冠guàn
Here the character means to rank first, so it is read guàn, not guān (a hat).“冠中秋”的“冠”是“位居第一”的意思，读 guàn，不读 guān（帽子）。
煞shà
Here the character means very, and is read shà.“可煞”的“煞”是“很、极”的意思，读 shà。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric praises the osmanthus. We do not know exactly when it was written; one view is that it comes from Li Qingzhao's earlier years. The osmanthus blooms in autumn. Its flowers are tiny and pale yellow, but its scent is very strong. In Li Sao, Qu Yuan wrote about many fragrant flowers and herbs, using them to stand for good character. Li Qingzhao felt the osmanthus was so fine but had not been given its due, so at the end she speaks up for it.

这是一首咏桂花的词，具体写作时间不能确定，一说是李清照前期的作品。桂花在秋天开放，花很小，颜色淡黄，却香气浓郁。屈原在《离骚》里写了许多香花香草，用来比喻美好的品德。李清照觉得桂花这么好，却没有得到应有的重视，于是在词的结尾替桂花“打抱不平”。

05

The poet作者

Li Qingzhao (1084–about 1155), who called herself the Lay Buddhist of Yi'an, was the most famous woman lyric poet of the Song. She grew up in a family of scholars, showed talent early, and collected old bronzes, stone rubbings, books and paintings with her husband Zhao Mingcheng. Her early lyrics are fresh and lively; later, after losing her country and her husband, her lyrics became deep and sad. This one is confident and bright, full of her own spirit.

李清照（1084—约1155），号易安居士，宋代最有名的女词人。她出身书香门第，从小就有才华，和丈夫赵明诚一起收藏金石书画。她前期的词清新活泼，后期经历国破家亡，词变得深沉哀伤。这首词写得自信爽朗，带着她特有的性格。

06

The mood意境

The poet starts by showing how plain the osmanthus looks: pale in color, tiny, and fond of growing far away. But at once she says it does not need bright colors, because it is already the finest of flowers. She says this with great confidence. It praises a flower, and it also seems to describe a person: truly good things win with their inner fragrance, not with bright looks. The second half is even more fun. She says the plum blossom will be jealous and the chrysanthemum ashamed, and she even scolds Qu Yuan a little for leaving the osmanthus out of his poem. It reads like a clever, confident person standing up for a friend, light and charming.

词人一开头就写桂花不起眼的样子：颜色淡，花又小，还爱长在远处。可是她马上说，桂花不需要鲜艳的颜色，它“自是花中第一流”。这句话说得很有底气，好像是在夸花，也像是在说一个人：真正好的东西，靠的是内在的香，不是外表的艳。下半首更有趣，她说梅花会嫉妒，菊花会害羞，甚至连屈原都被她“批评”了一下，怪他没把桂花写进诗里。读起来像是一个聪明、自信的人在替朋友说话，轻松又可爱。
