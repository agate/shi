To the Tune Rui He Xian: Wet Clouds Cling to the Wild Geese

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 湿云黏雁影Wet clouds hang low, as if stuck to the shapes of the wild geese.湿漉漉的云低低的，好像粘住了大雁的影子。
  2. 望征路愁迷Gazing down the long road, I am lost in sorrow,望着远行的路，愁得一片迷茫，
  3. 离绪难整and my feelings of parting are too tangled to sort out.离别的心情乱糟糟，理不清楚。
  4. 千金买光景I would pay a thousand pieces of gold to buy back happy days,我愿意花千金买回美好的时光，
  5. 但疏钟催晓but there are only scattered bells calling up the dawn,可是只有稀落的钟声催来天亮，
  6. 乱鸦啼暝and crows cawing in the dusk.乱叫的乌鸦啼来黄昏。
  7. 花悰暗省Quietly I recall our joy among the flowers.我悄悄回想在花前的欢乐，
  8. 许多情相逢梦境With so much love between us, we can meet only in dreams.那么多的情意，只能在梦里相逢。
  9. 便行云都不归来Even if you, like a drifting cloud, never come back,即使你像飘走的云一样，再也不回来，
  10. 也合寄将音信you ought at least to send me word.也应该寄个音信给我。
  11. 孤迥I am lonely, and you are far.我孤单单的，离你又远。
  12. 盟鸾心在The vow we made like a pair of magic birds is still in my heart,我们像鸾凤一样许下的誓约还在心里，
  13. 跨鹤程高but you seem to ride a crane, flying high and far,可你像骑着仙鹤，飞得又高又远，
  14. 后期无准and no one knows when we will meet again.下次见面的日子说不准。
  15. 情丝待翦I want to cut the threads of love,我想把情丝剪断，
  16. 翻惹得but that only stirs up反而惹起了
  17. 旧时恨the old regrets.从前的遗憾。
  18. 怕天教何处I fear that heaven will let, somewhere,只怕老天让哪个地方，
  19. 参差双燕a pair of swallows flitting high and low那一对飞得高高低低的燕子，
  20. 还染残朱剩粉still carry a trace of her rouge and powder.身上还沾着她留下的胭脂香粉。
  21. 对菱花与说相思Facing the mirror, I tell it of my longing,我对着镜子，像对她说说相思，
  22. 看谁瘦损to see which of us has grown thinner.看看我们谁更憔悴。
02

Words字词注释

湿云黏雁影shī yún nián yàn yǐng
shī yún nián yàn yǐng: low, wet clouds seem to stick to the shapes of the wild geese. Nián means to stick.湿云黏雁影：低低的湿云好像粘住了大雁的影子。黏，粘住。
征路zhēng lù
zhēng lù: the road of a long journey.征路：远行的路。
离绪难整lí xù nán zhěng
lí xù nán zhěng: the feelings of parting are tangled and cannot be sorted out.离绪难整：离别的心情乱糟糟，理不清。
光景guāng jǐng
guāng jǐng: time, here meaning happy days.光景：时光，这里指美好的日子。
疏钟shū zhōng
shū zhōng: a bell ringing now and then.疏钟：稀稀落落的钟声。
暝míng
míng: dusk, when it grows dark.暝：天黑，傍晚。
花悰huā cóng
huā cóng: the joy we had among the flowers. Cóng means joy.花悰：在花前的欢乐。悰，快乐。
暗省àn xǐng
àn xǐng: quietly recalling in one's heart.暗省：心里悄悄回想。
行云xíng yún
xíng yún: drifting clouds. In an old legend a goddess turned into morning clouds, so clouds stand for the loved one who has gone.行云：飘走的云。古代传说巫山神女早上化作云，这里借指远去的心上人。
也合yě hé
yě hé: still ought to.也合：也应该。
寄将jì jiāng
jì jiāng: to send. Jiāng after a verb adds no real meaning.寄将：寄来。将，用在动词后面，没有实在意思。
孤迥gū jiǒng
gū jiǒng: lonely and far away. Jiǒng means far.孤迥：孤单，又离得很远。迥，远。
盟鸾méng luán
méng luán: a vow made like a pair of luán birds. The luán is a magic bird in legends, a sign of lovers.盟鸾：像鸾凤一样结下的誓约。鸾，传说中的神鸟，常比喻相爱的人。
跨鹤程高kuà hè chéng gāo
kuà hè chéng gāo: riding a crane, flying high and far. The loved one is too far away to reach.跨鹤程高：骑着仙鹤，飞得又高又远。说对方离得太远，难以追上。
后期无准hòu qī wú zhǔn
hòu qī wú zhǔn: no one can say when we will meet again.后期无准：下次见面的日子说不准。
情丝待翦qíng sī dài jiǎn
qíng sī dài jiǎn: wanting to cut the threads of love, that is, to let the feeling go.情丝待翦：想剪断情丝，就是想把这份感情放下。翦，同“剪”。
翻fān
fān: instead, on the contrary.翻：反而。
天教tiān jiāo
tiān jiāo: heaven lets.天教：老天让。
残朱剩粉cán zhū shèng fěn
cán zhū shèng fěn: leftover rouge and face powder, the traces she left behind.残朱剩粉：剩下的胭脂和香粉，这里指她留下的痕迹。
菱花líng huā
líng huā: a mirror. Old bronze mirrors often had water-chestnut flowers carved on the back.菱花：镜子。古代铜镜背面常刻着菱花，所以叫菱花镜。
瘦损shòu sǔn
shòu sǔn: grown thin and worn.瘦损：瘦了，憔悴了。
03

How to read it读音

省xǐng
Here it means "to recall, to realize", so it is read xǐng, not shěng.这里是“回想、醒悟”的意思，读 xǐng，不读 shěng。
合hé
Here it means "ought to", read hé.合，这里是“应该”的意思，读 hé。
教jiāo
Here it means "to let, to make"; in old poems it is usually read jiāo.这里是“让、使”的意思，古诗词里多读 jiāo。
参cēn
In this word it is read cēn cī, meaning uneven.“参差”读 cēn cī，是长短不齐、高低不一的样子。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about longing after parting. After leaving the one he loves, the poet gazes at the far road and hears the bells and crows of morning and evening. He remembers their happy days among the flowers, and now they can meet only in dreams. He tries to let the feeling go, but the more he tries, the sadder he grows. No record tells when it was written or whom he missed, and some lines are read in different ways.

这是一首写离别相思的词。和心爱的人分开以后，作者看着远去的路，听着早晚的钟声和鸦叫，想起从前花前的欢乐，只能在梦里相见。他想放下这份感情，却越想越难过。这首词写于什么时候、思念的是谁，都没有留下记载。词里有些句子的意思也有不同的理解。

05

The poet作者

Lu Rui, also called Jingsi and Yunxi, came from Kuaiji, today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. He wrote lyrics in the late Southern Song; his dates are unknown. He passed the imperial exam and served as an official, but little is recorded of his life, and only a few of his lyrics survive. This one was chosen for Three Hundred Song Lyrics.

陆叡，字景思，号云西，会稽（今浙江绍兴）人，是南宋后期的词人，生卒年不详。他考中过进士，做过官，但生平留下的记载不多，流传下来的词也只有几首。这首《瑞鹤仙》被收进了《宋词三百首》。

06

The mood意境

The first line is striking: the clouds are so low and wet they seem to stick to the shapes of the wild geese. The geese can hardly fly, and the poet's heart feels just as heavy. He says he would pay a thousand pieces of gold to buy back good times, but time only slips away: bells call up the dawn, crows caw in the dusk. Past joys come back only in dreams, and he even complains: even if you never return, you should at least send word. The second stanza is harder still. The vow remains, but she is as far as someone flown off on a crane. He tries to cut the threads of love and only stirs up old regrets. He even fears some pair of swallows will carry a trace of her rouge and remind him of her again. At the end he speaks his longing to the mirror, wondering which of them has grown thinner. The question is a little foolish and very true, and it shows how deeply he misses her.

开头一句就很特别：云又低又湿，好像把大雁的影子都粘住了。雁飞不动，人的心也沉甸甸的。他说愿意花千金买回好时光，可是时间只会一天天过去：早上钟声催天亮，晚上乌鸦叫天黑。过去的欢乐只能在梦里重温，他甚至埋怨：你就算不回来，也该捎个信呀。下阕更苦：誓言还在，人却像骑鹤飞走一样远。想剪断情丝，反而勾起旧恨。他还怕哪对燕子身上沾着她的胭脂，又惹他想起她。最后他对着镜子说相思，想看看两个人谁更瘦。这一问很傻，也很真，让人感到他的思念有多深。
