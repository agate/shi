浣溪沙·淡荡春光寒食天

李清照Li Qingzhao · Song宋

Huan Xi Sha: Soft Spring Light on the Cold Food Day

 

dàn淡dàng荡chūn春guāng光hán寒shí食tiān天

yù玉lú炉chén沉shuǐ水niǎo袅cán残yān烟

mèng梦huí回shān山zhěn枕yǐn隐huā花diàn钿

hǎi海yàn燕wèi未lái来rén人dòu斗cǎo草

jiāng江méi梅yǐ已guò过liǔ柳shēng生mián绵

huáng黄hūn昏shū疏yǔ雨shī湿qiū秋qiān千

The source text writes chén (to sink) with an old form of the character, and has jiāng hǎi (river and sea) where the common text has jiāng méi (river plum), a copying slip. Both are corrected here to the common text.

底本“沉水”作“沈水”，“沈”是“沉”的古字；底本“江梅”误作“江海”。这里按通行本改正。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 淡荡春光寒食天Soft, gentle spring light: it is the Cold Food Festival.春光柔和暖洋洋，正是寒食节的天气。
  2. 玉炉沉水袅残烟In the fine burner the agarwood is nearly gone, and a last thread of smoke curls up.精美的香炉里，沉香快烧完了，只剩一缕轻烟袅袅上升。
  3. 梦回山枕隐花钿I wake from a dream, my flower ornament still hidden by the hill-shaped pillow.我从梦中醒来，头上的花钿还藏在山形的枕头边。
  4. 海燕未来人斗草The swallows have not come back yet, but the girls are already playing the grass game.燕子还没有飞回来，姑娘们已经在玩斗草的游戏了。
  5. 江梅已过柳生绵The river plums have finished blooming, and the willows are putting out catkins.江边的梅花已经开过了，柳树长出了白白的柳絮。
  6. 黄昏疏雨湿秋千At dusk a light rain falls and wets the swing.黄昏时下起稀疏的小雨，打湿了院子里的秋千。
02

Words字词注释

淡荡dàn dàng
dàn dàng: soft and gentle; spring light that feels mild and easy.淡荡：柔和、舒缓的样子，形容春光暖暖的、软软的。
寒食hán shí
hán shí: the Cold Food Festival, a day or two before Qingming. In old times people lit no fires that day and ate only cold food.寒食：寒食节，在清明节前一两天。古时这一天不生火做饭，只吃冷的食物。
玉炉yù lú
yù lú: a fine incense burner.玉炉：精美的香炉。
沉水chén shuǐ
chén shuǐ: agarwood, a costly incense. It sinks in water, so it was called sink-in-water.沉水：沉香，一种很名贵的香料，放进水里会沉下去，所以叫沉水。
袅残烟niǎo cán yān
niǎo cán yān: the last thread of incense smoke curls gently upward. Niǎo describes smoke winding up.袅残烟：剩下的一缕香烟轻轻地往上飘。袅，烟气缭绕上升的样子。
梦回mèng huí
mèng huí: to wake from a dream.梦回：从梦里醒来。
山枕shān zhěn
shān zhěn: a pillow high at both ends and low in the middle, shaped like hills.山枕：中间低、两头高的枕头，样子像山。
隐花钿yǐn huā diàn
yǐn huā diàn: her flower ornament lies hidden by the pillow; another reading says it is pressed into the pillow. A huā diàn is a flower-shaped hair ornament.隐花钿：头上的花钿藏在枕边。一说是花钿压在枕上。花钿，女子头上的花形首饰。
海燕hǎi yàn
hǎi yàn: swallows. People of old thought swallows flew in from over the sea.海燕：燕子。古人以为燕子是从海上飞来的。
斗草dòu cǎo
dòu cǎo: an old game for girls: comparing who picked more and rarer plants, or hooking grass stems together and pulling to see whose broke first.斗草：古时女孩子的游戏，比谁采的花草多、种类奇，或者用草茎互相拉，看谁的先断。
江梅jiāng méi
jiāng méi: plum trees by the river, a kind of wild plum.江梅：江边的梅花，一种野梅。
柳生绵liǔ shēng mián
liǔ shēng mián: the willow puts out catkins. Mián means fluff like cotton.柳生绵：柳树长出了柳絮。绵，像棉花一样的柳絮。
疏雨shū yǔ
shū yǔ: a light, scattered rain.疏雨：稀稀落落的小雨。
03

How to read it读音

钿diàn
In huā diàn (a flower-shaped hair ornament) the character is read diàn.“花钿”读 huā diàn，指女子头上的花形首饰。
斗dòu
In dòu cǎo (a grass contest game) the character is read dòu, meaning to compete.“斗草”是一种比赛的游戏，“斗”读 dòu。
04

Background创作背景

This poem shows a day at the Cold Food Festival. A young woman wakes from a nap in her room and looks at the spring scene outside. It is usually thought to be an early work of Li Qingzhao, from when she was a girl or newly married and life was easy. Its feelings are light and gentle, without the deep sorrow of her later poems. The exact year is not known.

这首词写的是寒食节的一天，一位少女在闺房里午睡醒来，看到的春日景色。一般认为这是李清照早年的作品，那时她还是少女，或者刚刚出嫁，生活安逸，词里的情绪轻轻的、淡淡的，还没有后来那种深深的悲伤。具体写作年份不详。

05

The poet作者

Li Qingzhao (1084–about 1155) came from Zhangqiu near Jinan. She was the most famous woman writer of song lyrics in the Song dynasty. Her early life was happy, and her lyrics then were lively and fresh. Later the Jin army invaded, her husband died, and she drifted in the south, so her style turned very sad. This poem is one of her early works.

李清照（1084—约1155），号易安居士，济南章丘人，宋代最有名的女词人。她早年生活幸福，词写得活泼清新；后来金兵南下，丈夫去世，她流落江南，词风变得十分悲凉。这首词属于她早期的作品。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a slow spring afternoon. It opens with warm spring light. The incense in the burner is nearly gone, and a thread of smoke drifts up. The room is very quiet. The young woman has just woken from a dream, and her flower ornament is still by the pillow. In the second half she looks outside. The swallows are not back yet, and other girls are already playing the grass game. The plum blossoms are over, and willow catkins are floating. The last line is the loveliest: at dusk a light rain falls and wets the swing. The swing is empty, no one is on it, only the rain. Spring is at its best, yet there is a quiet loneliness and laziness she cannot quite name.

这首词像一个慢慢的春日午后。开头是暖暖的春光，香炉里的香快烧完了，一缕轻烟慢慢往上飘，屋里静悄悄的。少女刚从梦中醒来，花钿还落在枕边。下阕她看向外面：燕子还没回来，别的姑娘已经在玩斗草；梅花开过了，柳絮飘起来了。最后一句最美：黄昏时下起小雨，把秋千打湿了。秋千空着，没人荡，只有雨。春天正好，却带着一点说不出的寂寞和慵懒。
