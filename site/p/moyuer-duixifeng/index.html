To the Tune Mo Yu Er: Facing the West Wind

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 对西风鬓摇烟碧Facing the west wind, the hair at my temples sways like dark green smoke.对着西风，鬓发飘动，乌青得像轻烟，
  2. 参差前事流水The past, all in a jumble, has flowed away like water.从前的事纷纷乱乱，都像流水一样过去了。
  3. 紫丝罗带鸳鸯结On a purple silk sash we tied a mandarin-duck love knot.紫色丝罗带上打着鸳鸯同心结，
  4. 的的镜盟钗誓Our vows over the mirror and the hairpin were clear and true.对着镜子、拿着钗子发过的誓，清清楚楚。
  5. 浑不记漫手织回文He has forgotten them all; in vain I weave palindrome poems by hand,他全都忘了，我白白地亲手织着回文诗，
  6. 几度欲心碎and many times my heart has nearly broken.好几次心都快碎了。
  7. 安花著蒂I try to put the fallen flower back on its stem,我想把落下的花重新安回花蒂上，
  8. 奈雨覆云翻but alas, his heart turns like clouds and rain.怎奈他的心像云雨一样翻来覆去，
  9. 情宽分窄The love was deep, but our fate was thin,感情很深，缘分却太浅，
  10. 石上玉簪脆like a jade hairpin on the whetstone, it snapped.就像石头上磨的玉簪，一下就断了。
  11. 朱楼外Outside the red tower,红楼外面，
  12. 愁压空云欲坠sorrow weighs so heavy the clouds seem about to fall.愁绪重得好像把天上的云都压得要掉下来。
  13. 月痕犹照无寐Faint moonlight still shines on me, sleepless.淡淡的月光还照着睡不着的我。
  14. 阴晴也只随天意Cloud or clear sky, it is all up to heaven,天阴天晴，都只能由老天决定，
  15. 枉了玉消香碎and I have wasted away for nothing.白白让我憔悴消瘦。
  16. 君且醉You, just get drunk for now.你姑且喝醉吧。
  17. 君不见长门青草春风泪Have you not seen Empress Chen at the Changmen Palace, weeping in the spring wind among green grass?你没看见吗？长门宫前青草萋萋，陈皇后在春风里流泪。
  18. 一时左计Once, for a moment, I chose wrong.当初一时打错了主意，
  19. 悔不早荆钗I regret I did not take a simple twig hairpin and a plain life sooner,后悔没有早早戴上荆钗，嫁个平凡人家，
  20. 暮天修竹and at dusk, by tall bamboo,在傍晚的天色里，守着高高的竹子，
  21. 头白倚寒翠grown white-haired, lean on the cold green stalks.白了头也靠着清冷的翠竹过日子。
02

Words字词注释

鬓摇烟碧bìn yáo yān bì
bìn yáo yān bì: the hair at her temples sways in the wind, dark as a wisp of blue-green smoke.鬓摇烟碧：鬓角的头发在风里飘动，乌青得像一缕烟。
参差cēn cī
cēn cī: jumbled and scattered. Another reading takes it as "almost, in a blink".参差：纷纷乱乱。一说是“差不多、转眼之间”的意思。
前事流水qián shì liú shuǐ
qián shì liú shuǐ: the past has all flowed away like water.前事流水：从前的事都像流水一样过去了。
鸳鸯结yuān yāng jié
yuān yāng jié: a love knot tied in a silk sash. Mandarin ducks always swim in pairs, so they stand for a loving couple.鸳鸯结：罗带打成的同心结。鸳鸯总是成对出现，象征夫妻相爱。
的的dí dí
dí dí: truly and clearly.的的：确确实实，清清楚楚。
镜盟钗誓jìng méng chāi shì
jìng méng chāi shì: vows made over a mirror and a hairpin. In old times lovers often gave each other mirrors and hairpins as tokens of love.镜盟钗誓：对着镜子、拿着金钗许下的誓言。古代男女常用镜子、钗子作为定情信物。
浑不记hún bú jì
hún bù jì: has quite forgotten. Hún means completely. It is the man who has forgotten the vows.浑不记：全都不记得了。浑，全，简直。这里说那个男子把誓言全忘了。
漫手织回文màn shǒu zhī huí wén
màn shǒu zhī huí wén: weaving a palindrome poem by hand, all in vain. In the Former Qin, Su Hui missed her husband and wove a poem into brocade that could be read forwards and backwards. Màn means for nothing.漫手织回文：白白地亲手织回文诗。前秦苏蕙思念丈夫，把诗织在锦缎上，正读反读都能成诗，叫回文诗。漫，徒然，白白地。
几度jǐ dù
jǐ dù: several times.几度：好几次。
安花著蒂ān huā zhuó dì
ān huā zhuó dì: putting a fallen flower back on its stem. It means trying to mend a broken love.安花著蒂：把落下的花重新安回花蒂上。比喻想挽回已经破裂的感情。
奈nài
nài: but alas, there is nothing to be done.奈：无奈，怎奈。
雨覆云翻yǔ fù yún fān
yǔ fù yún fān: turning the hand brings clouds, turning it back brings rain. It means a fickle heart.雨覆云翻：翻手为云，覆手为雨，比喻人心反复无常。
情宽分窄qíng kuān fèn zhǎi
qíng kuān fèn zhǎi: the love was wide, but the fate was narrow.情宽分窄：感情很深，缘分却很浅。
石上玉簪脆shí shàng yù zān cuì
shí shàng yù zān cuì: Bai Juyi wrote of grinding a jade hairpin on a stone, only for it to snap in the middle just as it was nearly done. It means love broken halfway.石上玉簪脆：白居易诗里写在石头上磨玉簪，簪快磨成时却从中间断了。比喻爱情半路破灭。
朱楼zhū lóu
zhū lóu: a red tower, where she lives.朱楼：红色的楼，指女子住的地方。
愁压空云欲坠chóu yā kōng yún yù zhuì
chóu yā kōng yún yù zhuì: her sorrow is so heavy it seems to press the clouds down from the sky.愁压空云欲坠：愁绪重得好像把天上的云都压得要掉下来。
月痕yuè hén
yuè hén: faint moonlight.月痕：淡淡的月光。
无寐wú mèi
wú mèi: unable to sleep. Mèi means sleep.无寐：睡不着。寐，睡。
阴晴也只随天意yīn qíng yě zhǐ suí tiān yì
yīn qíng yě zhǐ suí tiān yì: cloud or sunshine is up to heaven alone. Meeting and parting are out of our hands.阴晴也只随天意：天阴天晴，都只能由老天决定。比喻聚散离合由不得自己。
枉了wǎng le
wǎng le: all for nothing.枉了：白白地。
玉消香碎yù xiāo xiāng suì
yù xiāo xiāng suì: jade melting and fragrance shattered, meaning a woman wasting away, her youth and beauty worn out.玉消香碎：比喻女子憔悴消瘦，青春和美貌被折磨坏了。
且qiě
qiě: for now.且：姑且，暂且。
长门青草春风泪cháng mén qīng cǎo chūn fēng lèi
cháng mén qīng cǎo chūn fēng lèi: Empress Chen of Emperor Wu of Han lost his favor and was left alone in the Changmen Palace, where grass grew thick and she wept in the spring wind.长门青草春风泪：汉武帝的陈皇后失宠，被冷落在长门宫，宫前长满青草，她在春风里流泪。
左计zuǒ jì
zuǒ jì: a wrong plan, a mistake.左计：错误的打算。
荆钗jīng chāi
jīng chāi: a hairpin made of a thorny twig, worn by poor women. Here it means marrying into a plain family and living simply.荆钗：用荆条做的发钗，是穷人家女子戴的。这里指嫁给平凡人家，过朴素的日子。
修竹xiū zhú
xiū zhú: tall bamboo. Xiū means long.修竹：高高的竹子。修，长。
倚寒翠yǐ hán cuì
yǐ hán cuì: leaning on cold green bamboo. It echoes Du Fu's poem "The Beauty": "Her green sleeves are thin in the cold; at dusk she leans on the tall bamboo."倚寒翠：靠着清冷的翠竹。化用杜甫《佳人》“天寒翠袖薄，日暮倚修竹”。
03

How to read it读音

参cēn
This pair of characters, meaning uneven or tangled, is read cēn cī.“参差”读 cēn cī，意思是长短不齐、纷乱。
的dí
This doubled character means truly, clearly. It is usually read dí dí; some read it dì dì.“的的”是确确实实、清清楚楚的意思，一般读 dí dí；也有人读 dì dì。
著zhuó
Here this character means to attach, so it is read zhuó.“著”这里是附着、安上的意思，读 zhuó。
分fèn
Here this character means fate or one's lot, so it is read fèn.“分”这里是缘分、命分，读 fèn。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about a woman who has been abandoned. She and her lover once made vows, but he changed his heart, and nothing she did could win him back. In the end she regrets not choosing a plain, simple life. Another reading says the poet uses this woman to express his own grief after the fall of the Song, and his refusal to serve the new rulers. When it was written is not recorded.

这首词写一个被抛弃的女子。她和爱人曾经许下誓言，可是对方变了心，她怎么挽回也没有用，最后后悔当初没有选择平凡朴素的生活。一说作者借这个女子，寄托了宋朝灭亡以后自己的悲痛和不肯出来做官的心志。写作的具体时间没有记载。

05

The poet作者

Zhu Sifa lived at the end of the Song and the start of the Yuan. Little is recorded about his life. It is said that after the Song fell, the Yuan rulers offered him a post and he refused. Very few of his lyrics survive; this one is known because it was chosen for the anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics.

朱嗣发是宋末元初的人，留下的生平资料很少。据说宋朝灭亡以后，元朝想请他做官，他不肯去。他传下来的词很少，这首因为被选进《宋词三百首》而出名。

06

The mood意境

In this lyric a woman stands in the west wind remembering. She clearly remembers the love knot on the purple sash and the vows over mirror and hairpin, but he has forgotten them all. She wants to put the fallen flower back on the branch, like mending a snapped jade hairpin, but it cannot be done. In the second stanza it is night. Her sorrow is so heavy it seems to press the clouds down, and the moonlight finds her awake. She tells herself that cloud or sun is up to heaven, so just get drunk. At last she says: if only I had chosen a plain life sooner, leaning on bamboo into old age, it would have been better than this. Many old stories make it hard to read, but the feeling is clear: deep disappointment, with a touch of stubborn pride.

这首词里，一个女子在西风中回想往事。紫罗带上的同心结，镜子和钗子前的誓言，她都记得清清楚楚，可对方全忘了。她想把落花安回枝头，就像想把碎了的玉簪接起来，可是做不到。下片到了夜里，愁重得像要把云压下来，月光照着她睡不着。她劝自己：天阴天晴由不得人，喝醉吧。最后她说：要是早点过粗布荆钗的日子，到老靠着竹子，也比现在好。这里用了很多旧故事，读起来有点难，但心情很清楚：深深的失望，和一点倔强。
