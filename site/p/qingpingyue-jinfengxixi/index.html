yàn晏shū殊Yan Shu · Song宋

Qing Ping Yue: The Autumn Wind Blows Soft

 

jīn金fēng风xì细xì细

yè叶yè叶wú梧tóng桐zhuì坠

lǜ绿jiǔ酒chū初cháng尝rén人yì易zuì醉

yì一zhěn枕xiǎo小chuāng窗nóng浓shuì睡

zǐ紫wēi薇zhū朱jǐn槿huā花cán残

xié斜yáng阳què却zhào照lán阑gān干

shuāng双yàn燕yù欲guī归shí时jié节

yín银píng屏zuó昨yè夜wēi微hán寒

The source corpus writes the first word of line five with a different character (zi wei as in a star name). Common editions write it as the name of the crape myrtle flower, which is used here.

原始资料第五句作“紫微”，通行本多作“紫薇”，指紫薇花。这里用通行本。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 金风细细The autumn wind blows soft and light;秋风轻轻地吹着，
  2. 叶叶梧桐坠one by one the parasol leaves come down.梧桐叶一片一片地落下。
  3. 绿酒初尝人易醉Just a taste of new wine, and one is easily drunk;刚喝了一点新酒，人就容易醉了，
  4. 一枕小窗浓睡by the small window, on the pillow, one sleeps deep.靠在小窗边的枕头上，睡得又香又沉。
  5. 紫薇朱槿花残The crape myrtle and the hibiscus are fading;紫薇和朱槿的花都快谢了，
  6. 斜阳却照阑干the setting sun still shines on the railing.夕阳仍旧照着栏杆。
  7. 双燕欲归时节It is the season when the paired swallows are leaving;又到了成双的燕子要飞走的时候，
  8. 银屏昨夜微寒last night, by the silver screen, there was a slight chill.昨夜银屏边已经有了一丝凉意。
02

Words字词注释

金风jīn fēng
jīn fēng: the autumn wind. People of old linked autumn with metal, or gold, so they called the autumn wind the golden wind.金风：秋风。古人把秋天和“金”配在一起，所以叫秋风为金风。
细细xì xì
xì xì: soft and gentle.细细：轻轻的，微微的。
梧桐wú tóng
wú tóng: the parasol tree, which has big leaves and is one of the first to lose them in autumn.梧桐：一种叶子很大的树，秋天叶子最先落下。
坠zhuì
zhuì: to fall.坠：掉下来。
绿酒lǜ jiǔ
lǜ jiǔ: newly brewed wine, which had a thin pale-green froth on top.绿酒：新酿的酒，酒面上浮着一层淡绿色的泡沫，所以叫绿酒。
初尝chū cháng
chū cháng: having just begun to taste it.初尝：刚开始喝。
一枕yì zhěn
yī zhěn: on the pillow, here meaning a sleep.一枕：靠着枕头，这里指睡了一觉。
浓睡nóng shuì
nóng shuì: a deep, sound sleep.浓睡：睡得很香很沉。
紫薇zǐ wēi
zǐ wēi: crape myrtle, a tree with purple-red flowers from summer into autumn.紫薇：一种夏天到秋天开花的树，花是紫红色的。
朱槿zhū jǐn
zhū jǐn: Chinese hibiscus, a shrub with big red flowers.朱槿：一种开大红花的灌木，也叫扶桑。
花残huā cán
huā cán: the flowers are fading.花残：花快要谢了。
斜阳xié yáng
xié yáng: the setting sun.斜阳：快落山的太阳。
却照què zhào
què zhào: still shining on, shining again on.却照：仍旧照着，又照到了。
阑干lán gān
lán gān: a railing.阑干：栏杆。
双燕欲归shuāng yàn yù guī
shuāng yàn yù guī: a pair of swallows about to fly back south, as swallows do in autumn.双燕欲归：成对的燕子要飞回南方去了。燕子秋天往南飞。
时节shí jié
shí jié: the time of year.时节：时候，季节。
银屏yín píng
yín píng: a screen decorated with silver, set by the bed to keep off drafts.银屏：用银子装饰的屏风，摆在床边挡风。
微寒wēi hán
wēi hán: a little cold.微寒：有一点冷。
03

Background创作背景

This ci shows a quiet day in early autumn. The autumn wind blows and the parasol leaves fall. The poet drinks a little wine and sleeps by a small window. When he wakes, the flowers are fading and the setting sun lies on the railing. We do not know exactly when it was written. Yan Shu had a smooth career and an easy, wealthy life. Many of his ci, like this one, show a faint sadness that is hard to name, felt in calm days.

这首词写初秋时节一个安静的日子：秋风吹，梧桐落叶，词人喝了点酒，在小窗下睡了一觉，醒来看见花谢了，夕阳照着栏杆。写作的具体时间不清楚。晏殊一生做官顺利，生活富贵悠闲，他的很多词就像这首一样，写的是平静日子里一点淡淡的、说不清的愁。

04

The poet作者

Yan Shu (991–1055), styled Tongshu, came from Linchuan, today's Fuzhou in Jiangxi. He was a Northern Song ci poet. As a boy he was called a child genius, and later he became chief minister, living a long, comfortable life. His ci use clear, graceful words and quiet feelings, often the faint sadness that comes as the seasons change. His youngest son, Yan Jidao, was also a famous ci poet.

晏殊（991—1055），字同叔，抚州临川（今江西抚州）人，北宋词人。他小时候就被称为“神童”，后来做到宰相，一生富贵平顺。他的词语言清丽，感情含蓄，常写季节变换带来的淡淡惆怅。他的小儿子晏几道也是有名的词人。

05

The mood意境

There is not one word for sorrow in this ci, yet a soft autumn sadness is everywhere. At the start the wind is gentle, and the leaves fall one by one. The sounds are quiet and the movements slow. A little wine makes one drunk, and the sleep by the small window is deep, which seems pleasant. On waking, the flowers have faded and the setting sun slants across the railing: another day is gone. The swallows are about to leave, and last night there was a chill by the screen. The poet simply sets these small things out one after another. The season is quietly changing, and a hollow feeling slowly rises in the heart.

整首词没有一个“愁”字，却处处是秋天的淡淡愁绪。开头的风是“细细”的，叶子是“叶叶”落下的，声音很轻，动作很慢。喝一点酒就醉，在小窗下睡得很沉，看上去很舒服。醒来一看，花谢了，夕阳斜斜地照在栏杆上，一天又过去了。燕子要飞走了，昨夜屏风边已经有了凉意。词人只是把这些小事一件件摆出来，季节在悄悄变，人心里那一点空落落的感觉，也就慢慢出来了。
