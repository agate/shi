登科后

孟郊Meng Jiao · Tang唐

After Passing the Imperial Exam

 

xī昔rì日wò龌chuò龊bù不zú足kuā夸

jīn今zhāo朝fàng放dàng荡sī思wú无yá涯

chūn春fēng风dé得yì意mǎ马tí蹄jí疾

yī一rì日kàn看jìn尽cháng长ān安huā花

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 昔日龌龊不足夸The cramped and unhappy days of the past are not worth mentioning anymore.从前那些不如意、憋屈的日子，再也不值得一提了。
  2. 今朝放荡思无涯Today I feel free, and my thoughts fly without limits.今天心情舒畅，自由自在，思绪飞得没有边。
  3. 春风得意马蹄疾Proud in the spring breeze, I ride, and my horse's hooves fly.我在春风里骑着马，得意洋洋，马蹄跑得飞快。
  4. 一日看尽长安花In a single day I see every flower in Chang'an.一天之内，就把长安城的花全都看遍了。
02

Words字词注释

昔日xī rì
xī rì: the old days, the past.昔日：从前，过去的日子。
龌龊wò chuò
wò chuò: here it means a cramped, miserable life, not "dirty" as in modern Chinese.龌龊：这里指处境不好、心情憋屈，不是“脏”的意思。
不足夸bù zú kuā
bù zú kuā: not worth talking about anymore.不足夸：不值得再提了。
今朝jīn zhāo
jīn zhāo: today.今朝：今天。
放荡fàng dàng
fàng dàng: here it means free and carefree, not the bad sense it has today.放荡：这里指无拘无束、自由自在，不是现在说的坏意思。
思无涯sī wú yá
sī wú yá: thoughts that fly without limits. Yá means an edge or a shore.思无涯：心思飞得没有边。涯，边际。
春风得意chūn fēng dé yì
chūn fēng dé yì: riding proudly in the spring breeze. It later became an idiom for being happy because everything is going well.春风得意：在春风里骑着马，得意洋洋。后来成了成语，形容事情顺利、心情高兴。
疾jí
jí: fast.疾：快。
看尽kàn jìn
kàn jìn: to see all of, to see every one.看尽：看遍、全都看完。
长安花cháng ān huā
cháng ān huā: the flowers of Chang'an, the Tang capital, today's Xi'an in Shaanxi.长安花：长安城里的花。长安是唐朝的都城，在今天的陕西西安。
03

How to read it读音

朝zhāo
Here it means morning, today, so it is read zhāo, not cháo.这里指早晨、今天，读 zhāo，不读 cháo。
04

Background创作背景

Dēng kē means passing the imperial exam. When he was younger, Meng Jiao failed the top exam twice and felt very low. In 796 he finally passed, when he was already in his forties. In the Tang the results came out in spring, and the new graduates rode around Chang'an to enjoy the flowers and celebrate. Meng Jiao was overjoyed and wrote this poem.

“登科”就是考中科举。孟郊年轻时两次考进士都没考上，心里非常苦闷。唐德宗贞元十二年（796年），他终于考中了进士，这时他已经四十多岁了。唐朝放榜在春天，新考中的进士会在长安城里骑马游玩、赏花庆祝。孟郊高兴极了，写下了这首诗。

05

The poet作者

Meng Jiao (751–814) was a poet of the middle Tang. He was poor all his life and only held small posts. His poems often speak of hardship and sorrow, so people paired him with Jia Dao as "cold Meng and thin Jia". The well-known "Song of the Traveling Son", about a mother sewing for her son, is his. This poem is one of his rare happy ones.

孟郊（751—814），字东野，中唐诗人。他一生贫穷，做的官也很小，诗里常写贫苦和愁闷，和贾岛并称“郊寒岛瘦”。大家熟悉的《游子吟》“慈母手中线”就是他写的。这首《登科后》是他少有的欢快之作。

06

The mood意境

This poem shows joy in a direct, unrestrained way. The first two lines make a contrast: the hard days of the past are not worth a word, and today his heart is like a bird let out of a cage. The last two lines show him riding fast through Chang'an. The spring breeze is a real breeze, and also the happiness inside him. "In a single day I see every flower in Chang'an" - the city is huge and full of flowers, so how could he see them all in one day? That is the exaggeration: he is so happy that he wants to see every lovely thing at once. Reading it, we feel the joy of someone who waited many years and finally succeeded.

这首诗把“高兴”写得又直接又痛快。前两句一比：过去的苦日子不值一提，今天心里像打开了笼子，思绪飞得没有边。后两句写他骑着马在长安城里飞跑。“春风得意”四个字，春风是真的春风，也是他心里的喜气。“一日看尽长安花”，长安那么大，花那么多，一天怎么看得完？这正是夸张：他太高兴了，恨不得一下子把所有的美景都看遍。读这首诗，我们能感到一个等了很多年的人，终于成功时那种忍不住的快乐。
