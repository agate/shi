Dong Xian Ge: Written at Sizhou on Mid-Autumn Night

 

qīng青yān烟mì幂chù处

bì碧hǎi海fēi飞jīn金jìng镜

yǒng永yè夜xián闲jiē阶wò卧guì桂yǐng影

lù露liáng凉shí时

líng零luàn乱duō多shǎo少hán寒jiāng螀

shén神jīng京yuǎn远

wéi惟yǒu有lán蓝qiáo桥lù路jìn近

shuǐ水jīng晶lián帘bú不xià下

yún云mǔ母píng屏kāi开

lěng冷jìn浸jiā佳rén人dàn淡zhī脂fěn粉

dài待dōu都jiāng将xǔ许duō多míng明

fù付yǔ与jīn金zūn尊

tóu投xiǎo晓gòng共

liú流xiá霞qīng倾jìn尽

gèng更xié携qǔ取

hú胡chuáng床shàng上nán南lóu楼

kàn看yù玉zuò做rén人jiān间

sù素qiū秋qiān千qǐng顷

The database text ends with qīng (to pour), a slip for qǐng (a unit of area). This follows the common text.

资料库原文最后一个字作“倾”，是“顷”的误字，这里按通行本作“顷”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 青烟幂处Where blue-grey mist lies over the land,青色的烟雾笼罩着大地，
  2. 碧海飞金镜a golden mirror of a moon flies up into the blue sky.碧蓝的天空里飞出一面金镜般的圆月。
  3. 永夜闲阶卧桂影All the long night, the shadow of the cassia lies on the quiet steps.长夜里，安静的台阶上躺着桂树的影子。
  4. 露凉时When the dew turns cold,露水变凉的时候，
  5. 零乱多少寒螀how many cold cicadas call here and there.到处是寒蝉零零乱乱的叫声。
  6. 神京远The capital is far away;京城离这里很远，
  7. 惟有蓝桥路近only the road to Blue Bridge, to the moon's fairyland, seems near.只有通往月宫仙境的蓝桥路，好像很近。
  8. 水晶帘不下The crystal curtain is left up,水晶帘子不放下来，
  9. 云母屏开the mica screen stands open,云母屏风打开着，
  10. 冷浸佳人淡脂粉and cold moonlight soaks the lady's light make-up.清冷的月光浸透了美人淡淡的妆容。
  11. 待都将许多明I want to take all this moonlight我想把这么多的月光，
  12. 付与金尊and pour it into a golden cup,全都倒进金酒杯里，
  13. 投晓共and until dawn, together with everyone,一直到天亮，和大家一起，
  14. 流霞倾尽drink the wine of the immortals to the last drop.把这仙酒一样的美酒喝个干净。
  15. 更携取And I will also bring还要带上
  16. 胡床上南楼a folding chair and climb the South Tower,一把胡床，登上南楼，
  17. 看玉做人间to watch the moon make the world into jade:去看月光把人间变成白玉的世界，
  18. 素秋千顷a thousand qing of pure white autumn.千顷大地，一片洁白的秋色。
02

Words字词注释

青烟幂处qīng yān mì chù
qīng yān mì chù: where blue-grey mist covers everything. Mì means to cover.青烟幂处：青色的烟雾笼罩着的地方。幂，覆盖、笼罩。
碧海bì hǎi
bì hǎi: the blue-green sky, wide as the sea.碧海：青蓝色的天空，像大海一样。
金镜jīn jìng
jīn jìng: a golden mirror, that is, the full moon.金镜：金色的镜子，比喻圆圆的月亮。
永夜yǒng yè
yǒng yè: the long night.永夜：长长的夜晚。
闲阶xián jiē
xián jiē: quiet steps where no one walks.闲阶：没有人走动的台阶。
桂影guì yǐng
guì yǐng: the shadow of the cassia tree. Legend says a cassia tree grows on the moon; here it means the shadows cast by moonlight.桂影：桂树的影子。传说月亮上有一棵桂树，这里指月光下的影子。
零乱líng luàn
líng luàn: scattered and jumbled, rising here and there.零乱：声音杂乱，此起彼伏。
寒螀hán jiāng
hán jiāng: a kind of autumn cicada with a mournful call, also called the cold cicada.寒螀：秋天的一种蝉，叫声凄凉，也叫寒蝉。
神京shén jīng
shén jīng: the capital, here Bianjing, the Northern Song capital.神京：京城，这里指北宋都城汴京。
蓝桥lán qiáo
lán qiáo: Blue Bridge, in today's Lantian in Shaanxi. In a Tang legend, the scholar Pei Hang met the fairy Yunying there, and both became immortals. Here it means fairyland, the palace on the moon.蓝桥：在今陕西蓝田。传说唐代书生裴航在蓝桥遇到仙女云英，后来两人都成了仙。这里指仙境，也就是月宫。
水晶帘shuǐ jīng lián
shuǐ jīng lián: a curtain made of strings of crystal beads.水晶帘：用水晶珠子串成的帘子。
云母屏yún mǔ píng
yún mǔ píng: a screen decorated with mica, a clear, flaky mineral.云母屏：用云母（一种透亮的薄片矿石）装饰的屏风。
冷浸lěng jìn
lěng jìn: soaked in cold moonlight.冷浸：清冷的月光浸透。
淡脂粉dàn zhī fěn
dàn zhī fěn: light make-up. Zhī fěn is rouge and powder.淡脂粉：淡淡的妆。脂粉，胭脂和粉，女子的化妆品。
待dài
dài: to intend, to plan to.待：打算，想要。
都将dōu jiāng
dōu jiāng: to take all of.都将：全都拿来。
许多明xǔ duō míng
xǔ duō míng: so much moonlight.许多明：这么多月光。
付与金尊fù yǔ jīn zūn
fù yǔ jīn zūn: to pour into a golden wine cup. Zūn is a wine vessel.付与金尊：倒进金酒杯里。尊，酒杯。
投晓tóu xiǎo
tóu xiǎo: until dawn.投晓：直到天亮。
流霞liú xiá
liú xiá: "flowing clouds", the wine of the immortals in legend; here, fine wine.流霞：神话里仙人喝的酒，这里指美酒。
倾尽qīng jìn
qīng jìn: to drink to the last drop.倾尽：喝得一滴不剩。
携取xié qǔ
xié qǔ: to bring along.携取：带上。
胡床hú chuáng
hú chuáng: a light folding chair.胡床：一种可以折叠的轻便坐椅。
南楼nán lóu
nán lóu: the South Tower. In the Eastern Jin, Yu Liang once sat on a folding chair in the South Tower at Wuchang, enjoying the moon with friends. The poem borrows this story.南楼：东晋庾亮曾在武昌南楼坐着胡床和朋友赏月谈笑。这里借用这个故事，指登楼赏月。
看玉做人间kàn yù zuò rén jiān
kàn yù zuò rén jiān: to watch moonlight turn the world into jade.看玉做人间：看月光把人间变成了白玉做的世界。
素秋千顷sù qiū qiān qǐng
sù qiū qiān qǐng: a thousand qing of pure white autumn. Sù means white; a qing is a unit of area, and a thousand qing means a vast stretch.素秋千顷：千顷的大地一片洁白的秋色。素，白；顷，面积单位，千顷形容非常大。
03

How to read it读音

螀jiāng
This character is read jiāng, the same sound as the word for river.螀读 jiāng，和“江”同音。
尽jìn
Here it means "used up" and is read jìn.这里是“完、光”的意思，读 jìn。
04

Background创作背景

Chao Buzhi wrote this lyric on Mid-Autumn night at Sizhou, near today's Xuyi in Jiangsu. In 1110 he was sent to govern Sizhou, and he died there not long after. This lyric is generally thought to be his last work. On Mid-Autumn night he gazed at the bright moon, thought of the distant capital, and imagined the fairyland on the moon. Then he wrote this lyric about enjoying the moon.

这首词是晁补之在泗州（今江苏盱眙一带）过中秋时写的。宋徽宗大观四年（1110年），他到泗州做知州，不久就在那里去世。一般认为这首词是他最后的作品。中秋夜，他望着天上的明月，想到遥远的京城，又想象月宫仙境，写下了这首赏月词。

05

The poet作者

Chao Buzhi (1053–1110), courtesy name Wujiu, also called Guilaizi, came from Juye in Jizhou, today's Juye in Shandong. He was a Northern Song writer. With Huang Tingjian, Qin Guan and Zhang Lei he was one of the Four Scholars of the Su School, all students of Su Shi. Court power struggles got him sent away from the capital several times, and for some years later in life he lived quietly in the countryside. His lyrics are bold and free, a little like Su Shi's.

晁补之（1053—1110），字无咎，号归来子，济州巨野（今山东巨野）人，北宋文学家。他和黄庭坚、秦观、张耒一起被称为“苏门四学士”，都是苏轼的学生。他一生在朝廷的党争中几次被贬，晚年曾退居乡间多年。他的词风格豪放，有点像苏轼。

06

The mood意境

This whole lyric is about the moon, yet it never once uses the word for moon. At the start, the moon flies out of the blue mist like a golden mirror. Then the cassia's shadow lies on the steps, and autumn insects call in the cold dew. He thinks the capital is far, but the palace on the moon seems close at hand. The second stanza moves indoors. The curtain stays up, the screen stands open, and cold moonlight falls on a lady's face. The best part is his wish at the end. He wants to pour all the moonlight in the sky into his wine cup and drink with everyone until dawn. Then he wants to carry a chair up the South Tower and watch the moon turn the whole world into white jade. As you read, imagine an autumn night when everything glows silver, as if people lived in a world made of jade: cool, clear and wide open.

这首词从头到尾写的都是月亮，却一次也没有直接说“月”字。开头，月亮像一面金镜从青烟里飞出来；接着，台阶上躺着桂树的影子，秋虫在凉露里叫。他想起京城很远，月宫却好像近在眼前。下片写屋里：帘子不放，屏风打开，月光冷冷地照在美人脸上。最妙的是后面的想法：要把满天的月光都倒进酒杯，和大家喝到天亮；还要搬把椅子登上南楼，看月光把整个人间变成一片白玉。读这首词，可以想象一个秋夜，到处都是银白色的光，人好像住在玉做的世界里，清冷，又很开阔。
