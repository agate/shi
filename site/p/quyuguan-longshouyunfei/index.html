Qu Yu Guan: Clouds Fly over the Hilltops

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 陇首云飞Clouds fly over the hilltops.山头上云在飞，
  2. 江边日晚By the river the day grows late.江边天色已晚，
  3. 烟波满目凭阑久Misty waters fill my eyes, and I have leaned on the railing a long time.满眼都是雾蒙蒙的水波，我靠着栏杆站了很久。
  4. 立望关河萧索Standing and gazing, I see bleak passes and rivers,站着远望，关山河流一片冷落，
  5. 千里清秋a thousand li of clear, cold autumn.千里都是清冷的秋色，
  6. 忍凝眸How can I bear to keep looking?我怎么忍心一直看下去。
  7. 杳杳神京The capital is far out of sight,遥远的京城看不见，
  8. 盈盈仙子and the lovely, fairy-like one there,那位美丽如仙的人，
  9. 别来锦字终难偶since we parted, my letters can never reach her.分别以后，书信总是难以送到她手里。
  10. 断雁无凭The lone goose cannot carry my message;孤雁靠不住，不能替我传信，
  11. 冉冉飞下汀洲it only drifts slowly down onto the islet.只是慢慢地飞落到水中的小洲上。
  12. 思悠悠My longing goes on and on.我的思念绵绵没有尽头。
  13. 暗想当初Silently I think back暗暗回想当初，
  14. 有多少幽欢佳会to all our happy times together.我们有过多少欢乐的相聚，
  15. 岂知聚散难期Who knew that meeting and parting could not be foreseen,哪里知道聚散难以预料，
  16. 翻成雨恨云愁and that it would all turn into sorrow as endless as clouds and rain?反而变成了像云雨一样绵绵的愁恨。
  17. 阻追游Now we can no longer go out together.如今再不能一起游玩了。
  18. 每登山临水Every time I climb a hill or come to the water,每次登山临水，
  19. 惹起平生心事it stirs up the deepest feeling of my life,都会引起心中最深的那件心事，
  20. 一场消黯and I am lost in heartbreak.我伤心得失魂落魄，
  21. 永日无言All day long I say nothing,整天说不出一句话，
  22. 却下层楼and then I go back down the tower.只好又走下高楼。
02

Words字词注释

陇首lǒng shǒu
lǒng shǒu: the hilltops. Lǒng is a ridge.陇首：山头。陇，山冈。
烟波yān bō
yān bō: water covered in mist.烟波：雾气笼罩的水面。
凭阑píng lán
píng lán: leaning on the railing.凭阑：靠着栏杆。
关河guān hé
guān hé: mountain passes and rivers, the long way to far places.关河：关山和河流，指远方的路途。
萧索xiāo suǒ
xiāo suǒ: bleak and lonely.萧索：冷落、凄凉。
忍凝眸rěn níng móu
rěn níng móu: how can I bear to keep staring? Níng móu is to gaze without blinking.忍凝眸：怎么忍心一直看下去。凝眸，目不转睛地看。
杳杳yǎo yǎo
yǎo yǎo: so far away it cannot be seen.杳杳：遥远得看不见。
神京shén jīng
shén jīng: the capital, here Bianjing, capital of the Northern Song.神京：京城，这里指北宋的都城汴京。
盈盈仙子yíng yíng xiān zǐ
yíng yíng xiān zǐ: a graceful, fairy-like woman, the one he loves and misses.盈盈仙子：姿态美好的女子，指词人思念的心上人。
锦字jǐn zì
jǐn zì: love letters. Legend says Su Hui of the Former Qin wove a poem of longing into silk and sent it to her husband.锦字：情人之间的书信。传说前秦苏蕙把思念丈夫的诗织在锦缎上寄给他。
终难偶zhōng nán ǒu
zhōng nán ǒu: never able to reach her, never able to meet. Ǒu means to match or meet.终难偶：总是难以送到对方手里，难以相会。偶，相合、相遇。
断雁无凭duàn yàn wú píng
duàn yàn wú píng: the lone goose cannot be trusted to carry letters. People once said wild geese carried messages.断雁无凭：失群的孤雁靠不住，不能替人传信。古人有大雁传书的说法。
冉冉rǎn rǎn
rǎn rǎn: slowly.冉冉：慢慢地。
汀洲tīng zhōu
tīng zhōu: a small flat islet in the water.汀洲：水中的小块平地。
思悠悠sī yōu yōu
sī yōu yōu: longing that goes on and on.思悠悠：思念绵长，没有尽头。
幽欢佳会yōu huān jiā huì
yōu huān jiā huì: quiet happy times together, sweet meetings.幽欢佳会：私下的欢聚，美好的相会。
难期nán qī
nán qī: impossible to foresee.难期：难以预料。
翻成fān chéng
fān chéng: turned instead into.翻成：反而变成。
雨恨云愁yǔ hèn yún chóu
yǔ hèn yún chóu: sorrow and regret as endless as rain and clouds, usually the pain of lovers apart.雨恨云愁：像雨像云一样绵绵不断的愁和恨，多指男女离别之苦。
阻追游zǔ zhuī yóu
zǔ zhuī yóu: kept from going out together any more.阻追游：不能再在一起游玩。阻，阻隔。
平生心事píng shēng xīn shì
píng shēng xīn shì: the thing he always carries in his heart, his longing for her.平生心事：一直藏在心里的事，指对她的思念。
消黯xiāo àn
xiāo àn: heartbroken, so sad that one's spirit seems to melt away.消黯：黯然销魂，伤心得失魂落魄。
永日yǒng rì
yǒng rì: the whole long day.永日：整天。
却下层楼què xià céng lóu
què xià céng lóu: going back down from the high tower. Què here means then, again.却下层楼：又走下高楼。却，再，又。

04

Background创作背景

Liu Yong wrote this lyric while wandering far from home. The exact time and place are no longer known. For many years he was away from the capital, traveling from place to place. When he climbed high and looked into the distance, he often thought of the woman he loved in the capital. The lyric has three parts. The first shows him gazing from a tower on an autumn evening. The second shows him missing her in the capital. The third looks back on their happy times and sighs over their parting.

这首词是柳永在外漂泊时写的，具体时间和地点已经不清楚。他长年离开京城，四处奔波，常在登高望远时想起京城里的心上人。这首词分三段：第一段写秋天傍晚登楼远望，第二段写想念京城的她，第三段回想当初的欢聚，叹息如今的分离。

05

The poet作者

Liu Yong (about 987 to about 1053), first named Sanbian, came from Chong'an, today's Wuyishan in Fujian. He was a Northern Song poet. As the seventh child he was called Liu Qi, and after one of his posts he was also called Liu Tuntian. As a young man he failed the imperial exam several times and spent many years living it up in the city. Later he moved around as a minor official. He was a master of the long, slow ci form, and often wrote about the hardship of wandering and the sadness of parting.

柳永（约987—约1053），原名三变，字耆卿，崇安（今福建武夷山）人，北宋词人。他排行第七，人称“柳七”；做过屯田员外郎，又称“柳屯田”。他年轻时多次考试失意，长期流连在都市的繁华生活里，后来又四处做小官。他擅长写长调慢词，常写漂泊的辛苦和离别的伤感。

06

The mood意境

This lyric has an unusual shape. It has three parts. The first two have almost the same pattern of lines, like a pair of doors opening side by side. Only the third part pours out his heart. The first part is a wide shot: clouds fly over the hills, the sun sets by the river, autumn spreads for a thousand li. He has leaned on the railing so long that he cannot bear to look any more. The second part pushes the view far away. He cannot see the capital, and he cannot see her. Even the wild goose will not carry his letter, and only drifts down onto the islet. The third part turns inward. They had so many happy times; who knew it would all turn into such sorrow? The last lines feel very real. Every time he climbs high he thinks of her, and he is so sad he says nothing all day. In the end he can only walk quietly back downstairs. He went up to see far away, and he comes down because looking made it worse. That trip up and down holds all the helplessness of longing.

这首词的结构很特别：一共三段，前两段长短句式几乎一样，像两扇对开的门，第三段才把心事全说出来。第一段是大镜头：山上云飞，江边日落，千里秋色，他靠着栏杆站了很久，看得都不忍心再看。第二段把镜头拉向远方：京城看不见，她也看不见，连大雁都不肯帮他送信，只慢慢落在小洲上。第三段回到心里：当初那么多快乐，谁知道会变成这么多愁。最后几句特别真实：每次登高都想她，伤心到一整天说不出话，最后只能默默走下楼去。上楼是为了看远方，下楼是因为看了更难过，这一上一下，写尽了思念的无奈。
