Song on Climbing the Youzhou Terrace

 

qián前bú不jiàn见gǔ古rén人

hòu后bú不jiàn见lái来zhě者

niàn念tiān天dì地zhī之yōu悠yōu悠

dú独chuàng怆rán然ér而tì涕xià下

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 前不见古人Looking back, I cannot see the wise rulers of old;往前看，见不到古代那些重用人才的贤君；
  2. 后不见来者looking ahead, I cannot see the ones still to come.往后看，也见不到将来的贤君。
  3. 念天地之悠悠I think of how vast and endless the sky and earth are,想到天地这样广大、这样长久，
  4. 独怆然而涕下and alone, in sorrow, I let my tears fall.我独自一人，伤心得流下了眼泪。
02

Words字词注释

古人gǔ rén
gǔ rén: people of long ago. Here it means the wise rulers of the past who gave able people a chance.古人：古代的人，这里指古代那些重用人才的贤明君主。
来者lái zhě
lái zhě: those who will come later, meaning wise rulers of the future.来者：以后的人，这里指将来那些贤明的君主。
念niàn
niàn: to think of.念：想到。
悠悠yōu yōu
yōu yōu: endless; it describes how wide and how old the sky and earth are.悠悠：没有尽头的样子，形容天地又广又久。
独dú
dú: alone, by oneself.独：独自，一个人。
怆然chuàng rán
chuàng rán: full of sorrow.怆然：伤心、难过的样子。
涕下tì xià
tì xià: tears fall. In old Chinese, tì means tears.涕下：流下眼泪。涕，古代指眼泪。
03

Background创作背景

This poem is usually dated to 696, under Empress Wu Zetian. That year the Khitan people rose in revolt, and Chen Zi'ang went north with an army led by Wu Youyi. He offered plans several times. Wu Youyi ignored them and even lowered his rank. Feeling low, Chen climbed the Youzhou Terrace, near today's Beijing, and wrote this poem. Legend links the place to King Zhao of Yan, who long ago built a Golden Terrace to welcome talented people. The poem was first recorded in a life of Chen written by his friend Lu Zangyong.

这首诗一般认为写于武则天万岁通天元年（696年）。那年契丹作乱，陈子昂跟随武攸宜带兵北上。他多次出主意，武攸宜不但不听，还把他降了职。他心情郁闷，登上幽州台（在今北京一带），写下这首诗。传说这里和战国时燕昭王筑黄金台招揽人才有关。这首诗最早记在他朋友卢藏用写的传记里。

04

The poet作者

Chen Zi'ang (661–702) came from Shehong in today's Sichuan. He had big dreams and often gave advice to the court, but it was rarely taken. He disliked the fancy, empty poetry of his time. He believed poems should carry real feeling and high ideals, and this idea shaped later Tang poetry a great deal.

陈子昂（661—702），字伯玉，梓州射洪（今四川射洪）人。他很有抱负，常常向朝廷提意见，却多次不被采纳。他反对当时只讲究辞藻华丽的诗风，主张诗要有真情实感和远大的志气，对后来的唐诗影响很大。

05

The mood意境

Picture a man standing on a high terrace. Around him is empty open land, and above him is the endless sky. He thinks back: the rulers of old who knew how to use good people are long gone. He thinks ahead: he will not live to see the wise rulers of the future. Time is like a long river, and he stands on one tiny point in the middle. The sky and earth are so big and so old, and a person is so small and so alone, that he cannot hold back his tears. The poem has only four lines, some long and some short, like one long sigh. It is not only about one man whose talent was wasted. It is also about the loneliness anyone can feel facing the whole universe, and that is why it has moved readers for more than a thousand years.

想象一个人站在高高的台上，四周是空空的原野，头顶是无边的天。他往前想，古代那些懂得用人的君主早就不在了；往后想，将来的明君他也等不到。时间像一条长河，他只站在中间小小的一点上。天地那么大、那么久，人却这么小、这么孤单，他忍不住哭了。这首诗只有四句，句子有长有短，像一声长长的叹息。它写的不只是一个人没被重用的委屈，也是每个人面对天地时都会有的孤独感，所以一千多年来打动了很多人。
