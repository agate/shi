薄幸·淡妆多态

贺铸He Zhu · Song宋

Bo Xing: Light Makeup, Many Charms

 

dàn淡zhuāng妆duō多tài态

gèng更dí的dí的pín频huí回miǎn眄lài睐

biàn便rèn认de得qín琴xīn心xiān先xǔ许

yù欲wǎn绾hé合huān欢shuāng双dài带

jì记huà画táng堂fēng风yuè月féng逢yíng迎

qīng轻pín颦qiǎn浅xiào笑jiāo娇wú无nài奈

xiàng向shuì睡yā鸭lú炉biān边

xiáng翔yuān鸳píng屏lǐ里

xiū羞bǎ把xiāng香luó罗àn暗jiě解

zì自guò过le了shāo烧dēng灯hòu后

dōu都bú不jiàn见tà踏qīng青tiāo挑cài菜

jǐ几huí回píng凭shuāng双yàn燕

dīng丁níng宁shēn深yì意

wǎng往lái来què却hèn恨chóng重lián帘ài碍

yuē约hé何shí时zài再

zhèng正chūn春nóng浓jiǔ酒kùn困

rén人xián闲zhòu昼yǒng永wú无liáo聊lài赖

yóu犹yǒu有huā花shāo梢rì日zài在

The source text differs a lot from the common version. It opens with a different word for beauty, and has different wording in many places, such as the bed curtains, the slanting moon, the warm wine and the line about tying the sash. Here we use the common text, as in Three Hundred Song Ci.

原文与通行本差别较大：开头作“艳真多态”，又有“琴心相许”“与写宜男双带”“斜月朦胧”“轻颦微笑”“便翡翠屏开，芙蓉帐掩，与把香罗偷解”“往来翻恨”“春浓酒暖”等不同。这里采用《宋词三百首》等通行本的文字。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 淡妆多态Lightly made up, she was full of charm.她化着淡妆，姿态动人。
  2. 更的的频回眄睐Her bright eyes turned back to glance at me again and again.更有那明亮的眼睛，一次次回头看我。
  3. 便认得琴心先许Then I knew she had already given her heart,我就明白，她心里已经先答应了，
  4. 欲绾合欢双带and wished to tie our sashes in a love knot.想和我把一对衣带结成同心结。
  5. 记画堂风月逢迎I remember meeting in the painted hall, under the breeze and the moon,记得在华美的厅堂，清风明月下相会，
  6. 轻颦浅笑娇无奈her light frown and faint smile so lovely I was helpless.她轻轻皱眉、浅浅一笑，娇美得叫人没办法。
  7. 向睡鸭炉边Beside the sleeping-duck incense burner,在睡鸭香炉旁边，
  8. 翔鸳屏里behind the screen of flying mandarin ducks,在画着飞鸳鸯的屏风里，
  9. 羞把香罗暗解she shyly untied her fragrant silk sash.她害羞地悄悄解开了香罗带。
  10. 自过了烧灯后But ever since the lanterns of the Lantern Festival,可是自从过了元宵节点灯以后，
  11. 都不见踏青挑菜I have not seen her at the spring outings or among those gathering greens.踏青、挖野菜的时候，都没再见到她。
  12. 几回凭双燕Several times I asked a pair of swallows好几次托一对燕子，
  13. 丁宁深意to carry my deep feelings to her again and again,反复带去我深深的心意，
  14. 往来却恨重帘碍but as they flew back and forth, I hated the layered curtains that blocked them.可燕子来来往往，又恨那一层层帘子挡住了路。
  15. 约何时再When will we meet again?约好什么时候再见呢？
  16. 正春浓酒困It is deep spring, and I am drowsy with wine,正是春意浓浓、喝酒困倦的时候，
  17. 人闲昼永无聊赖idle through the long day, with nothing to lean on.人闲着，白天又长，心里空落落的。
  18. 厌厌睡起I wake up listless,没精打采地睡醒起来，
  19. 犹有花梢日在and the sun still hangs at the tips of the flowering branches.太阳还挂在花枝梢头。
02

Words字词注释

淡妆dàn zhuāng
dàn zhuāng: light makeup.淡妆：化着淡淡的妆。
多态duō tài
duō tài: full of charm in every move.多态：姿态很多，很有风韵。
的的dí dí
dí dí: bright, shining.的的：明亮的样子。
眄睐miǎn lài
miǎn lài: glancing sideways, casting looks.眄睐：斜着眼看，送眼波。
琴心先许qín xīn xiān xǔ
qín xīn xiān xǔ: she had already given her heart. In the Han dynasty, Sima Xiangru played the qin to show his love for Zhuo Wenjun; she understood the music and went away with him.琴心先许：心里已经先答应了。汉代司马相如弹琴表达对卓文君的爱慕，卓文君听懂了琴声，就跟他走了。
绾wǎn
wǎn: to tie into a knot.绾：把带子打成结。
合欢双带hé huān shuāng dài
hé huān shuāng dài: a pair of sashes tied in a love knot, a sign that two people are joined in love.合欢双带：打成同心结的一对衣带，表示两人结为恩爱。
画堂huà táng
huà táng: a beautifully decorated hall.画堂：装饰华美的厅堂。
风月逢迎fēng yuè féng yíng
fēng yuè féng yíng: meeting under the breeze and the moon.风月逢迎：在清风明月下相会。
轻颦qīng pín
qīng pín: a slight frown.轻颦：轻轻皱一下眉。颦，皱眉。
娇无奈jiāo wú nài
jiāo wú nài: so lovely that one is helpless before her.娇无奈：娇美得叫人没有办法。
睡鸭炉shuì yā lú
shuì yā lú: an incense burner shaped like a sleeping duck.睡鸭炉：做成睡着的鸭子样子的香炉。
翔鸳屏xiáng yuān píng
xiáng yuān píng: a screen painted with flying mandarin ducks.翔鸳屏：画着飞翔的鸳鸯的屏风。
香罗xiāng luó
xiāng luó: a fragrant silk sash.香罗：带着香气的丝绸衣带。
烧灯shāo dēng
shāo dēng: lighting lanterns, at the Lantern Festival on the fifteenth of the first month.烧灯：点灯，指正月十五元宵节点花灯。
踏青挑菜tà qīng tiāo cài
tà qīng tiāo cài: spring outings and gathering wild greens, an old custom around the second day of the second month.踏青挑菜：春天到郊外游玩、挖野菜。古时二月初二前后有挑菜的风俗。
凭píng
píng: to rely on, to entrust.凭：靠，托。
丁宁dīng níng
dīng níng: to tell again and again.丁宁：反复嘱咐，同“叮咛”。
重帘碍chóng lián ài
chóng lián ài: blocked by layers of curtains.重帘碍：一层层的帘子挡住了。碍，阻挡。
酒困jiǔ kùn
jiǔ kùn: drowsy from wine.酒困：喝了酒后困倦。
昼永zhòu yǒng
zhòu yǒng: the day is long.昼永：白天很长。
无聊赖wú liáo lài
wú liáo lài: empty, with nothing to lean on.无聊赖：心里空空的，没有什么可以寄托。
厌厌yān yān
yān yān: listless and weary.厌厌：没精神的样子，同“恹恹”。
花梢日在huā shāo rì zài
huā shāo rì zài: the sun still hangs at the tips of the flowering branches; the day is not yet over.花梢日在：太阳还挂在花枝梢头，说明白天还没过完。
03

How to read it读音

的dí
Read dí dí here, meaning bright; it describes her shining eyes.“的的”读 dí dí，意思是明亮的样子，这里形容眼睛明亮有神。
厌yān
Here the doubled character is read yān yān, meaning listless and weary.“厌厌”同“恹恹”，读 yān yān，意思是没精神、懒洋洋的。
重chóng
Chóng lián means layer upon layer of curtains, so the character is read chóng.“重帘”是一层又一层的帘子，“重”读 chóng。
宁níng
Dīng níng is an old way of writing the word for urging again and again.“丁宁”同“叮咛”，这里读 dīng níng。
04

Background创作背景

This ci tells a love story. The first stanza remembers meeting a woman, falling in love and their secret meeting. The second tells how, after they parted, he could not see her again and could only miss her through the spring days. It is usually read as written in a man's voice. There is no reliable record of when or for whom it was written. Several versions exist with quite different wording; here we use the common one.

这首词写一段恋情：上半首回忆和一位女子从相识、相爱到幽会的经过，下半首写分别以后再也见不到她，只能在春日里苦苦思念。一般认为是以男子的口吻来写的。写于何时、为谁而写，没有可靠的记载。这首词有好几个版本，文字差别不小，这里用的是通行本。

05

The poet作者

He Zhu (1052–1125), also called Qinghu Yilao, was a Northern Song ci poet. He was tall with a dark face, and people nicknamed him Ghost-head He. He was bold by nature, yet many of his ci are soft and tender. A famous line of his about rain when the plums turn yellow earned him the nickname Plum He. This ci belongs to his tender kind.

贺铸（1052—1125），字方回，号庆湖遗老，北宋词人。他长得高大，脸色青黑，人称“贺鬼头”，性格豪爽，可他的词却有很多写得十分柔美。他的《青玉案》里有“梅子黄时雨”的名句，人们又叫他“贺梅子”。这首词就属于他柔美细腻的一类。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is a sweet memory. She wears light makeup, and her bright eyes glance back at him again and again. He understands her heart at once. Later they meet in the painted hall, and her frown and smile are utterly charming. The second stanza turns cold. After the Lantern Festival he never sees her again on the spring outings. He sends word by the swallows, but even they cannot get past the layers of curtains. The livelier spring gets, the emptier he feels. He drinks, sleeps and wakes, and the sun is still hanging on the flowering branches. How long this day is! The last line never says I miss you. It only says the sun will not set, yet all the restless waiting is inside it.

上半首像一段甜甜的回忆：她化着淡妆，眼睛亮亮的，一次又一次回头看他。他一下子就懂了她的心意。后来在画堂里相会，她皱眉、浅笑，可爱极了。下半首一下子变冷：过了元宵节，春天的郊游里再也看不见她。他托燕子带话，可燕子也飞不进一层层的帘子。春天越热闹，他越无聊，喝了酒睡一觉醒来，太阳竟然还挂在花枝上，这一天怎么这样长。最后一句不说想念，只写太阳迟迟不落，可等待的心焦全在里面了。
