bái白jū居yì易Bai Juyi · Tang唐

Song of the Pipa

This page uses the text in current high-school textbooks. The corpus title is Pipa Yin, the original title in Bai Juyi's collected works. Textbooks call it Pipa Xing with Preface; the prose preface is not included in the lines here. Compared with the corpus, the main differences are: sè sè (rustling) where the corpus has suǒ suǒ; bù dé zhì where the corpus has bù dé yì (both mean "not getting what one wished"); bīng xià nán ("struggling under ice") where the corpus has shuǐ xià tān ("down the rapids"); bīng quán and níng jué ("icy spring", "frozen") where the corpus has shuǐ quán and yí jué; dōng chuán where the corpus has dōng zhōu (both mean "east boat"); míng shǔ ("listed in") where the corpus has a conversion slip; shàn cái fú where the corpus has a variant character for fú; yín bì ("silver comb") where the corpus has yún bì ("cloud comb"); and dì pì ("remote") where the corpus has xiǎo chù ("small place"). Several others are only variant forms of the same character. Scholars have long argued over bīng xià nán versus shuǐ xià tān; textbooks use bīng xià nán.

本文用现行高中语文教材的文本。语料库的题目是《琵琶引》，这是白居易文集里的原题；课本题作《琵琶行并序》，前面还有一段序，这里没有收进诗句。和语料库文本相比，主要不同有：“秋瑟瑟”语料库作“秋索索”；“不得志”作“不得意”；“幽咽泉流冰下难”作“水下滩”，“冰泉冷涩弦凝绝，凝绝不通”作“水泉冷涩弦疑绝，疑绝不通”；“东船西舫”作“东舟西舫”；“名属教坊”作“名蜀”（是转换错误）；“善才服”作“善才伏”；“钿头银篦”作“钿头云篦”；“浔阳地僻无音乐”作“浔阳小处无音乐”。还有几处只是异体字，如“捻、瓶、沉、蟆、妒、裙、翻、猿”。其中“冰下难”和“水下滩”历来有争论，课本用“冰下难”。

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 浔阳江头夜送客At night I saw a guest off on the bank of the Xunyang River.夜里，我在浔阳江边送别客人，
  2. 枫叶荻花秋瑟瑟Maple leaves and reed flowers rustled in the autumn wind.枫叶和荻花在秋风里瑟瑟作响。
  3. 主人下马客在船I got off my horse; my guest was already on the boat.我下了马，客人已经上了船，
  4. 举酒欲饮无管弦We raised our cups to drink, but there was no music.举起酒杯要喝，却没有音乐助兴。
  5. 醉不成欢惨将别Drinking brought no joy, only the sadness of parting.喝醉了也不快乐，只能伤心地分别，
  6. 别时茫茫江浸月As we parted, the moon lay soaking in the wide, dim river.分别时，茫茫江水里浸着一轮月亮。
  7. 忽闻水上琵琶声Suddenly we heard a pipa playing on the water.忽然听见水面上传来琵琶声，
  8. 主人忘归客不发I forgot to go home, and my guest did not set off.我忘了回去，客人也不出发了。
  9. 寻声暗问弹者谁Following the sound, we asked softly who was playing.循着声音小声问：弹琵琶的是谁？
  10. 琵琶声停欲语迟The music stopped. She wanted to answer but was slow to speak.琵琶声停了，她想回答，却迟迟没开口。
  11. 移船相近邀相见We moved our boat closer and asked her to meet us.我们把船移近，请她出来相见，
  12. 添酒回灯重开宴We poured more wine, lit the lamps again and began the feast anew.添了酒，重新点亮灯，又摆开了酒席。
  13. 千呼万唤始出来Only after we called a thousand times did she come out,叫了千百遍，她才肯出来，
  14. 犹抱琵琶半遮面still holding the pipa so it hid half her face.还抱着琵琶，遮住半边脸。
  15. 转轴拨弦三两声She turned the pegs and plucked two or three notes to test them.她拧动弦轴，拨了两三声试音，
  16. 未成曲调先有情Before any tune began, the notes were already full of feeling.还没成曲子，就已经满含感情。
  17. 弦弦掩抑声声思Every string sounded low and held back; every note was full of sorrow,每根弦都低沉压抑，每个声音都含着愁思，
  18. 似诉平生不得志as if telling of a whole life that went wrong.好像在诉说她一辈子的不如意。
  19. 低眉信手续续弹Head bent, she played on and on, letting her hands move freely,她低着头，随手连续不断地弹，
  20. 说尽心中无限事telling all the countless things in her heart.把心里无数的心事都说了出来。
  21. 轻拢慢捻抹复挑She pressed lightly, rolled slowly, swept down and flicked up.轻轻地拢，慢慢地捻，又抹又挑，
  22. 初为霓裳后六幺First she played Rainbow Skirt, then Green Waist.先弹《霓裳》，后弹《六幺》。
  23. 大弦嘈嘈如急雨The thick string pounded like a sudden downpour;粗弦声音沉重急促，像一阵急雨，
  24. 小弦切切如私语the thin string whispered like secret talk.细弦声音轻细，像有人说悄悄话。
  25. 嘈嘈切切错杂弹Heavy and soft notes poured out, woven together,重的轻的声音交错着弹出来，
  26. 大珠小珠落玉盘like big and small pearls falling on a jade plate.像大大小小的珍珠落在玉盘上。
  27. 间关莺语花底滑Now it was like an oriole singing smoothly beneath the flowers;一会儿像黄莺在花下宛转地唱，
  28. 幽咽泉流冰下难now like a spring sobbing as it struggles under ice.一会儿像泉水在冰下艰难地呜咽流淌。
  29. 冰泉冷涩弦凝绝The icy spring seemed to freeze, and the strings seemed to stop.泉水像是冻住了，弦声也像凝住了，
  30. 凝绝不通声暂歇Frozen and blocked, the sound paused for a while.凝住不通，声音暂时停了下来。
  31. 别有幽愁暗恨生Then a hidden sorrow and grief rose up;另有一种深藏的忧愁和怨恨生出来，
  32. 此时无声胜有声at that moment, silence said more than sound.这时没有声音，反而比有声音更动人。
  33. 银瓶乍破水浆迸Suddenly it was like a silver jar bursting and water splashing out,忽然像银瓶破裂，水一下子迸溅出来，
  34. 铁骑突出刀枪鸣like armored horsemen charging out, swords and spears clashing.又像铁甲骑兵冲出，刀枪铿锵作响。
  35. 曲终收拨当心画As the piece ended, she drew the plectrum hard across the middle,一曲终了，她收起拨子在琴中间一划，
  36. 四弦一声如裂帛and all four strings rang out at once, like silk being torn.四根弦同时发声，像撕裂丝绸一样。
  37. 东船西舫悄无言On the boats east and west, all was silent; no one spoke.东边西边的船上都静悄悄的，没人说话，
  38. 唯见江心秋月白We saw only the white autumn moon in the middle of the river.只看见江心映着一轮白白的秋月。
  39. 沉吟放拨插弦中Hesitating, she tucked the plectrum between the strings,她犹豫着把拨子插在弦中间，
  40. 整顿衣裳起敛容straightened her clothes, stood up and composed her face.整理好衣裳，站起来，脸色变得庄重。
  41. 自言本是京城女She said she had once been a girl of the capital, Chang'an,她说自己本来是京城长安的女子，
  42. 家在虾蟆陵下住living near Hama Ling.家住在虾蟆陵一带。
  43. 十三学得琵琶成At thirteen she had mastered the pipa,十三岁就学会了弹琵琶，
  44. 名属教坊第一部and her name was on the list of the best troupe of the court music office.名字列在教坊第一队里。
  45. 曲罢曾教善才服Every time she finished a piece, even the master musicians admired her;每弹完一曲，连琵琶高手都佩服她，
  46. 妆成每被秋娘妒every time she dressed up, the other beauties were jealous.每次打扮好，都让美丽的歌女们嫉妒。
  47. 五陵年少争缠头Rich young men of the capital fought to give her gifts;京城的富家子弟争着送她礼物，
  48. 一曲红绡不知数for one tune she got more red silk than she could count.弹一曲，得到的红绸多得数不清。
  49. 钿头银篦击节碎Silver combs with jeweled tops broke as she beat time with them;镶着花饰的银梳，因为打拍子而敲碎了，
  50. 血色罗裙翻酒污blood-red silk skirts were stained with spilled wine.血红的丝裙，被翻倒的酒弄脏了。
  51. 今年欢笑复明年She laughed this year, and laughed again the next,今年欢笑，明年还是欢笑，
  52. 秋月春风等闲度letting autumn moons and spring breezes slip by without a thought.秋月春风，好时光随随便便就过去了。
  53. 弟走从军阿姨死Then her brother went off to the army, and the woman who looked after her died.后来弟弟去参军，管她的阿姨也死了，
  54. 暮去朝来颜色故Evenings went and mornings came, and her looks faded.日子一天天过去，她的容貌也老了。
  55. 门前冷落鞍马稀Her gate grew lonely; few horses stopped there now.门前冷冷清清，来的客人越来越少，
  56. 老大嫁作商人妇Grown old, she married a merchant.年纪大了，只好嫁给一个商人做妻子。
  57. 商人重利轻别离A merchant cares for money and thinks little of being apart;商人看重钱，不把分别当回事，
  58. 前月浮梁买茶去last month he went off to Fuliang to buy tea.上个月就到浮梁买茶去了。
  59. 去来江口守空船Since he left, she has kept the empty boat at the river mouth,他走后，我在江口守着空船，
  60. 绕船月明江水寒with bright moonlight all around and the cold river water.围着船的是明亮的月光和寒冷的江水。
  61. 夜深忽梦少年事Deep in the night she suddenly dreamed of her young days,夜深了，忽然梦见年轻时的事，
  62. 梦啼妆泪红阑干and cried in her dream until tears red with makeup streaked her face.在梦里哭醒，泪水和着胭脂流了满脸。
  63. 我闻琵琶已叹息Hearing the pipa, I had already sighed;我听了琵琶声，已经在叹息，
  64. 又闻此语重唧唧hearing her story, I sighed again and again.又听了她这番话，更加叹息不止。
  65. 同是天涯沦落人We are both people fallen on hard times, far from home.我们都是流落天涯、失意的人，
  66. 相逢何必曾相识Now that we have met, why should it matter that we never knew each other before?今天相遇了，何必一定要从前就认识！
  67. 我从去年辞帝京Last year I left the capital,我从去年离开京城长安，
  68. 谪居卧病浔阳城and was sent down to Xunyang, where I often lie ill.被贬到浔阳城，常常生病躺着。
  69. 浔阳地僻无音乐Xunyang is remote and has no music;浔阳地方偏僻，没有什么音乐，
  70. 终岁不闻丝竹声all year I never hear strings or flutes.一整年都听不到弦乐和管乐的声音。
  71. 住近湓江地低湿I live by the Pen River, on low, damp ground,我住在湓江边，地势低又潮湿，
  72. 黄芦苦竹绕宅生with yellow reeds and bitter bamboo growing round my house.枯黄的芦苇和苦竹围着房子生长。
  73. 其间旦暮闻何物What do I hear here, morning and evening?在这里，早晚能听到什么呢？
  74. 杜鹃啼血猿哀鸣Only the cuckoo crying blood and the apes wailing.只有杜鹃悲啼和猿猴哀叫。
  75. 春江花朝秋月夜On spring mornings of flowers by the river, and autumn nights of moonlight,春天江边开花的早晨，秋天有月亮的夜晚，
  76. 往往取酒还独倾I often take some wine and drink it alone.我常常拿来酒，一个人独自喝。
  77. 岂无山歌与村笛Are there no mountain songs or village flutes?难道没有山歌和村里的笛声吗？
  78. 呕哑嘲哳难为听There are, but they are harsh and jarring, hard to listen to.有，可是声音杂乱刺耳，实在难听。
  79. 今夜闻君琵琶语Tonight, hearing your pipa speak,今晚听了你弹的琵琶，

03

How to read it读音

谁shuí
This character is often said shéi in speech; in poems textbooks usually mark shuí. Both are accepted.“谁”口语多读 shéi，读诗时课本常标 shuí，两种都对。
重chóng
Here it means "again", so it is read chóng.“重开宴”的“重”是“再一次”，读 chóng。
转zhuàn
Turning the pegs means rotating them, so it is read zhuàn; some books mark zhuǎn.转轴是旋转弦轴，按意思读 zhuàn；有的读本标 zhuǎn。
思sī
Here it means sad thoughts and is read sī today. In old usage this noun was read sì, and some books still mark sì.这里指愁思，现在读 sī。旧时作名词读 sì，有的读本仍标 sì。
抹mǒ
A pipa playing move, usually read mǒ; some read it mò.弹琵琶的指法，一般读 mǒ；一说读 mò。
挑tiǎo
Flicking the string upward is read tiǎo, not tiāo.往上拨弦，读 tiǎo，不读 tiāo。
为wéi
Here it means "to play, to do", read wéi.“初为”是“先弹”，读 wéi。
裳cháng
In ní cháng (rainbow skirt), this character is read cháng.“霓裳”读 ní cháng。
间jiān
In jiān guān, a word for birdsong, it is read jiān.“间关”读 jiān guān，形容鸟叫。
咽yè
Here it means a sob caught in the throat, read yè, not yān or yàn.“幽咽”的“咽”指哭声堵在喉咙里，读 yè，不读 yān 或 yàn。
骑qí
The standard reading today is qí. The old reading jì, meaning horseman, appears in some older textbooks.现在规范读 qí。旧读 jì，指骑兵，一些老教材标 jì。
裳cháng
In everyday speech yīshang; in old poems it is usually read yī cháng.现代口语“衣裳”读 yīshang；读古诗时一般读 yī cháng。
虾há
Há má here means a toad, read há, not xiā.“虾蟆”就是“蛤蟆”，读 há má，不读 xiā。
教jiào
Here it means "made, caused", read jiào.“教”是“使、让”的意思，读 jiào。
少shào
Nián shào means young people, read shào.“年少”指年轻人，读 shào。
血xuè
In written words like this, it is read xuè.“血色”在书面语里读 xuè。
朝zhāo
Here it means morning, read zhāo, not cháo.“朝”是早晨，读 zhāo，不读 cháo。
少shào

04

Background创作背景

In 815 the chief minister Wu Yuanheng was murdered in the streets of Chang'an. Bai Juyi was the first to write to the emperor, asking that the killers be caught at once. He was accused of meddling in matters that were not his job, was slandered as well, and was sent away as Sima of Jiangzhou. Jiangzhou is today's Jiujiang in Jiangxi. The next autumn, seeing off a guest at Penpu, he heard someone playing the pipa on a boat at night, in the style of the capital. The player turned out to be a former singer of Chang'an who had married a merchant when she grew old. Thinking of his own fate, Bai wrote this long poem. The poet tells all this in the preface before the poem.

元和十年（815年），宰相武元衡在长安街头被刺杀。白居易第一个上书，要求赶快捉拿凶手，却被说成是越职言事（管了不该自己管的事），又遭人诬陷，被贬为江州司马。江州就是今天的江西九江。第二年秋天，他在湓浦口送客，夜里听到船上有人弹琵琶，有京城的味道。一问，弹琴的是从前长安的歌女，年老后嫁给了商人。白居易想到自己的遭遇，写下了这首长诗。这些经过都写在诗前的序里。

05

The poet作者

Bai Juyi (772–846), also called Letian and the Hermit of Xiangshan, was a great Tang poet. He believed poems should show real society and real lives, in plain words that ordinary people could understand. Song of the Pipa and Song of Everlasting Sorrow are his two most famous long poems. When he wrote this one he was in his forties and had just been sent from the capital to remote Jiangzhou. He felt wronged and lost, so he understood the pipa player's feelings deeply.

白居易（772—846），字乐天，号香山居士，唐代大诗人。他主张诗要写真实的社会和人生，语言要平易，让普通人也听得懂。《琵琶行》和《长恨歌》是他最有名的两首长诗。写这首诗时，他四十多岁，刚从京城被贬到偏远的江州，心里满是委屈和失落，所以特别能体会琵琶女的心情。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a short film in several scenes. Scene one: seeing off a guest by the river on an autumn night. There is no music, only a cold moon soaking in the water. Scene two: the pipa begins. The woman comes out only after a thousand calls, still hiding half her face behind the pipa. Scene three is the best music writing in the whole poem: pounding rain, whispers, pearls falling on a jade plate, an oriole singing, a spring sobbing under ice. When the music stops, "silence says more than sound". Then it bursts out like a silver jar breaking and armored horsemen charging. Sound you cannot see becomes something you can see and almost touch. The last note is like tearing silk; then all is quiet, with only the autumn moon in the river. Scene four: the woman tells her life, from her days of fame to a lonely old age. Scene five: the poet tells of his own exile and says, "We are both people fallen on hard times, far from home; now that we meet, why must we have known each other before?" A singer and an official are very different, but their broken hopes are the same. At last the pipa plays again, everyone weeps, and the one who cries most is the poet himself.

这首诗像一部短电影，分成几场。第一场：秋夜江边送客，没有音乐，只有冷冷的月亮浸在江水里。第二场：琵琶声响起，女子千呼万唤才出来，还抱着琵琶遮住半边脸。第三场是全诗最精彩的音乐描写：急雨、私语、珠子落在玉盘上、黄莺唱歌、泉水在冰下呜咽，声音停下时“此时无声胜有声”，接着又像银瓶破裂、铁骑冲出。看不见的声音，被写得能看见、能摸到。最后一声像撕开丝绸，四周一片安静，只有江心一轮秋月。第四场：女子讲自己的一生，从红极一时到年老孤单。第五场：诗人说起自己被贬的苦，说出了“同是天涯沦落人，相逢何必曾相识”。一个歌女，一个官员，身份不同，失意的心却一样。最后琵琶再响，满座落泪，哭得最厉害的就是诗人自己。
