lán兰líng陵wáng王·chūn春hèn恨

zhāng张yuán元gān干Zhang Yuangan · Song宋

Prince of Lanling: Spring Sorrow

 

juǎn卷zhū珠bó箔

zhāo朝yǔ雨qīng轻yīn阴zhà乍gé阁

lán阑gān干wài外

yān烟liǔ柳nòng弄qíng晴

fāng芳cǎo草qīn侵jiē阶yìng映hóng红yào药

dōng东fēng风dù妒huā花è恶

chuī吹luò落

shāo梢tóu头nèn嫩è萼

píng屏shān山yǎn掩

chén沉shuǐ水juàn倦xūn熏

zhòng中jiǔ酒xīn心qíng情pà怕bēi杯sháo勺

xún寻sī思jiù旧jīng京luò洛

zhèng正nián年shào少shū疏kuáng狂

gē歌xiào笑mí迷zhuó著

zhàng障ní泥yóu油bì壁cuī催shū梳lüè掠

céng曾chí驰dào道tóng同zài载

shàng上lín林xié携shǒu手

dēng灯yè夜chū初guò过zǎo早gòng共yuē约

yòu又zhēng争xìn信piāo漂bó泊

jì寂mò寞

niàn念xíng行lè乐

shèn甚fěn粉dàn淡yī衣jīn襟

yīn音duàn断xián弦suǒ索

qióng琼zhī枝bì璧yuè月chūn春rú如zuó昨

chàng怅bié别hòu后huá华biǎo表

nǎ那huí回shuāng双hè鹤

xiāng相sī思chú除shì是

xiàng向zuì醉lǐ里zàn暂wàng忘què却

In line 1 the source text has zhū meaning "red" (a red curtain); common editions such as Three Hundred Song Lyrics have zhū meaning "pearl" (a beaded curtain), used here. The source text writes the first character of line 10 as shěn, a slip from machine conversion. The word is chén shuǐ, agarwood incense, written today with the character for chén. It is corrected here.

原始资料第一句作“朱箔”（红帘），通行本（如《宋词三百首》）多作“珠箔”（珠帘），这里从通行本。第十句作“沈水”，这是繁体转简体时的错字：“沈水”就是“沉水”（沉香），今天写作“沉”，这里改正。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  2. 朝雨轻阴乍阁The morning rain and light clouds have just stopped.早上的雨和淡淡的阴云刚刚停住。
  3. 阑干外Beyond the railing,栏杆外面，
  4. 烟柳弄晴misty willows sway in the sunshine,柳树像笼着轻烟，在晴光里摇摆，
  5. 芳草侵阶映红药and green grass climbs the steps beside red peonies.青草长上了台阶，映着红色的芍药。
  6. 东风妒花恶The east wind seems jealous of the flowers and blows hard,东风好像嫉妒花儿，吹得那么凶，
  7. 吹落blowing down吹落了
  8. 梢头嫩萼the tender buds on the branches.枝头嫩嫩的花苞。
  9. 屏山掩The screen is closed.屏风掩着，
  10. 沉水倦熏I am too weary to light the agarwood.沉香我也懒得去点，
  11. 中酒心情怕杯勺Sick from wine, I dread the sight of a cup.酒醉难受，现在一看到酒杯就怕。
  12. 寻思旧京洛I think back to the old capital,回想起旧日在京城的时候，
  13. 正年少疏狂when I was young and free,那时正年轻，无拘无束，
  14. 歌笑迷著lost in songs and laughter.沉醉在歌声和笑声里。

02

Words字词注释

珠箔zhū bó
zhū bó: a curtain of strung beads, or any fine curtain.珠箔：用珠子串成的帘子，泛指华美的帘子。
乍阁zhà gé
zhà gé: has just stopped.乍阁：刚刚停住。“阁”同“搁”，停止。
阑干lán gān
lán gān: a railing.阑干：栏杆。
烟柳弄晴yān liǔ nòng qíng
yān liǔ nòng qíng: willows that look wrapped in mist sway in the sunshine.烟柳弄晴：像笼着轻烟的柳树，在晴光里轻轻摇摆。
侵阶qīn jiē
qīn jiē: growing right up onto the steps.侵阶：长到了台阶上。
红药hóng yào
hóng yào: red peonies.红药：红色的芍药花。
妒花恶dù huā è
dù huā è: as if jealous of the flowers, blowing cruelly.妒花恶：好像嫉妒花儿，吹得很凶。
嫩萼nèn è
nèn è: tender flower buds. The è are the small green leaves around a flower.嫩萼：嫩嫩的花苞。萼是包在花外面的绿色小片。
屏山píng shān
píng shān: a folding screen, often painted with mountains, hence the name.屏山：屏风。上面常画着山水，所以叫屏山。
沉水chén shuǐ
chén shuǐ: agarwood, a fragrant wood burned as incense.沉水：沉香，一种很香的木头，可以点来熏屋子。
倦熏juàn xūn
juàn xūn: too weary to burn the incense.倦熏：懒得去点香。
杯勺bēi sháo
bēi sháo: wine cups and ladles, meaning drinking.杯勺：酒杯和舀酒的勺子，代指喝酒。
寻思xún sī
xún sī: to think back.寻思：回想。
旧京洛jiù jīng luò
jiù jīng luò: the old capital. The phrase first meant Luoyang, then any capital. Here it is the Northern Song capital, Bianjing (today's Kaifeng in Henan).旧京洛：旧日的京城。京洛本指洛阳，后来泛指京城，这里指北宋的都城汴京（今河南开封）。
疏狂shū kuáng
shū kuáng: free and wild, with no restraint.疏狂：无拘无束，任性放纵。
障泥zhàng ní
zhàng ní: mud flaps hung on both sides of a saddle; here it means the horse.障泥：挂在马鞍两边挡泥的垫子，这里代指马。
油壁yóu bì
yóu bì: a carriage with glossy painted sides, often ridden by women.油壁：油壁车，车壁漆得光亮的车子，古代女子常坐。
催梳掠cuī shū lüè
cuī shū lüè: urging her to hurry and do her hair.催梳掠：催她快点梳头打扮。
驰道chí dào
chí dào: the wide imperial road in the capital.驰道：京城里皇帝车马走的大道。
同载tóng zài
tóng zài: riding in the same carriage.同载：坐同一辆车。
上林shàng lín
shàng lín: the Shanglin Park, a Han royal garden; here the royal gardens of the capital.上林：上林苑，本是汉代皇家园林，这里指京城的皇家园林。
灯夜dēng yè
dēng yè: the night of the Lantern Festival, when lanterns hang everywhere.灯夜：元宵节晚上，家家挂灯。
早共约zǎo gòng yuē
zǎo gòng yuē: already made plans to meet again.早共约：就已经约好下一次相会。
争信zhēng xìn
zhēng xìn: how could one believe. Zhēng here means "how".争信：怎么能相信。“争”是“怎”的意思。
行乐xíng lè
xíng lè: the happy times of the past.行乐：从前一起玩乐的日子。
甚shèn
shèn: why, how is it that.甚：为什么、怎么会。
粉淡衣襟fěn dàn yī jīn
fěn dàn yī jīn: the face powder left on the robe has faded.粉淡衣襟：衣襟上留下的脂粉香已经淡了。
弦索xián suǒ
xián suǒ: instrument strings, meaning music.弦索：琴弦，代指弹奏的乐器。
琼枝璧月qióng zhī bì yuè
qióng zhī bì yuè: jade-like branches and a moon round as a jade disk. A poem at the court of the Chen dynasty ruler said "The jade moon is full every night, the jade trees are new every morning." Here it paints a splendid spring night in the capital.琼枝璧月：玉一样的树枝，玉璧一样圆的月亮。南朝陈后主宫中有诗句“璧月夜夜满，琼树朝朝新”，这里借来写京城繁华的春夜。
春如昨chūn rú zuó
chūn rú zuó: spring is just as it was before.春如昨：春天还和从前一样。
怅chàng
chàng: sad and lost.怅：惆怅，失落。
华表huá biǎo
huá biǎo: a tall stone pillar in front of a palace, city gate or tomb.华表：立在宫殿、城门或坟墓前的大石柱。
双鹤shuāng hè
shuāng hè: two cranes. Legend says Ding Lingwei became an immortal, turned into a crane, flew home and landed on the pillar at the city gate, sighing that the city was the same but the people had changed. Here it means the two lovers can never return together.双鹤：传说丁令威学道成仙，化成一只鹤飞回家乡，落在城门的华表上，感叹城还在、人已经不同了。这里说两人分别后，再也不能一起回到旧地。
除是chú shì
chú shì: unless, except.除是：除非是。
暂忘却zàn wàng què
zàn wàng què: forget for a while.暂忘却：暂时忘掉。
03

How to read it读音

朝zhāo
zhāo yǔ: morning rain. Here the character means "morning" and is read zhāo, not cháo.朝雨：早上的雨，“朝”是早晨的意思，读 zhāo，不读 cháo。
中zhòng
zhòng jiǔ: sick from too much wine. The character is read zhòng, as in "to be struck by".中酒：喝醉了酒，难受。“中”读 zhòng，像“中暑”的“中”。
著zhuó
mí zhuó: lost in it. The character is read zhuó here, which rhymes with the other line endings in this stanza.迷著：沉迷在里面。“著”同“着”，这里读 zhuó，和本段的“洛、掠、约”押韵。
那nǎ
nǎ huí: how could they ever come back. Here the character means "how, which" and is read nǎ.那回：哪里还会回来。“那”在这里同“哪”，读 nǎ。
04

Background创作背景

As a young man, Zhang Yuangan lived in Bianjing, the Northern Song capital, and had a lively, happy life. In 1127 the Jin army took Bianjing and the Northern Song fell. This is known as the Jingkang Incident. He later went south and spent his life moving from place to place. This lyric is about spring sorrow. It recalls days in the capital spent with a loved one. One view says that on the surface it is about love, but it also carries longing for the lost old capital. The exact date is not known.

张元干年轻时住在北宋京城汴京，过着热闹快活的日子。1127年，金兵攻破汴京，北宋灭亡，史称“靖康之变”。他后来到了南方，一直漂泊。这首词写春天的愁闷，回忆旧日在京城和心上人一起游玩的情景。一说它表面写男女相思，其实也寄托着对失去的故都的怀念。具体写作时间不详。

05

The poet作者

Zhang Yuangan (1091–c. 1161), courtesy name Zhongzong, came from Yongfu in Fujian and lived as the Northern Song gave way to the Southern Song. After the north fell, he firmly called for fighting the Jin and opposed making peace. He once wrote a farewell lyric for Hu Quan, a minister banished for wanting war, and was struck from the rolls of officials for it. His early lyrics are soft and graceful; his later ones often mourn his country's suffering with passion.

张元干（1091—约1161），字仲宗，号芦川居士，福建永福人，两宋之际的词人。北宋灭亡后，他坚决主张抗击金兵，反对求和，曾写词送别被贬的主战大臣胡铨，因此被朝廷削去官籍。他早年的词写得柔美，后来多写国家的苦难，悲壮激昂。

06

The mood意境

The lyric has three parts. It walks from the present back to the past, then returns to the present. The first part is the spring in front of him: the rain has stopped, willows sway, peonies bloom, but the east wind knocks down the tender buds. He cannot be bothered to light incense, he is sick from wine, and though spring is lovely he has no spirit for it. The second part leaps back to the capital of his youth: riding horses, sharing a carriage, walking hand in hand in the gardens, a date at the Lantern Festival. So lively. Then the line "How could I have believed I would end up drifting" breaks the sweet dream. The third part comes back to his loneliness now. The scent on his robe has faded, the music has stopped. Spring is the same as before, but he can never go back. At the end he says only being drunk lets him forget for a while. The happier the memories, the sadder his loneliness feels.

这首词分三段，像从“现在”走回“过去”，再回到“现在”。第一段是眼前的春天：雨停了，柳树摇着，芍药开着，可东风把嫩花苞吹落了。词人懒得点香，喝醉了难受，春天虽美，他却提不起精神。第二段一下子回到年轻时的京城：骑马、坐车、手拉手逛园子、元宵节约会，那么热闹。“又争信漂泊”一句，把美梦打断了。第三段回到眼前的寂寞：衣上的香淡了，琴声也断了，春天和从前一样，人却回不去了。最后说，只有喝醉，才能暂时忘掉。越是写当年的快乐，现在的孤单就越让人心酸。
