cháng长hèn恨gē歌

bái白jū居yì易Bai Juyi · Tang唐

The Song of Everlasting Sorrow

 

hàn汉huáng皇zhòng重sè色sī思qīng倾guó国

yù御yǔ宇duō多nián年qiú求bù不dé得

yáng杨jiā家yǒu有nǚ女chū初zhǎng长chéng成

yǎng养zài在shēn深guī闺rén人wèi未shí识

tiān天shēng生lì丽zhì质nán难zì自qì弃

yì一zhāo朝xuǎn选zài在jūn君wáng王cè侧

huí回móu眸yí一xiào笑bǎi百mèi媚shēng生

liù六gōng宫fěn粉dài黛wú无yán颜sè色

chūn春hán寒cì赐yù浴huá华qīng清chí池

wēn温quán泉shuǐ水huá滑xǐ洗níng凝zhī脂

shì侍ér儿fú扶qǐ起jiāo娇wú无lì力

shǐ始shì是xīn新chéng承ēn恩zé泽shí时

yún云bìn鬓huā花yán颜jīn金bù步yáo摇

fú芙róng蓉zhàng帐nuǎn暖dù度chūn春xiāo宵

chūn春xiāo宵kǔ苦duǎn短rì日gāo高qǐ起

cóng从cǐ此jūn君wáng王bù不zǎo早cháo朝

chéng承huān欢shì侍yàn宴wú无xián闲xiá暇

chūn春cóng从chūn春yóu游yè夜zhuān专yè夜

hòu后gōng宫jiā佳lì丽sān三qiān千rén人

sān三qiān千chǒng宠ài爱zài在yì一shēn身

jīn金wū屋zhuāng妆chéng成jiāo娇shì侍yè夜

yù玉lóu楼yàn宴bà罢zuì醉hé和chūn春

zǐ姊mèi妹dì弟xiōng兄jiē皆liè列tǔ土

kě可lián怜guāng光cǎi彩shēng生mén门hù户

suì遂lìng令tiān天xià下fù父mǔ母xīn心

bú不zhòng重shēng生nán男zhòng重shēng生nǚ女

lí骊gōng宫gāo高chù处rù入qīng青yún云

xiān仙yuè乐fēng风piāo飘chù处chù处wén闻

huǎn缓gē歌màn慢wǔ舞níng凝sī丝zhú竹

jìn尽rì日jūn君wáng王kàn看bù不zú足

yú渔yáng阳pí鼙gǔ鼓dòng动dì地lái来

jīng惊pò破ní霓cháng裳yǔ羽yī衣qǔ曲

jiǔ九chóng重chéng城què阙yān烟chén尘shēng生

qiān千shèng乘wàn万qí骑xī西nán南xíng行

cuì翠huá华yáo摇yáo摇xíng行fù复zhǐ止

xī西chū出dū都mén门bǎi百yú余lǐ里

liù六jūn军bù不fā发wú无nài奈hé何

wǎn宛zhuǎn转é蛾méi眉mǎ马qián前sǐ死

huā花diàn钿wěi委dì地wú无rén人shōu收

cuì翠qiáo翘jīn金què雀yù玉sāo搔tóu头

jūn君wáng王yǎn掩miàn面jiù救bù不dé得

huí回kàn看xuè血lèi泪xiāng相hé和liú流

huáng黄āi埃sàn散màn漫fēng风xiāo萧suǒ索

yún云zhàn栈yíng萦yū纡dēng登jiàn剑gé阁

é峨méi嵋shān山xià下shǎo少rén人xíng行

jīng旌qí旗wú无guāng光rì日sè色bó薄

shǔ蜀jiāng江shuǐ水bì碧shǔ蜀shān山qīng青

shèng圣zhǔ主zhāo朝zhāo朝mù暮mù暮qíng情

xíng行gōng宫jiàn见yuè月shāng伤xīn心sè色

yè夜yǔ雨wén闻líng铃cháng肠duàn断shēng声

tiān天xuán旋rì日zhuǎn转huí回lóng龙yù驭

dào到cǐ此chóu踌chú躇bù不néng能qù去

mǎ马wéi嵬pō坡xià下ní泥tǔ土zhōng中

bú不jiàn见yù玉yán颜kōng空sǐ死chù处

jūn君chén臣xiāng相gù顾jìn尽zhān沾yī衣

dōng东wàng望dū都mén门xìn信mǎ马guī归

guī归lái来chí池yuàn苑jiē皆yī依jiù旧

tài太yè液fú芙róng蓉wèi未yāng央liǔ柳

fú芙róng蓉rú如miàn面liǔ柳rú如méi眉

duì对cǐ此rú如hé何bú不lèi泪chuí垂

chūn春fēng风táo桃lǐ李huā花kāi开yè夜

qiū秋yǔ雨wú梧tóng桐yè叶luò落shí时

xī西gōng宫nán南nèi内duō多qiū秋cǎo草

luò落yè叶mǎn满jiē阶hóng红bù不sǎo扫

lí梨yuán园dì弟zǐ子bái白fà发xīn新

jiāo椒fáng房ā阿jiàn监qīng青é娥lǎo老

xī夕diàn殿yíng萤fēi飞sī思qiǎo悄rán然

gū孤dēng灯tiǎo挑jìn尽wèi未chéng成mián眠

chí迟chí迟zhōng钟gǔ鼓chū初cháng长yè夜

gěng耿gěng耿xīng星hé河yù欲shǔ曙tiān天

yuān鸳yāng鸯wǎ瓦lěng冷shuāng霜huá华zhòng重

fěi翡cuì翠qīn衾hán寒shuí谁yǔ与gòng共

yōu悠yōu悠shēng生sǐ死bié别jīng经nián年

hún魂pò魄bù不céng曾lái来rù入mèng梦

lín临qióng邛dào道shì士hóng鸿dū都kè客

néng能yǐ以jīng精chéng诚zhì致hún魂pò魄

wèi为gǎn感jūn君wáng王zhǎn辗zhuǎn转sī思

suì遂jiāo教fāng方shì士yīn殷qín勤mì觅

pái排kōng空yù驭qì气bēn奔rú如diàn电

shēng升tiān天rù入dì地qiú求zhī之biàn遍

shàng上qióng穷bì碧luò落xià下huáng黄quán泉

liǎng两chù处máng茫máng茫jiē皆bú不jiàn见

hū忽wén闻hǎi海shàng上yǒu有xiān仙shān山

shān山zài在xū虚wú无piāo缥miǎo缈jiān间

lóu楼gé阁líng玲lóng珑wǔ五yún云qǐ起

qí其zhōng中chuò绰yuē约duō多xiān仙zǐ子

zhōng中yǒu有yì一rén人zì字tài太zhēn真

xuě雪fū肤huā花mào貌cēn参cī差shì是

jīn金què阙xī西xiāng厢kòu叩yù玉jiōng扃

zhuǎn转jiāo教xiǎo小yù玉bào报shuāng双chéng成

wén闻dào道hàn汉jiā家tiān天zǐ子shǐ使

jiǔ九huá华zhàng帐lǐ里mèng梦hún魂jīng惊

lǎn揽yī衣tuī推zhěn枕qǐ起pái徘huái徊

zhū珠bó箔yín银píng屏lǐ逦yǐ迤kāi开

yún云bìn鬓bàn半piān偏xīn新shuì睡jué觉

huā花guān冠bù不zhěng整xià下táng堂lái来

fēng风chuī吹xiān仙mèi袂piāo飘yáo飖jǔ举

yóu犹sì似ní霓cháng裳yǔ羽yī衣wǔ舞

yù玉róng容jì寂mò寞lèi泪lán阑gān干

lí梨huā花yì一zhī枝chūn春dài带yǔ雨

hán含qíng情níng凝dì睇xiè谢jūn君wáng王

yì一bié别yīn音róng容liǎng两miǎo渺máng茫

zhāo昭yáng阳diàn殿lǐ里ēn恩ài爱jué绝

péng蓬lái莱gōng宫zhōng中rì日yuè月cháng长

huí回tóu头xià下wàng望rén人huán寰chù处

bú不jiàn见cháng长ān安jiàn见chén尘wù雾

wéi唯jiāng将jiù旧wù物biǎo表shēn深qíng情

diàn钿hé合jīn金chāi钗jì寄jiāng将qù去

chāi钗liú留yì一gǔ股hé合yí一shàn扇

chāi钗bò擘huáng黄jīn金hé合fēn分diàn钿

dàn但jiāo教xīn心sì似jīn金diàn钿jiān坚

tiān天shàng上rén人jiān间huì会xiāng相jiàn见

lín临bié别yīn殷qín勤chóng重jì寄cí词

cí词zhōng中yǒu有shì誓liǎng两xīn心zhī知

qī七yuè月qī七rì日cháng长shēng生diàn殿

yè夜bàn半wú无rén人sī私yǔ语shí时

zài在tiān天yuàn愿zuò作bǐ比yì翼niǎo鸟

zài在dì地yuàn愿wéi为lián连lǐ理zhī枝

tiān天cháng长dì地jiǔ久yǒu有shí时jìn尽

cǐ此hèn恨mián绵mián绵wú无jué绝qī期

This page uses the commonly taught text. The database text differs in a few places: it has older or variant forms of several characters (for pí gǔ, jiàn gé, lí, zhǎn zhuǎn, biàn, pái huái, jì mò and mián mián), and two real wording differences: "West Palace, South Garden" where the taught text has "West Palace, South Palace" (nán nèi), and "palace leaves fill the steps" where the taught text has "fallen leaves fill the steps" (luò yè). Most of these are just old and new ways of writing the same word. Some editions also have tiān xuán dì zhuǎn for tiān xuán rì zhuǎn, and piāo piāo for piāo yáo.

本文用通行的写法。数据库原文有几处不同：“鞞鼓”作“鼙鼓”，“劒阁”作“剑阁”，“西宫南苑”作“西宫南内”，“宫叶满阶红不埽”作“落叶满阶红不扫”，“棃”作“梨”，“展转”作“辗转”，“求之徧”作“求之遍”，“裴回”作“徘徊”，“寂莫”作“寂寞”，“緜緜”作“绵绵”。其中多数只是古今字形不同，意思一样。另外，有的版本“天旋日转”作“天旋地转”，“飘飖”作“飘飘”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 汉皇重色思倾国The Han emperor (really the Tang emperor Xuanzong) loved beauty and longed for a woman lovely beyond compare.汉朝的皇帝（其实是唐玄宗）喜爱美色，一心想找一位绝世美人。
  2. 御宇多年求不得He ruled the land for many years but could not find her.他做了多年皇帝，一直没有找到。
  3. 杨家有女初长成The Yang family had a daughter who had just grown up.杨家有一个女儿，刚刚长大成人。
  4. 养在深闺人未识She lived in the inner rooms, and no outsider knew her.她住在深深的闺房里，外人都不认识她。
  5. 天生丽质难自弃Her beauty was a gift from heaven; it could not stay hidden.她天生美丽，这样的美貌不会一直被埋没。
  6. 一朝选在君王侧One day she was chosen to be at the emperor's side.有一天，她被选进宫，来到皇帝身边。
  7. 回眸一笑百媚生When she turned and smiled, a hundred charms came alive.她回头一笑，千娇百媚。
  8. 六宫粉黛无颜色All the made-up beauties of the palace looked dull beside her.后宫里化着妆的美人，和她一比都失去了光彩。
  9. 春寒赐浴华清池In the chill of early spring, the emperor let her bathe in the Huaqing Pool.早春还有些冷，皇帝让她到华清池洗温泉。
  10. 温泉水滑洗凝脂The smooth warm spring water washed her skin, white and soft as cream.滑润的温泉水，洗着她洁白细嫩的皮肤。
  11. 侍儿扶起娇无力Maids helped her up; she was so delicate she seemed to have no strength.宫女扶她起来，她娇柔得没有一点力气。
  12. 始是新承恩泽时This was when she first won the emperor's favor.这正是她刚开始得到皇帝宠爱的时候。
  13. 云鬓花颜金步摇Hair like clouds, a face like a flower, a gold hairpin swaying as she moved.她鬓发如云，容颜如花，头上的金步摇轻轻摇动。
  14. 芙蓉帐暖度春宵Inside the warm lotus curtains, they passed the spring nights together.在温暖的芙蓉帐里，她陪皇帝度过春天的夜晚。
  15. 春宵苦短日高起The spring nights felt far too short; they rose only when the sun was high.只嫌春夜太短，太阳升得老高才起床。
  16. 从此君王不早朝From then on, the emperor stopped holding court in the early morning.从此皇帝不再一早上朝处理国事。
  17. 承欢侍宴无闲暇Pleasing him and serving at his feasts, she never had a free moment.她讨皇帝欢心，陪皇帝饮宴，没有一点空闲。
  18. 春从春游夜专夜In spring she joined him on every outing; every night she alone was with him.春天陪他游春，夜夜都由她一个人陪伴。
  19. 后宫佳丽三千人There were three thousand beauties in the palace,后宫的美女有三千人，
  20. 三千宠爱在一身but the love meant for three thousand fell on her alone.可是三千人的宠爱都集中在她一个人身上。
  21. 金屋妆成娇侍夜Made up in a golden chamber, she kept him charming company through the night.在华丽的宫室里打扮好，娇媚地陪皇帝过夜。
  22. 玉楼宴罢醉和春When the feast in the jade tower ended, she was tipsy and warm as spring.玉楼上的宴会散了，她带着醉意，和春天一样温柔。
  23. 姊妹弟兄皆列土Her sisters and brothers were all given titles and lands.她的姐妹兄弟都封了爵位，得了封地。
  24. 可怜光彩生门户How enviable it was: glory shone from the Yang family gate.杨家门庭光彩照人，真让人羡慕。
  25. 遂令天下父母心And so the hearts of parents everywhere changed:于是天下做父母的，心思都变了，
  26. 不重生男重生女they no longer prized sons, but prized daughters.不再看重生男孩，反而看重生女孩。
  27. 骊宫高处入青云The palace on Mount Li rose high into the clouds,骊山上的宫殿高高耸入云霄，
  28. 仙乐风飘处处闻and music fit for immortals drifted on the wind, heard everywhere.仙乐一样美妙的音乐随风飘送，到处都听得见。
  29. 缓歌慢舞凝丝竹Slow songs and gentle dances moved to the lingering sound of strings and flutes,舒缓的歌，柔美的舞，配着悠长的乐声，
  30. 尽日君王看不足and the emperor watched all day long and never had enough.皇帝整天看着，怎么也看不够。
  31. 渔阳鼙鼓动地来Then the war drums of Yuyang came, shaking the earth,忽然，渔阳的战鼓震天动地地响起来，
  32. 惊破霓裳羽衣曲and shattered the Rainbow Skirt and Feather Coat dance.打断了宫中的霓裳羽衣曲。
  33. 九重城阙烟尘生Smoke and dust rose over the many-gated capital.京城里烟尘滚滚，战火烧来了。
  34. 千乘万骑西南行Thousands of chariots and horsemen fled to the southwest.皇帝带着成千上万的车马向西南逃去。
  35. 翠华摇摇行复止The emperor's kingfisher banner swayed; the column moved and stopped, moved and stopped.皇帝的翠羽旗摇摇晃晃，队伍走走停停。
  36. 西出都门百余里They went west, a hundred li and more beyond the city gate.向西走出京城一百多里。
  37. 六军不发无奈何The army would march no farther, and the emperor was helpless.护驾的军队不肯再走，皇帝也没有办法，
  38. 宛转蛾眉马前死The lovely lady with moth-wing brows died in agony before the horses.美丽的杨贵妃在军马前痛苦地死去。
  39. 花钿委地无人收Her flower ornaments fell to the ground, and no one picked them up,她的花钿掉在地上，没有人去收拾，
  40. 翠翘金雀玉搔头nor the kingfisher plumes, the golden-bird pins, the jade hairpins.还有翠翘、金雀钗和玉簪，也都散落在地。
  41. 君王掩面救不得The emperor covered his face; he could not save her.皇帝捂着脸，救不了她。
  42. 回看血泪相和流Looking back, he wept tears mixed with blood.回头再看，眼泪和血一起流下来。
  43. 黄埃散漫风萧索Yellow dust spread everywhere, and the wind blew cold and bleak.黄色的尘土四处飘散，风冷冷清清地吹着。
  44. 云栈萦纡登剑阁Along winding plank roads high in the clouds, they climbed to Jiange Pass.沿着高入云端、弯弯曲曲的栈道，登上剑阁。
  45. 峨嵋山下少人行Below Mount Emei, few people passed.峨眉山下，很少有人走过。
  46. 旌旗无光日色薄The banners lost their color, and the sunlight was thin and pale.旗子失去了光彩，阳光也暗淡无力。
  47. 蜀江水碧蜀山青The rivers of Shu were green-blue, the mountains of Shu were green,蜀地的江水碧绿，蜀地的山青翠，
  48. 圣主朝朝暮暮情and from morning to night the emperor longed for her.皇帝从早到晚都在思念她。
  49. 行宫见月伤心色In his traveling palace, even the moonlight looked heartbroken;在行宫里看见月亮，月色也让人伤心；
  50. 夜雨闻铃肠断声on rainy nights, the sound of bells broke his heart.夜雨里听到铃声，声声让人肝肠寸断。
  51. 天旋日转回龙驭Then the world turned again, and the emperor's carriage headed home.局势大变，唐军收复了京城，皇帝的车驾往回走。
  52. 到此踌躇不能去Reaching that place, he lingered and could not bring himself to leave.走到马嵬坡，他徘徊犹豫，不忍离开。
  53. 马嵬坡下泥土中In the earth below Mawei Slope,在马嵬坡下的泥土里，
  54. 不见玉颜空死处her lovely face was nowhere to be seen; only the place where she died remained.再也见不到她美丽的容颜，只剩下她死去的地方。
  55. 君臣相顾尽沾衣The emperor and his ministers looked at one another, their clothes wet with tears.皇帝和大臣们互相看着，泪水打湿了衣裳。
  56. 东望都门信马归Gazing east toward the capital, they let the horses carry them home.向东望着京城的城门，任由马儿慢慢走回去。
  57. 归来池苑皆依旧Back home, the ponds and gardens were all as before:回到宫中，池塘园林都和从前一样，
  58. 太液芙蓉未央柳the lotuses of Taiye Pool, the willows of Weiyang Palace.太液池的荷花，未央宫的柳树，还是老样子。

Words字词注释

汉皇hàn huáng
hàn huáng: the Han emperor. It really means the Tang emperor Xuanzong. Tang poets often said "Han" when they meant their own Tang dynasty.汉皇：汉朝的皇帝。这里其实是指唐玄宗李隆基。唐朝诗人写本朝的事，常借“汉”来说“唐”。
重色zhòng sè
zhòng sè: to care a lot about beauty, to love beautiful women.重色：看重美色，喜欢美女。
倾国qīng guó
qīng guó: a woman so beautiful she could topple a kingdom; a beauty beyond compare.倾国：美得能让一个国家倾倒的女子，就是绝世美人。
御宇yù yǔ
yù yǔ: to rule the land. Yǔ means the whole world under heaven.御宇：统治天下。宇，天下。
初长成chū zhǎng chéng
chū zhǎng chéng: had just grown up.初长成：刚刚长大成人。
深闺shēn guī
shēn guī: the inner rooms where young women lived, deep inside the house, out of sight.深闺：女子住的内室，在家里很深的地方，外人看不到。
丽质lì zhì
lì zhì: beauty of face and bearing.丽质：美丽的容貌和气质。
难自弃nán zì qì
nán zì qì: could not be left unnoticed; sooner or later it would be found.难自弃：不会被埋没，迟早会被发现。
一朝yì zhāo
yī zhāo: one day.一朝：有一天。
君王侧jūn wáng cè
jūn wáng cè: by the emperor's side.君王侧：皇帝身边。侧，旁边。
回眸huí móu
huí móu: to turn and look back. Móu is the pupil of the eye.回眸：回头看。眸，眼珠。
百媚生bǎi mèi shēng
bǎi mèi shēng: a hundred kinds of charm appear.百媚生：显出各种各样的美。
六宫liù gōng
liù gōng: the six palaces, where the empress and consorts lived; the inner palace.六宫：皇后和妃子们住的地方，就是后宫。
粉黛fěn dài
fěn dài: face powder and eyebrow paint. Here it means the made-up beauties of the palace.粉黛：擦脸的白粉和画眉的黑颜料，这里指后宫里化着妆的美女。
无颜色wú yán sè
wú yán sè: to lose all color; to be outshone.无颜色：失去光彩，被比下去了。
赐浴cì yù
cì yù: the emperor granted her a bath there, a special favor.赐浴：皇帝让她去洗浴。这是一种特别的宠爱。
华清池huá qīng chí
huá qīng chí: the Huaqing hot-spring pool at the foot of Mount Li, in today's Lintong, Xi'an, Shaanxi.华清池：骊山脚下华清宫里的温泉池，在今天陕西西安临潼。
凝脂níng zhī
níng zhī: set fat or cream, white and smooth; used to describe soft, fair skin.凝脂：凝固的油脂，又白又滑，用来形容皮肤白嫩。
侍儿shì ér
shì ér: a maid who waits on someone.侍儿：服侍人的宫女。
娇无力jiāo wú lì
jiāo wú lì: so soft and delicate she has no strength.娇无力：娇柔得没有力气。
承恩泽chéng ēn zé
chéng ēn zé: to receive the emperor's favor. Ēn zé means kindness that falls like rain and dew.承恩泽：得到皇帝的宠爱。恩泽，像雨露一样的恩惠。
云鬓yún bìn
yún bìn: hair at the temples, thick and soft like clouds.云鬓：像云一样又多又蓬松的鬓发。鬓，耳边的头发。
金步摇jīn bù yáo
jīn bù yáo: a gold hairpin hung with beads and jade that swayed with every step.金步摇：一种金首饰，插在头上，上面挂着珠玉，走一步摇一下。

fú róng zhàng: bed curtains embroidered with lotus flowers. Fú róng is the lotus.芙蓉帐：绣着荷花的帐子。芙蓉，荷花。

苦短kǔ duǎn
kǔ duǎn: painfully short.苦短：只恨太短。
早朝zǎo cháo
zǎo cháo: the early-morning court, where the emperor met his ministers to run the country.早朝：皇帝清早上朝，和大臣们商量国家大事。

shì yàn: to keep the emperor company at feasts.侍宴：陪皇帝饮酒吃饭。

xián xiá: free time.闲暇：空闲的时候。

jiā lì: beautiful women.佳丽：美女。

yù lóu: a jade tower, a splendid building.玉楼：华丽的楼阁。

yú yáng: a Tang district around today's Beijing and Tianjin. An Lushan launched his rebellion from this area.渔阳：唐代的一个郡，在今天北京、天津一带。安禄山就是从这一带起兵造反的。

04

Background创作背景

This poem was written in the winter of 806. Bai Juyi was thirty-five and served as a minor county official in Zhouzhi, today's Zhouzhi County near Xi'an, Shaanxi. One day he visited Xianyou Temple with his friends Chen Hong and Wang Zhifu. They talked about the story of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei from fifty years before, and all were deeply moved. Wang Zhifu asked Bai Juyi to turn it into a poem, and Chen Hong wrote a prose account of it. Behind the story is the An Lushan Rebellion. In 755 An Lushan rose in revolt. The next year Xuanzong fled toward Shu, and at Mawei Slope his soldiers forced him to have Yang Guifei put to death.

这首诗写于唐宪宗元和元年（806年）冬天。那年白居易三十五岁，在盩厔（今天陕西西安的周至县）做县尉，是县里的一个小官。有一天，他和朋友陈鸿、王质夫到仙游寺游玩，聊起五十年前唐玄宗和杨贵妃的故事，大家都很感慨。王质夫请白居易把它写成诗，陈鸿又写了一篇文章《长恨歌传》。故事背后是安史之乱：755年安禄山起兵造反，第二年玄宗逃往蜀地，走到马嵬坡时，士兵逼他让杨贵妃死去。

05

The poet作者

Bai Juyi (772–846), also called Letian, was a great Tang poet. He believed poems should care about real life and be easy to understand. Legend says he read each new poem to an old woman who could not read, and changed it until she understood. "The Song of Everlasting Sorrow" and "The Pipa Player" are his two most famous long poems. Even in his lifetime this poem had spread far and wide, and even children could chant it.

白居易（772—846），字乐天，号香山居士，唐代大诗人。他主张诗要关心现实，要写得明白好懂。传说他写完诗会念给不识字的老婆婆听，直到她听懂为止。《长恨歌》和《琵琶行》是他最有名的两首长诗。他还活着的时候，这首诗就已经传遍各地，连小孩子都会吟唱。

06

The mood意境

This is a long story in verse, and it can be read in four parts. Part one, up to the war drums, tells how Yang Guifei came to the palace and won the emperor's whole heart, so that he stopped caring for the country. Part two: war suddenly arrives, the emperor flees toward Shu, and at Mawei Slope he loses her. On the road, the moon and the rain only bring him grief. Part three: back in Chang'an, the lotuses, willows, fallen leaves, lonely lamp and cold quilt all remind him of her, and he cannot sleep at night. Part four is like a fairy tale. A Daoist priest searches heaven and earth and at last finds her on an island of immortals. She weeps like pear blossoms in the rain, sends back keepsakes, and recalls the vow they made on the night of Qixi. Many of these scenes come from the poet's imagination. You can first read it as a moving love story. You can also think about how an emperor who cared only for pleasure brought disaster to his country and to himself. Some readers say the poem is mainly about love, others say it mainly criticizes the emperor, and some say it is both. The last two lines say this sorrow will last even longer than heaven and earth, and they have been famous ever since.

这是一首很长的故事诗，可以分成四段来读。第一段（开头到“惊破霓裳羽衣曲”之前）写杨贵妃进宫，得到皇帝独一无二的宠爱，皇帝也因此不理朝政。第二段写战乱突然到来，皇帝逃往蜀地，在马嵬坡失去了她，一路上看月、听雨都是伤心。第三段写皇帝回到长安，宫里的荷花、柳树、落叶、孤灯、冷被子，处处让他想起她，夜夜睡不着。第四段最像童话：道士上天入地，终于在海上仙山找到她。她哭得像带雨的梨花，送回信物，说出两人在七夕夜里许下的誓言。诗里很多情节是诗人的想象。读这首诗，可以先把它当成一个动人的爱情故事；也可以想一想，一个皇帝只顾玩乐，给国家和自己都带来了多大的悲剧。一说这首诗主要是写爱情，一说主要是讽刺，也有人认为两者都有。最后两句“天长地久有时尽，此恨绵绵无绝期”，把这份遗憾说得比天地还长，成了千古名句。
