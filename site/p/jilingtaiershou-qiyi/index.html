集灵台二首·其一

张祜Zhang Hu · Tang唐

The Jiling Terrace, No. 1

 

rì日guāng光xié斜zhào照jí集líng灵tái台

hóng红shù树huā花yíng迎xiǎo晓lù露kāi开

zuó昨yè夜shàng上huáng皇xīn新shòu授lù箓

tài太zhēn真hán含xiào笑rù入lián帘lái来

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 日光斜照集灵台Slanting sunlight falls on the Jiling Terrace.阳光斜斜地照在集灵台上，
  2. 红树花迎晓露开Red trees open their blossoms to the morning dew.满树红花迎着早晨的露水开放。
  3. 昨夜上皇新授箓Last night the emperor had just given her the Daoist scroll,昨天夜里，皇上刚刚给她授了道箓，
  4. 太真含笑入帘来and Taizhen comes in through the curtain, smiling.太真就含着笑走进帘子里来了。
02

Words字词注释

集灵台jí líng tái
the Jiling Terrace: a place in the Huaqing Palace for worshipping the immortals.集灵台：华清宫里祭祀神仙的地方。
红树hóng shù
hóng shù: trees full of red blossoms.红树：开满红花的树。
晓露xiǎo lù
xiǎo lù: morning dew.晓露：早晨的露水。
上皇shàng huáng
shàng huáng: the emperor; here Emperor Xuanzong.上皇：指唐玄宗。
授箓shòu lù
shòu lù: a Daoist rite. A new believer is given a scroll listing the names of gods, which makes them a Daoist priest or nun. Lù is that scroll.授箓：道教的一种仪式，给入道的人发一份写着神名的文书，表示正式成为道士。箓，就是这种文书。
太真tài zhēn
Taizhen: the Daoist name Yang Yuhuan took as a nun; she later became Lady Yang, the emperor's consort.太真：杨玉环做女道士时的道号，后来她成了杨贵妃。
入帘来rù lián lái
rù lián lái: comes in through the curtain, into the emperor's rooms.入帘来：走进帘子里来，指进入皇帝的寝宫。
03

Background创作背景

Yang Yuhuan was first the wife of Prince Shou, a son of Emperor Xuanzong. The histories say the emperor wanted her for himself. To cover this up, he first had her become a Daoist nun named Taizhen, and some years later made her his consort. This poem is about that. The Jiling Terrace was a place for worshipping the gods in the Huaqing Palace and should be pure and solemn. By placing this secret palace affair there, the poet makes his mockery clear.

杨玉环原来是唐玄宗儿子寿王的妃子。据史书记载，玄宗想把她留在自己身边，先让她出家做女道士，道号“太真”，几年后再封她为贵妃，用这个办法遮掩真相。这首诗就写这件事。集灵台是华清宫里祭神的地方，本该清净庄严，诗人却把它和这段宫中秘事放在一起，讽刺的意味很明显。

04

The poet作者

Zhang Hu was a Tang poet who never held a high post and traveled widely. He wrote many palace poems, poems about life at court. One that begins with home three thousand miles away and twenty years in the deep palace is very famous. His palace poems say little on the surface but carry deep meaning.

张祜，唐代诗人，一生没有做什么大官，常在各地漫游。他写了很多宫词，就是写宫廷生活的诗，有一首“故国三千里，深宫二十年”很有名。他写宫中故事，常常话不多说，却意味深长。

05

The mood意境

The first two lines are lovely: sunlight slants across the terrace of the gods, and red flowers open with dew, like a fairyland. The last two lines tell something shameful. Last night the emperor made her a nun, and today she walks smiling through his curtain. Becoming a nun should mean leaving the world for a pure life, but here it is a step into the emperor's rooms. The poet adds no comment. He just sets the holy place beside the deed, and readers see what he is mocking.

前两句写得很美：阳光斜照在祭神的高台上，红花带着露水开放，好像一片仙境。后两句却讲了一件不光彩的事：昨夜皇帝给她授了道箓，让她“出家”，今天她就含笑走进了皇帝的帘子里。本来做道士是要离开尘世、清心修行的，这里却成了进宫的台阶。诗人什么评论都不加，只把神圣的地方和这件事摆在一起，读者自然明白他在讽刺什么。
