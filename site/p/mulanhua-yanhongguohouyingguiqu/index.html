mù木lán兰huā花·yàn燕hóng鸿guò过hòu后yīng莺guī归qù去

yàn晏shū殊Yan Shu · Song宋

Magnolia Blossoms: After the Swallows and Geese Have Passed

 

yàn燕hóng鸿guò过hòu后yīng莺guī归qù去

xì细suàn算fú浮shēng生qiān千wàn万xù绪

cháng长yú于chūn春mèng梦jǐ几duō多shí时

sàn散sì似qiū秋yún云wú无mì觅chù处

wén闻qín琴jiě解pèi佩shén神xiān仙lǚ侣

wǎn挽duàn断luó罗yī衣liú留bú不zhù住

quàn劝jūn君mò莫zuò作dú独xǐng醒rén人

làn烂zuì醉huā花jiān间yīng应yǒu有shù数

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 燕鸿过后莺归去The swallows and geese have passed, and the orioles have flown away.燕子和大雁都飞过去了，黄莺也飞走了，
  2. 细算浮生千万绪When I count up this floating life, it holds a thousand tangled threads.仔细算一算这短短一生，心事千头万绪。
  3. 长于春梦几多时How much longer than a spring dream do good times last?美好的时光比春梦能长多少？
  4. 散似秋云无觅处They scatter like autumn clouds, and cannot be found again.散去时像秋云一样，再也找不到。
  5. 闻琴解佩神仙侣Lovers as fine as immortals, like the girl who heard the qin, like the goddesses who gave their pendants,像卓文君、像汉水仙女那样神仙般的伴侣，
  6. 挽断罗衣留不住even if you pull their silk sleeves until they tear, you cannot make them stay.就算拉断了丝绸衣裳，也留不住。
  7. 劝君莫作独醒人I urge you, don't be the only one who stays sober.劝你不要做那个独自清醒的人，
  8. 烂醉花间应有数Getting drunk among the flowers makes good sense.在花丛中喝个烂醉，也是有道理的。
02

Words字词注释

莺归去yīng guī qù
yīng guī qù: even the orioles fly away; spring is over.莺归去：黄莺也飞走了，表示春天过去了。
长于春梦几多时cháng yú chūn mèng jǐ duō shí
cháng yú chūn mèng jǐ duō shí: how much longer than a spring dream is life? Jǐ duō shí means how long.长于春梦几多时：人生比春天的梦能长多少呢？几多时，多少时间。
散似秋云sàn sì qiū yún
sàn sì qiū yún: scattering as quickly as autumn clouds.散似秋云：散得像秋天的云一样快。
无觅处wú mì chù
wú mì chù: nowhere to be found. Mì means to look for.无觅处：没有地方可以找到。觅，找。
燕鸿yàn hóng
yàn hóng: swallows and wild geese. Swallows come in spring and leave in autumn; geese come in autumn and leave in spring. One comes as the other goes, so they seldom meet.燕鸿：燕子和大雁。燕子春天来、秋天走，大雁秋天来、春天走，一来一去，很难相见。
浮生fú shēng
fú shēng: a human life. People of old felt life drifts like something floating, short and unsure.浮生：人的一生。古人觉得人生像漂浮不定的东西，短暂又靠不住。
千万绪qiān wàn xù
qiān wàn xù: a thousand threads; more thoughts than one can sort out.千万绪：千头万绪，心事多得理不清。
闻琴wén qín
wén qín: in the Han dynasty, Zhuo Wenjun heard Sima Xiangru play the qin zither, fell in love and ran away with him.闻琴：汉代卓文君听了司马相如弹琴，爱上了他，跟他一起走了。
解佩jiě pèi
jiě pèi: legend says Zheng Jiaofu met two goddesses by the Han River. They untied the jade pendants they wore and gave them to him, then vanished in a moment.解佩：传说郑交甫在汉水边遇到两位仙女，仙女解下身上的玉佩送给他，转眼就不见了。
神仙侣shén xiān lǚ
shén xiān lǚ: a partner as lovely as an immortal.神仙侣：神仙一样美好的伴侣。
挽断罗衣wǎn duàn luó yī
wǎn duàn luó yī: pulling at a silk robe until it tears. Wǎn means to pull.挽断罗衣：拉着丝绸衣服，拉断了也不放手。挽，拉。
独醒人dú xǐng rén
dú xǐng rén: the one person who stays sober when all others are drunk. It comes from Qu Yuan's words: everyone is drunk, I alone am sober.独醒人：大家都醉了，只有自己清醒的人。这话出自屈原“众人皆醉我独醒”。
烂醉làn zuì
làn zuì: very drunk.烂醉：醉得很厉害。
应有数yīng yǒu shù
yīng yǒu shù: one reading: there is good sense in it; another: such chances are numbered.应有数：一说是“应该是有道理的”；一说是“这样的机会也是有限的”。
03

Background创作背景

We do not know when or why this lyric was written. From its words, Yan Shu is looking back on lovely people and times he once knew. He sighs that they could not be kept, like a spring dream or autumn clouds, and so he urges people to enjoy life while they can. There is a nearly identical lyric to the tune Yu Lou Chun under the name of Ouyang Xiu, with only a few words different. The lyric spread widely, and accounts of its author differ.

这首词写的时间和缘由都不清楚。从词意看，是晏殊回想过去遇到的美好的人和事，感叹它们像春梦秋云一样留不住，于是劝人及时行乐。词集中另有一首几乎一样的《玉楼春》，署名欧阳修，只有几个字不同，可见这首词流传很广，作者也有不同说法。

04

The poet作者

Yan Shu (991–1055) was a Northern Song lyric poet. He was a child prodigy, sent to the court at fourteen, and later became chief minister. His life was calm and comfortable. His lyrics are gentle and refined, and under their calm there is often sorrow at time slipping away. He helped the careers of Ouyang Xiu, Fan Zhongyan and others.

晏殊（991—1055），字同叔，北宋词人。他从小就是“神童”，十四岁被推荐进朝廷，后来做到宰相。他一生生活安稳富贵，词写得温婉雅致，常常在平静中透出对时光流逝的感伤。欧阳修、范仲淹等人都受过他的提拔。

05

The mood意境

The first line makes us feel a season leaving: swallows, geese and orioles fly off one by one, and spring goes with them. Then the poet counts up life. It is full of tangled threads, yet good times are as short as a spring dream and scatter like autumn clouds. The second half uses two stories, Zhuo Wenjun and the river goddesses, people lovely and hard to keep. Even if you tear their sleeves, they will not stay. At last he says: don't be the only one sober and sad; get drunk among the flowers. On the surface he is urging us to drink; underneath is a deep helplessness.

开头一句就让人感到季节的离去：燕子、大雁、黄莺一个个飞走了，春天也走了。接着词人算起人生的账：千头万绪，好时光却像春梦一样短，像秋云一样散。下片用了两个故事，卓文君和汉水仙女，都是美好又难留的人，就算拉断了衣服也留不住。最后他劝人：别做那个独自清醒、独自难过的人，在花间醉一场吧。表面上是劝人喝酒，底下其实是很深的无奈。
