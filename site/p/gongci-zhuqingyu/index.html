Palace Poem

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 寂寂花时闭院门It is the season of flowers, yet the courtyard gate is shut and all is silent.百花盛开的春天，院门却紧紧关着，冷冷清清。
  2. 美人相并立琼轩Lovely palace women stand side by side on the fine veranda.几个美丽的宫女并排站在华美的长廊上。
  3. 含情欲说宫中事Full of feeling, they want to talk about life in the palace,她们满怀心事，想说说宫里的事情，
  4. 鹦鹉前头不敢言but with the parrot right in front of them, no one dares to say a word.可是鹦鹉就在面前，谁也不敢开口。
02

Words字词注释

寂寂jì jì
jì jì: silent and lonely.寂寂：冷冷清清，没有声音。
花时huā shí
huā shí: the time of flowers, that is, spring.花时：花开的季节，指春天。
闭院门bì yuàn mén
bì yuàn mén: the courtyard gate is shut.闭院门：院子的门关着。
相并xiāng bìng
xiāng bìng: side by side.相并：一个挨着一个，并排。
琼轩qióng xuān
qióng xuān: a fine veranda. Qióng is a beautiful jade, used here to mean splendid.琼轩：华美的长廊或带窗的小屋。琼，美玉，形容华美。
含情hán qíng
hán qíng: holding feelings inside.含情：心里藏着感情、心事。
欲说yù shuō
yù shuō: wanting to speak.欲说：想要说。
鹦鹉yīng wǔ
yīng wǔ: a parrot, a bird that can copy human speech.鹦鹉：一种会学人说话的鸟。
前头qián tóu
qián tóu: in front of.前头：前面。
03

Background创作背景

This is another palace lament, about the unfree and unhappy lives of palace women. Once a girl entered the palace, she often could never leave. We do not know the year it was written. The title is sometimes given as "Song from the Palace". The poet does not show the women crying or complaining. He catches one small moment: they want to talk, but they are afraid the parrot will repeat their words.

这也是一首宫怨诗，写皇宫里宫女们不自由、不快乐的生活。宫女一旦进宫，往往一辈子都出不去。这首诗写于哪一年不清楚。诗题一作《宫中词》。诗人没有写宫女怎么哭、怎么诉苦，只抓住了一个小小的场面：她们想说话，却怕被鹦鹉学了去。

04

The poet作者

Zhu Qingyu came from Yuezhou, today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. He passed the highest imperial exam in 826. The poet Zhang Ji thought highly of him. His most famous poem was written to Zhang Ji before the exam. In it, a new bride asks if her makeup looks right, which is his way of asking whether his poems are good enough.

朱庆馀（也写作朱庆余），名可久，以字行，越州（今浙江绍兴）人，唐敬宗宝历二年（826年）考中进士。他得到诗人张籍的赏识，最有名的是考试前写给张籍的《近试上张水部》，用新娘问妆比喻自己的诗写得好不好。

05

The mood意境

The first line already holds a contrast. Outside it is spring and flowers are blooming, a time that should be lively. But the gate is shut and all is quiet. The lovelier the spring, the lonelier the women locked inside. A few of them stand together on the veranda. They finally have a moment together and have so much to say. But a parrot hangs nearby. Parrots copy what they hear. If it repeated their complaints to someone, they could be in great trouble. So no one dares to speak. They cannot even share their true feelings. That alone tells us how heavy palace life was. The last line is clever, and also very sad.

第一句就很矛盾：外面是花开的春天，本该热闹，院门却关着，静悄悄的。春天越美，关在里面的人越寂寞。几个宫女站在长廊上，好不容易聚在一起，心里有好多话想说。可是旁边挂着一只鹦鹉。鹦鹉会学舌，万一把她们的抱怨学给别人听，就会惹来大祸。于是谁也不敢开口。连说句心里话都不敢，宫里的日子有多压抑，就不用再多说了。最后一句很有意思，也很让人心酸。
