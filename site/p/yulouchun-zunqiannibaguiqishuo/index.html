Song lyric · ci (song lyric)宋词 · 词

yù玉lóu楼chūn春·zūn尊qián前nǐ拟bǎ把guī归qī期shuō说

ōu欧yáng阳xiū修Ouyang Xiu · Song宋

Jade Tower Spring: Over the Wine I Meant to Name the Day I Leave

 

zūn尊qián前nǐ拟bǎ把guī归qī期shuō说

wèi未yǔ语chūn春róng容xiān先cǎn惨yè咽

rén人shēng生zì自shì是yǒu有qíng情chī痴

cǐ此hèn恨bù不guān关fēng风yǔ与yuè月

lí离gē歌qiě且mò莫fān翻xīn新què阕

yì一qǔ曲néng能jiào教cháng肠cùn寸jié结

zhí直xū须kàn看jìn尽luò洛chéng城huā花

shǐ始gòng共chūn春fēng风róng容yì易bié别

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 尊前拟把归期说At the feast I was about to tell the day I would leave.在酒宴上，我正打算说出离开的日子，
  2. 未语春容先惨咽Before I spoke, the lovely woman was already choking with sobs.还没开口，美人就已经伤心得哽咽了。
  3. 人生自是有情痴People are simply born full of feeling, almost foolish with love.人生下来就是重感情、痴情的，
  4. 此恨不关风与月This pain of parting has nothing to do with the wind or the moon.这种离别的痛苦，和清风明月没有关系。
  5. 离歌且莫翻新阕For now, do not write new words for the farewell song.离别的歌暂且不要再填新词了，
  6. 一曲能教肠寸结One song alone already ties our insides into knots.只唱一首，就已经让人肝肠寸断。
  7. 直须看尽洛城花We must first see every flower in Luoyang,一定要把洛阳城的花看个够，
  8. 始共春风容易别and only then can we say goodbye to the spring wind more easily.才能轻松一些地和春风告别。
02

Words字词注释

尊前zūn qián
zūn qián: before the wine cup, that is, at the feast. Zūn is an old word for a wine cup.尊前：酒杯前，指在酒宴上。尊，同“樽”，酒杯。
拟nǐ
nǐ: to plan to.拟：打算。
归期guī qī
guī qī: the day of going back, the day of leaving.归期：回去、离开的日子。
春容chūn róng
chūn róng: a face as lovely as spring; it means the beautiful woman at the feast.春容：像春天一样美的容貌，指在座的美人。
惨咽cǎn yè
cǎn yè: so sad that she chokes with sobs and cannot speak.惨咽：伤心得哽咽，说不出话。
有情痴yǒu qíng chī
yǒu qíng chī: so full of feeling that one is almost foolish with it.有情痴：重感情到了痴迷的地步。
恨hèn
hèn: the pain of parting, regret.恨：遗憾、离别的痛苦。
不关风与月bù guān fēng yǔ yuè
bù guān fēng yǔ yuè: has nothing to do with the wind and the moon, that is, with the scenery.不关风与月：和清风明月这些景物无关。
离歌lí gē
lí gē: a song sung at a farewell.离歌：送别时唱的歌。
且莫qiě mò
qiě mò: for now, do not.且莫：暂且不要。
翻新阕fān xīn què
fān xīn què: to set new words to an old tune. Què means a song, or one part of a song.翻新阕：按旧曲调填写新的歌词。阕，一首歌或词的一段。
教肠寸结jiào cháng cùn jié
jiào cháng cùn jié: makes one's insides tie up in knots inch by inch, meaning terrible sadness.教肠寸结：让人的肠子一寸一寸地打结，形容极其悲伤。
直须zhí xū
zhí xū: must, should surely.直须：一定要。
洛城花luò chéng huā
luò chéng huā: the flowers of Luoyang, a city famous for its peonies.洛城花：洛阳城的花，洛阳的牡丹最有名。
始shǐ
shǐ: only then.始：才。
容易róng yì
róng yì: lightly, without too much pain.容易：轻松、不那么难过。
03

How to read it读音

咽yè
Here the character means to choke with sobs, so it is read yè, not yàn or yān.“惨咽”的“咽”是哭得说不出话，读 yè，不读 yàn 或 yān。
曲qǔ
Here the character means a song, so it is read qǔ.“一曲”指一首歌，“曲”读 qǔ。
教jiào
Here the character means to make, and is usually read jiào today. The meter wants a level tone here, so some readers say jiāo.“能教”的“教”是“使、让”的意思，现在一般读 jiào。按词的格律这里要用平声，所以也有人读 jiāo。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually dated to 1034. Ouyang Xiu had worked in Luoyang for three years, his term was over, and he had to leave. Luoyang was where he and his friends drank, enjoyed flowers and wrote poems together, and he loved it dearly. He wrote this lyric at a farewell feast. Some think it was written at a different farewell, and accounts differ on the details.

一般认为，这首词写于宋仁宗景祐元年（1034年）。那时欧阳修在洛阳做官三年，任期满了，要离开洛阳。洛阳是他和好友们一起饮酒赏花、写诗作文的地方，他非常留恋。在饯别的酒宴上，他写下了这首词。也有人认为写的是别的送别场合，具体情况说法不一。

05

The poet作者

Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072), who called himself the Old Drunkard and later the Retired Scholar of Six Ones, was a Northern Song writer and one of the Eight Great Prose Masters of Tang and Song. As a young official in Luoyang he often went out and wrote with friends such as Mei Yaochen; those were his happiest days. His essays are serious and reasoned, but his lyrics are fresh and tender, often about spring, flowers and parting.

欧阳修（1007—1072），号醉翁，晚年又号六一居士，北宋文学家，“唐宋八大家”之一。他年轻时在洛阳做官，和梅尧臣等朋友常常一起游玩、写作，那是他最快乐的日子。他写文章讲道理很严肃，写词却清新深情，常写春天、花和离别。

06

The mood意境

It opens with one small moment. The poet is about to say when he will leave, but before he speaks, the person across from him is already crying. This picture makes the parting feel real at once. The next two lines step back to say something true: people are born full of feeling, and the pain of parting lives in the heart itself, so you cannot blame the wind or the moon. These lines say it so clearly that they became famous. The second half says: do not write new farewell songs; one is enough to break a heart. At the end the poet finds a way out. First see all of Luoyang's flowers, then say goodbye to spring, and maybe it will hurt less. It sounds easygoing, but it really shows how hard it is for him to leave.

开头是一个很小的动作：词人刚想说出离开的日子，还没开口，对面的人已经哭了。这个画面让离别一下子变得很真实。接着两句跳出来讲道理：人天生就重感情，离别的痛苦是人心里本来就有的，不能怪风怪月。这两句说得很透，成了名句。下半首说，送别的歌别再翻新了，一首就够让人心碎。最后词人想出一个办法：先把洛阳的花看够，再和春天告别，也许就不那么难受。这话听起来豁达，其实正说明他太舍不得了。
