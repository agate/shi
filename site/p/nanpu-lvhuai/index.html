Nan Pu: Thoughts on the Road

 

fēng风bēi悲huà画jiǎo角

tīng听chán单yú于sān三nòng弄luò落qiáo谯mén门

tóu投sù宿qīn骎qīn骎zhēng征qí骑

fēi飞xuě雪mǎn满gū孤cūn村

jiǔ酒shì市jiàn渐lán阑dēng灯huǒ火

zhèng正qiāo敲chuāng窗luàn乱yè叶wǔ舞fēn纷fēn纷

sòng送shù数shēng声jīng惊yàn雁

zhà乍lí离yān烟shuǐ水

liáo嘹lì唳dù度hán寒yún云

hǎo好zài在bàn半lóng胧xī溪yuè月

dào到rú如jīn今wú无chù处bù不xiāo销hún魂

gù故guó国méi梅huā花guī归mèng梦

chóu愁sǔn损lǜ绿luó罗qún裙

wèi为wèn问àn暗xiāng香xián闲yàn艳

yě也xiāng相sī思wàn万diǎn点fù付tí啼hén痕

Common editions read jian lan (the lamps slowly going out) and zha li (just leaving). The source text has jian xian and xia li, likely a different edition or a copying slip.

通行本作“酒市渐阑灯火”“乍离烟水”。这里的底本作“渐闲”“下离”，可能是版本不同或传抄的差别。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 风悲画角In the wind, the painted horn sounds sad.风声里，画角的声音很悲凉，
  2. 听单于三弄落谯门I hear the tune Little Chanyu played three times, falling from the gate tower.听那《小单于》吹了三遍，从城门楼上传下来。
  3. 投宿骎骎征骑Riding hard, I look for a place to spend the night.我骑着马急急忙忙找地方过夜，
  4. 飞雪满孤村Snow fills the lonely village.大雪落满了孤零零的村子。
  5. 酒市渐阑灯火The lamps of the wine shops slowly go out.酒店的灯火慢慢熄了，
  6. 正敲窗乱叶舞纷纷Just then, scattered leaves whirl about and tap at the window.这时乱叶纷纷飞舞，敲打着窗户。
  7. 送数声惊雁A few cries of startled geese come to me.几声受惊大雁的叫声传来，
  8. 乍离烟水They have just left the misty water,它们刚离开雾蒙蒙的水面，
  9. 嘹唳度寒云and cry loud and sad as they pass through the cold clouds.高声哀鸣着飞过寒冷的云层。
  10. 好在半胧溪月The dim moon over the stream is just as it was,那朦朦胧胧的溪上月亮，还是老样子，
  11. 到如今无处不销魂yet now everything everywhere breaks my heart.到现在，处处都让我伤心。
  12. 故国梅花归梦In dreams I go home to the plum blossoms,梦里回到故乡，看到梅花，
  13. 愁损绿罗裙and she in her green silk skirt must be worn thin with sorrow.心爱的她一定愁得消瘦了。
  14. 为问暗香闲艳Let me ask the quietly fragrant plum blossoms:请问那幽香淡雅的梅花，
  15. 也相思万点付啼痕are you longing too, sending me ten thousand tear stains?你也在相思，把万点泪痕寄给我吗？
  16. 算翠屏应是I suppose by her green screen right now,想来她这时应该在翠绿的屏风边，
  17. 两眉馀恨倚黄昏she leans into the dusk, sorrow still between her brows.皱着双眉，带着满心的愁，靠在那里看黄昏。
02

Words字词注释

画角huà jiǎo
huà jiǎo: a painted horn used in old armies. Its sound was loud and sad.画角：古代军中的号角，外面画着彩色花纹，声音高亢悲凉。
单于chán yú
chán yú: short for the tune Little Chanyu, played on the horn.单于：曲子名《小单于》，是用号角吹的曲子。
三弄sān nòng
sān nòng: played three times. Nòng is one playing of a tune.三弄：吹了三遍。弄，演奏一遍曲子。
谯门qiáo mén
qiáo mén: the watchtower over a city gate.谯门：城门上用来瞭望的楼。
投宿tóu sù
tóu sù: to find a place to stay the night.投宿：找地方住下过夜。
骎骎qīn qīn
qīn qīn: horses running fast.骎骎：马跑得很快的样子。
征骑zhēng qí
zhēng qí: the horse of a traveller on a long journey.征骑：远行人骑的马。
渐阑jiàn lán
jiàn lán: slowly coming to an end. The lamps in the wine shops are going out one by one.渐阑：慢慢快要完了。这里是说酒店的灯火渐渐熄了。
惊雁jīng yàn
jīng yàn: wild geese startled into flight.惊雁：受惊飞起的大雁。
乍zhà
zhà: just now.乍：刚刚。
烟水yān shuǐ
yān shuǐ: water covered in mist.烟水：雾气笼罩的水面。
嘹唳liáo lì
liáo lì: the loud, mournful cry of wild geese.嘹唳：大雁响亮又凄凉的叫声。
度dù
dù: to pass over.度：越过，飞过。
好在hǎo zài
hǎo zài: an old everyday phrase meaning still as before, still well.好在：唐宋时的口语，意思是还像从前一样，别来无恙。
半胧bàn lóng
bàn lóng: dim, half hidden.半胧：朦朦胧胧，不太亮。
销魂xiāo hún
xiāo hún: so sad that one's soul seems to melt away.销魂：伤心得好像魂都没了。
故国gù guó
gù guó: the old home.故国：故乡。
愁损chóu sǔn
chóu sǔn: worn thin by sorrow.愁损：愁得消瘦憔悴。
绿罗裙lǜ luó qún
lǜ luó qún: a green silk skirt, standing for the woman he loves back home.绿罗裙：穿绿色丝裙的人，指家乡心爱的女子。
暗香闲艳àn xiāng xián yàn
àn xiāng xián yàn: faint fragrance and quiet beauty, both meaning plum blossom.暗香闲艳：淡淡的香气，安静的美丽，都是指梅花。
啼痕tí hén
tí hén: tear stains. Ten thousand of them are like countless tears, and also like dots of plum blossom.啼痕：泪痕。万点啼痕，像无数泪滴，也像点点梅花。
翠屏cuì píng
cuì píng: a green screen in a woman's room.翠屏：绿色的屏风，在女子的房间里。
两眉馀恨liǎng méi yú hèn
liǎng méi yú hèn: lingering sorrow between her two brows.两眉馀恨：两道眉毛间留着没散的愁。
03

How to read it读音

单chán
In chan yu this character is read chán. Chanyu was the title of the Xiongnu ruler; here it names a horn tune.“单于”的“单”读 chán。单于本是匈奴首领的称号，这里指曲子《小单于》。
骑qí
Today this is read qí. In old usage, as a noun meaning a horse and rider, it was read jì.按今天的习惯读 qí。古时“骑”作名词（骑马的人、坐骑）读 jì。
为wèi
In wei wen this character is read wèi, meaning let me, or on someone's behalf.“为问”的“为”读 wèi，意思是替、请让我。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric describes one winter night on the road, stopping at an inn. The horn, the wind and snow, the whirling leaves and the startled geese all make the traveller feel very alone. In a dream he goes home, sees the plum blossoms, and thinks of the woman he loves. We no longer know exactly when or where it was written. It was chosen for the Three Hundred Song Lyrics and is Kong Yi's best-known work.

这首词写冬天在旅途中投宿的一夜。号角声、风雪、乱叶、惊雁，让一个远行的人格外孤单。他在梦里回到故乡，看见梅花，想起家中的心上人。词的具体写作时间和地点已经不清楚。它被收进《宋词三百首》，是孔夷流传最广的作品。

05

The poet作者

Kong Yi lived in the Northern Song. He came from Longxing in Ruzhou, around today's Baofeng in Henan. He is usually said to have never taken office and to have lived quietly in the countryside, calling himself the Fisherman of the Zhi Riverbank. Few of his lyrics survive, and this one, clear and sad, is the most famous.

孔夷，北宋人，字方平，汝州龙兴（今河南宝丰一带）人。一般说他没有做官，隐居在乡间，自号“滍皋渔父”。他的词留下来的很少，这首《南浦》写得清丽凄凉，最有名。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is all sound and cold: the sad horn in the wind, the tune played again and again on the tower, snow over a lonely village, leaves tapping at the window, startled geese crying in cold clouds. A man riding hard on a long road stops for the night in a strange little village. The second stanza turns inward. The moon is the same moon, but everything hurts now. He dreams of the plum blossoms at home, thinks of her, and asks the blossoms whether they are weeping too. The last line pictures her leaning by the screen at dusk, her brows drawn tight. The poet does not say outright that he misses her. He imagines her missing him, so the longing belongs to both of them.

上阕全是声音和寒冷：风里悲凉的号角，城楼上一遍遍的曲子，雪满孤村，乱叶敲窗，惊雁在冷云里哀叫。一个骑马赶路的人，就在这样的夜里住进陌生的小村。下阕转到心里：月亮还是那个月亮，人却处处伤心。他梦见故乡的梅花，又想起家里的她，问梅花是不是也在流泪。最后一句设想她靠着屏风望黄昏，眉头紧锁。词人不直接说自己想她，而是写她想自己，这样思念就变成了两个人的。
