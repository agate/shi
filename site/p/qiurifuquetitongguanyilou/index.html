xǔ许hún浑Xu Hun · Tang唐

Written on the Tongguan Post Tower on an Autumn Journey to the Capital

 

hóng红yè叶wǎn晚xiāo萧xiāo萧

cháng长tíng亭jiǔ酒yì一piáo瓢

cán残yún云guī归tài太huà华

shū疏yǔ雨guò过zhōng中tiáo条

shù树sè色suí随shān山jiǒng迥

hé河shēng声rù入hǎi海yáo遥

dì帝xiāng乡míng明rì日dào到

yóu犹zì自mèng梦yú渔qiáo樵

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 红叶晚萧萧In the evening, red leaves rustle in the autumn wind;傍晚，秋风吹得红叶沙沙作响，
  2. 长亭酒一瓢at the roadside pavilion I drink a ladle of wine.我在长亭里喝了一瓢酒。
  3. 残云归太华Scattered clouds drift back to Mount Hua;零散的云飘回了华山，
  4. 疏雨过中条a light rain passes over the Zhongtiao Mountains.稀疏的雨洒过了中条山。
  5. 树色随山迥The colors of the trees stretch far away with the hills;树木的颜色随着山势伸向远方，
  6. 河声入海遥the roar of the Yellow River flows on toward the distant sea.黄河的涛声一路流向遥远的大海。
  7. 帝乡明日到Tomorrow I will reach the capital,明天就要到京城了，
  8. 犹自梦渔樵yet I still dream of a life of fishing and cutting wood.我心里却还梦着打鱼砍柴的日子。

03

How to read it读音

华huà
Read huà; tài huà is Mount Hua, and the mountain's name is read huà.读 huà，“太华”就是华山，山名读 huà。
04

Background创作背景

Fù què means going to the capital. One autumn, Xu Hun travelled to Chang'an. On the way he passed Tongguan, stayed at the post station, climbed its tower and wrote this poem on its wall. Tongguan, in today's Shaanxi, was the main gateway to Chang'an. Mount Hua lies to its south, and across the Yellow River to the north lie the Zhongtiao Mountains. We do not know the exact year of this trip.

“赴阙”就是到京城去。许浑在一个秋天前往长安，途中经过潼关，住在驿站里，登上驿楼写下这首诗，题在楼上。潼关在今天陕西潼关县，是进长安的要道，南边是华山，北边隔着黄河是中条山。这次去京城的具体年份说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Xu Hun (about 791–about 858) was a late Tang poet. He was very good at regulated verse with neat paired lines, and he loved writing about water and rain, so people joked that his thousand poems were all wet. He served as a local official and often wished to go home and live in retreat. The feeling in this poem, nearing the capital but dreaming of a quiet life, is common in his work.

许浑（约791—约858），字用晦，晚唐诗人。他特别擅长写律诗，对仗工整，又爱写水、写雨，有人说“许浑千首湿”。他做过地方官，常常想回乡隐居。这首诗里那种“快到京城却想着隐居”的心情，在他的诗里很常见。

06

The mood意境

The poem opens on an autumn evening: red leaves rustle, and the poet drinks a little wine at a roadside pavilion. It feels a bit lonely. The middle four lines are the best part. They show the grand view from the post tower at Tongguan: Mount Hua to the south, the Zhongtiao Mountains to the north, forests stretching away, and the Yellow River rushing to the sea. The clouds move, the rain moves, the river moves, and the scene is vast and far. The last two lines turn: tomorrow he reaches the capital and should be glad, yet he still dreams of fishing and cutting wood. It shows he is not very eager to be an official. His heart longs for the freedom of hills and rivers.

开头写秋天傍晚，红叶沙沙响，诗人在长亭里喝一点酒，有些冷清。中间四句是全诗最精彩的部分，写的是从潼关驿楼上看到的大景色：南边的华山，北边的中条山，远去的树林，奔流入海的黄河。云在动，雨在动，河水也在动，画面又大又远。最后两句一转：明天就到京城了，按说该高兴，诗人却还梦着打鱼砍柴的隐居生活。这说明他对做官并不那么热心，心里更向往山水之间的自由。
