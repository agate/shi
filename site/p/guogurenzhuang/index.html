No. 32 · Tang poem · five-character regulated verse第 32 首 · 唐诗 · 五言律诗

guò过gù故rén人zhuāng庄

mèng孟hào浩rán然Meng Haoran · Tang唐

Visiting an Old Friend's Farm

 

gù故rén人jù具jī鸡shǔ黍

yāo邀wǒ我zhì至tián田jiā家

lǜ绿shù树cūn村biān边hé合

qīng青shān山guō郭wài外xié斜

kāi开xuān轩miàn面cháng场pǔ圃

bǎ把jiǔ酒huà话sāng桑má麻

dài待dào到chóng重yáng阳rì日

huán还lái来jiù就jú菊huā花

The corpus text has kāi yán, to lay out a feast, in line five. Textbooks have kāi xuān, to open the window.

语料本第五句作“开筵面场圃”。课本作“开轩面场圃”，意思是打开窗户。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 故人具鸡黍My old friend made chicken and millet老朋友准备了鸡肉和黄米饭，
  2. 邀我至田家and asked me to his farmhouse.请我到他的农家去做客。
  3. 绿树村边合Green trees ring the village;绿树环绕着村庄，
  4. 青山郭外斜green hills slope away beyond its walls.青山在村外连绵起伏。
  5. 开轩面场圃We open the window to the threshing floor and garden,打开窗子，对着打谷场和菜园，
  6. 把酒话桑麻and with cups in hand we talk of mulberry and hemp.我们端着酒杯，聊着种桑种麻的事。
  7. 待到重阳日When the Double Ninth Festival comes,等到九月初九重阳节那天，
  8. 还来就菊花I will come again to see the chrysanthemums.我还要再来这里赏菊花。
02

Words字词注释

故人gù rén
gù rén: an old friend.故人：老朋友。
具jù
jù: to prepare.具：准备，置办。
鸡黍jī shǔ
jī shǔ: chicken and millet, a good farm meal for guests. Shǔ is yellow millet.鸡黍：鸡和黄米饭，指农家待客的好饭菜。黍，黄米。
田家tián jiā
tián jiā: a farming family's home.田家：种田人的家，农家。
合hé
hé: to surround. Green trees ring the village.合：环绕。绿树把村子围了起来。
郭guō
guō: the outer wall. Old towns had an inner and an outer wall; the outer one was the guō. Here it means outside the village.郭：外城。古代城墙分内外两层，外面的一层叫郭。这里指村子外面。
斜xié
xié: the green hills slope away along the edge.斜：青山斜斜地横在那里，连绵起伏。
开轩kāi xuān
kāi xuān: to open the window. Xuān is a window.开轩：打开窗户。轩，窗户。
面miàn
miàn: to face.面：面对着。
场圃cháng pǔ
cháng pǔ: the threshing floor and the vegetable garden.场圃：打谷场和菜园。
把酒bǎ jiǔ
bǎ jiǔ: to hold up one's wine cup.把酒：端起酒杯。把，拿着。
话桑麻huà sāng má
huà sāng má: to chat about mulberry trees and hemp, that is, about farm work.话桑麻：聊种桑养蚕、种麻这些农家事。
重阳日chóng yáng rì
chóng yáng rì: the Double Ninth Festival, the ninth day of the ninth lunar month, when people climb hills and enjoy chrysanthemums.重阳日：农历九月初九，重阳节，古人这天登高、赏菊。
还huán
huán: again.还：再，又。
就菊花jiù jú huā
jiù jú huā: to come close to the chrysanthemums, that is, to enjoy them. Jiù means to go near.就菊花：靠近菊花，指来赏菊。就，靠近。
03

How to read it读音

斜xié
This character is read xié today. In old pronunciation it rhymed with the other line endings, so some read it xiá.“斜”现在读 xié。古音和“家、麻、花”押韵，有人按古音读 xiá。
场cháng
In cháng pǔ, the character means a flat open ground for drying and threshing grain, read cháng.“场圃”的“场”指平坦的空地，用来晒粮食、打谷子，读 cháng。
还huán
Here this character means again. It is usually read huán; some read it hái.“还”在这里是“再、又”的意思，一般读 huán，也有人读 hái。
04

Background创作背景

Meng Haoran spent most of his life in his hometown, Xiangyang in today's Hubei. For a time he lived quietly on Lumen Mountain outside the city. An old friend in the countryside cooked a meal and invited him to his farm. He went, ate, drank and talked about farming with his friend, and was very happy. Then he wrote this poem. In the title, guò means to visit and zhuāng means a farm. We do not know the year.

孟浩然大半生住在家乡襄阳（今湖北襄阳），曾在城外的鹿门山隐居。一位住在乡下的老朋友准备了饭菜，请他去农庄做客。他去了，和朋友吃饭、喝酒、聊农事，非常开心，就写下了这首诗。题目里的“过”是拜访的意思，“庄”是农庄。具体是哪一年写的，已经不清楚了。

05

The poet作者

Meng Haoran (689–740), from Xiangyang, was a Tang poet. He never held office. He spent most of his life living quietly at home or traveling. He wrote about hills, rivers and farms, and he is named together with Wang Wei as the two masters of this kind of poem. His poems are plain and natural, like friendly talk, and this one is a fine example.

孟浩然（689—740），襄阳人，唐代诗人。他一生没有做过官，大部分时间在家乡隐居和四处游历。他擅长写山水田园诗，和王维并称“王孟”。他的诗平淡自然，像在聊家常，这首就是代表。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a diary of a visit. An old friend invites him to a meal. It is only chicken and rice, but it is made with care. At the edge of the village, green trees ring the houses, and green hills lie beyond. What a lovely place. Inside, the window opens onto the threshing floor and the garden. The two drink and talk about how the crops are growing. There are no polite speeches, and no talk of officials. At the end the poet himself says he will come back at the Double Ninth Festival to see the chrysanthemums. He never says he is happy, yet we can feel the simple, warm friendship between them.

这首诗像一篇做客日记。老朋友请吃饭，菜是普通的鸡和米饭，却很有心意。走到村口，绿树围着村子，远处是青山，环境多么好。进了屋，打开窗子就是打谷场和菜园，两个人一边喝酒，一边聊庄稼长得怎么样，没有客套，也不谈官场的事。最后诗人主动说，重阳节我还要来看菊花。不用说“我很开心”，读者也能感觉到这份朋友之间淳朴的情谊。
