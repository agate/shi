Du Jiang Yun San Fan: Qingming at West Lake

This keeps the database text "wan feng wei luo" (blown down by the evening wind). Another version writes the second-to-last character as a word meaning "not yet".

这里按资料库原文作“晚风为落”。另一种版本作“晚风未落”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 羞红颦浅恨The red flowers blush and frown a little, with a light sorrow;红花像害羞的姑娘，微微皱着眉，带着一点哀怨，
  2. 晚风为落the evening wind blows them down,被晚风吹落了，
  3. 片绣点重茵and they dot the thick grass like pieces of embroidery.像一片片刺绣，点缀在厚厚的草地上。
  4. 旧堤分燕尾The old causeways split the lake like a swallow's tail;西湖的老堤像燕尾一样把湖水分开，
  5. 桂棹轻鸥fine boats glide along as light as gulls;精美的游船像鸥鸟一样轻快，
  6. 宝勒倚残云jewel-bridled horses rest beneath the last wisps of cloud.华丽的马停在淡淡的云影下。
  7. 千丝怨碧A thousand willow threads hang green, as if full of sorrow.千万条柳丝绿得像在诉说哀怨。
  8. 渐路入The path goes deeper and deeper小路渐渐深入，
  9. 仙坞迷津into a fairy hollow, where one loses the way to the ferry.像走进了仙人的山谷，迷失了渡口。
  10. 肠漫回My heart turns over and over in vain:我的心里白白地千回百转，
  11. 隔花时见now and then, through the flowers, I catch sight of隔着花丛，时不时能看见
  12. 背面楚腰身her slender figure, turned away.她背过身去时苗条的身影。
  13. 逡巡I hesitate, pacing back and forth.我犹豫着走来走去。
  14. 题门惆怅Like Cui Hu writing his poem on the door, I am full of sorrow;像崔护在门上题诗一样，满心失落，
  15. 堕履牵萦the keepsake she dropped still tugs at my heart;她留下的信物还牵挂在我心上，
  16. 数幽期难准I count over our secret meetings, and none can be counted on.细数那些秘密的约会，都说不准还能不能实现。
  17. 还始觉Only now do I see到这时我才发觉，
  18. 留情缘眼that love began with a single look,动情是因为当初那一眼，
  19. 宽带因春and my belt grew loose because of spring longing.衣带变宽是因为春天的相思。
  20. 明朝事与孤烟冷Tomorrow, everything will be as cold as the lonely Cold Food smoke,明天的事情，会像寒食节的孤烟一样冷清，
  21. 做满湖and the whole lake will be满湖都会是
  22. 风雨愁人wind and rain to sadden me.让人发愁的风雨。
  23. 山黛暝The dark hills fade into dusk,青黑的山色渐渐昏暗，
  24. 尘波澹绿无痕and the pale green waves leave no trace at all.湖上的波浪淡绿一片，没有留下一点痕迹。
02

Words字词注释

羞红xiū hóng
xiū hóng: red flowers that seem to blush.羞红：害羞似的红花。
颦浅恨pín qiǎn hèn
pín qiǎn hèn: frowning a little, with a light sorrow. Pín means to knit the brows. The flowers are written as if they were people.颦浅恨：微微皱着眉，带着一点哀怨。颦，皱眉。这里把花写成了人。
为落wèi luò
wèi luò: blown down.为落：被吹落。
片绣piàn xiù
piàn xiù: petals like pieces of embroidery.片绣：一片片像刺绣一样的花瓣。
重茵chóng yīn
chóng yīn: a thick cushion, here meaning a thick carpet of grass.重茵：厚厚的垫子，这里比喻厚厚的草地。
旧堤分燕尾jiù dī fēn yàn wěi
jiù dī fēn yàn wěi: the old causeways of West Lake split the water like a swallow's forked tail.旧堤分燕尾：西湖的老堤把湖水分开，像燕子分叉的尾巴。
桂棹guì zhào
guì zhào: an oar of cassia wood, that is, a fine pleasure boat.桂棹：桂木做的船桨，指精美的游船。
轻鸥qīng ōu
qīng ōu: light and quick as gulls.轻鸥：像鸥鸟一样轻快。
宝勒bǎo lè
bǎo lè: a jewelled bridle, that is, people out riding.宝勒：装饰华美的马笼头，指骑马游玩的人。
倚残云yǐ cán yún
yǐ cán yún: resting beneath the last wisps of cloud.倚残云：停在淡淡的云影下面。
千丝怨碧qiān sī yuàn bì
qiān sī yuàn bì: a thousand green willow threads, as if full of sorrow.千丝怨碧：千万条绿色的柳丝，好像带着哀怨。
仙坞xiān wù
xiān wù: a fairy hollow. Wù is a low place with high ground all around.仙坞：仙人住的山谷。坞，四面高、中间低的地方。
迷津mí jīn
mí jīn: losing the way to the ferry. It hints at the Peach Blossom Spring story, where no one who left could find the way back.迷津：找不到渡口，迷了路。这里暗用桃花源的故事：进去过的人，再也找不到原来的路。
肠漫回cháng màn huí
cháng màn huí: the heart twists and turns, all in vain.肠漫回：心里白白地千回百转。
楚腰chǔ yāo
chǔ yāo: a slender waist. The King of Chu was said to love slender waists, so the phrase came to mean a woman's slim figure.楚腰：细腰。传说楚王喜欢细腰的人，后来用“楚腰”指女子苗条的腰身。
逡巡qūn xún
qūn xún: to hesitate, pacing back and forth.逡巡：犹豫不前，走来走去。
题门tí mén
tí mén: writing a poem on a door. It recalls the Tang story of Cui Hu, who went back a year later to find a girl he had met under the peach blossoms. The door was locked, so he wrote a poem on it about the girl being gone while the peach flowers still smiled in the spring wind.题门：在门上题诗。用唐代崔护的故事：他第二年再去找桃花下遇见的姑娘，门锁着，就在门上写下了“人面不知何处去，桃花依旧笑春风”。
惆怅chóu chàng
chóu chàng: disappointed and sad.惆怅：失落难过。
堕履duò lǚ
duò lǚ: a dropped shoe, usually read as a keepsake the woman left behind; sources differ on where the image comes from.堕履：掉下的鞋子。一般理解为女子留下的信物，具体出处说法不一。
牵萦qiān yíng
qiān yíng: to tug at the heart, to linger in the mind.牵萦：牵挂，缠绕在心上。
幽期yōu qī
yōu qī: a secret meeting.幽期：秘密的约会。
难准nán zhǔn
nán zhǔn: hard to be sure of.难准：说不准，没法确定。
留情缘眼liú qíng yuán yǎn
liú qíng yuán yǎn: love began through the eyes. Yuán means "because of".留情缘眼：动情是因为眼睛看见了她。缘，因为。
宽带因春kuān dài yīn chūn
kuān dài yīn chūn: because of spring longing he has grown thin, and his belt has become loose.宽带因春：因为春天的相思，人瘦了，衣带都变宽了。
明朝míng zhāo
míng zhāo: tomorrow.明朝：明天。
孤烟冷gū yān lěng
gū yān lěng: the lonely smoke is cold. On the Cold Food Festival no fires were lit; Qingming comes right after it.孤烟冷：寒食节不生火，炊烟也冷清了。清明紧挨着寒食。
做zuò
zuò: to bring about, to make.做：造成，弄成。
山黛shān dài
shān dài: dark blue-green hills, like a woman's painted eyebrows. Dài is the dark pigment for painting brows.山黛：青黑色的山，像女子画的眉。黛，青黑色的画眉颜料。
暝míng
míng: growing dark.暝：天色昏暗。
尘波chén bō
chén bō: the waves on the lake. Some read it as the troubles of the human world.尘波：湖上的波浪。一说指人世间的风波。
澹绿dàn lǜ
dàn lǜ: pale green. Dàn here means pale.澹绿：淡淡的绿色。澹，同“淡”。
03

How to read it读音

为wèi
Here it means "by, because of" and is read wèi.这里是“被”的意思，读 wèi。
重chóng
Here it means "layered, thick" and is read chóng.这里是“层层、厚厚”的意思，读 chóng。
勒lè
In bǎo lè the character means a bridle and is read lè.“宝勒”的勒指马笼头，读 lè。
数shǔ
Here it is usually read shǔ, "to count over"; another reading takes it as shuò, "many times".这里一般理解为“细数”，读 shǔ；一说是“屡次”的意思，读 shuò。
朝zhāo
Míng zhāo means tomorrow; here the character is read zhāo.“明朝”是明天早上、明天的意思，朝读 zhāo。
澹dàn
Here it is used for "pale" and is read dàn.这里同“淡”，读 dàn。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric describes a spring outing at West Lake in Hangzhou at Qingming, mixed with longing for a woman. It is generally thought to recall how Wu Wenying first met his sweetheart by West Lake. He glimpsed her back through the flowers; later they had secret meetings, but in the end they parted. Now it is Qingming again, he visits the lake once more, and the old love floods back. The exact year it was written is no longer known.

这首词写的是清明时节在杭州西湖游春的情景，又夹着对一位女子的思念。一般认为，词里写的是吴文英当年在西湖边遇见心上人的往事：隔着花丛看见她的背影，后来两人有过秘密的约会，最终却分开了。如今又到清明，他重游西湖，旧情涌上心头。具体写于哪一年，已经说不清。

05

The poet作者

Wu Wenying (about 1200 to about 1260), courtesy name Junte, also called Mengchuang, came from Siming, today's Ningbo in Zhejiang. He was a Southern Song ci poet. He never held high office and often lived as a guest of officials around Suzhou and Hangzhou. His lyrics use rich words and jump from image to image like a dream, so they are hard to follow. "San fan" in the tune name means a tune made by joining parts of several melodies.

吴文英（约1200—约1260），字君特，号梦窗，四明（今浙江宁波）人，南宋词人。他一辈子没有做大官，常在苏州、杭州一带给官员做门客。他的词用字华丽，意思常常跳来跳去，像梦一样，不太好懂。“三犯”是一种把几个曲调拼在一起的词调。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is like a scroll painting of a spring outing at West Lake. Red flowers, like shy girls, are blown by the evening wind onto the grass. The causeways split the lake like a swallow's tail, boats glide like gulls, and fine horses rest beneath the clouds. There are a thousand willow threads, and the path goes deeper, as if into fairyland. Just then, through the flowers, he sees her from behind. It is only a glimpse of her back, yet it sets his heart turning over and over. The second stanza tells what came after. He hesitates and feels lost, like Cui Hu who could never find the girl under the peach blossoms again. Their meetings cannot be counted on, and he grows thin with longing. Then the poem turns to tomorrow: after Qingming the whole lake will be cold wind and rain. The ending shows only hills and water at dusk. The waves are pale and leave no trace, just like that love. Wu Wenying's lyrics are often dreamlike, with one picture jumping to the next. You do not need to understand every line. Just follow the pictures and feel the mood, lovely and sad at once.

上片像一卷西湖春游图：红花像害羞的姑娘，被晚风吹落在草地上；长堤把湖分成燕尾，游船像鸥鸟，骏马停在云下；柳丝千万条，路越走越深，像进了仙境。就在这时，隔着花丛，他看见了她的背影。只是一个背影，却让他的心千回百转。下片写后来的事：他犹豫、失落，像崔护再也找不到桃花下的姑娘；约会说不准，人却因为相思瘦了。最后笔锋转向明天：清明过后，满湖都会是冷清的风雨。结尾只写暮色里的山和湖，波浪淡淡的，什么痕迹也没留下，就像那段感情一样。吴文英的词常常像梦，画面一个接一个地跳，读的时候不必句句弄懂，跟着画面去感受那种又美又惆怅的心情就好。
