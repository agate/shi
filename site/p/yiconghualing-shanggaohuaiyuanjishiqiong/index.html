Yi Cong Hua Ling: When Will This Longing End

The old edition writes the word for deep in an older form that looks like the surname Shen. It is read chén; we use the common modern form.

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 伤高怀远几时穷Climbing high, longing for one far away, when will this sorrow end?登高怀念远方的人，这伤心什么时候才有尽头？
  2. 无物似情浓Nothing in the world is as strong as love.世上没有什么比爱情更浓烈。
  3. 离愁正引千丝乱The grief of parting tosses like a thousand willow strands,离别的愁绪正像千万条柳丝乱飘，
  4. 更东陌飞絮蒙蒙And on the eastern lane, willow fluff fills the air.更何况东边小路上，柳絮漫天飞舞。
  5. 嘶骑渐遥His neighing horse moves farther and farther away;他骑的马嘶叫着越走越远，
  6. 征尘不断The dust of his road keeps rising,扬起的尘土一路不断，
  7. 何处认郎踪Where can I find a trace of him now?到哪里才能找到情郎的踪影？
  8. 双鸳池沼水溶溶On the wide, gentle pond, mandarin ducks swim in pairs;池塘里一对对鸳鸯在宽阔的水面上游着，
  9. 南北小桡通Little boats come and go from south to north.南来北往的小船可以随意来去。
  10. 梯横画阁黄昏后After dusk, the ladder to the painted pavilion lies put away;黄昏以后，画阁的梯子已经收起横放，
  11. 又还是斜月帘栊Once again only the slanting moon shines on the curtained window.又只剩下斜斜的月光照着帘窗。
  12. 沉恨细思Thinking over this deep sorrow,把这深深的愁恨仔细想一想，
  13. 不如桃杏I am worse off than the peach and apricot blossoms,我还不如那桃花杏花，
  14. 犹解嫁东风For they at least know how to marry the east wind.它们还懂得嫁给春风。
02

Words字词注释

伤高怀远shāng gāo huái yuǎn
shāng gāo huái yuǎn: climbing high and longing for someone far away, feeling sad.伤高怀远：登上高处，想念远方的人，心里伤感。
几时穷jǐ shí qióng
jǐ shí qióng: when will it ever end? Qióng means to come to an end.几时穷：什么时候才有尽头。穷，尽。
无物似情浓wú wù sì qíng nóng
wú wù sì qíng nóng: nothing is as strong as love.无物似情浓：没有什么东西比爱情更浓烈。
千丝乱qiān sī luàn
qiān sī luàn: a thousand willow strands tossing about, like the tangle of sorrow at parting.千丝乱：千万条柳丝乱飘，像心里乱成一团的离愁。
东陌dōng mò
dōng mò: the eastern lane, where they parted.东陌：东边的小路，指当初分别的地方。
飞絮蒙蒙fēi xù méng méng
fēi xù méng méng: willow fluff fills the air like a haze.飞絮蒙蒙：柳絮漫天飞舞，看不清楚。
嘶骑sī qí
sī qí: a neighing horse, the one her lover rode away on.嘶骑：嘶叫着的马，指情郎骑的马。
征尘zhēng chén
zhēng chén: the dust raised by hooves on a long journey.征尘：远行时马蹄扬起的尘土。
郎踪láng zōng
láng zōng: a trace of her beloved.郎踪：情郎的踪影。
双鸳shuāng yuān
shuāng yuān: a pair of mandarin ducks, a common symbol of a loving couple.双鸳：成双成对的鸳鸯，常用来比喻恩爱的夫妻。
溶溶róng róng
róng róng: water broad and gently flowing.溶溶：水面宽阔、缓缓流动的样子。
小桡xiǎo ráo
xiǎo ráo: a small boat. Ráo means oar.小桡：小船。桡，船桨。
梯横tī héng
tī héng: the ladder lies flat, put away. No one will climb up to her now.梯横：梯子横放着，收了起来，说明不会再有人上楼来了。
画阁huà gé
huà gé: a painted pavilion.画阁：有彩绘装饰的楼阁。
帘栊lián lóng
lián lóng: a curtained window. Lóng means a window frame.帘栊：挂着帘子的窗户。栊，窗格。
沉恨chén hèn
chén hèn: deep sorrow and regret.沉恨：深深的怨恨和愁苦。
解jiě
jiě: to know how to.解：懂得，知道。
嫁东风jià dōng fēng
jià dōng fēng: to marry the east wind. Peach and apricot blossoms open in the spring wind and fly off with it, as if they had married it.嫁东风：把自己嫁给春风。桃花杏花在春风里开放，又随春风飘去，好像嫁给了春风。
03

How to read it读音

骑qí
Sī qí means a neighing horse. Today this character is read qí. In old usage, as a noun for a mount, it was read jì.“嘶骑”指嘶叫的马，“骑”按现在的规范读 qí。古代当名词“骑的马”讲时读 jì。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric tells of a woman longing for her lover after they part. A Song dynasty book of notes says that as a young man, Zhang Xian loved a woman who lived in a small pavilion on a pond. They could only meet at night by a ladder, and later they had to part, so he wrote this lyric. Whether the story is true can no longer be known. It is also said that Ouyang Xiu loved the ending so much that he nicknamed Zhang Xian "the official of peach and apricot marrying the east wind".

这首词写一位女子和情人分别后的思念。据宋人笔记《过庭录》记载，张先年轻时爱上一位住在池中小阁里的女子，两人只能在夜里靠梯子相见，后来不得不分开，他便写了这首词。这个故事是否属实已无法确定。又据说欧阳修很喜欢这首词的结尾，称张先为“桃杏嫁东风郎中”。

05

The poet作者

Zhang Xian (990–1078) came from Wucheng, today's Huzhou in Zhejiang. He was a Northern Song lyric poet and served as a court official. He lived past eighty and knew Yan Shu, Ouyang Xiu and Su Shi. He was especially good at writing about shadows, as in his famous line about clouds parting and flowers playing with their shadows in the moonlight, so people called him "Zhang of the Three Shadows". His lyrics are soft and delicate, often about longing between lovers.

张先（990—1078），字子野，乌程（今浙江湖州）人，北宋词人，做过都官郎中。他活了八十多岁，和晏殊、欧阳修、苏轼都有来往。他特别会写“影”，有“云破月来花弄影”等名句，人称“张三影”。他的词细腻柔美，常写男女之间的思念。

06

The mood意境

It opens with a sigh: when will the pain of gazing far for him ever end? Nothing is stronger than love. Then she looks around. Willow strands toss and fluff fills the sky, just like the tangle in her heart. She remembers him riding away. The horse went farther, the dust rose all along the road, and at last not even his shadow could be seen. The second stanza shows where she lives. Ducks swim in pairs on the pond and little boats come and go freely, yet she is alone. After dusk the ladder is put away and only the slanting moon shines on her window, day after day. At the end comes a strange, sudden thought: I am worse off than the peach and apricot blossoms, for at least they get to marry the spring wind! The image is fresh and heartbreaking. It says her loneliness and bitterness completely, and it became a famous line for all time.

开头就是一声叹问：登高望远的伤心，什么时候才完？世上没有比爱情更浓的东西了。接着写眼前：柳丝乱飘，柳絮满天，就像她心里乱成一团的愁。她想起情郎骑马离开时，马越走越远，尘土一路扬起，最后连影子也找不到了。下片写她住的地方：池塘里鸳鸯成双，小船自由来往，可她却只能一个人待着。黄昏后梯子收起，只有斜月照着窗户，一天又一天都是这样。最后她忽发奇想：我还不如桃花杏花呢，它们至少还能嫁给春风！这个比喻又新奇又心酸，把她的孤单和怨恨说得淋漓尽致，成了千古名句。
