Song lyric · ci lyric to the tune Yan Shan Ting, two stanzas宋词 · 词·燕山亭（双调）

yān燕shān山tíng亭·yōu幽mèng梦chū初huí回

zhāng张zī镃Zhang Zi · Song宋

Yan Shan Ting: Just Back from a Dim Dream

 

yōu幽mèng梦chū初huí回

chóng重yīn阴wèi未kāi开

xiǎo晓sè色chuī吹chéng成shū疏yǔ雨

zhú竹jiàn槛qì气hán寒

huì蕙wǎn畹shēng声yáo摇

xīn新lǜ绿àn暗tōng通nán南pǔ浦

wèi未yǒu有rén人xíng行

cái才bàn半qǐ启

huí回láng廊zhū朱hù户

wú无xù绪

kōng空wàng望jí极ní霓jīng旌

jǐn锦shū书nán难jù据

tái苔jìng径zhuī追yì忆céng曾yóu游

niàn念shuí谁bàn伴

qiū秋qiān千cǎi彩shéng绳fāng芳zhù柱

xī犀lián奁dài黛juǎn卷

fèng凤zhěn枕yún云gū孤

yīng应yě也jǐ几fān番níng凝zhù伫

zěn怎dé得yī伊lái来

huā花wù雾rào绕

xiǎo小táng堂shēn深chù处

liú留zhù住

zhí直dào到lǎo老

bú不jiào教guī归qù去

The database text writes cǎi (coloured) with a simpler character that also reads cǎi; this follows the usual form. The tune name Yan Shan Ting is also written with a different first character.

资料库原文“彩绳”作“采绳”，这里按通行写法作“彩绳”。词牌“燕山亭”也写作“宴山亭”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 幽梦初回Just back from a dim dream,迷迷糊糊的梦刚刚醒来，
  2. 重阴未开the heavy clouds have not yet cleared;一层层阴云还没有散开，
  3. 晓色吹成疏雨the dawn sky is blown into thin rain.清晨的天色被风吹成了稀稀落落的小雨。
  4. 竹槛气寒The bamboo railing feels cold;竹栏杆边透着寒气，
  5. 蕙畹声摇the orchid beds rustle in the wind;蕙兰园里的叶子被风吹得沙沙响，
  6. 新绿暗通南浦fresh green quietly stretches all the way to the southern shore.新长出的绿色悄悄地一直连到南边的水岸。
  7. 未有人行No one is about yet;还没有人走动，
  8. 才半启only half opened只半开着
  9. 回廊朱户are the winding walkway's red doors.曲折的走廊和红色的门。
  10. 无绪I am in no mood for anything.我没有一点心思。
  11. 空望极霓旌In vain I gaze toward where her rainbow banners went;白白地望向远方她离去的方向，
  12. 锦书难据letters cannot be counted on.书信也靠不住。
  13. 苔径追忆曾游On the mossy path I remember where we once walked,走在长满青苔的小路上，回想从前一起游玩的地方，
  14. 念谁伴and wonder who keeps her company now想到如今有谁陪着她
  15. 秋千彩绳芳柱by the swing with its coloured ropes.在彩绳秋千边玩耍。
  16. 犀奁黛卷By her make-up box her brows must be knitted;她的梳妆盒旁，她该是皱着眉头，
  17. 凤枕云孤on her phoenix pillow she sleeps alone;绣凤的枕头上，她一个人独睡，
  18. 应也几番凝伫she too must have stood lost in thought, more than once.想必也有好几回站着出神想我。
  19. 怎得伊来How can I bring her here,怎样才能让她回来，
  20. 花雾绕wrapped in a mist of flowers,在花香如雾的环绕中，
  21. 小堂深处deep inside this little hall,住在小堂的深处，
  22. 留住and keep her here,把她留住，
  23. 直到老until we are old,一直到老，
  24. 不教归去never letting her go away again?再也不让她离开。
02

Words字词注释

幽梦初回yōu mèng chū huí
yōu mèng chū huí: just waking from a dim, deep dream.幽梦初回：迷迷糊糊的梦刚刚醒来。幽，隐约、深沉。
重阴未开chóng yīn wèi kāi
chóng yīn wèi kāi: the heavy layers of cloud have not yet cleared.重阴未开：一层层的阴云还没有散开。
晓色xiǎo sè
xiǎo sè: the colour of the sky at dawn.晓色：清晨的天色。
疏雨shū yǔ
shū yǔ: thin, scattered rain.疏雨：稀稀落落的小雨。
竹槛zhú jiàn
zhú jiàn: a bamboo railing.竹槛：竹子做的栏杆。
蕙畹huì wǎn
huì wǎn: a garden plot of orchids. Huì is a fragrant orchid; wǎn is an old unit of land, here a garden bed.蕙畹：种着蕙兰的园地。蕙，一种香草；畹，古代的田地面积单位，这里指园圃。
声摇shēng yáo
shēng yáo: rustling in the wind.声摇：被风吹得沙沙作响。
新绿xīn lǜ
xīn lǜ: fresh new green leaves and grass.新绿：刚长出来的绿草绿叶。
暗通àn tōng
àn tōng: quietly stretching all the way to.暗通：悄悄地一直连到。
南浦nán pǔ
nán pǔ: the southern shore. In old poems it often means a place of parting.南浦：南边的水边。古诗词里常用来指送别的地方。
回廊huí láng
huí láng: a winding covered walkway.回廊：曲折环绕的走廊。
朱户zhū hù
zhū hù: red-lacquered doors.朱户：红漆的门。
无绪wú xù
wú xù: in no mood for anything.无绪：没有心思，什么也不想做。
空望极kōng wàng jí
kōng wàng jí: gazing as far as the eye can reach, in vain.空望极：白白地望到最远处。
霓旌ní jīng
ní jīng: rainbow-coloured banners, said to be carried by immortals on their journeys. Here they stand for the carriage of the loved one who has gone far away.霓旌：彩虹般的旗子，传说是仙人出行时用的。这里比喻心上人远去的车驾。
锦书jǐn shū
jǐn shū: a letter, often one between lovers.锦书：书信，常指情人之间的信。
难据nán jù
nán jù: cannot be relied on.难据：靠不住，指不上。
苔径tái jìng
tái jìng: a path covered with moss.苔径：长满青苔的小路。
曾游céng yóu
céng yóu: where we once wandered together.曾游：从前一起游玩的地方。
念niàn
niàn: to think of.念：想到。
彩绳芳柱cǎi shéng fāng zhù
cǎi shéng fāng zhù: the coloured ropes and lovely posts of the swing.彩绳芳柱：秋千上彩色的绳子和漂亮的柱子。
犀奁xī lián
xī lián: a make-up box decorated with rhinoceros horn.犀奁：用犀牛角装饰的梳妆盒。
黛卷dài juǎn
dài juǎn: usually read as knitted brows. Dài is the dark pigment women used on their eyebrows, and here means the brows.黛卷：一般理解为眉头皱着。黛，女子画眉用的青黑色颜料，这里指眉毛。
凤枕fèng zhěn
fèng zhěn: a pillow embroidered with a phoenix.凤枕：绣着凤凰的枕头。
云孤yún gū
yún gū: cloud-like hair resting alone, that is, sleeping alone.云孤：像云一样的头发孤零零地枕着，指一个人独睡。
凝伫níng zhù
níng zhù: standing still for a long time, lost in thought.凝伫：久久地站着出神。
怎得zěn dé
zěn dé: how can I make...怎得：怎样才能。
伊yī
yī: she.伊：她。
不教bú jiào
bù jiào: not letting.不教：不让。
03

How to read it读音

重chóng
Here it means "layer upon layer" and is read chóng.这里是“层层”的意思，读 chóng。
槛jiàn
Here it means a railing and is read jiàn. Only as "threshold" is it read kǎn.这里指栏杆，读 jiàn。“门槛”的槛才读 kǎn。
应yīng
Here it means "must surely" and is read yīng.这里是“应该、想必”的意思，读 yīng。
教jiào
Here it means "to let, to make" and is read jiào.这里是“让、使”的意思，读 jiào。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is set on a spring morning. Someone wakes from a dream and misses a loved one far away. The first stanza shows the scene before him and his listless mood. The second stanza recalls the times they spent together, imagines that she misses him too, and ends with the wish that she come back and never leave again. The lyric does not say when or where it was written.

这首词写的是一个春天的清晨，一个人从梦里醒来，想念远方的心上人。上片写他眼前的景色和没精打采的心情；下片写他回忆从前和她一起游玩，又想象她那边也在想念自己，最后盼望她能回来，永远不再分开。词中没有写明写作的时间和地点。

05

The poet作者

Zhang Zi was a ci poet of the Southern Song. His courtesy name was Gongfu, and he called himself Yuezhai. He was the great-grandson of the famous general Zhang Jun. Born into a rich family, he wrote poems and lyrics and was also good at painting bamboo and rocks. His lyrics were gathered as the Yuzhaotang Lyrics. Some records keep only the surname Zhang, with no given name. The lyric was chosen for Three Hundred Song Lyrics under Zhang Zi's name, which shows how highly it is valued.

张镃，南宋词人，字功甫，号约斋，是南宋名将张俊的曾孙。他生在富贵人家，会写诗填词，也擅长画竹子和石头，词集叫《玉照堂词》。有的资料只记下作者姓张，没有名字。这首词被选进了《宋词三百首》，署名张镃，可见写得很好。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza begins with waking. The dream has just ended, the sky is grey, and thin rain drifts in the dawn light. The bamboo railing is a little cold, the orchid leaves rustle, and fresh green spreads all the way to the southern shore. In old poems the southern shore is where people part, so as the green reaches there, his thoughts seem to drift to the place where they said goodbye. No one is in the courtyard and the doors are only half open. He does not feel like doing anything. He only stares into the distance, and he cannot even count on a letter. The second stanza first looks back. Walking on the mossy path, he remembers playing on the swing together, and wonders who keeps her company now. Then he imagines her side: she must be frowning too, sleeping alone, often standing lost in thought. Writing from the other person's side turns the longing into something they share. The last lines are the most moving. How can he bring her back, hide her in the little hall deep in a mist of flowers, and keep her there until they are old? The words are plain and as stubborn as a child's, yet they are very true.

上片从醒来写起：梦刚醒，天阴着，晨光里飘着小雨。竹栏杆有点冷，兰叶沙沙响，新绿一直铺到南边的水边。南浦是古人送别的地方，绿色通到那里，好像心思也跟着飘到了离别的地方。院子里没人，门只开了一半，他什么也不想做，只是望着远方，可连信都指望不上。下片先回忆：走在长满青苔的小路上，想起当年一起荡秋千，现在不知道谁陪她了。接着他换到她那边去想：她大概也皱着眉，一个人睡，常常站着发呆。这是从对方写起的写法，让思念变成了两个人的。最后几句最动人：怎样才能让她回来，藏在花雾深处的小屋里，留住她，一直到老也不让她走。话很直白，像孩子一样任性，却特别真。
