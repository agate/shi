To the Tune Jiang Cheng Zi: Hunting at Mizhou

 

lǎo老fū夫liáo聊fā发shào少nián年kuáng狂

zuǒ左qiān牵huáng黄

yòu右qíng擎cāng苍

jǐn锦mào帽diāo貂qiú裘

qiān千qí骑juǎn卷píng平gāng冈

wèi为bào报qīng倾chéng城suí随tài太shǒu守

qīn亲shè射hǔ虎

kàn看sūn孙láng郎

jiǔ酒hān酣xiōng胸dǎn胆shàng尚kāi开zhāng张

bìn鬓wēi微shuāng霜

yòu又hé何fáng妨

chí持jié节yún云zhōng中

hé何rì日qiǎn遣féng冯táng唐

huì会wǎn挽diāo雕gōng弓rú如mǎn满yuè月

xī西běi北wàng望

shè射tiān天láng狼

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 老夫聊发少年狂This old man will let out a young man's wild spirit for once:我这老头子暂且也来发一发年轻人的豪情，
  2. 左牵黄a yellow dog on a leash in my left hand,左手牵着黄狗，
  3. 右擎苍a grey hawk on my right arm.右臂托着苍鹰。
  4. 锦帽貂裘My men in brocade hats and sable coats,随从们戴着锦帽，穿着貂皮衣，
  5. 千骑卷平冈a thousand riders sweep across the flat hills like the wind.上千人马像风一样卷过平坦的山冈。
  6. 为报倾城随太守To thank the whole town for following their governor out,为了报答全城百姓跟着我出来看打猎，
  7. 亲射虎I will shoot the tiger myself,我要亲自射杀猛虎，
  8. 看孙郎and let them see a hero like Sun Quan.让大家看看像孙权那样的英姿。
  9. 酒酣胸胆尚开张Warm with wine, my heart grows bolder and wider still.酒喝得正痛快，胸怀更加开阔，胆气更加豪壮。
  10. 鬓微霜So my temples are going white,两鬓已经有些白了，
  11. 又何妨what does it matter?那又有什么关系！
  12. 持节云中When will the court send someone with the emperor's token,朝廷什么时候能像汉文帝派冯唐拿着符节去云中那样，
  13. 何日遣冯唐as the Han emperor once sent Feng Tang to Yunzhong, to call me back to service?派人来重新信任我、起用我呢？
  14. 会挽雕弓如满月Then I will draw my carved bow round as a full moon,到那时我一定把雕弓拉得像满月一样圆，
  15. 西北望look to the northwest,朝着西北方向望去，
  16. 射天狼and shoot down the Wolf Star of the invaders.射向那象征侵略者的天狼星。
02

Words字词注释

老夫lǎo fū
lǎo fū: this old man, Su Shi calling himself. He was actually only thirty-nine.老夫：老头子，苏轼自称。那年他其实才三十九岁。
聊发liáo fā
liáo fā: liáo means "just for now"; liáo fā: to let out for a while.聊：姑且，暂且。聊发：暂且发一发。
少年狂shào nián kuáng
shào nián kuáng: the wild high spirits of a young man.少年狂：年轻人的豪迈劲儿。
牵黄qiān huáng
qiān huáng: leading a yellow hunting dog.黄：黄狗。牵黄：牵着黄狗。
擎苍qíng cāng
qíng cāng: qíng means to hold up; cāng is a grey hawk. Holding a hunting hawk on his arm.擎：举。苍：苍鹰，一种猎鹰。擎苍：手臂上架着猎鹰。
锦帽貂裘jǐn mào diāo qiú
jǐn mào diāo qiú: a hat of brocade silk and a coat of sable fur, the outfit of a hunting party.锦帽貂裘：戴着锦缎做的帽子，穿着貂皮做的衣服，是打猎的装束。
千骑qiān qí
qiān qí: a thousand riders, meaning a very large crowd on horseback.千骑：一千个骑马的人，形容跟随的人马很多。
卷平冈juǎn píng gāng
juǎn píng gāng: sweeping over the flat hills like a gust of wind.卷平冈：像风卷过一样，飞快地越过平坦的山冈。
为报wèi bào
wèi bào: to repay, to thank.为报：为了报答。
倾城qīng chéng
qīng chéng: the whole town turning out.倾城：全城的人都出来了。
太守tài shǒu
tài shǒu: the governor of a prefecture, here Su Shi himself.太守：一个州郡的长官，这里是苏轼自己。
孙郎sūn láng
sūn láng: Young Master Sun: Sun Quan of the state of Wu in the Three Kingdoms, who once shot a tiger himself from horseback. Su Shi compares himself to him.孙郎：三国时吴国的孙权。他曾经亲自骑马射虎。苏轼以孙权自比。
酒酣jiǔ hān
jiǔ hān: when the wine has warmed him up.酒酣：酒喝得正痛快的时候。
胸胆尚开张xiōng dǎn shàng kāi zhāng
xiōng dǎn shàng kāi zhāng: his heart is open and his courage high. Shàng here means "all the more".胸胆尚开张：胸怀开阔，胆气豪壮。尚，更加。
微霜wēi shuāng
wēi shuāng: turning a little white.微霜：微微有些白了。
何妨hé fáng
hé fáng: what does it matter?何妨：有什么关系呢？
持节云中chí jié yún zhōng
chí jié yún zhōng: jié is a tally, a token the emperor gave a messenger to prove he spoke for the emperor. Yúnzhōng was a frontier region in the Han dynasty, in today's Inner Mongolia.节：符节，皇帝派使者时给的凭证。云中：汉代郡名，在今内蒙古一带。
遣冯唐qiǎn féng táng
qiǎn féng táng: to send Feng Tang. Under Emperor Wen of Han, Wei Shang, governor of Yunzhong, lost his post over a small fault. Feng Tang spoke up for him, so the emperor sent Feng Tang with the tally to pardon Wei Shang and give him his job back. Su Shi uses this story to hope the court will trust him and use him again.遣冯唐：派冯唐去。汉文帝时，云中太守魏尚因为小错被免职，冯唐替他说话，文帝就派冯唐拿着符节去赦免魏尚，让他官复原职。苏轼用这个故事，希望朝廷也能信任、重用自己。
会huì
huì: will, one day.会：将要，总有一天会。
挽雕弓如满月wǎn diāo gōng rú mǎn yuè
wǎn diāo gōng rú mǎn yuè: wǎn is to draw; a carved bow drawn so far it is round like a full moon.挽：拉。雕弓：刻着花纹的弓。如满月：把弓拉得像圆圆的满月。
天狼tiān láng
tiān láng: the Wolf Star (Sirius). People in old times linked it with invaders. Here it stands for Western Xia, which was raiding the Song border in the northwest (some say it also means the Liao state in the north).天狼：星名，古人认为它代表侵略。这里比喻当时在西北边境侵扰宋朝的西夏（一说也包括北方的辽）。
03

How to read it读音

骑qí
Read qí under today's standard pronunciation. In the past, when it meant "one rider and one horse", it was read jì, and many teachers and older editions still read it that way.现在按普通话审音读 qí。过去指“一人一马”时读 jì，很多老师和旧注本仍读 jì。
为wèi
Read wèi here, meaning "for the sake of".这里读 wèi，意思是“为了”。
看kàn
Read kàn today. The tune needs a level tone here, and in old times this character could also be said kān.现在读 kàn。按词的格律这里应是平声，古代“看”也可以读平声 kān。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric was written in the winter of 1075, when Su Shi was governor of Mizhou, today's Zhucheng in Shandong. He disagreed with the reforms led by Wang Anshi, so he had been sent away from the capital to work in the provinces, and felt a little let down. Once he went hunting outside the town with his officials, and the whole town came out to watch. In high spirits, he wrote this lyric. The Song border in the northwest was often raided by Western Xia, and he wanted to serve his country there.

这首词作于宋神宗熙宁八年（1075）冬天，苏轼当时在密州（今山东诸城）做知州。那时他和主持变法的王安石意见不同，被派到地方做官，心里有些失意。一次他和同僚出城打猎，全城百姓都跟来看热闹。他兴致很高，写下了这首词。北宋西北边境常受西夏侵扰，他也想为国家出力。

05

The poet作者

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Dongpo, came from Meishan in Sichuan and was a great writer of the Northern Song. Before him, lyrics were mostly about love and sorrow. Su Shi brought big subjects like hunting and serving the country into lyrics, and started the bold, free style. This hunting lyric is one of his first great works in that style.

苏轼（1037—1101），号东坡居士，眉州眉山（今属四川）人，北宋大文学家。以前的词大多写儿女情长，苏轼却把打猎、报国这样的大事写进词里，开创了豪放的风格。这首《密州出猎》就是他早期豪放词的代表作。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is like a lively hunting film. A governor who calls himself "this old man" leads a dog in one hand and holds a hawk on the other arm. A thousand riders follow, rushing over the hills like the wind. The whole town watches, and he is ready to shoot a tiger himself, like Sun Quan. The second stanza turns from the hunt to what is in his heart. With wine in him he is bolder still, and white hair does not bother him. He thinks of the story of Feng Tang and hopes the court will trust him again one day. At the end he draws his bow round as a full moon and aims northwest at the Wolf Star. What he really wants is to go to war and guard the border. The whole lyric rings loud and wide, and it stirs the blood.

上阕像一部热闹的打猎电影。一个自称“老夫”的太守，左手牵狗，右手架鹰，后面跟着上千人马，像风一样冲过山冈。全城百姓都来看，他就要像孙权一样亲手射虎。下阕从打猎转到心事。喝了酒，胆气更大，头发白了也不在乎。他想起冯唐的故事，盼着朝廷有一天重新信任他。最后他把雕弓拉成满月，朝西北射向天狼星，其实是想上战场保卫边疆。整首词声音响亮，画面开阔，读起来让人热血沸腾。
