On the Set Theme "Evening Rain", Seeing Off Li Zhou

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 楚江微雨里The Yangtze lies wrapped in fine rain,长江笼罩在蒙蒙细雨里，
  2. 建业暮钟时just as the evening bells ring out in Jianye.正是建业城响起晚钟的时候。
  3. 漠漠帆来重Through the mist come sails, heavy with rain;雨雾迷蒙，船帆湿透了，慢慢驶来；
  4. 冥冥鸟去迟in the dark sky the birds fly slowly away.天色昏暗，鸟儿飞得很迟缓。
  5. 海门深不见The river's mouth at the sea is too deep and far to see;长江入海的地方太深太远，看不见；
  6. 浦树远含滋the trees on the far shore drip with moisture.远处江边的树木，湿漉漉地含着水汽。
  7. 相送情无限Seeing you off, my feelings have no end;送别朋友，我的情意没有尽头，
  8. 沾襟比散丝tears wet my robe, like these scattered threads of rain.眼泪打湿衣襟，就像这纷乱的雨丝。
02

Words字词注释

楚江chǔ jiāng
chǔ jiāng: the Yangtze River. The Nanjing area once belonged to the old state of Chu, hence the name.楚江：长江。南京一带古时属于楚国，所以这样叫。
微雨wēi yǔ
wēi yǔ: light rain, drizzle.微雨：小雨，毛毛雨。
建业jiàn yè
jiàn yè: today's Nanjing in Jiangsu. It was called Jianye when it was the capital of Wu in the Three Kingdoms period.建业：今天的江苏南京，三国时吴国的都城叫建业。
暮钟mù zhōng
mù zhōng: the evening bell rung at temples.暮钟：傍晚寺庙里敲的钟。
漠漠mò mò
mò mò: misty and blurred, hard to see through.漠漠：雨雾迷蒙、看不清的样子。
帆来重fān lái zhòng
fān lái zhòng: the sails come in heavy, soaked with rain, and the boats move slowly.帆来重：船帆被雨打湿，显得沉重，船走得很慢。
冥冥míng míng
míng míng: dark and dim.冥冥：天色昏暗的样子。
鸟去迟niǎo qù chí
niǎo qù chí: the birds fly off slowly, their wings wet with rain.鸟去迟：鸟的翅膀沾了雨，飞得很慢。
海门hǎi mén
hǎi mén: the Sea Gate, where the Yangtze flows into the sea.海门：长江入海的地方。
浦树pǔ shù
pǔ shù: trees on the riverbank. Pǔ means a shore.浦树：江边的树。浦，水边。
含滋hán zī
hán zī: holding moisture, soaked and dripping.含滋：含着水汽，湿润润的。
沾襟zhān jīn
zhān jīn: tears wetting the front of the robe. Jīn is the front of a garment.沾襟：泪水打湿了衣襟。襟，衣服胸前的部分。
比散丝bǐ sàn sī
bǐ sàn sī: like scattered threads, meaning threads of rain. The Western Jin poet Zhang Xie once wrote, "thick rain like scattered silk."比散丝：好比散乱的丝线，指雨丝。西晋张协写过“密雨如散丝”。
03

Background创作背景

"Fu de" was a way of writing poems in old times. At a gathering, a theme was set first, and then everyone wrote a poem on it. Here the theme was "Evening Rain", and the poem was also used to see off a friend, Li Zhou. Some editions give the friend's name as Li Cao. The poem is set on the Yangtze at Jianye, today's Nanjing. We no longer know who Li Zhou was, or in what year the poem was written.

“赋得”是古人的一种写诗方式：大家聚会时先定一个题目，再按题目写诗。这首诗的题目就是“暮雨”，又用来送别朋友李胄。题目中的人名一作“李曹”。诗里写的是建业，也就是今天南京一带的长江边。李胄是谁、诗写于哪一年，现在都不清楚。

04

The poet作者

Wei Yingwu (about 737–about 791) came from Chang'an. As a young man he was a palace guard and lived wildly. Later he threw himself into study and served as governor of Chuzhou, Jiangzhou and Suzhou, so he is also called Wei Suzhou. His poems are calm and quiet, and he was very good at painting landscapes in words.

韦应物（约737—约791），长安人，唐代诗人。他年轻时在宫里做侍卫，很放纵；后来发愤读书，做过滁州、江州、苏州的刺史，所以人们也叫他“韦苏州”。他的诗清淡安静，很会写山水景物。

05

The mood意境

The theme of this poem is "Evening Rain", so nearly every line is about rain, yet the word itself hardly appears. The first line gives the drizzle on the river, and the second gives the evening bell, setting the time and place at once. Lines three and four are the cleverest. The sails are "heavy" because they are wet, and the birds are "slow" because they are wet. Rain is never named, but it is everywhere. Lines five and six look into the distance. The river mouth cannot be seen, and the far trees drip. Everything is blurry, like the empty feeling of saying goodbye. Only the last two lines speak of the parting: my tears mix with the threads of rain, and you cannot tell which is which.

这首诗的题目是“暮雨”，所以几乎每一句都在写雨，却很少直接用“雨”字。第一句是江上的小雨，第二句是傍晚的钟声，一下子把时间和地点都交代了。三四句最妙：船帆因为湿了才“重”，鸟因为湿了才“迟”，没说雨，却处处是雨。五六句把眼光放远：江口看不见，远树湿漉漉的，一片迷茫，就像离别时心里的空落。最后两句才说到送别：我的眼泪和雨丝混在一起，分不清哪是雨，哪是泪。
