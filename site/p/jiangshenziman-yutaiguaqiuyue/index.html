jiāng江shén神zǐ子màn慢·yù玉tái台guà挂qiū秋yuè月

tián田wéi为Tian Wei · Song宋

Jiang Shen Zi Man: An Autumn Moon Hangs Over the Jade Stand

 

yù玉tái台guà挂qiū秋yuè月

qiān铅sù素qiǎn浅

méi梅huā花fù傅xiāng香xuě雪

bīng冰zī姿jié洁

jīn金lián莲chèn衬

xiǎo小xiǎo小líng凌bō波luó罗wà袜

yǔ雨chū初xiē歇

lóu楼wài外gū孤hóng鸿shēng声jiàn渐yuǎn远

yuǎn远shān山wài外

xíng行rén人yīn音xìn信jué绝

cǐ此hèn恨duì对yǔ语yóu犹nán难

nǎ那kān堪gèng更jì寄shū书shuō说

jiào教rén人hóng红xiāo销cuì翠jiǎn减

jué觉yī衣kuān宽jīn金lǚ缕

dōu都wèi为qīng轻bié别

tài太qíng情qiè切

xiāo消hún魂chù处

huà画jiǎo角huáng黄hūn昏shí时jié节

shēng声wū呜yè咽

luò落jìn尽tíng庭huā花chūn春qù去yě也

yín银chán蟾jiǒng迥

wú无qíng情yuán圆yòu又quē缺

In the second-to-last line the corpus uses an old form of the character yú (lingering); the usual simplified text uses the common form, followed here. The tune name is also known as Jiang Cheng Zi Man. The lyric has no title, so its first line is used.

语料库最后一句写作“馀香”，“馀”是“余”的旧写法，通行本简体作“余香”，这里用“余”。语料库词牌作“江神子慢”，又名“江城子慢”。原词没有题目，这里用首句作题。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 玉台挂秋月On the jade dressing stand the round mirror hangs like an autumn moon.玉梳妆台上的圆镜，像挂着一轮秋月。
  2. 铅素浅Her face has only a light touch of powder,她脸上淡淡地搽着粉，
  3. 梅花傅香雪and her face is like plum blossom dusted with fragrant snow.脸庞像梅花敷上了香雪。
  4. 冰姿洁She looks as pure as ice.姿容像冰一样洁白。
  5. 金莲衬Her small feet are set off小脚穿着
  6. 小小凌波罗袜by tiny silk stockings, and she walks as lightly as over waves.小巧的丝袜，走起来轻盈得像踏着水波。
  7. 雨初歇The rain has just stopped.雨刚刚停了。
  8. 楼外孤鸿声渐远Outside the tower, the cry of a lone goose fades away;楼外一只孤雁的叫声越来越远，
  9. 远山外beyond the far hills,在远山的那一边，
  10. 行人音信绝the traveler has sent no word at all.远行的人一点消息也没有了。
  11. 此恨对语犹难This sorrow is hard to speak even face to face,这份愁苦，就算面对面也难说出口，
  12. 那堪更寄书说so how could it be put into a letter and sent?哪里还能写成书信寄过去说清楚？
  13. 教人红销翠减It has made my looks fade and wither;让我脸色憔悴，容颜消减，
  14. 觉衣宽金缕I find my gold-threaded dress has grown loose,只觉得金线绣的衣服都变宽了，
  15. 都为轻别all because we parted so lightly.全是因为当初轻易地分别。
  16. 太情切The feeling is too deep, too urgent.这份感情太深太急切。
  17. 消魂处The saddest moment of all最伤心的时候，
  18. 画角黄昏时节is at dusk, when the painted horn is blown,是黄昏画角吹响的时候，
  19. 声呜咽its sound like sobbing.那声音像在哭泣。
  20. 落尽庭花春去也The courtyard flowers have all fallen; spring is gone.院子里的花都落光了，春天走了。
  21. 银蟾迥The silver moon hangs far away,月亮高高地挂在远处，
  22. 无情圆又缺heartlessly growing full and then waning again.无情地圆了又缺。
  23. 恨伊不似余香I resent him, for he is not like the lingering scent我恨他，不像那留下的香气，
  24. 惹鸳鸯结that still clings to the lovers' knot.还能缠在鸳鸯结上不走。
02

Words字词注释

玉台挂秋月yù tái guà qiū yuè
yù tái guà qiū yuè: a round mirror hangs on the jade dressing stand, like an autumn moon. Another view: it compares the woman's face to the bright autumn moon.玉台挂秋月：玉做的梳妆台上挂着圆镜，像一轮秋月。一说是比喻女子的脸像秋月一样明亮。
铅素浅qiān sù qiǎn
qiān sù qiǎn: only a light layer of white face powder. Qiān was a white powder used for makeup in old times.铅素浅：脸上只搽了淡淡的白粉。铅，古代化妆用的白粉。
傅fù
fù: to put on, to dab on.傅：同“敷”，搽、抹。
梅花傅香雪méi huā fù xiāng xuě
méi huā fù xiāng xuě: her face is like plum blossom dusted with fragrant snow, white and sweet. Another view: it means plum-blossom makeup painted on her brow.梅花傅香雪：她的脸像梅花上敷了一层香香的白雪，又白又香。一说指额上画的梅花妆。
冰姿bīng zī
bīng zī: a look as pure and white as ice.冰姿：像冰一样洁白的样子。
金莲jīn lián
jīn lián: golden lotus, an old name for a woman's small feet.金莲：古人称女子的小脚。
凌波líng bō
líng bō: stepping lightly over the waves, describing a woman's graceful walk. It comes from Cao Zhi's "Rhapsody on the Goddess of the Luo River".凌波：在水波上轻轻走，形容女子步子轻盈。出自曹植《洛神赋》“凌波微步，罗袜生尘”。
罗袜luó wà
luó wà: silk stockings.罗袜：丝织的袜子。
孤鸿gū hóng
gū hóng: a lone wild goose. People of old said wild geese could carry letters.孤鸿：一只孤单的大雁。古人说大雁能传书信。
行人xíng rén
xíng rén: a person on a long journey, here the one she misses.行人：出远门的人，指她想念的人。
音信绝yīn xìn jué
yīn xìn jué: no news at all.音信绝：一点消息也没有了。
对语犹难duì yǔ yóu nán
duì yǔ yóu nán: even face to face it would be hard to say.对语犹难：就算面对面，也很难说清楚。
那堪nǎ kān
nǎ kān: how could one, how could one bear to.那堪：怎么能够、哪里受得了。
红销翠减hóng xiāo cuì jiǎn
hóng xiāo cuì jiǎn: red and green both fading, meaning she grows thin and loses her looks.红销翠减：红色和绿色都褪了，比喻人憔悴、不再美丽。
衣宽金缕yī kuān jīn lǚ
yī kuān jīn lǚ: her gold-threaded dress has grown loose, showing she has grown thin.衣宽金缕：金线绣的衣服变宽松了，说明人瘦了。
轻别qīng bié
qīng bié: parting too easily.轻别：轻易地分别。
情切qíng qiè
qíng qiè: deep and urgent feeling.情切：感情深，心里急切。
消魂xiāo hún
xiāo hún: so sad it feels as if your soul is melting away.消魂：伤心得像魂都没了。
画角huà jiǎo
huà jiǎo: a painted horn blown in army camps in old times, often at dusk.画角：古代军中吹的号角，外面有彩绘，傍晚会吹响。
呜咽wū yè
wū yè: a low sound like sobbing.呜咽：低低的、像哭一样的声音。
银蟾yín chán
yín chán: the moon. Legend says a toad lives in the moon, so it was called the silver toad.银蟾：月亮。传说月亮里有蟾蜍，所以这样叫。
迥jiǒng
jiǒng: far away.迥：遥远。
伊yī
yī: he, the one she misses.伊：他，指她想念的人。
余香yú xiāng
yú xiāng: lingering fragrance.余香：留下来的香气。
鸳鸯结yuān yāng jié
yuān yāng jié: a paired knot, a sign of lovers or a couple staying together.鸳鸯结：一种成对打的结，象征夫妻、恋人在一起。
03

How to read it读音

那nǎ
Here nǎ means "how", the same as the word for "which", and is usually read nǎ.“那堪”的“那”同“哪”，意思是“怎么”，一般读 nǎ。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about a woman missing her beloved far away. When it was written and what happened are not known. The poet, Tian Wei, was a musician at the end of the Northern Song. He served in the Dasheng Office, which ran music at court, so his lyrics pay close attention to sound and tune. It was later chosen for the "Three Hundred Song Lyrics".

这首词写一位女子思念远方的心上人。写作的时间和具体的事情都不清楚。作者田为是北宋末年的音乐家，在朝廷管音乐的大晟府任职，所以他的词很讲究音律。这首词后来被选进《宋词三百首》。

05

The poet作者

Tian Wei, whose courtesy name was Bufa, lived at the end of the Northern Song; his birth and death years are unknown. He was skilled at the pipa and knew music well, and held a music post in the Dasheng Office under Emperor Huizong. Few of his lyrics survive. Most are about lovers missing each other, with delicate words and smooth music.

田为，字不伐，北宋末年人，生卒年不详。他擅长弹琵琶，懂音乐，在宋徽宗时的大晟府做过管音乐的官。他留下来的词不多，大多写男女相思，语言细腻、音调和谐。

06

The mood意境

The first half paints a beautiful woman: a mirror like the autumn moon, light makeup, a face like plum blossom in snow, and a light step. Yet this lovely person is lonely. The rain stops, the lone goose flies off, and there is no word from the one far away. There is so much in her heart that it would be hard to say face to face, and even harder to write. The second half tells how missing him has made her thin, so her dress hangs loose. At dusk the horn sounds like weeping, the flowers fall, spring leaves, and the moon waxes and wanes, but he never comes back. The last line is special. She resents him for being worse than the scent left on her clothes, for at least the scent still clings to the lovers' knot. By comparing a person to a wisp of fragrance, it shows the feeling of missing and blaming someone at once, truly and finely.

上半首先画出一位美丽的女子：明镜像秋月，淡淡的妆，脸像带雪的梅花，脚步轻盈。可是这么美的人却很孤单。雨停了，孤雁飞远了，远方的人一点消息都没有。心里的话太多，见面都说不清，写信更说不出。下半首写她因为思念变得消瘦，衣服都宽了。黄昏的画角声像哭，花落了，春天走了，月亮圆了又缺，就是不见人回来。最后一句很特别：她恨那个人，还不如衣服上留下的香气，至少香气还肯缠在鸳鸯结上。用一缕香来比人，把“想念又埋怨”的心情写得又真又细。
