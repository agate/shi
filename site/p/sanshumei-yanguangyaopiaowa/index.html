San Shu Mei: Misty Light Shimmers on the Blue Tiles

Our source gives only the tune name, San Shu Mei. Here the first line is added as the title.

底本题目只有词牌“三姝媚”，这里用首句作题目。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 烟光摇缥瓦Misty light shimmers on the pale blue tiles.阳光透过薄雾，在青白的琉璃瓦上闪动，
  2. 望晴檐多风Under the eaves on this clear day, the wind blows often,晴天的屋檐下风很多，
  3. 柳花如洒and willow fluff flies everywhere as if sprinkled down.柳絮像洒下来一样到处飞。
  4. 锦瑟横床A fine se lies across the bed.华美的锦瑟横放在床上，
  5. 想泪痕尘影I imagine it is marked with tears and covered in dust,想来上面满是泪痕和灰尘，
  6. 凤弦常下its strings often loose, with no one playing them.琴弦常常松着，没人去弹。
  7. 倦出犀帷She is too weary to come out from behind her curtain,她懒得走出帐子，
  8. 频梦见王孙骄马but she often dreams of me coming back on a proud horse.却常常梦见我骑着骏马回来。
  9. 讳道相思She will not admit how much she misses me,她不肯说出自己的相思，
  10. 偷理绡裙but as she quietly smooths her silk skirt,偷偷整理薄绸的裙子，
  11. 自惊腰衩she is startled to find it loose at the waist.才吃惊地发现裙腰已经松了。
  12. 惆怅南楼遥夜What saddens me most is a long night in the south tower.最叫人难过的是南楼那漫长的夜，
  13. 记翠箔张灯I remember the lamps lit behind the green curtain,我还记得翠帘里点着灯，
  14. 枕肩歌罢and how she leaned on my shoulder when her song was done.她靠着我的肩膀，唱完了歌。
  15. 又入铜驼Now I come again to the busy streets of the capital,如今我又来到京城繁华的街上，
  16. 遍旧家门巷and walk through every lane by her old door,走遍她旧日住的门巷，
  17. 首询声价asking first of all for news of her.先打听她的消息。
  18. 可惜东风Alas, the spring wind was cruel:可惜春风无情，
  19. 将恨与闲花俱谢she faded away with the fallen flowers, taking her regret with her.她带着遗憾，和落花一起凋谢了。
  20. 记取崔徽模样I will hold on to her face, as lovely as Cui Hui's,我要牢牢记住她像崔徽一样的模样，
  21. 归来暗写and when I get home, I will paint it in secret.回去以后偷偷把她画下来。
02

Words字词注释

烟光yān guāng
yān guāng: sunlight shining through light mist.烟光：阳光照在薄雾上的光彩。
缥瓦piǎo wǎ
piǎo wǎ: pale blue-white glazed tiles.缥瓦：青白色的琉璃瓦。
晴檐qíng yán
qíng yán: the eaves on a clear day.晴檐：晴天里的屋檐。
柳花如洒liǔ huā rú sǎ
liǔ huā rú sǎ: willow fluff blown about as if sprinkled down.柳花如洒：柳絮被风吹得像洒下来的一样。
锦瑟jǐn sè
jǐn sè: a finely decorated se, a plucked instrument with many strings.锦瑟：装饰华美的瑟。瑟，一种有很多根弦的弹拨乐器。
泪痕尘影lèi hén chén yǐng
lèi hén chén yǐng: tear stains and dust on the instrument.泪痕尘影：瑟上留着泪痕，落满了灰尘。
凤弦常下fèng xián cháng xià
fèng xián cháng xià: the strings are often left loose and unplayed. Fèng xián, phoenix strings, is a fine name for an instrument's strings.凤弦常下：瑟弦常常松着放下，不再弹了。凤弦，指琴瑟的弦。
犀帷xī wéi
xī wéi: a bed curtain decorated with rhinoceros horn.犀帷：用犀角做装饰的帐子。
王孙骄马wáng sūn jiāo mǎ
wáng sūn jiāo mǎ: a young lord on a proud horse. Here it means the man she misses, that is, the poet himself.王孙骄马：贵公子骑着高大神气的马。这里指她思念的人，也就是词人自己。
讳道相思huì dào xiāng sī
huì dào xiāng sī: refusing to admit that she is pining. Huì means to avoid saying.讳道相思：不肯说出自己在想念人。讳，避开不说。
绡裙xiāo qún
xiāo qún: a skirt of thin silk.绡裙：薄绸做的裙子。
自惊腰衩zì jīng yāo chà
zì jīng yāo chà: she is startled to find her skirt loose at the waist. It means she has grown thin.自惊腰衩：自己吃惊地发现，裙腰变宽松了，说明人瘦了。
遥夜yáo yè
yáo yè: a long night.遥夜：长夜。
翠箔cuì bó
cuì bó: a green curtain.翠箔：翠绿色的帘子。
枕肩歌罢zhěn jiān gē bà
zhěn jiān gē bà: she leaned on my shoulder and finished her song.枕肩歌罢：她靠在我肩上，唱完了歌。
铜驼tóng tuó
tóng tuó: Bronze Camel Street, a busy street in old Luoyang. Here it stands for the busy streets of the capital.铜驼：洛阳有铜驼街，是热闹的街市。这里借指京城繁华的街道。
首询声价shǒu xún shēng jià
shǒu xún shēng jià: first asking about her. Shēng jià means fame and standing; here it means how she is now.首询声价：先打听她的消息。声价，名声和身价，这里指她的近况。
将恨与闲花俱谢jiāng hèn yǔ xián huā jù xiè
jiāng hèn yǔ xián huā jù xiè: she withered away with the fallen flowers, carrying her regret. That is, she has died.将恨与闲花俱谢：她带着满心的遗憾，和落花一起凋谢了，也就是说她已经去世。
崔徽cuī huī
cuī huī: Cui Hui, a Tang singing girl. After parting from her love, she had a painter make her portrait and sent it to him. Later she died of longing.崔徽：唐代的一位歌女，和心上人分别后，请画师画了自己的像寄给他，后来因为相思病死了。
暗写àn xiě
àn xiě: to draw in secret. Here xiě means to paint a portrait.暗写：偷偷地画下来。写，这里是画像的意思。
03

How to read it读音

缥piǎo
In the word for "misty, faint" this character is read piāo. Here it means pale blue-white and is read piǎo.“缥”在“缥缈”里读 piāo；这里指青白色，读 piǎo。“缥瓦”是青白色的琉璃瓦。
衩chà
This character means the slit at the side of a garment and is read chà. It rhymes with the other line endings here.“衩”指衣服下边开口的地方，读 chà。这里也和“瓦、洒、下、马”等押韵。
模mú
In "mú yàng" (looks, appearance) the character is read mú.“模样”的“模”读 mú。
04

Background创作背景

We do not know when this lyric was written. It is usually read this way: the poet comes back to the capital to look for a singing girl he once loved, and learns that she has died of longing for him. The first stanza imagines how she wasted away after he left. The second stanza remembers the past and tells of his return. At the end, the story of Cui Hui shows how much he misses her.

这首词的写作时间不清楚。一般理解为：词人重回京城，去找从前相爱的一位歌女，却得知她因为思念他，已经病死了。上片想象她在他走后憔悴的样子，下片写回忆和重访，最后用崔徽的故事，表达对她的怀念。

05

The poet作者

Shi Dazu, courtesy name Bangqing, pen name Meixi, came from Bian (today's Kaifeng in Henan). He was a Southern Song lyric poet, and his dates are unknown. He worked as a clerk for the chief minister Han Tuozhou, and when Han fell, he was punished too. He was best at lyrics about objects; his lyric on a pair of swallows is famous. His style is delicate and finely crafted.

史达祖，字邦卿，号梅溪，汴（今河南开封）人，南宋词人，生卒年不详。他曾在宰相韩侂胄手下做文书，韩侂胄失败后，他也受到处罚。他最擅长写咏物词，《双双燕·咏燕》很有名。他的词细腻工巧。

06

The mood意境

In the first stanza the poet is imagining her. Willow fluff flies in the sunshine. Her se is covered in dust, its strings slack, because she has no heart to play. She is too tired to go out, yet in her dreams she always sees him riding home. She will not say she misses him. But when she smooths her skirt, she finds she has grown thin. One small detail tells all her longing. The second stanza returns to the poet. He remembers a night in the south tower, when she leaned on his shoulder and sang by lamplight, such a warm moment. But when he comes back to the capital and searches the lanes where she lived, he learns that she has faded away with the spring flowers. At the end he says he will hold on to her face and paint it in secret at home, like the story of Cui Hui. From imagining, to remembering, to the sad news, the feeling grows deeper at every step.

上片是词人在想象：晴天里柳絮乱飞，她的锦瑟落满灰尘，弦也松了，没心思弹。她懒得出门，梦里却总见他骑马回来。她嘴上不承认想他，可一整理裙子，才发现自己瘦了。一个细节就把相思写透了。下片回到词人自己。他记得南楼的夜晚，灯下她靠着他的肩唱歌，那是多么温暖的时刻。可当他再回到京城，走遍她住过的小巷去打听，才知道她已经和春天的落花一起凋谢了。最后他说，要记住她的样子，回去偷偷画下来，就像崔徽的故事一样。从想象到回忆，再到噩耗，感情一层比一层深。
