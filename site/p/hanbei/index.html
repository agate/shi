hán韩bēi碑

lǐ李shāng商yǐn隐Li Shangyin · Tang唐

Han Yu's Stele

 

yuán元hé和tiān天zǐ子shén神wǔ武zī姿

bǐ彼hé何rén人zāi哉xuān轩yǔ与xī羲

shì誓jiāng将shàng上xuě雪liè列shèng圣chǐ耻

zuò坐fǎ法gōng宫zhōng中cháo朝sì四yí夷

huái淮xī西yǒu有zéi贼wǔ五shí十zǎi载

fēng封láng狼shēng生chū䝙chū䝙shēng生pí罴

bú不jù据shān山hé河jù据píng平dì地

cháng长gē戈lì利máo矛rì日kě可huī麾

dì帝dé得shèng圣xiàng相xiàng相yuē曰dù度

zéi贼zhuó斫bù不sǐ死shén神fú扶chí持

yāo腰xuán悬xiàng相yìn印zuò作dū都tǒng统

yīn阴fēng风cǎn惨dàn澹tiān天wáng王qí旗

sù愬wǔ武gǔ古tōng通zuò作yá牙zhǎo爪

yí仪cáo曹wài外láng郎zài载bǐ笔suí随

xíng行jūn军sī司mǎ马zhì智qiě且yǒng勇

shí十sì四wàn万zhòng众yóu犹hǔ虎pí貔

rù入cài蔡fù缚zéi贼xiàn献tài太miào庙

gōng功wú无yǔ与ràng让ēn恩bù不zī訾

dì帝yuē曰rǔ汝dù度gōng功dì第yī一

rǔ汝cóng从shì事yù愈yí宜wéi为cí辞

yù愈bài拜qǐ稽shǒu首dǎo蹈qiě且wǔ舞

jīn金shí石kè刻huà画chén臣néng能wéi为

gǔ古zhě者shì世chēng称dà大shǒu手bǐ笔

cǐ此shì事bú不xì系yú于zhí职sī司

dāng当rén仁zì自gǔ古yǒu有bú不ràng让

yán言qì讫lǚ屡hàn颔tiān天zǐ子yí颐

gōng公tuì退zhāi斋jiè戒zuò坐xiǎo小gé阁

rú濡rǎn染dà大bǐ笔hé何lín淋lí漓

diǎn点cuàn窜yáo尧diǎn典shùn舜diǎn典zì字

tú涂gǎi改qīng清miào庙shēng生mín民shī诗

wén文chéng成pò破tǐ体shū书zài在zhǐ纸

qīng清chén晨zài再bài拜pū铺dān丹chí墀

biǎo表yuē曰chén臣yù愈mèi昧sǐ死shàng上

yǒng咏shén神shèng圣gōng功shū书zhī之bēi碑

bēi碑gāo高sān三zhàng丈zì字rú如dǒu斗

fù负yǐ以líng灵áo鳌pán蟠yǐ以chī螭

jù句qí奇yǔ语zhòng重yù喻zhě者shǎo少

chán谗zhī之tiān天zǐ子yán言qí其sī私

cháng长shéng绳bǎi百chǐ尺zhuài拽bēi碑dǎo倒

cū粗shā砂dà大shí石xiāng相mó磨zhì治

gōng公zhī之sī斯wén文ruò若yuán元qì气

xiān先shí时yǐ已rù入rén人gān肝pí脾

tāng汤pán盘kǒng孔dǐng鼎yǒu有shù述zuò作

jīn今wú无qí其qì器cún存qí其cí辞

wū呜hū呼shèng圣huáng皇jí及shèng圣xiàng相

xiāng相yǔ与xuǎn烜hè赫liú流chún淳xī熙

gōng公zhī之sī斯wén文bú不shì示hòu后

hé曷yǔ与sān三wǔ五xiāng相pān攀zhuī追

yuàn愿shū书wàn万běn本sòng诵wàn万guò过

kǒu口jiǎo角liú流mò沫yòu右shǒu手zhī胝

chuán传zhī之qī七shí十yòu有èr二dài代

yǐ以wéi为fēng封shàn禅yù玉jiǎn检míng明táng堂jī基

This is the text in the common editions of Three Hundred Tang Poems. Two characters, áo ("giant sea turtle") and cū ("coarse"), appear in the source in old forms and are written in today's standard forms here. The rare character chū, the name of a fierce beast, is kept as it is.

这里用的是通行本《唐诗三百首》的文字。原始文字里的“鼇”“麤”是旧写法，现在写作“鳌”“粗”。“䝙”是一种猛兽的名字，规范字就写作“䝙”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 元和天子神武姿The Yuanhe emperor was wise and brave, like a god.元和年间的皇帝英明勇武，像神一样，
  2. 彼何人哉轩与羲Who were the Yellow Emperor and Fuxi? He could stand beside them.古代的黄帝和伏羲又是什么人呢？他完全可以和他们相比。
  3. 誓将上雪列圣耻He swore to wipe away the shame of the emperors before him,他发誓要洗刷前面几代皇帝留下的耻辱，
  4. 坐法宫中朝四夷and to sit in his main hall as peoples from every side came to bow.坐在正殿里，让四方各族都来朝拜。
  5. 淮西有贼五十载For fifty years rebels had held Huaixi,淮西被叛贼占据了五十年，
  6. 封狼生䝙䝙生罴like a wolf that bore a chu, and a chu that bore a bear, each fiercer than the last.像大狼生䝙、䝙又生罴，一代比一代凶。
  7. 不据山河据平地They held no mountains or rivers, just ruled the open plain,他们不靠山河险要，就在平原上称霸，
  8. 长戈利矛日可麾and with long spears and sharp blades, thought they could wave the sun back.拿着长戈利矛，狂妄得以为能把太阳挥回去。
  9. 帝得圣相相曰度The emperor found a wise chief minister, named Pei Du.皇帝得到了一位贤明的宰相，名叫裴度，
  10. 贼斫不死神扶持Assassins cut him but he did not die, as if a god protected him.刺客砍他却没砍死，好像有神保佑。
  11. 腰悬相印作都统With the minister's seal at his waist, he became commander-in-chief,他腰挂宰相大印，当上了总指挥，
  12. 阴风惨澹天王旗and the emperor's banners flew in a cold, gloomy wind.冷风阴沉，皇家的大旗迎风飘扬。
  13. 愬武古通作牙爪Li Su, Han Gongwu, Li Daogu and Li Wentong were his fighting arms,李愬、韩公武、李道古、李文通是他得力的武将，
  14. 仪曹外郎载笔随and an official of the Ministry of Rites came along with brush in hand.礼部员外郎带着笔跟在军中。
  15. 行军司马智且勇The army's senior staff officer was clever and brave,行军司马又聪明又勇敢，
  16. 十四万众犹虎貔and the hundred and forty thousand soldiers were fierce as tigers.十四万大军像老虎和貔一样勇猛。
  17. 入蔡缚贼献太庙They broke into Caizhou, tied up the rebel and presented him at the Great Temple.他们攻进蔡州，捉住叛贼，献到太庙，
  18. 功无与让恩不訾No deed could match theirs, and the emperor's rewards were beyond counting.功劳大得没人比得上，皇帝的赏赐数也数不清。
  19. 帝曰汝度功第一The emperor said, "Pei Du, yours is the first deed,皇帝说：“裴度，你的功劳第一，
  20. 汝从事愈宜为辞so your officer Han Yu should write the words."你的部下韩愈应该来写碑文。”
  21. 愈拜稽首蹈且舞Han Yu knelt, bowed to the ground, and danced his thanks, saying:韩愈跪下磕头，又跳又舞地谢恩，说：
  22. 金石刻画臣能为"Writing to be carved on bronze and stone: this I can do.“刻在铜器石碑上的文章，我能写。
  23. 古者世称大手笔Since ancient times, people have called on the great writers,自古以来，人们都说要请大手笔来写，
  24. 此事不系于职司and the task does not depend on one's post.这件事不看担任什么官职。
  25. 当仁自古有不让When a thing is right to do, one has never stepped aside."遇到该做的事，自古就不推让。”
  26. 言讫屡颔天子颐When he finished, the emperor nodded again and again.他说完，皇帝连连点头。
  27. 公退斋戒坐小阁Han Yu went home, made himself clean, and sat in his small room,韩公回家后斋戒，坐在小屋里，
  28. 濡染大笔何淋漓soaked his big brush in ink, and how freely it flowed!把大笔蘸饱墨汁，写得多么酣畅！
  29. 点窜尧典舜典字He reshaped words from the chapters on Yao and Shun in the Book of Documents,他化用了《尚书》里《尧典》《舜典》的字句，
  30. 涂改清庙生民诗and reworked the poems "Pure Temple" and "Birth of the People" from the Book of Songs.改写了《诗经》里《清庙》《生民》的诗篇。
  31. 文成破体书在纸When the piece was done, in a style that broke the rules, he wrote it out on paper,文章写成，打破常规，写在纸上，
  32. 清晨再拜铺丹墀and early in the morning bowed again and laid it out on the red palace steps.清早再次下拜，把它摆在宫殿的红台阶前。
  33. 表曰臣愈昧死上His letter said, "Your servant Han Yu presents this at the risk of death:奏章上写着：“臣韩愈冒死呈上，
  34. 咏神圣功书之碑words praising Your Majesty's great deeds, to be carved on a stele."歌颂圣上的伟大功业，请刻在碑上。”
  35. 碑高三丈字如斗The stele was three zhang tall, each character as big as a grain bucket,石碑高三丈，字有斗那么大，
  36. 负以灵鳌蟠以螭carried on a sacred turtle and topped with coiled dragons.下面有神龟驮着，上面盘着螭龙。
  37. 句奇语重喻者少But its sentences were unusual and its words weighty, and few understood them.可是文章句子奇特、用词庄重，懂的人很少，
  38. 谗之天子言其私Someone slandered it to the emperor, saying it played favorites.有人在皇帝面前说坏话，说碑文偏心。
  39. 长绳百尺拽碑倒So with a rope a hundred feet long they dragged the stele down,于是用百尺长的绳子把碑拉倒，
  40. 粗砂大石相磨治and ground off its words with coarse sand and big stones.用粗砂大石把上面的字磨掉。
  41. 公之斯文若元气Yet Han Yu's writing was like the first energy of heaven and earth:可是韩公的文章像天地元气一样，
  42. 先时已入人肝脾long before, it had already sunk deep into people's hearts.早在这之前就深深印在人们心里了。
  43. 汤盘孔鼎有述作King Tang's washbasin and the Kong family's cauldron once bore carved words;商汤的浴盘、孔家的铜鼎都刻过文字，
  44. 今无其器存其辞the objects are gone today, but the words remain.现在器物没了，文字还在流传。
  45. 呜呼圣皇及圣相Ah, the wise emperor and the wise minister:唉，圣明的皇帝和贤明的宰相，
  46. 相与烜赫流淳熙together they won great glory and left a bright, pure legacy.一起建立了辉煌的功业，留下美好的影响。
  47. 公之斯文不示后If Han Yu's writing is not shown to those who come later,如果韩公的文章不能给后人看到，
  48. 曷与三五相攀追how can they stand beside the ancient sage kings?他们怎么能和三皇五帝并列呢？
  49. 愿书万本诵万过I wish to copy it ten thousand times and read it ten thousand times,我愿意把它抄一万份，读一万遍，
  50. 口角流沫右手胝until my mouth foams and my right hand grows calluses.读得嘴角冒沫，抄得右手长茧。
  51. 传之七十有二代Let it be handed down for seventy-two generations,让它传上七十二代，
  52. 以为封禅玉检明堂基to be the jade seal of the grand rites and the foundation stone of the Bright Hall.成为封禅大典的玉检和明堂的基石。
02

Words字词注释

元和天子yuán hé tiān zǐ
yuán hé tiān zǐ: Emperor Xianzong of Tang. Yuanhe (806–820) was the name of his reign.元和天子：唐宪宗。元和是他的年号（806—820）。
神武姿shén wǔ zī
shén wǔ zī: a look as wise and brave as a god.神武姿：像神一样英明勇武的样子。
彼何人哉bǐ hé rén zāi
bǐ hé rén zāi: what kind of men were they? Meaning Xianzong could stand beside them.彼何人哉：他们是什么样的人呢？意思是宪宗可以和他们相比。
轩与羲xuān yǔ xī
xuān yǔ xī: Xuanyuan (the Yellow Emperor) and Fuxi, wise rulers of legend.轩与羲：轩辕（黄帝）和伏羲，传说中上古的圣明帝王。
上雪列圣耻shàng xuě liè shèng chǐ
shàng xuě liè shèng chǐ: to wipe away the shame left by the emperors before him. Xuě, "snow", here means to wash clean.上雪列圣耻：洗刷前面几代皇帝留下的耻辱。雪，洗掉。列圣，历代先帝。
法宫fǎ gōng
fǎ gōng: the main hall where the emperor ran the government.法宫：皇帝处理朝政的正殿。
朝四夷cháo sì yí
cháo sì yí: to have peoples from every side come and bow to him.朝四夷：让四方各族都来朝拜。
淮西huái xī
huái xī: the Huaixi region, in today's southeast Henan. Its warlords refused the court's orders for many years.淮西：唐代的淮西镇，在今天河南东南部一带，那里的军阀长期不听朝廷的命令。
有贼五十载yǒu zéi wǔ shí zǎi
yǒu zéi wǔ shí zǎi: rebels had held it for about fifty years.有贼五十载：叛贼占据了五十年左右。
封狼生䝙䝙生罴fēng láng shēng chū chū shēng pí
fēng láng shēng chū chū shēng pí: the great wolf gave birth to a chu, and the chu to a bear. Chu and pí are fierce beasts. It means the rebel leaders followed one after another, each fiercer than the last.封狼生䝙䝙生罴：大狼生出䝙，䝙又生出罴。封狼，大狼。䝙、罴，都是猛兽。比喻淮西的叛将一代接一代，越来越凶。
不据山河据平地bú jù shān hé jù píng dì
bù jù shān hé jù píng dì: they did not hold mountains and rivers for defense, but took over the open plain.不据山河据平地：不是靠着高山大河的险要，而是在平原上占地称霸。
长戈利矛cháng gē lì máo
cháng gē lì máo: long dagger-axes and sharp spears, old weapons.长戈利矛：长长的戈，锋利的矛，都是古代兵器。
日可麾rì kě huī
rì kě huī: in an old story, a warrior waved his dagger-axe and the setting sun moved back. Here it means the rebels were so proud they thought they could do anything. Huī means to wave.日可麾：传说古时有人打仗时挥动戈，让快落山的太阳退了回来。这里说叛军很狂妄，以为什么都能做到。麾，挥。
圣相shèng xiàng
shèng xiàng: a wise chief minister.圣相：贤明的宰相。
相曰度xiàng yuē dù
xiàng yuē dù: the minister was named Pei Du.相曰度：这个宰相名叫裴度。
贼斫不死zéi zhuó bù sǐ
zéi zhuó bù sǐ: in 815 assassins killed the chief minister Wu Yuanheng. Pei Du was also cut, but he did not die. Zhuó means to cut with a blade.贼斫不死：元和十年，刺客刺杀宰相武元衡，裴度也被砍伤，却没有死。斫，砍。
神扶持shén fú chí
shén fú chí: as if a god were protecting him.神扶持：好像有神在保佑他。
腰悬相印yāo xuán xiàng yìn
yāo xuán xiàng yìn: with the chief minister's seal hanging at his waist.腰悬相印：腰上挂着宰相的大印。
都统dū tǒng
dū tǒng: commander-in-chief over all the armies.都统：统率各路军队的总指挥。
阴风惨澹yīn fēng cǎn dàn
yīn fēng cǎn dàn: a cold wind under a gloomy sky.阴风惨澹：冷风吹着，天色阴沉。
天王旗tiān wáng qí
tiān wáng qí: the banner of the emperor's army.天王旗：皇帝军队的大旗。
愬武古通sù wǔ gǔ tōng
sù wǔ gǔ tōng: four generals: Li Su, Han Gongwu, Li Daogu and Li Wentong.愬武古通：四位将领：李愬、韩公武、李道古、李文通。
牙爪yá zhǎo
yá zhǎo: teeth and claws, that is, strong fighting officers. Here it is praise.牙爪：爪牙，就是得力的武将。这里是夸奖的话，说他们勇猛能干。
仪曹外郎yí cáo wài láng
yí cáo wài láng: an official in the Ministry of Rites, here Li Zongmin.仪曹外郎：礼部员外郎，一种官名，这里指李宗闵。
载笔随zài bǐ suí
zài bǐ suí: going along with the army, brush in hand, to write its papers.载笔随：带着笔跟随军队，负责写文书。
行军司马xíng jūn sī mǎ
xíng jūn sī mǎ: a senior staff officer of the army, here Han Yu.行军司马：军中的高级参谋官，这里指韩愈。
虎貔hǔ pí
hǔ pí: tigers and pí, fierce beasts, standing for brave soldiers.虎貔：老虎和貔，都是猛兽，比喻勇猛的士兵。
入蔡缚贼rù cài fù zéi
rù cài fù zéi: breaking into Caizhou and tying up the rebel leader Wu Yuanji. Caizhou was the main city of Huaixi.入蔡缚贼：攻进蔡州城，把叛军首领吴元济绑了起来。蔡，蔡州，淮西的中心城市。
献太庙xiàn tài miào
xiàn tài miào: presenting the captive at the Great Temple, where the emperor honored his ancestors.献太庙：把俘虏献到太庙。太庙，皇帝祭祀祖先的地方。
功无与让gōng wú yǔ ràng
gōng wú yǔ ràng: the deed was so great no one could match it, so there was no one to yield to.功无与让：功劳大，没有人比得上，用不着推让。
恩不訾ēn bù zī
ēn bù zī: the emperor's rewards were beyond counting. Zī means to count.恩不訾：皇帝给的赏赐多得数不清。訾，计算。
汝度rǔ dù
rǔ dù: you, Pei Du. Rǔ means "you".汝度：你裴度。汝，你。
从事愈cóng shì yù
cóng shì yù: Han Yu, your officer. Cóng shì means a staff officer under someone.从事愈：你的部下韩愈。从事，下属官员。
宜为辞yí wéi cí
yí wéi cí: should write the words. Cí here means the text for the stele.宜为辞：应该来写碑文。辞，文章，这里指碑文。
拜稽首bài qǐ shǒu
bài qǐ shǒu: kneeling and bowing with the head to the ground.拜稽首：跪下磕头行大礼。
蹈且舞dǎo qiě wǔ
dǎo qiě wǔ: stepping and dancing, an old way for an official to show thanks and joy before the emperor.蹈且舞：又跳又舞，古代臣子向皇帝表示感谢和高兴的一种礼节。
金石刻画jīn shí kè huà
jīn shí kè huà: writing carved on bronze and stone.金石刻画：刻在铜器、石碑上的文字。
大手笔dà shǒu bǐ
dà shǒu bǐ: a great writer trusted with the most important texts.大手笔：写重要文章的大作家。
不系于职司bú xì yú zhí sī
bù xì yú zhí sī: does not depend on what post he holds.不系于职司：不看他担任什么职务。系，关系。职司，职务。
当仁dāng rén
dāng rén zì gǔ yǒu bù ràng: when it is right to act, one has never stepped aside. It comes from the Analects: "When it comes to doing good, do not yield even to your teacher."当仁自古有不让：遇到该做的事，自古以来就不推让。出自《论语》“当仁，不让于师”。

yán qì: having finished speaking.言讫：说完。讫，完毕。
屡颔lǚ hàn
lǚ hàn tiān zǐ yí: the emperor nodded again and again. Hàn means to nod; yí is the chin.屡颔天子颐：皇帝一次次点头。颔，点头。颐，下巴。
公gōng
gōng: Han Yu, a polite way to name him.公：指韩愈，是尊称。
斋戒zhāi jiè
zhāi jiè: bathing, changing clothes, and giving up wine and meat to make body and mind clean, to show respect.斋戒：洗澡、换衣、不喝酒吃肉，让身心干净，表示郑重。

rú rǎn dà bǐ: soaking a big brush in ink. Rú means to wet.濡染大笔：把大笔蘸饱墨汁。濡，沾湿。

qīng miào, shēng mín: two poems in the Book of Songs that praise ancestors and their deeds. The two lines mean Han Yu learned from and reshaped the oldest classics.清庙、生民：《诗经》里的两篇，用来歌颂祖先和功德。两句说韩愈学习、化用了最古老的经典文章。

丹墀dān chí
dān chí: the red-painted steps before the palace hall, standing for the court.丹墀：宫殿前涂成红色的台阶，代指朝廷。
表biǎo
biǎo: a formal letter from an official to the emperor.表：臣子给皇帝的一种奏章。
昧死上mèi sǐ shàng
mèi sǐ shàng: "I present this at the risk of death", a set phrase of respect used when writing to the emperor.昧死上：冒着死罪呈上。这是古代给皇帝上书时表示恭敬的套话。

yǒng shén shèng gōng: praising the emperor's great deeds. Shén shèng here means the emperor.咏神圣功：歌颂皇帝的伟大功业。神圣，指皇帝。

sān zhàng: about ten meters. Zhàng is an old unit of length.三丈：约十米。丈，长度单位。

fù yǐ líng áo: carried on the back of a stone turtle. Áo is a giant turtle of legend.负以灵鳌：碑下面用石刻的神龟驮着。鳌，传说中的大海龟。

pán yǐ chī: with chi coiled on top. Chī is a hornless dragon of legend.蟠以螭：碑顶刻着盘绕的螭。螭，传说中没有角的龙。

jù qí yǔ zhòng: sentences that are unusual, words that are weighty.句奇语重：句子奇特，用语庄重。

rù rén gān pí: entered people's livers and spleens, that is, sank deep into their hearts.入人肝脾：深深地印在人们心里。肝脾，代指内心。
汤盘tāng pán

kǒng dǐng: the bronze cauldron of Zheng Kaofu, an ancestor of Confucius, carved with words.孔鼎：孔子祖先正考父的鼎，上面刻着铭文。

xuǎn hè: shining, glorious.烜赫：声名显赫，非常光彩。

bù shì hòu: is not shown to later people.不示后：不能给后人看到。

三五sān wǔ
sān wǔ: the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, wise rulers of legend.三五：三皇五帝，传说中上古的圣明君主。

xiāng pān zhuī: to catch up with them and stand beside them.相攀追：赶上、和他们并列。

04

Background创作背景

In 817, under Emperor Xianzong, the chief minister Pei Du went to the front himself. General Li Su attacked Caizhou on a snowy night and captured the rebel leader Wu Yuanji, ending the Huaixi revolt. Han Yu was Pei Du's senior staff officer and was ordered to write the Stele on Pacifying Huaixi. The text gave most of the credit to Pei Du. Li Su's wife, the daughter of a princess, complained to the emperor that it was unfair. Xianzong had the stele pulled down and its words ground away, and ordered Duan Wenchang to write a new one. Decades later Li Shangyin wrote this poem to speak up for Han Yu's text. The exact year it was written is not known.

唐宪宗元和十二年（817年），宰相裴度亲自督战，将军李愬雪夜攻进蔡州，捉住了叛将吴元济，平定了淮西。韩愈当时是裴度的行军司马，奉命写了《平淮西碑》。碑文把功劳主要归给裴度，李愬的妻子是公主的女儿，她向皇帝哭诉说碑文不公平。宪宗就下令把碑拉倒、磨掉字，改让段文昌重写。几十年后，李商隐写了这首诗，为韩愈的碑文鸣不平。具体写作时间不能确定。

05

The poet作者

Li Shangyin (about 813 to about 858), also called Yishan, was a famous poet of the late Tang. He is best known for subtle, dreamlike love poems and his "Untitled" poems. This poem is different. It is direct and forceful, full of old, difficult words, and deliberately copies Han Yu's style. It shows how much he admired Han Yu.

李商隐（约813—约858），字义山，号玉谿生，晚唐著名诗人。他最出名的是写得含蓄、朦胧的爱情诗和《无题》诗。这首《韩碑》却写得直接有力，用了很多古奥的字句，有意学习韩愈的文风，可以看出他对韩愈非常敬佩。

06

The mood意境

This poem tells a whole story in three parts. Part one is the war. The emperor is wise, the Huaixi rebels are fierce, and Pei Du leads strong generals and 140,000 soldiers into Caizhou to win. Part two is the writing of the stele. The emperor names Han Yu, who accepts with a dance of thanks, goes home, cleanses himself, and writes it in one bold sweep. The stele is three zhang tall with characters as big as buckets. It is grand. Part three is the stele's fate. Someone slanders it, and it is pulled down and ground blank. But the poet says great writing is like the first energy of heaven and earth: it is already in people's hearts. Old bronzes are lost, yet their words remain. At the end he says he will copy it and read it ten thousand times, until his mouth foams and his hand is callused, so it can be handed down. The poem is hard and heavy, like a stone stele itself, and you can feel the poet's anger at the injustice and his deep respect for Han Yu.

这首诗像在讲一个完整的故事，可以分成三段。第一段写打仗：皇帝英明，淮西叛贼凶狠，裴度带着猛将和十四万大军攻进蔡州，大获全胜。第二段写写碑：皇帝点名让韩愈写，韩愈又跳又舞地接下任务，回家斋戒，用大笔一挥而就，碑高三丈，字大如斗，气派极了。第三段写碑的命运：有人说坏话，碑被拉倒、字被磨掉。可诗人说，好文章像天地间的元气，早就进了人们心里，就像古代铜器没了，上面的话还在。最后他说愿意抄一万遍、读一万遍，读到嘴冒沫、手起茧，让它传下去。诗写得又硬又重，就像一块石碑，读起来能感到诗人心里的不平和敬佩。
