wáng王wéi维Wang Wei · Tang唐

Autumn Evening in the Mountains

 

kōng空shān山xīn新yǔ雨hòu后

tiān天qì气wǎn晚lái来qiū秋

míng明yuè月sōng松jiān间zhào照

qīng清quán泉shí石shàng上liú流

zhú竹xuān喧guī归huàn浣nǚ女

lián莲dòng动xià下yú渔zhōu舟

suí随yì意chūn春fāng芳xiē歇

wáng王sūn孙zì自kě可liú留

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 空山新雨后In the empty mountains, after fresh rain,空旷的山里刚下过一场雨，
  2. 天气晚来秋the evening air brings the feel of autumn.傍晚的空气里透着秋天的凉意。
  3. 明月松间照Bright moonlight shines between the pines;明亮的月光从松林间照下来，
  4. 清泉石上流a clear spring flows over the stones.清清的泉水在石头上流淌。
  5. 竹喧归浣女Laughter in the bamboo: the washing girls are going home;竹林里一阵说笑，是洗衣的姑娘们回家了；
  6. 莲动下渔舟the lotus leaves stir: a fishing boat is coming down.荷叶摇动，是渔船顺水下来了。
  7. 随意春芳歇Let the flowers of spring fade as they will;任凭春天的花草都凋谢吧，
  8. 王孙自可留I can stay here in these hills.我自然可以在这山中留下来。
02

Words字词注释

空山kōng shān
kōng shān: the empty mountains. People do live there, but it is so quiet it feels empty.空山：空旷、幽静的山。山里有人，只是很安静，好像没有人一样。
新雨xīn yǔ
xīn yǔ: rain that has just fallen.新雨：刚下过的雨。
天气tiān qì
tiān qì: here, the look and feel of the sky and air.天气：这里指天色和气候。
晚来秋wǎn lái qiū
wǎn lái qiū: as evening comes, the cool of autumn is felt more.晚来秋：傍晚的时候，秋天的凉意更明显了。
松间照sōng jiān zhào
sōng jiān zhào: moonlight shines down between the pines.松间照：月光从松树的缝隙间照下来。
清泉qīng quán
qīng quán: a clear spring.清泉：清清的泉水。
竹喧zhú xuān
zhú xuān: voices and laughter ring out from the bamboo. Xuān means noisy and lively.竹喧：竹林里传来说笑声。喧，声音嘈杂、热闹。
归浣女guī huàn nǚ
guī huàn nǚ: girls going home from washing clothes. Huàn means to wash clothes.归浣女：洗完衣服回家的姑娘们。浣，洗衣服。
莲动lián dòng
lián dòng: the lotus leaves sway.莲动：荷叶摇动。
下渔舟xià yú zhōu
xià yú zhōu: a fishing boat comes down the stream.下渔舟：渔船顺流而下。
随意suí yì
suí yì: let it be as it will.随意：任凭。
春芳歇chūn fāng xiē
chūn fāng xiē: the flowers of spring have faded. Xiē means to fade away.春芳歇：春天的花草凋谢了。歇，消散、凋谢。
王孙wáng sūn
wáng sūn: once meaning sons of noble families; here the poet means himself.王孙：本来指贵族子弟，这里指诗人自己。
自可留zì kě liú
zì kě liú: may well stay on.自可留：自然可以留下来。
03

Background创作背景

In the title, míng means dusk. In his middle years Wang Wei had a country house at Wangchuan, below the Zhongnan Mountains near Chang'an. He often held office and at the same time lived a quiet life in the hills. The poem is usually taken to describe an autumn evening after rain during those days in the mountains. We cannot say in which year it was written.

题目里的“暝”是傍晚、天黑的意思。王维中年以后，在长安附近的终南山下有一处辋川别墅，常常一边做官，一边在山里过隐居的日子。一般认为这首诗写的就是他隐居山中时，一个秋天雨后的傍晚。具体写在哪一年，已经说不清了。

04

The poet作者

Wang Wei (701–761, or by another account 699–761), courtesy name Mojie, was a Tang poet, painter and musician. He was a Buddhist and is called the Buddha of Poetry. He was a master of mountain and country scenes. Su Shi said there were paintings in his poems and poems in his paintings. This poem is like a painting of the mountains in autumn.

王维（701—761，一说699—761），字摩诘，唐代诗人，也是画家和音乐家。他信佛，被称为“诗佛”。他特别会写山水田园，苏轼说他“诗中有画，画中有诗”。这首诗就像一幅山中秋景图。

05

The mood意境

After rain the mountains are very clean, and the air is cool. Moonlight falls between the pines, and a spring tinkles over the stones. It is a quiet picture. Then sound and people come in. Girls laugh in the bamboo. The lotus leaves move, and a fishing boat appears. The poet hears the sound and sees the movement first, and only then the people, just as it happens in real life. Life in the hills is simple and happy. The last two lines turn around an old poem that said the noble son should come home, because one cannot stay long in the mountains. Wang Wei says the opposite: never mind that spring's flowers are gone; in autumn hills this lovely, I am glad to stay.

雨后的山特别干净，空气也特别清凉。月光照在松树间，泉水在石头上叮叮咚咚地流，这是一幅安静的画。接着画里有了声音和人：竹林里传来姑娘们的说笑，荷叶一动，渔船划了出来。诗人先听到声音、看到动静，后才看见人，写得很自然。山里的人生活得简单快乐。最后两句化用了古诗“王孙归来，山中不可久留”的意思，反过来说：春天的花谢了也没关系，这么美的秋山，我愿意留下来。
