ruǎn阮láng郎guī归·xiāng湘tiān天fēng风yǔ雨pò破hán寒chū初

qín秦guān观Qin Guan · Song宋

Ruan Lang Gui: Wind and Rain over Hunan Break the Early Cold

 

xiāng湘tiān天fēng风yǔ雨pò破hán寒chū初

shēn深chén沉tíng庭yuàn院xū虚

lì丽qiáo谯chuī吹bà罢xiǎo小chán单yú于

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 湘天风雨破寒初Wind and rain sweep the Hunan sky as the cold first sets in,湖南的天上风雨交加，寒冷刚刚到来，
  2. 深沉庭院虚and the deep courtyard is empty.幽深的庭院空空荡荡。
  3. 丽谯吹罢小单于On the watchtower the horn has finished the tune 'Little Chanyu',城楼上吹完了《小单于》的曲子，
  4. 迢迢清夜徂and the long, cold night slowly slips away.漫长清冷的夜晚慢慢过去。
  5. 乡梦断My dream of home is broken,回家乡的梦断了，
  6. 旅魂孤my wandering soul is all alone,漂泊在外的心多么孤单。
  7. 峥嵘岁又除and another hard year comes to its last night.艰难的一年又到了除夕。
  8. 衡阳犹有雁传书In Hengyang there are still geese to carry letters,衡阳还有大雁帮人捎信，
  9. 郴阳和雁无but here in Chenzhou there is not even a goose.可郴州这里，连大雁都没有。
02

Words字词注释

湘天xiāng tiān
xiāng tiān: the sky over Hunan. Xiāng is a short name for Hunan.湘天：湖南的天空。湘，湖南的简称。
破寒初pò hán chū
pò hán chū: the cold just breaking in. One reading says the storm breaks through the cold.破寒初：刚刚打破寒冷，冬天刚开始变冷的时候。一说是冲破了寒冷。
虚xū
xū: empty.虚：空空的。
丽谯lì qiáo
lì qiáo: a fine watchtower on the city wall, where horns sounded the hours.丽谯：华丽的城楼，上面有人吹号报时。
小单于xiǎo chán yú
xiǎo chán yú: 'Little Chanyu', the name of a tune played on the horn.小单于：一支用号角吹的曲子的名字。
迢迢tiáo tiáo
tiáo tiáo: long, drawn-out.迢迢：长长的样子。
徂cú
cú: to pass, to slip away.徂：过去、消逝。
乡梦xiāng mèng
xiāng mèng: a dream of home.乡梦：梦见家乡的梦。
旅魂lǚ hún
lǚ hún: the soul of a traveler far from home.旅魂：在外漂泊的人的心。
峥嵘zhēng róng
zhēng róng: steep and rugged, here meaning the year was hard to get through.峥嵘：本指山高险，这里形容这一年过得很艰难。
岁又除suì yòu chú
suì yòu chú: another year is gone; it is New Year's Eve.岁又除：一年又过去了，指除夕。
衡阳héng yáng
héng yáng: a city in today's Hunan. Legend says wild geese flying south stop at Huiyan Peak near Hengyang, so letters can still come by goose there.衡阳：在今湖南。传说大雁南飞到衡阳的回雁峰就不再往南，所以衡阳还能有雁捎信。
雁传书yàn chuán shū
yàn chuán shū: geese carrying letters. People of old said wild geese could bring letters.雁传书：大雁传送书信。古人说大雁能帮人带信。
郴阳chēn yáng
chēn yáng: Chenzhou, south of Hengyang, the place where the poet was sent in exile.郴阳：郴州，在衡阳更南边，就是词人被贬去的地方。
和雁无hé yàn wú
hé yàn wú: not even a goose.和雁无：连大雁也没有。
03

How to read it读音

单chán
In chán yú (the title of a Xiongnu chief), the first character is read chán, not dān. Here 'Little Chanyu' is the name of a tune.单于：古代匈奴首领的称号，单读 chán。这里《小单于》是曲子名。
和hé
Here the character means 'even', and is read hé.和：这里是“连”的意思，读 hé。
04

Background创作背景

Under Emperor Zhezong, the New Policies party took power. Because Qin Guan was close to Su Shi, he was demoted and sent away again and again. In the Shaosheng years he was exiled to Chenzhou in today's Hunan. This lyric shows him spending New Year's Eve there; one view dates it to the end of 1096. Far from home, on the last night of the year, he could only listen to the horn on the tower and get through the long night alone.

宋哲宗时，新党掌权，秦观因为和苏轼关系密切，一再被贬。绍圣年间，他被贬到郴州（今湖南郴州）。这首词写的是他在郴州过除夕的情景，一说作于绍圣三年（1096年）年底。离家很远，又是一年的最后一夜，他只能听着城楼的号角，一个人熬过长夜。

05

The poet作者

Qin Guan (1049–1100) was a Northern Song lyric poet, one of the Four Scholars around Su Shi. His lyrics are tender and often about the pain of parting. In his later years he was exiled again and again to far places such as Chenzhou, Hengzhou and Leizhou, and his lyrics grew sadder. He died on the road back north.

秦观（1049—1100），字少游，号淮海居士，北宋词人，是“苏门四学士”之一。他的词感情细腻，多写离愁。晚年一再被贬，到过郴州、横州、雷州等偏远地方，词也越写越凄苦，最后死在北归的路上。

06

The mood意境

The first half shows the scene on New Year's Eve. Wind and rain, biting cold, a deep and empty courtyard. The horn on the tower has finished, and the night seems endless. Everything is cold and quiet, which sets off the man's loneliness. The second half speaks of his heart directly. The dream of home is broken, his soul is alone, and another hard year has passed. The last two lines are the saddest. People said geese could carry letters, and geese stop at Hengyang, so letters from home can still reach there. Chenzhou is even farther south. There is not even a goose, so no news from home can come. Ending on the word 'none', the poet shows the deepest despair of an exile far from home.

上片写除夕夜的环境：风雨交加，寒气袭人，庭院又深又空。城楼上的号角吹完了，夜还长得看不到头。这些景物都很冷清，正好衬出人的孤独。下片直接说心情：回乡的梦断了，心很孤单，艰难的一年又过去了。最后两句最让人难过。古人说大雁能捎信，大雁飞到衡阳就停下了，那里还能收到家书；郴州比衡阳更远，连大雁都没有，家里的消息根本传不来。一个“无”字收尾，把被贬远方的绝望写到了极点。
