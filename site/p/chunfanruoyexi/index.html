Boating on Ruoye Creek in Spring

 

yōu幽yì意wú无duàn断jué绝

cǐ此qù去suí随suǒ所ǒu偶

wǎn晚fēng风chuī吹xíng行zhōu舟

huā花lù路rù入xī溪kǒu口

jì际yè夜zhuǎn转xī西hè壑

gé隔shān山wàng望nán南dǒu斗

tán潭yān烟fēi飞róng溶róng溶

lín林yuè月dī低xiàng向hòu后

shēng生shì事qiě且mí弥màn漫

yuàn愿wéi为chí持gān竿sǒu叟

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 幽意无断绝My love for quiet places has never faded,我向往清幽的心意从来没有断过，
  2. 此去随所偶so on this trip I will enjoy whatever I meet.这次出游，遇到什么美景就欣赏什么。
  3. 晚风吹行舟The evening wind pushes my little boat晚风吹着我的小船，
  4. 花路入溪口along a waterway lined with flowers into the creek.沿着开满鲜花的水路进入溪口。
  5. 际夜转西壑As night falls, the boat turns into the western valley,到了夜里，船转进了西边的山谷，
  6. 隔山望南斗and across the hills I see the Southern Dipper.隔着山峰望见南斗星。
  7. 潭烟飞溶溶Mist drifts softly over the pool,水潭上雾气轻轻飘散，
  8. 林月低向后and the moon in the woods sinks low behind the boat.林间的月亮渐渐低落，退到船后。
  9. 生事且弥漫The affairs of life are a wide blur, hard to see through,人世间的事一片茫茫，难以看清，
  10. 愿为持竿叟so I would rather be an old man with a fishing rod.我愿做一个手拿钓竿的老人。
02

Words字词注释

幽意yōu yì
yōu yì: a love of quiet, hidden places.幽意：喜欢清幽、隐居的心意。
随所偶suí suǒ ǒu
suí suǒ ǒu: enjoying whatever comes along. Ǒu means to meet by chance.随所偶：遇到什么就欣赏什么，随它去。偶，遇到。
溪口xī kǒu
xī kǒu: the mouth of Ruoye Creek.溪口：若耶溪的入口。
际夜jì yè
jì yè: when night came.际夜：到了夜里。
西壑xī hè
xī hè: the valley to the west.西壑：西边的山谷。
南斗nán dǒu
nán dǒu: the Southern Dipper, six stars in the southern sky shaped like a ladle.南斗：南方天空的一组星，有六颗，形状像个斗。
潭烟tán yān
tán yān: mist rising from a pool.潭烟：水潭上升起的雾气。
溶溶róng róng
róng róng: spreading and drifting softly.溶溶：水汽弥漫、慢慢流动的样子。
低向后dī xiàng hòu
dī xiàng hòu: the moon sinks low and seems to slide away behind the boat.低向后：月亮渐渐落下，好像在往船后面退去。
生事shēng shì
shēng shì: the affairs of life.生事：世上的事、人生的事。
弥漫mí màn
mí màn: a wide blur, like how life's affairs are hard to see clearly.弥漫：一片茫茫，看不清，比喻人生的事难以预料。
持竿叟chí gān sǒu
chí gān sǒu: an old man holding a fishing rod, someone living quietly by the water.持竿叟：拿着钓竿的老人，指隐居钓鱼的人。
03

How to read it读音

斗dǒu
In nán dǒu (the Southern Dipper), the second character is read dǒu, not dòu.南斗：星宿名，斗读 dǒu。
为wéi
In yuàn wéi (wish to become), the second character means 'to be, to become' and is read wéi, not wèi.愿为：愿意做。为是“做、当”的意思，读 wéi。
04

Background创作背景

Ruoye Creek lies south of today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. Legend says the beauty Xi Shi once washed silk there, and it was a famous scenic spot. This poem follows the poet boating on the creek one spring evening into the night. The year is not known. Along the way he watches flowers, stars and the moon, grows calmer and calmer, and at last wishes to live hidden from the world.

若耶溪在今浙江绍兴南边，传说西施曾在这里洗纱，是有名的风景地。这首诗写诗人春天的一个傍晚到深夜，坐船在若耶溪游玩的经过。具体写于哪一年已经不清楚。诗人一路看花、看星、看月，心里越来越安静，最后生出隐居的念头。

05

The poet作者

Qiwu Qian, whose family name Qiwu has two syllables, was a poet of the High Tang. His exact dates are unknown. He passed the imperial exam in the Kaiyuan years and served as an official, then gave it up and went back east of the Yangtze. He was a friend of Wang Wei and other poets. His poems are mostly about mountains, rivers and the hermit's life, quiet and clear in style.

綦毋潜，复姓綦毋，名潜，盛唐诗人，生卒年不详。他是开元年间的进士，做过官，后来辞官回到江东。他和王维等诗人有来往，诗多写山水和隐居生活，风格清幽。

06

The mood意境

The poem follows one night trip in order of time. At dusk, the evening wind pushes the boat along a flowery waterway into the creek, easy and lovely. At night the boat turns into the western valley. Looking up across the hills he sees the Southern Dipper, and the world suddenly feels high and wide. Then come two beautiful pictures: mist drifting gently over the pool, and the moon in the woods sinking low as if sliding behind the boat. The boat moves, the scene changes, and the poet's heart grows quieter. At last he thinks that life's affairs are a blur, and he would rather be an old fisherman. The beauty helps him set down his worries and makes him want to leave fame and gain behind.

这首诗按时间顺序写一次夜游。傍晚，晚风吹着小船，沿着开满花的水路进入溪口，轻松又美好。夜里，船转进西边的山谷，抬头隔着山看到南斗星，天地一下子变得又高又远。接着是两个很美的画面：潭上的雾轻轻飘，林间的月亮慢慢低下，好像在往船后退。船在走，景在变，诗人的心越来越静。最后他想到人生的事茫茫不清，不如做一个钓鱼的老人。美景让他放下了烦恼，也让他想离开名利。
