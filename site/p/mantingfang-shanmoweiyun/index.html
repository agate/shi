满庭芳·山抹微云

秦观Qin Guan · Song宋

Man Ting Fang: Thin Clouds Brushed on the Hills

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 山抹微云Faint clouds are brushed across the hills,山上轻轻抹着淡淡的云，
  2. 天连衰草dry grass runs to the edge of the sky,枯草一直连到天边，
  3. 画角声断谯门and the painted horn on the gate tower falls silent.城楼上画角的声音停了。
  4. 暂停征棹For a while I stop my boat for the long journey,我暂时停下远行的船，
  5. 聊共引离尊and lift the parting cup with her.和她一起举起送别的酒杯。
  6. 多少蓬莱旧事So many things happened at Penglai Pavilion.在蓬莱阁有过多少往事，
  7. 空回首I look back in vain:白白地回头去想，
  8. 烟霭纷纷all I see is drifting mist.只看见一片迷茫的烟雾。
  9. 斜阳外Beyond the setting sun,斜阳的那一边，
  10. 寒鸦万点countless winter crows dot the sky,寒鸦点点，
  11. 流水绕孤村and a stream winds round a lonely village.流水绕着孤零零的村子。
  12. 销魂My heart breaks.真让人伤心啊！
  13. 当此际At this moment在这个时候，
  14. 香囊暗解she quietly unties her scent pouch for me,她悄悄解下香袋送我，
  15. 罗带轻分and gently we part our silk sashes.我们轻轻分开了衣带。
  16. 谩赢得All I am left with白白落得
  17. 青楼薄幸名存is a name for being heartless to a singing girl.一个在青楼负心的名声。
  18. 此去何时见也When will we meet again, once I am gone?这一走，什么时候才能再见？
  19. 襟袖上On my robe and sleeves衣襟和袖子上，
  20. 空惹啼痕are tear stains, for nothing.白白沾满了泪痕。
  21. 伤情处What hurts most is this:最伤心的是，
  22. 高城望断I look back at the high city wall until it is out of sight,回头望高高的城墙，已经看不见了，
  23. 灯火已黄昏and the lamps are lit, for dusk has come.城里的灯火已经亮起，天黑了。
02

Words字词注释

抹mǒ
mǒ: to brush on, as if with a light stroke of a brush.抹：涂抹，像用笔轻轻抹了一下。
微云wēi yún
wēi yún: faint, thin clouds.微云：淡淡的云。
天连衰草tiān lián shuāi cǎo
tiān lián shuāi cǎo: dry grass stretches to the edge of the sky, as if joined to it. Some versions say the sky "sticks to" the grass.天连衰草：枯草一直长到天边，好像和天连在一起。衰草，枯黄的草。一本作“天粘衰草”。
画角huà jiǎo
huà jiǎo: a painted horn blown in the army in old times. Its sound was sad and long.画角：古代军中吹的号角，上面有彩色的画，声音悲凉。
谯门qiáo mén
qiáo mén: a city gate with a watchtower on top.谯门：城门上建有瞭望楼的城门。
征棹zhēng zhào
zhēng zhào: the boat for a long journey. A zhào is an oar, standing for the boat.征棹：远行的船。棹，划船的桨，这里代指船。
聊共liáo gòng
liáo gòng: for now, together.聊共：姑且一起。
引离尊yǐn lí zūn
yǐn lí zūn: to lift the cup of parting wine. Zūn is a wine cup.引离尊：举起送别的酒杯。尊，酒杯。
蓬莱旧事péng lái jiù shì
péng lái jiù shì: things that happened at Penglai Pavilion, a building in Kuaiji, today's Shaoxing. It means the happy times the two had together.蓬莱旧事：在蓬莱阁发生的往事。蓬莱阁是会稽（今浙江绍兴）的一座楼阁，这里指两人在一起的美好时光。
空回首kōng huí shǒu
kōng huí shǒu: to look back in vain.空回首：白白地回头去想。
烟霭纷纷yān ǎi fēn fēn
yān ǎi fēn fēn: mist and haze everywhere, nothing clear.烟霭纷纷：烟雾一片迷茫，看不清楚。
寒鸦hán yā
hán yā: crows in the cold season.寒鸦：秋冬时节的乌鸦。
销魂xiāo hún
xiāo hún: so sad it is as if the soul melts away.销魂：难过得好像魂都没了。
当此际dāng cǐ jì
dāng cǐ jì: at this moment.当此际：在这个时候。
香囊暗解xiāng náng àn jiě
xiāng náng àn jiě: quietly untying a scent pouch to give as a keepsake. A xiāng náng is a small bag of sweet-smelling spices.香囊暗解：悄悄解下香袋送给对方做纪念。香囊，装香料的小袋子。
罗带轻分luó dài qīng fēn
luó dài qīng fēn: gently parting silk sashes. In old times a knotted sash stood for love, so parting it stands for saying goodbye.罗带轻分：轻轻分开丝绸的衣带，表示分别。古人常用打结的衣带表示相爱。
赢得yíng dé
yíng dé: to end up with, to be left with.赢得：落得，只得到。
青楼薄幸名存qīng lóu bó xìng míng cún
qīng lóu bó xìng míng cún: all that is left is a name for being heartless toward a singing girl. Qīng lóu was where singing girls lived; bó xìng means heartless. The line borrows from the Tang poet Du Mu.青楼薄幸名存：只留下一个在青楼负心的名声。青楼，歌女住的地方；薄幸，薄情、负心。这句化用了唐代杜牧的诗“赢得青楼薄幸名”。
空惹啼痕kōng rě tí hén
kōng rě tí hén: stained with tears, all for nothing.空惹啼痕：白白沾上了眼泪的痕迹。
伤情处shāng qíng chù
shāng qíng chù: what hurts most.伤情处：最让人伤心的地方。
望断wàng duàn
wàng duàn: to look until nothing can be seen.望断：望到看不见为止。
03

How to read it读音

谩màn
Here it is read màn, the same as "in vain". Read mán it means "to deceive", which is not the sense here.谩，这里读 màn，同“漫”，意思是“白白地、徒然”。读 mán 时是“欺骗”的意思，不是这里的用法。
薄bó
In bó xìng, "heartless", it is read bó.薄幸的“薄”读 bó。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually thought to have been written when Qin Guan was leaving Kuaiji, today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang, by boat after a stay there, around the end of 1079. Before he left, he said a sad goodbye to a singing girl he loved, and wrote these words. The opening phrase about clouds brushed on the hills was so admired that people called him "Scholar Qin of the brushed clouds".

一般认为，这首词是秦观在会稽（今浙江绍兴）住了一段时间后，要坐船离开时写的，时间大约在宋神宗元丰二年（1079年）年底。临走时，他和一位相好的歌女依依惜别，写下了这首词。开头“山抹微云”写得特别好，人们因此叫他“山抹微云秦学士”。

05

The poet作者

Qin Guan (1049–1100), also called Shaoyou, was a Northern Song poet and a student of Su Shi, one of the "Four Scholars of the Su School". His lyrics use fine, clear words and gentle feelings, often about parting and sadness. He is a leading poet of the delicate style. Su Shi greatly admired his talent.

秦观（1049—1100），字少游，北宋词人，是苏轼的学生，“苏门四学士”之一。他的词语言清丽，感情细腻，多写离别和伤感，是婉约词的代表作家。苏轼很喜欢他的才华。

06

The mood意境

The first three lines are like a pale ink painting: thin clouds brushed on the hills, dry grass reaching the sky, the horn on the gate tower falling quiet. The words "brushed" and "joined" make the far view light and distant. The boat is about to leave, so the two stop for one cup of parting wine. Looking back on the past, he sees only mist. Then comes the famous scene: beyond the setting sun, winter crows dot the sky, and a stream winds round a lonely village. It is cold and empty, just like his heart. The second stanza shows the parting itself: she unties her scent pouch, and they part their sashes. He mocks himself for being left with nothing but a name for heartlessness, and his sleeves are wet with tears. At the end the boat is far away. He looks back, but the city is gone, and only the lamps of dusk remain. Scene and feeling are woven together, and the lyric grows darker and sadder as it goes.

开头三句像一幅淡淡的水墨画：山上抹着薄云，枯草连到天边，城楼的号角声停了。一个“抹”字，一个“连”字，把远景写得又轻又远。船要开了，两人只好暂停一下，喝一杯送别酒。回想过去的事，只看见一片烟雾。接着是有名的景：夕阳那边，寒鸦点点，流水绕着孤村，冷清又寂寞，正像他的心情。下阕写分别的动作：她解下香袋，两人分开衣带。他自嘲只落得个负心的名声，衣袖上全是泪痕。最后船走远了，回头再望，城已经看不见，只剩黄昏的灯火。全词景和情交织，越写越暗，越写越伤心。
