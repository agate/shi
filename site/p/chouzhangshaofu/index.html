chóu酬zhāng张shào少fǔ府

wáng王wéi维Wang Wei · Tang唐

In Reply to Vice-Prefect Zhang

 

wǎn晚nián年wéi唯hào好jìng静

wàn万shì事bù不guān关xīn心

zì自gù顾wú无cháng长cè策

kōng空zhī知fǎn返jiù旧lín林

sōng松fēng风chuī吹jiě解dài带

shān山yuè月zhào照tán弹qín琴

jūn君wèn问qióng穷tōng通lǐ理

yú渔gē歌rù入pǔ浦shēn深

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 晚年唯好静In my late years I love only quiet;到了晚年，我只喜欢安静，
  2. 万事不关心nothing in the world troubles my mind.什么事情都不再放在心上。
  3. 自顾无长策Looking at myself, I have no good plan to offer,想想自己，也没有什么报国的好办法，
  4. 空知返旧林so all I know is to go back to my old woods.只知道回到老家的山林里去。
  5. 松风吹解带The wind through the pines blows on me as I loosen my sash;松林的风吹来，我解开衣带，吹个痛快；
  6. 山月照弹琴the mountain moon shines on me as I play the qin.山间的月亮，照着我弹琴。
  7. 君问穷通理You ask me why some fail and some succeed in life?你问我人生失意和得意的道理，
  8. 渔歌入浦深Listen: a fisherman's song drifts deep into the shore.听，渔夫的歌声正飘进水边深处。
02

Words字词注释

唯好静wéi hào jìng
wéi hào jìng: to love only quiet. Wéi means only, hào means to like.唯好静：只喜欢安静。唯，只；好，喜欢。
不关心bù guān xīn
bù guān xīn: not to care about, not to keep in mind.不关心：不放在心上。
自顾zì gù
zì gù: looking at himself, thinking it over.自顾：自己看看自己，自己想想。
长策cháng cè
cháng cè: a good plan for the long run, here a good way to serve the country.长策：好的计策、长远的办法。这里指为国家出力的好主意。
空知kōng zhī
kōng zhī: only knowing how to.空知：只知道。
返旧林fǎn jiù lín
fǎn jiù lín: going back to the woods he knew, that is, to live away from the world.返旧林：回到以前住过的山林，就是回去隐居。
解带jiě dài
jiě dài: untying his sash, a sign of being relaxed and free.解带：解开衣带，指放松、自在的样子。
弹琴tán qín
tán qín: playing the qin, an old seven-string instrument.弹琴：弹古琴，一种七根弦的古老乐器。
君jūn
jūn: you, meaning Zhang. Shào fǔ was another name for a county official.君：你，指张少府。少府，县里的官“县尉”的别称。
穷通理qióng tōng lǐ
qióng tōng lǐ: the reason why a person fails or succeeds in life. Qióng is failing, tōng is doing well.穷通理：人为什么不得志、为什么得志的道理。穷，不得志；通，顺利、做大官。
渔歌yú gē
yú gē: songs sung by fishermen.渔歌：渔夫唱的歌。
浦pǔ
pǔ: the shore, where a stream meets a river or lake.浦：水边，河流入江、入湖的地方。
03

How to read it读音

好hào
Here it means to like, read hào.这里是“喜欢”的意思，读 hào。
04

Background创作背景

Chóu means to answer someone with a poem. A man surnamed Zhang, a county official, sent Wang Wei a poem or a question, and Wang Wei wrote this poem in reply. Who this Zhang was is no longer known. The poem says "late years", so it was written when Wang Wei was old and lived half as an official and half as a hermit. At that time he had a country house at Wangchuan, below the Zhongnan Mountains, and often lived quietly there.

酬，就是用诗回答别人。一位姓张的少府（县尉）写诗或问话给王维，王维写了这首诗作答。这位张少府是谁，已经不清楚了。诗里说“晚年”，可见是王维年纪大了、半官半隐的时候写的。那时他在终南山下的辋川有一处别墅，常常在那里过安静的生活。

05

The poet作者

Wang Wei (about 701–761), courtesy name Mojie, was a great Tang poet and painter, and he also knew music well. He was a Buddhist, and his poems often feel quiet and airy, so people called him the Buddha of Poetry. The Song writer Su Shi said there are paintings in his poems and poems in his paintings. In his late years he still held office, but his heart longed for the hills, and this poem shows that feeling.

王维（约701—761），字摩诘，唐代大诗人、画家，也懂音乐。他信佛，诗里常有安静、空灵的味道，人们叫他“诗佛”。宋代的苏轼说他“诗中有画，画中有诗”。他晚年做着官，心却向往山林，这首诗就写出了这种心情。

06

The mood意境

In the first four lines the poet speaks his mind. He is old, loves only quiet, and no longer cares about the affairs of the world. He has no good plan to offer, so he goes back to the woods. He says it calmly, but there is a little helplessness in it too. Lines five and six are a picture. A breeze comes through the pines and he loosens his sash; the moon shines on him as he plays the qin. Wind, moon and music all feel clean and fresh, and the man is free. The last two lines are the best. His friend asks him the big question of success and failure. He does not answer. He only says, "Listen, a fisherman's song is drifting deep into the cove." It seems to mean: why ask? Just sing freely like the fisherman. The answer is left for the reader to think about.

前四句是诗人自己说心里话：老了，只爱安静，世上的事都不管了；自己也没什么好办法，只好回山林。话说得很平淡，里面其实也有一点无奈。五、六句是一幅画：松林里吹来风，他解开衣带；月亮照着他弹琴。风、月、琴，都清清爽爽，人也自由自在。最后两句最妙：朋友问人生得失的大道理，他不回答，只说“你听，渔歌飘进了水湾深处”。意思好像是：何必问呢？像渔夫一样自在地唱歌就好了。答案留给读的人自己去想。
