hè贺xīn新láng郎·fù赋pí琵pá琶

xīn辛qì弃jí疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

He Xin Lang: On the Pipa

 

fèng凤wěi尾lóng龙xiāng香bō拨

zì自kāi开yuán元ní霓cháng裳qǔ曲bà罢

jǐ几fān番fēng风yuè月

zuì最kǔ苦xún浔yáng阳jiāng江tóu头kè客

huà画gě舸tíng亭tíng亭dài待fā发

jì记chū出sài塞huáng黄yún云duī堆xuě雪

mǎ马shàng上lí离chóu愁sān三wàn万lǐ里

wàng望zhāo昭yáng阳gōng宫diàn殿gū孤hóng鸿mò没

xián弦jiě解yǔ语

hèn恨nán难shuō说

liáo辽yáng阳yì驿shǐ使yīn音chén尘jué绝

suǒ琐chuāng窗hán寒qīng轻lǒng拢màn慢niǎn捻

lèi泪zhū珠yíng盈jié睫

tuī推shǒu手hán含qíng情hái还què却shǒu手

yì一mǒ抹liáng梁zhōu州āi哀chè彻

qiān千gǔ古shì事yún云fēi飞yān烟miè灭

hè贺lǎo老dìng定chǎng场wú无xiāo消xī息

xiǎng想chén沉xiāng香tíng亭běi北fán繁huá华xiē歇

tán弹dào到cǐ此

wèi为wū呜yè咽

The old edition reads niān (a different character for "pinch") and writes the Chenxiang Pavilion with an old form of chén. Common editions use the same character niǎn as Bai Juyi's "Song of the Pipa", and the modern form of chén; the old form was not changed when the text was converted by machine.

古籍本里写作“轻拢慢拈”“沈香亭”。通行本作“轻拢慢捻”，和白居易《琵琶行》的写法一样；“沈香亭”就是“沉香亭”，“沈”在这里是“沉”的古写，机器转换时没有改过来。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 凤尾龙香拨This pipa has a phoenix-tail end and a plectrum of fragrant cypress.这把琵琶，尾部雕着凤凰，配着龙香柏木的拨子。
  2. 自开元霓裳曲罢Since the Rainbow Skirt music of the Kaiyuan years fell silent,自从开元年间《霓裳羽衣曲》停了以后，
  3. 几番风月how many years have passed!已经过去了多少年。
  4. 最苦浔阳江头客Saddest of all was the guest by the Xunyang River,最苦的是浔阳江边的白居易，
  5. 画舸亭亭待发his painted boat standing tall, waiting to sail.他的船高高停着，等着出发。
  6. 记出塞黄云堆雪I remember Zhaojun going past the frontier, yellow dust in the sky and snow in heaps.还记得王昭君出塞，黄沙满天，雪积成堆。
  7. 马上离愁三万里On horseback she carried her sorrow thirty thousand miles,她在马上带着离愁，走了三万里，
  8. 望昭阳宫殿孤鸿没looking back at the Han palace, where a lone goose flew off and vanished.回头望汉宫，只见一只孤雁越飞越远，消失了。
  9. 弦解语The strings seem able to speak,琴弦好像会说话，
  10. 恨难说yet the sorrow is hard to put into words.可心里的恨很难说清。
  11. 辽阳驿使音尘绝Her husband guards Liaoyang, and no messenger brings word anymore.丈夫在辽阳守边，送信的人再也没来过。
  12. 琐窗寒轻拢慢捻By a cold carved window she presses and rolls the strings softly,她在寒冷的雕花窗下，轻轻按弦，慢慢揉弦，
  13. 泪珠盈睫tears hanging on her lashes.泪珠挂满了睫毛。
  14. 推手含情还却手Her hand pushes forward, full of feeling, then pulls back again,手往前推，满含深情，又往回收，
  15. 一抹梁州哀彻and one stroke of the Liangzhou tune is sorrow itself.一曲《梁州》，悲哀到了极点。
  16. 千古事云飞烟灭The stories of a thousand years have drifted off like cloud and smoke.千年的往事，都像云烟一样散去了。
  17. 贺老定场无消息He Huaizhi, whose playing hushed the hall, is long gone without a trace,能让满场安静的贺怀智，早已没有消息，
  18. 想沉香亭北繁华歇and the splendor north of the Agarwood Pavilion must have ended long ago.想来沉香亭北的繁华也早已结束了。
  19. 弹到此When the playing reaches this point,琵琶弹到这里，
  20. 为呜咽one cannot help but sob.不由得低声哭泣起来。
02

Words字词注释

凤尾fèng wěi
fèng wěi: the end of the pipa, shaped like a phoenix tail.凤尾：琵琶的尾部，做成凤尾的样子。
龙香拨lóng xiāng bō
lóng xiāng bō: a plectrum made of fragrant cypress wood. A plectrum is the small piece used to pluck the strings. Legend says Yang Guifei's pipa had one.龙香拨：用龙香柏木做的拨子。拨子是弹琵琶用的小片。传说杨贵妃的琵琶就配着这种拨子。
开元kāi yuán
kāi yuán: the reign name of Emperor Xuanzong of Tang, the richest years of the dynasty.开元：唐玄宗的年号，是唐朝最繁荣的时候。
霓裳曲ní cháng qǔ
ní cháng qǔ: the "Rainbow Skirt and Feather Coat" music, a famous court dance of Xuanzong's time.霓裳曲：《霓裳羽衣曲》，唐玄宗时宫里有名的乐舞。
罢bà
bà: to end, to stop.罢：结束、停了。
几番风月jǐ fān fēng yuè
jǐ fān fēng yuè: how many years have passed. Fēng yuè, wind and moon, stands for the passing years.几番风月：过了多少年。风月，清风明月，指岁月。
浔阳江头客xún yáng jiāng tóu kè
xún yáng jiāng tóu kè: the guest by the Xunyang River, meaning Bai Juyi. Demoted to Jiangzhou, he was seeing off a friend by the river when he heard a woman play the pipa, and wrote "Song of the Pipa".浔阳江头客：指白居易。他被贬到江州，在浔阳江边送客，听一位女子弹琵琶，写了《琵琶行》。
画舸huà gě
huà gě: a painted boat.画舸：装饰得很漂亮的船。
亭亭tíng tíng
tíng tíng: standing tall and straight.亭亭：高高直立的样子。
待发dài fā
dài fā: waiting to set off.待发：等着出发。
出塞chū sài
chū sài: going out past the frontier, meaning Wang Zhaojun sent north to marry the Xiongnu ruler.出塞：走出边塞，指王昭君远嫁匈奴。
黄云堆雪huáng yún duī xuě
huáng yún duī xuě: on the frontier, yellow dust fills the sky and snow lies in heaps.黄云堆雪：边塞黄沙漫天，积雪成堆。
昭阳zhāo yáng
zhāo yáng: the Zhaoyang Hall of the Han palace, standing for the Han court.昭阳：汉代的昭阳殿，这里代指汉朝皇宫。
孤鸿没gū hóng mò
gū hóng mò: a lone wild goose flies off and disappears.孤鸿没：一只孤单的大雁飞远，看不见了。
弦解语xián jiě yǔ
xián jiě yǔ: the strings seem able to speak, playing out what is in the heart. Jiě means to know how.弦解语：琴弦好像懂得说话，能弹出人的心事。解，懂得、会。
辽阳liáo yáng
liáo yáng: around today's Liaoning, a frontier post in old times.辽阳：今辽宁一带，古时是边防的地方。
驿使yì shǐ
yì shǐ: a messenger who carried letters.驿使：送信的人。
音尘绝yīn chén jué
yīn chén jué: no news at all.音尘绝：一点消息都没有了。
琐窗suǒ chuāng
suǒ chuāng: a window carved with linked patterns.琐窗：刻着连环花纹的窗子。
轻拢慢捻qīng lǒng màn niǎn
qīng lǒng màn niǎn: ways of playing the pipa, pressing the strings lightly and rolling them slowly. The words come from Bai Juyi's "Song of the Pipa".轻拢慢捻：弹琵琶的指法，轻轻按弦，慢慢揉弦。出自白居易《琵琶行》。
盈睫yíng jié
yíng jié: filling the eyelashes.盈睫：挂满了睫毛。
推手tuī shǒu
tuī shǒu, què shǒu: people of old said that plucking with the hand moving forward was called pí, and moving back was called pá; that is how the pipa got its name. Què means back.推手、却手：古人说，手向前推着弹叫“琵”，向后收回来弹叫“琶”，琵琶就是这么得名的。却，退、往回。
梁州liáng zhōu
liáng zhōu: the name of a tune, also written as Liangzhou, a popular long piece in Tang times.梁州：曲子名，也写作“凉州”，是唐代流行的大曲。
哀彻āi chè
āi chè: sad to the utmost.哀彻：悲哀到了极点。
云飞烟灭yún fēi yān miè
yún fēi yān miè: like clouds drifting off and smoke vanishing, nothing remains.云飞烟灭：像云飘散、烟消失一样，什么都没有留下。
贺老hè lǎo
hè lǎo: old He, that is He Huaizhi, a famous pipa player in Xuanzong's time.贺老：贺怀智，唐玄宗时弹琵琶的名手。
定场dìng chǎng
dìng chǎng: to make the whole hall fall silent by playing.定场：一弹琵琶，全场就安静下来。
沉香亭chén xiāng tíng
chén xiāng tíng: the Agarwood Pavilion in the Tang palace, where Xuanzong and Yang Guifei often admired peonies.沉香亭：唐朝宫里的亭子，唐玄宗和杨贵妃常在那里赏牡丹。
繁华歇fán huá xiē
fán huá xiē: all the splendor has ended.繁华歇：热闹繁华都结束了。
呜咽wū yè
wū yè: to sob quietly.呜咽：低声哭泣。
03

How to read it读音

还hái
Here it means "again" and is usually read hái today. Some read it huán, meaning to turn back.还：又、再，现在一般读 hái。一说读 huán，是“回转”的意思。
抹mǒ
A way of plucking the pipa, read mǒ.抹：弹琵琶的一种指法，读 mǒ。
为wèi
Meaning "because of this", read wèi.为：因为、为了这个，读 wèi。
04

Background创作背景

This is a lyric about an object, using one thing to pour out feelings. The title means "writing about the pipa". Xin Qiji strings together stories from history linked to the pipa: Yang Guifei's pipa, Bai Juyi's "Song of the Pipa", Wang Zhaojun going past the frontier, a lonely wife playing the pipa, and the master player He Huaizhi. The date is unknown. It is usually read as a lament: through the lost splendor of the high Tang, the poet mourns the lost northern capital and his country's decline.

这是一首“咏物词”，就是借写一样东西来抒发感情。题目“赋琵琶”，意思是写琵琶。辛弃疾把和琵琶有关的历史故事串在一起：杨贵妃的琵琶、白居易的《琵琶行》、王昭君出塞、思妇弹琵琶、贺怀智的演奏。写作时间不详。一般认为，词人借盛唐繁华的消失，感叹北宋旧都失去、国家衰落。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) was a great lyric poet of the Southern Song. As a young man he fought the Jin in the north, and all his life he hoped to win back the central lands, but he was often left without a post. Most of his lyrics are bold, but this one is soft and sorrowful. He was a master of allusion, using old stories to speak of present feelings; almost every line here has a source.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），南宋大词人，年轻时在北方抗金，一生想收复中原，却常被闲置。他的词多半豪放，但这首写得婉转哀伤。他很会用典故，也就是借古人古事说今天的感受，这首词几乎每一句都有来历。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is like a pipa solo, playing one sorrow after another. It begins with Yang Guifei's pipa: the songs and dances of the high Tang stopped long ago. Then comes Bai Juyi hearing the pipa by the river, and Wang Zhaojun playing it on horseback as she goes far away: one the pain of exile, the other the pain of a far-off marriage. The second stanza shows a woman playing by a cold window. Her husband is on the distant frontier with no news, and she weeps as she plays. At the end, He Huaizhi and the Agarwood Pavilion are gone, and a thousand years of stories have drifted off like smoke. Playing to this point, the player sobs too. Every story is tied to the pipa, and every one is about parting and loss. As you read, imagine the music now fast, now slow, growing sadder and sadder.

这首词像一场琵琶独奏，一段一段奏出不同的悲伤。开头从杨贵妃的琵琶说起，盛唐的歌舞早就停了。然后是白居易在江边听琵琶，王昭君在马上弹琵琶远去，一个是贬官的苦，一个是远嫁的苦。下片写一个女子在寒窗下弹琴，丈夫远在边疆，音信全无，她边弹边流泪。最后说贺怀智、沉香亭都不见了，千年往事像云烟散去。弹到这里，人也哭了。每个故事都和琵琶有关，又都是离别和失去。读的时候，可以想象琵琶声一阵急一阵慢，越弹越悲。
