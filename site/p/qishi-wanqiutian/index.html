Words字词注释

yī shà: a moment, a very short time.一霎：一会儿，很短的时间。
庭轩tíng xuān
tíng xuān: the courtyard and the open hall beside it.庭轩：院子和带窗的长廊。
槛菊jiàn jú
jiàn jú: chrysanthemums by the railing. Jiàn means a railing.槛菊：栏杆边的菊花。槛，栏杆。
萧疏xiāo shū
xiāo shū: thin and sparse, bleak.萧疏：稀稀落落，冷落的样子。
井梧jǐng wú
jǐng wú: the parasol tree by the well.井梧：井边的梧桐树。
惹残烟rě cán yān
rě cán yān: caught in thin, lingering mist.惹残烟：沾着、缠着淡淡的烟雾。
凄然qī rán
qī rán: sad and lonely at heart.凄然：心里凄凉悲伤。
江关jiāng guān
jiāng guān: a pass on the river; here, the distant land along the river.江关：江上的关口，这里指远处的江山。
黯淡àn dàn
àn dàn: dim, gloomy.黯淡：昏暗，不明亮。
宋玉悲感sòng yù bēi gǎn

临水与登山lín shuǐ yǔ dēng shān
lín shuǐ yǔ dēng shān: coming to the water and climbing the hills. Jiu Bian has a line about climbing hills and facing water to see someone off.临水与登山：来到水边，登上高山。《九辩》里有“登山临水兮送将归”的句子。
迢递tiáo dì
tiáo dì: far, far away.迢递：路途遥远的样子。
凄楚qī chǔ
qī chǔ: sad and miserable.凄楚：凄凉痛苦。
陇水lǒng shuǐ
lǒng shuǐ: water flowing down from Mount Long. Old songs said its sound was like sobbing and made people far from home sad. Here it means any sad sound of flowing water.陇水：陇山上流下的水。古诗里说陇头流水声音像哭声，听了让离家的人伤心。这里借指让人愁苦的流水声。
潺湲chán yuán
chán yuán: the sound of water flowing slowly.潺湲：水慢慢流动的声音。
蝉吟败叶chán yín bài yè
chán yín bài yè: cicadas singing among withered leaves.蝉吟败叶：蝉在枯叶间鸣叫。
蛩qióng
qióng: a cricket.蛩：蟋蟀。
衰草shuāi cǎo
shuāi cǎo: withered grass.衰草：枯黄的草。
喧喧xuān xuān
xuān xuān: noisy, many sounds together.喧喧：声音嘈杂。
孤馆gū guǎn
gū guǎn: a lonely inn.孤馆：孤零零的旅店。

dù rì rú nián: each day feels as long as a year; time drags.度日如年：过一天像过一年那么长，形容日子难熬。

qiāo qiāo: silent, very quiet.悄悄：寂静无声。
更阑gēng lán
gēng lán: late at night. People in old times split the night into five watches; lán means nearly over.更阑：夜深了。古人把一夜分成五更，阑是快完了。

jiàng hé: the Milky Way.绛河：银河。

hào yuè: the bright white moon.皓月：洁白明亮的月亮。
婵娟chán juān
chán juān: lovely and graceful; here, the beauty of the moon.婵娟：姿态美好的样子，这里形容月亮美。

lì míng: money and fame.利名：钱财和名声。

How to read it读音

槛jiàn
Here it means a railing and is read jiàn. Read kǎn, it means a doorsill.“槛”在这里是栏杆，读 jiàn；读 kǎn 时是门槛。
间jiān
It means in the setting sun, so it is read jiān.“夕阳间”是夕阳里，“间”读 jiān。
应yìng
It means answering each other, so it is read yìng.“相应”是互相应和，“应”读 yìng。
更gēng
Here it is a night watch, an old unit of night time, so it is read gēng.“更阑”指夜深，“更”是古代夜里计时的单位，读 gēng。
那nǎ
This word means how can one bear it. The character is used like the question word nǎ, so it is read nǎ.“那堪”是哪能忍受，“那”同“哪”，读 nǎ。
少shào
It means young days, so it is read shào.“少日”是年轻的时候，“少”读 shào。
朝zhāo
It means morning, so it is read zhāo.“朝欢”是早上也寻欢作乐，“朝”是早晨，读 zhāo。
长cháng
It means for a long time, so it is read cháng.“长萦绊”是长久地被缠住，“长”读 cháng。
咽yè
In the word for sobbing it is read yè.“呜咽”是低低的哭泣声，“咽”读 yè。
数shù
It means a few, so it is read shù.“数声”是几声，“数”读 shù。
04

Background创作背景

The tune Qi Shi first appears in Liu Yong's own collection. It has 212 characters in three stanzas, one of the longest tunes in Song ci. This lyric follows one late autumn night. The writer is alone in an inn, from dusk until dawn. He remembers his happy young days in the capital and sighs that he now runs from place to place for an official career. It is usually thought to come from his middle or later years, when he traveled and served in posts away from the capital. The exact year and place are debated. Because it mentions Song Yu, one view says it was written in the old land of Chu, around today's Hubei.

《戚氏》这个词调最早见于柳永的词集，全词二百一十二个字，分三段，是宋词里最长的词调之一。这首词写一个深秋的夜晚，作者独自住在旅店里，从傍晚写到天亮，回想年轻时在京城的快乐日子，感叹为了功名到处奔波。一般认为写于柳永中年以后在外地做官、漂泊的时候。具体的年代和地点说法不一，词中提到宋玉，一说写于湖北一带的古楚地。

05

The poet作者

Liu Yong (about 984 to about 1053) was first named Sanbian, with the courtesy name Qiqing. He came from Chong'an in Fujian. He was the seventh child in his generation, so people called him Liu Qi, Liu the Seventh. When young he often wrote songs for singing girls in the capital, but he failed the imperial exams many times and passed only late in life. He served as an official in charge of farmland, which gave him another nickname. He wrote many long, slow tunes and was good at laying out scenery, memories and feelings layer by layer, as this lyric does.

柳永（约984—约1053），原名三变，字耆卿，福建崇安人，排行第七，人称“柳七”。他年轻时常在京城的歌楼里为歌女写词，科举却多次考不中，很晚才中进士，做过屯田员外郎，所以也叫“柳屯田”。他大量写作长调慢词，善于把景物、往事和心情一层层铺开来写，这首词就是典型。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is like a film shot in time order. The first stanza is dusk: light rain, sparse chrysanthemums, messy parasol leaves, a dim sunset, and cicadas and crickets that never stop. The writer remembers Song Yu, who grieved for autumn more than two thousand years ago, and feels that he too is a lonely traveler. The second stanza is deep night. Everything is quiet, the Milky Way is pale, and the moon is lovely. The more beautiful it is, the less a lonely man can sleep, and he starts counting the years on his fingers. The third stanza goes back to the past. When he was young in the capital, he drank and sang with friends every day. But time flew like a shuttle, and now he is worn out chasing fame and money. At last the water clock moves on, horn calls come and fade, and he sits by the lamp until dawn with only his shadow for company. Scenery, memory and feeling unfold layer by layer, and you feel the loneliness of a long night and a longing for lost youth.

这首词像一部按时间拍的电影。第一段是傍晚：小雨、稀疏的菊花、零乱的梧桐、昏暗的夕阳，还有蝉和蟋蟀吵个不停。作者想起两千多年前同样悲秋的宋玉，觉得自己也是一个漂泊在外的人。第二段是深夜：四下安静，银河很浅，月亮很美。越是美，越让孤单的人睡不着，他开始屈指数算从前的日子。第三段回到往事：年轻时在京城，和朋友们天天喝酒唱歌。可是时光像梭子一样快，如今为了名利憔悴奔波。最后，漏壶的刻度移动，号角声断断续续，他守着灯一直坐到天亮，只有自己的影子陪着。景物、回忆、心情一层一层铺开，读完能感到长夜的寂寞和对青春的怀念。
