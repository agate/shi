Song lyric · ci to the tune Zhu Ma Zi (also called Zhu Ma Er), in two stanzas宋词 · 词，词牌《竹马子》（又名《竹马儿》），双调

zhú竹mǎ马zǐ子·dēng登gū孤lěi垒huāng荒liáng凉

liǔ柳yǒng永Liu Yong · Song宋

Zhu Ma Zi: Climbing the Lonely Ramparts

 

dēng登gū孤lěi垒huāng荒liáng凉

wēi危tíng亭kuàng旷wàng望

jìng静lín临yān烟zhǔ渚

duì对cí雌ní霓guà挂yǔ雨

xióng雄fēng风fú拂jiàn槛

wēi微shōu收fán烦shǔ暑

jiàn渐jué觉yí一yè叶jīng惊qiū秋

cán残chán蝉zào噪wǎn晚

sù素shāng商shí时xù序

lǎn览jǐng景xiǎng想qián前huān欢

zhǐ指shén神jīng京

fēi非wù雾fēi非yān烟shēn深chù处

xiàng向cǐ此chéng成zhuī追gǎn感

xīn新chóu愁yì易jī积

gù故rén人nán难jù聚

píng凭gāo高jìn尽rì日níng凝zhù伫

yíng赢dé得xiāo消hún魂wú无yǔ语

jí极mù目jì霁ǎi霭fēi霏wēi微

míng暝yā鸦líng零luàn乱

xiāo萧suǒ索jiāng江chéng城mù暮

nán南lóu楼huà画jiǎo角

yòu又sòng送cán残yáng阳qù去

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 登孤垒荒凉I climb the bleak and lonely rampart.登上荒凉孤寂的旧城垒，
  2. 危亭旷望From the tall pavilion I gaze far away,在高高的亭子里向远处眺望，
  3. 静临烟渚quietly facing a misty islet in the river.静静地面对雾气笼罩的江中小洲。
  4. 对雌霓挂雨A pale rainbow hangs in the sky after rain.看着淡淡的彩虹挂在雨后的天上，
  5. 雄风拂槛A strong cool wind brushes the railing,凉爽的大风吹过栏杆，
  6. 微收烦暑and the stifling heat eases a little.闷热的暑气稍稍退去。
  7. 渐觉一叶惊秋Slowly I see one falling leaf announce autumn.渐渐发觉，一片落叶惊动了秋天，
  8. 残蝉噪晚Late cicadas shrill in the evening.残留的秋蝉在傍晚吵闹地叫，
  9. 素商时序The season of autumn has come.已经是秋天的时节了。
  10. 览景想前欢Looking at the view, I think of past joys.看着这些景物，想起从前的欢乐，
  11. 指神京I point toward the capital,我指向京城的方向，
  12. 非雾非烟深处deep in a haze that is neither mist nor smoke.它在那像雾又像烟的云霭深处。
  13. 向此成追感Here, I am filled with sad memories.到这时，不由得回想往事，满心伤感，
  14. 新愁易积New sorrows pile up easily,新的愁绪容易堆积，
  15. 故人难聚but old friends are hard to gather.老朋友却难得相聚。
  16. 凭高尽日凝伫All day I stand still on the heights,我在高处整天久久站立，
  17. 赢得消魂无语and gain only a grief too deep for words.只落得伤心到说不出话。
  18. 极目霁霭霏微As far as I can see, thin mist drifts after the rain.尽力远望，雨后的雾气轻轻飘散，
  19. 暝鸦零乱Crows at dusk scatter in disorder,黄昏的乌鸦零零乱乱地飞，
  20. 萧索江城暮and the river town grows bleak at nightfall.江城在暮色里一片冷落。
  21. 南楼画角The painted horn on the south tower sounds,南楼上的画角声响起，
  22. 又送残阳去sending the setting sun away once more.又把夕阳送走了。
02

Words字词注释

孤垒gū lěi
gū lěi: a lonely old rampart.孤垒：孤零零的旧城墙、营垒。
危亭wēi tíng
wēi tíng: a tall pavilion. Wēi here means high.危亭：高高的亭子。危，高。
旷望kuàng wàng
kuàng wàng: to gaze far into the distance.旷望：向远处眺望。
烟渚yān zhǔ
yān zhǔ: a misty islet. Zhǔ is a small piece of land in the water.烟渚：雾气笼罩的水中小洲。渚，水中的小块陆地。
雌霓cí ní
cí ní: the paler outer rainbow. People of old called the bright bow male and the paler one female.雌霓：彩虹外圈颜色较淡的那道虹。古人把颜色鲜艳的叫“虹”，算雄的；淡的叫“霓”，算雌的。
挂雨guà yǔ
guà yǔ: hanging in the sky after rain.挂雨：雨后挂在天上。
雄风xióng fēng
xióng fēng: a strong, cool wind, a phrase from Song Yu's Rhapsody on the Wind.雄风：强劲凉爽的风。语出宋玉《风赋》。
拂槛fú jiàn
fú jiàn: brushing the railing.拂槛：吹过栏杆。
微收烦暑wēi shōu fán shǔ
wēi shōu fán shǔ: eases the stifling heat a little.微收烦暑：让闷热稍稍退去。
一叶惊秋yí yè jīng qiū
yī yè jīng qiū: one falling leaf startles him into knowing autumn is here. An old saying goes: one leaf falls, and all the world knows it is autumn.一叶惊秋：看到一片落叶，惊觉秋天到了。古话说“一叶落而知天下秋”。
噪晚zào wǎn
zào wǎn: shrilling in the evening.噪晚：在傍晚吵闹地叫。
素商sù shāng
sù shāng: autumn. People of old matched autumn with the colour white and the musical note shang.素商：指秋天。古人把秋天配白色（素）和五音里的商音。
时序shí xù
shí xù: the season.时序：季节。
前欢qián huān
qián huān: past joys.前欢：从前的欢乐。
神京shén jīng
shén jīng: the capital, Bianjing, today's Kaifeng in Henan.神京：京城，指北宋都城汴京（今河南开封）。
非雾非烟fēi wù fēi yān
fēi wù fēi yān: not quite mist, not quite smoke: lucky coloured clouds, and the haze that hides the far-off capital.非雾非烟：像雾又不是雾，像烟又不是烟，指吉祥的彩云，也形容京城远在云霭深处。
向此xiàng cǐ
xiàng cǐ: here, at this moment.向此：到了这里，在这时。
追感zhuī gǎn
zhuī gǎn: sad thoughts of the past.追感：回想往事而感伤。
故人gù rén
gù rén: old friends.故人：老朋友。
凭高píng gāo
píng gāo: standing up high.凭高：登在高处。
尽日jìn rì
jìn rì: all day long.尽日：整天。
凝伫níng zhù
níng zhù: standing still for a long time.凝伫：一动不动地久久站着。
赢得yíng dé
yíng dé: all one gains is.赢得：只落得。
消魂xiāo hún
xiāo hún: so sad that one seems to lose one's soul.消魂：非常伤心，好像魂都没了。
极目jí mù
jí mù: looking as far as the eye can see.极目：尽力远望。
霁霭jì ǎi
jì ǎi: mist after rain. Jì means the rain has cleared.霁霭：雨后的雾气。霁，雨停天晴。
霏微fēi wēi
fēi wēi: drifting thin and light.霏微：雾气轻轻飘散的样子。
暝鸦míng yā
míng yā: crows at dusk. Míng means dark.暝鸦：黄昏时的乌鸦。暝，天黑。
萧索xiāo suǒ
xiāo suǒ: bleak and desolate.萧索：冷落、凄凉。
画角huà jiǎo
huà jiǎo: a painted horn, used in old army camps to mark the time.画角：画着花纹的号角，古代军中用来报时。
03

How to read it读音

槛jiàn
Here this character means a railing and is read jiàn. Read kǎn, it means a doorsill.“槛”指栏杆，读 jiàn。读 kǎn 时是门槛的意思。
角jiǎo
Huà jiǎo is a painted horn, so this character is read jiǎo.“画角”是画着花纹的号角，“角”读 jiǎo。
04

Background创作背景

In this ci the poet climbs an old rampart in a riverside town and gazes far from a tall pavilion. Summer has just ended and autumn is arriving. He looks toward the capital, remembers the happy days he had there, and thinks how hard it is to see old friends again. Liu Yong spent much of his life travelling from post to post, and he wrote this while far from home. We do not know which town it was or in what year.

这首词写作者在一座江边城市登上旧城垒，从高高的亭子里远望，正是夏天刚过、秋天刚来的时候。他望着京城的方向，想起从前在那里的欢乐日子，又想到老朋友难再相聚。柳永一生多半在外地奔波，这首词就是他漂泊在外时写的。具体是哪座城、哪一年，现在说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Liu Yong (about 984 – about 1053) came from Chong'an, today's Wuyishan in Fujian. As a young man he lived in the capital and wrote songs for singing girls. He failed the exams many times before passing, then held only small posts and drifted for years. He was a master of long, slow ci, and wrote most movingly about the loneliness of travel and the pain of parting.

柳永（约984—约1053），原名三变，字耆卿，崇安（今福建武夷山）人。他年轻时在京城生活，常为歌女写词，考了很多次才中进士，后来只做过小官，长年漂泊。他擅长写长调慢词，写旅途的孤单和离别的愁苦最动人。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is the view from the heights. Lonely ramparts, a tall pavilion, and below, a misty islet in the river. After rain a rainbow hangs in the sky, a cool wind brushes the railing, and the summer heat eases a little. One falling leaf makes him realise autumn has come, and late cicadas still shrill in the evening. He looks toward the capital, but it lies deep in clouds and he cannot see it. The second stanza turns to his heart. The more he thinks, the sadder he gets. New sorrows pile up easily, but old friends are hard to gather. He stands up high all day and gains nothing but a speechless ache. The last picture is lovely and cold. Thin mist drifts after the rain, crows fly in disorder, the river town grows bleak, and the horn on the south tower sends the setting sun away once more. The scene grows darker, and his mood grows heavier.

上片是登高看到的景。荒凉的城垒，高高的亭子，下面是雾气笼罩的江中小洲。雨后挂着彩虹，凉风吹过栏杆，夏天的闷热稍稍退去。一片落叶让人惊觉秋天来了，傍晚残蝉还在叫。这时他望向京城，那里在云霭深处，看不见。下片转入心情：越想越伤感，新的愁容易堆起来，老朋友却难相聚。他在高处站了一整天，只换来说不出话的难过。最后的画面很美也很冷：雨后的雾气轻轻飘着，乌鸦乱飞，江城一片萧条，南楼的号角声又把夕阳送走了。景物越来越暗，心情也越来越沉。
