xíng行lù路nán难·qí其sān三

lǐ李bái白Li Bai · Tang唐

The Road Is Hard, No. 3

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 有耳莫洗颍川水If you have ears, do not wash them in the Ying River like Xu You.有耳朵，不要学许由去颍水边洗耳朵；
  2. 有口莫食首阳蕨If you have a mouth, do not live on ferns on Mount Shouyang like Bo Yi and Shu Qi.有嘴巴，不要学伯夷、叔齐去首阳山吃野菜。
  3. 含光混世贵无名Hide your shine and live among the crowd; having no fame is what counts.藏起光芒，和大家一样过日子，没有名声才可贵；
  4. 何用孤高比云月Why make yourself as proud and lofty as the clouds and the moon?何必把自己看得像白云明月一样清高？
  5. 吾观自古贤达人I look at the gifted and famous people since ancient times.我看自古以来那些有才有名的人，
  6. 功成不退皆殒身Those who did not step down after their great deeds all lost their lives.立了功却不肯退下，最后都丢了性命。
  7. 子胥既弃吴江上Wu Zixu was thrown into the river in Wu.伍子胥死后被扔进了吴地的江里，
  8. 屈原终投湘水滨Qu Yuan at last threw himself into the water by the Xiang.屈原最后投进了湘水边的江中。
  9. 陆机雄才岂自保Lu Ji had great talent, yet could he save himself?陆机才华那么高，哪里保住了自己？
  10. 李斯税驾苦不早Li Si, sadly, did not retire soon enough.李斯可惜没有早一点退隐。
  11. 华亭鹤唳讵可闻How could Lu Ji ever hear the cranes of Huating again?家乡华亭的鹤叫声，陆机哪里还能再听到？
  12. 上蔡苍鹰何足道And Li Si's hunts with a hawk outside Shangcai: what use to speak of them?上蔡东门牵狗架鹰打猎的日子，李斯哪里还说得上？
  13. 君不见吴中张翰称达生Have you not seen Zhang Han of Wu, who was called a man who saw life clearly?你没看见吗？吴中的张翰被称为看得开的人，
  14. 秋风忽忆江东行When the autumn wind rose, he suddenly missed home and went back east of the river.秋风一起，他忽然想起家乡，就回江东去了。
  15. 且乐生前一杯酒So let us enjoy a cup of wine while we are alive.还是趁活着的时候，快快乐乐喝一杯酒吧，
  16. 何须身后千载名Why should we need a name that lasts a thousand years after we are gone?何必去求死后流传千年的名声？
02

Words字词注释

洗颍川水xǐ yǐng chuān shuǐ
xǐ yǐng chuān shuǐ: legend says the ruler Yao offered the whole land to the hermit Xu You. Xu You felt the offer had dirtied his ears, so he went to wash them in the Ying River, in today's Henan.洗颍川水：传说尧想把天下让给隐士许由，许由觉得这话弄脏了耳朵，就跑到颍水边去洗耳朵。颍川，颍水，在今天的河南。
食首阳蕨shí shǒu yáng jué
shí shǒu yáng jué: the brothers Bo Yi and Shu Qi opposed the Zhou overthrowing the Shang. They refused to eat Zhou grain, hid on Mount Shouyang eating wild plants, and finally starved. Jué is a wild fern people could eat.食首阳蕨：伯夷、叔齐反对周朝灭商，不肯吃周朝的粮食，躲到首阳山上采野菜吃，最后饿死。蕨，一种山野里的野菜。
莫mò
mò: do not.莫：不要。
含光混世hán guāng hùn shì
hán guāng hùn shì: to hide your own shine and live among ordinary people like one of them.含光混世：把自己的光芒藏起来，像普通人一样混在人群里过日子。
贵无名guì wú míng
guì wú míng: to value having no fame.贵无名：以没有名声为可贵。
何用hé yòng
hé yòng: why should one, what need is there to.何用：何必，为什么要。
孤高比云月gū gāo bǐ yún yuè
gū gāo bǐ yún yuè: to hold yourself proud and apart, as high and pure as clouds and the moon.孤高比云月：把自己看得清高孤傲，好像天上的白云和明月。
贤达人xián dá rén
xián dá rén: people of talent and fame.贤达人：有才能、有名望的人。
功成不退gōng chéng bú tuì
gōng chéng bù tuì: having done great deeds, not stepping down in time.功成不退：立了大功以后，不肯及时离开官场。
殒身yǔn shēn
yǔn shēn: to lose one's life.殒身：丢了性命。
子胥既弃吴江上zǐ xū jì qì wú jiāng shàng
zǐ xū jì qì wú jiāng shàng: Wu Zixu did great service for the state of Wu, but King Fuchai forced him to kill himself, and his body was sewn into a leather bag and thrown into the river. Jì means already.子胥既弃吴江上：伍子胥帮吴国立了大功，后来却被吴王夫差逼死，尸体被装在皮袋里扔进江中。既，已经。
屈原终投湘水滨qū yuán zhōng tóu xiāng shuǐ bīn
qū yuán zhōng tóu xiāng shuǐ bīn: Qu Yuan, a minister of Chu, was driven from court and at last drowned himself in the Miluo River, which flows into the Xiang. Bīn means the water's edge.屈原终投湘水滨：楚国大臣屈原被赶出朝廷，最后投汨罗江而死。汨罗江流进湘水。滨，水边。
陆机雄才岂自保lù jī xióng cái qǐ zì bǎo
lù jī xióng cái qǐ zì bǎo: Lu Ji, a great writer of the Western Jin, got caught in fights between princes and was killed. Qǐ means how could.陆机雄才岂自保：西晋文学家陆机很有才华，后来卷进王族之间的争斗，被杀。岂，哪里能。
李斯税驾苦不早lǐ sī tuō jià kǔ bù zǎo
lǐ sī tuō jià kǔ bù zǎo: Li Si, chief minister of Qin, did not step down while at the top, and was later put to death. Tuō jià means to unhitch the horses, that is, to stop and retire. Kǔ bù zǎo means sadly too late.李斯税驾苦不早：秦朝丞相李斯，位置最高时没有及时退下来，后来被害死。税驾，解下拉车的马，比喻停下来休息、退隐。苦不早，可惜太晚了。
华亭鹤唳讵可闻huá tíng hè lì jù kě wén
huá tíng hè lì jù kě wén: before he died, Lu Ji sighed, "Can I ever hear the cranes cry at Huating again?" Huating, his home, is in today's Songjiang, Shanghai. Lì is a crane's cry. Jù means how.华亭鹤唳讵可闻：陆机临死前叹气说：“家乡华亭的鹤叫声，还能再听到吗？”华亭，在今天的上海松江。唳，鹤叫。讵，哪里，怎么。
上蔡苍鹰何足道shàng cài cāng yīng hé zú dào
shàng cài cāng yīng hé zú dào: before his death Li Si told his son he wished they could once more go hunting out of the east gate of Shangcai, his hometown in today's Henan, with a yellow dog and a grey hawk. But it was too late. Hé zú dào means what is the use of speaking of it.上蔡苍鹰何足道：李斯临死前对儿子说，想和他再牵着黄狗、架着苍鹰，出上蔡东门去打猎，可是再也做不到了。上蔡，李斯的家乡，在今天的河南。何足道，哪里还值得说。
君不见jūn bú jiàn
jūn bù jiàn: have you not seen? A common phrase in old poems to catch the reader's attention.君不见：你没有看见吗？古诗里常用来提醒读者。
张翰称达生zhāng hàn chēng dá shēng
zhāng hàn chēng dá shēng: Zhang Han, a man of the Western Jin who held office in Luoyang, was called someone who saw life clearly. Dá shēng means seeing through life and not being tied up by fame or gain.张翰称达生：西晋人张翰在洛阳做官，被人称为看得开的人。达生，看透人生，不被名利捆住。
秋风忽忆江东行qiū fēng hū yì jiāng dōng xíng
qiū fēng hū yì jiāng dōng xíng: when Zhang Han saw the autumn wind rise, he missed the wild rice stems, water-shield soup and perch of his home in Wu, so he quit his post and went back east of the river.秋风忽忆江东行：张翰看见秋风吹起，想起家乡吴中的菰菜、莼菜汤和鲈鱼，就辞官回了江东老家。
且qiě
qiě: for now, let us just.且：姑且，暂且。
何须hé xū
hé xū: why should we need.何须：何必要。
千载名qiān zǎi míng
qiān zǎi míng: fame that lasts a thousand years.千载名：流传千年的名声。
03

How to read it读音

税tuō
Here the character usually read shuì stands for tuō, "to take off", and is read tuō. Some readers keep the reading shuì.税驾的“税”通“脱”，意思是解下。这里读 tuō。也有人照字面读 shuì。
载zǎi
When this character means "year", as in "a thousand years", it is read zǎi, not zài.“载”表示“年”时读 zǎi，比如“千载”“三年五载”。
04

Background创作背景

"The Road Is Hard" is an old folk-song title, usually used to write about how hard life's road is. Li Bai wrote three poems under it; this is the third. They are usually dated to around 744. Li Bai was then serving the emperor in Chang'an as a court writer, but powerful officials pushed him out. The emperor gave him some gold and sent him away from the capital. Another view says the poems were written earlier, after his first failed attempt to find an official post in Chang'an.

《行路难》是乐府旧题，原来多写人生道路的艰难。李白一共写了三首，这是第三首。一般认为这组诗写于唐玄宗天宝三年（744年）前后。那时李白在长安做翰林供奉，受到权贵排挤，被“赐金放还”，也就是皇帝给了些钱，让他离开京城。一说这组诗写于他更早一次到长安求官失败的时候。

05

The poet作者

Li Bai (701–762) was a great Tang poet, called the Immortal of Poetry. He badly wanted a high post to help the emperor rule well, but never got one. In this poem he says it is better to be like Zhang Han: drink, go home, and forget fame. That is partly real calm, and partly the bitter talk of a disappointed man.

李白（701—762），唐代大诗人，被称为“诗仙”。他很想做大官，帮皇帝治理天下，可是一生都没能实现。这首诗里他说不如学张翰，喝酒回家，不要名声。这有一半是看开，也有一半是失望之后的气话。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a history lesson. Li Bai names one old figure after another. Xu You washing his ears and Bo Yi starving were too proud and pure. Wu Zixu, Qu Yuan, Lu Ji and Li Si did great deeds but did not step back, and paid with their lives. Lu Ji longed to hear the cranes of home, and Li Si longed to go hunting once more, but their regret came too late. Only Zhang Han, who went home for perch as soon as the autumn wind blew, lived freely. The last two lines, enjoy a cup of wine while alive, why want fame after death, sound carefree. But think about it: a man who so badly wanted to do great things now says fame does not matter. Underneath the letting go there is hurt and unwillingness.

这首诗像一堂历史课。李白一口气讲了好几个古人：许由洗耳、伯夷饿死，是太清高；伍子胥、屈原、陆机、李斯，是立了功不肯退，结果送了命。陆机想听家乡的鹤叫，李斯想再去打猎，到死才后悔，已经晚了。只有张翰，秋风一吹就回家吃鲈鱼，活得自在。最后两句“且乐生前一杯酒，何须身后千载名”，说得很潇洒。可我们读的时候要想一想：一个那么想建功立业的人，偏偏说名声不重要，这里面藏着多少不甘心。表面是放下，底下是委屈。
