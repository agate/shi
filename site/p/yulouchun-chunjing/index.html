yù玉lóu楼chūn春·chūn春jǐng景

sòng宋qí祁Song Qi · Song宋

Jade Tower Spring: A Spring Scene

 

dōng东chéng城jiàn渐jué觉fēng风guāng光hǎo好

hú縠zhòu皱bō波wén纹yíng迎kè客zhào棹

lǜ绿yáng杨yān烟wài外xiǎo晓hán寒qīng轻

hóng红xìng杏zhī枝tóu头chūn春yì意nào闹

fú浮shēng生cháng长hèn恨huān欢yú娱shǎo少

kěn肯ài爱qiān千jīn金qīng轻yí一xiào笑

wèi为jūn君chí持jiǔ酒quàn劝xié斜yáng阳

qiě且xiàng向huā花jiān间liú留wǎn晚zhào照

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 东城渐觉风光好On the east side of the city, the scenery grows lovelier and lovelier.城东的风景，让人越来越觉得美好，
  2. 縠皱波纹迎客棹Ripples like crinkled silk welcome the visitors' boats.水面上细细的波纹，像绉纱一样，迎接着游人的小船。
  3. 绿杨烟外晓寒轻Beyond the willows wrapped in mist, the morning chill is light,绿柳像笼着一层轻烟，早晨只有一点点凉意，
  4. 红杏枝头春意闹and on the branches of the red apricots, spring is bustling.红杏开满枝头，春天热闹极了。
  5. 浮生长恨欢娱少Life is short, and I often regret how little joy it holds.人生短暂，常常遗憾快乐的时候太少，
  6. 肯爱千金轻一笑How could I cling to gold and think little of a happy smile?怎么能爱惜千金，却轻视欢乐的一笑呢？
  7. 为君持酒劝斜阳For your sake I raise my cup to the setting sun,为了你，我端起酒杯劝夕阳，
  8. 且向花间留晚照and ask it to linger a while among the flowers.请它在花丛间多留一会儿晚霞。
02

Words字词注释

东城dōng chéng
dōng chéng: the east side of the city.东城：城东，城的东边。
渐觉jiàn jué
jiàn jué: slowly coming to feel.渐觉：慢慢觉得。
风光fēng guāng
fēng guāng: the scenery.风光：风景。
縠皱波纹hú zhòu bō wén
hú zhòu bō wén: ripples on the water like crinkled silk. Hú is a very thin silk cloth with fine wrinkles.縠皱波纹：水上的波纹像有皱纹的绉纱一样。縠，一种很薄、有细皱纹的丝织品。
客棹kè zhào
kè zhào: the boats of visitors. Zhào is an oar, and here means a boat.客棹：游人坐的船。棹，船桨，这里指船。
烟yān
yān: here, the thin mist that hangs around the willows like smoke.烟：这里指柳树周围像烟一样的薄雾。
晓寒轻xiǎo hán qīng
xiǎo hán qīng: the morning chill is only slight.晓寒轻：早晨只有一点点凉意。
春意闹chūn yì nào
chūn yì nào: spring is bustling, as if the blossoms were making a noise.春意闹：春天的气息热热闹闹，好像花在吵闹一样。
浮生fú shēng
fú shēng: life. People of old felt life was like something floating on water, short and uncertain.浮生：人生。古人觉得人生像水上漂浮的东西，短暂又不定。
长恨cháng hèn
cháng hèn: often to regret.长恨：常常遗憾。
欢娱huān yú
huān yú: joy, good times.欢娱：快乐。
肯爱千金轻一笑kěn ài qiān jīn qīng yí xiào
kěn ài qiān jīn qīng yí xiào: how could one cling to a thousand pieces of gold and think little of a smile? That is, better to spend a fortune than to miss a moment of joy. Kěn here means how would one be willing.肯爱千金轻一笑：怎么肯爱惜千金，却轻视美人的一笑呢？意思是宁可花千金，也要换来欢笑。肯，哪里肯。
为君wèi jūn
wèi jūn: for you.为君：为了你。
持酒劝斜阳chí jiǔ quàn xié yáng
chí jiǔ quàn xié yáng: raising a cup and offering a drink to the setting sun.持酒劝斜阳：端起酒杯，劝夕阳喝一杯。
晚照wǎn zhào
wǎn zhào: the evening sunlight.晚照：傍晚的阳光。
03

How to read it读音

长cháng
In this line the character means often, so it is read cháng, not zhǎng.“浮生长恨”的“长”是“常常”的意思，读 cháng，不读 zhǎng。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about the joy of a spring outing on the east side of the city. The line about spring bustling on the red apricot branches made Song Qi so famous that people called him the Red Apricot Minister. We no longer know in which year it was written.

这首词写春天到城东游玩的快乐。宋祁因为“红杏枝头春意闹”这一句名气很大，当时的人叫他“红杏尚书”。这首词具体写于哪一年，已经说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Song Qi (998–1061) was a Northern Song writer and historian. He and his elder brother Song Xiang passed the top exam in the same year and were called the Two Songs. He helped write the history book New Book of Tang, and rose to be Minister of Works. Few of his lyrics survive, and this one is the most famous.

宋祁（998—1061），字子京，北宋文学家、史学家。他和哥哥宋庠同年考中进士，被人称为“二宋”。他参加编写了史书《新唐书》，官做到工部尚书。他的词留下来的不多，这首最出名。

06

The mood意境

The first half paints spring. East of the city the scenery grows lovelier. Fine ripples like silk welcome the boats. The willows are wrapped in light mist, the morning is just a little cool, and red apricot blossoms crowd the branches. The best part is the word nào, bustling. Flowers make no sound, yet the poet says spring is noisy. We seem to see trees full of red blossoms, hear bees humming, and feel spring rushing at us. The second half turns to feeling. Life is short and joy is rare, so rather than hold on to money, enjoy the good times. At the end he raises his cup and asks the setting sun to go down slowly, hoping to keep the lovely moment a little longer.

上半首写春景：城东风光越来越好，水上波纹细细的，像绉纱一样迎接小船；柳树笼着轻烟，早晨微微有点凉；红杏开满枝头。最妙的是一个“闹”字：花本来不会出声，词人却说春意在“闹”，让人好像看到了满树红花，听到了蜜蜂嗡嗡，感到春天热热闹闹扑面而来。下半首写心情：人生短，快乐少，与其守着钱，不如好好享受欢乐。最后他举杯劝夕阳慢点落下，想把美好的时光多留一会儿。
