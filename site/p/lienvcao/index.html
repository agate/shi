Song of a Faithful Wife

bō波lán澜shì誓bù不qǐ起

qiè妾xīn心jǐng井zhōng中shuǐ水

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 梧桐相待老The wu tree and the tong tree keep each other company until they grow old.梧树和桐树相互陪伴，一起到老；
  2. 鸳鸯会双死Mandarin ducks live in pairs and will die in pairs.鸳鸯成双成对，死也要死在一起。
  3. 贞妇贵徇夫A faithful wife holds it most dear to follow her husband,忠贞的妻子最看重的，是随丈夫而去，
  4. 舍生亦如此and gives up her life just as they do.舍弃生命，也像它们一样。
  5. 波澜誓不起I vow no wave will ever rise in my heart.我发誓心里不会再起一点波澜，
  6. 妾心井中水My heart is like the water in a well, still and calm.我的心就像井里的水一样平静。
02

Words字词注释

梧桐wú tóng
wú tóng: the parasol tree. People of old said wu was the male tree and tong the female, growing old side by side.梧桐：一种树。古人说梧是雄树，桐是雌树，两棵树一起长、一起老。
相待老xiāng dài lǎo
xiāng dài lǎo: to stay with each other and grow old together.相待老：互相陪伴，一起变老。
鸳鸯yuān yāng
yuān yāng: mandarin ducks, water birds that always go in pairs. People of old believed that if one died, the other would not live on alone.鸳鸯：一种水鸟，总是成双成对。古人认为它们一只死了，另一只也不独活。
会huì
huì: will surely.会：一定会。
贞妇zhēn fù
zhēn fù: a wife who stays faithful to her husband.贞妇：对丈夫忠贞不变心的妻子。
贵guì
guì: to value, to hold most dear.贵：看重，认为最宝贵。
徇夫xùn fū
xùn fū: to give one's life for one's husband, to follow him in death.徇夫：为丈夫而死，跟随丈夫而去。
舍生shě shēng
shě shēng: to give up one's life.舍生：舍弃生命。
波澜bō lán
bō lán: waves on water. Here it stands for a wavering heart.波澜：水面上的波浪，这里比喻心里的动摇。
井中水jǐng zhōng shuǐ
jǐng zhōng shuǐ: the water in a well, deep and still, with no waves.井中水：井里的水，又深又静，不起波浪。
03

Background创作背景

"Lienü Cao" is an old title for a song played on the qin, a kind of zither. Cao is a type of qin song, and lienü means women of firm virtue. Meng Jiao uses this old title and speaks in the voice of a widow. She says she will never marry again after her husband's death, and her heart will stay still. This was the old ideal of a wife's faithfulness. Another view says Meng Jiao uses the faithful wife to speak of his own firm principles. We do not know when he wrote it.

《列女操》是一个古老的琴曲题目。“操”是琴曲的一种，“列女”指有节操的女子。孟郊用这个旧题目，借一位女子的口吻，说丈夫死后她决不改嫁、心如止水。这是古代推崇的“贞节”观念。一说孟郊是借女子守节，表达自己坚守节操、不随便改变心志。这首诗的写作时间不详。

04

The poet作者

Meng Jiao (751–814) was a Tang poet who was poor all his life. He failed the imperial exam many times and passed only in his forties. He worked hard over every word, and his style is cool and spare. People paired him with Jia Dao as "Meng the cold, Jia the thin". His best-known poem is "Song of the Traveling Son".

孟郊（751—814），唐代诗人，一生贫穷，考了很多次才中进士，四十多岁才中。他写诗喜欢苦心琢磨字句，风格清冷瘦硬，和贾岛并称“郊寒岛瘦”。他最有名的诗是《游子吟》。

05

The mood意境

The first two lines use comparisons. The trees grow old side by side, and the ducks live and die together. Lines three and four state the point: a faithful wife should do the same. The last two lines are the most famous. She compares her heart to water in a well, deep and still. No wind can stir it, and no wave will rise again. The image is fresh and easy to grasp. Today no one would ask a person to die for someone else. That was an old belief. But the poem's firm, unchanging will still leaves a strong mark.

前两句用梧桐和鸳鸯打比方：树会相伴到老，鸟会同生共死。三四句说出主题：忠贞的妻子也要这样。最后两句最有名：“波澜誓不起，妾心井中水。”她把自己的心比成井里的水，又深又静，风吹不动，再也不起波浪。这个比喻很新鲜，也很好懂。今天我们不会要求谁为别人去死，这是古代的观念。但诗里那种认定了就不改变的坚定，还是让人印象很深。
