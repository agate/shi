chūn春yè夜xǐ喜yǔ雨

dù杜fǔ甫Du Fu · Tang唐

Welcome Rain on a Spring Night

 

hǎo好yǔ雨zhī知shí时jié节

dāng当chūn春nǎi乃fā发shēng生

suí随fēng风qián潜rù入yè夜

rùn润wù物xì细wú无shēng声

yě野jìng径yún云jù俱hēi黑

jiāng江chuán船huǒ火dú独míng明

xiǎo晓kàn看hóng红shī湿chù处

huā花zhòng重jǐn锦guān官chéng城

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 好雨知时节The good rain seems to know its season;好雨好像知道该在什么时候下，
  2. 当春乃发生just when spring needs it, it comes.正当春天万物需要它的时候，它就来了。
  3. 随风潜入夜It follows the wind and slips quietly into the night,它随着春风，在夜里悄悄地落下，
  4. 润物细无声watering everything, fine and soundless.细细的，没有一点声音，滋润着万物。
  5. 野径云俱黑The field paths and the clouds are all black;田野的小路和天上的云一片漆黑，
  6. 江船火独明only a lamp on a river boat shines bright.只有江上船里的一点灯火格外明亮。
  7. 晓看红湿处At dawn, look where the red is wet with rain:等到天亮，去看那些被雨打湿的红花，
  8. 花重锦官城all of Chengdu is heavy with flowers.整个成都城都开满了沉甸甸的花朵。
02

Words字词注释

知时节zhī shí jié
zhī shí jié: knows the right time. Shí jié means the season.知时节：知道什么时候该下。时节，季节、时候。
当春dāng chūn
dāng chūn: right in spring.当春：正当春天。
乃nǎi
nǎi: then, just then.乃：就。
发生fā shēng
fā shēng: to sprout and grow. Here the spring rain falls and makes everything start to grow.发生：萌发生长。这里指春雨落下，让万物发芽生长。
潜qián
qián: quietly, without anyone noticing.潜：悄悄地，不让人发觉。
润物rùn wù
rùn wù: to moisten all things, giving the plants water to drink.润物：滋润万物，让花草树木喝饱水。
野径yě jìng
yě jìng: paths through the fields.野径：田野里的小路。
俱jù
jù: all, both.俱：都。
江船火jiāng chuán huǒ
jiāng chuán huǒ: the lamp on a boat on the river.江船火：江上船里的灯火。
晓xiǎo
xiǎo: dawn, when the sky is just getting light.晓：天刚亮的时候。
红湿处hóng shī chù
hóng shī chù: the places where red flowers are wet with rain.红湿处：被雨打湿的红花开着的地方。
花重huā zhòng
huā zhòng: the flowers are heavy, full of rain water.花重：花朵沾满雨水，显得沉甸甸的。
锦官城jǐn guān chéng
jǐn guān chéng: another name for Chengdu. Chengdu was famous for brocade, a richly patterned silk, and the court kept an official there to look after it, hence "Brocade Official City".锦官城：成都的别称。古时成都织锦（一种花纹漂亮的丝织品）很有名，朝廷在这里设官管理，所以叫锦官城。
03

How to read it读音

重zhòng
Read zhòng, meaning heavy: the flowers are weighed down with rain. It is not chóng.读 zhòng，是“沉重”的意思：花沾了雨水，变得沉甸甸的。不读 chóng。
04

Background创作背景

In the spring of 761, Du Fu was living in his thatched cottage on the western edge of Chengdu. Before that he had spent years fleeing war. In Chengdu his life was finally a little more settled. He grew vegetables and flowers himself and cared a lot about the crops. One spring night a gentle rain came just when it was needed. He was very happy, and wrote this poem.

上元二年（761年）春天，杜甫住在成都西郊的草堂。在这之前，他经历了几年战乱和逃难，到成都后才过上了比较安定的生活。他亲手种菜种花，很关心庄稼的收成。一个春夜，下起了及时的小雨，他非常高兴，就写下了这首诗。

05

The poet作者

Du Fu (712–770) was a great Tang poet, called the Sage of Poetry. Most of his poems are heavy, about war and the people's suffering. In his years at the Chengdu cottage he wrote many fresh, light poems, and this is the most famous of them.

杜甫（712—770），唐代大诗人，被称为“诗圣”。他的诗大多沉重，写战乱和百姓的苦。在成都草堂的几年，他写了不少清新、轻快的诗，这首《春夜喜雨》就是其中最有名的一首。

06

The mood意境

The title has the word "happy", but the poem never says it again. Still, the joy is everywhere. Du Fu writes the spring rain like a thoughtful person: it knows when to come, comes quietly at night, falls fine and soundless, bothers no one, and simply waters everything. "Watering everything, fine and soundless" is now often used for the quiet care of a good teacher or parent. The third couplet shows the night: all around is black, with only one lamp on a river boat, so the rain must be thick and steady. At the end the poet imagines tomorrow morning: the whole city full of red flowers, wet, heavy and bright, and all the beauty of spring right before your eyes.

题目里有个“喜”字，可整首诗没有一个“喜”字，喜悦却处处都在。诗人把春雨写得像一个懂事的人：它知道什么时候该来，悄悄地在夜里来，细细的，不出声，不打扰人，只默默地滋润万物。“润物细无声”后来常被用来比喻好老师、好父母对孩子不声不响的关怀。第三联写夜景：四周一片黑，只有江上一点灯火，说明雨下得又密又久。最后诗人想象明天早上，满城红花沾着雨水，沉甸甸的，又湿又艳，春天的美好全在眼前。
