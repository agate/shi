dù杜mù牧Du Mu · Tang唐

Mooring on the Qinhuai River

 

yān烟lǒng笼hán寒shuǐ水yuè月lǒng笼shā沙

yè夜bó泊qín秦huái淮jìn近jiǔ酒jiā家

shāng商nǚ女bù不zhī知wáng亡guó国hèn恨

gé隔jiāng江yóu犹chàng唱hòu后tíng庭huā花

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 烟笼寒水月笼沙Mist lies over the cold water, and moonlight lies over the sand.轻烟笼罩着清冷的河水，月光笼罩着河边的沙滩。
  2. 夜泊秦淮近酒家At night I moor my boat on the Qinhuai, close to the wine houses.夜里我把船停在秦淮河边，靠近岸上的酒楼。
  3. 商女不知亡国恨The singing girls do not understand the grief of a fallen country.卖唱的歌女不懂得国家灭亡的悲痛，
  4. 隔江犹唱后庭花Across the water, they are still singing "Flowers in the Back Court".隔着江水，还在唱那首《玉树后庭花》。
02

Words字词注释

烟yān
yān: the mist over the water.烟：水面上的雾气。
笼lǒng
lǒng: to cover over, like a lid placed on top.笼：笼罩，像罩子一样盖住。
寒水hán shuǐ
hán shuǐ: the cold river water.寒水：清冷的河水。
沙shā
shā: the sandy bank by the river.沙：河边的沙滩。
泊bó
bó: to moor, to tie a boat up at the shore.泊：把船停靠在岸边。
秦淮qín huái
qín huái: the Qinhuai River, which flows through Jinling, today's Nanjing. Its banks were lined with wine houses and full of life.秦淮：秦淮河，流过金陵（今江苏南京）城里。河两岸有很多酒楼，很热闹。
近酒家jìn jiǔ jiā
jìn jiǔ jiā: close to the wine houses.近酒家：靠近酒楼。
商女shāng nǚ
shāng nǚ: singing girls who sang for money in the wine houses.商女：在酒楼里卖唱的歌女。
亡国恨wáng guó hèn
wáng guó hèn: the grief of a country that has fallen.亡国恨：国家灭亡的悲痛。
隔江gé jiāng
gé jiāng: across the water, coming from the other bank.隔江：隔着江水，从河对岸传过来。
犹yóu
yóu: still.犹：还，仍然。
后庭花hòu tíng huā
hòu tíng huā: the song "Flowers in the Back Court", written by Chen Shubao, the last emperor of the Chen dynasty in the south. He spent his days in pleasure and ignored the state, and Chen soon fell. Later people called the song "the music of a fallen country".后庭花：歌曲《玉树后庭花》，是南朝陈的最后一个皇帝陈叔宝（陈后主）写的。他整天享乐，不管国事，陈朝很快就灭亡了。后人把这首歌叫作“亡国之音”。
03

How to read it读音

笼lǒng
Here lǒng means to cover over and is now usually read lǒng. Some read it lóng.“笼”在这里是笼罩的意思，现在一般读 lǒng。也有人读 lóng。
笼lǒng
As above, read lǒng.同上，读 lǒng。
泊bó
Bó means to moor a boat. It is read bó, not pō.“泊”是停船的意思，读 bó，不读 pō。
04

Background创作背景

Du Mu wrote this poem when he passed Jinling, today's Nanjing, by boat at night. The exact year is not known. Jinling had been the capital of several southern dynasties, and the Chen dynasty fell there. By Du Mu's time the Tang was in decline, yet many rich and powerful people cared only about feasting and fun. When Du Mu heard a singing girl sing the song of a fallen country, he felt deep sorrow and wrote this poem.

这首诗是杜牧夜里坐船经过金陵（今江苏南京）时写的，具体年份不清楚。金陵在南朝时做过好几个朝代的都城，陈朝就亡在这里。到了杜牧的时代，唐朝已经走下坡路，可是很多有钱有权的人还是只顾吃喝玩乐。杜牧听到歌女唱着亡国的歌，心里很难过，写下了这首诗。

05

The poet作者

Du Mu (803–852) was a poet of the late Tang, from the capital area, today's Xi'an. He cared about the affairs of the state and read a great deal of history. He liked to use events of the past to speak about the present. Such poems are called poems on history, and he was a master of them, as in "Red Cliff" and this poem.

杜牧（803—852），唐朝后期的诗人，字牧之，京兆万年（今陕西西安）人。他关心国家大事，读了很多历史书，喜欢借古代的事来说眼前的事。这类诗叫“咏史诗”，他写得特别好，比如《赤壁》《泊秦淮》。

06

The mood意境

The first line is a hazy picture: mist covers the water, moonlight covers the sand. The word lǒng, to cover, appears twice and makes the whole night grey and chilly. The second line says the poet moors next to the wine houses, a lively place. The last two lines are the heart of the poem. A singing girl is still singing "Flowers in the Back Court", a song that reminds people of a fallen kingdom. On the surface the poet blames the girl. But she only sings what the guests ask for. The ones who truly "do not understand the grief" are the officials and rich men listening and having fun. The poet worries: if things go on like this, will the Tang fall just like the Chen? The scene is cold, and the heart is heavy.

第一句是一幅朦胧的画：雾气罩着河水，月光罩着沙滩，两个“笼”字让整个夜晚都灰蒙蒙、冷清清的。第二句说诗人把船停在酒楼旁边，这里本来很热闹。第三、四句是全诗的重点。歌女还在唱《玉树后庭花》，那是一首让人想起亡国的歌。表面上是说歌女不懂事，其实歌女只是唱客人点的歌，真正“不知亡国恨”的，是那些听歌享乐的达官贵人。诗人担心：再这样下去，唐朝会不会也像陈朝一样？景是冷的，心是沉的。
