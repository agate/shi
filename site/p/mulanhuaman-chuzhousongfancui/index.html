Song lyric · ci to the tune Mu Lan Hua Man, in two stanzas宋词 · 词，词牌《木兰花慢》，双调

mù木lán兰huā花màn慢·chú滁zhōu州sòng送fàn范cuì倅

xīn辛qì弃jí疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

Mu Lan Hua Man: Seeing Off Deputy Fan at Chuzhou

 

lǎo老lái来qíng情wèi味jiǎn减

duì对bié别jiǔ酒qiè怯liú流nián年

kuàng况qū屈zhǐ指zhōng中qiū秋

shí十fēn分hǎo好yuè月

bú不zhào照rén人yuán圆

wú无qíng情shuǐ水dōu都bù不guǎn管

gòng共xī西fēng风zhǐ只děng等sòng送guī归chuán船

qiū秋wǎn晚chún莼lú鲈jiāng江shàng上

yè夜shēn深ér儿nǚ女dēng灯qián前

zhēng征shān衫

biàn便hǎo好qù去cháo朝tiān天

yù玉diàn殿zhèng正sī思xián贤

xiǎng想yè夜bàn半chéng承míng明

liú留jiào教shì视cǎo草

què却qiǎn遣chóu筹biān边

cháng长ān安gù故rén人wèn问wǒ我

dào道xún寻cháng常nì泥jiǔ酒zhǐ只yī依rán然

mù目duàn断qiū秋xiāo霄luò落yàn雁

zuì醉lái来shí时xiǎng响kōng空xián弦

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 老来情味减As I grow old, my zest for life fades.人老了，对什么都提不起兴致，
  2. 对别酒怯流年Facing the farewell wine, I fear the passing years.对着送别的酒，更怕时光飞逝。
  3. 况屈指中秋What is more, counting the days, Mid-Autumn is near.何况屈指一算，中秋就要到了，
  4. 十分好月The moon will be perfectly full,月亮十分圆满，
  5. 不照人圆yet it will not shine on us together.却照不到人的团圆。
  6. 无情水都不管The heartless river cares for nothing.无情的江水什么都不管，
  7. 共西风只等送归船With the west wind, it only waits to carry your boat away.只顾和西风一起送走你回去的船。
  8. 秋晚莼鲈江上On autumn evenings you will eat water shield and perch on the river,秋天傍晚，你会在江上吃到莼菜和鲈鱼，
  9. 夜深儿女灯前and late at night gather with your children by the lamp.深夜和儿女在灯前团聚。
  10. 征衫Put on your travelling clothes.穿上出行的衣服，
  11. 便好去朝天It is just the time to go and see the emperor,正好去京城朝见皇帝，
  12. 玉殿正思贤for the court is longing for able men.朝廷正在寻求贤才。
  13. 想夜半承明I picture you at midnight in the palace,我想象你半夜在宫中，
  14. 留教视草kept there to draft decrees,被留下来起草诏书，
  15. 却遣筹边then sent to plan the border defence.又被派去谋划边防大事。
  16. 长安故人问我If old friends in the capital ask about me,京城的老朋友要是问起我，
  17. 道寻常泥酒只依然tell them I am the same as ever, still hooked on wine.就说我还是老样子，照旧贪杯。
  18. 目断秋霄落雁I watch the geese in the autumn sky until they vanish,我望着秋空里的飞雁，直到看不见，
  19. 醉来时响空弦and when drunk, I can only twang an empty bowstring.喝醉的时候，只能拉响空空的弓弦。
02

Words字词注释

情味qíng wèi
qíng wèi: zest, the joy of living.情味：兴致，对生活的热情。
怯qiè
qiè: to fear.怯：害怕。
流年liú nián
liú nián: time flowing away like water.流年：像水一样流走的时光。
况kuàng
kuàng: what is more.况：何况，更何况。
屈指qū zhǐ
qū zhǐ: counting on one's fingers; it is coming soon.屈指：弯着手指数日子，指算来很快就到。
十分shí fēn
shí fēn: completely, perfectly.十分：非常，完全。
不照人圆bú zhào rén yuán
bù zhào rén yuán: the moon is round, but it does not shine on people reunited.不照人圆：月亮圆了，却照不到人团圆。
共西风gòng xī fēng
gòng xī fēng: together with the west wind, which is the autumn wind.共西风：和秋风一起。西风就是秋风。
只等zhǐ děng
zhǐ děng: only cares to. Some editions have a word meaning the same.只等：只顾着。有的本子作“只管”。
莼鲈chún lú
chún lú: water shield and perch. In the Jin dynasty, Zhang Han, serving far from home, missed the water shield soup and perch of his hometown when the autumn wind rose, so he quit and went home. It came to mean longing for home.莼鲈：莼菜和鲈鱼。晋代张翰在外做官，秋风起时想起家乡的莼菜羹和鲈鱼，就辞官回家了。后来用它代指思乡、回家。
征衫zhēng shān
zhēng shān: travelling clothes.征衫：出远门穿的衣服。
便好biàn hǎo
biàn hǎo: it is just the time to.便好：正好，正可以。
朝天cháo tiān
cháo tiān: to go to the capital and see the emperor.朝天：去京城朝见皇帝。
玉殿yù diàn
yù diàn: the jade palace; the court.玉殿：皇宫，指朝廷。
思贤sī xián
sī xián: longing for able men.思贤：想得到有才能的人。
承明chéng míng
chéng míng: the Chengming lodge, where Han officials stayed on duty in the palace; here, the court.承明：承明庐，汉代官员在宫中值班住的地方，这里指朝廷。
视草shì cǎo
shì cǎo: to draft decrees for the emperor.视草：替皇帝起草诏书。
却遣què qiǎn
què qiǎn: then sends you.却遣：又派你去。
筹边chóu biān
chóu biān: to plan the defence of the borders.筹边：谋划边防的大事。
长安cháng ān
cháng ān: the old Han and Tang capital; here it stands for Lin'an, today's Hangzhou, the Southern Song capital.长安：本是汉唐的都城，这里借指南宋的京城临安（今浙江杭州）。
寻常xún cháng
xún cháng: as usual. Some editions word this line a little differently, with the same meaning of being hooked on wine.寻常：平常，照旧。有的本子这一句作“道愁肠殢酒只依然”。
泥酒nì jiǔ
nì jiǔ: being hooked on wine.泥酒：贪酒，离不开酒。
依然yī rán
yī rán: still the same.依然：还是老样子。
目断mù duàn
mù duàn: gazing until it is out of sight.目断：一直望到看不见。
秋霄qiū xiāo
qiū xiāo: the autumn sky.秋霄：秋天的天空。
响空弦xiǎng kōng xián
xiǎng kōng xián: to twang a bow with no arrow. In the Warring States era, the archer Geng Lei brought down a wounded goose just by twanging an empty string. Here it shows the poet has skill but no chance to use it.响空弦：拉响没搭箭的弓弦。战国时更羸只拉空弦，就把一只受过伤的大雁吓得掉了下来。这里写自己空有本领，没地方施展。
03

How to read it读音

朝cháo
In cháo tiān, to go and see the emperor, this character is read cháo.“朝天”是朝见皇帝，“朝”读 cháo。
教jiào
Here this character means to let or have someone do. Textbooks usually read it jiào in this sense. Some read it jiāo.“教”在这里是让、使的意思，现在课本里这种用法一般读 jiào（如“不教胡马度阴山”）。也有人读 jiāo。
泥nì
In nì jiǔ, to be hooked on wine, this character is read nì, meaning to cling to.“泥酒”是贪酒、离不开酒，“泥”读 nì，是缠住、沉迷的意思。
04

Background创作背景

In 1172, under Emperor Xiaozong, Xin Qiji was the governor of Chuzhou, in today's Anhui. The word cui in the title means a deputy governor. This deputy, surnamed Fan (usually thought to be Fan Ang), had finished his term and was leaving, around the time of the Mid-Autumn Festival. Xin wrote this ci to see him off. He wishes Fan a great welcome at court, and also lets out his own frustration: he wants to serve his country but gets no chance.

宋孝宗乾道八年（1172年），辛弃疾在滁州（今安徽滁州）做知州，也就是一州的长官。“倅”是通判，知州的副手。这位姓范的通判（一般认为是范昂）任满要离开，正赶上中秋前后，辛弃疾写了这首词给他送行。词里一面祝他回朝受到重用，一面也说出自己想为国家出力却没有机会的苦闷。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) came from Licheng, today's Jinan in Shandong. When he was born, the north was already ruled by the Jin. In his twenties he led an uprising against them and then came south to the Song. His great dream was to win back the north, but he was usually sent to govern local towns instead of going to war. His ci are bold and strong, and often sad that his great plans never came true.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），字幼安，号稼轩，历城（今山东济南）人。他出生时北方已被金国占领，二十多岁就带兵起义，后来回到南宋。他一生最大的心愿是收复北方，却常常被派去做地方官，不能上战场。他的词豪迈有力，也常带着壮志难酬的悲凉。

06

The mood意境

He begins: as I grow old, my zest for life fades, and facing the farewell wine, I fear how fast time flies. It is the Mid-Autumn Festival, the moon is perfectly round, yet people must part. The heartless river only joins the west wind to send the homebound boat away. Then he imagines his friend's warm homecoming: water shield soup and perch on the river in autumn, and family gathered by the lamp at night. The second stanza turns to encouragement. Put on your travelling clothes and go to the emperor; the court is looking for able men. Perhaps you will be kept late at night to draft decrees, or sent to plan the border defence. Last, he asks his friend to carry a message to old friends in the capital: tell them I am the same as ever, fond of wine. Watching geese in the autumn sky, when drunk, he can only twang an empty bowstring. This last line shows a hero with no battle to fight, and it hits hard.

开头说：人老了，兴致也淡了，对着送别的酒，更怕时光飞逝。偏偏又是中秋，月亮十分圆满，人却要分开。江水无情，只管和西风一起送走归去的船。接着他替朋友想象回家的温暖：秋天江上吃莼菜鲈鱼，夜里和儿女在灯前团聚。下片换成鼓励：穿上行装去朝见皇帝吧，朝廷正在寻找人才。想象你深夜被留下来起草诏书，又被派去谋划边防大事。最后他托朋友带话给京城的老友：就说我还是老样子，爱喝酒。望着秋空里的飞雁，喝醉了只能拉一拉空弓弦。这一句写出了英雄没处用武的苦闷，很有力量。
