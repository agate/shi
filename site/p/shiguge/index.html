shí石gǔ鼓gē歌

hán韩yù愈Han Yu · Tang唐

Song of the Stone Drums

The corpus uses several old or variant character forms, here written in standard simplified form: jiàn (sword), xiān (immortal), zhēng (summon), zhān (felt) and yán (eaves). In line 40 the corpus has zhī (respectfully), a slip in the machine conversion; the original character means 'only', and the common text writes zhǐ (only). In line 64 the corpus writes biàn with the 'distinguish' character; the common text uses the 'debate' character.

语料里有几处旧字形或异体字，这里改用通行的简体：“劒”作“剑”，“僊”作“仙”，“徴”作“征”，“氊”作“毡”，“簷”作“檐”。“十鼓祗载”的“祗”是繁简转换的错误，原字是“祇”，意思是“只”，通行本写作“只”。“愿借辨口”的“辨”，通行本作“辩”。

02

Words字词注释

张生zhāng shēng
zhāng shēng: a scholar named Zhang. Usually taken to be the poet Zhang Ji; another view says Zhang Che.张生：姓张的读书人。一般认为是诗人张籍，一说是张彻。
石鼓文shí gǔ wén
shí gǔ wén: the writing carved on the Stone Drums. Here it means a paper rubbing of it.石鼓文：刻在石鼓上的文字。这里指从石鼓上拓下来的纸本。
少陵shào líng
shào líng: Du Fu, who called himself the old man of Shaoling.少陵：指杜甫，他自称“少陵野老”。
谪仙zhé xiān
zhé xiān: Li Bai. He Zhizhang called him an immortal sent down from heaven.谪仙：指李白。贺知章说他是从天上被贬到人间的仙人。
才薄cái bó
cái bó: of small talent, a modest way to speak of oneself.才薄：才华浅薄，是谦虚的话。
将奈石鼓何jiāng nài shí gǔ hé
jiāng nài shí gǔ hé: what can I do with the Stone Drums? That is: how could I write well enough about them?将奈石鼓何：能拿石鼓怎么办呢？意思是写不好石鼓。
周纲zhōu gāng
zhōu gāng: the rule and laws of Zhou. Gāng is the main rope of a net, a picture for a state's laws.周纲：周朝的统治和法度。纲，原来指渔网上的总绳，比喻国家的法纪。
陵迟líng chí
líng chí: to decline, getting worse day by day.陵迟：衰落，一天天坏下去。
四海沸sì hǎi fèi
sì hǎi fèi: the whole land boiling like water, meaning great unrest.四海沸：天下像开水一样翻滚，形容大乱。
宣王xuān wáng
xuān wáng: King Xuan of Zhou, a Western Zhou king who made the weakened Zhou strong again for a time.宣王：周宣王，西周的君王，他让衰落的周朝一度重新强盛。
天戈tiān gē
tiān gē: the Son of Heaven's spear, meaning the king's army.天戈：天子的兵器，指天子的军队。
明堂míng táng
míng táng: the great hall where ancient kings held ceremonies and received the lords.明堂：古代天子举行大典、接见诸侯的大殿。
朝贺cháo hè
cháo hè: to come before the king and congratulate him.朝贺：来朝见天子，向他祝贺。
剑佩jiàn pèi
jiàn pèi: swords and jade pendants, worn by nobles in ancient times.剑佩：剑和玉佩，古代贵族挂在身上。
鸣相磨míng xiāng mó
míng xiāng mó: knocking and rubbing together, making a ringing sound.鸣相磨：互相碰撞摩擦，发出声音。
搜sōu
sōu: to hunt.搜：打猎。
岐阳qí yáng
qí yáng: the south side of Mount Qi, in today's Shaanxi. In old Chinese the south side of a hill is called yáng.岐阳：岐山的南面，在今天的陕西。古人把山的南面叫“阳”。
骋雄俊chěng xióng jùn
chěng xióng jùn: letting brave men and fine horses gallop freely.骋雄俊：让勇士和骏马尽情奔驰。
遮罗zhē luó
zhē luó: to block in and catch with nets.遮罗：拦住，用网捉住。
镌功勒成juān gōng lè chéng
juān gōng lè chéng: to carve the king's deeds in stone. Both juān and lè mean to carve.镌功勒成：把功业刻在石头上。镌、勒都是刻的意思。
告万世gào wàn shì
gào wàn shì: to tell people of all ages to come.告万世：告诉千秋万代的后人。
隳huī
huī: to break down; here, to cut into.隳：毁坏，这里指凿开。
嵯峨cuó é
cuó é: high, steep hills.嵯峨：高高的山。
从臣cóng chén
cóng chén: the officials who followed the king.从臣：跟随君王的大臣。
咸xián
xián: all.咸：都。
拣选jiǎn xuǎn
jiǎn xuǎn: to choose.拣选：挑选。
撰刻zhuàn kè
zhuàn kè: to compose the words and then carve them.撰刻：写好文字，再刻上去。
山阿shān ē
shān ē: the bend or foot of a hill.山阿：山弯、山脚。

04

Background创作背景

The Stone Drums are ten drum-shaped stones found in the early Tang near today's Baoji in Shaanxi. They are carved with very old writing, called the Stone Drum script. Han Yu believed they were made in the time of King Xuan of Zhou; most scholars today think they are carvings from the state of Qin in the Spring and Autumn or Warring States period. In 806, when Han Yu was an Erudite at the national academy, he asked for the drums to be moved to the Imperial Academy for safekeeping, but nothing was done. Later his friend Zhang brought him a rubbing of the writing and asked for a poem, and he wrote this long one. Because the poem says 'six years I have looked west', it is usually dated to around 811. Today the drums are kept in the Palace Museum in Beijing.

石鼓是唐朝初年在今天陕西宝鸡一带发现的十块鼓形石头，上面刻着很古老的文字，叫石鼓文。韩愈认为它们是周宣王时刻的；现在的学者大多认为是春秋战国时秦国的刻石。元和元年（806年），韩愈做国子博士，曾请求把石鼓运到太学保护起来，没有被采纳。后来朋友张生拿来石鼓文的拓本，请他作诗，他就写了这首长诗。诗里说“六年西顾”，所以一般认为写于元和六年（811年）左右。今天，石鼓收藏在北京故宫博物院。

05

The poet作者

Han Yu (768–824), also called Han Tuizhi, was a Tang writer and the first of the Eight Great Prose Masters of Tang and Song. He urged people to write plain, strong prose like the ancients, not just pretty words. His poems, too, love rare words, hard-edged lines and long arguments. The chain of strange comparisons in this poem is his style. He honored Confucian learning, so he cared deeply about ancient relics like the Stone Drums.

韩愈（768—824），字退之，唐代文学家，“唐宋八大家”之首。他提倡学习先秦两汉朴实有力的古文，反对只讲辞藻的文章。他写诗也爱用奇特的字、硬朗的句子和大段议论。这首诗里一连串奇特的比喻，正是他的风格。他尊崇儒学，所以格外看重石鼓这样的上古文物。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a long essay written in verse, in three parts. The first tells where the drums came from: King Xuan of Zhou hunted and won glory, stone was cut and carved, and the drums stood in the wild through wind and rain, as if guarded by spirits. The second part describes the writing. Han Yu piles up comparisons: the strokes are like dragons cut apart, like soaring phoenixes, like crossing coral trees, like golden ropes and iron chains. They let us 'see' the power and life of the old script. Then he complains that Confucius left it out of the Book of Songs. The third part is about himself. He once tried to move the drums to the academy to protect them, but the high officials dragged their feet, and the drums were left for herd boys to strike and oxen to rub. Here you can feel the worry and heartache of someone who loves old things and watches a treasure being ruined. The last line, 'Alas, my wish will come to nothing', is one long sigh.

这首诗像一篇用诗写成的长文章，分三段。第一段讲石鼓的来历：周宣王打猎立功，凿石刻字，石鼓在山野里经历风雨，好像有鬼神守护。第二段写石鼓上的字：韩愈一口气用了好几个比喻，说笔画像被砍断的蛟龙，像飞舞的凤凰，像交错的珊瑚树，像金绳铁索，让我们“看见”这些古字的力量和生气。接着他埋怨孔子编《诗经》漏掉了它。第三段讲自己：他曾想把石鼓运到太学保护，大官们却拖着不办，石鼓只能被牧童敲打、被牛磨角。读到这里，你能感到一个爱古物的人眼看宝贝被糟蹋时的着急和心痛。最后一句“呜呼吾意其蹉跎”，是一声长长的叹息。
