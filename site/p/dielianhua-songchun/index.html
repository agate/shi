dié蝶liàn恋huā花·sòng送chūn春

Butterflies Love Flowers: Seeing Spring Off

yù欲xì系qīng青chūn春

mò莫yě也chóu愁rén人kǔ苦

In the fourth line the common text has yóu zì, "still", where the corpus has dú zì, "alone". We follow the common text.

第四句通行本作“犹自风前飘柳絮”，语料作“独自风前飘柳絮”。这里用通行本。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 楼外垂杨千万缕Outside the tower the weeping willows hang down in thousands of strands.楼外的垂柳，垂下千万根柳条，
  2. 欲系青春I want to use them to tie spring down,我想用它们拴住春天，
  3. 少住春还去but spring stayed only a little while and left all the same.春天只停了一会儿，还是走了。
  4. 犹自风前飘柳絮The willow fluff still drifts before the wind,柳絮还在风中飘着，
  5. 随春且看归何处as if following spring to see where it goes home.好像要跟着春天，去看看它回到哪里。
  6. 绿满山川闻杜宇Green fills the hills and rivers, and I hear the cuckoo cry.山川一片碧绿，耳边传来杜鹃的叫声。
  7. 便做无情Even if the cuckoo has no feelings,就算杜鹃是无情的鸟，
  8. 莫也愁人苦could it be grieving for my sorrow too?难道它也在为人的愁苦而悲啼吗？
  9. 把酒送春春不语I raise my cup to see spring off, but spring says not a word.我端起酒杯送别春天，春天却一句话也不说。
  10. 黄昏却下潇潇雨And at dusk, of all things, a heavy rain begins to fall.到了黄昏，偏偏又下起了潇潇的雨。
02

Words字词注释

垂杨chuí yáng
chuí yáng: weeping willows, with long hanging branches.垂杨：垂柳，枝条长长地垂下来。
千万缕qiān wàn lǚ
qiān wàn lǚ: thousands of willow strands, like threads.千万缕：成千上万根柳条，像一缕缕丝线。
欲系yù xì
yù xì: to want to tie down.欲系：想要拴住。
青春qīng chūn
qīng chūn: here it means springtime.青春：这里指春天。
少住shǎo zhù
shǎo zhù: to stay a little while.少住：稍微停留一会儿。
犹自yóu zì
yóu zì: still, even now.犹自：仍然、还在。
柳絮liǔ xù
liǔ xù: willow fluff, the willow's seeds with white down that fly in the wind in late spring.柳絮：柳树的种子，带着白色绒毛，春末随风飘飞。
随春且看suí chūn qiě kàn
suí chūn qiě kàn: to follow spring for now and see where it goes. Qiě means for the time being.随春且看：跟着春天去，看看它到底回到哪里。且，姑且、暂且。
绿满山川lǜ mǎn shān chuān
lǜ mǎn shān chuān: hills and rivers all green, as in early summer.绿满山川：山山水水都是一片绿，已是初夏的样子。
杜宇dù yǔ
dù yǔ: the cuckoo. Legend says an ancient king of Shu named Du Yu turned into this bird after he died. Its cry is mournful and sounds like "better to go home".杜宇：杜鹃鸟，又叫子规。传说是古代蜀国国王杜宇死后变的，叫声很悲，像在说“不如归去”。
便做biàn zuò
biàn zuò: even if.便做：即使，就算是。
莫也mò yě
mò yě: could it be that... too?莫也：难道也……
愁人苦chóu rén kǔ
chóu rén kǔ: to grieve over people's sorrow.愁人苦：为人的苦而发愁。
把酒bǎ jiǔ
bǎ jiǔ: holding a cup of wine.把酒：端着酒杯。
却què
què: instead, of all things.却：反而，偏偏。
潇潇xiāo xiāo
xiāo xiāo: the sound of wind and rain, a heavy, steady rain.潇潇：形容风雨声，雨下得又急又密。

04

Background创作背景

This is a lyric about seeing spring off, about not wanting it to leave. Zhu Shuzhen's marriage was unhappy, and she often carried a sorrow she could not speak of. So when she writes about spring leaving, she is also grieving her own youth slipping away. The year it was written is not known. Many of her works were burned after her death. What survives was later gathered into a book called Duanchang Ci, Lyrics of a Broken Heart.

这是一首送春词，写春天离去时的不舍。朱淑真一生婚姻不顺，心里常有说不出的愁，所以她写春天的离去，也带着对自己青春流逝的感伤。这首词具体写于哪一年已经不清楚。她的许多作品在她死后被烧掉，留下来的由后人收进《断肠词》。

05

The poet作者

Zhu Shuzhen, who called herself the Hermit of Quiet Dwelling, was a well-known woman poet of the Song dynasty, usually said to come from Qiantang, today's Hangzhou. Very little is recorded about her life, and her dates are not known. It is said her marriage was unhappy and she lived in sorrow. Her poems are tender and often about loneliness and grief. People often mention her alongside Li Qingzhao.

朱淑真，号幽栖居士，宋代有名的女词人，一般认为是钱塘（今浙江杭州）人。她的生平记载很少，生卒年也不清楚。据说她婚姻不幸福，一生抑郁。她的诗词感情细腻，多写孤独和愁苦，后人常把她和李清照相提并论。

06

The mood意境

This lyric treats spring like a friend who is about to leave. The opening is full of imagination. Outside the tower hang thousands of willow strands, and she wants to use them to tie spring down. But spring stays only a moment and goes anyway. The willow fluff drifts in the wind, as if it cannot bear to part with spring and wants to follow it home. In the second half the hills and rivers are all green, and the cuckoo is crying. She asks: even if the cuckoo has no feelings, is it grieving for people too? At the end she raises a cup to see spring off. Spring says nothing, but at dusk the rain begins to fall. The rain seems like spring's answer, and also like her tears.

这首词把春天当成一个要走的朋友。开头很有想象力：楼外有千万根柳条，她想用柳条把春天拴住，可春天只停了一下，还是走了。柳絮在风里飘，好像舍不得春天，要跟着去看看它到底回哪里。下半首，山川全绿了，杜鹃在叫。她说，杜鹃就算无情，难道也在为人发愁吗？最后她端着酒为春天送行，春天一句话也不说，黄昏时却下起了雨。这场雨，像是春天的回答，也像是她的眼泪。
