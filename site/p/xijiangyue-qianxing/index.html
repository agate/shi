xī西jiāng江yuè月·qiǎn遣xìng兴

xīn辛qì弃jí疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

West River Moon: Venting My Feelings

 

zuì醉lǐ里qiě且tān贪huān欢xiào笑

yào要chóu愁nǎ那dé得gōng工fū夫

jìn近lái来shǐ始jué觉gǔ古rén人shū书

xìn信zhuó著quán全wú无shì是chù处

zuó昨yè夜sōng松biān边zuì醉dǎo倒

wèn问sōng松wǒ我zuì醉hé何rú如

zhǐ只yí疑sōng松dòng动yào要lái来fú扶

yǐ以shǒu手tuī推sōng松yuē曰qù去

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 醉里且贪欢笑When I am drunk, let me laugh and be merry for now;喝醉了，就先尽情地欢笑吧，
  2. 要愁那得工夫if I wanted to be sad, where would I find the time?想要发愁，哪里有这个时间？
  3. 近来始觉古人书Only lately have I found that the books of the ancients,最近我才发觉，古人书里的话，
  4. 信著全无是处if you believe them fully, are no use at all.要是全都相信，就一点用也没有。
  5. 昨夜松边醉倒Last night I fell down drunk beside a pine,昨天夜里，我醉倒在松树旁边，
  6. 问松我醉何如and asked the pine, 'How drunk am I?'问松树：“我醉得怎么样？”
  7. 只疑松动要来扶I thought the pine was moving, coming over to help me up,只觉得松树摇摇晃晃，要过来扶我，
  8. 以手推松曰去so I pushed it with my hand and said, 'Go away!'我用手一推松树，说：“走开！”
02

Words字词注释

且qiě
qiě: for now, for the time being.且：暂且、姑且，先这样。
贪欢笑tān huān xiào
tān huān xiào: to laugh and be merry to the full. Tān means not wanting to stop.贪欢笑：尽情地欢笑。贪，舍不得停。
要愁yào chóu
yào chóu: if I wanted to be sad.要愁：要想发愁。
那得工夫nǎ dé gōng fū
nǎ dé gōng fū: where would I find the time?那得工夫：哪有时间。
始觉shǐ jué
shǐ jué: only now realise.始觉：才觉得、才发现。
古人书gǔ rén shū
gǔ rén shū: books written by the sages of old.古人书：古代圣贤写的书。
信著xìn zhuó
xìn zhuó: to believe in them.信著：相信它。著，同“着”。
全无是处quán wú shì chù
quán wú shì chù: no use at all, not right at all. He says this in anger: what the old books teach does not work in the real world.全无是处：一点用处也没有，一点也不对。这是气话：古书上讲的道理，在现实里根本行不通。
何如hé rú
hé rú: how is it?何如：怎么样。
只疑zhǐ yí
zhǐ yí: I suspected, it seemed to me.只疑：只怀疑、只觉得。
曰yuē
yuē: to say.曰：说。
去qù
qù: go away!去：走开！
03

How to read it读音

那nǎ
Nǎ dé means 'how could there be'. This character is used for the question word nǎ (how, where) and is read nǎ.“那得”就是“哪得”，“那”在这里是疑问词“哪”，读 nǎ。
夫fū
In modern speech gōng fu ends with a light, toneless fu. When reading the poem aloud it is usually read fū.“工夫”在现代汉语里读 gōngfu，“夫”读轻声；读诗时一般读 fū。
著zhuó
This character is the old form of zhuó here, meaning to hold on to, to stick to.“信著”的“著”同“着”，读 zhuó，有“附着、执着”的意思。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually thought to have been written while Xin Qiji, removed from office, was living at Daihu Lake in Shangrao, in today's Jiangxi. He longed to lead an army and win back the lost north, but the court would not use him, so he spent many years in the countryside. With nowhere to pour out his frustration, he turned to wine, and wrote this lyric that looks funny but is really sad. The title means letting out one's feelings.

这首词一般认为是辛弃疾被罢官、闲居上饶带湖（今江西上饶）时写的。他一心想带兵收复北方失地，朝廷却不用他，只能在乡下一住就是很多年。心里的苦闷没处说，他就借喝酒来排遣，写下了这首看起来好笑、其实很心酸的词。“遣兴”就是抒发心情的意思。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207), known as Jiaxuan, was a great ci writer of the Southern Song. As a young man he led troops against the Jin in the north. After coming south he kept urging the court to win back the lost land, but he was removed from office many times and lived in the countryside for nearly twenty years in all. His lyrics often use wine and landscapes to voice his anger at injustice, bold on the surface and full of grief beneath.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），号稼轩，南宋大词人。他年轻时在北方带兵抗金，南下以后一直主张收复失地，却多次被罢官，在乡间闲居了将近二十年。他的词常常借喝酒、山水来写心中的不平，豪放中带着悲愤。

06

The mood意境

The first half sounds like drunken talk: he is too busy laughing to be sad, and the old books are worthless. But he means the opposite. The books of the sages teach loyalty to one's ruler and country, yet when he tried to serve his country he was sent home. That is why he says their teachings do not work. The second half is a funny little story. Drunk beside a pine, he asks it how drunk he is. When the pine seems to sway, he thinks it wants to help him up, so he shoves it and says, 'Go away!' That shove shows his stubborn pride: he will not give in, and he will not take help. It makes us laugh, and after the laugh we feel his hurt and his refusal to accept his fate.

上半首像醉话：只顾笑，没空愁；古人的书，信了也白信。其实这是反话。他读的圣贤书教人忠君爱国，可他想报国却被赶回了家，所以才说书里的道理行不通。下半首是一个好玩的小故事：醉倒在松树边，问松树自己醉得怎样；看松树晃动，以为它要来扶，就推它一把说“去！”。这一推，推出了他不服输、不要人帮的倔强。读起来让人发笑，笑过之后，又能感到他心里的委屈和不甘。
