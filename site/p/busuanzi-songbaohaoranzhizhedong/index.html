卜算子·送鲍浩然之浙东

王观Wang Guan · Song宋

Bu Suan Zi: Seeing Bao Haoran Off to Eastern Zhejiang

 

shuǐ水shì是yǎn眼bō波héng横

shān山shì是méi眉fēng峰jù聚

yù欲wèn问xíng行rén人qù去nǎ那biān边

méi眉yǎn眼yíng盈yíng盈chù处

cái才shǐ始sòng送chūn春guī归

yòu又sòng送jūn君guī归qù去

ruò若dào到jiāng江dōng东gǎn赶shàng上chūn春

qiān千wàn万hé和chūn春zhù住

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 水是眼波横The water is like a lovely woman's flowing glance;水，像美人流动的眼波；
  2. 山是眉峰聚the hills are like her knitted brows.山，像美人皱起的眉峰。
  3. 欲问行人去那边I want to ask my travelling friend: where are you going?想问远行的朋友要到哪里去？
  4. 眉眼盈盈处To the place where hills and waters are lovely as eyes and brows.就是那山水像眉眼一样美好的地方。
  5. 才始送春归I have only just seen spring off,刚刚才送走了春天，
  6. 又送君归去and now I must see you off too.现在又要送你回去。
  7. 若到江东赶上春If you reach the east of the river and catch up with spring,如果你到了江东，赶上了春天，
  8. 千万和春住be sure to stay there with spring.千万要和春天住在一起，留住它。
02

Words字词注释

眼波横yǎn bō héng
yǎn bō héng: like the flowing glance of a beautiful woman's eyes. Yǎn bō means eyes as bright and lively as ripples.眼波横：像美人流动的目光。眼波，形容眼睛像水波一样明亮流动。横，流动的样子。
眉峰聚méi fēng jù
méi fēng jù: like a beautiful woman's knitted brows. Méi fēng is the arch of the eyebrow; jù means drawn together.眉峰聚：像美人皱起的眉头。眉峰，眉毛的高处。聚，皱在一起。
行人xíng rén
xíng rén: a traveller, here the poet's friend Bao Haoran.行人：出远门的人，这里指朋友鲍浩然。
那边nǎ biān
nǎ biān: which way, where.那边：哪边，哪里。
眉眼盈盈处méi yǎn yíng yíng chù
méi yǎn yíng yíng chù: where the hills and waters are lovely as eyes and brows, that is, the beautiful land of eastern Zhejiang. Some say it also hints at the loved ones waiting at home. Yíng yíng means lovely.眉眼盈盈处：山水像眉眼一样美好的地方，指浙东的好山好水。一说也指朋友家乡等着他的亲人。盈盈，美好的样子。
才始cái shǐ
cái shǐ: just now.才始：刚刚。
送春归sòng chūn guī
sòng chūn guī: to see spring off.送春归：送走春天。
君jūn
jūn: you, meaning Bao Haoran.君：你，指鲍浩然。
江东jiāng dōng
jiāng dōng: the land south of the lower Yangtze, here eastern Zhejiang.江东：长江下游的南岸一带，这里指浙东。
千万qiān wàn
qiān wàn: be sure to.千万：一定，务必。
和春住hé chūn zhù
hé chūn zhù: to stay together with spring, to keep spring.和春住：和春天住在一起，留住春天。
03

How to read it读音

那nǎ
Here this character works like the word for "which" and is read nǎ, so the phrase asks "which way". Some read it nà, meaning "over there".“那”在这里同“哪”，读 nǎ，“那边”就是“哪边”，问去哪里。一说读 nà，指那一边。
04

Background创作背景

This is a farewell lyric. Wang Guan's friend Bao Haoran was going home to eastern Zhejiang, and Wang Guan wrote this lyric to see him off. Little is known today about Bao Haoran's life. From the lyric, we can tell the parting came just as spring had ended.

这是一首送别词。王观的朋友鲍浩然要回浙东（今浙江东部）的家乡，王观写了这首词送他。鲍浩然的生平今天已经不太清楚了。从词里看，送别的时候正是春天刚刚过去。

05

The poet作者

Wang Guan, courtesy name Tongsou, was a Northern Song lyric poet from Rugao (today's Rugao, Jiangsu). He passed the highest exam and served as an official, but was later removed from office and called himself the Banished Guest. His lyrics have fresh ideas and playful language, and this one is his most famous.

王观，字通叟，如皋（今江苏如皋）人，北宋词人。他考中进士，做过官，后来被罢官，自号“逐客”。他的词构思新奇，语言俏皮，这首《卜算子》就是他最有名的作品。

06

The mood意境

Most farewell poems are sad, but this one is playful and warm. The opening is fresh. It does not say the water is like eyes and the hills like brows. It says the water is a glance and the hills are brows, as if all the land of eastern Zhejiang is lovingly waiting for the friend to come home. The second stanza says: I have just seen spring off, and now I see you off. Two partings at once bring a little sadness. But the poet does not weep. He tells his friend: if you reach the east and catch up with spring there, be sure to stay with it. His good wishes sound light and lovely, and they make the reader smile.

送别的诗大多很伤感，这首却写得又俏皮又温暖。开头就很新奇：不说水像眼睛、山像眉毛，而是反过来说，水就是眼波，山就是眉峰，好像整个浙东的山水都在含情脉脉地等着朋友回家。下阕说：刚送走春天，又要送你，两次送别叠在一起，有一点不舍。可是词人没有哭哭啼啼，而是叮嘱朋友：到了江东要是还能赶上春天，千万要和春天住在一起。把对朋友的祝福说得轻松又美好，读了让人会心一笑。
