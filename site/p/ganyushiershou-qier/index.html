Thoughts Stirred by Encounters (No. 2 of 12)

In this collection line one begins with "quiet woods" instead of "quiet man" as in the common text. In line seven it uses the old form of the character for "sink"; the common text uses the modern form, with the same meaning.

这本诗集里第一句作“幽林归独卧”，通行本作“幽人归独卧”；第七句“飞沈”，“沈”是“沉”的古写法，通行本作“飞沉”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 幽人归独卧I, a man in retreat, come home to live alone;我这个隐居的人，回到家里独自闲居，
  2. 滞虑洗孤清I wash away the worries stuck in my heart and keep it lonely and clean.洗掉积在心里的忧虑，只留下孤独清白。
  3. 持此谢高鸟I would hand this feeling to a high-flying bird,我想把这份心意，托付给高飞的鸟，
  4. 因之传远情and ask it to carry my longing far away.请它把我的思念带到远方。
  5. 日夕怀空意Morning and night I hold this feeling in vain;从早到晚，我白白怀着这份心意，
  6. 人谁感至精who will ever be moved by my deepest sincerity?有谁能被我最真诚的心感动呢？
  7. 飞沉理自隔The flying bird and the sunken fish are far apart by nature;高飞的鸟和水底的鱼，本来就隔得很远，
  8. 何所慰吾诚what then could comfort my faithful heart?又能用什么来安慰我这片诚心呢？

Words字词注释

幽人yōu rén
yōu rén: a person living in retreat, here the poet himself.幽人：隐居的人，这里是诗人自己。
独卧dú wò
dú wò: lying alone, that is, living quietly by himself.独卧：一个人躺着，指独自闲居。
滞虑zhì lǜ
zhì lǜ: worries stuck in the heart that will not go away.滞虑：积在心里、放不下的忧虑。
洗孤清xǐ gū qīng
xǐ gū qīng: washing the worries away, leaving a lonely, clean heart.洗孤清：把忧虑洗掉，只剩下孤独、清白的心。
持此chí cǐ
chí cǐ: holding this feeling.持此：拿着这份心意。
谢xiè
xiè: to tell, to entrust to.谢：告诉，托付。
高鸟gāo niǎo
gāo niǎo: a bird flying high.高鸟：高飞的鸟。
因之yīn zhī
yīn zhī: through it, that is, by way of the bird.因之：通过它，就是托这只鸟。
远情yuǎn qíng
yuǎn qíng: feelings for someone far away, meaning the court and the emperor.远情：对远方的人（指朝廷、君王）的思念和心意。
日夕rì xī
rì xī: from morning to night.日夕：从早到晚。
怀空意huái kōng yì
huái kōng yì: holding on to a feeling that comes to nothing. Kōng means in vain.怀空意：怀着一片心意，却没有用处。空，白白地。
至精zhì jīng
zhì jīng: the most sincere heart.至精：最真诚的心。
飞沉fēi chén
fēi chén: the bird flying in the sky and the fish deep in the water, a picture of those high up and those low down.飞沉：天上飞的鸟和水里沉的鱼，比喻地位高的人和地位低的人。
理自隔lǐ zì gé
lǐ zì gé: by nature they are far apart.理自隔：按道理本来就隔得很远。

hé suǒ wèi: with what could I comfort.何所慰：用什么来安慰。

wú chéng: my sincere heart. Wú means I or my.吾诚：我的一片诚心。吾，我。
03

Background创作背景

"Thoughts Stirred by Encounters" is a set of twelve poems by Zhang Jiuling. It is usually thought that he wrote them after he was removed as chief minister and sent to be an official in Jingzhou, in today's Hubei. The scheming minister Li Linfu had spoken ill of him to Emperor Xuanzong, and he was pushed out of the court. He was still loyal at heart but had no chance to say so, so he wrote about birds, flowers and plants to express what he felt. In the set of twelve in the Complete Tang Poems, this is No. 2. Three Hundred Tang Poems picks only four of them and puts this one third, so some books call it No. 3.

《感遇》是张九龄写的一组诗，一共十二首。一般认为，这组诗是他被罢去宰相、贬为荆州长史（在今天湖北荆州）以后写的。当时奸臣李林甫在唐玄宗面前说他的坏话，他被排挤出了朝廷。他心里仍然忠于朝廷，却没有机会再表达，就借写鸟、写花草来说自己的心事。这一首在《全唐诗》所收十二首里排第二；《唐诗三百首》只选了其中四首，把它排在第三，所以有的书叫它“其三”。

04

The poet作者

Zhang Jiuling (678–740), courtesy name Zishou, came from Qujiang in Shaozhou, today's Shaoguan in Guangdong. He was a famous chief minister under Emperor Xuanzong, upright and brave enough to speak the truth. He was also a fine poet; his lines about the bright moon rising over the sea, shared by people far apart, are very famous. His "Encounters" poems are plain and quiet, and often speak of his heart through things in nature.

张九龄（678—740），字子寿，韶州曲江（今广东韶关）人，唐玄宗开元年间有名的宰相，为人正直，敢说真话。他也是好诗人，《望月怀远》里的“海上生明月，天涯共此时”非常有名。他的《感遇》诗清淡、含蓄，常常借物说心事。

05

The mood意境

This poem is about a loyal heart that has nowhere to speak. At the start, the poet lives alone and tries to wash his worries away, keeping only a clean heart. But he cannot let go. He wants a high-flying bird to carry his feelings far away, that is, to the emperor in the distant capital. Yet he holds this feeling all day long, and who will be moved by it? The last two lines give the reason. A bird flies in the sky and a fish sinks in the water, so they are far apart by nature. A man of high rank and a man sent away are just the same. His sincere heart finds no comfort. The poem never blames anyone. It only sighs quietly, and it feels very lonely.

这首诗写的是一颗忠心找不到地方说。开头，诗人一个人闲居，想把心里的忧愁洗干净，只留下清白。可是心事放不下，他想托高飞的鸟把心意带到远方，也就是带给远在京城的皇帝。可是整天怀着这份心，有谁能被感动呢？最后两句说出原因：鸟在天上飞，鱼在水里沉，两者本来就隔得远；地位高的人和被贬的人，也是这样。这份诚心，找不到安慰。全诗没有一句骂人，只是安安静静地叹气，读起来很孤独。
