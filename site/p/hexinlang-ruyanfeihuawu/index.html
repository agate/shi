苏轼Su Shi · Song宋

He Xin Lang: Young Swallows Fly in the Fine House

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 乳燕飞华屋Young swallows fly around the fine house.小燕子在漂亮的房子边飞来飞去。
  2. 悄无人All is quiet, with no one around.四下静悄悄的，没有一个人。
  3. 桐阴转午The shade of the parasol tree moves round; it is noon.梧桐的树影慢慢移动，已经到了中午。
  4. 晚凉新浴In the cool of evening, she has just bathed.傍晚天凉，她刚刚洗完澡。
  5. 手弄生绡白团扇In her hand she holds a round fan of white silk.她手里拿着一把白绸做的圆扇子。
  6. 扇手一时似玉The fan and her hand are both as white as jade.扇子和手一样白，都像美玉。
  7. 渐困倚Slowly she grows sleepy and leans back,她渐渐困了，靠在那里，
  8. 孤眠清熟and sleeps soundly, all alone.一个人睡得很香。
  9. 帘外谁来推绣户Who is that, pushing the door outside the curtain?帘子外面是谁来推门？
  10. 枉教人For nothing at all, it白白地让人
  11. 梦断瑶台曲wakes her from a dream deep in the immortals' jade terrace.从瑶台仙境的美梦里惊醒。
  12. 又却是It turns out, once again,原来又是
  13. 风敲竹to be only the wind knocking the bamboo.风吹得竹子沙沙作响。
  14. 石榴半吐红巾蹙The pomegranate flower is half open, like a crumpled red silk scarf.石榴花半开着，像一条皱起来的红丝巾。
  15. 待浮花When all the showy flowers等到那些轻浮的花
  16. 浪蕊都尽have fallen,都落尽了，
  17. 伴君幽独it comes to keep you company in your loneliness.它才来陪伴孤独的你。
  18. 秾艳一枝细看取Look closely at this one bright branch:仔细看看这一枝艳丽的花，
  19. 芳心千重似束its heart is layer on layer, tied tight like secret worries.花心里一层一层，像捆紧的心事。
  20. 又恐被And yet I fear又怕被
  21. 秋风惊绿the autumn wind will blow, leaving only green leaves.秋风一吹，只剩绿叶。
  22. 若待得君来向此If you wait until you come here,如果等到你来到这里，
  23. 花前对酒不忍触facing the flower with wine, you will not bear to touch it.对着花，端着酒，都不忍心去碰它。
  24. 共粉泪Your tears你的眼泪
  25. 两簌簌and the falling petals both drop, one after another.和落花一起纷纷往下掉。
02

Words字词注释

乳燕rǔ yàn
rǔ yàn: young swallows, just hatched.乳燕：刚出生不久的小燕子。
华屋huá wū
huá wū: a fine, beautiful house.华屋：漂亮讲究的房子。
悄无人qiǎo wú rén
qiǎo wú rén: all quiet, with no one around.悄无人：静悄悄的，一个人也没有。
桐阴转午tóng yīn zhuǎn wǔ
tóng yīn zhuǎn wǔ: the shade of the parasol tree moves round as it turns noon.桐阴转午：梧桐树的影子移动着，时间到了中午。
晚凉新浴wǎn liáng xīn yù
wǎn liáng xīn yù: it is cool in the evening, and she has just bathed.晚凉新浴：傍晚天凉了，她刚洗过澡。
弄nòng
nòng: to play with something in your hand.弄：拿在手里玩。
生绡shēng xiāo
shēng xiāo: thin silk woven from raw silk, very white and light.生绡：没有煮过的生丝织成的薄绸，很白很轻。
团扇tuán shàn
tuán shàn: a round fan.团扇：圆形的扇子。
扇手一时似玉shàn shǒu yì shí sì yù
shàn shǒu yī shí sì yù: the white fan and her hand look the same, both like white jade.扇手一时似玉：白扇子和她的手一样洁白，都像白玉。
渐困倚jiàn kùn yǐ
jiàn kùn yǐ: slowly getting sleepy and leaning against something.渐困倚：慢慢困了，靠着东西。
清熟qīng shú
qīng shú: sleeping soundly and peacefully.清熟：睡得很安稳、很香。
绣户xiù hù
xiù hù: a finely decorated door, meaning a woman's room.绣户：装饰华美的门窗，指女子的房间。
枉教人wǎng jiào rén
wǎng jiào rén: making someone, all for nothing. Wǎng means in vain.枉教人：白白地让人。枉，白白地。
瑶台yáo tái
yáo tái: in old stories, a tall jade terrace where immortals live.瑶台：神话里神仙住的玉做的高台。
又却是yòu què shì
yòu què shì: it turns out to be, once again.又却是：原来又是。
风敲竹fēng qiāo zhú
fēng qiāo zhú: the wind knocking the bamboo, which sounds like someone at the door.风敲竹：风吹动竹子发出的声音，听起来像有人敲门。
半吐bàn tǔ
bàn tǔ: a flower half open.半吐：花刚开了一半。
红巾蹙hóng jīn cù
hóng jīn cù: the crinkled pomegranate petals look like a crumpled red silk scarf. Cù means wrinkled.红巾蹙：石榴花的花瓣皱皱的，像一条揉皱的红丝巾。蹙，皱起来。
浮花fú huā
fú huā: showy flowers that bloom early and fall early.浮花：轻浮、早开早落的花。
浪蕊làng ruǐ
làng ruǐ: flowers that bloom carelessly, much like fú huā.浪蕊：随便乱开的花。和“浮花”意思差不多。
幽独yōu dú
yōu dú: quiet and alone. Here the late pomegranate flower keeps the lonely woman company.幽独：安静孤单的样子。这里说石榴花晚开，陪伴孤独的她。
秾艳nóng yàn
nóng yàn: rich in color and very bright.秾艳：颜色浓、很艳丽。
细看取xì kàn qǔ
xì kàn qǔ: look closely. Qǔ here just adds tone and has no real meaning.细看取：仔细地看。取，语气词，没有实在意思。
芳心千重似束fāng xīn qiān chóng sì shù
fāng xīn qiān chóng sì shù: the heart of the flower has layer on layer of petals, as if tied tight. It also stands for the layers of worry in her heart.芳心千重似束：花心里花瓣一层一层，像被捆紧了一样。也比喻她心里有重重的心事。
秋风惊绿qiū fēng jīng lǜ
qiū fēng jīng lǜ: the autumn wind blows, the flowers fall, and only green leaves are left.秋风惊绿：秋风一吹，花落了，只剩下绿叶。
君jūn
jūn: you. Here it means her. Some think it means the person she is waiting for.君：你，这里指她。也有人认为是她等的那个人。
向此xiàng cǐ
xiàng cǐ: to come here.向此：到这里来。
不忍触bù rěn chù
bù rěn chù: cannot bear to touch it.不忍触：不忍心去碰。
粉泪fěn lèi
fěn lèi: a woman's tears. Women in old times powdered their faces, so tears were "powdered".粉泪：女子的眼泪。古代女子脸上搽粉，所以这样说。
两簌簌liǎng sù sù
liǎng sù sù: petals and tears both fall, one after another. Sù sù describes things falling steadily.两簌簌：花瓣和眼泪一起簌簌地往下落。簌簌，纷纷落下的样子。
03

How to read it读音

曲qū
Here qū means a deep, winding corner, so it is read qū, not qǔ as in "song". Some readers take it as "a song of the jade terrace", which would be read qǔ.这里的“曲”是“深处、弯弯曲曲的地方”，读 qū，不是“歌曲”的 qǔ。也有人理解成“瑶台的曲子”，那就读 qǔ。
04

Background创作背景

No one knows for sure when this lyric was written or for whom. A Song dynasty story says that when Su Shi was an official in Hangzhou, a singing girl named Xiulan came late to a party, and he wrote this lyric for her. But even in the Song dynasty, people said the story could not be trusted. Today it is usually read as using a lonely, beautiful woman and a late-blooming pomegranate flower to carry the poet's own feelings.

这首词写在什么时候、为谁写的，没有确切的说法。宋代有一个故事说，苏轼在杭州做官时，有一位叫秀兰的歌女参加宴会迟到了，苏轼为她写了这首词。但宋代就有人指出这个故事不可信。现在一般认为，这首词借一位孤独美丽的女子和晚开的石榴花，寄托作者自己的心情。

05

The poet作者

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Dongpo, was a great Northern Song writer. He was good at poems, lyrics, essays, calligraphy and painting. His lyrics are often bold and broad, but some, like this one, are soft and delicate. He was sent away from court several times, and knew the loneliness of not being understood.

苏轼（1037—1101），号东坡居士，北宋大文学家，诗、词、文章、书法、绘画样样都好。他的词常常豪放开阔，但也有像这首一样细腻柔美的作品。他一生几次被贬官，心里有不少不被理解的孤独。

06

The mood意境

The first half is like a slow-motion film. On a summer noon, swallows fly and tree shadows move. In the house, a woman who has just bathed holds a white fan and slowly falls asleep. Suddenly it seems someone pushes the door, and she wakes from a sweet dream. But it is only the wind in the bamboo. This small letdown shows that she is waiting for someone and feels lonely. The second half is about the pomegranate flower. Other flowers bloomed and fell long ago; only it blooms late, keeping a lonely person company. Yet it too will be blown away by the autumn wind. At the end, the woman and the flower shed tears together. The flower is the woman, and the woman is the flower: beautiful, yet no one treasures them. Many readers feel Su Shi's own loneliness of not being understood is in it too.

上半首像一部慢镜头：夏天的中午，燕子飞，树影移，屋里一个刚洗完澡的女子拿着白扇子，慢慢睡着了。忽然好像有人推门，她从美梦里醒来，结果只是风吹竹子。这一点小小的失望，写出了她在等人，心里很孤单。下半首写石榴花。别的花早就开过、落了，只有它晚开，陪着孤独的人。可是它也会被秋风吹落。最后写人和花一起落泪。花就是人，人就是花，美丽却没人懂得珍惜。很多人认为，这里也有苏轼自己不被理解的心情。
