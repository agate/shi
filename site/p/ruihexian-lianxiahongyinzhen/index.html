To the Tune Rui He Xian: Her Rosy Cheek Marked by the Pillow

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 脸霞红印枕Her cheek is rosy as a sunset cloud, marked red by the pillow.她的脸像彩霞一样红，枕头在上面压出了印子，
  2. 睡觉来冠儿还是不整She has woken up, and her little cap still sits crooked.睡醒以后，头上的冠儿还歪歪的没戴好。
  3. 屏间麝煤冷By the screen, the incense in the burner has gone cold.屏风边熏炉里的香已经冷了。
  4. 但眉峰压翠Her painted brows are tightly knit,只见她紧紧皱着眉头，
  5. 泪珠弹粉and her tears fall and smudge her powder.眼泪落下来，冲花了脸上的粉。
  6. 堂深昼永The house is deep, and the day seems endless.房子深深，白天长得没有尽头。
  7. 燕交飞风帘露井Swallows fly in pairs past the wind-blown curtains and the open well.燕子成双飞舞，风吹着帘子，井口敞着。
  8. 恨无人How sad that there is no one可恨没有人
  9. 与说相思to hear her tell how much she misses him,可以听她诉说相思，
  10. 近日带围宽尽and lately her belt hangs loose, for she has grown so thin.最近她的腰带越来越松，人瘦了一大圈。
  11. 重省Again she thinks back:她又一次回想从前：
  12. 残灯朱幌the lamp burning low, the red bed curtains,快要燃尽的灯，红色的帷帐，
  13. 淡月纱窗pale moonlight on the gauze window,淡淡的月光照在纱窗上，
  14. 那时风景that was how things were back then.那就是当时的情景。
  15. 阳台路迥Now the place where they met is far away,如今相会的地方那么遥远，
  16. 云雨梦and even meeting him in dreams连在梦里相会，
  17. 便无准cannot be counted on.也靠不住。
  18. 待归来When he comes home,等他回来的时候，
  19. 先指花梢教看she will first point to the flowering branches and make him look,她要先指着花枝让他看看，
  20. 却把心期细问and then ask him closely about the promise he made.再细细地问他当初许下的诺言。
  21. 问因循过了青春She will ask: you kept putting it off and let the best of spring and youth slip by,问他：你一拖再拖，让大好的春光和年华都过去了，
  22. 怎生意稳so how can your heart be at peace?你的心怎么能安呢？
02

Words字词注释

脸霞liǎn xiá
liǎn xiá: a rosy glow on the cheek, like the colour of clouds at sunset.脸霞：脸上像彩霞一样的红晕。
红印枕hóng yìn zhěn
hóng yìn zhěn: the pillow has left a red mark on her cheek while she slept.红印枕：睡觉时脸压在枕头上，留下了红红的印子。
睡觉来shuì jué lái
shuì jué lái: after waking up. Jué means to wake.睡觉来：睡醒了以后。觉，醒。
冠儿guān ér
guān ér: a small cap or hair ornament worn by a woman.冠儿：女子头上戴的小帽子或发饰。
屏间píng jiān
píng jiān: beside the screen. A screen is a standing panel used in a room to block wind or divide space.屏间：屏风旁边。屏风是放在屋里挡风、隔开空间的家具。
麝煤shè méi
shè méi: incense mixed with musk, burned in a burner to scent the room. Another view says it means ink mixed with musk.麝煤：掺了麝香的香料，放在熏炉里烧，让屋子变香。一说指加了麝香的墨。
眉峰压翠méi fēng yā cuì
méi fēng yā cuì: her painted dark brows are tightly knit. Cuì here is the dark green-black colour used to paint eyebrows.眉峰压翠：用青黑色画过的眉毛紧紧皱在一起。翠，青黑色，指画眉的颜色。
泪珠弹粉lèi zhū tán fěn
lèi zhū tán fěn: falling tears smudge the powder on her face.泪珠弹粉：眼泪落下来，冲花了脸上的脂粉。
堂深昼永táng shēn zhòu yǒng
táng shēn zhòu yǒng: the house is deep and quiet, and the day feels very long. Yǒng means long.堂深昼永：房子又深又静，白天显得特别长。永，长。
燕交飞yàn jiāo fēi
yàn jiāo fēi: swallows fly about in pairs.燕交飞：燕子成双成对地飞来飞去。
风帘露井fēng lián lù jǐng
fēng lián lù jǐng: curtains moving in the wind, and a well with no cover over it.风帘露井：被风吹动的帘子，没有盖的水井。露，露在外面。
与说yǔ shuō
yǔ shuō: to tell someone, to pour out one's heart to.与说：跟他说，对他诉说。
带围宽尽dài wéi kuān jìn
dài wéi kuān jìn: the belt has grown very loose, meaning she has become thin from missing him.带围宽尽：腰带变得很松，说人因为想念瘦了很多。
重省chóng xǐng
chóng xǐng: to think back again. Xǐng means to recall.重省：再回想一遍。省，回想。
朱幌zhū huǎng
zhū huǎng: red bed curtains.朱幌：红色的帷帐。
纱窗shā chuāng
shā chuāng: a window covered with thin gauze.纱窗：糊着薄纱的窗户。
阳台yáng tái
yáng tái: Sunlit Terrace, where in legend the goddess of Mount Wu met a king of Chu. Here it means the place where lovers meet.阳台：传说中巫山神女和楚王相会的地方，这里借指和心上人相会的地方。
迥jiǒng
jiǒng: far away.迥：远。
云雨梦yún yǔ mèng
yún yǔ mèng: also from the story of the goddess of Mount Wu; a dream of meeting one's beloved.云雨梦：也来自巫山神女的故事，指梦见和心上人相会。
便无准biàn wú zhǔn
biàn wú zhǔn: is not to be counted on either.便无准：也没有准儿，靠不住。
花梢huā shāo
huā shāo: the tips of flowering branches.花梢：花枝的尖儿。
心期xīn qī
xīn qī: the promise made in one's heart, the vow made earlier.心期：心里的约定，当初许下的诺言。
因循yīn xún
yīn xún: putting things off again and again.因循：拖拖拉拉，一拖再拖。
青春qīng chūn
qīng chūn: the good years of youth, and also the lovely spring.青春：年轻的好时光，也指美好的春天。
怎生意稳zěn shēng yì wěn
zěn shēng yì wěn: how could your heart be at peace? Zěn shēng means how.怎生意稳：怎么能心安呢。怎生，怎么。
03

How to read it读音

觉jué
Here shuì jué means waking up from sleep, so the second character is read jué, not jiào.“睡觉”在这里是“睡醒”的意思，“觉”读 jué，不读 jiào。
弹tán
Here it means to flick or splash down, so it is read tán, not dàn.这里是“弹落、溅落”的意思，读 tán，不读 dàn。
重chóng
Here it means "again", so it is read chóng.这里是“再一次”的意思，读 chóng。
省xǐng
Here it means to think back, so it is read xǐng, not shěng.这里是“回想、醒悟”的意思，读 xǐng，不读 shěng。
教jiào
Here it means "to let". Today's dictionaries read it jiào; some people read it jiāo.“教”在这里是“让”的意思，按现在的字典读 jiào。也有人读 jiāo。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about a young woman who misses her beloved far away. The first stanza shows her waking in the daytime and the lonely house. The second shows her remembering the past and imagining what she will ask him when he comes back. We do not know whom it was written for or in what year. An old notebook links it to a singing girl Lu Song once loved, but that is only a story.

这首词写一位女子思念远方的心上人。上阕写她白天睡醒后的样子和冷清的房子，下阕写她回想当初，又想象他回来后要怎样问他。这首词具体写给谁、写在哪一年，现在不清楚。有笔记说它和陆淞爱过的一位歌女有关，但这只是传说。

05

The poet作者

Lu Song, also called Ziyi, came from Shanyin, today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. He was the elder brother of the great Southern Song poet Lu You. Very few of his lyrics survive. This one was chosen for the famous anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics and is his best-known work. It is soft and delicate, and shows a young woman's feelings very truly.

陆淞，字子逸，号云溪，山阴（今浙江绍兴）人，是南宋大诗人陆游的哥哥。他留下来的词很少，这首《瑞鹤仙》被选进《宋词三百首》，是他最有名的作品。词写得细腻柔美，把女子的心事写得很真。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is like a set of slow-motion shots. First her face: she has just woken, the pillow has left a red mark, and her cap is crooked. Then the room: the incense is cold, she frowns, and her tears smudge her powder. Outside, swallows fly in pairs, which makes her loneliness clearer. She wants to talk, but no one is there to listen, and she has grown thin. In the second stanza she goes back to the past: the lamp, the red curtains, the moonlight, the gauze window, she remembers each one. Now even dreams cannot be trusted. The best part is the ending. She has it all planned. When he comes home she will point to the flowers to show him spring is nearly over, then ask about his promise. In that question there is blame, there is teasing, and there is deep love.

这首词像一组慢镜头。先拍脸：睡醒了，脸上还有枕头压出的红印，帽子也没戴好。再拍屋子：香冷了，她皱着眉，眼泪冲花了脂粉。屋外燕子成双飞，更显出她一个人的孤单。她想说说心里话，却没人听，人都瘦了。下阕她回到从前：灯、红帐、月光、纱窗，一样一样都记得。现在连梦都靠不住了。最妙的是结尾，她想好了：等他回来，先指指花枝让他看，春天都快过完了，再问他当初的诺言。这一问里有埋怨，有撒娇，也有深深的爱。
