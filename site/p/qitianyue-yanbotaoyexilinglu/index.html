Qi Tian Le: Misty Waves on the Peach-Leaf Road to Xiling

 

yān烟bō波táo桃yè叶xī西líng陵lù路

shí十nián年duàn断hún魂cháo潮wěi尾

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 烟波桃叶西陵路On the misty water lies the road to Xiling, where we once parted, like lovers at Peach Leaf Ferry;烟雾笼罩的水上，是当年在桃叶渡般的西陵路上送别的地方，
  2. 十年断魂潮尾for ten years, at every ebbing tide, my soul has ached.十年来，每到潮水退尽的时候，我就伤心得像丢了魂。
  3. 古柳重攀Once more I pull down the old willow's branches;我又一次攀住那棵老柳树，
  4. 轻鸥聚别light gulls gather and part.轻盈的鸥鸟聚了又散，
  5. 陈迹危亭独倚In the high pavilion full of old traces, I lean on the rail alone.在这留着旧日痕迹的高亭上，我一个人靠着栏杆。
  6. 凉飔乍起A cool wind suddenly rises.凉风忽然吹起来。
  7. 渺烟碛飞帆Far off by the misty sandbanks, sails speed away;远处烟雾笼罩的沙滩边，船帆飞快地远去，
  8. 暮山横翠the evening hills lie green across the sky.傍晚的青山横在眼前。
  9. 但有江花Only the river flowers只有江边的花，
  10. 共临秋镜照憔悴look with me into the autumn mirror of the water, and see how worn I am.和我一起对着秋水这面镜子，照出我的憔悴。
  11. 华堂烛暗送客I remember: in the fine hall the candles burned low as she saw her guests out;记得那时华美的厅堂里烛光暗了，她送客人出门，
  12. 眼波回盼处the glance she gave me as she turned back,她回头看我的那一眼，
  13. 芳艳流水and now that beauty has flowed away like water.如今那美好的容颜已像流水一样逝去。
  14. 素骨凝冰Her skin was white as ice,她的肌肤像冰一样洁白，
  15. 柔葱蘸雪her fingers like tender scallions dipped in snow;手指像嫩葱蘸了雪，
  16. 犹忆分瓜深意I still remember the deep feeling when she cut a melon for me.我还记得她为我切瓜时的深情。
  17. 清尊未洗The wine cup has not yet been washed.那时的酒杯还没有洗。
  18. 梦不湿行云Even in dreams I cannot reach that drifting cloud;梦里也碰不到那片行云，
  19. 漫沾残泪I only wet my face with leftover tears.只是白白沾湿了残留的泪水。
  20. 可惜秋宵What a pity, this autumn night,可惜这样一个秋夜，
  21. 乱蛩疏雨里passing away among crying crickets and scattered rain.就在蟋蟀的乱叫和稀稀落落的雨声里过去了。
02

Words字词注释

烟波yān bō
yān bō: misty water.烟波：雾气笼罩的水面。
桃叶táo yè
táo yè: Peach Leaf, the name of the beloved of the Eastern Jin calligrapher Wang Xianzhi. He saw her off at a ferry and wrote songs for her. Later the name came to stand for a beloved woman, or a ferry where lovers part.桃叶：东晋书法家王献之的爱妾名叫桃叶，王献之曾在渡口送她，作《桃叶歌》。后来“桃叶”常用来指心爱的女子或送别的渡口。
西陵xī líng
xī líng: the name of a ferry crossing. Some say it was on the Qiantang River; others say it means Xiling by West Lake in Hangzhou.西陵：渡口名。一说在钱塘江边，一说指杭州西湖边的西泠。
断魂duàn hún
duàn hún: so sad it is as if one's soul were lost.断魂：伤心得像丢了魂。
潮尾cháo wěi
cháo wěi: the tail end of the ebbing tide.潮尾：潮水退去的末尾。
古柳重攀gǔ liǔ chóng pān
gǔ liǔ chóng pān: pulling down the branches of the old willow once more. People used to break off willow twigs as a parting gift.古柳重攀：又一次去拉老柳树的枝条。古人送别时常折柳相赠。
轻鸥聚别qīng ōu jù bié
qīng ōu jù bié: light gulls gather and part, like people meeting and leaving.轻鸥聚别：轻盈的鸥鸟聚了又散，像人的相聚和分别。
陈迹chén jì
chén jì: traces of the past.陈迹：旧日留下的痕迹。
危亭wēi tíng
wēi tíng: a pavilion high up. Wēi means high.危亭：高处的亭子。危，高。
凉飔liáng sī
liáng sī: a cool wind.凉飔：凉风。飔，凉风。
乍起zhà qǐ
zhà qǐ: suddenly rises.乍起：忽然吹起来。
渺miǎo
miǎo: so far it is hard to see.渺：远得看不清。
烟碛yān qì
yān qì: misty sandbanks. Qì is a bank of sand and stones in or by the water.烟碛：烟雾笼罩的沙滩。碛，水中或水边的沙石滩。
横翠héng cuì
héng cuì: green hills lying across the view.横翠：青翠的山横在眼前。
但有dàn yǒu
dàn yǒu: there is only.但有：只有。
秋镜qiū jìng
qiū jìng: the clear autumn river, like a mirror.秋镜：秋天清澈的江水，像一面镜子。
憔悴qiáo cuì
qiáo cuì: thin and worn.憔悴：又瘦又疲倦的样子。
华堂huá táng
huá táng: a fine hall.华堂：华美的厅堂。
眼波回盼yǎn bō huí pàn
yǎn bō huí pàn: turning back to look, her glance rippling like water.眼波回盼：转过头来看人，目光像水波一样流动。
芳艳流水fāng yàn liú shuǐ
fāng yàn liú shuǐ: her beauty flowed away like running water.芳艳流水：美好的容貌像流水一样逝去了。
素骨凝冰sù gǔ níng bīng
sù gǔ níng bīng: her skin was white as frozen ice.素骨凝冰：形容女子肌肤洁白，像凝结的冰。
柔葱蘸雪róu cōng zhàn xuě
róu cōng zhàn xuě: her fingers were slender and white, like tender scallions dipped in snow.柔葱蘸雪：形容手指又白又细，像嫩葱蘸了雪。
分瓜fēn guā
fēn guā: cutting a melon to share. Some read it as a sign of love between the two; others think it hints at the girl's age.分瓜：切瓜分给人吃。一说指两人分吃瓜，是相爱的表示；一说暗指女子的年纪。
清尊qīng zūn
qīng zūn: a cup of clear wine.清尊：盛着清酒的酒杯。
行云xíng yún
xíng yún: drifting cloud. In an old legend, the goddess of Mount Wu was a cloud in the morning and rain in the evening, so the cloud often stands for a loved woman.行云：天上飘动的云。古代传说巫山神女“早上是云，傍晚是雨”，所以常用来指相爱的女子。
漫màn
màn: in vain.漫：白白地。
秋宵qiū xiāo
qiū xiāo: an autumn night.秋宵：秋天的夜晚。
蛩qióng
qióng: cricket.蛩：蟋蟀。
03

How to read it读音

重chóng
Here it means "again" and is read chóng.这里是“再一次”的意思，读 chóng。
飔sī
This character is read sī and means a cool wind.飔读 sī，意思是凉风。
碛qì
This character is read qì and means a sandbank.碛读 qì，指沙石滩。
04

Background创作背景

This is one of Wu Wenying's lyrics in memory of a lost love. It is generally thought that as a young man he had a love affair in Hangzhou. Later he and the woman parted and never met again, and he wrote many lyrics about her. In this one he comes back to the ferry where they parted, ten years later. Alone in a pavilion he gazes into the distance and recalls their time together. The exact year it was written is no longer known.

这是吴文英怀念旧日恋人的一首词。一般认为，他年轻时在杭州有过一段恋情，后来和那位女子分别，再也没有见面，他为此写了很多词。这首词里，他重新来到当年分别的渡口，已经过了十年。他独自登亭远望，回想起两人在一起时的情景。具体写于哪一年，已经说不清。

05

The poet作者

Wu Wenying (about 1200 to about 1260), courtesy name Junte, also called Mengchuang, came from Siming, today's Ningbo in Zhejiang. He was a Southern Song ci poet. He never held high office and often lived as a guest of officials around Suzhou and Hangzhou. His lyrics use rich words and jump from image to image like a dream, so they are not easy to follow. He wrote many lyrics in memory of a lost love.

吴文英（约1200—约1260），字君特，号梦窗，四明（今浙江宁波）人，南宋词人。他一辈子没有做大官，常在苏州、杭州一带给官员做门客。他的词用字华丽，意思常常跳来跳去，像梦一样，不太好懂。他写了很多怀念旧日爱人的词。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza shows the present. He has come back to the Xiling ferry. Ten years have passed, and every ebbing tide still makes him sad. He pulls at the old willow, watches the gulls gather and part, and leans alone in the pavilion. A cool wind rises, distant sails grow smaller, and green hills lie across the dusk. No one is with him; only the river flowers look with him into the autumn water, each seeing how worn the other is. The second stanza suddenly jumps back to the past. In a hall where the candles burned low, she saw her guests out and turned back to look at him. Her skin was like ice, her fingers like tender scallions dipped in snow, and she once cut a melon for him. These details are so clear, as if it were yesterday. Then in a blink we are back in the present: the wine cup is not yet washed, but she is far away. He cannot see her even in dreams, and is left with only tears. At the end, the autumn night slips by to the sound of crickets and thin rain. The warm past and the cold present keep taking turns, like a person in memory, now awake and now dreaming.

上片写眼前：他又来到西陵渡口，十年过去了，每次看到退潮都伤心。他攀着老柳，看鸥鸟聚散，一个人靠在亭子里。凉风吹起，远处帆影越来越小，暮色里青山横着。没有人陪他，只有江边的花和他一起照着秋水，看见彼此的憔悴。下片忽然跳回从前：烛光暗下来的厅堂里，她送走客人，回头看了他一眼。她的肌肤像冰，手指像蘸雪的嫩葱，还为他切过瓜。这些细节写得那么清楚，好像就在昨天。可是一转眼又回到现在：酒杯还没洗，人却已远，连梦里都见不到，只剩一枕眼泪。最后，秋夜在蟋蟀声和疏雨声里过去。从前的温暖和现在的冷清来回交替，就像人在回忆里一会儿醒、一会儿梦。
