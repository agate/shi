No. 45 · Tang poem · yuefu song (an old-style poem of mixed line lengths, mostly seven characters)第 45 首 · 唐诗 · 乐府诗（七言为主的杂言古诗）

qiāng将jìn进jiǔ酒

lǐ李bái白Li Bai · Tang唐

Bring In the Wine

 

jūn君bú不jiàn见huáng黄hé河zhī之shuǐ水tiān天shàng上lái来

bēn奔liú流dào到hǎi海bú不fù复huí回

jūn君bú不jiàn见gāo高táng堂míng明jìng镜bēi悲bái白fà发

zhāo朝rú如qīng青sī丝mù暮chéng成xuě雪

rén人shēng生dé得yì意xū须jìn尽huān欢

mò莫shǐ使jīn金zūn樽kōng空duì对yuè月

tiān天shēng生wǒ我cái材bì必yǒu有yòng用

qiān千jīn金sàn散jìn尽huán还fù复lái来

pēng烹yáng羊zǎi宰niú牛qiě且wéi为lè乐

huì会xū须yì一yǐn饮sān三bǎi百bēi杯

cén岑fū夫zǐ子

dān丹qiū丘shēng生

qiāng将jìn进jiǔ酒

bēi杯mò莫tíng停

yǔ与jūn君gē歌yì一qǔ曲

qǐng请jūn君wèi为wǒ我qīng倾ěr耳tīng听

zhōng钟gǔ鼓zhuàn馔yù玉bù不zú足guì贵

dàn但yuàn愿cháng长zuì醉bú不fù复xǐng醒

gǔ古lái来shèng圣xián贤jiē皆jì寂mò寞

wéi惟yǒu有yǐn饮zhě者liú留qí其míng名

chén陈wáng王xī昔shí时yàn宴píng平lè乐

dǒu斗jiǔ酒shí十qiān千zì恣huān欢xuè谑

zhǔ主rén人hé何wèi为yán言shǎo少qián钱

jìng径xū须gū沽qǔ取duì对jūn君zhuó酌

wǔ五huā花mǎ马

qiān千jīn金qiú裘

hū呼ér儿jiāng将chū出huàn换měi美jiǔ酒

yǔ与ěr尔tóng同xiāo销wàn万gǔ古chóu愁

The commonly taught text differs from the source text in a few places. It has jīn zūn (wine cup), qīng ěr tīng (tilt your ear) and gū qǔ (buy wine), where the source uses older characters or cè ěr tīng (turn your ear). The meanings are the same. In the line about staying drunk, some editions end with bù yuàn xǐng (unwilling to wake) or bù yòng xǐng (no need to wake); this text keeps bù fù xǐng (never wake again).

课本和通行本与这里用的古籍原文有几处不同：通行本作“金樽”“倾耳听”“沽取”，原文作“金尊”“侧耳听”“酤取”。“樽”和“尊”、“沽”和“酤”意思相同。“但愿长醉不复醒”一句，有的版本作“不愿醒”或“不用醒”，这里保留“不复醒”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 君不见黄河之水天上来Haven't you seen the Yellow River's waters pour down as if from the sky,你没看见吗？黄河的水像从天上奔下来，
  2. 奔流到海不复回rushing on to the sea and never coming back?一路奔流到大海，再也不会回来。
  3. 君不见高堂明镜悲白发Haven't you seen people in tall halls grieve at the white hair in their bright mirrors,你没看见吗？高堂上的人对着明镜，为白发伤心，
  4. 朝如青丝暮成雪hair black as silk at dawn, white as snow by dusk?早上还是黑亮的头发，到傍晚就白得像雪。
  5. 人生得意须尽欢When life goes your way, enjoy it to the full;人生得意的时候，就应该尽情欢乐，
  6. 莫使金樽空对月don't let your golden cup stand empty under the moon.不要让金杯空空地对着明月。
  7. 天生我材必有用Heaven gave me my talents, and they must be of use;老天生下我的才能，一定有用处，
  8. 千金散尽还复来spend a thousand pieces of gold, and they will come back again.千金花光了，还会再回来。
  9. 烹羊宰牛且为乐Cook the lamb, kill the ox, and let us make merry for now;煮羊杀牛，暂且痛快作乐吧，
  10. 会须一饮三百杯once we start, we ought to drink three hundred cups.一喝就应该喝它三百杯。
  11. 岑夫子Master Cen,岑夫子啊，
  12. 丹丘生my friend Danqiu,丹丘生啊，
  13. 将进酒please, drink up,请喝酒吧，
  14. 杯莫停don't let the cups stop.酒杯不要停下来。
  15. 与君歌一曲Let me sing you a song;我给你们唱一首歌，
  16. 请君为我倾耳听please lend me your ears and listen.请你们为我侧耳细听。
  17. 钟鼓馔玉不足贵Bells, drums and food fine as jade are not worth prizing;敲钟打鼓、山珍海味的富贵生活，不值得看重，
  18. 但愿长醉不复醒all I want is to stay drunk and never wake again.我只希望一直醉着，不再醒来。
  19. 古来圣贤皆寂寞Since ancient times the sages and the wise have been lonely and forgotten;自古以来，圣人贤人都冷冷清清，被人忘记，
  20. 惟有饮者留其名only the drinkers have left their names behind.只有会喝酒的人留下了名声。
  21. 陈王昔时宴平乐Long ago the Prince of Chen held a feast at Pingle Hall,当年陈王曹植在平乐观大摆酒宴，
  22. 斗酒十千恣欢谑wine at ten thousand coins a measure, laughing and joking as he pleased.一斗酒值十千钱，大家尽情说笑。
  23. 主人何为言少钱Why should I, the host, say I am short of money?我这个主人为什么要说钱不够呢？
  24. 径须沽取对君酌Just go and buy more wine, and drink with you.只管去买酒来，和你们对饮。
  25. 五花马My fine dappled horse,名贵的五花马，
  26. 千金裘my fur coat worth a thousand gold,价值千金的皮衣，
  27. 呼儿将出换美酒call the boy to take them and trade them for good wine,叫孩子拿去换美酒，
  28. 与尔同销万古愁and with you I'll drown the sorrows of ten thousand ages.我要和你们一起，消掉这万古的忧愁。
02

Words字词注释

君不见jūn bú jiàn
jūn bù jiàn: haven't you seen? A common opening in old songs, to make the listener pay attention. Jūn means you.君不见：你没有看见吗？乐府诗里常用的开头，用来引起大家注意。君，你。
天上来tiān shàng lái
tiān shàng lái: the Yellow River comes down from very high land, so from far away it looks as if it falls from the sky.天上来：黄河从很高的地方流下来，远远看去，好像是从天上来的。
不复回bú fù huí
bù fù huí: never comes back. Fù means again.不复回：不再回来。复，再。
高堂gāo táng
gāo táng: a tall hall in a house. Another reading says it means one's parents.高堂：高大的厅堂。一说指父母。
悲白发bēi bái fà
bēi bái fà: to look in the mirror and feel sad about one's white hair.悲白发：对着镜子，为满头白发而伤心。
青丝qīng sī
qīng sī: black hair (literally, dark silk threads).青丝：黑头发。
暮成雪mù chéng xuě
mù chéng xuě: by evening it is white as snow. Morning to evening stands for how fast a person grows old.暮成雪：到了傍晚就白得像雪。早晚之间，说的是人老得很快。
得意dé yì
dé yì: when things go as you wish, when you are happy.得意：称心如意，高兴的时候。
须尽欢xū jìn huān
xū jìn huān: should enjoy it to the full. Xū means should.须尽欢：应该尽情地快乐。须，应当。
金樽jīn zūn
jīn zūn: a fine wine cup. Zūn is an old vessel for wine.金樽：精美的酒杯。樽，古代盛酒的器具。
空对月kōng duì yuè
kōng duì yuè: an empty cup facing the moon, that is, wasting a lovely night without drinking.空对月：空着酒杯对着月亮，意思是不喝酒，白白浪费了好时光。
我材wǒ cái
wǒ cái: my talent. Here cái is the same as the word for talent.我材：我的才能。材，同“才”。
千金qiān jīn
qiān jīn: a thousand pieces of gold, a great deal of money.千金：很多很多的钱。
烹pēng
pēng: to cook, to boil.烹：煮。
且为乐qiě wéi lè
qiě wéi lè: let us just make merry for now. Qiě means for the time being.且为乐：姑且作乐，先痛快一下。且，暂且。
会须huì xū
huì xū: really ought to.会须：应当，正应该。
三百杯sān bǎi bēi
sān bǎi bēi: three hundred cups, a playful way of saying a great many.三百杯：说喝很多酒，是夸张的说法。
岑夫子cén fū zǐ
cén fū zǐ: Master Cen, that is, Cen Xun, a friend of Li Bai. Fūzǐ is a polite way to address a man.岑夫子：指岑勋，李白的朋友。夫子，对男子的尊称。
丹丘生dān qiū shēng
dān qiū shēng: Danqiu, that is, Yuan Danqiu, a close friend of Li Bai who lived as a hermit. Shēng is a way to address a scholar.丹丘生：指元丹丘，李白的好朋友，是一位隐士。生，对读书人的称呼。
将进酒qiāng jìn jiǔ
qiāng jìn jiǔ: please drink. Qiāng means please. This is also the poem's title, the name of an old Han dynasty song.将进酒：请喝酒吧。将，请。这也是这首诗的题目，本是汉代乐府的旧曲名。
杯莫停bēi mò tíng
bēi mò tíng: don't let the cups stop.杯莫停：酒杯不要停下来。
与君yǔ jūn
yǔ jūn: for you all.与君：给你们，为你们。
倾耳听qīng ěr tīng
qīng ěr tīng: to tilt your ear and listen closely.倾耳听：侧着耳朵认真听。
钟鼓zhōng gǔ
zhōng gǔ: bells and drums. Rich families had music played at their meals, so this stands for a life of wealth.钟鼓：富贵人家吃饭时要敲钟打鼓奏乐，这里代指富贵的生活。
馔玉zhuàn yù
zhuàn yù: food as fine as jade. Zhuàn means food.馔玉：像玉一样精美的饭菜。馔，饭食。
不足贵bù zú guì
bù zú guì: not worth valuing.不足贵：不值得看重。
长醉cháng zuì
cháng zuì: to stay drunk for a long time.长醉：一直醉着。
圣贤shèng xián
shèng xián: sages and worthy people, those of great virtue and talent.圣贤：圣人和贤人，品德和才能都很高的人。
寂寞jì mò
jì mò: lonely, left out, soon forgotten.寂寞：冷清，被人冷落，默默无闻。
饮者yǐn zhě
yǐn zhě: drinkers.饮者：喝酒的人。
陈王chén wáng
chén wáng: the Prince of Chen, that is, Cao Zhi of the Three Kingdoms period, a very gifted poet.陈王：指三国时魏国的曹植，他被封为陈王，很有才华。
宴平乐yàn píng lè
yàn píng lè: to hold a feast at the Pingle Hall, a palace building in Luoyang from Han times. Cao Zhi once wrote of coming home to feast at Pingle with wine at ten thousand coins a measure.宴平乐：在平乐观摆酒宴。平乐观，汉代洛阳的一座宫观。曹植写过“归来宴平乐，美酒斗十千”。
斗酒十千dǒu jiǔ shí qiān
dǒu jiǔ shí qiān: one measure of wine cost ten thousand coins, meaning very costly wine.斗酒十千：一斗酒值十千钱，形容酒非常贵。
恣欢谑zì huān xuè
zì huān xuè: to laugh and joke as much as one likes. Zì means freely; xuè means to joke.恣欢谑：尽情地欢笑玩乐。恣，放纵、任意；谑，开玩笑。
主人zhǔ rén
zhǔ rén: the host. Here Li Bai means himself.主人：请客的人，这里是李白自称。
何为hé wèi
hé wèi: why.何为：为什么。
言少钱yán shǎo qián
yán shǎo qián: to say there is not enough money.言少钱：说钱不够。
径须jìng xū
jìng xū: just go ahead and.径须：只管，干脆。
沽取gū qǔ
gū qǔ: to go and buy. Gū means to buy wine.沽取：买来。沽，买酒。
酌zhuó
zhuó: to pour wine, to drink.酌：倒酒，喝酒。
五花马wǔ huā mǎ
wǔ huā mǎ: a fine, costly horse. One reading says its mane was trimmed into five petals; another says its coat had patterns of five colours.五花马：名贵的好马。一说马的鬃毛剪成五瓣花的样子，一说马身上有五种颜色的花纹。
千金裘qiān jīn qiú
qiān jīn qiú: a fur coat worth a thousand gold pieces. Qiú is a fur coat.千金裘：值千金的皮衣。裘，皮衣。
呼儿hū ér
hū ér: to call the young servant.呼儿：叫来年轻的仆人。
将出jiāng chū
jiāng chū: to take out. Jiāng means to take.将出：拿去。将，拿。
尔ěr
ěr: you all.尔：你们。
销xiāo
xiāo: to melt away, to get rid of.销：消除，解除。
万古愁wàn gǔ chóu
wàn gǔ chóu: endless sorrow, the sorrow people have felt since ancient times.万古愁：无穷无尽的忧愁，从古到今人人都有的愁。
03

How to read it读音

发fà
Here it means hair, so it is read fà, not fā.这里是“头发”的意思，读 fà，不读 fā。
朝zhāo
Here it means morning, so it is read zhāo, not cháo.这里是“早晨”的意思，读 zhāo，不读 cháo。
还huán
Here it means to come back, and is usually read huán. Some read it hái, meaning "will still".这里是“回来”的意思，一般读 huán。也有人读 hái，当“还会”讲。
为wéi
Wéi lè means to make merry, so it is read wéi.“为乐”是“作乐、寻欢”，读 wéi。
将qiāng
In the title phrase qiāng jìn jiǔ, this character means "please" and is read qiāng.“将进酒”的“将”是“请”的意思，读 qiāng。
为wèi
Wèi wǒ means for me, so it is read wèi.“为我”是“给我、替我”，读 wèi。
斗dǒu
Here it is an old measure for wine, read dǒu, not dòu.“斗”是古代量酒的器具，读 dǒu，不读 dòu。
为wèi
Hé wèi means why, so it is read wèi.“何为”就是“为什么”，读 wèi。
将jiāng
Jiāng chū means to take out. Here it is read jiāng, unlike the qiāng in line thirteen.“将出”是“拿出去”，这里读 jiāng，和第十三句的 qiāng 不同。
04

Background创作背景

Qiang Jin Jiu, "please drink", was the title of an old Han dynasty song. The poem is usually dated to the Tianbao years of Emperor Xuanzong; one view puts it around 752. By then Li Bai had left the capital, Chang'an, several years before, and his hopes of serving at court had come to nothing. With his friend Cen Xun he visited Yuan Danqiu at his home in the hills. The three drank and sang, and in that mood Li Bai wrote this poem. Scholars do not agree on the exact year.

“将进酒”是汉代乐府的旧题目，意思是“请喝酒”。这首诗一般认为写于唐玄宗天宝年间，一说是天宝十一载（752年）前后。那时李白离开长安已经好几年，在朝廷里的抱负没有实现。他和朋友岑勋一起，到元丹丘的山中住处做客，三人喝酒唱歌，李白借着酒兴写下了这首诗。写作的具体年份，学者说法不一。

05

The poet作者

Li Bai (701–762) was a great Tang poet, often called the Immortal of Poetry. He was bold and free, loved wine and loved to roam the mountains, and his poems fly as freely as a heavenly horse. He once wrote poems for the emperor in Chang'an, but was never given real work and soon left. The wild confidence in this poem, mixed with a sadness he cannot quite name, is Li Bai through and through.

李白（701—762），唐代大诗人，被人称为“诗仙”。他性格豪放，爱喝酒，爱游山玩水，诗写得像天马行空一样自由。他曾在长安为皇帝写诗，但不被真正重用，不久就离开了。这首诗里那种狂放和自信，又带着说不出的苦闷，正是李白的样子。

06

The mood意境

The poem opens like a floodgate. The Yellow River crashes down from the sky and never returns. Black hair in the mirror turns white as snow in a single day. Time and life run away so fast, so what should we do? Li Bai's answer: while you are happy, drink to the full! Then he cries out that heaven gave him talent for a reason, and money spent will come back. That is pure self-belief. In the middle he calls his friends by name and urges them to drink, as lively as a real party. But under the noise there is pain. When he says the sages were lonely and only drinkers are remembered, he is really protesting that gifted people like him go unused. At the end he would even trade his fine horse and fur coat for wine, just to wash away a sorrow as old as time. Reading it, you feel a force rushing like the Yellow River, and under that force, a deep sadness.

诗一开头就像打开了闸门：黄河从天上冲下来，一去不回；镜子里的黑发，一天就变成白雪。时间和生命走得这样快，怎么办？李白的回答是：趁着高兴，尽情喝酒！接着他大声说“天生我材必有用，千金散尽还复来”，这是对自己满满的信心。中间他喊朋友的名字劝酒，像真的在酒桌上一样热闹。可是热闹里藏着苦：他说圣贤都寂寞，只有喝酒的人留下名字，其实是在说有才能却不被重用的不平。最后他连好马和皮衣都要拿去换酒，只为消掉“万古愁”。读这首诗，你能感到一股像黄河一样奔腾的气势，也能感到气势下面深深的忧愁。
