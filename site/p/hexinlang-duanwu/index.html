02

Words字词注释

榴花吐liú huā tǔ
liú huā tǔ: the pomegranate flowers open. They bloom around the Dragon Boat Festival.榴花吐：石榴花开了。端午前后正是石榴花开的时候。
画帘huà lián
huà lián: a painted curtain.画帘：有彩画的帘子。
綀衣shū yī
shū yī: clothes of coarse hemp cloth, cool to wear in summer.綀衣：粗麻布做的衣服，夏天穿着凉快。
纨扇wán shàn
wán shàn: a round fan of fine silk.纨扇：用细绢做的团扇。
清暑qīng shǔ
qīng shǔ: to clear away the summer heat.清暑：驱散暑热，让人觉得清凉。
结束jié shù
jié shù: here it means one's dress and outfit, not the ending.结束：这里是打扮、装束的意思。
钗符chāi fú
chāi fú: a charm fixed to a hairpin, worn at the festival to keep evil away.钗符：插在发钗上的符，端午节用来避邪。
艾虎ài hǔ
ài hǔ: a little tiger made of mugwort, worn at the festival to keep evil away.艾虎：用艾草做成的小老虎，端午节戴在身上避邪。
观渡guān dù
guān dù: to watch the dragon boat races.观渡：看赛龙舟。
老大lǎo dà
lǎo dà: grown old.老大：年纪大了。
逢场féng chǎng
féng chǎng zuò xì: to join in the fun when the chance comes. Yōng means too lazy to.逢场作戏：碰到热闹的场合就凑凑热闹。慵，懒得。
任rèn
rèn: to let, to leave it to.任：随便，听凭。
陌头mò tóu
mò tóu: the roadside.陌头：路边。
争旗鼓zhēng qí gǔ
zhēng qí gǔ: to compete with flags and drums, that is, to race dragon boats.争旗鼓：举着旗、敲着鼓比赛，指赛龙舟。
灵均líng jūn
líng jūn: a name of Qu Yuan. He was a great poet of the state of Chu, more than two thousand years ago. Legend says he drowned himself in a river on the fifth day of the fifth month, and the festival remembers him.灵均：屈原的字。屈原是战国时楚国的大诗人，传说他在五月初五投江而死，端午节就是纪念他。
标致biāo zhì
biāo zhì: bearing and character.标致：风度、品格。
如许rú xǔ
rú xǔ: so, like this.如许：这样，如此。
纫兰佩rèn lán pèi
rèn lán pèi: to string orchids together and wear them. Qu Yuan wrote this in his poem Li Sao, to show his pure character.纫兰佩：把兰草串起来佩戴在身上。屈原在《离骚》里这样写，比喻自己品德高洁。
椒糈jiāo xǔ
jiāo xǔ: pepper and fine rice, used as offerings to the gods. Li Sao mentions carrying them.椒糈：花椒和精米，是祭神用的东西。《离骚》里有“怀椒糈而要之”。
骚魂sāo hún
sāo hún: the soul of Qu Yuan, who wrote Li Sao.骚魂：屈原的魂。屈原写过《离骚》，所以这样称呼他。
垂涎chuí xián
chuí xián: to drool, to long to eat.垂涎：流口水，想吃。
角黍jiǎo shǔ
jiǎo shǔ: zongzi, rice dumplings.角黍：粽子。
蛟馋龙怒jiāo chán lóng nù
jiāo chán lóng nù: legend says the rice thrown into the river for Qu Yuan was eaten by greedy dragons, so people wrapped it in leaves and tied it with coloured thread to keep the dragons away.蛟馋龙怒：传说投到江里祭屈原的米被蛟龙抢吃了，所以要用叶子包好、用彩线扎紧，蛟龙才不敢吃。
把似bǎ sì
bǎ sì: if, suppose.把似：假如，倘若。
醒到了xǐng dào liǎo
xǐng dào liǎo: to stay sober to the very end. Qu Yuan said that everyone else was drunk and he alone was sober.醒到了：一直清醒到最后。屈原说过“众人皆醉我独醒”。
料liào
liào: to guess, to suppose.料：料想。
差无苦chā wú kǔ
chā wú kǔ: there would have been almost no pain.差无苦：大概就没有痛苦了。
聊liáo
liáo: for now, just.聊：姑且，暂且。
吊千古diào qiān gǔ
diào qiān gǔ: to mourn the man of a thousand years ago.吊千古：凭吊千年前的古人。吊，悼念。
03

How to read it读音

綀shū
This word means clothes of coarse hemp cloth. The first character is read shū.綀衣，粗麻布做的衣服。“綀”读 shū。
载zǎi
Qiān zǎi means a thousand years. When the character means year, it is read zǎi.千载，一千年。“载”表示年时读 zǎi。
角jiǎo
Jiǎo shǔ is an old name for zongzi, the pointed rice dumplings, so the character is read jiǎo.角黍，粽子，因为有尖角而得名。“角”读 jiǎo。
了liǎo
Here the character means to the very end, so it is read liǎo.醒到了，一直醒着到最后。“了”是完结的意思，读 liǎo。
差chā
Here the character means roughly, more or less, and is read chā. Some read it chà.差无苦，大概没有痛苦了。“差”是稍微、大致的意思，读 chā；也有人读 chà。
04

Background创作背景

The Dragon Boat Festival falls on the fifth day of the fifth lunar month. People make zongzi, race dragon boats and hang mugwort, and legend says this is all to remember Qu Yuan. In this ci, Liu Kezhuang first describes the lively customs of the day, then turns to Qu Yuan, and jokes about the folk idea that Qu Yuan waits under the water for zongzi. He calls himself old, so it was written later in his life, but the exact year is not known.

端午节在农历五月初五，人们包粽子、赛龙舟、挂艾草，传说是为了纪念屈原。刘克庄这首词先写端午热闹的风俗，再写屈原，还对“屈原在水底等着吃粽子”这类民间说法开了个玩笑。词里自称“老大”，可见是年纪较大时写的，具体年份不详。

05

The poet作者

Liu Kezhuang (1187–1269), also called Houcun, came from Putian in Fujian. He was a major poet and ci writer of the late Southern Song. He was honest and dared to speak the truth, and lost his posts several times because of it. His ci follow Xin Qiji; he liked to argue a point in a ci, in words that are witty yet bitter. This ci is a good example.

刘克庄（1187—1269），号后村，福建莆田人，南宋后期的大诗人、词人。他为人正直，敢说真话，几次因此被罢官。他的词学辛弃疾，喜欢在词里发议论，语言风趣又带着辛酸，这首端午词就是一个例子。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is like a painting of festival customs: red pomegranate flowers, a curtain rolled up, children comparing their hairpin charms and mugwort tigers, everyone off to watch the dragon boats. But the poet says he is too old to join in. He only watches the hard rain on the stream and the leaping waves, so a little loneliness shows through the noise. The second stanza turns to Qu Yuan. He wore orchids and had a pure heart, yet later people say his soul drools for zongzi under the water and fears the dragons will steal them. The poet feels this belittles Qu Yuan. At the end he thinks: Qu Yuan suffered so much because he saw too clearly; had he died drunk, he would have suffered less. It sounds like a joke, but it is full of sympathy for honest people who suffer in the world, and hides the poet's own bitterness too.

上阕像一幅端午风俗画：石榴花红，帘子卷起，孩子们戴着钗符艾虎互相比美，人们都去看龙舟。词人却说自己老了，懒得凑热闹，只看到溪上急雨和翻飞的浪花，热闹里透出一点冷清。下阕写屈原。他身佩兰草，心地高洁，可后人却说他的魂在水底馋粽子，还怕被蛟龙抢了去，词人觉得这太小看屈原了。最后他想：屈原就是因为太清醒才这么痛苦，要是当年醉死，反倒少受些苦。这话听起来是玩笑，其实满是对正直的人在世上受苦的同情，也藏着词人自己的不平。
