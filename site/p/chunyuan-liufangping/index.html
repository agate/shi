chūn春yuàn怨

liú刘fāng方píng平Liu Fangping · Tang唐

Spring Sorrow

 

shā纱chuāng窗rì日luò落jiàn渐huáng黄hūn昏

jīn金wū屋wú无rén人jiàn见lèi泪hén痕

jì寂mò寞kōng空tíng庭chūn春yù欲wǎn晚

lí梨huā花mǎn满dì地bù不kāi开mén门

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 纱窗日落渐黄昏The sun sets beyond the gauze window, and dusk slowly falls.太阳从纱窗外落下，天色慢慢暗了，到了黄昏。
  2. 金屋无人见泪痕In the golden house, no one sees the traces of her tears.华美的屋子里没有人看见她脸上的泪痕。
  3. 寂寞空庭春欲晚The empty courtyard is lonely, and spring is nearly gone;空荡荡的院子冷冷清清，春天也快要过去了，
  4. 梨花满地不开门pear blossoms cover the ground, and the gate stays shut.梨花落了满地，院门却一直紧紧关着。
02

Words字词注释

纱窗shā chuāng
shā chuāng: a window covered with thin gauze.纱窗：蒙着薄纱的窗户。
渐黄昏jiàn huáng hūn
jiàn huáng hūn: slowly turning to dusk.渐黄昏：慢慢到了傍晚。
金屋jīn wū
jīn wū: a golden house, a splendid home. As a boy, Emperor Wu of Han said that if he married Ajiao he would build her a golden house, but later she lost his favor. Here it means the palace rooms of a woman who has lost the emperor's love.金屋：华美的房子。汉武帝小时候说过，如果娶到阿娇，要造金屋给她住，后来阿娇却失宠了。这里指失宠女子住的宫室。
泪痕lèi hén
lèi hén: the marks left by tears.泪痕：眼泪流过留下的痕迹。
寂寞jì mò
jì mò: lonely and quiet.寂寞：冷清，孤单。
空庭kōng tíng
kōng tíng: an empty courtyard.空庭：空荡荡的院子。
春欲晚chūn yù wǎn
chūn yù wǎn: spring is nearly over.春欲晚：春天快要过去了。
03

Background创作背景

This is a palace-lament poem. Such poems are about women in the palace who have lost, or never won, the emperor's love, and about their loneliness and grief. The poem does not say who the woman is, and the year it was written is not known. Through the dusk, the empty courtyard and the fallen pear blossoms, the poet shows her shut away alone, with no one to care for her.

这是一首宫怨诗。宫怨诗写的是住在宫里、得不到皇帝宠爱的女子的孤单和哀怨。诗里没有说这个女子是谁，具体写作年份也不清楚。诗人借黄昏、空院和满地梨花，写出她一个人关在屋里、无人问候的心情。

04

The poet作者

Liu Fangping was a Tang poet from Luoyang. His exact dates are not known; he lived around the time of Emperor Xuanzong. He spent most of his life as a recluse and never held office, and he was both a poet and a painter. Not many of his poems survive. His quatrains are his best work: with a few small things he could show delicate feelings, as this poem does.

刘方平，唐代诗人，洛阳人，生卒年不详，大约生活在唐玄宗前后。他一生大多隐居，没有做过官，会写诗，也会画画。他的诗流传下来的不多，以绝句写得最好，常常用几个小小的景物写出细腻的感情，这首诗就是一个例子。

05

The mood意境

From start to finish the poem has no speech. It is just one picture after another. The sun goes down and the sky grows dark: time is slipping away. However splendid the house, no one comes to see her, and she must wipe her own tears. The courtyard is empty and spring is leaving, as if her youth were slipping away too. The last line is the most beautiful and the saddest. White pear blossoms cover the ground, but the gate is shut. No one comes, and she does not want to go out. No one sees the flowers bloom or fall, and she too has been forgotten here. As you read, picture a quiet, darkening dusk and a ground covered with white petals.

这首诗从头到尾没有写一句话，只是一个画面接一个画面。太阳落了，天黑了，这是时间在流走。屋子再华美，也没有人来看她，她的眼泪只能自己擦。院子空空的，春天要走了，她的青春好像也一样在溜走。最后一句最美也最伤心：洁白的梨花落满一地，门却关着，没有人来，她也不想出去。花开花落都没人看，人也一样被忘在了这里。读的时候，可以想象一个安静、发暗的黄昏，和满地白白的花瓣。
