Song lyric · ci to the tune Jiang Cheng Zi, 70 characters in two stanzas宋词 · 词，词牌《江城子》，双调七十字

Jiang Cheng Zi: Willows of the West City Play with Soft Spring

 

xī西chéng城yáng杨liǔ柳nòng弄chūn春róu柔

dòng动lí离yōu忧

lèi泪nán难shōu收

yóu犹jì记duō多qíng情

céng曾wèi为xì系guī归zhōu舟

bì碧yě野zhū朱qiáo桥dāng当rì日shì事

rén人bú不jiàn见

shuǐ水kōng空liú流

sháo韶huá华bú不wèi为shào少nián年liú留

hèn恨yōu悠yōu悠

jǐ几shí时xiū休

fēi飞xù絮luò落huā花shí时hòu候yì一dēng登lóu楼

biàn便zuò做chūn春jiāng江dōu都shì是lèi泪

liú流bú不jìn尽

xǔ许duō多chóu愁

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 西城杨柳弄春柔In the west city, willows sway softly in spring,西城的杨柳在春风里轻柔地摆动，
  2. 动离忧Stirring up the sorrow of parting,勾起了我离别的忧愁，
  3. 泪难收And my tears cannot be held back.眼泪怎么也止不住。
  4. 犹记多情I still remember the one so full of feeling,还记得那位多情的人，
  5. 曾为系归舟Who once tied my returning boat to the willows.曾经为我把归来的船拴在柳树上。
  6. 碧野朱桥当日事Green fields and a red bridge, those days are still before my eyes,绿色的原野，红色的小桥，那些往日的事情还在眼前，
  7. 人不见But the one I loved is gone,可是人已经不见了，
  8. 水空流And only the river flows on in vain.只有河水白白地流着。
  9. 韶华不为少年留Lovely youth will not stay for the young;美好的青春不会为年轻人停留，
  10. 恨悠悠This regret goes on and on,这遗憾绵绵不断，
  11. 几时休When will it ever end?什么时候才能停止？
  12. 飞絮落花时候一登楼In the season of flying catkins and falling flowers, I climb the tower.在柳絮飞舞、落花纷纷的时候，我登上了高楼。
  13. 便做春江都是泪Even if the whole spring river were made of my tears,即使整条春江都变成我的眼泪，
  14. 流不尽It could not carry away也流不完，
  15. 许多愁All of my sorrow.我心中这么多的愁。
02

Words字词注释

西城xī chéng
xī chéng: the west side of the city. One view says it is the western outskirts of Bianjing, the Northern Song capital, today's Kaifeng in Henan.西城：城的西边。一说指北宋都城汴京（今河南开封）西郊。
弄春柔nòng chūn róu
nòng chūn róu: willow branches sway gently in the spring wind, soft and lovely.弄春柔：柳枝在春风里轻轻摆动，显出柔美的样子。
离忧lí yōu
lí yōu: the sorrow of parting.离忧：离别的忧愁。
多情duō qíng
duō qíng: someone full of tender feeling; it can also mean the caring willows.多情：指情意深厚的人，也可以理解成有情的杨柳。
系归舟xì guī zhōu
xì guī zhōu: tying the returning boat to a willow on the bank.系归舟：把回来的船拴在岸边的柳树上。
碧野朱桥bì yě zhū qiáo
bì yě zhū qiáo: green fields and a red bridge.碧野朱桥：碧绿的原野，红色的小桥。
当日事dāng rì shì
dāng rì shì: what happened in those days.当日事：当年发生的事。
水空流shuǐ kōng liú
shuǐ kōng liú: the water flows on for nothing.水空流：河水白白地流着。
韶华sháo huá
sháo huá: the lovely days of youth.韶华：美好的时光，青春。
悠悠yōu yōu
yōu yōu: long and endless.悠悠：长久不断的样子。
几时休jǐ shí xiū
jǐ shí xiū: when will it ever stop?几时休：什么时候才能停止。
便做biàn zuò
biàn zuò: even if.便做：即使，就算。
03

How to read it读音

系xì
Here this character means to tie, and is read xì.“系”这里是拴、绑的意思，读 xì。
为wèi
Céng wèi means once did it for me, so this character is read wèi.“曾为”是曾经替我，“为”读 wèi。
为wèi
Bù wèi shào nián liú means does not stay for the young, so this character is read wèi.“不为少年留”是不为年轻人停留，“为”读 wèi。
04

Background创作背景

In this lyric the poet returns to an old place in late spring. He remembers meeting and parting from someone he loved here. Now that person is gone, and only he is left with his sorrow. It is usually dated to around 1094. At that time Qin Guan was sent away from the capital because of fights between court factions, and he wrote this lyric before leaving Bianjing. Other views give a different date.

这首词写词人在暮春时节重回旧地，想起当年和心爱的人在这里相会、分别，如今人已不见，只剩他一个人伤感。一般认为写于宋哲宗绍圣元年（1094年）前后，当时秦观因为朝廷里的党派斗争，被贬出京城，离开汴京前写下了这首词。一说写作时间另有不同。

05

The poet作者

Qin Guan (1049–1100), known as the Lay Buddhist of Huaihai, came from Gaoyou near Yangzhou, in today's Jiangsu. He was a student of Su Shi and one of the Four Scholars of Su's circle. His career went badly, and he was sent to faraway posts again and again. His lyrics are tender and soft in language, and he was best at writing about parting and sorrow. He is a leading poet of the gentle, delicate style of ci.

秦观（1049—1100），字少游，号淮海居士，扬州高邮（今属江苏）人。他是苏轼的学生，“苏门四学士”之一。他在政治上很不顺利，一次次被贬到远方。他的词感情细腻，语言柔美，最擅长写离别和愁思，是婉约派的代表词人。

06

The mood意境

It opens with spring willows swaying softly. The scene is lovely, yet it stirs his grief at once, and his tears will not stop. That is because he remembers the past. By willows just like these, the one who loved him once tied up his returning boat. The green fields and red bridge are still here, but that person is not, and the river flows on for nothing. The second stanza widens from one parting to time itself. Youth stays for no one, and regret never ends. Climbing a tower while catkins fly and petals fall only adds to the sorrow. The last three lines are the peak: even if the whole spring river were my tears, it could not carry away all my sorrow. He compares sorrow to a river, and the river to tears. It is an exaggeration, but it feels true, and once you read it you remember it.

开头写春天的杨柳，柔柔地摆动，本来很美，却一下子勾起了离愁，眼泪止不住。因为他想起了从前：也是在这样的柳树边，那个多情的人曾为他系住归来的小船。绿野、红桥都还在，人却不在了，只有河水白白流着。下片从个人的离别扩大到时光：青春不会为谁停留，遗憾没有尽头。在柳絮纷飞、落花满地的时候登上高楼，愁就更多了。最后三句是全词的高潮：就算整条春江都是我的眼泪，也流不完我的愁。用江水比愁，又用眼泪比江水，夸张却真实，让人一读就记住。
