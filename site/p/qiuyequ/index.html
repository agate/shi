秋夜曲

王涯Wang Ya · Tang唐

Autumn Night Song

 

guì桂pò魄chū初shēng生qiū秋lù露wēi微

qīng轻luó罗yǐ已bó薄wèi未gēng更yī衣

yín银zhēng筝yè夜jiǔ久yīn殷qín勤nòng弄

xīn心qiè怯kōng空fáng房bù不rěn忍guī归

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 桂魄初生秋露微The moon has just risen, and the autumn dew is still light.月亮刚刚升起，秋天的露水还很少；
  2. 轻罗已薄未更衣Her silk dress already feels too thin, yet she has not changed it.身上的丝绸衣服已经显得单薄，她却还没有换上厚衣。
  3. 银筝夜久殷勤弄Deep into the night she keeps playing her silver zheng,夜深了，她还在一遍遍地弹着银筝，
  4. 心怯空房不忍归afraid of the empty room, she cannot bear to go back.因为害怕冷清的空房，舍不得回去。
02

Words字词注释

桂魄guì pò
guì pò: the moon. Legend says an osmanthus tree grows on the moon, and pò means moonlight.桂魄：月亮。传说月亮里有桂树；魄，指月亮的光。
初生chū shēng
chū shēng: just rising.初生：刚刚升起。
微wēi
wēi: slight, just a little.微：少，一点点。
轻罗qīng luó
qīng luó: light silk clothes.轻罗：轻薄的丝绸衣服。
更衣gēng yī
gēng yī: to change clothes.更衣：换衣服。
银筝yín zhēng
yín zhēng: a zheng decorated with silver. The zheng is a plucked string instrument.银筝：用银子装饰的筝。筝是一种弹拨的弦乐器。
殷勤弄yīn qín nòng
yīn qín nòng: playing again and again with great care. Nòng means to play.殷勤弄：一遍又一遍、很用心地弹。弄，弹奏。
心怯xīn qiè
xīn qiè: afraid at heart.心怯：心里害怕。
不忍bù rěn
bù rěn: cannot bear to.不忍：不愿意，狠不下心。
03

How to read it读音

薄bó
This character, meaning thin, has a reading-style pronunciation bó. In everyday speech, used alone, it is often báo. Both are heard.薄：读书音 bó，口语里单说“薄”常读 báo，两种读法都有人用。
更gēng
Here the character means to change, so it is read gēng, not gèng.更：这里是“更换”的意思，读 gēng，不读 gèng。
04

Background创作背景

This poem uses a Yuefu song title. It shows a woman playing the zheng alone on an autumn night. The one she loves is not with her, so she does not want to go back to her empty room. Old books disagree on the author. "Ten Thousand Tang Quatrains" and the Wang Ya section of the "Complete Tang Poems" name Wang Ya. The "Yuefu Poetry Collection" and "Three Hundred Tang Poems" name Wang Wei. "Records of Tang Poetry" says Zhang Zhongsu. This page follows Wang Ya. When it was written is unknown.

这首诗是乐府题目，写一位女子在秋夜里独自弹筝。她的丈夫或心上人不在身边，所以她不愿回到空荡荡的房间。作者在古书里记法不一：《万首唐人绝句》和《全唐诗》的王涯卷署名王涯；《乐府诗集》和《唐诗三百首》署名王维；《唐诗纪事》又说是张仲素。这里按王涯写。写作时间不详。

05

The poet作者

Wang Ya (died 835) was a Tang poet who rose to chancellor. In 835 came the Sweet Dew Incident, when officials plotted to remove the powerful palace eunuchs and failed. Wang Ya was killed. He wrote many short palace poems and Yuefu songs in fresh, clear language.

王涯（？—835），唐代诗人，中唐时做到宰相。835年发生“甘露之变”，大臣们谋划除掉掌权的宦官，失败了，王涯也被杀害。他写过不少宫词和乐府小诗，语言清新。

06

The mood意境

Not one word says "I miss him", yet longing is everywhere. The first line sets the scene: the moon has just risen and the dew is light. It is an early autumn night. In the second line her clothes feel thin, but she does not bother to change, so her mind is elsewhere. In the third line it is late, and she keeps playing over and over. Only the last line gives the reason: she is afraid to go back to the empty room, where she would feel even lonelier. She is not playing to make lovely music, but to pass the lonely hours. Picture a figure in the moonlight, and the music going on deep into the night.

诗里没有一个字直接说“想念”，却处处是想念。第一句写景：月亮刚升，露水还少，是初秋的夜晚。第二句写她觉得衣服薄了，却懒得换，可见心思不在这上面。第三句说夜已深，她还在弹筝，一遍又一遍。最后一句才说出原因：她怕回到空房里，一个人更孤单。弹筝不是为了好听，而是为了打发孤独。读的时候，可以想象月光下一个人影，琴声一直响到深夜。
