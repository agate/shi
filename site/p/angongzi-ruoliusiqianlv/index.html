An Gongzi: A Thousand Threads of Young Willow

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 弱柳丝千缕Soft willows hang down a thousand threads,柔弱的柳枝垂下千万条丝，
  2. 嫩黄匀遍鸦啼处and where the crows call, tender yellow buds cover them evenly.乌鸦啼叫的柳树上，到处匀匀地长满了嫩黄的新芽。
  3. 寒入罗衣春尚浅The cold slips into my thin silk clothes; spring is still young,寒气钻进薄薄的丝衣，春天还刚刚开始，
  4. 过一番风雨and another round of wind and rain has passed.又经过了一场风雨。
  5. 问燕子来时I ask the swallows: when you flew here,问问燕子：你飞来的时候，
  6. 绿水桥边路along the road by the bridge over the green water,经过绿水桥边那条路，
  7. 曾画楼见个人人否did you see my dear one in that painted house?可曾在那座画楼上，见到我心爱的那个人？
  8. 料静掩云窗I guess she sits quietly behind her closed window,我猜她正静静地关着窗子，
  9. 尘满哀弦危柱and dust covers the sad strings and tall bridges of her zither.琴上的哀弦和高柱都落满了灰尘。
  10. 庾信愁如许Like Yu Xin, I have this much sorrow.我像庾信一样，愁有这么多。
  11. 为谁都著眉端聚For whom does it all gather at my brow?为了谁，这些愁都聚在眉头上？
  12. 独立东风弹泪眼I stand alone in the east wind, wiping my tears,我独自站在东风里，擦着泪眼，
  13. 寄烟波东去and trust my longing to the misty waters flowing east.把思念托给烟雾里的江水，向东流去。
  14. 念永昼春闲I think of the long, idle spring days:想到春天的白天又长又闲，
  15. 人倦如何度so weary, how will I get through them?人这样困倦，该怎么熬过去？
  16. 闲傍枕百啭黄鹂语Idly I lean on my pillow while the orioles sing on and on.我无聊地靠着枕头，听黄鹂婉转地叫个不停。
  17. 唤觉来厌厌Woken by them, I am still dull and drowsy,被叫醒以后，还是昏昏沉沉的，
  18. 残照依然花坞and the last of the sunset still lies on the flower hollow.落日的余光仍旧照在花坞上。
02

Words字词注释

弱柳ruò liǔ
ruò liǔ: soft, slender willow branches.弱柳：柔软的柳枝。
千缕qiān lǚ
qiān lǚ: a thousand strands.千缕：千条。缕，一根根的细丝。
嫩黄匀遍nèn huáng yún biàn
nèn huáng yún biàn: tender yellow buds spread evenly everywhere.嫩黄匀遍：嫩黄的新芽均匀地长满了。
鸦啼处yā tí chù
yā tí chù: where the crows call, that is, in the willows.鸦啼处：乌鸦叫的地方，就是柳树上。
罗衣luó yī
luó yī: clothes made of thin, soft silk.罗衣：用轻软的丝绸做的衣服。
春尚浅chūn shàng qiǎn
chūn shàng qiǎn: spring is still young.春尚浅：春天还刚开始，不深。
画楼huà lóu
huà lóu: a painted, finely decorated house.画楼：雕梁画栋的漂亮楼房。
个人人gè rén rén
gè rén rén: that dear one. Doubling the word for person was a fond way to name a loved one.个人人：那个人。“人人”是对心爱的人亲昵的叫法。
料liào
liào: to guess, to imagine.料：料想，猜想。
云窗yún chuāng
yún chuāng: a window carved with cloud patterns, meaning a woman's room.云窗：雕着云纹的窗子，指女子住的房间。
哀弦危柱āi xián wēi zhù
āi xián wēi zhù: the sad-sounding strings and tall bridges of a zither. Covered in dust, they show she has not played for a long time.哀弦危柱：琴瑟上发出哀伤声音的弦和高高的弦柱。尘满，说明她很久没弹了。
庾信yǔ xìn
yǔ xìn: Yu Xin, a writer of the Southern and Northern Dynasties. Stuck in the north in later life, he longed for his southern home and wrote a piece on sorrow. Later poets used his name for a person full of grief.庾信：南北朝时的文学家，晚年流落北方，常思念南方故乡，写过《愁赋》，后来人用他代指满怀愁绪的人。
如许rú xǔ
rú xǔ: this much.如许：这么多。
都著眉端聚dōu zhuó méi duān jù
dōu zhuó méi duān jù: all gathered at the brow, that is, frowning with worry.都著眉端聚：全都聚在眉头上，就是愁得皱眉。
弹泪眼tán lèi yǎn
tán lèi yǎn: flicking away the tears from the eyes.弹泪眼：擦去、弹落眼中的泪。
寄烟波东去jì yān bō dōng qù
jì yān bō dōng qù: trusting my longing to the misty water and letting it carry it east.寄烟波东去：把思念托给烟雾笼罩的江水，让它带着向东流去。
永昼yǒng zhòu
yǒng zhòu: the long day.永昼：长长的白天。
如何度rú hé dù
rú hé dù: how to get through it.如何度：怎么熬过去。
傍枕bàng zhěn
bàng zhěn: leaning on the pillow.傍枕：靠着枕头。
百啭bǎi zhuàn
bǎi zhuàn: birds singing on and on in many turns. Zhuàn is birdsong.百啭：鸟儿婉转地叫个不停。啭，鸟叫。
黄鹂huáng lí
huáng lí: the oriole, a yellow bird with a lovely song.黄鹂：一种黄色的鸟，叫声好听。
唤觉来huàn jué lái
huàn jué lái: after being woken by the birds. Jué means to wake.唤觉来：被鸟叫声唤醒以后。觉，醒。
厌厌yān yān
yān yān: listless and drowsy.厌厌：没精神、昏昏沉沉的样子。
残照cán zhào
cán zhào: the last light of the setting sun.残照：落日的余光。
花坞huā wù
huā wù: a sheltered hollow full of flowers.花坞：四周高中间低、种满花的地方。
03

How to read it读音

著zhuó
Here it means to rest on, to gather at, so it is read zhuó.“著”在这里是附着、聚在的意思，读 zhuó，同“着（zhuó）”。
厌yān
Here the doubled word means the same as yān yān, listless and drowsy, so it is read yān, not yàn.“厌厌”同“恹恹”，是没精神的样子，读 yān yān，不读 yàn。
厌yān
As above, the doubled word is read yān yān.同上，“厌厌”读 yān yān。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about missing a loved one far away in early spring. Exactly when it was written, and for whom, is no longer known. The first stanza starts with the willows and the wind and rain in front of him. Then he asks the swallows whether they saw her, and imagines that she too is lonely and missing him. The second stanza shows his own sorrow: frowning, tears, long spring days. He can only lean on his pillow and listen to the birds as another day slips by.

这首词写早春时节对远方心上人的思念，具体写作时间和对象已经不清楚。上片从眼前的柳树、风雨写起，再问燕子有没有见到她，猜想她也在孤单地想念自己。下片写自己愁得皱眉流泪，春日漫长，只能靠着枕头听鸟叫，一天又过去了。

05

The poet作者

Yuan Quhua was a Southern Song poet from Fengxin, in today's Jiangxi. His dates of birth and death are not known. He passed the top imperial exam under Emperor Gaozong and served as a minor local official. Not many of his lyrics survive. They are clear, graceful and gentle, good at setting scenes and showing feelings, and several were chosen for the famous collection Three Hundred Song Lyrics.

袁去华，字宣卿，南宋词人，奉新（今属江西）人，生卒年不详。他在宋高宗绍兴年间考中进士，做过地方小官。他的词留下来的不算多，风格清丽婉转，善于写景抒情，《宋词三百首》选了他好几首。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is about a longing that cannot be said aloud. It opens with beauty: a thousand willow threads and tender yellow buds. But the spring cold is still here and a storm has just passed, so the beauty feels chilly. Then, instead of saying "I miss her", he asks the swallows: you flew past her house, did you see her? Of course there is no answer, but the question makes his longing sweet. He even imagines her side: she must keep her window shut, and her zither gathers dust because she has no heart to play. The second stanza returns to him. Sorrow gathers at his brow, and he sends his tears down the river flowing east. The last picture is very still. He leans on his pillow, the orioles keep singing, and when he wakes the sunset still lights the flowers. Time seems to stop, and only the longing goes on.

这首词写的是一种“说不出口的想念”。开头很美：千条柳丝，嫩黄的新芽，可春寒还在，风雨刚过，美里带着冷。接着他不直接说想她，而是去问燕子：你从她家门前飞过，见到她了吗？这个问题当然没有答案，却把想念写得很可爱。他还替对方想：她一定也关着窗，琴都落了灰，没心思弹了。下片回到自己，愁聚在眉头，眼泪托给东流的江水。最后一个画面很安静：他靠着枕头，黄鹂叫个不停，醒来时夕阳还照着花丛。日子好像停住了，只有想念一直在。
