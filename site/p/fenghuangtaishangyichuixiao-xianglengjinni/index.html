Remembering the Flute on Phoenix Terrace: The Incense Is Cold

bèi被fān翻hóng红làng浪

The corpus has a different version of this lyric, with many changes. For example, it has "I have not yet combed my hair" for "I cannot bother to comb my hair", "this year I am thin" for "lately I am thin", "tomorrow morning" for "enough, let it go", "spring is late in Wuling, clouds lock the many-storeyed tower" for "the man of Wuling is far, mist locks the Qin tower", and "from now I will count how many new sorrows" for "from now one more new sorrow". Here we use the text in common anthologies.

语料库是另一个版本，有多处不同：“起来慵自梳头”作“起来人未梳头”，“任宝奁尘满”作“任宝奁闲掩”，“生怕离怀别苦”作“生怕闲愁暗恨”，“新来瘦”作“今年瘦”，“休休”作“明朝”，“也则难留”作“也即难留”，“念武陵人远，烟锁秦楼”作“念武陵春晚，云锁重楼”，“惟有楼前流水”作“记取楼前绿水”，“从今又添，一段新愁”作“从今更数，几段新愁”。这里用常见选本的文字。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 香冷金猊The incense in the lion-shaped burner has gone cold.狮子香炉里的香已经冷了，
  2. 被翻红浪The red quilt lies tossed like rolling waves.红被子乱摊着像翻起的波浪，
  3. 起来慵自梳头I get up and cannot bother to comb my hair.起床后我懒得梳头。
  4. 任宝奁尘满I let the make-up box gather dust,任凭梳妆盒落满了灰，
  5. 日上帘钩while the sun climbs as high as the curtain hook.太阳已经升到了帘钩那么高。
  6. 生怕离怀别苦What I fear most is the pain of parting.最怕的是离别的愁苦，
  7. 多少事So many things are on my mind,有多少心事，
  8. 欲说还休I want to say them, then stop.想说出来又停住了。
  9. 新来瘦Lately I have grown thin,最近我瘦了，
  10. 非干病酒not because wine has made me ill,不是因为喝酒喝病了，
  11. 不是悲秋and not because autumn makes me sad.也不是因为秋天让人伤心。
  12. 休休Enough, let it go!算了吧，算了吧！
  13. 这回去也This time he is going.这一次他要走了，
  14. 千万遍阳关Even if we sang the farewell song ten thousand times,就算把送别的歌唱上千万遍，
  15. 也则难留he still could not be kept.也还是留不住他。
  16. 念武陵人远I think of him, gone far away,想到远行的人已经走远，
  17. 烟锁秦楼while mist closes round my tower.烟雾笼罩着我住的楼。
  18. 惟有楼前流水Only the stream in front of the tower只有楼前的流水，
  19. 应念我must remember me,应该会记得我，
  20. 终日凝眸gazing out all day long.一整天都在望着远方。
  21. 凝眸处There where I gaze,在我凝望的地方，
  22. 从今又添from today on there will be从今天起又要添上
  23. 一段新愁one more new sorrow.一段新的愁苦。
02

Words字词注释

金猊jīn ní
jīn ní: a bronze incense burner shaped like a lion. Ní is a lion-like beast from old stories.金猊：狮子形状的铜香炉。猊，狻猊，传说中像狮子的猛兽。
被翻红浪bèi fān hóng làng
bèi fān hóng làng: the red quilt lies tossed on the bed like rolling red waves.被翻红浪：红色的被子乱摊在床上，像翻起的红色波浪。
慵自yōng zì
yōng: lazy, without spirit.慵：懒，没有精神。
任rèn
rèn: to let it be.任：任凭，随它去。
宝奁bǎo lián
bǎo lián: a fine box for combs and make-up.宝奁：精美的梳妆盒。
日上帘钩rì shàng lián gōu
rì shàng lián gōu: the sun has climbed as high as the curtain hook, so it is already late in the morning.日上帘钩：太阳升到挂帘子的钩子那么高了，就是说时间已经不早。
生怕shēng pà
shēng pà: to fear most of all.生怕：最怕，非常怕。
离怀别苦lí huái bié kǔ
lí huái bié kǔ: the sorrow and pain of parting.离怀别苦：离别的愁思和痛苦。
欲说还休yù shuō hái xiū
yù shuō hái xiū: wanting to speak, but stopping.欲说还休：想说又停住不说了。
新来xīn lái
xīn lái: lately.新来：近来。
非干病酒fēi gān bìng jiǔ
fēi gān bìng jiǔ: it is not because wine has made her ill.非干病酒：不是因为喝酒喝病了。非干，不关。
悲秋bēi qiū
bēi qiū: feeling sad because autumn has come.悲秋：因为秋天到了而伤心。
休休xiū xiū
xiū xiū: enough, let it go.休休：算了吧，罢了。
这回去也zhè huí qù yě
zhè huí qù yě: this time, he is going away.这回去也：这一次离开呀。
阳关yáng guān
yáng guān: the Song of Yangguan, a farewell song set to Wang Wei's parting poem. Singing it ten thousand times means singing it over and over.阳关：指《阳关曲》，是根据王维的送别诗谱成的送别歌。唱千万遍，是说送别的歌唱了无数遍。
也则难留yě zé nán liú
yě zé nán liú: still he cannot be kept.也则难留：也还是留不住。
武陵人远wǔ líng rén yuǎn
wǔ líng rén yuǎn: the man of Wuling, from an old story of someone who wandered into a land of immortals. It stands for her husband, now far away.武陵人远：武陵人，用古代传说里进入仙境的人，代指远行的丈夫。人远，人已经走远了。
烟锁秦楼yān suǒ qín lóu
yān suǒ qín lóu: mist locks in the Qin tower. In legend this was where Nongyu, daughter of Duke Mu of Qin, lived and played the flute with her husband Xiao Shi. Here it means her own room.烟锁秦楼：烟雾笼罩着秦楼。秦楼，传说中秦穆公的女儿弄玉和丈夫萧史吹箫住的楼，这里指自己住的楼。
凝眸níng móu
níng móu: to gaze fixedly, without looking away.凝眸：目不转睛地看，一直盯着看。
又添yòu tiān
yòu tiān: adds once more.又添：又增加了。
03

How to read it读音

还hái
In yù shuō hái xiū it is usually read hái, "and yet". Some read it huán.欲说还休的“还”，现在一般读 hái，意思是“又、却”。也有人读 huán。
干gān
In fēi gān it is read gān, "to concern", not gàn.非干的“干”读 gān，意思是“关系到”，不读 gàn。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about the sorrow Li Qingzhao felt at home when her husband Zhao Mingcheng was setting off on a long journey. It is usually thought to have been written around the time they parted, but sources disagree on the year. The tune name comes from a legend. Nongyu, daughter of Duke Mu of Qin, and her husband Xiao Shi both loved to play the flute, and in the end they flew away together on phoenixes. So the tune suits a lyric about a husband and wife apart.

这首词写的是丈夫赵明诚要出远门，李清照在家中送别时的愁苦。一般认为是她和丈夫分别前后写的，但具体是哪一年，说法不一。词牌“凤凰台上忆吹箫”来自一个传说：秦穆公的女儿弄玉和丈夫萧史都爱吹箫，后来一起乘着凤凰飞走了。这个词牌很适合写夫妻离别。

05

The poet作者

Li Qingzhao (1084 to about 1155), also called Yi'an, was the most famous woman poet of the Song. She and her husband Zhao Mingcheng loved each other deeply and collected old bronzes, stone rubbings, books and paintings together. Her early lyrics are mostly about life at home and missing someone far away. Her words are plain yet delicate, a model of the gentle style.

李清照（1084—约1155），号易安居士，宋代最有名的女词人。她和丈夫赵明诚感情很好，两人一起收藏金石书画。她前期的词多写闺中生活和离别相思，语言浅白又细腻，是婉约词的代表。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is about parting, yet it almost never says "I am sad" outright. It begins with one morning: the incense burner is cold, the quilt is tossed, she gets up but cannot bother to comb her hair, the make-up box gathers dust, and the sun is already high. These small things show that she has no heart for anything. She says she has much to say but cannot say it. She has grown thin, but not from wine and not from autumn. She leaves the real reason unsaid, and the reader understands at once: her husband is leaving. The second stanza opens with "enough, enough", like a long sigh. No number of farewell songs can keep him. He is far away now, mist locks in her tower, and only the stream below keeps her company as she gazes out all day. At the end she says the place where she gazes now holds one more new sorrow. Her longing is drawn out thin and long.

这首词写离别，却几乎不直接说“我很难过”。开头只写一个早晨：香炉冷了，被子乱着，人起来了却懒得梳头，梳妆盒积了灰，太阳都老高了。这些小事一摆出来，就能看出她没心思做任何事。她说有很多话想说又说不出口；人瘦了，却不是因为喝酒，也不是因为秋天。没说出的原因，读者一看就懂：是因为丈夫要走。下阕一声“休休”，像叹了一口长气：唱多少遍送别歌也留不住他。人走远了，楼被烟雾锁住，只有楼前的流水陪她整天张望。最后说望的地方又多了一段新愁，把思念写得又细又长。
