To the Tune Yi Jian Mei: Sailing Past Wujiang

The commonly taught text has "qiū niáng dù yǔ tài niáng qiáo" in line four, two place names in Wujiang: Qiuniang Ferry and Tainiang Bridge. The corpus text has two different characters that mean "pass" and "charming", usually thought to be a copying difference; textbooks and most anthologies use "ferry" and "bridge". The corpus also writes the tune name with an old form of the character for "cut".

通行本第四句作“秋娘渡与泰娘桥”，两个都是吴江的地名。这里的古籍底本作“秋娘度与泰娘娇”，一般认为是传抄时的差异，课本和多数选本用“渡”和“桥”。词牌名“一剪梅”，底本写作“一翦梅”，“翦”是“剪”的旧写法。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 一片春愁待酒浇A heart full of spring sorrow waits for wine to drown it.满怀春愁，等着用酒来浇灭。
  2. 江上舟摇My boat rocks on the river,船在江上摇晃，
  3. 楼上帘招and a wine shop's sign waves from a building on the bank.岸边楼上的酒旗在招呼客人。
  4. 秋娘渡与泰娘桥I pass Qiuniang Ferry and Tainiang Bridge;船经过秋娘渡和泰娘桥，
  5. 风又飘飘the wind blows and blows,风飘飘地吹，
  6. 雨又萧萧the rain patters and patters.雨萧萧地下。
  7. 何日归家洗客袍When can I go home and wash my travelling robe?什么时候才能回到家，洗去旅途的风尘？
  8. 银字笙调Play the silver-marked sheng,吹着标了银字的笙，
  9. 心字香烧light the heart-shaped incense.点起心字形的香。
  10. 流光容易把人抛Time so easily leaves us behind:时光很容易就把人远远甩在后面，
  11. 红了樱桃the cherries turn red,樱桃红了，
  12. 绿了芭蕉the banana leaves turn green.芭蕉绿了。
02

Words字词注释

春愁chūn chóu
chūn chóu: the sadness that comes with spring.春愁：春天里生出的愁绪。
待酒浇dài jiǔ jiāo
dài jiǔ jiāo: waiting to be drowned in wine, that is, hoping wine will wash the sorrow away.待酒浇：等着用酒来浇灭，也就是想借酒消愁。
舟摇zhōu yáo
zhōu yáo: the boat rocks on the river.舟摇：船在江上摇晃。
帘招lián zhāo
lián zhāo: lián here is a wine shop's cloth sign; it flaps in the wind as if beckoning to customers.帘：酒旗，酒店门口挂的布招牌。帘招：酒旗在风里飘动，好像在招呼客人。
秋娘渡与泰娘桥qiū niáng dù yǔ tài niáng qiáo
qiū niáng dù yǔ tài niáng qiáo: Qiuniang Ferry and Tainiang Bridge, two places in Wujiang, in today's Suzhou, Jiangsu.秋娘渡、泰娘桥：都是吴江的地名。吴江在今江苏苏州。
飘飘piāo piāo
piāo piāo: blowing and gusting.飘飘：风吹的样子。
萧萧xiāo xiāo
xiāo xiāo: the sound of rain falling.萧萧：下雨的声音。
客袍kè páo
kè páo: the robe of a traveller.客袍：出门在外穿的衣服。
银字笙yín zì shēng
yín zì shēng: a sheng with its notes marked in silver. The sheng is a mouth organ made of bamboo pipes.银字笙：用银粉在上面写着字、标出音高的笙。笙是一种竹管做的吹奏乐器。
心字香xīn zì xiāng
xīn zì xiāng: incense coiled into the shape of the character for "heart".心字香：盘成“心”字形状的香。
流光liú guāng
liú guāng: time that flows away like water.流光：像水一样流走的时光。
把人抛bǎ rén pāo
bǎ rén pāo: leaves people behind.把人抛：把人甩在后面。
红了樱桃hóng le yīng táo
hóng le yīng táo: the cherries have turned red.红了樱桃：樱桃变红了。
绿了芭蕉lǜ le bā jiāo
lǜ le bā jiāo: the banana leaves have turned green. Bājiāo is a plant with huge leaves.绿了芭蕉：芭蕉叶变绿了。芭蕉是一种叶子很大的植物。

Read tiáo here, meaning "to play, to tune", not diào.这里是“调弄、吹奏”的意思，读 tiáo，不读 diào。

Read le here, a little grammar word meaning the change has happened.这里是助词，读 le。
04

Background创作背景

Jiang Jie lived at the very end of the Southern Song. After the Song fell, he refused to serve the new Yuan rulers. He drifted from place to place and later lived as a hermit on Zhushan by Lake Tai. This lyric describes sailing past Wujiang, in today's Suzhou, through wind and rain, and missing home. It is usually thought to come from his wandering years after the Song fell; some think it was written before. The exact year is not known.

蒋捷生活在南宋末年。南宋灭亡后，他不愿意到元朝做官，四处漂泊，后来隐居在太湖边的竹山。这首词写他坐船经过吴江（今江苏苏州吴江区）时，遇上风雨，想起家乡。一般认为写于宋亡后漂泊的时候，也有人认为写于宋亡前，具体年份不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Jiang Jie, courtesy name Shengyu, also called Zhushan, came from Yangxian, today's Yixing in Jiangsu. His birth and death years are not known. He passed the highest imperial exam in 1274, and soon after the Southern Song fell. He was a famous lyric poet of the late Song. Because the lines "the cherries turn red, the banana leaves turn green" were so loved, people called him "the Cherry Scholar".

蒋捷，字胜欲，号竹山，阳羡（今江苏宜兴）人，生卒年不详。宋度宗咸淳十年（1274）考中进士，不久南宋就灭亡了。他是宋末有名的词人。因为这首词的“红了樱桃，绿了芭蕉”写得太好，人们叫他“樱桃进士”。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is about sorrow on the road. Spring has come, but he is still on a rocking boat. A wine shop's sign waves from the bank, as if saying: come and have a drink. The boat passes two places with pretty names, but the weather is bad, the wind keeps blowing and the rain keeps falling. The word "again" in both lines shows how weary he is. The second stanza longs for home. How good it would be: to wash off the dust of travel, play the sheng, light the incense, and live a calm life. But time waits for no one. In a blink the cherries are red, the banana leaves are green, and spring is passing again. The last two lines name only two colours, yet they let us see time moving and people growing old. The lines are short and the sounds light, so it reads like a song.

上阕写路上的愁。春天到了，他却还坐在摇晃的船上。岸上的酒旗在招手，好像在说：来喝一杯吧。船经过两个名字好听的地方，可天气不好，风吹个不停，雨下个不停。“又飘飘”“又萧萧”，一个“又”字，听得出他有多烦。下阕想回家。回家多好：洗掉旅途的灰尘，吹吹笙，点上香，过安稳日子。可时光不等人，转眼樱桃红了，芭蕉绿了，春天又要过去了。最后两句只写两种颜色，却让人一下子看到时间在走，人在变老。全词句子短，字音轻快，读起来像一首歌。
