题李凝幽居

贾岛Jia Dao · Tang唐

Written at Li Ning's Secluded Home

 

xián闲jū居shǎo少lín邻bìng并

cǎo草jìng径rù入huāng荒yuán园

niǎo鸟sù宿chí池biān边shù树

sēng僧qiāo敲yuè月xià下mén门

guò过qiáo桥fēn分yě野sè色

yí移shí石dòng动yún云gēn根

zàn暂qù去huán还lái来cǐ此

yōu幽qī期bú不fù负yán言

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 闲居少邻并My friend lives here quietly, with few neighbors around.朋友在这里清闲地住着，周围很少有邻居；
  2. 草径入荒园A grassy path leads into a wild garden.一条长满野草的小路，通进荒凉的园子。
  3. 鸟宿池边树Birds roost in the trees beside the pond,鸟儿在池塘边的树上睡了，
  4. 僧敲月下门and I, a monk, knock at his door under the moon.我这个和尚，在月光下敲着他的门。
  5. 过桥分野色Crossing the bridge, I see the fields split into different colors.走过小桥，看见桥两边田野的景色不同；
  6. 移石动云根Clouds drift by, and the rocks beneath them seem to move.云影移动，好像山石也跟着在动。
  7. 暂去还来此I am leaving for now, but I will come back here.我暂时离开，还会回到这里，
  8. 幽期不负言I will keep my promise to return to this quiet place.一起隐居的约定，我决不会失信。
02

Words字词注释

闲居xián jū
xián jū: a quiet home.闲居：清闲的住处。
少邻并shǎo lín bìng
shǎo lín bìng: few neighbors nearby.少邻并：很少有邻居。邻并，邻居。
草径cǎo jìng
cǎo jìng: a little path overgrown with grass.草径：长满野草的小路。
荒园huāng yuán
huāng yuán: a wild, untended garden.荒园：荒凉的园子。
僧sēng
sēng: a monk. Here it is the poet himself, who was a monk when young.僧：和尚。这里是诗人自己，贾岛年轻时当过和尚。
分野色fēn yě sè
fēn yě sè: the bridge divides the colors of the fields on either side.分野色：桥两边的田野景色被分开了。
云根yún gēn
yún gēn: "cloud roots". People long ago thought clouds grew out of rocks, so they called stones cloud roots.云根：古人认为云是从山石中生出来的，所以把石头叫作“云根”。
移石动云根yí shí dòng yún gēn
yí shí dòng yún gēn: one reading is that cloud shadows move over the rocks so the rocks seem to move; another is that the poet moves a stone as he walks.移石动云根：一说是云影在石头上移动，好像石头在动；一说是诗人移动了脚下的石头。
暂去zàn qù
zàn qù: to leave for a while.暂去：暂时离开。
幽期yōu qī
yōu qī: a promise to come back to this quiet place.幽期：再来隐居、一起游玩的约定。
不负言bú fù yán
bù fù yán: I will not break my word.不负言：不会违背诺言。
03

How to read it读音

还huán
Huán lái means to come back, so the character is read huán.“还来”是回来、再来的意思，读 huán。
04

Background创作背景

In this poem Jia Dao goes to visit his friend Li Ning, but finds him out, so he writes the poem at his door. We no longer know who Li Ning was; he was probably a hermit. There is a famous story about the line "a monk knocks at the door under the moon". Riding his donkey, Jia Dao kept trying to choose between "push" and "knock", and without noticing he bumped into the procession of the official Han Yu. Han Yu thought for a moment and said "knock" was better. That is where the Chinese word tuī qiāo, "push and knock", meaning to weigh one's words carefully, comes from. But the story was written down later and may not be wholly true.

这首诗写贾岛去拜访朋友李凝，李凝不在家，他就在门上题了这首诗。李凝是谁，生平已经不清楚，大概是一位隐士。关于“僧敲月下门”有一个有名的传说：贾岛骑着驴，一直在想用“推”字还是“敲”字，不知不觉撞上了韩愈的车队。韩愈想了想说，用“敲”字好。“推敲”这个词就是这样来的。不过这个故事是后人记下来的，未必完全是真的。

05

The poet作者

Jia Dao (779–843) was a Tang poet. As a young man he became a Buddhist monk, then later returned to ordinary life to sit the imperial exams, but he never did well. He worked very hard on his poems, weighing every word. He once said it took him three years to get two lines right. People paired him with the poet Meng Jiao, calling Meng's poems "cold" and Jia's "lean".

贾岛（779—843），字浪仙，唐代诗人。他年轻时出家当和尚，法号无本，后来还俗参加科举，但一直不太得志。他写诗非常认真，一个字一个字地反复琢磨，自己说“两句三年得，一吟双泪流”。人们把他和孟郊并称“郊寒岛瘦”。

06

The mood意境

The most famous lines are the third and fourth. The night is still. The birds are asleep. Under the moon, a monk knocks softly at a door. The word "knock" adds a small sound, a few taps, and that makes the night feel even quieter. The word "push" would not do this. Lines five and six show the way back. After the bridge, the fields change color. Clouds drift, and the rocks seem to move with them. The last two lines say: I will come again, and I will not forget my promise. The whole poem is quiet and calm, and shows how lovely a hermit's life can be.

这首诗最出名的是“鸟宿池边树，僧敲月下门”两句。夜很静，鸟都睡了，月光下，一个和尚轻轻敲门。用“敲”字，就有了一点声音，“笃笃”几下，反而让夜显得更安静了。如果用“推”，就没有这个效果。五、六句写回去路上的景色：过了桥，田野的颜色不一样了；云在飘，山石好像也在动。最后两句说：我会再来，约定不会忘。整首诗清幽安静，写出了隐居生活的美好。
