周密Zhou Mi · Song宋

Gao Yang Tai: Seeing Off Chen Junheng, Summoned North

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 照野旌旗Banners light up the open fields,旗子多得照亮了原野，
  2. 朝天车马carriages and horses head for the court.车马成队，去京城朝见皇帝。
  3. 平沙万里天低Flat sand runs ten thousand li, and the sky hangs low.万里平沙，天好像压得很低。
  4. 宝带金章You wear a jeweled belt and a gold seal,你系着宝带，挂着金印，
  5. 尊前茸帽风欹and at the feast the wind tilts your fur hat.酒席前，毛皮帽子被风吹歪。
  6. 秦关汴水经行地Passes of the old Qin lands, the Bian River: you will travel past them all.秦地的关口，汴河的流水，都是你要经过的地方，
  7. 想登临When you climb high and look far,我想你登高远望的时候，
  8. 都付新诗you will put it all into new poems.一定会把感慨都写进新诗。
  9. 纵英游You will go out freely with gifted friends,你会尽情地和英才们游玩，
  10. 叠鼓清笳with drum after drum and the clear hujia pipe,听着一阵阵鼓声和清亮的胡笳，
  11. 骏马名姬fine horses and famous singers at your side.身边是骏马和有名的歌女。
  12. 酒酣应对燕山雪When the wine runs high, you will be facing the snow of the Yan Mountains,酒喝到痛快的时候，你该正对着燕山的大雪，
  13. 正冰河月冻the rivers frozen, even the moonlight seeming frozen,那时河水结冰，月光也像冻住了，
  14. 晓陇云飞and clouds flying over the hills at dawn.清晨山冈上云在飞。
  15. 投老残年I am old now, with few years left.我已经老了，只剩下不多的岁月，
  16. 江南谁念方回Here in the south, who will think of me, an old poet like He Zhu?留在江南，还有谁会想起我这个“贺方回”？
  17. 东风渐绿西湖柳The east wind slowly turns the West Lake willows green.东风渐渐吹绿了西湖边的柳树，
  18. 雁已还The wild geese have already come back,大雁已经飞回来了，
  19. 人未南归but you have not yet come home to the south.你却还没有回到南方。
  20. 最关情What touches my heart most:最让我放不下的是：
  21. 折尽梅花even if I pick every plum blossom,就算折光了梅花，
  22. 难寄相思I can hardly send my longing to you.也难把我的思念寄给你。
02

Words字词注释

照野zhào yě
zhào yě: lighting up the fields. The flags are so many and so bright.照野：照亮了原野。形容旗子又多又鲜艳。
旌旗jīng qí
jīng qí: flags and banners.旌旗：各种旗子。
朝天cháo tiān
cháo tiān: going to the capital to see the ruler.朝天：到京城去朝见皇帝。
平沙píng shā
píng shā: flat sandy land, the wide plains of the north.平沙：平坦的沙地，指北方的大平原。
宝带金章bǎo dài jīn zhāng
bǎo dài jīn zhāng: a jeweled belt and a gold seal of office. This is how a high official dresses.宝带金章：镶宝石的腰带，金子做的官印。这是做大官的打扮。
尊前zūn qián
zūn qián: in front of the wine cup, that is, at the feast.尊前：酒杯前，指在酒席上。尊，同“樽”，酒杯。
茸帽róng mào
róng mào: a fur hat, worn in the cold north.茸帽：毛皮做的帽子，北方人冬天戴。
秦关汴水qín guān biàn shuǐ
qín guān biàn shuǐ: the mountain passes of the old Qin lands and the Bian River. They are all in the north. Bianjing, by the Bian River, was once the capital of the Northern Song.秦关汴水：秦地的关口，汴河的流水。这些都在北方，汴水边的汴京原来是北宋的都城。
经行地jīng xíng dì
jīng xíng dì: the places you will pass on the way.经行地：路上会经过的地方。
登临dēng lín
dēng lín: climbing high to look far.登临：登高望远。
都付新诗dōu fù xīn shī
dōu fù xīn shī: put it all into new poems.都付新诗：都写进新的诗里。
纵英游zòng yīng yóu
zòng yīng yóu: enjoying outings freely with fine, gifted friends. Zòng means letting go, as much as you like.纵英游：尽情地和英才们一起游玩。纵，放开、尽情。
叠鼓dié gǔ
dié gǔ: drums beaten again and again.叠鼓：一阵接一阵地打鼓。
清笳qīng jiā
qīng jiā: the clear sound of the hujia, a reed pipe played in the north.清笳：清亮的胡笳声。胡笳是北方的一种吹奏乐器。
名姬míng jī
míng jī: famous singing girls.名姬：有名的歌女。
酒酣jiǔ hān
jiǔ hān: in the full joy of drinking.酒酣：酒喝得正痛快。
燕山yān shān
yān shān: the Yan Mountains, around today's Beijing. The Yuan capital Dadu was there.燕山：山名，在今天北京一带。元朝的都城大都就在这里。
晓陇xiǎo lǒng
xiǎo lǒng: hills at dawn. Lǒng means a ridge or high ground.晓陇：清晨的山冈。陇，山冈、高地。
投老tóu lǎo
tóu lǎo: having reached old age.投老：到了老年。
残年cán nián
cán nián: the few years left.残年：剩下不多的岁月。
方回fāng huí
fāng huí: He Zhu, a Northern Song poet, whose courtesy name was Fanghui. He wrote famous lines about sorrow in the south. Here Zhou Mi uses him to mean himself.方回：北宋词人贺铸，字方回，他写过江南愁思的名句。这里周密借他来说自己。
绿lǜ
lǜ: to turn something green.绿：使……变绿。
南归nán guī
nán guī: to come back south.南归：回到南方来。
关情guān qíng
guān qíng: touching the heart, the thing one cannot let go.关情：牵动感情，最让人放不下。
折尽梅花zhé jìn méi huā
zhé jìn méi huā: in an old story a man broke off a plum branch and sent it to a friend far away. Here all the plum blossoms are picked, yet the longing still cannot be sent.折尽梅花：古人有折梅花寄给远方朋友的故事。这里说梅花折完了，相思还是寄不出去。
03

How to read it读音

朝cháo
It means going to court to see the ruler, so the character is read cháo.朝天，去朝廷拜见皇帝，“朝”读 cháo。
欹qī
It means tilted to one side, read qī.欹，歪斜，读 qī。
应yīng
Here it means must be, or surely will, so it is read yīng.应，应当、想必，读 yīng。
燕yān
Yan Mountains is a place name, so the character is read yān, not yàn as in swallow.燕山，山名，“燕”读 yān，不读燕子的 yàn。
还huán
It means to come back, so it is read huán.还，回来，读 huán。
04

Background创作背景

Chen Junheng was Chen Yunping, Zhou Mi's friend and also a ci poet. After the Southern Song fell, the Yuan rulers summoned well-known scholars from the south to their capital Dadu, today's Beijing. Chen was summoned too, and Zhou Mi wrote this to see him off. The first half imagines the grand, busy journey north. The second half tells of Zhou Mi alone in the south, missing his friend.

陈君衡就是陈允平，是周密的朋友，也是词人。南宋灭亡以后，元朝征召江南有名的读书人到北方的大都（今北京）去。陈允平也被召去，周密写了这首词给他送行。上半首想象朋友一路北上的热闹和风光，下半首写自己留在江南的孤单和思念。

05

The poet作者

Zhou Mi, also called Caochuang, was a ci poet at the end of the Southern Song. With Wu Wenying he was known as the Two Windows. He lived around Hangzhou. After the Song fell he refused to serve the Yuan, and wrote many books and lyrics remembering the lost country. The sadness in this lyric is tied to that loss too.

周密，号草窗，南宋末年的词人，和吴文英并称“二窗”。他住在杭州一带，宋朝灭亡后不肯去元朝做官，写了很多回忆故国的书和词。这首词里的伤感，也和亡国之痛连在一起。

06

The mood意境

The first half is full of noise and color: banners light the fields, carriages roll on, and the friend in official dress drinks, writes poems and hears drums in the north wind. But the old Qin passes and the Bian River were once Song land, and now they belong to others. Under the noise lies a pain that cannot be spoken. The second half turns cold. Heavy snow on the Yan Mountains, frozen rivers and dawn clouds are the north the friend must face. Green willows at West Lake and returning geese are the south where Zhou Mi stays. The geese come back, but the friend does not. At the end, even every plum blossom picked cannot carry his longing. Friendship and grief for the lost country both run deep and long.

上半首写得很热闹：旗子照亮原野，车马成队，朋友穿着官服，在北风里喝酒、写诗、听鼓乐。可是“秦关汴水”是原来宋朝的土地，现在却成了别人的，热闹里藏着说不出的苦。下半首一下子冷下来：燕山的大雪、冰冻的河、清晨的云，是朋友要面对的北方；西湖的绿柳、回来的大雁，是周密留守的江南。雁回来了，人还没回来。最后折尽梅花也寄不出相思，把朋友情和故国情都写得又深又长。
