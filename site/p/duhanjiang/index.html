渡汉江

宋之问Song Zhiwen · Tang唐

Crossing the Han River

 

lǐng岭wài外yīn音shū书duàn断

jīng经dōng冬fù复lì历chūn春

jìn近xiāng乡qíng情gèng更qiè怯

bù不gǎn敢wèn问lái来rén人

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 岭外音书断Exiled beyond the southern ranges, I had no news from home,被贬到五岭以南，和家里断了音信，
  2. 经冬复历春through a whole winter and then a spring.熬过了冬天，又熬过了春天。
  3. 近乡情更怯Now, nearing home, my heart grows more afraid;离家乡越来越近，心里反而越来越害怕，
  4. 不敢问来人I dare not ask the people coming from there.不敢向从家乡来的人打听家里的消息。
02

Words字词注释

岭外lǐng wài
lǐng wài: beyond the Five Ranges, the land to the south, today's Guangdong and Guangxi. In the Tang dynasty it was remote, and officials in disgrace were often sent there.岭外：五岭以南，就是今天的广东、广西一带。唐代那里很偏远，犯了错的官员常被贬到那里。
音书yīn shū
yīn shū: letters, news.音书：书信，消息。
断duàn
duàn: cut off.断：断绝，没有了。
经冬复历春jīng dōng fù lì chūn
jīng dōng fù lì chūn: through winter and then through spring, meaning a long time has passed.经冬复历春：过了冬天，又过了春天。形容时间很长。
近乡jìn xiāng
jìn xiāng: getting close to home.近乡：离家乡越来越近。
更怯gèng qiè
gèng qiè: even more timid and afraid.更怯：更加胆怯、害怕。
来人lái rén
lái rén: people coming from the direction of home.来人：从家乡那边来的人。
03

Background创作背景

This poem is usually thought to have been written when Song Zhiwen fled back north from the far south and crossed the Han River. He had been close to Zhang Yizhi, a favorite of Empress Wu Zetian, so when Emperor Zhongzong came to the throne he was exiled to Longzhou in the far south. Before long he slipped away and headed home. Crossing the Han River, he was not far from home, but he had heard nothing from his family for a long time. Some sources say the poet was Li Pin, but most standard editions name Song Zhiwen.

一般认为，这首诗是宋之问从岭南逃回北方、渡过汉江时写的。他因为依附武则天的宠臣张易之，在唐中宗即位后被贬到岭南的泷州。不久他偷偷逃回，路过汉江，离家乡已经不远了。那时他和家里很久没有联系，不知道家人怎么样。这首诗一作李频所作，但通行本多题宋之问。

04

The poet作者

Song Zhiwen (about 656–712) was a Tang poet. Together with Shen Quanqi, he helped fix the rules of regulated verse, and the two are known together as Shen and Song. His poems were fine, but people often criticized his character, because he sided with powerful men. He was later exiled and finally ordered to take his own life. Yet this little poem captures a feeling everyone knows.

宋之问（约656—712），唐代诗人。他和沈佺期一起，把律诗的格律定得更完整，人们并称“沈宋”。他的诗写得好，但为人常被批评，因为他依附权贵，后来被贬，最后被赐死。这首小诗却写出了人人都有的真实感受。

05

The mood意境

Normally, the closer you get to home, the happier you should feel, and the more eager for news. But the poet says the opposite: the closer he gets, the more afraid he is, so afraid he dares not even ask. Why? Because he has had no word from home for so long. Is his family well? Has something happened? If he does not ask, he can still hope. If he asks, what if the news is bad? Wanting to know and fearing to know at the same time is a feeling many travelers have had. The poet puts this tangled feeling into just ten characters, which is why these two lines are still quoted today.

按常理，离家越近，应该越高兴，越想快点知道家里的消息。可诗人偏偏说：越近越害怕，连问都不敢问。为什么？因为他很久没有家里的音信了。家人还好吗？会不会出了什么事？不问，还能抱着希望；一问，万一是坏消息呢？这种又想知道、又怕知道的心情，很多出远门的人都有过。诗人只用了十个字，就把这种复杂的心情写得清清楚楚，所以这两句流传至今。
