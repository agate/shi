01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 潇洒江梅The river plum stands free and graceful,江边的梅花自在大方，
  2. 向竹梢疏处where the bamboo tips grow thin,在竹梢稀疏的地方，
  3. 横两三枝reaching out two or three branches.横斜地伸出两三枝。
  4. 东君也不爱惜Even the god of spring does not cherish it,掌管春天的神也不爱惜它，
  5. 雪压霜欺letting snow weigh it down and frost bully it.任凭大雪压它，寒霜欺它。
  6. 无情燕子The heartless swallows,无情的燕子，
  7. 怕春寒afraid of the spring chill,怕春天寒冷，
  8. 轻失花期carelessly miss its time of bloom.轻易错过了梅花开放的时候。
  9. 却是有But there are倒是有
  10. 年年塞雁the frontier geese, year after year,每年从北方来的大雁，
  11. 归来曾见开时who see it open as they fly home.飞回去时见过它开花的样子。
  12. 清浅小溪如练The clear, shallow stream is like a strip of white silk.清清浅浅的小溪像一条白绸，
  13. 问玉堂何似Ask: how could a hall of jade请问那华丽的玉堂，
  14. 茅舍疏篱compare with this thatched hut and its thin fence?哪里比得上这茅屋和稀疏的篱笆？
  15. 伤心故人去后Sadly, since my old friend went away,让人伤心的是，老朋友走了以后，
  16. 冷落新诗new poems for it have been forgotten.再也没有人为它写新诗了。
  17. 微云淡月Faint clouds, a pale moon,淡淡的云，淡淡的月，
  18. 对江天facing the sky over the river:对着江上的天空，
  19. 分付他谁to whom can I give this beauty?这样的美景又能交给谁呢？
  20. 空自忆I can only remember it alone, for nothing.只能白白地自己回想，
  21. 清香未减Its clear fragrance has not faded at all;梅花的清香一点也没有减少，
  22. 风流不在人知its grace never needed anyone to know of it.它的高雅本来就不在乎有没有人知道。
02

Words字词注释

潇洒xiāo sǎ
xiāo sǎ: free and graceful, at ease.潇洒：自然大方、不拘束的样子。
江梅jiāng méi
jiāng méi: wild plum trees growing by the river.江梅：长在江边、野外的梅花。
竹梢疏处zhú shāo shū chù
zhú shāo shū chù: where the bamboo tips are sparse.竹梢疏处：竹子顶上稀疏的地方。
横两三枝héng liǎng sān zhī
héng liǎng sān zhī: two or three branches reaching out sideways.横两三枝：横斜地伸出两三枝。
东君dōng jūn
dōng jūn: the Lord of the East, the god in charge of spring.东君：传说中掌管春天的神。
雪压霜欺xuě yā shuāng qī
xuě yā shuāng qī: weighed down by snow and bullied by frost.雪压霜欺：被雪压着，被霜欺负。
轻失花期qīng shī huā qī
qīng shī huā qī: carelessly missing the time the plum is in bloom. Swallows only come in spring, after the plum blossoms are gone.轻失花期：轻易错过了梅花开放的日子。燕子春天才来，那时梅花已经谢了。
却是有què shì yǒu
què shì yǒu: but there are, on the other hand.却是有：倒是有。
塞雁sài yàn
sài yàn: wild geese from the northern frontier, who spend the winter in the south. They fly back north in early spring, just as the plum blooms.塞雁：从北方边塞飞来过冬的大雁。它们早春北归，正赶上梅花开放。
曾见开时céng jiàn kāi shí
céng jiàn kāi shí: have seen the plum in bloom.曾见开时：见过梅花开放的样子。
如练rú liàn
rú liàn: like a strip of white silk.如练：像一条白绸子。练，白色的丝绸。
玉堂yù táng
yù táng: a grand hall of jade, also a high government office.玉堂：华丽的殿堂，也指朝廷里的官署。
何似hé sì
hé sì: how could it compare with.何似：哪里比得上。
茅舍疏篱máo shè shū lí
máo shè shū lí: a thatched hut and a thin fence, the country home where the plum grows.茅舍疏篱：茅草房和稀疏的篱笆，指梅花生长的乡野人家。
故人gù rén
gù rén: an old friend. One view is that it means Lin Bu, a Northern Song poet who loved plum blossoms.故人：老朋友。一说指北宋爱梅的诗人林逋。
冷落新诗lěng luò xīn shī
lěng luò xīn shī: no one writes new poems for the plum any more.冷落新诗：再也没有人为梅花写新诗了。
分付他谁fēn fù tā shuí
fēn fù tā shuí: to whom can it be given? Fēn fù means to hand over, to entrust.分付他谁：交给谁呢？分付，交付、托付。
空自忆kōng zì yì
kōng zì yì: remembering alone, for nothing.空自忆：白白地自己回想。
风流fēng liú
fēng liú: here, the plum's elegant grace.风流：这里指梅花高雅的风度。
不在人知bú zài rén zhī
bù zài rén zhī: does not depend on whether anyone knows.不在人知：不在乎别人知道不知道。
03

How to read it读音

子zǐ
In yàn zǐ (swallow), the last character is unstressed in everyday speech, but in verse it is usually read zǐ.燕子：口语里“子”读轻声 zi，读诗词时一般读 zǐ。
塞sài
In sài yàn (frontier geese), the character means the northern frontier and is read sài, not sāi or sè.塞雁：从北方边塞飞来的大雁。“塞”指边塞，读 sài。
04

Background创作背景

This is a lyric in praise of plum blossoms, written about the plum alone. No reliable record tells when or why it was written. The plum here grows by the river, beside bamboo and a thatched hut's fence. The god of spring does not cherish it, and the swallows miss it, yet it still blooms freely and smells sweet from afar. Sources disagree about the author. It is usually given to Li Bing; one view says it was Chao Chongzhi.

这是一首咏梅词，就是专门写梅花的词。写作的时间和缘由都没有留下可靠的记载。词里的梅花长在江边竹林旁、茅屋篱笆边，不被春神爱惜，也被燕子错过，却照样开得潇洒、香得清远。这首词的作者有不同说法，一般认为是李邴，一说是晁冲之。

05

The poet作者

Li Bing (1085–1146), courtesy name Hanlao, was a Song lyric poet. He passed the highest level of the imperial exams in 1106, under the Northern Song, and early in the Southern Song he served as a vice chief minister. Later he lived quietly in Quanzhou for more than ten years. Few of his lyrics survive, and this one about plum blossoms is the best known.

李邴（1085—1146），字汉老，宋代词人。北宋崇宁五年（1106年）考中进士，南宋初年做过参知政事，也就是副宰相，后来在泉州闲居了十多年。他的词留下来的不多，这首写梅花的词最有名。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is about plum blossoms, but it also seems to describe a person of noble character. The plum blooms in the coldest time. The god of spring does not care for it, the swallows find it too cold to visit, and only the geese flying north have seen it flower. It does not grow before a grand hall of jade, but by a little stream and a thatched hut. The old friend who understood it and wrote poems for it has gone, and there is no one to share the moonlit river scene with. But the ending turns: the plum's fragrance has not faded at all, and its beauty never needed anyone to know of it. Here we understand that true goodness does not depend on praise.

这首词写梅花，也像在写一个品格高洁的人。梅花开在最冷的时候，春神不心疼它，燕子也嫌冷不来看，只有北归的大雁见过它开花。它不长在华丽的玉堂前，而是长在小溪边、茅屋旁。懂它、为它写诗的老朋友走了，月下江边的美景也没人可以分享。可是结尾一转：梅花的清香一点没少，它的美本来就不需要别人知道。读到这里，我们会明白：真正的好，不靠别人夸奖。
