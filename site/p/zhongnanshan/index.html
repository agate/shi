zhōng终nán南shān山

wáng王wéi维Wang Wei · Tang唐

The Zhongnan Mountains

 

tài太yǐ乙jìn近tiān天dū都

lián连shān山jiē接hǎi海yú隅

bái白yún云huí回wàng望hé合

qīng青ǎi霭rù入kàn看wú无

fēn分yě野zhōng中fēng峰biàn变

yīn阴qíng晴zhòng众hè壑shū殊

yù欲tóu投rén人chù处sù宿

gé隔shuǐ水wèn问qiáo樵fū夫

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 太乙近天都The tall Zhongnan Mountains nearly touch the city of Heaven.高高的终南山，快要挨到天帝住的地方了；
  2. 连山接海隅Peak after peak, they stretch to the edge of the sea.山连着山，一直延伸到海边。
  3. 白云回望合I look back, and the white clouds have closed together behind me.回头一看，白云已经在身后合成一片；
  4. 青霭入看无The blue mist I saw ahead is gone when I walk into it.青色的雾气远看有，走近了却不见了。
  5. 分野中峰变The central peak divides the land under different stars.中间的主峰一隔，两边就分属不同的星区；
  6. 阴晴众壑殊In the many valleys, some lie in shadow and some in sun.山谷众多，有的阴，有的晴，各不相同。
  7. 欲投人处宿Wanting to find a home where I can spend the night,想找个有人家的地方过夜，
  8. 隔水问樵夫I call across the stream to ask a woodcutter.我隔着溪水，向砍柴的人打听。
02

Words字词注释

太乙tài yǐ
tài yǐ: another name for the Zhongnan Mountains, south of today's Xi'an.太乙：终南山的别名，在今天陕西西安的南面。
天都tiān dū
tiān dū: the city of the Lord of Heaven. The line means the mountain is so high it almost reaches the sky. Some think it means the capital, Chang'an.天都：天帝住的地方。这里说山很高，好像快挨到天了。也有人认为指唐朝的都城长安。
连山lián shān
lián shān: mountain after mountain in a chain.连山：山连着山。
海隅hǎi yú
hǎi yú: the edge of the sea. This is an exaggeration: the range seems to run all the way to the sea.海隅：海边。这是夸张，说山脉很长，一直连到海边。
回望合huí wàng hé
huí wàng hé: looking back, the white clouds have closed together behind me.回望合：回头看，白云在身后合拢成一片。
青霭qīng ǎi
qīng ǎi: blue-gray mist in the mountains.青霭：山中青色的雾气。
入看无rù kàn wú
rù kàn wú: walk into it and look, and the mist is gone.入看无：走进去看，雾气又不见了。
分野fēn yě
fēn yě: the old idea that each region of the sky matches a region of the land below.分野：古人把天上的星区和地上的地区一一对应，叫作分野。
中峰变zhōng fēng biàn
zhōng fēng biàn: on either side of the central peak, the matching star region changes. That is how huge the mountain is.中峰变：以中间的主峰为界，两边的分野都不一样了，说明山非常大。
众壑zhòng hè
zhòng hè: the many valleys. Hè is a gully or valley.众壑：许多山谷。壑，山沟、山谷。
殊shū
shū: different.殊：不同。
投tóu
tóu: to look for a place to stay the night.投：投宿，找地方住下。
樵夫qiáo fū
qiáo fū: a woodcutter.樵夫：上山砍柴的人。
03

How to read it读音

都dū
Tiān dū is the city where the Lord of Heaven lives, so the character is read dū, not dōu.“天都”是天帝住的地方，“都”读 dū。
看kàn
Today it is usually read kàn. By the tone rules of regulated verse this spot needs a level tone, so in old reading it was kān.今天一般读 kàn。按律诗的平仄，这里应是平声，古音读 kān。
处chù
Rén chù means a place where people live, so the character is read chù.“人处”是有人住的地方，“处”读 chù。
04

Background创作背景

The Zhongnan Mountains lie south of the Tang capital, Chang'an. They are tall and run a very long way, and many people lived there as hermits. In his middle years Wang Wei lived for a time in the Zhongnan area and often wandered in the hills. This poem shows what he saw on one of his trips there. It is usually dated to around 740, but the exact time is not certain.

终南山在唐朝都城长安的南边，山势高大，连绵很远，当时有很多人在山里隐居。王维中年以后曾在终南山一带隐居过，常在山中游玩。这首诗写的是他游终南山时看到的景象。一般认为写于开元末或天宝初年，具体时间不能确定。

05

The poet作者

Wang Wei (701–761 by one account) was a great Tang poet and also a painter. He was best at writing about mountains and rivers, and people say his poems hold paintings. This poem is like a large landscape painting, with a far view, a near view and even a small figure in it. It shows a painter's eye.

王维（一说701—761），唐代大诗人，也是画家。他最擅长写山水，人们说他的诗“诗中有画”。这首《终南山》就像一幅大山水画，有远景，有近景，还有人物，很能看出他画家的眼光。

06

The mood意境

In just forty characters this poem shows a whole mountain range. The first two lines look from far away. The mountain is so high it almost touches the sky, and so long it reaches the sea. This is exaggeration, and it makes us feel how grand the mountains are. Lines three and four walk inside the mountains. Look back, and the clouds close up. Walk ahead, and the mist disappears. The scenery keeps changing, full of mystery. Lines five and six look from up high. One main peak splits the land, and among the valleys some are shady and some sunny. In the last two lines a tiny person suddenly appears, calling across the water to a woodcutter to ask where to stay. Next to that small person, the mountain seems even bigger.

这首诗用短短四十个字，写出了一座大山。前两句从远处看：山高得快挨着天，长得一直连到海边，这是夸张，让人感到山的雄伟。三、四句写人在山里走：回头看，白云合拢了；往前走，雾气又消失了，山里的景色变来变去，非常神秘。五、六句又从高处看：一座主峰就把天地分开，山谷里有的阴有的晴。最后两句忽然出现一个小小的人，隔着水问樵夫哪里能住。大山和小人一比，山就显得更大了。
