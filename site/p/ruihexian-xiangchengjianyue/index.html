01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 绀烟迷雁迹Dusky blue mist blurs the paths of the wild geese.青红色的暮烟弥漫，看不见大雁的踪影。
  2. 渐断鼓零钟The drums fall broken, the bells ring here and there,鼓声断断续续，钟声零零落落，
  3. 街喧初息and the noise of the streets has just died down.街上的吵闹声刚刚停下来。
  4. 风檠背寒壁A lamp flickers in the wind with its back to the cold wall.风中摇晃的灯，背对着冰冷的墙。
  5. 放冰蜍飞到The moon lets its icy light fly in,月亮放出清冷的光，飞进屋里，
  6. 丝丝窗隙thread by thread through the cracks of the window.一丝一丝穿过窗缝。
  7. 琼瑰暗泣Quietly I shed tears.我悄悄地流下了眼泪。
  8. 念乡关霜芜似织I think of my home town, where frosty weeds grow thick as cloth.想起家乡，盖着霜的野草密得像织成一片。
  9. 漫将身化鹤归来In vain I have come home, like the man who flew back as a crane,我白白地像丁令威化鹤那样回到家乡，
  10. 忘却旧游端的and I have forgotten what my old haunts were really like.从前游玩的地方，已经记不清是什么样子了。
  11. 欢极Back then, joy was at its height.那时真是快乐到了极点。
  12. 蓬壶蕖浸Lotus flowers floated in fairy-like ponds,仙境般的池塘里泡着荷花，
  13. 花院梨溶pear blossoms in the courtyard seemed to melt in the moonlight,院子里的梨花在月下白得像要化开，
  14. 醉连春夕and we were drunk on one spring night after another.一个个春夜，我们接连喝醉。
  15. 柯云罢弈But like the immortals' game that ended while an axe handle rotted, those days are gone.可就像仙人一盘棋下完，时光早已飞逝。
  16. 樱桃在The cherries are still here,樱桃还在，
  17. 梦难觅but that sweet dream can never be found again.那场好梦却再也找不回来了。
  18. 劝清光So I say to the clear moonlight:我劝劝这清亮的月光：
  19. 乍可幽窗相伴you had better keep me company at my lonely window,你宁可在我冷清的窗前陪着我，
  20. 休照红楼夜笛and do not shine on the red towers where flutes play at night.别去照那红楼上夜里吹笛的人家。
  21. 怕人间换谱伊凉I fear the world below now plays new frontier tunes like Yizhou and Liangzhou,只怕人间已经换上了《伊州》《凉州》那样的新曲子，
  22. 素娥未识tunes that even Chang'e in the moon would not know.月亮里的嫦娥也听不懂了。
02

Words字词注释

绀烟gàn yān
gàn yān: the dusky mist at nightfall, dark blue with a touch of red. Gàn is that colour.绀烟：天黑时青里带红的暮色烟雾。绀，深青透红的颜色。
迷雁迹mí yàn jì
mí yàn jì: the mist is so thick that the flying geese can no longer be seen.迷雁迹：雾气浓得看不见大雁飞过的影子。
断鼓零钟duàn gǔ líng zhōng
duàn gǔ líng zhōng: broken drumbeats and scattered bell strokes, the sounds that told the time in an old town.断鼓零钟：断断续续的鼓声，零零落落的钟声，是古代城里报时的声音。
街喧jiē xuān
jiē xuān: the noise of the street.街喧：街上的吵闹声。
风檠fēng qíng
fēng qíng: a lamp flickering in the wind. Qíng is a lamp stand, here the lamp.风檠：在风里摇晃的灯。檠，灯架，这里指灯。
背寒壁bèi hán bì
bèi hán bì: the lamp stands with its back to the cold wall, so the wall side is dim.背寒壁：灯放在冰冷的墙边，光照向别处，墙这边暗暗的。
冰蜍bīng chú
bīng chú: the moon. Legend says a toad lives in the moon, and moonlight is cold and white like ice.冰蜍：月亮。传说月亮里有一只蟾蜍（癞蛤蟆），月光又冷又白，所以叫冰蜍。
丝丝窗隙sī sī chuāng xì
sī sī chuāng xì: moonlight slips in through the cracks of the window in thin threads.丝丝窗隙：月光像细丝一样，从窗缝里一条条照进来。
琼瑰qióng guī
qióng guī: beads like fine jade. In an old book a man dreamed that his tears turned into such beads, so here it means tears.琼瑰：美玉一样的珠子。古书里有人梦见自己哭出的眼泪变成了琼瑰，这里指眼泪。
乡关xiāng guān
xiāng guān: one's home town.乡关：家乡。
霜芜似织shuāng wú sì zhī
shuāng wú sì zhī: frosty weeds grow so thick they look woven together. Wú means weeds.霜芜似织：盖着霜的野草又密又乱，像织在一起。芜，杂草。
漫màn
màn: in vain.漫：徒然，白白地。
化鹤归来huà hè guī lái
huà hè guī lái: legend says a man named Ding Lingwei became an immortal, turned into a crane and flew home. The town walls were the same, but all the people had changed. Here the poet has come home to find everything changed.化鹤归来：传说汉代丁令威学道成仙，变成一只鹤飞回家乡，只见城墙还在，人却全换了。这里说自己回到家乡，物是人非。
旧游jiù yóu
jiù yóu: the places where one used to go about and have fun.旧游：从前游玩过的地方。
端的duān dì
duān dì: the exact details, what things were really like.端的：底细，具体是什么样子。
欢极huān jí
huān jí: joy at its height, speaking of the old days.欢极：快乐到了极点，说的是从前的日子。
蓬壶péng hú
péng hú: a magic island in the sea, said to be shaped like a pot. Here it stands for the fairy-like pavilions and ponds of the old home gardens.蓬壶：传说中海上的仙山，形状像壶。这里借指家乡园林里像仙境一样的楼台池塘。
蕖浸qú jìn
qú jìn: lotus flowers resting in the water. Qú means lotus.蕖浸：荷花泡在水里。蕖，荷花。
梨溶lí róng
lí róng: pear blossoms so white in the moonlight they seem to melt.梨溶：梨花在月光下白得像要化开一样。
醉连春夕zuì lián chūn xī
zuì lián chūn xī: drunk on one spring evening after another.醉连春夕：一个个春天的夜晚，接连喝得醉醺醺。
柯云罢弈kē yún bà yì
kē yún bà yì: legend says a woodcutter named Wang Zhi watched immortals play a board game in the hills. When the game ended, the wooden handle of his axe had rotted away, and back home everyone he knew was gone. Here it means the good times vanished in a flash.柯云罢弈：传说晋代王质上山砍柴，看仙人下棋，一盘棋下完，斧头的木柄（柯）已经烂了，回到村里，同时代的人都不在了。这里说美好时光一下子就过去了。
樱桃在yīng táo zài
yīng táo zài: a Tang story tells of a scholar who fell asleep in a temple. He dreamed of a maid carrying cherries, then of marrying and becoming a high official, and woke to find it all gone. Here the past is like that dream.樱桃在：唐代有个故事，一个书生在寺里睡着，梦见一个穿青衣、端着樱桃的女孩，又梦见自己娶妻做官，醒来一场空。这里说往日像一场梦。
梦难觅mèng nán mì
mèng nán mì: such a dream can never be found again. Mì means to look for.梦难觅：那样的梦再也找不回来了。觅，找。
清光qīng guāng
qīng guāng: the clear moonlight.清光：清亮的月光。
乍可zhà kě
zhà kě: would rather.乍可：宁可，还不如。
幽窗yōu chuāng
yōu chuāng: a dim, lonely window, the poet's own room.幽窗：冷清昏暗的窗户，指诗人自己的屋子。
红楼夜笛hóng lóu yè dí
hóng lóu yè dí: flutes played at night in the red towers of rich families.红楼夜笛：富贵人家的楼上，夜里吹笛作乐。
换谱伊凉huàn pǔ yī liáng
huàn pǔ yī liáng: the music has changed to frontier tunes such as Yizhou and Liangzhou, both named after places in the far northwest. It hints that the country has new rulers and even the songs are different.换谱伊凉：换成了《伊州》《凉州》这样的边地曲子。伊州、凉州都在西北边地。这里暗说天下换了主人，连音乐都变了。
素娥sù é
sù é: Chang'e, the lady in the moon, standing here for the moon.素娥：月里的嫦娥，这里代指月亮。
03

How to read it读音

的dì
In duān dì, the second character is read dì and the word means "the exact details". It rhymes with jì, xī, bì and jí.“端的”的“的”读 dì，意思是“底细、究竟”。它和“迹、息、壁、极”等字押韵。
04

Background创作背景

Jiang Jie lived at the very end of the Southern Song. In 1279 the Song fell and the Yuan took over the land. He refused to serve the new rulers and wandered from place to place. This lyric tells how he felt when he came back to his home town and saw the moon. The town was still there, but the people and the times had changed, and even the music in the streets was new. We do not know the exact year it was written.

蒋捷是南宋末年的人。1279年南宋灭亡，元朝统治了天下，他不肯出来做官，到处漂泊。这首词写他回到家乡，在城里看见月亮时的心情。家乡还在，可是人和事都变了，连街上的音乐都换成了新的曲调。具体写在哪一年，现在说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Jiang Jie, also called Zhushan, came from Yangxian, today's Yixing in Jiangsu. His birth and death years are not known. He passed the highest exam near the end of the Southern Song, and after the Song fell he lived in hiding and refused office. People called him Master Zhushan. He is one of the Four Great Lyric Poets of the late Song. His lyrics often carry the sorrow of a lost country.

蒋捷，字胜欲，号竹山，阳羡（今江苏宜兴）人，生卒年不详。他在南宋末年考中进士，宋亡后隐居不出，人们称他“竹山先生”。他和周密、王沂孙、张炎被并称为“宋末四大家”。他的词常写亡国之后的悲凉，最有名的一句是“流光容易把人抛，红了樱桃，绿了芭蕉”。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza shows the present. Night falls, the mist hides the geese, the drums and bells fade, and the streets go quiet. In the room a lamp shakes in the wind, and moonlight comes in like thin threads through the cracks of the window. The poet weeps. He thinks of his home overgrown with frosty weeds, and feels like the man who came home as a crane and found nothing he knew. The second stanza goes back to the past: lotus, pear blossoms, spring nights and wine, joy at its height. But in a blink it is gone, like the immortals' game, like the scholar's dream. At the end he speaks to the moon. Stay with me, he says, and do not shine on the people still playing flutes. The songs of the world have changed, and you would not know them. The words are soft, but they hide the great grief of a fallen country.

上阕写眼前。天黑了，暮烟遮住了大雁，鼓声钟声渐渐停了，街上也安静下来。屋里一盏灯在风里摇，月光像细丝一样从窗缝照进来。诗人悄悄流泪，想到家乡长满了霜草，自己像化鹤归来的丁令威，从前的地方都认不出了。下阕先回到过去：荷花、梨花、春夜、美酒，快乐到了极点。可是一转眼，就像仙人下完一盘棋，像书生醒了一场梦，全都没了。最后诗人对月亮说话：你就陪着我吧，别去照那些还在吹笛作乐的人家，人间的曲子都变了，你也听不懂了。这句话很轻，却藏着国家灭亡的大悲伤。
