Tang poem · seven-character old-style poem唐诗 · 七言古诗

yǐn饮zhōng中bā八xiān仙gē歌

dù杜fǔ甫Du Fu · Tang唐

Song of the Eight Immortals of the Wine Cup

 

zhī知zhāng章qí骑mǎ马sì似chéng乘chuán船

yǎn眼huā花luò落jǐng井shuǐ水dǐ底mián眠

rǔ汝yáng阳sān三dǒu斗shǐ始cháo朝tiān天

dào道féng逢qū曲chē车kǒu口liú流xián涎

hèn恨bù不yí移fēng封xiàng向jiǔ酒quán泉

zuǒ左xiàng相rì日xìng兴fèi费wàn万qián钱

yǐn饮rú如cháng长jīng鲸xī吸bǎi百chuān川

xián衔bēi杯lè乐shèng圣chēng称bì避xián贤

zōng宗zhī之xiāo潇sǎ洒měi美shào少nián年

jǔ举shāng觞bái白yǎn眼wàng望qīng青tiān天

jiǎo皎rú如yù玉shù树lín临fēng风qián前

sū苏jìn晋cháng长zhāi斋xiù绣fó佛qián前

zuì醉zhōng中wǎng往wǎng往ài爱táo逃chán禅

lǐ李bái白yì一dǒu斗shī诗bǎi百piān篇

cháng长ān安shì市shàng上jiǔ酒jiā家mián眠

tiān天zǐ子hū呼lái来bú不shàng上chuán船

zì自chēng称chén臣shì是jiǔ酒zhōng中xiān仙

zhāng张xù旭sān三bēi杯cǎo草shèng圣chuán传

tuō脱mào帽lù露dǐng顶wáng王gōng公qián前

huī挥háo毫luò落zhǐ纸rú如yún云yān烟

jiāo焦suì遂wǔ五dǒu斗fāng方zhuó卓rán然

gāo高tán谈xióng雄biàn辩jīng惊sì四yán筵

We follow the common editions. The corpus differs in two places: it has "praised as worthy by the world" where common editions have bì xián, "making way for the worthy", and it writes the word for debate with a look-alike character. Bì xián comes from Li Shizhi's own line about stepping down as chief minister "to make way for the worthy". Also, qū in qū chē is the simplified form of the word for the yeast used to make wine.

这里按通行本。语料有两处不同：“称避贤”作“称世贤”，“雄辩”作“雄辨”。“避贤”出自李适之自己的诗句“避贤初罢相”，通行本多作“避贤”。另外，“曲车”的“曲”是“麴”（酒曲）的简化字。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 知章骑马似乘船He Zhizhang, drunk on horseback, sways as if he were on a boat;贺知章喝醉了骑马，摇摇晃晃像坐船一样，
  2. 眼花落井水底眠blurry-eyed, he falls into a well and sleeps at the bottom.醉眼昏花掉进井里，竟在井底睡着了。
  3. 汝阳三斗始朝天The Prince of Ruyang drinks three measures before going to court,汝阳王喝了三斗酒才去朝见皇帝，
  4. 道逢曲车口流涎meets a cart of wine yeast on the road, and drools,路上碰到装酒曲的车，口水都流出来了，
  5. 恨不移封向酒泉wishing his lands could be moved to Wine Spring.恨不得把封地换到酒泉去。
  6. 左相日兴费万钱Chief Minister Li spends ten thousand coins a day on his love of wine;左丞相李适之每天的酒兴，要花掉上万的钱，
  7. 饮如长鲸吸百川he drinks like a great whale swallowing a hundred rivers,喝起酒来像巨鲸吞吸百条河水，
  8. 衔杯乐圣称避贤cup in hand, he loves clear wine and says he has stepped aside for the worthy.举着酒杯爱喝清酒，说自己是给贤人让位。
  9. 宗之潇洒美少年Cui Zongzhi is a handsome, carefree young man;崔宗之是个潇洒的美少年，
  10. 举觞白眼望青天raising his cup, he rolls his eyes at the blue sky,举起酒杯，翻着白眼望着青天，
  11. 皎如玉树临风前bright and fine as a jade tree in the wind.洁白明亮，像玉树站在风里。
  12. 苏晋长斋绣佛前Su Jin keeps a long meatless fast before an embroidered Buddha,苏晋长年吃素，供奉着绣成的佛像，
  13. 醉中往往爱逃禅but when drunk, he often slips free of the Buddhist rules.可喝醉了，常常把佛门戒律抛在脑后。
  14. 李白一斗诗百篇Li Bai drinks one measure and writes a hundred poems;李白喝一斗酒，就能写诗一百篇，
  15. 长安市上酒家眠he sleeps in the wine shops of Chang'an's market.常常醉睡在长安街上的酒馆里。
  16. 天子呼来不上船When the emperor calls, he cannot get onto the boat,皇帝召他，他醉得上不了船，
  17. 自称臣是酒中仙and calls himself: "Your servant is an immortal of the wine cup."还自称：“我是酒中的神仙。”
  18. 张旭三杯草圣传Zhang Xu drinks three cups, and his fame as the sage of cursive spreads;张旭喝了三杯，草书圣手的名声就传开了，
  19. 脱帽露顶王公前before princes and lords he takes off his hat and bares his head,他在王公贵族面前摘下帽子，露出头顶，
  20. 挥毫落纸如云烟and his brush on paper flows like clouds and mist.挥笔落在纸上，字像云烟一样飞动。
  21. 焦遂五斗方卓然Jiao Sui drinks five measures before he truly comes alive;焦遂喝了五斗酒，才精神焕发，
  22. 高谈雄辩惊四筵his bold talk and brilliant arguments amaze the whole feast.高谈阔论，雄辩滔滔，满座的客人都惊呆了。
02

Words字词注释

知章zhī zhāng
Zhizhang: He Zhizhang, a Tang poet who loved wine and was free-spirited. He came from Zhejiang, where people were used to boats.知章：贺知章，唐代诗人，喜欢喝酒，为人豪放。他是浙江人，那里的人习惯坐船。
似乘船sì chéng chuán
sì chéng chuán: drunk on horseback, swaying as if on a boat.似乘船：喝醉了骑在马上摇摇晃晃，像坐船一样。
眼花yǎn huā
yǎn huā: blurry-eyed from drink.眼花：喝醉了眼睛看不清。
水底眠shuǐ dǐ mián
shuǐ dǐ mián: falling into a well and simply sleeping at the bottom. It is a playful exaggeration.水底眠：掉进井里，竟然就在井底睡着了。这是夸张的说法。
汝阳rǔ yáng
Ruyang: Li Jin, the Prince of Ruyang, a nephew of Emperor Xuanzong.汝阳：汝阳王李琎，唐玄宗的侄子。
三斗sān dǒu
sān dǒu: three measures of wine. A dǒu is a wine vessel.三斗：喝了三斗酒。斗，盛酒的器具。
朝天cháo tiān
cháo tiān: to go to court to see the emperor.朝天：去朝见皇帝。
曲车qū chē
qū chē: a cart of wine yeast, the stuff used to make wine, which smells of wine.曲车：装着酒曲的车。酒曲是用来酿酒的东西，闻起来有酒香。
流涎liú xián
liú xián: to drool.流涎：流口水。
移封yí fēng
yí fēng: to move one's fief. Princes long ago were given land of their own to rule.移封：把封地换到别处。古代王爷有自己的封地。
酒泉jiǔ quán
Jiuquan: "Wine Spring", a place in today's Gansu. Legend says a spring there tasted like wine.酒泉：酒泉郡，在今甘肃。传说那里城下有泉，泉水像酒一样。
左相zuǒ xiàng
zuǒ xiàng: Li Shizhi, Chief Minister of the Left, a high official at court.左相：左丞相李适之，是朝中的大官。
日兴rì xìng
rì xìng: his daily urge to drink.日兴：每天的酒兴。
长鲸cháng jīng
cháng jīng: a huge whale.长鲸：巨大的鲸鱼。
百川bǎi chuān
bǎi chuān: a hundred rivers.百川：许多条河流。
衔杯xián bēi
xián bēi: holding a cup to the lips, drinking.衔杯：嘴里含着酒杯，就是喝酒。
乐圣lè shèng
lè shèng: enjoying clear wine. People long ago jokingly called clear wine a sage and cloudy wine a worthy man.乐圣：喜欢喝清酒。古人把清酒叫“圣人”，浊酒叫“贤人”。
称避贤chēng bì xián
chēng bì xián: saying he stepped aside for worthier men. Li Shizhi lost his post to a rival and wrote a poem saying he had stepped down "to make way for the worthy" and would now enjoy his cup. The line borrows his words, with a pun: he "avoids" cloudy wine too.称避贤：说自己是给贤人让位。李适之被人排挤丢了相位，写诗说“避贤初罢相，乐圣且衔杯”，这里借用他的话，也有双关：不喝浊酒（贤人）。
宗之zōng zhī
Zongzhi: Cui Zongzhi, a young nobleman of the time.宗之：崔宗之，当时的一位贵族青年。
潇洒xiāo sǎ
xiāo sǎ: easy, natural and carefree.潇洒：自然大方，不拘束。
觞shāng
shāng: a wine cup.觞：酒杯。
白眼bái yǎn
bái yǎn: rolling the eyes up to show the whites, a sign of looking down on ordinary worldly people.白眼：眼睛向上翻，露出眼白，表示看不起世俗的人。
皎jiǎo
jiǎo: bright and pure white.皎：洁白明亮。
玉树临风yù shù lín fēng
yù shù lín fēng: like a jade tree in the wind; describes a handsome, graceful young man.玉树临风：像玉做的树站在风里，形容人长得好看、风度好。
苏晋sū jìn
Su Jin: an official of the time who followed Buddhism.苏晋：当时的一位官员，信佛。
长斋cháng zhāi
cháng zhāi: keeping a meatless diet for a long time.长斋：长期吃素。
绣佛xiù fó
xiù fó: an embroidered image of the Buddha.绣佛：用丝线绣成的佛像。
逃禅táo chán
táo chán: escaping the Buddhist rules. Buddhism forbids wine, but when he got drunk he forgot the rules.逃禅：逃离佛门的戒律。佛教不许喝酒，他喝醉了就把戒律忘了。
一斗诗百篇yì dǒu shī bǎi piān
yì dǒu shī bǎi piān: one measure of wine, a hundred poems. A playful way to say how fast his talent flowed.一斗诗百篇：喝一斗酒，就能写一百首诗。夸张地说李白才思敏捷。
天子呼来不上船tiān zǐ hū lái bú shàng chuán
tiān zǐ hū lái bú shàng chuán: the emperor called him, and he was too drunk to board the boat. Legend says Emperor Xuanzong was boating in the palace and summoned Li Bai to write, but Li Bai was already drunk.天子呼来不上船：皇帝叫他去，他醉得上不了船。传说唐玄宗在宫中游船，召李白来写文章，李白已经醉了。
张旭zhāng xù
Zhang Xu: a Tang calligrapher, best at the cursive style.张旭：唐代书法家，最擅长草书。
草圣cǎo shèng
cǎo shèng: the sage of cursive script. Cursive is a fast, flowing style of handwriting with joined strokes.草圣：写草书的圣手。草书是一种写得很快、连笔很多的书法。
脱帽露顶tuō mào lù dǐng
tuō mào lù dǐng: taking off his hat and baring his head, which was very rude before great lords long ago. It shows that drunk, he cared nothing for manners.脱帽露顶：摘下帽子，露出头顶。古人在大官面前这样做是很不礼貌的，说明他醉后不拘礼节。
王公wáng gōng
wáng gōng: princes and high officials.王公：王爷和大官。
挥毫huī háo
huī háo: wielding the writing brush.挥毫：挥动毛笔写字。
如云烟rú yún yān
rú yún yān: the characters flow from his brush like clouds and mist.如云烟：笔下的字像云烟一样飞动。
焦遂jiāo suì
Jiao Sui: a commoner who never held office, known for drinking and for brilliant talk.焦遂：一个普通百姓，没做过官，以能喝酒、会说话出名。
卓然zhuó rán
zhuó rán: lively and standing out from everyone.卓然：精神焕发、与众不同的样子。
雄辩xióng biàn
xióng biàn: powerful, convincing argument.雄辩：很有力、很有说服力的辩论。
四筵sì yán
sì yán: all the guests around the feast. Yán means a feast.四筵：满座的客人。筵，酒席。
03

How to read it读音

斗dǒu
dǒu: a wine vessel, also a unit of measure. Read dǒu, not dòu.斗：古代的酒器，也是容量单位，读 dǒu，不读 dòu。
朝cháo
cháo tiān: to go to court and see the emperor. Read cháo.朝天：朝见皇帝，朝读 cháo。
曲qū
qū chē: a cart carrying wine yeast. Read qū.曲车：装酒曲的车，曲读 qū。
相xiàng
zuǒ xiàng: the Chief Minister of the Left. Read xiàng.左相：官名，相读 xiàng。
兴xìng
xìng: high spirits, the mood for something. Read xìng.兴：兴致，读 xìng。
乐lè
lè shèng: to enjoy clear wine. Here lè means to enjoy.乐圣：喜欢喝清酒，乐读 lè。
少shào
shào nián: a young man. Read shào.少年：年轻人，少读 shào。
长cháng
cháng zhāi: keeping a meatless diet for a long time. Read cháng.长斋：长期吃素，长读 cháng。
传chuán
chuán: to be passed on, to spread. Read chuán.传：流传，读 chuán。
04

Background创作背景

This poem is usually thought to have been written while Du Fu was in Chang'an, in the Tianbao years of Emperor Xuanzong; one view dates it to around 746. The Tang was then at its height, and Chang'an was full of famous, free-spirited people who loved wine. Du Fu picked eight of them and drew a drunken portrait of each, calling them the Eight Immortals of the Wine Cup. The eight were not all in Chang'an at the same time; Du Fu put them together in one poem.

这首诗一般认为是杜甫在长安时写的，大约在唐玄宗天宝年间，一说是天宝五载（746年）前后。那时唐朝正强盛，长安城里有许多爱喝酒、性情豪放的名人。杜甫挑了八个人，给他们每人画了一幅“醉像”，合起来叫“饮中八仙”。这八个人并不都在同一时间待在长安，杜甫是把他们放在一起来写的。

05

The poet作者

Du Fu (712–770) was a great Tang poet, often called the Sage of Poetry. Most of his poems are heavy, about war and the suffering of ordinary people. But this poem was written when he was younger, before the An Lushan Rebellion, and its tone is light and funny, which is rare for him. Li Bai, who appears in the poem, was Du Fu's good friend, and Du Fu wrote many poems missing him.

杜甫（712—770），唐代大诗人，被人称为“诗圣”。他的诗大多沉重，写战乱和百姓的苦难。可是这首诗写在他年轻、还没经历安史之乱的时候，笔调轻松幽默，很少见。诗里的李白是杜甫的好朋友，杜甫一生写过不少想念李白的诗。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a comic strip, one panel per person, eight panels in all. He Zhizhang is so drunk that riding a horse feels like a boat, and he can sleep at the bottom of a well. The Prince of Ruyang drools at the sight of a wine-yeast cart. The chief minister drinks like a whale sucking up rivers. Cui Zongzhi rolls his eyes, handsome as a jade tree. Su Jin keeps a Buddhist fast, yet forgets the rules once he is drunk. Li Bai writes a hundred poems on one measure of wine and ignores even the emperor's call. Zhang Xu, after three cups, bares his head before great lords and writes. Last is Jiao Sui, a commoner: once he has had enough wine, everyone is stunned when he speaks. Each portrait has a different length; Li Bai gets four lines, the most. Every line rhymes, so the poem rushes along fast and lively. Picture Chang'an at the height of the Tang: people free and full of talent, charming even when drunk.

这首诗像一组漫画，一个人一格，八个人八格。贺知章醉得骑马像坐船，掉进井里还睡得着；汝阳王看见酒曲车就流口水；左丞相喝酒像鲸鱼吸水；崔宗之翻着白眼，帅得像玉树；苏晋明明吃素念佛，一醉就忘了戒律；李白喝一斗写百篇，连皇帝叫都不理；张旭喝了三杯，当着大官的面脱帽写字；最后的焦遂是个普通人，喝足了酒，一开口全场都惊呆了。每个人的写法长短不一样，写李白用了四句，最多。全诗每一句都押韵，读起来一气呵成，又快又热闹。读的时候可以想象盛唐的长安：人们自由自在，才华横溢，连喝醉的样子都那么可爱。
