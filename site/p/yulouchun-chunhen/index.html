yù玉lóu楼chūn春·chūn春hèn恨

yàn晏shū殊Yan Shu · Song宋

Jade Tower Spring: Spring Sorrow

 

lǜ绿yáng杨fāng芳cǎo草cháng长tíng亭lù路

nián年shào少pāo抛rén人róng容yì易qù去

lóu楼tóu头cán残mèng梦wǔ五gēng更zhōng钟

huā花dǐ底lí离chóu愁sān三yuè月yǔ雨

wú无qíng情bú不sì似duō多qíng情kǔ苦

yí一cùn寸hái还chéng成qiān千wàn万lǚ缕

tiān天yá涯dì地jiǎo角yǒu有qióng穷shí时

zhǐ只yǒu有xiāng相sī思wú无jìn尽chù处

The commonly taught text has lí chóu (the sorrow of parting) in the fourth line; the source text has lí qíng (feelings of parting). The meaning is close.

通行本第四句作“花底离愁三月雨”，这里的底本作“离情”，意思相近。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 绿杨芳草长亭路On the road by the parting pavilion, the willows are green and the grass is sweet.长亭外的路上，杨柳青青，芳草萋萋，
  2. 年少抛人容易去The young man left me as lightly as if it were nothing.那个年轻人轻易地丢下我走了。
  3. 楼头残梦五更钟Up in the tower, my dream is broken by the bell before dawn;楼上的梦被五更的钟声惊醒，
  4. 花底离愁三月雨beneath the flowers, my sorrow falls like the endless rain of the third month.花下的离愁像三月的雨一样绵绵不断。
  5. 无情不似多情苦Those without feelings do not suffer as those with deep feelings do.没有感情的人，不像多情的人这样痛苦，
  6. 一寸还成千万缕One small heart turns into thousands of threads of sorrow.小小的一颗心，竟变成了千丝万缕的愁绪。
  7. 天涯地角有穷时Even the edge of the sky and the corner of the earth have an end,天边地角再远，也总有到头的时候，
  8. 只有相思无尽处but longing alone goes on without end.只有相思，永远没有尽头。
02

Words字词注释

长亭cháng tíng
cháng tíng: a roadside pavilion where travellers rested. People often said goodbye there.长亭：古时路边供人休息的亭子，人们常在这里送别。
年少nián shào
nián shào: a young man, here the one who left.年少：年轻人，这里指离开的那个人。
抛人pāo rén
pāo rén: to leave me behind.抛人：丢下我。
容易去róng yì qù
róng yì qù: to go off lightly, as if it were nothing.容易去：轻易地就走了。
楼头lóu tóu
lóu tóu: up in the tower.楼头：楼上。
残梦cán mèng
cán mèng: a broken dream, cut off when one wakes.残梦：没做完的梦，被惊醒的梦。
五更钟wǔ gēng zhōng
wǔ gēng zhōng: the bell just before dawn. People once split the night into five watches; the fifth watch is just before daybreak.五更钟：天快亮时的钟声。古人把一夜分成五更，五更就是快天亮的时候。
花底huā dǐ
huā dǐ: beneath the flowers.花底：花丛下。
离愁lí chóu
lí chóu: the sorrow of parting.离愁：分别的愁苦。
三月雨sān yuè yǔ
sān yuè yǔ: rain in the third month, late spring, when rain knocks the flowers down.三月雨：暮春三月的雨，花被雨打落。
无情不似多情苦wú qíng bú sì duō qíng kǔ
wú qíng bú sì duō qíng kǔ: those without feelings do not suffer the way those with deep feelings do.无情不似多情苦：没有感情的人，不像多情的人这样痛苦。
一寸yí cùn
yí cùn: an inch, meaning the heart. People of old said the heart is only about an inch across.一寸：一寸长的心，指人的心。古人说心只有方寸那么大。
千万缕qiān wàn lǚ
qiān wàn lǚ: thousands of threads, meaning countless sad thoughts.千万缕：千丝万缕，像无数根丝线，指数不清的愁思。
天涯地角tiān yá dì jiǎo
tiān yá dì jiǎo: the edge of the sky and the corner of the earth, the farthest places there are.天涯地角：天的边、地的角，最远的地方。
有穷时yǒu qióng shí
yǒu qióng shí: there is a point where they end.有穷时：总有到头的时候。
无尽处wú jìn chù
wú jìn chù: no end at all.无尽处：没有尽头。
03

How to read it读音

还hái
In this line the character means yet or even so, and is usually read hái today. Some readers read it huán, taking it as turning into.“一寸还成千万缕”的“还”，意思是“却、竟然”，现在多读 hái；也有读本读作 huán（还原、变成）。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about a woman in spring who misses the man who has left her. The title means spring sorrow, the pain of parting felt in spring. Song poets often wrote about parting and longing in a woman's voice. We no longer know in which year it was written.

这首词写一位女子在春天想念离开的爱人。“春恨”就是春天里分别的愁苦。古人写词，常常借女子的口吻写离别和相思。这首词具体写于哪一年，已经说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Yan Shu (991–1055) was a Northern Song poet who served as chancellor. He was a child prodigy and was sent to court at fourteen. His lyrics are gentle and elegant, often about quiet sorrow and parting. The famous lines about flowers falling and swallows returning are also his.

晏殊（991—1055），字同叔，北宋词人，做过宰相。他从小就是“神童”，十四岁就被推荐进朝廷。他的词温和、典雅，常写闲愁和离别。著名的“无可奈何花落去，似曾相识燕归来”也是他写的。

06

The mood意境

The first half shows both the scene and the people. Outside the pavilion the willows are green and the grass is fresh, a lovely spring. Yet the young man walked off as if it were nothing. Up in the tower, her dream is broken by the dawn bell. Under the flowers, her sorrow keeps falling like spring rain. The bell and the rain make her loneliness something you can hear. The second half speaks straight from the heart. People without feelings do not suffer; only those who love deeply do. One small heart can grow thousands of threads of sorrow. The last two lines are the most famous. Even the farthest places on earth have an end, but longing has none. Comparing it with the farthest places shows how long longing lasts.

上半首写景也写人：长亭外柳绿草青，本是美好的春天，可那个年轻人却轻轻松松地走了。楼上，梦被五更钟声惊醒；花下，离愁像三月的雨下个不停。钟声和雨声，把孤单写得有声有色。下半首直接说心里话：不懂感情的人反倒不痛苦，多情的人才苦。一颗小小的心，能生出千丝万缕的愁。最后两句最有名：天涯海角再远也有尽头，只有相思没有尽头。用最远的地方来比，更显出相思之长。
