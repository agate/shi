fàn范chéng成dà大Fan Chengda · Song宋

Yan Er Mei: Sudden Clear Skies on the Road at Pingxiang

 

hān酣hān酣rì日jiǎo脚zǐ紫yān烟fú浮

yán妍nuǎn暖pò破qīng轻qiú裘

kùn困rén人tiān天sè色

zuì醉rén人huā花qì气

wǔ午mèng梦fú扶tóu头

yí一piàn片hú縠wén纹chóu愁

róng溶róng溶yì泄yì泄

dōng东fēng风wú无lì力

The corpus gives only the tune name. The ci has a short preface: "On the road at Pingxiang the sky suddenly cleared. Lying in my sedan chair I felt very drowsy, and rested a while by a willow pond." Common editions use its first words as the title.

语料只题词牌“眼儿媚”。这首词原有小序：“萍乡道中乍晴，卧舆中困甚，小憩柳塘。”通行本多用小序开头几个字作题目。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 酣酣日脚紫烟浮Warm beams of sun slant through the clouds, and a purple haze floats up,暖洋洋的阳光从云缝里照下来，紫色的雾气飘浮着，
  2. 妍暖破轻裘so bright and warm that even a light fur coat is too much.晴朗暖和，连薄皮袄都穿不住了。
  3. 困人天色The weather makes you sleepy,这天气让人发困，
  4. 醉人花气the scent of flowers makes you feel drunk,花香让人像喝醉了一样，
  5. 午梦扶头and after a midday nap your head is still heavy.午睡醒来，头还昏昏沉沉的。
  6. 春慵恰似春塘水This lazy spring mood is just like the water of a spring pond,春天这种懒洋洋的心情，正像春天池塘里的水，
  7. 一片縠纹愁spread with fine ripples, touched with faint sorrow.泛起一片细细的波纹，带着淡淡的愁。
  8. 溶溶泄泄The water sways slowly and gently,水波慢悠悠地荡漾着，
  9. 东风无力the east wind is soft and weak,东风软软的，没有力气，
  10. 欲皱还休about to wrinkle the water, then letting it rest.想让水面起皱，却又停下了。
02

Words字词注释

酣酣hān hān
hān hān: rich and warm, as if tipsy.酣酣：浓浓的、暖洋洋的样子，像喝醉了一样。
日脚rì jiǎo
rì jiǎo: beams of sunlight slanting down through gaps in the clouds, literally "the sun's feet".日脚：从云缝里照下来的一道道阳光。
紫烟zǐ yān
zǐ yān: mist lit by the sun, with a faint purple glow.紫烟：阳光照着的雾气，带着淡淡的紫色。
妍暖yán nuǎn
yán nuǎn: bright and warm. Yán means lovely.妍暖：晴朗暖和。妍，美好。
破轻裘pò qīng qiú
pò qīng qiú: so warm that even a light fur coat is too much. Qiú is a fur coat.破轻裘：暖得连薄皮袄都穿不住了。裘，皮衣。
困人kùn rén
kùn rén: making people sleepy.困人：让人犯困。
醉人zuì rén
zuì rén: making people feel drunk.醉人：让人像喝醉了一样。
扶头fú tóu
fú tóu: the head feels so heavy it must be propped up. One reading says it is the name of a strong wine.扶头：头昏昏沉沉，要用手撑着。一说“扶头”是一种让人易醉的酒。
春慵chūn yōng
chūn yōng: the lazy feeling of spring. Yōng means lazy, languid.春慵：春天里懒洋洋的感觉。慵，懒。
縠纹hú wén
hú wén: fine ripples on water, like the crinkles on crepe silk. Hú is a light, crinkled gauze.縠纹：水面上细细的波纹，像绉纱上的花纹。縠，一种有皱纹的轻纱。
溶溶泄泄róng róng yì yì
róng róng yì yì: water swaying slowly and gently.溶溶泄泄：水波慢慢荡漾、舒舒缓缓的样子。
欲皱还休yù zhòu hái xiū
yù zhòu hái xiū: the water seems about to ripple, then settles again.欲皱还休：水面好像要起波纹，又平静下来了。
03

How to read it读音

泄yì
yì yì: slow and easy, read yì, not xiè (to leak).泄泄：舒缓、慢悠悠的样子，读 yì yì，不读 xiè xiè。
还hái
yù zhòu hái xiū: about to ripple, then stopping again. Here the character means "yet, again", read hái.欲皱还休：想要起皱又停下了。“还”是“又、却”的意思，读 hái。
04

Background创作背景

In his preface Fan Chengda says that on the road at Pingxiang, in today's Jiangxi, the sky suddenly cleared. Lying in his sedan chair he felt terribly sleepy, so he rested a while by a pond lined with willows. It is usually thought he wrote it in the spring of 1173, passing Pingxiang on his way from Suzhou to a new post in Guilin.

范成大在小序里说：在萍乡（今江西萍乡）的路上，天忽然放晴，他躺在轿子里困得不得了，就在一个长着柳树的池塘边歇了一会儿。一般认为这是1173年春天，他从苏州去桂林上任，路过萍乡时写的。

05

The poet作者

Fan Chengda (1126–1193), also known as the Lay Buddhist of Stone Lake, was a Southern Song poet from Suzhou. He served in many places during his life and once went as an envoy to the Jin state. He had a sharp eye for small everyday scenes, and his poems on farm life through the seasons are the most famous. This ci captures one spring afternoon on a journey, seen up close.

范成大（1126—1193），号石湖居士，南宋诗人，苏州人。他一生到过很多地方做官，还出使过金国。他很会观察身边的小景物，写农村生活的《四时田园杂兴》最有名。这首词写旅途中的一个春日午后，看得很细。

06

The mood意境

The first half is about the weather. After rain the sky suddenly clears, beams of sun slant down, the mist glows purple, and it is so warm that even a light fur coat feels hot. The weather makes you sleepy, the flowers make you tipsy, and after a nap your head is still fuzzy. Reading it, you almost want to yawn too. The second half is the cleverest part. The poet says this lazy, hard-to-name spring sadness is like the water in a spring pond. The water sways gently. The east wind is too soft to do much: it starts to make ripples, then stops. His mood is the same: a little sad, but not quite able to be sad. Turning a feeling you cannot see into ripples you can see is a fresh and exact picture.

上片写天气。雨后忽然放晴，阳光一道道照下来，雾气带着紫色，暖得连薄皮袄都嫌热。天色让人困，花香让人醉，午睡起来头还是晕晕的。读着读着，自己好像也要打哈欠了。下片最妙：词人说，春天这种懒懒的、有点说不清的愁，就像池塘里的春水。水面轻轻荡着，东风软得没力气，想吹出波纹，吹了一下又停了。人的心情也是这样，好像有点愁，又愁不起来。把看不见的心情，写成看得见的水波，这个比喻又新又准。
