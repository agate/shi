No. 81 · Tang poem · seven-character regulated verse第 81 首 · 唐诗 · 七言律诗

jì寄lǐ李dān儋yuán元xī锡

wéi韦yìng应wù物Wei Yingwu · Tang唐

To Li Dan, Courtesy Name Yuanxi

 

qù去nián年huā花lǐ里féng逢jūn君bié别

jīn今rì日huā花kāi开yǐ已yì一nián年

shì世shì事máng茫máng茫nán难zì自liào料

chūn春chóu愁àn黯àn黯dú独chéng成mián眠

shēn身duō多jí疾bìng病sī思tián田lǐ里

yì邑yǒu有liú流wáng亡kuì愧fèng俸qián钱

wén闻dào道yù欲lái来xiāng相wèn问xùn讯

xī西lóu楼wàng望yuè月jǐ几huí回yuán圆

In the last line, the old source writes huí (time, round) with a rarer variant form of the same character. The common text uses the standard form. The meaning is the same.

最后一句的“回”，这里的古籍底本写作“廻”，是“回”的另一种写法，意思一样。通行本用“回”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 去年花里逢君别Last year, among the flowers, we met and then parted;去年花开的时候，我们相会又分别，
  2. 今日花开已一年today the flowers bloom again, and a whole year has gone.今天花又开了，已经过去整整一年。
  3. 世事茫茫难自料The affairs of the world are dim and hard to foresee;世上的事模糊难测，自己也预料不到，
  4. 春愁黯黯独成眠heavy with spring sorrow, I fall asleep alone.春天的愁绪让我心情低落，只能一个人睡下。
  5. 身多疾病思田里Often ill, I long to go home and farm;我身体多病，常常想辞官回乡种田，
  6. 邑有流亡愧俸钱while people in my district still flee, I am ashamed to take my pay.我管的地方还有百姓逃亡，拿着俸禄真觉得惭愧。
  7. 闻道欲来相问讯I hear you mean to come and visit me;听说你要来看望我，
  8. 西楼望月几回圆from the west tower I watch the moon, which has grown full time and again.我在西楼望着月亮，它已经圆了好几回。
02

Words字词注释

逢君别féng jūn bié
féng jūn bié: met you, and then parted from you.逢君别：和你相会，又和你分别。
世事shì shì
shì shì: the affairs of the world, here mainly the state of the country.世事：世上的事，这里主要指国家大事。
茫茫máng máng
máng máng: vague, with no end in sight.茫茫：模糊不清，看不到头。
难自料nán zì liào
nán zì liào: hard to foresee for oneself.难自料：自己很难预料。
黯黯àn àn
àn àn: gloomy and low in spirits.黯黯：心情低落、暗淡的样子。
独成眠dú chéng mián
dú chéng mián: falling asleep alone.独成眠：一个人睡下。
思田里sī tián lǐ
sī tián lǐ: longing to go back to the countryside, meaning to give up office and go home.思田里：想回到乡下种田，也就是想辞官回家。
邑yì
yì: a town or district; here Chuzhou, which the poet governed.邑：这里指诗人管理的滁州。
流亡liú wáng
liú wáng: people fleeing their homes because of poverty.流亡：百姓因为穷困，离开家乡逃到外地。
愧俸钱kuì fèng qián
kuì fèng qián: feeling ashamed to take his pay; fèng qián is an official's salary.愧俸钱：拿着朝廷发的工资，心里觉得惭愧。俸钱，官员的薪水。

wén dào: I hear that.闻道：听说。
问讯wèn xùn
wèn xùn: to visit and ask after someone.问讯：探望、问候。

jǐ huí yuán: the moon has been full several times, meaning he has waited many months.几回圆：月亮已经圆了好几次，意思是盼了好几个月。
03

Background创作背景

This poem is usually dated to the spring of 784. The year before, Wei Yingwu had left Chang'an to become prefect of Chuzhou, today's Chuzhou in Anhui, that is, the head official of the area. The country had just been hit by a revolt, and even the emperor had fled Chang'an. Times were very unsettled. He wrote to his old friend Li Dan to share his worries. Li Dan's courtesy name was Yuanxi. Another view says Yuanxi was a different person, and the poem was sent to two friends at once.

这首诗一般认为写于唐德宗兴元元年（784年）春天。前一年，韦应物离开长安，到滁州（今安徽滁州）做刺史，也就是当地的长官。当时国家刚经历叛乱，皇帝都逃出了长安，局势很乱。他写信给老朋友李儋，说出自己的忧愁。李儋字元锡，是韦应物的好朋友；一说元锡是另一个人，这首诗是同时寄给两位朋友的。

04

The poet作者

Wei Yingwu (about 737 to about 792) was a Tang poet. As a young man he was a guard to the emperor and lived wildly. Later he studied hard, became a local official, and cared deeply for the people. His poems are plain, clear and natural, often about the countryside and nature. He once served as prefect of Suzhou, so people also call him Wei Suzhou.

韦应物（约737—约792），唐代诗人。他年轻时做过皇帝的侍卫，性格豪放；后来发奋读书，做了地方官，很关心百姓。他的诗清淡自然，多写田园山水。他做过苏州刺史，所以人们也叫他“韦苏州”。

05

The mood意境

The first two lines count time by the flowers. They parted when the flowers bloomed last year, and now the flowers bloom again. This simple contrast shows how fast time flies, and how much he misses his friend. Lines three and four say the country's future is unclear, he himself feels lost, and spring gloom leaves him to sleep alone. Lines five and six are the most famous. He is often ill and wants to go home and farm. But people in his district are still fleeing from hunger, and he is ashamed to take his pay. It is rare for an official to blame himself like this, and many later readers praised these lines. At the end he says he has heard his friend will come, and he waits in the west tower every night; the moon has already been full several times.

开头两句用花开花落来记时间：去年花开时分别，今年花又开了。简单的对比，写出了时间过得快，也写出了思念。三、四句说国家大事看不清，自己也很迷茫，春天的愁闷让人只能独自睡去。五、六句最有名：身体不好，想回家种田；可是自己管的地方还有百姓在逃荒，拿着工资心里很惭愧。一个当官的人能这样自责，非常难得，后来很多人都称赞这两句。最后说，听说朋友要来，自己天天在西楼等，月亮都圆了好几回了。
