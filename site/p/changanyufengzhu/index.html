长安遇冯著

韦应物Wei Yingwu · Tang唐

Meeting Feng Zhu in Chang'an

 

kè客cóng从dōng东fāng方lái来

yī衣shàng上bà灞líng陵yǔ雨

wèn问kè客hé何wèi为lái来

cǎi采shān山yīn因mǎi买fǔ斧

míng冥míng冥huā花zhèng正kāi开

yáng飏yáng飏yàn燕xīn新rǔ乳

zuó昨bié别jīn今yǐ已chūn春

bìn鬓sī丝shēng生jǐ几lǚ缕

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 客从东方来My guest has come from the east,客人从东方来，
  2. 衣上灞陵雨with the rain of Baling still on his clothes.衣服上还带着灞陵的雨水。
  3. 问客何为来I ask him why he has come,问客人为什么来长安，
  4. 采山因买斧and he says he is going to live in the hills, so he came to buy an axe.他说要进山砍柴，所以来买把斧头。
  5. 冥冥花正开The flowers are in full bloom, thick and deep,花开得正盛，密密层层，
  6. 飏飏燕新乳and the swallows dart about, their chicks just hatched.燕子轻快地飞，刚孵出了小燕。
  7. 昨别今已春It feels like we parted only yesterday, and now it is spring again.好像昨天才分别，现在又已经是春天了，
  8. 鬓丝生几缕How many new grey hairs have grown at your temples?你的鬓角又添了几根白发？
02

Words字词注释

客kè
kè: the guest, meaning Feng Zhu.客：客人，指冯著。
灞陵bà líng
bà líng: the tomb of Emperor Wen of Han, east of Chang'an near the Ba River. Travellers from the east passed it on the way into the city.灞陵：汉文帝的陵墓，在长安城东边，附近有灞水。从东方来长安，要经过这里。
何为hé wèi
hé wèi: why.何为：为什么。
采山cǎi shān
cǎi shān: to go into the hills to cut wood and gather herbs, that is, to live as a hermit.采山：上山砍柴、采药，意思是在山里过隐居的生活。
因yīn
yīn: so, therefore.因：所以，于是。
斧fǔ
fǔ: an axe.斧：斧头。
冥冥míng míng
míng míng: so thick and deep you cannot see the edge, describing masses of flowers. Another reading says it describes a misty spring drizzle.冥冥：花开得又密又深、看不清边的样子。一说指春雨迷蒙的样子。
飏飏yáng yáng
yáng yáng: flying lightly and quickly, like a bird on the wind.飏飏：鸟儿轻快飞翔的样子。
新乳xīn rǔ
xīn rǔ: have just hatched their young. Rǔ here means to give birth or hatch.新乳：刚刚孵出小燕子。乳，生育、孵化。
昨别zuó bié
zuó bié: parted not long ago, meaning last year.昨别：不久前分别，指去年分手。
鬓丝bìn sī
bìn sī: the hair at the temples, here meaning grey hairs.鬓丝：鬓角的头发，这里指白发。
几缕jǐ lǚ
jǐ lǚ: how many strands.几缕：几根，几丝。
03

How to read it读音

为wèi
Hé wèi here means "why", so the character is read wèi. Some read it hé wéi, meaning "to do what".“何为”在这里是“为什么”的意思，“为”读 wèi。也有人读 hé wéi，意思是“来做什么”。
04

Background创作背景

Feng Zhu was a friend of Wei Yingwu. He was very talented, but never did well as an official. One spring, Feng came to Chang'an from the east, passing Baling, and Wei met him and wrote this poem. The line about buying an axe is Feng's answer. It is usually read as a joke with a bitter smile: he had come to the capital hoping to find a post, failed, and so said he had only come to buy an axe so he could go and live in the hills. Which year it was written is not agreed.

冯著是韦应物的朋友，很有才华，但做官一直不顺利。这一年春天，冯著从东边来到长安，经过灞陵，韦应物遇见他，写了这首诗。“采山因买斧”是冯著的回答，一般理解为一句带着苦笑的玩笑话：他到长安本想谋个出路，没有成功，只好说自己是来买斧头、准备进山隐居的。具体写于哪一年，说法不一。

05

The poet作者

Wei Yingwu was a mid-Tang poet from Chang'an. As a young man he was a guard of Emperor Xuanzong and lived rather wildly. Only after the An Lushan Rebellion did he start to study hard. Later he governed Chuzhou, Jiangzhou and Suzhou, and people called him Wei of Suzhou. His poems are plain and natural, and often show care for his friends. This poem about meeting a friend is warm and friendly, just like his style.

韦应物，唐代中期诗人，长安人。他年轻时做过唐玄宗的侍卫，很任性；安史之乱以后才发愤读书。后来他做过滁州、江州、苏州的刺史（一州的长官），人称“韦苏州”。他的诗平淡自然，常写对朋友的关心。这首诗写朋友见面，语气亲切，正是他的风格。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a short chat between two old friends. First we see Feng Zhu, tired from the road, with rain still on his clothes. Asked why he has come, he jokes that he is here to buy an axe so he can cut wood in the hills. He says it lightly, but he is disappointed inside. Wei Yingwu does not comfort him directly. Instead he points to the spring around them: flowers thick on the branches, swallows with newly hatched chicks, new life everywhere. With spring so lovely, why be too sad? The last two lines tease a little too. We parted only a short while ago, and already it is spring again. How many new grey hairs have you got? All the care and sympathy are in this gentle question.

这首诗像两位老朋友的一段对话。开头写冯著风尘仆仆，衣服上还沾着路上的雨。问他来干什么，他笑着说来买斧头上山砍柴，话说得轻松，心里却有失意。韦应物没有直接安慰他，而是指给他看眼前的春天：花开得正密，燕子刚孵出小燕，到处都是新生命。春天这么好，朋友又何必太难过呢？最后两句又带点玩笑：才分别没多久，又一年春天了，你的鬓角又多了几根白发吧？关心和惋惜，全在这句轻轻的问话里。
