The edition used here titles it Lang Tao Sha. The common text calls it Lang Tao Sha Man, because it is a long slow lyric, different from the short tune Lang Tao Sha. In tì yún yóu yǔ, the edition uses a rare variant form of the first character; this page uses the common form.

这里用的整理本题作《浪淘沙》，通行本题作《浪淘沙慢》，因为它是长调慢词，和常见的短调《浪淘沙》不同。“殢云尤雨”的“殢”，整理本是一个生僻的异体字，这里照通行本写作“殢”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 梦觉I wake from a dream:从梦中醒来，
  2. 透窗风一线a thread of wind slips in through the window一丝风从窗缝里透进来，
  3. 寒灯吹息and blows out the lonely lamp of the cold night.把寒夜的孤灯吹灭了。
  4. 那堪酒醒How can I bear it? I have just sobered up,哪里受得了，酒刚醒，
  5. 又闻空阶and again I hear, on the empty steps,又听见空荡荡的台阶上，
  6. 夜雨频滴the night rain dripping on and on.夜雨一滴一滴落个不停。
  7. 嗟因循Alas, I have let things drift,唉，我拖拖拉拉，
  8. 久作天涯客and been a wanderer at the edge of the world so long;在天涯漂泊了这么久，
  9. 负佳人I have failed my beloved辜负了心爱的人，
  10. 几许盟言and all the vows we made.和那么多的誓言，
  11. 便忍把How could I bear to take竟然忍心把
  12. 从前欢会all our happy times together从前那些快乐的相聚，
  13. 陡顿翻成忧戚and turn them, all at once, into grief?一下子变成了忧愁和悲伤。
  14. 愁极My sorrow is at its height.愁到了极点。
  15. 再三追思Again and again I think back:一遍又一遍地回想，
  16. 洞房深处in her deep inner room,在那深深的内室里，
  17. 几度饮散歌阑how many times the wine was done and the songs had ended,多少次酒宴散了，歌也唱完，
  18. 香暖鸳鸯被the mandarin-duck quilt warm and sweet;鸳鸯被里又香又暖，

  20. 费伊心力She gave me all her heart.她为我费尽了心思。
  21. 殢云尤雨We were so close we could not bear to part,我们那样亲密，难舍难分，
  22. 有万般千种with a thousand thousand ways有千千万万种

02

Words字词注释

梦觉mèng jué
mèng jué: waking from a dream.梦觉：从梦中醒来。
风一线fēng yí xiàn
fēng yī xiàn: a thin thread of wind slipping in through a crack in the window.风一线：一丝细细的风，从窗缝里钻进来。
吹息chuī xī
chuī xī: blowing the lamp out. Xī means to go out.吹息：把灯吹灭了。息，熄灭。
那堪nǎ kān
nǎ kān: how can one bear it.那堪：哪里受得了。
空阶kōng jiē
kōng jiē: the empty steps outside.空阶：空荡荡的台阶。
频滴pín dī
pín dī: dripping again and again.频滴：一滴接一滴，不停地滴。
嗟jiē
jiē: alas, a sound of sighing.嗟：唉，叹气的声音。
因循yīn xún
yīn xún: putting things off, letting things drift.因循：拖拖拉拉，一直没有改变。
天涯客tiān yá kè
tiān yá kè: a traveller far from home. Tiān yá, "the edge of the sky", means a very distant place.天涯客：漂泊在远方的游子。天涯，天边，指很远的地方。
负fù
fù: to let someone down, to fail their love.负：辜负，对不起别人的情意。
几许盟言jǐ xǔ méng yán
jǐ xǔ méng yán: so many vows. Méng yán are promises lovers make to each other.几许盟言：多少山盟海誓。盟言，相爱的人许下的誓言。
便忍把biàn rěn bǎ
biàn rěn bǎ: how could I bear to take.便忍把：竟然忍心把。
欢会huān huì
huān huì: happy times together.欢会：快乐的相聚。
陡顿dǒu dùn
dǒu dùn: suddenly, all at once.陡顿：突然、一下子。
忧戚yōu qī
yōu qī: sorrow and grief.忧戚：忧愁悲伤。
愁极chóu jí
chóu jí: sorrow at its utmost.愁极：愁到了极点。
追思zhuī sī
zhuī sī: to think back on the past.追思：回想过去的事。
洞房dòng fáng
dòng fáng: a deep inner room where a lady lived. It does not mean a bridal chamber here.洞房：深深的内室，女子住的房间。这里不是“新婚的房间”。
饮散歌阑yǐn sàn gē lán
yǐn sàn gē lán: the drinking ended and the songs were done. Lán means to come to an end.饮散歌阑：酒席散了，歌也唱完了。阑，尽、结束。
鸳鸯被yuān yāng bèi
yuān yāng bèi: a quilt embroidered with mandarin ducks, birds that always go in pairs and stand for lovers.鸳鸯被：绣着鸳鸯的被子。鸳鸯总是成双成对，比喻相爱的人。
岂暂时疏散qǐ zàn shí shū sàn
qǐ zàn shí shū sàn: when were we ever apart for even a moment? Shū sàn means to drift apart.岂暂时疏散：哪里有一时一刻分开过？疏散，分开、冷落。
费伊心力fèi yī xīn lì
fèi yī xīn lì: she spent all her heart on me. Yī means she.费伊心力：让她费尽了心思。伊，她。
殢云尤雨tì yún yóu yǔ
tì yún yóu yǔ: "clinging to the clouds and the rain", meaning two lovers who cannot bear to be apart.殢云尤雨：形容两人非常亲密，难舍难分。
万般千种wàn bān qiān zhǒng
wàn bān qiān zhǒng: thousands upon thousands of ways.万般千种：千千万万种。
相怜相惜xiāng lián xiāng xī
xiāng lián xiāng xī: loving and cherishing each other.相怜相惜：互相疼爱，互相珍惜。
恰到如今qià dào rú jīn
qià dào rú jīn: and now, of all times.恰到如今：偏偏到了现在。
天长漏永tiān cháng lòu yǒng
tiān cháng lòu yǒng: the night seems endless. A lòu was a water clock; yǒng means long.天长漏永：夜晚长得没有尽头。漏，古代用滴水来计时的器具；永，长。
无端wú duān
wú duān: for no reason at all.无端：没来由地，说不出为什么。
自家疏隔zì jiā shū gé
zì jiā shū gé: we ourselves have drifted apart. Zì jiā means oneself.自家疏隔：我们自己却分开了。自家，自己。
拥yōng
yōng: to hold in one's arms.拥：抱着。
秦云态qín yún tài
qín yún tài: a fairy-like beauty, meaning the beloved.秦云态：像仙女一样美好的样子，指心爱的女子。
低帏昵枕dī wéi nì zhěn
dī wéi nì zhěn: letting down the bed curtains and lying close on the pillow. Wéi is a curtain; nì means close and fond.低帏昵枕：放下帐子，靠在枕边亲亲热热。帏，帐子；昵，亲近。
说与shuō yǔ
shuō yǔ: to tell her.说与：说给她听。
江乡jiāng xiāng
jiāng xiāng: a riverside land, the southern water country where the poet is wandering.江乡：江边的地方，指词人漂泊所在的南方水乡。
寒更hán gēng
hán gēng: the night watches of a cold night. A night was split into five watches of about two hours each.寒更：寒夜里的更。古代把一夜分成五更，每更大约两个小时。
思忆sī yì
sī yì: to miss someone.思忆：想念。
03

How to read it读音

觉jué
Mèng jué means waking from a dream, so this character is read jué, not jiào as in "to sleep".“梦觉”是从梦里醒来，读 jué，不是“睡觉”的 jiào。
那nǎ
Nǎ kān means "how can one bear", so this character is read nǎ.“那堪”是“哪里受得了”的意思，读 nǎ，同“哪”。
伊yī
This character means "she" and is read yī.“伊”是“她”的意思，读 yī。
殢tì
This rare character is read tì and means to cling to, to linger over.殢读 tì，是“纠缠、留恋不放”的意思。
数shǔ
Shǔ hán gēng means counting the cold night watches one by one, so this character is a verb, read shǔ.“数寒更”是一更一更地数着时间，“数”是动词，读 shǔ。
更gēng
In hán gēng this character is a night watch, a unit of night time, so it is read gēng, not gèng as in "even more".“寒更”的“更”是夜里的计时单位，读 gēng，不是“更加”的 gèng。
04

Background创作背景

Liu Yong did badly in the exams for years and spent much of his life on the road. He wrote many ci about travel far from home and about missing someone. This one shows a traveller in a southern river town. He wakes at midnight after drinking, hears the rain, and thinks of the woman he loves far away. He regrets staying away so long and letting her down. Whether it tells of his own life or of travellers in general, and when and where it was written, can no longer be said for sure.

柳永一生考试不顺，常年在外漂泊，写了很多羁旅和相思的词。羁旅，就是在外乡奔波、寄住。这首词写一个漂泊在南方水乡的人，半夜酒醒，听着雨声，想起远方的心上人，后悔自己一直在外，辜负了她。词里写的是他自己的经历还是一般的游子之情，具体写于哪一年、在什么地方，都已经说不清了。

05

The poet作者

Liu Yong (about 984 – about 1053), first named Sanbian and also called Qiqing, was the seventh son, so people called him Liu Qi, "Liu the Seventh". He was a Northern Song ci poet from Chong'an in Fujian. He failed the top exam many times and often wrote songs for singing girls in wine houses. His songs spread so far that people said "wherever there is a well, people sing Liu's ci". He passed the exam only late in life and served as an official in the farm-land office, so he was also called Liu Tuntian. He was a master of long, slow ci, and this is one of them.

柳永（约984—约1053），原名三变，字耆卿，排行第七，人称“柳七”，北宋词人，福建崇安人。他多次考进士都没考上，常在歌楼酒馆里为歌女写词，作品传唱很广，有“凡有井水饮处，即能歌柳词”的说法。他晚年才中进士，做过屯田员外郎，所以又叫“柳屯田”。他特别擅长写长调慢词，这首就是一例。

06

The mood意境

The ci has three parts, like the thoughts of someone who wakes at midnight, coming out layer by layer. The first part is what is around him: a thread of cold wind blows out the lamp, he has sobered up, and he hears only the night rain dripping on the steps. On such a night it is easiest to think of the one you love, and easiest to feel regret: he has wandered too long, broken his vows and turned happiness into sorrow. The second part goes back to the past: her deep inner room, the end of the feast, the warm quilt, how she gave him all her heart and they could not bear to part. The third part returns to now: the night is so long, and yet they are apart. In the end he only hopes to see her again one day and tell her softly that every night by the river he counted the night watches, missing her. There are no big ups and downs here. Like the rain, it is soft and steady, and it makes the longing feel very real.

这首词分三段，像一个人半夜醒来后的心事，一层一层说出来。第一段是眼前：一丝冷风吹灭了灯，酒醒了，只听见台阶上的夜雨一滴一滴落下。这样的夜，最容易想起心爱的人，也最容易后悔：自己漂泊太久，辜负了誓言，把快乐变成了忧愁。第二段回到过去：深深的内室里，酒宴散后，暖暖的被子，她为他费尽心思，两人难舍难分。第三段又回到现在：夜那么长，两人却分开了。最后他只盼望有一天能重新见到她，轻轻告诉她，自己在江边的每个夜里，都数着更声在想她。整首词没有大起大落，像雨声一样细细的、绵绵的，把思念写得很真。
