辋川闲居赠裴秀才迪

王维Wang Wei · Tang唐

Living Quietly at Wangchuan, for Scholar Pei Di

 

hán寒shān山zhuǎn转cāng苍cuì翠

qiū秋shuǐ水rì日chán潺yuán湲

yǐ倚zhàng杖chái柴mén门wài外

lín临fēng风tīng听mù暮chán蝉

dù渡tóu头yú余luò落rì日

xū墟lǐ里shàng上gū孤yān烟

fù复zhí值jiē接yú舆zuì醉

kuáng狂gē歌wǔ五liǔ柳qián前

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 寒山转苍翠The cold autumn hills turn a deeper green,秋天的山变得更加苍翠，
  2. 秋水日潺湲and the autumn stream flows gently on, day after day.秋水一天天缓缓地流着。
  3. 倚杖柴门外Leaning on my stick outside my wooden gate,我拄着拐杖站在柴门外，
  4. 临风听暮蝉I face the wind and listen to the cicadas at dusk.迎着风，听傍晚的蝉在叫。
  5. 渡头余落日At the ferry landing, only the setting sun is left.渡口那边，只剩下快要落山的夕阳；
  6. 墟里上孤烟From the village, one thread of cooking smoke rises.村子里，升起了一缕炊烟。
  7. 复值接舆醉And then I meet you, a wild one like Jieyu, drunk again,又碰上你这位像接舆一样的狂人喝醉了，
  8. 狂歌五柳前singing loudly in front of me, your Master Five Willows.在我这个“五柳先生”面前放声高歌。

mù chán: cicadas at dusk, late cicadas of autumn.暮蝉：傍晚的蝉，也指秋后的蝉。

dù tóu: the ferry landing, where boats cross the river.渡头：渡口，坐船过河的地方。

fù zhí: and then I happen to meet.复值：又碰上。
接舆jiē yú
jiē yú: Jieyu, a hermit of the state of Chu long ago who acted mad and once sang a song as he passed Confucius's carriage. Here he stands for Pei Di.接舆：春秋时楚国的一个隐士，假装疯癫，曾唱着歌从孔子车前走过。这里用来比喻裴迪。

03

Background创作背景

In his middle years Wang Wei bought a country home at Wangchuan in Lantian, near today's Xi'an. He often lived there almost like a hermit. His good friend Pei Di came often. The two walked the hills, wrote poems back and forth, and made a collection together called the Wangchuan poems. Wang Wei wrote this poem for Pei Di while living quietly there. It shows an autumn evening and their friendship. The exact year is not known.

王维中年以后在蓝田（今属陕西西安）的辋川买了一座别墅，常在那里过隐居一样的生活。好友裴迪也常来，两人一起游山玩水、写诗唱和，还合写过《辋川集》。这首诗是王维在辋川闲住时写给裴迪的，写的是秋天傍晚的景色和两人的友情。具体写于哪一年不详。

04

The poet作者

Wang Wei (701–761 by one account) was a great Tang poet, and also a painter and musician. He is best known for poems about hills, rivers and farm life. The Song poet Su Shi said his poems hold paintings and his paintings hold poems. He was a Buddhist, and in later life he was half an official and half a hermit. His poems often feel quiet and far-reaching.

王维（一说701—761），字摩诘，唐代大诗人，也是画家和音乐家。他写山水田园最有名，苏轼说他“诗中有画，画中有诗”。他信佛，晚年过着半官半隐的生活，诗里常有安静、淡远的味道。

05

The mood意境

This poem is like a painting of an autumn evening. The hills are deep green, the stream flows slowly, and the poet stands outside his wooden gate on his stick, listening to cicadas in the wind, completely at ease. The most famous lines are the fifth and sixth. Far off at the ferry, the sun is about to set. Close by in the village, one thread of smoke rises straight up. One thing sinks, one thing rises, and the scene is quiet and warm. The last two lines show Pei Di arriving drunk and singing. The poet compares him to the wild hermit Jieyu, and himself to Tao Yuanming. Two friends who care little for official life and love nature are free and happy here.

这首诗就像一幅秋日黄昏图。山是苍翠的，水是慢慢流的，诗人拄着拐杖站在柴门外，听着风里的蝉声，非常悠闲。“渡头余落日，墟里上孤烟”是最有名的两句：远处渡口，太阳快要落下；近处村子，一缕炊烟直直升起。一个往下沉，一个往上升，画面安静又温暖。最后两句写裴迪喝醉了来唱歌，诗人把他比作狂人接舆，把自己比作陶渊明。两个不爱官场、爱自然的朋友，在这里过得自在快活。
