Written at My Wangchuan Farm After Long Rain

 

jī积yǔ雨kōng空lín林yān烟huǒ火chí迟

zhēng蒸lí藜chuī炊shǔ黍xiǎng饷dōng东zī菑

mò漠mò漠shuǐ水tián田fēi飞bái白lù鹭

yīn阴yīn阴xià夏mù木zhuàn啭huáng黄lí鹂

shān山zhōng中xí习jìng静guān观zhāo朝jǐn槿

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 积雨空林烟火迟After many days of rain, the woods are quiet, and the cooking smoke rises slowly.雨下了很多天，林子里静悄悄的，炊烟慢慢升起。
  2. 蒸藜炊黍饷东菑People steam wild greens and cook millet, and carry the food to the workers in the eastern fields.人们蒸好野菜，煮好黄米饭，送给东边田里干活的人。
  3. 漠漠水田飞白鹭Over the wide flooded rice fields, white egrets fly.一大片水田上，白鹭飞来飞去。
  4. 阴阴夏木啭黄鹂In the deep shade of the summer trees, orioles sing.浓密的夏日树荫里，黄鹂在婉转歌唱。
  5. 山中习静观朝槿In the mountains I practice calm, watching the rose of Sharon that blooms at dawn and fades at dusk.我在山里练习静心，看着早开晚落的木槿花。
  6. 松下清斋折露葵Under the pines I keep a plain meal, picking mallow greens wet with dew.在松树下吃素斋，摘下带露水的葵菜。
  7. 野老与人争席罢This old countryman has long stopped standing apart from other people.我这个乡下老人早就和别人不分你我了。
  8. 海鸥何事更相疑So why, you gulls, would you still be wary of me?海鸥啊，你们为什么还要怀疑我呢？
02

Words字词注释

积雨jī yǔ
jī yǔ: rain that has gone on for many days.积雨：下了很久的雨。
空林kōng lín
kōng lín: a quiet wood with hardly anyone in it.空林：安静、没有什么人的树林。
烟火迟yān huǒ chí
yān huǒ chí: in the damp air, the smoke from cooking fires rises slowly.烟火迟：雨天空气潮湿，做饭的炊烟慢慢地升起来。
藜lí
lí: a wild plant whose young leaves can be eaten.藜：一种野菜，嫩叶可以吃。
黍shǔ
shǔ: broomcorn millet, a kind of grain.黍：黄米，一种粮食。
饷xiǎng
xiǎng: to bring food to people working in the fields.饷：给在地里干活的人送饭。
东菑dōng zī
dōng zī: the fields to the east. Zī means farmland that has been cleared.东菑：东边的田地。菑，开垦过的田。
漠漠mò mò
mò mò: spreading wide, as far as the eye can see.漠漠：一大片、望不到边的样子。
阴阴yīn yīn
yīn yīn: thick and shady, as under leafy trees.阴阴：树木茂密、浓荫一片的样子。
啭zhuàn
zhuàn: for a bird to sing in a rolling, tuneful way.啭：鸟儿婉转地叫。
黄鹂huáng lí
huáng lí: the oriole, a yellow bird with a sweet song.黄鹂：一种黄色的鸟，叫声好听。
习静xí jìng
xí jìng: practicing calm, like sitting quietly to settle the mind.习静：练习让心安静下来，类似静坐修心。
朝槿zhāo jǐn
zhāo jǐn: the rose of Sharon. Its flowers open in the morning and fade by evening.朝槿：木槿花。它早上开花，到傍晚就凋谢了。
清斋qīng zhāi
qīng zhāi: eating plain vegetarian food and living simply.清斋：吃素，过清淡简单的日子。
露葵lù kuí
lù kuí: mallow greens with dew on them. Mallow was a common vegetable in old times.露葵：带着露水的葵菜。葵菜是古代常吃的一种蔬菜。
野老yě lǎo
yě lǎo: an old man of the countryside. Here Wang Wei means himself.野老：乡下老人，这里是王维自称。
争席zhēng xí
zhēng xí: fighting over seats. It comes from the book Zhuangzi. When a certain man first arrived at an inn, everyone gave up their seats to him politely. Later he dropped his proud airs, and people pushed him for a seat like anyone else. It means mixing freely with ordinary people.争席：争座位。出自《庄子》：有个人刚来时旅店的人都恭敬地给他让座，后来他放下了架子，大家就随便和他争座位了。意思是和普通人打成一片，没有隔阂。
海鸥何事更相疑hǎi ōu hé shì gèng xiāng yí
hǎi ōu hé shì gèng xiāng yí: this comes from the book Liezi. A man by the sea loved the gulls, and they played with him every day. His father told him to catch one. The next day the gulls only circled in the sky and would not come down. Here it means: I no longer have any scheming in my heart, so why would the gulls still doubt me?海鸥何事更相疑：出自《列子》：海边有人喜欢海鸥，海鸥天天和他一起玩。他父亲叫他抓一只回来，第二天海鸥就只在天上飞，不肯下来了。这里说：我已经没有一点机心，海鸥为什么还要怀疑我呢？
03

How to read it读音

菑zī
Here this character means a farm field and is read zī, not zāi.菑在这里指田地，读 zī，不读 zāi。
朝zhāo
In this word the character means morning, so it is read zhāo.朝槿的“朝”是早晨的意思，读 zhāo。
04

Background创作背景

Wangchuan is in the mountains of today's Lantian County in Shaanxi. In his middle years Wang Wei bought a country house there and often stayed in it. He kept his government post but lived much like a hermit. This poem describes the farm scenes he saw at Wangchuan after many days of summer rain, and his own quiet life. We do not know for sure which year it was written.

辋川在今天陕西蓝田县的山里。王维中年以后在这里买下一座别墅，常常住在里面，一边做官，一边过着隐居的日子。这首诗写的是夏天连日下雨后，他在辋川别墅看到的田园景色，和自己安静修心的生活。具体写于哪一年，没有确切的说法。

05

The poet作者

Wang Wei (701?–761) was a great Tang poet, and also a painter and musician. He was a devoted Buddhist and is called the Buddha of Poetry. The later writer Su Shi said there were paintings in his poems and poems in his paintings. He was best at mountains, rivers and farm life, and this poem reads like a painting of the countryside after rain.

王维（701？—761），唐代大诗人，也是画家和音乐家，信奉佛教，被人称为“诗佛”。苏轼说他“诗中有画，画中有诗”。他写山水田园最拿手，这首诗就像一幅雨后的田园画。

06

The mood意境

The first four lines are a picture of a farm after rain. The rain has gone on so long that the woods are still, and cooking smoke drifts up slowly. Families cook and carry meals to the fields. Then the view widens. White egrets fly over broad wet fields: that is what we see. Orioles sing in the thick shade: that is what we hear. The doubled words mò mò and yīn yīn make the picture feel wide and deep. The last four lines are about the poet himself. He watches flowers that open at dawn and fall at dusk, and thinks how everything changes. He eats plain food and picks greens. At the end he uses two old stories to say he has let go of all scheming. He can live at ease with country people and even with birds. The whole poem is quiet and damp with rain, and reading it calms the heart.

前四句是一幅农家雨后图。雨下久了，林子很静，炊烟慢慢升起；农家做好饭，送到田里。接着镜头拉远：一大片水田上白鹭飞着，这是看到的；浓浓树荫里黄鹂唱着，这是听到的。“漠漠”“阴阴”两个叠词，让画面又宽又深。后四句写自己：看木槿花早开晚谢，想到万物都会变；吃素、摘葵菜，过得清清淡淡。最后用两个古代故事说，我已经放下了一切心机，和乡下人、和鸟儿都能自在相处。整首诗安静、湿润，读起来心也跟着静了。
