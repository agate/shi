cǎi采sāng桑zǐ子·qīng轻zhōu舟duǎn短zhào棹xī西hú湖hǎo好

ōu欧yáng阳xiū修Ouyang Xiu · Song宋

Cai Sang Zi: In a Light Boat with a Short Oar, West Lake Is Lovely

 

qīng轻zhōu舟duǎn短zhào棹xī西hú湖hǎo好

lǜ绿shuǐ水wēi逶yí迤

fāng芳cǎo草cháng长dī堤

yǐn隐yǐn隐shēng笙gē歌chù处chù处suí随

wú无fēng风shuǐ水miàn面liú琉lí璃huá滑

bù不jué觉chuán船yí移

wēi微dòng动lián涟yī漪

jīng惊qǐ起shā沙qín禽lüè掠àn岸fēi飞

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 轻舟短棹西湖好In a light boat with a short oar, West Lake is so lovely.驾着轻快的小船，划着短桨，西湖真美好。
  2. 绿水逶迤The green water winds on and on,碧绿的湖水弯弯曲曲伸向远方，
  3. 芳草长堤fragrant grass covers the long bank,长长的堤岸上长满芳香的青草，
  4. 隐隐笙歌处处随and faint music and songs follow the boat everywhere.隐隐约约的乐声歌声，到处跟着小船。
  5. 无风水面琉璃滑There is no wind, and the water is smooth as glass;没有风，水面像琉璃一样光滑，
  6. 不觉船移I do not even feel the boat moving.感觉不到船在移动，
  7. 微动涟漪Only small ripples spread out softly,只有细细的波纹轻轻荡开，
  8. 惊起沙禽掠岸飞startling the water birds on the sand, which fly off skimming the shore.惊起了沙滩上的水鸟，擦着湖岸飞走了。
02

Words字词注释

轻舟qīng zhōu
qīng zhōu: a light little boat.轻舟：轻快的小船。
短棹duǎn zhào
duǎn zhào: a short oar. Zhào is an oar for rowing a boat.短棹：短短的船桨。棹，划船用的桨。
西湖xī hú
xī hú: West Lake. Here it is the West Lake of Yingzhou, in today's Fuyang, Anhui, not the famous one in Hangzhou.西湖：这里指颍州西湖，在今安徽阜阳，不是杭州西湖。
逶迤wēi yí
wēi yí: winding and going on and on.逶迤：弯弯曲曲、连绵不断的样子。
芳草fāng cǎo
fāng cǎo: sweet-smelling green grass.芳草：散发香气的青草。
长堤cháng dī
cháng dī: a long bank of earth by the lake that people can walk on.长堤：湖边长长的土坝，可以在上面走路。
隐隐yǐn yǐn
yǐn yǐn: faintly, not quite clear.隐隐：隐隐约约，听得不太清楚。
笙歌shēng gē
shēng gē: music and singing. Shēng is a mouth organ made of bamboo pipes.笙歌：音乐和歌声。笙，一种用竹管做的乐器。
处处随chù chù suí
chù chù suí: follows everywhere. Many people are out on the lake, so music can be heard wherever the boat goes.处处随：到哪里都跟着。说的是游湖的人很多，到处都能听到乐声。
琉璃liú lí
liú lí: a shiny glass-like material. Here it means the water is smooth and bright like glass.琉璃：一种光亮的玻璃一样的材料。这里形容水面又平又亮。
不觉bù jué
bù jué: without noticing.不觉：没有察觉，感觉不到。
涟漪lián yī
lián yī: tiny ripples on water.涟漪：细小的水波。
沙禽shā qín
shā qín: water birds that live on the sandy shore.沙禽：在沙滩上生活的水鸟。
掠岸飞lüè àn fēi
lüè àn fēi: fly skimming past the bank. Lüè means to brush past lightly.掠岸飞：擦着湖岸飞过去。掠，轻轻擦过。
03

How to read it读音

迤yí
In the word wēi yí (winding), this character is read yí. Used alone to mean "toward", it is read yǐ.“迤”在“逶迤”里读 yí。单用表示“向、往”时读 yǐ。
04

Background创作背景

Ouyang Xiu loved the West Lake of Yingzhou (today's Fuyang, Anhui). He had served there as an official when younger and wanted to spend his old age there. In 1071 he retired and moved to Yingzhou. He wrote ten lyrics to the tune Cai Sang Zi. The first line of each ends with "West Lake is lovely", and each praises the lake at a different time. They are usually thought to be written in his last years in Yingzhou. This is the first of the ten.

欧阳修很喜欢颍州（今安徽阜阳）的西湖。他早年在颍州做过官，就想以后在这里养老。宋神宗熙宁四年（1071年），他辞官退休，住到颍州。他写了十首《采桑子》，每首第一句都以“西湖好”结尾，夸西湖不同时候的美景。一般认为这组词是他晚年在颍州写的，这是第一首。

05

The poet作者

Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072), called the Old Drunkard and later the Retired Scholar Six-One, was a Northern Song writer and one of the Eight Great Prose Masters of Tang and Song. He held high office and helped many young talents, such as Su Shi. His lyrics are fresh and natural, often about scenery and everyday feelings. In old age he lived quietly in Yingzhou, and this poem carries that calm, easy mood.

欧阳修（1007—1072），号醉翁，晚年又号六一居士，北宋文学家，“唐宋八大家”之一。他做过大官，还提拔过苏轼等很多人才。他的词清新自然，常写山水风景和日常心情。晚年退居颍州，过着悠闲的日子，这首词就带着那种轻松安静的心情。

06

The mood意境

This lyric is like a camera moving slowly. The first stanza shows the wide view: the little boat, the green water, the grassy bank, and music drifting from far away. It is lively but relaxed. The second stanza comes close, and grows quieter and quieter. There is no wind. The water is smooth as glass, and you cannot feel the boat move. Only a few ripples spread from the boat and startle some birds, which fly off along the shore. That one startle and flight makes the still picture move for a moment, and the lake seems even calmer. As you read, picture yourself sitting in the boat, drifting slowly.

这首词像一段慢慢移动的镜头。上阕写远景：小船、绿水、芳草长堤，还有远远传来的乐声，热闹又悠闲。下阕写近景，而且越来越安静：没有风，水面光得像玻璃，船走了都感觉不到。只有船边泛起一点点涟漪，惊起几只水鸟，贴着湖岸飞走。最后这一“惊”一“飞”，让静静的画面忽然动了一下，反而显得湖面更加安静。读的时候，可以想象自己正坐在船上，慢慢地漂。
