嫦娥

李商隐Li Shangyin · Tang唐

Chang'e

 

yún云mǔ母píng屏fēng风zhú烛yǐng影shēn深

cháng长hé河jiàn渐luò落xiǎo晓xīng星chén沉

The source text writes the goddess's name with an older form of the first character, in the title and the third line, and uses an older form of chen (to sink) in the second line. Today's textbooks use the modern forms, which are used here.

底本题目和第三句写作“常娥”，第二句写作“晓星沈”。“常娥”“沈”是古代的写法，现在课本和通行本写作“嫦娥”“晓星沉”，这里按课本。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 云母屏风烛影深On the mica screen the candle's shadows grow deeper and darker.云母屏风上，烛光越来越暗，影子越来越深，
  2. 长河渐落晓星沉The Milky Way slowly sinks, and the morning star goes down.银河渐渐西斜，启明星也慢慢沉落了。
  3. 嫦娥应悔偷灵药Chang'e must regret stealing the magic medicine,嫦娥想必后悔偷吃了长生不老药，
  4. 碧海青天夜夜心for now she faces only the sea-blue sky, lonely night after night.如今只能面对碧海一样的青天，夜夜孤单。
02

Words字词注释

云母屏风yún mǔ píng fēng
yún mǔ píng fēng: a screen decorated with mica. Mica is a shiny mineral that splits into thin sheets. A screen is a piece of furniture that blocks drafts and divides a room.云母屏风：用云母装饰的屏风。云母是一种亮晶晶的矿石，可以剥成薄片。屏风是放在屋里挡风、隔开空间的家具。
烛影深zhú yǐng shēn
zhú yǐng shēn: the candle is burning low, the light is dim, and the shadows are deep.烛影深：蜡烛快燃尽了，烛光暗淡，影子显得很深。
长河cháng hé
cháng hé: the long river, the Milky Way.长河：银河。
渐落jiàn luò
jiàn luò: slowly sinking. Near dawn the Milky Way seems to slide down the sky.渐落：慢慢地往下落。天快亮时，银河看起来渐渐西斜。
晓星xiǎo xīng
xiǎo xīng: the dawn star, the morning star.晓星：天快亮时的星星，指启明星。
沉chén
chén: to sink out of sight.沉：落下去，看不见了。
嫦娥cháng é
cháng é: Chang'e, the goddess of the moon in myth. The story says she secretly took her husband Hou Yi's elixir of eternal life and floated up to the moon.嫦娥：神话里住在月亮上的仙女。传说她偷吃了丈夫后羿的长生不老药，飞到了月宫。
应悔yīng huǐ
yīng huǐ: surely regrets.应悔：想必会后悔。
灵药líng yào
líng yào: the magic medicine of eternal life.灵药：长生不老的仙药。
碧海青天bì hǎi qīng tiān
bì hǎi qīng tiān: the blue sky, wide as the sea. Every day Chang'e faces only this endless sky.碧海青天：碧蓝如海的天空。嫦娥每天面对的只有这无边的天空。
夜夜心yè yè xīn
yè yè xīn: a heart that is lonely night after night.夜夜心：每一夜都孤单寂寞的心。
03

How to read it读音

悔huǐ
Here it is read yīng, meaning surely, must, not yìng (to answer).应，这里读 yīng，意思是应该、想必，不读 yìng。
04

Background创作背景

We do not know when this poem was written. The poet stays awake all night. He watches the candle burn down and the stars set, and he thinks of Chang'e on the moon. People have long read the poem in different ways. Some think it is simply about Chang'e's loneliness. One view says it uses Chang'e to write about a Daoist nun who left the world to seek immortality. Others think the poet is writing about himself: proud, lonely and not understood.

这首诗写作的时间不清楚。诗人一夜没睡，看着蜡烛燃尽、星星落下，想到了月亮上的嫦娥。这首诗的意思历来有不同的说法：有人认为就是写嫦娥的孤独；一说是借嫦娥写学道修仙的女道士；也有人认为是诗人借嫦娥写自己，清高孤独，不被人理解。

05

The poet作者

Li Shangyin (about 813–858) was a late Tang poet. His poems are rich in language and deep in feeling. He often used myths and old stories to speak of what was in his heart, so his meaning is veiled and can be read in many ways. His career never went well, and he often felt lonely and let down. Many readers feel that the loneliness in this poem is his own.

李商隐（约813—约858），晚唐诗人。他的诗语言华美，感情深细，常常借神话和典故来说心事，意思含蓄，可以有多种理解。他一生仕途不顺，心里常有孤独和失意，这首诗里的寂寞也让很多人想到他自己。

06

The mood意境

The first two lines show a long night. Indoors, the candlelight grows dimmer. Outside, the Milky Way tilts and the morning star sets. The whole night has passed and the poet has not slept. His loneliness needs no words; it hides in these slowly changing things. In the last two lines he looks up and thinks of Chang'e on the moon. She won eternal life, but must face the endless sky alone forever. The words "heart, night after night" stretch that loneliness out without end. Chang'e's loneliness and the poet's seem to mirror each other across the sky.

前两句写一个长夜：屋里烛光越来越暗，窗外银河斜了，晨星也落了。一夜过去，诗人还没有睡，这份孤独不用说出来，就藏在这些慢慢变化的景物里。后两句，诗人抬头想到月亮上的嫦娥：她得到了长生不老，却永远一个人面对无边的天空。“夜夜心”三个字，把这份寂寞拉得无限长。嫦娥的孤独和诗人的孤独，好像隔着天空互相照见。
