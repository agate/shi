宴清都·连理海棠

吴文英Wu Wenying · Song宋

Yan Qing Du: The Twin-Linked Crabapple Trees

 

xiù绣wò幄yuān鸳yāng鸯zhù柱

hóng红qíng情mì密

nì腻yún云dī低hù护qín秦shù树

fāng芳gēn根jiān兼yǐ倚

huā花shāo梢diàn钿hé合

jǐn锦píng屏rén人dù妒

dōng东fēng风shuì睡zú足jiāo交zhī枝

zhèng正mèng梦zhěn枕yáo瑶chāi钗yàn燕gǔ股

In the eighth line, the common edition reads "meng zhen" (dreaming on the pillow). We follow it here. The corpus text has "meng hua" (dreaming of flowers) instead.

第八句通行本作“正梦枕、瑶钗燕股”，这里依通行本。古籍库原文作“正梦花”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 绣幄鸳鸯柱The shade is like an embroidered tent; the trunks stand together like mandarin ducks.花荫像绣花的帐幕，两棵树干像一对鸳鸯靠在一起。
  2. 红情密How thick the red blossoms are,红花的情意多么浓密，
  3. 腻云低护秦树and soft cloud-like leaves bend low to shelter the two trees.像云一样柔润的绿叶低低护着这两棵海棠。
  4. 芳根兼倚Their fragrant roots lean on each other.它们的根互相依靠，
  5. 花梢钿合Their treetops meet like the two halves of a jewel box,树梢连在一起，像合起来的首饰盒，
  6. 锦屏人妒so the beauty behind the brocade screen is jealous.连锦屏后的美人都嫉妒。
  7. 东风睡足交枝In the east wind the twined branches seem to have slept their fill,东风里花枝交缠，像睡足了一样，
  8. 正梦枕瑶钗燕股like the two prongs of a jade hairpin joined in a dream on the pillow.正像枕上梦里，玉钗的两股相连。
  9. 障滟蜡满照欢丛Candles, shielded from the wind, shine full on the joyful flowers,人们护着明亮的蜡烛，把热闹的花丛照得通亮，
  10. 嫠蟾冷落羞度and the lonely moon, cold and forlorn, is too shy to pass by.孤单的月亮冷冷清清，不好意思从旁边经过。
  11. 人间万感幽单The world is full of loneliness,人世间有太多孤独冷清的感受，
  12. 华清惯浴but these flowers are like the royal lady who often bathed at Huaqing,可它们像当年常在华清池洗浴的贵妃，
  13. 春盎风露brimming with spring, bathed in wind and dew.满是春意，沐着风和露水。
  14. 连鬟并暖Hair beside hair, they keep each other warm,发髻相连，相依取暖，
  15. 同心共结tying one love knot together,一起打着同心结，
  16. 向承恩处in the days of being loved.正是受宠爱的时候。
  17. 凭谁为歌长恨Who now will sing the Song of Everlasting Regret for them?如今靠谁来为她们唱那曲《长恨歌》？
  18. 暗殿锁秋灯夜语A dark hall locks away their whispers by an autumn lamp.幽暗的宫殿里，锁着秋夜灯下的悄悄话。
  19. 叙旧期不负春盟Recalling their old promise, they keep their spring vow:说起旧日的约定，不辜负春天的誓言，
  20. 红朝翠暮red flowers at dawn, green leaves at dusk, together always.早上红花相伴，晚上绿叶相随，永远在一起。
02

Words字词注释

绣幄xiù wò
xiù wò: an embroidered tent; here the thick shade of the blossoms.绣幄：绣花的帐幕，这里比喻浓密的花荫。
鸳鸯柱yuān yāng zhù
yuān yāng zhù: trunks standing together like a pair of mandarin ducks. Mandarin ducks are water birds always seen in pairs.鸳鸯柱：像一对鸳鸯一样挨在一起的树干。鸳鸯是总成双成对的水鸟。
红情hóng qíng
hóng qíng: the feeling of the red flowers; the red blossoms are rich.红情：红花的情意，指红花开得浓。
腻云nì yún
nì yún: soft, glossy clouds; the thick green leaves.腻云：柔润的云，比喻浓密的绿叶。
秦树qín shù
qín shù: the two crabapple trees. Some say they are called this because crabapples from the Qin region, around today's Shaanxi, were the most famous.秦树：指这两棵海棠。一说海棠以秦地（今陕西一带）的最有名，所以这样叫。
芳根兼倚fāng gēn jiān yǐ
fāng gēn jiān yǐ: the roots of the two trees lean on each other.芳根兼倚：两棵树的根互相依靠。
钿合diàn hé
diàn hé: a gold-inlaid jewel box in two halves that close together. Legend says Emperor Xuanzong gave Yang Guifei a gold hairpin and such a box.钿合：镶金的首饰盒，分上下两半，能合在一起。传说唐玄宗曾送给杨贵妃金钗和钿合。
锦屏人妒jǐn píng rén dù
jǐn píng rén dù: even the beauty behind the brocade screen is jealous of them.锦屏人妒：连屏风后面的美人都嫉妒它们。
睡足shuì zú
shuì zú: has slept enough. Legend says Emperor Xuanzong called the tipsy Yang Guifei a crabapple that had not slept enough.睡足：睡够了。传说唐玄宗说杨贵妃醉后像“海棠睡未足”。
交枝jiāo zhī
jiāo zhī: branches twined together.交枝：枝条互相交缠。
瑶钗燕股yáo chāi yàn gǔ
yáo chāi yàn gǔ: a jade hairpin with two prongs like a swallow's tail; a picture of the two joined branches.瑶钗燕股：玉钗的两股像燕子的尾巴，比喻两枝相连。
障滟蜡zhàng yàn là
zhàng yàn là: shield the bright candles from the wind. Yàn means shimmering.障滟蜡：挡住风，护着明亮的蜡烛。滟，光闪闪的样子。
欢丛huān cóng
huān cóng: the cheerful mass of flowers.欢丛：开得热闹欢喜的花丛。
嫠蟾lí chán
lí chán: the lonely moon. Lí means a widow; chán, the toad said to live in the moon, stands for the moon.嫠蟾：孤单的月亮。嫠，寡妇；蟾，传说月中有蟾蜍，代指月亮。
羞度xiū dù
xiū dù: too shy to pass by.羞度：不好意思从这里经过。
幽单yōu dān
yōu dān: lonely and cold.幽单：孤独冷清。
华清惯浴huá qīng guàn yù
huá qīng guàn yù: often bathing at Huaqing. Huaqing was the Tang royal hot spring, where the Song of Everlasting Regret says Yang Guifei bathed.华清惯浴：常在华清池洗浴。华清池是唐代皇家温泉，《长恨歌》写杨贵妃“春寒赐浴华清池”。
春盎chūn àng
chūn àng: full of spring. Àng means brimming.春盎：春意很浓。盎，满。
连鬟并暖lián huán bìng nuǎn
lián huán bìng nuǎn: two heads of hair side by side, warm together.连鬟并暖：两人的发髻挨在一起，相依取暖。
同心tóng xīn
tóng xīn: a love knot tied from silk ribbon, a sign of love.同心：同心结，用丝带打的结，表示相爱。
承恩chéng ēn
chéng ēn: receiving the emperor's love and favour.承恩：受到皇帝的宠爱。
长恨cháng hèn
cháng hèn: the Song of Everlasting Regret by Bai Juyi, about the sad love of Xuanzong and Yang Guifei.长恨：指白居易的《长恨歌》，写唐玄宗和杨贵妃的爱情悲剧。
暗殿锁秋灯夜语àn diàn suǒ qiū dēng yè yǔ
àn diàn suǒ qiū dēng yè yǔ: whispers by lamplight on an autumn night in a dark palace hall. The Song of Everlasting Regret tells of their midnight whispers in the Hall of Long Life.暗殿锁秋灯夜语：幽暗的宫殿里，秋夜灯下的悄悄话。《长恨歌》写他们七月七日在长生殿夜半私语。
叙旧期xù jiù qī
xù jiù qī: speaking of their old promise.叙旧期：说起过去的约定。
春盟chūn méng
chūn méng: a vow made in spring.春盟：春天里许下的誓言。
红朝翠暮hóng zhāo cuì mù
hóng zhāo cuì mù: red flowers by morning, green leaves by evening; together every morning and evening, for ever.红朝翠暮：早上看红花，晚上看绿叶，指朝朝暮暮永远相伴。
03

How to read it读音

钿diàn
In diàn hé, a jewel box inlaid with gold, this character is read diàn.“钿合”是镶着金花的首饰盒，“钿”读 diàn。
为wèi
In wèi gē, to sing for them, this character is read wèi.“为歌”是替他们唱，“为”读 wèi。
朝zhāo
Here this character means morning and is read zhāo.“朝”在这里是早晨，读 zhāo。
04

Background创作背景

Two trees are called lianli when their branches grow together. People of old used them as a picture of a loving couple. This ci is about two crabapple trees joined in this way. It draws on many stories of Emperor Xuanzong of Tang and his beloved Yang Guifei. Legend says the emperor once compared the sleepy Yang Guifei to a crabapple flower, and Bai Juyi's "Song of Everlasting Regret" tells of their love. We do not know when or where this ci was written. Some think it also hides the poet's longing for a lost love of his own.

“连理”是说两棵树的枝干长到了一起，古人拿它比喻相爱的夫妻。这首词写的是两棵连在一起的海棠花。词里用了很多唐玄宗和杨贵妃的故事：传说唐玄宗曾把醉后没睡醒的杨贵妃比作海棠，白居易的《长恨歌》也写了他们的爱情。这首词写于何时何地，已经说不清。一说词里也藏着作者对自己旧日恋人的怀念。

05

The poet作者

Wu Wenying (about 1200 – about 1260), also called Mengchuang, came from Siming, today's Ningbo in Zhejiang. He never held office and spent many years as an adviser in other men's households. His ci use rich, ornate words and hide their meaning deep. He often jumps from image to image, so his lines feel like a string of bright pictures and can be hard to follow.

吴文英（约1200—约1260），号梦窗，四明（今浙江宁波）人。他一生没有做官，长期在别人家里做幕客。他的词用字华丽，意思藏得很深，常常一句接一句地跳着写，读起来像一串串彩色的画面，比较难懂。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza is about the flowers. The shade is like an embroidered tent, and the joined trunks stand like a pair of mandarin ducks. The red blossoms are thick, and leaves like clouds bend low to shelter them. Root leans on root and twig joins twig, like the two halves of a jewel box, so that even the beauty behind the screen feels jealous. In the east wind the branches twine like a sleeper who has slept enough. At night people light candles to shine on the flowers, and the lonely moon is too shy to pass by. The second stanza turns to Yang Guifei. She bathed at Huaqing, her hair lay side by side with the emperor's, and their hearts were tied in one knot. That was when she was most loved. But who will sing the Song of Everlasting Regret? In a dark hall on an autumn night they whispered a vow never to part. The last line, "red at dawn, green at dusk", wishes the trees to stay together like red flowers by morning and green leaves by evening, for ever. The whole ci turns two trees into a pair of lovers. It is beautiful, with a touch of sadness.

上片写花。绣花帐子一样的花荫，挨在一起的树干像一对鸳鸯；红花浓密，绿叶像云一样低低护着。两棵树根靠根、枝连枝，就像能合在一起的首饰盒，连屏风后的美人都嫉妒。东风里花枝交缠，像睡足了的人；夜里人们点亮蜡烛照花，孤单的月亮都不好意思经过。下片转到杨贵妃：华清池沐浴，鬓发相连，同心结在一起，那是最受宠爱的时候。可是谁来唱那首《长恨歌》呢？秋夜深殿里的悄悄话，许下了一辈子不分开的誓言。最后一句“红朝翠暮”，愿它们像早上的红花和晚上的绿叶一样，永远相伴。整首词把花写成了一对恋人，美丽里带着一点哀愁。
