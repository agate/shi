Frost Leaves Flying: The Double Ninth

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 断烟离绪Wisps of broken mist, like the sad thoughts left after parting.一缕缕断续的烟雾，像我分别后的愁思。
  2. 关心事They stir up old memories.勾起心里的往事。
  3. 斜阳红隐霜树The red of the setting sun hides among frost-reddened trees.夕阳的红光，隐在经霜的红树里。
  4. 半壶秋水荐黄花With half a jug of clear water I offer up chrysanthemums,我用半壶清水供着菊花，
  5. 香噀西风雨and their scent is sprayed about by the west wind and rain.菊香被西风秋雨吹散，四处飘洒。
  6. 纵玉勒轻飞迅羽Others ride out with loose reins and let their swift hawks fly.别人纵马放鹰，去郊外游猎。
  7. 凄凉谁吊荒台古But who will go to the lonely old terrace to remember the past?可是谁会去凄凉的古台，怀念从前的人呢？
  8. 记醉蹋南屏I remember walking drunk on Nanping Hill,还记得那年我醉了，走在南屏山上，
  9. 彩扇咽寒蝉倦梦her song choked off behind the painted fan, the autumn cicadas faint, and I was drowsy,她的歌声在扇子后面停住，秋蝉低鸣，我困倦欲睡，
  10. 不知蛮素and I hardly noticed the woman I loved beside me.竟没有留意身边心爱的人。
  11. 聊对旧节传杯Now I can only face the old festival and pass the cup.如今只好对着老节日，随便喝几杯酒。
  12. 尘笺蠹管My paper is dusty, my brush eaten by worms,信纸落满灰尘，毛笔被虫蛀了，
  13. 断阕经岁慵赋and the unfinished lyric has waited a year; I am too weary to write.没写完的词，一年了也懒得写下去。
  14. 小蟾斜影转东篱The slanting light of the thin moon moves across the eastern fence,一弯新月的斜影移过东边种菊花的篱笆，
  15. 夜冷残蛩语the night is cold, and a few last crickets murmur.夜晚很冷，只剩几只蟋蟀还在低声叫着。
  16. 早白发缘愁万缕My hair turned white early, from ten thousand strands of sorrow.我早早白了头发，是因为有千丝万缕的愁。
  17. 惊飙从卷乌纱去Let the sudden wind carry off my black hat.任凭狂风把我的帽子卷走吧。
  18. 漫细将茱萸看I just look closely at the dogwood in my hand,我姑且仔细地看看手里的茱萸，
  19. 但约明年and hope only to promise that next year只盼着约好明年，
  20. 翠微高处we will climb high again to the green hills.还到那青山高处去登高。
02

Words字词注释

断烟duàn yān
duàn yān: wisps of mist that break and drift.断烟：一缕一缕、时断时续的烟。
离绪lí xù
lí xù: the sad thoughts left after parting.离绪：分别以后的愁思。
关心事guān xīn shì
guān xīn shì: old memories that tug at the heart.关心事：牵动心里的往事。
霜树shuāng shù
shuāng shù: trees touched by frost, their leaves turned red.霜树：经过霜打、叶子变红的树。
荐jiàn
jiàn: to offer up.荐：献上、供奉。
黄花huáng huā
huáng huā: chrysanthemums, which people admire at the Double Ninth.黄花：菊花。重阳节有赏菊的习俗。
噀xùn
xùn: to spray.噀：喷洒。
纵zòng
zòng: to let loose.纵：放开，任由。
玉勒yù lè
yù lè: a jade-trimmed bridle; here, riding horses.玉勒：装饰着玉的马笼头，这里指骑马。
迅羽xùn yǔ
xùn yǔ: swift wings; the hawks used in hunting.迅羽：飞得很快的鸟，指打猎用的鹰。
吊diào
diào: to visit an old site and remember the past.吊：凭吊，到古迹前怀念古人。
荒台huāng tái
huāng tái: a ruined terrace. It is usually taken as the Horse-Riding Terrace, where people of old climbed on the Double Ninth.荒台：荒废的高台。一般认为指戏马台，古人重阳节登高的地方。
蹋tà
tà: the same as "to tread", to walk.蹋：同“踏”，走。
南屏nán píng
nán píng: Nanping Hill, by West Lake in Hangzhou.南屏：南屏山，在杭州西湖边。
彩扇cǎi shàn
cǎi shàn: the painted fan a singing girl held; here, the singer herself.彩扇：歌女跳舞唱歌时拿的彩色扇子，这里指唱歌的女子。
寒蝉倦梦hán chán juàn mèng
hán chán juàn mèng: autumn cicadas fading, and the poet drowsy with wine.寒蝉倦梦：秋天的蝉声低弱，人醉得昏昏欲睡。
蛮素mán sù
mán sù: the Tang poet Bai Juyi had two singing girls, Xiaoman and Fansu. Here the names stand for the woman the poet loved.蛮素：唐代诗人白居易有两位歌女，叫小蛮和樊素。这里借指诗人心爱的女子。
聊liáo
liáo: just, for now.聊：姑且，随便。
旧节jiù jié
jiù jié: the old festival that comes every year, here the Double Ninth.旧节：年年都有的老节日，这里指重阳节。
传杯chuán bēi
chuán bēi: passing the wine cup around.传杯：互相传着酒杯喝酒。
尘笺chén jiān
chén jiān: writing paper covered in dust.尘笺：落满灰尘的信纸。
蠹管dù guǎn
dù guǎn: a writing brush eaten by insects. Dù is a bookworm.蠹管：被虫子蛀了的毛笔。蠹，蛀虫。
断阕duàn què
duàn què: a lyric left unfinished.断阕：没写完的词。
慵赋yōng fù
yōng fù: too weary to write. Yōng means listless.慵赋：懒得再写。慵，懒。
小蟾xiǎo chán
xiǎo chán: a thin new moon. Legend puts a toad in the moon, so the toad stands for the moon.小蟾：弯弯的新月。传说月亮里有蟾蜍，所以用蟾代指月亮。
东篱dōng lí
dōng lí: the eastern fence where chrysanthemums grow, from Tao Yuanming's line about picking them there.东篱：东边的篱笆，种菊花的地方。陶渊明有诗“采菊东篱下”。
残蛩cán qióng
cán qióng: crickets of late autumn, their chirping almost gone.残蛩：深秋快要叫不动的蟋蟀。
缘yuán
yuán: because of.缘：因为。
惊飙jīng biāo
jīng biāo: a sudden gust of wind.惊飙：突然刮起的狂风。
从cóng
cóng: let it, never mind.从：任凭，随它。
乌纱wū shā
wū shā: a black gauze hat. An old story tells how Meng Jia lost his hat to the wind on a Double Ninth climb and did not even notice.乌纱：黑色的纱帽。古代有个故事：晋朝的孟嘉在重阳节登山，风把他的帽子吹掉了，他也不在意。
漫màn
màn: idly, just.漫：随意，姑且。
细将xì jiāng
xì jiāng: holding carefully.细将：仔细地拿着。
茱萸zhū yú
zhū yú: a fragrant plant that people wore on the Double Ninth to keep away harm.茱萸：一种有香气的植物。重阳节人们插茱萸，说可以避邪。
约yuē
yuē: to make a promise to meet.约：约定。
翠微cuì wēi
cuì wēi: green mountain slopes, here the hills climbed on the Double Ninth.翠微：青翠的山腰，这里指重阳登高的山。
03

How to read it读音

勒lè
Here this character is read lè; yù lè is a jade-trimmed bridle. It is read lēi only when it means to tie tight.勒在这里读 lè，玉勒是马笼头。用绳子捆紧的“勒紧”才读 lēi。
咽yè
Here this character is read yè, a voice choking off, not yàn, to swallow.咽在这里读 yè，是声音堵住、哽咽的意思，不读吞咽的 yàn。
看kàn
Usually read kàn today. The line borrows from Du Fu's "drunk, I hold the dogwood and look at it closely", where this character rhymes and is read kān.现在一般读 kàn。这句化用杜甫的诗“醉把茱萸仔细看”，在杜甫诗里“看”押平声韵，读 kān。
04

Background创作背景

The Double Ninth is the festival on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month. People used to climb hills, admire chrysanthemums, wear dogwood and drink wine that day. Wu Wenying wrote this lyric on that festival. He remembers drinking on Nanping Hill in Hangzhou with a woman he loved. She is usually taken to be a lover who had died or left him, but who she was, and which year he wrote this, are not known.

重九就是农历九月初九重阳节。古人这一天要登高、赏菊、插茱萸、喝酒。这首词是吴文英在重阳节写的，回忆起从前和一位心爱的女子在杭州南屏山喝酒的情景。一般认为词里怀念的是他已经去世或已经分开的爱人，但她是谁、这首词写在哪一年，都说不清楚。

05

The poet作者

Wu Wenying (about 1200–about 1260), also called Mengchuang, was a Southern Song ci poet. He never held high office and often lived as a guest of officials around Suzhou and Hangzhou. His lyrics use rich words and jump from image to image like a dream, so they are not easy to follow. Many of them mourn a lost love, and this is one.

吴文英（约1200—约1260），号梦窗，南宋词人。他一辈子没有做大官，常在苏州、杭州一带给官员做门客。他的词用字华丽，意思跳来跳去，像梦一样，不太好懂。他写了很多怀念旧日爱人的词，这首也是其中之一。

06

The mood意境

This lyric sets the noise of a festival beside a cold, quiet heart. Others ride out to hunt and climb on the Double Ninth. The poet only sets chrysanthemums in half a jug of water and stares at the sunset and red leaves. Late in the first stanza he suddenly jumps back in time. That year on Nanping Hill he was drunk, she sang behind her painted fan, and he was too dazed to treasure the moment. The second stanza returns to now. His brush is broken, his paper dusty, and a lyric has gone unfinished for a year. At night there are only the new moon, the fence and a few fading crickets. He says sorrow turned his hair white, and he does not care if the wind steals his hat. At last, dogwood in hand, he can only hope to climb again next year. Will she be there? The lyric does not say, and leaves an empty ache behind.

这首词把节日的热闹和心里的冷清放在一起。别人在重阳节骑马打猎、登高游玩，诗人却只供着半壶水里的菊花，看着夕阳和红叶发呆。上片后半突然跳回过去：那年在南屏山上喝醉了，她拿着彩扇唱歌，他却迷迷糊糊没有珍惜。下片回到现在：笔也坏了，纸也脏了，词一年都写不完；夜里只有新月、篱笆和快没声音的蟋蟀。他说头发是愁白的，帽子被风吹走也不管了。最后他拿着茱萸，只能盼望明年再去登高。可那时她还会在吗？词没有说，留下一片空空的伤感。
