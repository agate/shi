No. 43 · Tang poem · old-style song with lines of mixed length第 43 首 · 唐诗 · 杂言古体诗（歌行）

mèng梦yóu游tiān天mǔ姥yín吟liú留bié别

lǐ李bái白Li Bai · Tang唐

A Dream Journey to Mount Tianmu: A Farewell Song

 

hǎi海kè客tán谈yíng瀛zhōu洲

yān烟tāo涛wēi微máng茫xìn信nán难qiú求

yuè越rén人yǔ语tiān天mǔ姥

yún云xiá霞míng明miè灭huò或kě可dǔ睹

tiān天mǔ姥lián连tiān天xiàng向tiān天héng横

shì势bá拔wǔ五yuè岳yǎn掩chì赤chéng城

tiān天tāi台sì四wàn万bā八qiān千zhàng丈

duì对cǐ此yù欲dǎo倒dōng东nán南qīng倾

wǒ我yù欲yīn因zhī之mèng梦wú吴yuè越

yí一yè夜fēi飞dù度jìng镜hú湖yuè月

hú湖yuè月zhào照wǒ我yǐng影

sòng送wǒ我zhì至shàn剡xī溪

xiè谢gōng公sù宿chù处jīn今shàng尚zài在

lù渌shuǐ水dàng荡yàng漾qīng清yuán猿tí啼

jiǎo脚zhuó著xiè谢gōng公jī屐

shēn身dēng登qīng青yún云tī梯

bàn半bì壁jiàn见hǎi海rì日

kōng空zhōng中wén闻tiān天jī鸡

qiān千yán岩wàn万zhuǎn转lù路bú不dìng定

mí迷huā花yǐ倚shí石hū忽yǐ已míng暝

xióng熊páo咆lóng龙yín吟yǐn殷yán岩quán泉

lì栗shēn深lín林xī兮jīng惊céng层diān巅

yún云qīng青qīng青xī兮yù欲yǔ雨

shuǐ水dàn澹dàn澹xī兮shēng生yān烟

liè列quē缺pī霹lì雳

qiū丘luán峦bēng崩cuī摧

dòng洞tiān天shí石fēi扉

hōng訇rán然zhōng中kāi开

qīng青míng冥hào浩dàng荡bú不jiàn见dǐ底

rì日yuè月zhào照yào耀jīn金yín银tái台

ní霓wéi为yī衣xī兮fēng风wéi为mǎ马

yún云zhī之jūn君xī兮fēn纷fēn纷ér而lái来xià下

hǔ虎gǔ鼓sè瑟xī兮luán鸾huí回chē车

xiān仙zhī之rén人xī兮liè列rú如má麻

hū忽hún魂jì悸yǐ以pò魄dòng动

huǎng恍jīng惊qǐ起ér而cháng长jiē嗟

wéi惟jué觉shí时zhī之zhěn枕xí席

shī失xiàng向lái来zhī之yān烟xiá霞

shì世jiān间xíng行lè乐yì亦rú如cǐ此

gǔ古lái来wàn万shì事dōng东liú流shuǐ水

bié别jūn君qù去xī兮hé何shí时huán还

qiě且fàng放bái白lù鹿qīng青yá崖jiān间

xū须xíng行jí即qí骑fǎng访míng名shān山

ān安néng能cuī摧méi眉zhé折yāo腰shì事quán权guì贵

shǐ使wǒ我bù不dé得kāi开xīn心yán颜

This is the text taught in schools today. Old collections differ in a few places. Yún xiá (“rosy clouds”) appears as yún ní (“rainbow clouds”). Shí fēi (“stone door”) appears as shí shàn (“stone door leaf”). Bié jūn qù xī (“I leave you, oh”) appears as bié jūn qù shí (“when I leave you”). A few characters are written in older forms. The poem is also titled “Dreaming of Mount Tianmu, a Farewell to Friends in Eastern Lu”.

这里用的是现在课本里的通行文字。古籍收录的文字有几处不同：“云霞明灭”一作“云霓明灭”；“洞天石扉”一作“洞天石扇”；“别君去兮何时还”一作“别君去时何时还”。“睹”“猿”“恍”在古籍里写作“覩”“猨”“怳”。这首诗又题作《梦游天姥山别东鲁诸公》。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 海客谈瀛洲Seafarers talk of the island of Yingzhou,航海的人谈起瀛洲，
  2. 烟涛微茫信难求hidden in misty waves, truly hard to find.它藏在迷蒙的烟波里，实在难以找到。
  3. 越人语天姥The people of Yue talk of Mount Tianmu,越地的人说起天姥山，
  4. 云霞明灭或可睹glimpsed now and then through bright and fading clouds.在忽明忽暗的云霞里，也许能看见。
  5. 天姥连天向天横Mount Tianmu reaches the sky and stretches across it,天姥山高耸入云，横在天边，
  6. 势拔五岳掩赤城higher than the Five Great Mountains, towering over Chicheng.山势超过五岳，压倒了赤城山。
  7. 天台四万八千丈Mount Tiantai, forty-eight thousand zhang tall,天台山虽然有四万八千丈高，
  8. 对此欲倒东南倾seems to bow before it and lean away to the southeast.对着它也像要拜倒一样，向东南倾斜。
  9. 我欲因之梦吴越Following these words, I travel in a dream to Wu and Yue,我根据越人的话，梦游吴越，
  10. 一夜飞度镜湖月and in one night fly across Mirror Lake under the moon.一夜之间飞过了月光下的镜湖。
  11. 湖月照我影The moon on the lake lights up my shadow湖上的月光照着我的影子，
  12. 送我至剡溪and carries me all the way to the Shan Stream.一直把我送到剡溪。
  13. 谢公宿处今尚在The place where Xie Lingyun stayed is still there,谢灵运住过的地方如今还在，
  14. 渌水荡漾清猿啼clear water rippling, apes calling in clear voices.清水荡漾，猿声清亮。
  15. 脚著谢公屐On my feet I wear Xie's wooden hiking shoes,我脚穿谢公的木屐，
  16. 身登青云梯and I climb stone steps into the blue clouds.登上高入云霄的石阶。
  17. 半壁见海日Halfway up I see the sun rise from the sea,到了半山腰，就看见海上升起的太阳，
  18. 空中闻天鸡and in midair I hear the heavenly rooster crow.在半空中听见天鸡的啼叫。
  19. 千岩万转路不定The path winds among a thousand cliffs, never going one way;山路在千万座山岩间回转，没有定向，
  20. 迷花倚石忽已暝charmed by flowers, resting on rocks, I find it is suddenly dark.我迷恋山花，靠着山石，不知不觉天已经黑了。
  21. 熊咆龙吟殷岩泉Bears roar and dragons drone, booming through rocks and springs,熊在怒吼，龙在长鸣，声音震响着山岩和泉水，
  22. 栗深林兮惊层巅making the deep woods tremble and the high peaks shake.让深林颤抖，让层层山峰惊动。
  23. 云青青兮欲雨The clouds hang dark, about to rain;乌云黑沉沉的，就要下雨了，
  24. 水澹澹兮生烟the water ripples softly and gives off mist.水波轻轻起伏，升起了水汽。
  25. 列缺霹雳Lightning flashes and thunder cracks,电光闪闪，雷声隆隆，
  26. 丘峦崩摧hills and peaks crumble.山峰都崩塌了。
  27. 洞天石扉The stone door of the immortals' cave神仙洞府的石门，
  28. 訇然中开bursts open from the middle with a boom.“轰”的一声从中间打开了。
  29. 青冥浩荡不见底Inside, the blue sky stretches endlessly, with no bottom in sight,里面是一片青色的天空，宽广得看不到底，
  30. 日月照耀金银台and the sun and moon shine on palaces of gold and silver.日月照耀着金银筑成的宫殿。
  31. 霓为衣兮风为马With rainbows for clothes and the wind for horses,仙人们用彩虹做衣裳，把风当马骑，
  32. 云之君兮纷纷而来下the lords of the clouds come down one after another.云中的神仙一个接一个地降落下来。
  33. 虎鼓瑟兮鸾回车Tigers play the zither and phoenixes draw their carriages,老虎弹着瑟，鸾鸟拉着车，
  34. 仙之人兮列如麻and the immortals stand in rows as thick as hemp.仙人们排成行，密密麻麻。
  35. 忽魂悸以魄动Suddenly my spirit jumps in fright,忽然我心惊魂动，
  36. 恍惊起而长嗟I wake with a start and let out a long sigh.猛地醒来坐起，长长叹了一口气。
  37. 惟觉时之枕席Waking, I find only my pillow and mat;醒来时只有枕头和席子，
  38. 失向来之烟霞the misty fairyland of a moment ago is gone.刚才的云霞仙境全都不见了。
  39. 世间行乐亦如此The pleasures of this world are just like that,人世间的欢乐也像这梦一样，
  40. 古来万事东流水and since ancient times all things flow away like water going east.自古以来，万事都像东流的水，一去不回。
  41. 别君去兮何时还I say goodbye and leave you; when will I return?告别你们离开，什么时候才回来呢？
  42. 且放白鹿青崖间For now I will let a white deer roam among the green cliffs,暂且把白鹿放在青山之间，
  43. 须行即骑访名山and when I need to go, I will ride it to visit famous mountains.要走的时候就骑上它去拜访名山。
  44. 安能摧眉折腰事权贵How could I bow my head and bend my back to serve the powerful,我怎么能低头弯腰去伺候有权有势的人，
  45. 使我不得开心颜and never be happy at heart again!让自己不能开心快乐呢！
02

Words字词注释

海客hǎi kè
hǎi kè: seafarers, people who sail the sea and have heard of faraway things.海客：在海上航行的人，见过很多远方的事。
瀛洲yíng zhōu
yíng zhōu: a magic island of immortals said to lie in the Eastern Sea, one of the Three Divine Mountains with Penglai and Fangzhang.瀛洲：传说中东海里的一座仙山，和蓬莱、方丈合称三神山。
烟涛yān tāo
yān tāo: misty, smoke-like waves.烟涛：像烟雾一样迷蒙的波涛。
微茫wēi máng
wēi máng: dim and blurry.微茫：模模糊糊，看不清楚。
信难求xìn nán qiú
xìn nán qiú: truly hard to find. Xìn means truly.信难求：实在难以找到。信，确实。
越人yuè rén
yuè rén: people of Yue, the region of today's Zhejiang.越人：越地的人。越地就是今天的浙江一带。
语yǔ
yǔ: to talk about.语：说起。
天姥tiān mǔ
tiān mǔ: Mount Tianmu, east of today's Xinchang County in Zhejiang. Legend says climbers could hear a heavenly old woman singing, hence the name.天姥：天姥山，在今浙江新昌县东。相传登山的人能听到仙女（天姥）唱歌，所以叫这个名字。姥，读 mǔ。
明灭míng miè
míng miè: now bright, now dim.明灭：一会儿亮，一会儿暗。
或可睹huò kě dǔ
huò kě dǔ: may perhaps be seen. Huò means perhaps; dǔ means to see.或可睹：也许能看到。或，也许；睹，看见。
连天lián tiān
lián tiān: joined to the sky, meaning extremely high.连天：和天连在一起，形容极高。
向天横xiàng tiān héng
xiàng tiān héng: stretching across the sky. Héng means to lie across.向天横：向天边伸展开去，挡住了天空。横，横着伸展。
势拔五岳shì bá wǔ yuè
shì bá wǔ yuè: its height rises above the Five Great Mountains. Bá means to rise above; the Five Great Mountains are Tai, Hua, Heng (in Hunan), Heng (in Shanxi) and Song.势拔五岳：山势超过了五岳。拔，超出；五岳，泰山、华山、衡山、恒山、嵩山五座名山。
掩yǎn
yǎn: to overshadow.掩：遮盖，压倒。
赤城chì chéng
chì chéng: Mount Chicheng, north of today's Tiantai County in Zhejiang, known for its red rocks.赤城：赤城山，在今浙江天台县北，山上的土石是红色的。
天台tiān tāi
tiān tāi: Mount Tiantai, north of today's Tiantai County in Zhejiang.天台：天台山，在今浙江天台县北。这里“台”读 tāi。
四万八千丈sì wàn bā qiān zhàng
sì wàn bā qiān zhàng: an exaggeration, meaning very, very high.四万八千丈：夸张的说法，形容非常高。
对此欲倒东南倾duì cǐ yù dǎo dōng nán qīng
duì cǐ yù dǎo dōng nán qīng: facing Mount Tianmu, it seems ready to bow down and lean away to the southeast. Cǐ refers to Mount Tianmu.对此欲倒东南倾：对着天姥山，好像要拜倒在它脚下，向东南方向倾斜。此，指天姥山。
因之yīn zhī
yīn zhī: because of what the people of Yue said. Yīn means based on; zhī refers to their words.因之：根据越人说的话。因，依据；之，指越人的话。
梦吴越mèng wú yuè
mèng wú yuè: to travel in a dream to Wu and Yue, around today's Jiangsu and Zhejiang.梦吴越：在梦里游吴越一带。吴越，今江苏、浙江一带。
度dù
dù: to cross.度：越过。
镜湖jìng hú
jìng hú: Mirror Lake, also called Jian Lake, south of today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. Its water is as smooth as a mirror.镜湖：又叫鉴湖，在今浙江绍兴南。湖水像镜子一样平。
剡溪shàn xī
shàn xī: the Shan Stream, south of today's Shengzhou in Zhejiang.剡溪：水名，在今浙江嵊州南。剡，读 shàn。
谢公xiè gōng
xiè gōng: Xie Lingyun, a poet of the Southern Dynasties. When he visited Mount Tianmu, he stayed by the Shan Stream.谢公：南朝诗人谢灵运。他游天姥山时，曾在剡溪住过。
宿处sù chù
sù chù: the place where he stayed.宿处：住过的地方。
渌水lù shuǐ
lù shuǐ: clear water.渌水：清澈的水。
荡漾dàng yàng
dàng yàng: rippling gently.荡漾：水波一起一伏地晃动。
清猿啼qīng yuán tí
qīng yuán tí: apes calling in clear voices.清猿啼：猿猴发出清亮的叫声。
著zhuó
zhuó: to wear.著：穿。这里读 zhuó。
谢公屐xiè gōng jī
xiè gōng jī: Xie Lingyun's special wooden hiking shoes. The soles had removable spikes: he took off the front ones going uphill and the back ones going downhill, so he walked steadily.谢公屐：谢灵运特制的一种登山木鞋，鞋底装着可以拆下的齿。上山时去掉前齿，下山时去掉后齿，走起来更稳。
青云梯qīng yún tī
qīng yún tī: stone steps climbing high into the clouds.青云梯：高入云霄的石阶山路。
半壁bàn bì
bàn bì: halfway up the mountain.半壁：半山腰。
海日hǎi rì
hǎi rì: the sun rising from the sea.海日：从海上升起的太阳。
天鸡tiān jī
tiān jī: the heavenly rooster of myth. It lived in a great tree on a mountain in the southeast. When the sun first lit the tree, it crowed, and all the roosters in the world crowed after it.天鸡：神话里的鸡。传说东南有座桃都山，山上大树上住着天鸡，太阳一照到这棵树，天鸡就叫，天下的鸡都跟着叫。
千岩万转qiān yán wàn zhuǎn
qiān yán wàn zhuǎn: the path twisting among a thousand cliffs.千岩万转：山路在无数山岩间弯来转去。
路不定lù bú dìng
lù bù dìng: the way keeps changing direction.路不定：路的方向一直在变。
迷花倚石mí huā yǐ shí
mí huā yǐ shí: charmed by flowers, resting against a rock. Mí means to be enchanted.迷花倚石：被花迷住了，靠在石头上休息。迷，迷恋。
忽已暝hū yǐ míng
hū yǐ míng: suddenly it is already dark. Míng means dusk.忽已暝：不知不觉天已经黑了。暝，天色昏暗。
咆páo
páo: to roar.咆：怒吼。
吟yín
yín: to drone or hum long and low.吟：低沉地长鸣。
殷岩泉yǐn yán quán
yǐn yán quán: the sound booms like thunder among the rocks and springs. Yǐn describes the rumble of thunder.殷岩泉：声音在山岩和泉水间像雷一样轰响。殷，这里读 yǐn，形容雷声。
栗深林兮惊层巅lì shēn lín xī jīng céng diān
lì shēn lín xī jīng céng diān: making the deep woods tremble and the layered peaks shake. Lì means to tremble; céng diān means peak after peak.栗深林兮惊层巅：使深林战栗，使层层山峰惊动。栗，发抖；层巅，层层叠叠的山顶。
兮xī
xī: a word for rhythm, like “oh”.兮：语气词，相当于“啊”。
青青qīng qīng
qīng qīng: dark and heavy.青青：黑沉沉的样子。
欲雨yù yǔ
yù yǔ: about to rain.欲雨：快要下雨。
澹澹dàn dàn
dàn dàn: gently rippling.澹澹：水波轻轻起伏的样子。
生烟shēng yān
shēng yān: giving off mist.生烟：升起水汽。
列缺liè quē
liè quē: lightning.列缺：闪电。
霹雳pī lì
pī lì: a thunderclap.霹雳：响雷。
丘峦崩摧qiū luán bēng cuī
qiū luán bēng cuī: hills and peaks crumble. Qiū luán means hills and peaks.丘峦崩摧：山峰崩塌。丘峦，山丘和山峰。
洞天dòng tiān
dòng tiān: a cave home of immortals, with a whole world inside.洞天：神仙住的洞府，洞里另有一片天地。
石扉shí fēi
shí fēi: a stone door. Fēi means a door.石扉：石门。扉，门扇。
訇然hōng rán
hōng rán: with a loud boom.訇然：形容很大的声音，“轰”的一声。
中开zhōng kāi
zhōng kāi: opening from the middle.中开：从中间打开。
青冥qīng míng
qīng míng: the blue sky.青冥：青色的天空。
浩荡hào dàng
hào dàng: vast and endless.浩荡：广阔无边的样子。
金银台jīn yín tái
jīn yín tái: palaces of gold and silver, where immortals live.金银台：金银筑成的宫阙，指神仙住的地方。
霓为衣ní wéi yī
ní wéi yī: rainbows for clothes. Ní is a kind of rainbow.霓为衣：用彩虹做衣裳。霓，彩虹的一种。
风为马fēng wéi mǎ
fēng wéi mǎ: the wind for horses.风为马：把风当马来骑。
云之君yún zhī jūn
yún zhī jūn: the lords of the clouds, gods who live in the clouds.云之君：云里的神仙。
纷纷fēn fēn
fēn fēn: one after another, in great numbers.纷纷：一个接一个，很多的样子。
来下lái xià
lái xià: to come down.来下：降落下来。
鼓瑟gǔ sè
gǔ sè: to play the sè, a stringed instrument like a zither.鼓瑟：弹瑟。瑟，一种像古筝的弦乐器。
鸾luán
luán: the luán, a magic bird like a phoenix.鸾：传说中像凤凰一样的神鸟。
回车huí chē
huí chē: to turn the carriage around.回车：拉着车子回转。
仙之人xiān zhī rén
xiān zhī rén: the immortals.仙之人：仙人们。

04

Background创作背景

In 742, Emperor Xuanzong called Li Bai to Chang'an to serve at court, writing for the emperor. But he could not stand the powerful officials, and they pushed him out. In less than three years he was sent away with a gift of gold. Afterwards he lived for a while in Eastern Lu, around today's Shandong. The poem is usually dated to 745 (another view says 746), when he was leaving Eastern Lu to travel south to Wu and Yue, and wrote it as a goodbye to his friends there. Yín in the title is a kind of song-poem, and liú bié means leaving a poem behind as a farewell.

天宝元年（742年），李白被唐玄宗召到长安，做了翰林供奉，就是陪在皇帝身边写文章的文人。可是他看不惯朝廷里的权贵，也受到排挤，不到三年就被“赐金放还”，也就是皇帝给些钱财，让他离开。后来他在东鲁（今山东一带）住了一段时间。一般认为天宝四载（745年）他要离开东鲁南游吴越，写下这首诗告别东鲁的朋友（一说写于天宝五载）。诗题里的“吟”是一种诗歌体裁，“留别”就是临走时留下诗来告别。

05

The poet作者

Li Bai (701–762), also called Taibai, was a great Tang poet of wild imagination, known as the Immortal of Poetry. He loved wandering in the mountains and seeking immortals. He was proud and would not bow to powerful people. After his setback in Chang'an, he longed even more for a free life. The dream fairyland in this poem, and its last cry against serving the powerful, show exactly who he was.

李白（701—762），字太白，号青莲居士，唐代伟大的浪漫主义诗人，被称为“诗仙”。他喜欢游山玩水、求仙访道，性格骄傲，不愿意向有权势的人低头。在长安受挫后，他更加向往自由自在的生活。这首诗里的梦中仙境和最后一句“安能摧眉折腰事权贵”，正是他这种性格的写照。

06

The mood意境

This poem tells of a wonderful dream. It begins with legends: the island of immortals cannot be found, but Mount Tianmu might be seen, and it is so high that Mount Tiantai seems to bow before it. Then the poet sets off in his dream. The moonlight carries him across Mirror Lake. He puts on Xie Lingyun's hiking shoes and climbs step by step into the clouds, watching the sunrise and hearing the heavenly rooster halfway up. Night falls. Bears roar, dragons drone, lightning flashes, thunder cracks, and the peaks crumble. A stone door bursts open, and inside are palaces of gold and silver. Immortals dressed in rainbows ride the wind and fly down in crowds. Just when it is most exciting, he wakes with a start, and only his pillow and mat are left. Waking, he understands that the joys of this world pass like a dream too. At the end he speaks his heart aloud: he would rather ride a white deer to famous mountains than bow and bend to please the powerful. The whole poem is like a film that goes from quiet to wild and then suddenly quiet again, and the last two lines ring out proud and loud.

这首诗讲了一场奇妙的梦。开头先从传说说起：仙山瀛洲找不到，天姥山却可能看得见，而且高得让天台山都要拜倒。于是诗人在梦里出发了：月光送他飞过镜湖，他穿上谢灵运的登山鞋，一步步爬上云梯，半山腰看日出，听天鸡叫。接着天黑了，熊吼龙鸣，电闪雷鸣，山峰崩塌，一扇石门“轰”地打开，里面是金银宫殿，神仙们穿着彩虹衣服、骑着风，成群地飞下来。就在最热闹的时候，他一下子惊醒，只剩下枕头和席子。从梦里醒来，他明白了：人间的快乐也像梦一样会过去。最后他大声说出心里话：我宁愿骑着白鹿去游名山，也不愿低头弯腰去讨好权贵。整首诗像一部从安静到热闹、再突然安静下来的电影，结尾的两句骄傲又响亮。
