卜算子·我住长江头

李之仪Li Zhiyi · Song宋

Bu Suan Zi: I Live at the Head of the Yangtze

 

wǒ我zhù住cháng长jiāng江tóu头

jūn君zhù住cháng长jiāng江wěi尾

rì日rì日sī思jūn君bú不jiàn见jūn君

gòng共yǐn饮cháng长jiāng江shuǐ水

cǐ此shuǐ水jǐ几shí时xiū休

cǐ此hèn恨hé何shí时yǐ已

zhǐ只yuàn愿jūn君xīn心sì似wǒ我xīn心

dìng定bú不fù负xiāng相sī思yì意

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 我住长江头I live at the head of the Yangtze,我住在长江的上游，
  2. 君住长江尾and you live at its tail.你住在长江的下游。
  3. 日日思君不见君Every day I miss you but cannot see you,天天想你，却见不到你，
  4. 共饮长江水yet we drink the water of the same river.我们一起喝着同一条长江的水。
  5. 此水几时休When will this river stop flowing?这江水什么时候才会停止流淌？
  6. 此恨何时已When will this sorrow of parting end?这离别的痛苦什么时候才会结束？
  7. 只愿君心似我心I only hope your heart is like mine,只希望你的心像我的心一样，
  8. 定不负相思意and you will never let down our love for each other.一定不会辜负我们互相思念的情意。
02

Words字词注释

长江头cháng jiāng tóu
cháng jiāng tóu: the upper part of the Yangtze River, near where it begins.长江头：长江的上游。
君jūn
jūn: you, a polite way to address someone.君：你，是对人的尊称。
长江尾cháng jiāng wěi
cháng jiāng wěi: the lower part of the Yangtze, near the sea.长江尾：长江的下游。
共饮gòng yǐn
gòng yǐn: drink together, drink the same water.共饮：一起喝。
几时jǐ shí
jǐ shí: when?几时：什么时候。
休xiū
xiū: to stop.休：停止。
此恨cǐ hèn
cǐ hèn: this pain of being apart. Hèn here means sorrow and regret, not hatred.此恨：这份离别的痛苦。恨，这里是遗憾、痛苦的意思，不是仇恨。
已yǐ
yǐ: to end, to stop.已：停止，结束。
似sì
sì: to be like.似：像。

bù fù: not let down.不负：不辜负，不让对方失望。

xiāng sī yì: the love of missing each other.相思意：互相思念的情意。
03

Background创作背景

In his later years, Li Zhiyi was sent away in disgrace to Taipingzhou (today's Dangtu, Anhui), right on the Yangtze. This lyric is usually thought to be written there. One account says it was written for a local singer named Yang Shu. The words are very simple, like a folk song. It tells of someone missing a loved one far away.

李之仪晚年被贬到太平州（今安徽当涂），那里就在长江边上。一般认为这首词是他在那里写的。一说是写给当地一位叫杨姝的歌女。词里用的都是很简单的话，像民歌一样，写的是一个人对远方心上人的思念。

04

The poet作者

Li Zhiyi (about 1048 to about 1117), also called the Retired Scholar of Guxi, was a Northern Song lyric poet. He was a good friend of Su Shi and once worked on his staff. Later he offended powerful officials and was demoted. His lyrics are fresh and natural, and this one is his most famous.

李之仪（约1048—约1117），北宋词人，号姑溪居士。他和苏轼是好朋友，做过苏轼的幕僚。后来因为得罪了权臣被贬官。他的词清新自然，这首《卜算子》是他最有名的作品。

05

The mood意境

This lyric is like a folk song: simple lines, deep feeling. One river puts two people at its two ends, far apart. Yet they drink the water of the same river, so they also seem joined. Far and near at once, the longing feels both bitter and sweet. The second stanza says the river will never stop, and neither will his longing. In the end he no longer complains. He only hopes the other heart is like his own and will stay true. As you read, picture a long, long river with one person at each end, both gazing at the same water.

这首词像一首民歌，句子简单，却很深情。一条长江，把两个人分在两头，离得很远；可是两个人喝的是同一条江的水，又好像连在一起。这一远一近，把思念写得又苦又甜。下阕说：江水不会停，我的思念也不会停。最后他不再抱怨，只希望对方的心和自己一样，不辜负这份感情。读这首词，可以想象一条长长的江，两头各站着一个人，望着同一片江水。
