马嵬坡

郑畋Zheng Tian · Tang唐

Mawei Slope

 

xuán玄zōng宗huí回mǎ马yáng杨fēi妃sǐ死

yún云yǔ雨suī虽wáng亡rì日yuè月xīn新

zhōng终shì是shèng圣míng明tiān天zǐ子shì事

jǐng景yáng阳gōng宫jǐng井yòu又hé何rén人

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 玄宗回马杨妃死When Xuanzong turned back toward Chang'an, Consort Yang was already dead.唐玄宗回到长安的时候，杨贵妃已经死了；
  2. 云雨虽亡日月新Their love was gone, but the land was made new again.恩爱虽然没有了，国家却重新安定下来。
  3. 终是圣明天子事Giving her up at Mawei was, after all, the act of a wise emperor.在马嵬坡忍痛舍弃杨妃，终究是英明天子做的事；
  4. 景阳宫井又何人And the Chen ruler who hid in the Jingyang Palace well with his consorts, what sort of man was he?那个带着妃子躲进景阳宫井里的陈后主，又算什么人呢？
02

Words字词注释

玄宗xuán zōng
xuán zōng: Emperor Xuanzong of Tang, Li Longji.玄宗：唐玄宗李隆基。
回马huí mǎ
huí mǎ: turning his horse back, meaning Xuanzong's return from Sichuan to Chang'an.回马：指唐玄宗从四川回到长安。
杨妃yáng fēi
yáng fēi: Consort Yang, Yang Guifei, the emperor's most beloved consort.杨妃：杨贵妃，唐玄宗最宠爱的妃子。
云雨yún yǔ
yún yǔ: clouds and rain, here meaning the love between the emperor and Consort Yang.云雨：这里指唐玄宗和杨贵妃的恩爱。
日月新rì yuè xīn
rì yuè xīn: the sun and moon are new again, meaning the country is put back in order.日月新：日月换了新样子，指国家重新安定下来。
圣明天子shèng míng tiān zǐ
shèng míng tiān zǐ: a wise and clear-sighted emperor.圣明天子：英明的皇帝。
景阳宫井jǐng yáng gōng jǐng
jǐng yáng gōng jǐng: the well in Jingyang Palace of the Chen dynasty. When Sui troops broke in, the last Chen ruler hid in this well with two favorite consorts and was dragged out.景阳宫井：南朝陈的景阳宫里的一口井。隋兵打进来时，陈后主带着两个宠妃躲进这口井，被抓了出来。
又何人yòu hé rén
yòu hé rén: and what kind of man was that?又何人：那又是什么样的人呢？
03

Background创作背景

In 755 An Lushan rose in revolt. This was the An–Shi Rebellion. The next year Emperor Xuanzong fled toward Sichuan. At Mawei Slope his soldiers refused to go on and demanded that Consort Yang be put to death. The emperor had to agree. Later Chang'an was won back and Xuanzong returned from Sichuan. Mawei is in today's Xingping, Shaanxi. Zheng Tian lived in the late Tang. This poem gives his view of that history. We do not know exactly when he wrote it.

公元755年，安禄山起兵造反，这就是“安史之乱”。第二年唐玄宗逃往四川，走到马嵬坡时，士兵不肯前进，要求处死杨贵妃，玄宗只好答应。后来长安收复，玄宗从四川回来。马嵬坡在今天陕西兴平。郑畋是晚唐人，这首诗是他借这段历史发表的看法，具体写作时间不详。

04

The poet作者

Zheng Tian was a minister and poet of the late Tang who served as chancellor. When Huang Chao's rebel army took Chang'an, he gathered troops around Fengxiang to fight back. He lived through the Tang dynasty's troubles himself, so the story of Xuanzong moved him deeply.

郑畋，晚唐大臣、诗人，做过宰相。黄巢起义军攻进长安时，他在凤翔一带组织兵力抵抗。他亲身经历了唐朝的动乱，所以对唐玄宗那段历史很有感触。

05

The mood意境

Many poems were written about Mawei. Most pity Consort Yang or blame Xuanzong. This one takes another angle. The first two lines say: she died and the love was gone, but the land found peace again. The last two lines compare him with the last ruler of Chen. When his state fell, that ruler could not part with his consorts and hid with them in a well. He lost his country and was captured anyway. Xuanzong was heartbroken, but he gave up Consort Yang and kept the country, so he can still be called a wise emperor. Some readers think there is a touch of irony here: he was only a little better than a ruler who lost everything. As you read, ask yourself whether the poet is praising or sighing.

写马嵬坡的诗很多，大多是同情杨贵妃，或者批评唐玄宗。这首诗角度不一样。前两句说：杨妃死了，爱情没了，可是国家又安定了。后两句拿陈后主来比：亡国的时候，陈后主舍不得妃子，带着她们躲进井里，最后国也亡了，人也被抓。唐玄宗虽然痛苦，却舍弃了杨妃，保住了国家，总算还是“圣明天子”。也有人认为这里带着一点讽刺：只是比亡国之君好一点罢了。读的时候可以想想，作者到底是在夸，还是在叹。
