和张仆射塞下曲·其四

卢纶Lu Lun · Tang唐

Frontier Songs in Reply to Minister Zhang, No. 4

 

yě野mù幕chǎng敞qióng琼yán筵

qiāng羌róng戎hè贺láo劳xuán旋

zuì醉hé和jīn金jiǎ甲wǔ舞

léi雷gǔ鼓dòng动shān山chuān川

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 野幕敞琼筵Out in the open, under big tents, a rich feast is laid out.在野外搭起大帐篷，摆开丰盛的酒席。
  2. 羌戎贺劳旋The border peoples come to cheer and reward the soldiers back from victory.边地各族的人都来祝贺，慰劳胜利归来的将士。
  3. 醉和金甲舞Drunk with joy, the soldiers dance in their armor.将士们喝醉了，穿着铠甲就跳起舞来。
  4. 雷鼓动山川The drums roar like thunder and shake the mountains and rivers.鼓声像打雷一样，震动了山山水水。
02

Words字词注释

野幕yě mù
yě mù: big tents put up out in the open.野幕：在野外搭起的大帐篷。
敞chǎng
chǎng: to lay out, to open wide.敞：摆开，敞开。
琼筵qióng yán
qióng yán: a feast as fine as jade, a rich banquet. Qióng means fine jade.琼筵：像美玉一样珍贵的酒席，指丰盛的宴会。琼，美玉。
羌戎qiāng róng
qiāng róng: peoples of the northwest border. Here, the peoples who had joined the Tang side.羌戎：西北边地的少数民族，这里指归附唐朝的各族人。
贺hè
hè: to congratulate.贺：祝贺。
劳旋láo xuán
láo xuán: to reward the soldiers coming home in victory. Láo means to thank and reward; xuán means to return in triumph.劳旋：慰劳打了胜仗回来的将士。劳，慰劳；旋，凯旋，胜利归来。
和hé
hé: together with, still wearing.和：连同，带着。
金甲jīn jiǎ
jīn jiǎ: metal armor, the hard suit soldiers wear in battle.金甲：金属做的铠甲，打仗时穿的护身衣。
雷鼓léi gǔ
léi gǔ: drums as loud as thunder. Another reading says it is a large eight-sided drum.雷鼓：鼓声像打雷一样响。一说“雷鼓”是一种八面的大鼓。
山川shān chuān
shān chuān: mountains and rivers, meaning the whole land.山川：山和河，这里指整片大地。
03

How to read it读音

和hé
Here this character means "together with, still wearing", so it is read hé.这里的“和”是“连同、带着”的意思，读 hé。
04

Background创作背景

Lu Lun wrote a set of six "Frontier Songs" in reply to poems by a Minister Zhang. To write "in reply" means to answer someone's poem with one of your own. "Minister" here stands for a very high office. Who this Zhang was is not agreed. One view says Zhang Yanshang, another says Zhang Jianfeng. The six poems show army life on the border: giving orders, hunting, chasing the enemy, and celebrating. This is the fourth. It shows the feast after a victory.

卢纶写过一组《塞下曲》，共六首，是和一位张仆射的诗。“和”就是照着别人的诗写一首来回应。仆射是很高的官名。这位张仆射是谁，说法不一，一说是张延赏，一说是张建封。六首诗写边塞军营里的生活：发令、射猎、追敌、庆功等。这是第四首，写打了胜仗以后的庆功宴。

05

The poet作者

Lu Lun was a Tang poet, one of the "Ten Talents of the Dali Era". He once worked on a general's staff, giving advice and writing documents, so he saw border army life with his own eyes. That is why his Frontier Songs feel so lively and strong.

卢纶，唐代诗人，“大历十才子”之一。他曾在军中做过幕僚，就是给将军出主意、写文书的人，亲眼见过边塞军营的生活。所以他写的《塞下曲》有声有色，很有气势。

06

The mood意境

The earlier poems in the set are about fighting. This one is about celebrating, and the mood turns loud and happy. The first line paints a wide scene: tents spread across the open land, and tables full of food and wine. The second line shows the border peoples coming to cheer too, so the victory was a great one and everyone is glad. The third line is the most vivid. The soldiers do not even take off their armor. They get drunk and start to dance, bold and carefree. The last line ends with sound. The drums boom like thunder, and the mountains and rivers seem to shake along. As you read, picture yourself by a campfire, hearing the drums and watching soldiers dance in their armor.

前三首写打仗，这一首写庆功，气氛一下子热闹起来。第一句是一幅大场面：旷野上帐篷连成一片，酒席摆得满满的。第二句写边地各族的人也来祝贺，说明这一仗打得好，大家都高兴。第三句最传神：将士们连铠甲都来不及脱，喝醉了就跳起舞来，豪爽极了。最后一句用声音收尾，鼓声像雷，好像连山河都跟着震动。读的时候，可以想象自己站在篝火旁，听见咚咚的鼓声，看见满身铠甲的士兵在跳舞。
