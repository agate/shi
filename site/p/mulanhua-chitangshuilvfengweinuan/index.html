Song lyric · ci (song lyric)宋词 · 词

mù木lán兰huā花·chí池táng塘shuǐ水lǜ绿fēng风wēi微nuǎn暖

yàn晏shū殊Yan Shu · Song宋

Magnolia Blossoms: The Pond Is Green, the Breeze a Little Warm

 

chí池táng塘shuǐ水lǜ绿fēng风wēi微nuǎn暖

jì记dé得yù玉zhēn真chū初jiàn见miàn面

chóng重tóu头gē歌yùn韵xiǎng响chēng琤cóng琮

rù入pò破wǔ舞yāo腰hóng红luàn乱xuán旋

yù玉gōu钩lán阑xià下xiāng香jiē阶pàn畔

zuì醉hòu后bù不zhī知xié斜rì日wǎn晚

dāng当shí时gòng共wǒ我shǎng赏huā花rén人

diǎn点jiǎn检rú如jīn今wú无yí一bàn半

The commonly taught text ends the third line with chēng cóng, a clear ringing sound. The source text writes the first of these two characters as zhēng. Both forms describe a clear, ringing sound, so the meaning is about the same.

通行本第三句作“重头歌韵响琤琮”。这里的底本作“铮琮”。“琤”和“铮”都可以形容清脆的声音，意思差不多。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 池塘水绿风微暖The pond water has turned green, and the breeze is a little warm.池塘里的水绿了，风吹来微微暖和。
  2. 记得玉真初见面I still remember the first time I saw that girl, lovely as a fairy.我还记得第一次见到那位像仙女一样美的姑娘。
  3. 重头歌韵响琤琮When she sang the repeated part of the song, her voice rang clear like jade.她唱到重复的那一段，歌声像玉石相碰一样清脆。
  4. 入破舞腰红乱旋When the music sped up, her waist turned and her red skirt whirled.舞曲节奏变快，她腰身转动，红裙飞旋。
  5. 玉钩阑下香阶畔Below the fine railing, beside the petal-scented steps,在精美的栏杆下，在花香飘飘的台阶旁，
  6. 醉后不知斜日晚I was so drunk I did not notice the sun was setting.我喝醉了，连太阳快落山了都不知道。
  7. 当时共我赏花人Of the people who enjoyed the flowers with me back then,当年和我一起赏花的那些人，
  8. 点检如今无一半if I count them now, not even half are left.如今数一数，已经不到一半了。
02

Words字词注释

玉真yù zhēn
yù zhēn: a fairy; here it means a singer and dancer as lovely as a fairy.玉真：本来指仙女，这里指一位像仙女一样美的歌舞女子。
重头chóng tóu
chóng tóu: a song in two halves, where the second half repeats the tune of the first.重头：一首曲子分前后两段，后段的曲调重复前段，叫“重头”。
歌韵gē yùn
gē yùn: the tune of the singing.歌韵：歌声的音调。
琤琮chēng cóng
chēng cóng: the clear sound of jade pieces knocking together; here it describes a bright, clear voice.琤琮：玉石互相碰撞的清脆声音，这里形容歌声清亮。
入破rù pò
rù pò: a part of a long Tang and Song dance suite where the beat suddenly speeds up and the dance grows fast.入破：唐宋大曲里的一段，到这里节奏突然变快，舞蹈也跟着急促起来。
舞腰红乱旋wǔ yāo hóng luàn xuán
wǔ yāo hóng luàn xuán: the dancer's waist turns and her red skirt whirls fast.舞腰红乱旋：跳舞的人腰身转动，红色的衣裙飞快地旋转。
玉钩阑yù gōu lán
yù gōu lán: a finely decorated railing.玉钩阑：装饰精美的栏杆。
香阶xiāng jiē
xiāng jiē: steps scented by fallen petals.香阶：落着花瓣、带着香气的台阶。
畔pàn
pàn: beside.畔：旁边。
斜日xié rì
xié rì: the setting sun, low in the sky.斜日：快要落山的太阳。
共我gòng wǒ
gòng wǒ: together with me.共我：和我一起。
点检diǎn jiǎn
diǎn jiǎn: to count one by one.点检：一个一个地清点、数。
03

How to read it读音

重chóng
Here the character means to repeat, so it is read chóng, not zhòng.“重头”的“重”是“重复”的意思，读 chóng，不读 zhòng。
旋xuán
Here the character means to spin, so it is read xuán.“乱旋”的“旋”是旋转的意思，读 xuán。
04

Background创作背景

We do not know exactly when this was written. Yan Shu held high office all his life, and his home often had banquets with singers and dancers. The lyric looks back to a spring many years ago, when he first saw a young singer and dancer at a feast. At the end it turns: counting the people who enjoyed the flowers with him then, he finds not even half are left. The lyric also appears under Ouyang Xiu's name with a few words changed, but it is usually taken as Yan Shu's.

这首词的写作时间不能确定。晏殊一生做大官，家里常常摆酒宴，请歌女唱歌跳舞。这首词回忆多年前一个春天，在宴会上第一次见到一位歌舞女子的情景。最后笔锋一转，写如今再数当年一起赏花的人，已经不到一半。这首词也被收在欧阳修名下，文字稍有不同，一般认为是晏殊的作品。

05

The poet作者

Yan Shu (991–1055) was an early Northern Song lyric poet and also served as chief minister. As a boy he was sent to court as a child prodigy, and his career went smoothly all his life. His lyrics are gentle and graceful, often about banquets, spring light and a light sadness. His son Yan Jidao was also a famous lyric poet; the two are called the Two Yans.

晏殊（991—1055），北宋前期的词人，也做过宰相。他小时候被当作神童推荐到朝廷，一生做官都很顺利。他的词温和、典雅，常写宴会、春光和淡淡的惆怅。他的儿子晏几道也是有名的词人，父子俩被合称为“二晏”。

06

The mood意境

The first half is like a colorful memory. The spring pond is green, the wind is warm, and the girl appears for the first time. She sings, and her voice rings like jade. She dances, and her red skirt spins like a flame. As you read, you can picture yourself at the feast, lost in the music. The second half goes on with that happy day: drinking by the railing and the steps, so drunk that no one noticed the sunset. Then the last two lines jump back to now. Of the friends who enjoyed the flowers together, not even half are left. Putting the lively past next to the lonely present makes us feel how fast time flies and how nothing stays the same. The sense of loss is spoken softly, but it goes deep.

上半首像一段彩色的回忆。春天的池塘绿了，风暖暖的，那位姑娘第一次出现。她唱歌，声音像玉石叮当作响；她跳舞，红裙转成一团火。读的时候，可以想象自己也坐在宴会上，听得入了迷。下半首先接着写那天的快乐：在栏杆下、台阶旁喝得大醉，太阳下山了都不知道。最后两句一下子回到现在：当年一起赏花的人，数一数已经不到一半。热闹的过去和冷清的现在放在一起，让人感到时光飞逝、人事无常，那种失落说得很轻，却很深。
