sū苏shì轼Su Shi · Song宋

Gazing South of the River: Written on the Chaoran Terrace

 

chūn春wèi未lǎo老

fēng风xì细liǔ柳xié斜xié斜

shì试shàng上chāo超rán然tái台shàng上kàn看

bàn半háo壕chūn春shuǐ水yì一chéng城huā花

yān烟yǔ雨àn暗qiān千jiā家

hán寒shí食hòu后

jiǔ酒xǐng醒què却zī咨jiē嗟

xiū休duì对gù故rén人sī思gù故guó国

qiě且jiāng将xīn新huǒ火shì试xīn新chá茶

shī诗jiǔ酒chèn趁nián年huá华

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 春未老Spring has not yet grown old.春天还没有老去，
  2. 风细柳斜斜A soft breeze blows, and the willow twigs lean and sway.微风轻轻吹，柳条斜斜地摆动。
  3. 试上超然台上看I climb the Chaoran Terrace and look out.我登上超然台向远处看，
  4. 半壕春水一城花The moat is half full of spring water, and the whole town is in flower.护城河里涨了半河春水，满城都是花。
  5. 烟雨暗千家Misty rain dims thousands of homes.蒙蒙烟雨，让千家万户都暗了下来。
  6. 寒食后After the Cold Food Festival,寒食节过后，
  7. 酒醒却咨嗟I sober up, and yet I cannot help sighing.酒醒了，我却忍不住叹气。
  8. 休对故人思故国Don't long for home in front of old friends.不要在老朋友面前想念故乡了，
  9. 且将新火试新茶Let's light the new fire and try the new tea instead.姑且点起新火，来煮一壶新茶吧。
  10. 诗酒趁年华Write poems and drink wine while the good years last.写诗喝酒，要趁着大好年华。
02

Words字词注释

斜斜xié xié
xié xié: leaning gently, swaying lightly.斜斜：微微倾斜、轻轻摆动的样子。
超然台chāo rán tái
chāo rán tái: a high terrace on the north wall of Mizhou, today's Zhucheng in Shandong. Su Shi repaired it and his brother Su Zhe named it. Chāo rán means rising above worldly worries.超然台：密州（今山东诸城）城北的一座高台，苏轼修好后，他的弟弟苏辙给它取了这个名字。“超然”是不被外物烦扰的意思。
壕háo
háo: the moat, the water dug around a city wall.壕：护城河，围着城墙挖的河。
暗千家àn qiān jiā
àn qiān jiā: thousands of homes are dimmed and blurred by misty rain.暗千家：千家万户都被烟雨遮得暗暗的、朦朦胧胧的。
寒食hán shí
hán shí: the Cold Food Festival, a day or two before Qingming. By custom no fires were lit and people ate only cold food.寒食：寒食节，在清明前一两天。这几天按习俗不生火，只吃冷的食物。
咨嗟zī jiē
zī jiē: to sigh.咨嗟：叹气。
故人gù rén
gù rén: old friends.故人：老朋友。
故国gù guó
gù guó: one's home region; here Su Shi's hometown of Meishan, in today's Sichuan.故国：故乡，这里指苏轼的家乡眉山（在今四川）。
新火xīn huǒ
xīn huǒ: the new fire lit again after the Cold Food Festival.新火：寒食节过后重新点起的火。
新茶xīn chá
xīn chá: new tea leaves picked around Qingming.新茶：清明前后新采的茶叶。
趁年华chèn nián huá
chèn nián huá: while the good years and the good season last.趁年华：趁着大好的年纪和时光。

04

Background创作背景

In 1074 Su Shi went to Mizhou, today's Zhucheng in Shandong, as its prefect, the head of the district. The next year he repaired an old terrace on the north side of the town, and his brother Su Zhe named it the Chaoran Terrace. This lyric is usually dated to late spring 1076, just after the Cold Food Festival. Climbing the terrace, Su Shi saw spring all over the town and thought of the home he had left long ago. Then he told himself to set the sadness down and enjoy the days in front of him.

宋神宗熙宁七年（1074年），苏轼到密州（今山东诸城）做知州（一个州的长官）。第二年，他把城北一座旧台修好，弟弟苏辙给它取名“超然台”。一般认为这首词写于熙宁九年（1076年）暮春，寒食节刚过。苏轼登台远望，看到满城春色，想起离开很久的家乡，又劝自己放下愁思，好好过眼前的日子。

05

The poet作者

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Dongpo, was a great writer of the Northern Song. He was a master of poems, lyrics, prose, calligraphy and painting. He was sent away from the capital many times, yet he always found a way to look on the bright side and find joy in hard days. After leaving his hometown of Meishan he seldom went back. His poems often show homesickness, and often show him easing it himself.

苏轼（1037—1101），号东坡居士，北宋大文学家，诗、词、文章、书法、绘画样样精通。他一生多次被贬到外地，但总能看得开，在苦日子里找到乐趣。他离开家乡眉山后就很少回去，诗词里常常流露思乡之情，又常常自己把它化解掉。

06

The mood意境

The first half is a spring scene seen from high up: a soft wind, leaning willows, a moat half full of water, a town full of flowers, and then a misty rain that wraps every home in haze. The colors move from bright to soft. The second half turns inward. After the Cold Food Festival he sobers up and starts to sigh, because he misses home. But Su Shi does not let himself stay sad. Don't long for home in front of friends, he says; light the new fire and make a pot of new tea. The last line, about poems and wine while the good years last, speaks to himself and to every reader: good times do not wait, so enjoy them.

上片像一幅从高处看下去的春景图：细风、斜柳、半河春水、满城鲜花，最后一场烟雨，把千家万户都罩得朦朦胧胧。颜色由明亮慢慢变得柔和。下片转到心里：寒食过后，酒醒了，人反而叹起气来，因为想家了。可苏轼不让自己一直难过，他说：别在朋友面前想家了，点起新火，煮一壶新茶吧。最后一句“诗酒趁年华”，像是对自己，也像是对每个读者说：好时光不等人，要好好享受。
