Tang poem · seven-character quatrain唐诗 · 七言绝句

jiāng江pàn畔dú独bù步xún寻huā花·qí其liù六

dù杜fǔ甫Du Fu · Tang唐

Walking Alone by the River in Search of Flowers (No. 6)

 

huáng黄sì四niáng娘jiā家huā花mǎn满xī蹊

qiān千duǒ朵wàn万duǒ朵yā压zhī枝dī低

liú留lián连xì戏dié蝶shí时shí时wǔ舞

zì自zài在jiāo娇yīng莺qià恰qià恰tí啼

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 黄四娘家花满蹊The path by Huang Siniang's house is full of flowers.黄四娘家门前的小路上，开满了鲜花，
  2. 千朵万朵压枝低Thousands and thousands of blossoms weigh the branches down.成千上万朵花，把树枝都压弯了。
  3. 留连戏蝶时时舞Playful butterflies linger and dance on and on,贪玩的蝴蝶舍不得离开，不停地飞舞，
  4. 自在娇莺恰恰啼And carefree orioles sing, qià qià, sweet and clear.自由自在的黄莺，“恰恰”地唱个不停。
02

Words字词注释

黄四娘huáng sì niáng
Huáng Sìniáng: Du Fu's neighbor in Chengdu. "Fourth lady" means she was the fourth child in her family.黄四娘：杜甫在成都的邻居。“四娘”是说她在家里排行第四。
蹊xī
xī: a small path.蹊：小路。
压枝低yā zhī dī
yā zhī dī: so many flowers that the branches bend down.压枝低：花太多，把树枝都压弯了。
留连liú lián
liú lián: lingering, not wanting to leave.留连：舍不得离开。
戏蝶xì dié
xì dié: playful butterflies.戏蝶：玩耍的蝴蝶。
时时shí shí
shí shí: again and again, all the time.时时：时常，不停地。
自在zì zài
zì zài: free and easy.自在：自由自在，无拘无束。
娇莺jiāo yīng
jiāo yīng: a lovely oriole, a bird with a sweet song.娇莺：可爱的黄莺。黄莺是一种叫声好听的鸟。
恰恰qià qià
qià qià: the sound of the bird's call. Another reading says it means "just then": the bird sings right at that moment.恰恰：形容鸟叫的声音，像“恰恰、恰恰”。一说是“正好”的意思，指正好在这时叫起来。
03

How to read it读音

蹊xī
xī: a small path. Textbooks read it xī here; it is read qī only in the word qī qiāo, "strange".蹊：小路，现在课本读 xī。“蹊跷”的“蹊”才读 qī。
04

Background创作背景

After years of war and wandering, Du Fu came to Chengdu and built a thatched cottage by Washing Flower Creek. At last his life was settled. It is usually said that in the spring of 761 he walked alone along the river enjoying the flowers, and wrote seven quatrains along the way. This is the sixth. Huang Siniang was his neighbor, and the flowers by her house were especially fine.

经历了多年战乱和漂泊，杜甫来到成都，在浣花溪边盖了一座草堂，生活总算安定下来。一般认为，761年春天，他一个人沿着江边散步赏花，一路写了七首绝句，这是第六首。黄四娘是他的邻居，她家门前花开得特别好。

05

The poet作者

Du Fu (712–770) was a great Tang poet, called the Sage of Poetry. Most of his poems are about war and the suffering of ordinary people, and they feel heavy. His few years at the thatched cottage in Chengdu were a rare peaceful time in his life, so he wrote many light, cheerful little poems then.

杜甫（712—770），唐代大诗人，被称为“诗圣”。他的诗大多写战乱和百姓的苦难，读起来很沉重。在成都草堂的几年，是他一生中难得的安稳日子，所以这时候他写了不少轻松、明快的小诗。

06

The mood意境

This poem is like a lively spring painting. First look at the path: flowers on both sides. Then the branches: thousands of blossoms bending them low. Then the picture starts to move. Butterflies flit around the flowers and do not want to leave. Orioles sing freely in the trees. There is color, movement and sound. The repeated words, "thousands upon thousands", "again and again", "qià qià", make it bounce like a nursery rhyme. Du Fu was usually full of worry, but this time he was in a wonderful mood, and reading it makes us happy too.

这首诗像一幅热闹的春天的画。先看小路：两边开满了花。再看树枝：千朵万朵，把枝条都压弯了。然后画面动起来：蝴蝶围着花飞来飞去，舍不得走；黄莺在树上自由自在地唱歌。有颜色，有动作，还有声音。“千朵万朵”“时时”“恰恰”这些重复的词，读起来像儿歌一样轻快。平时愁眉苦脸的杜甫，这一回心情好极了，我们读着也跟着开心起来。
