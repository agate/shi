Mooring Below Beigu Mountain

 

kè客lù路qīng青shān山wài外

xíng行zhōu舟lǜ绿shuǐ水qián前

cháo潮píng平liǎng两àn岸kuò阔

fēng风zhèng正yì一fān帆xuán悬

hǎi海rì日shēng生cán残yè夜

jiāng江chūn春rù入jiù旧nián年

xiāng乡shū书hé何chù处dá达

guī归yàn雁luò洛yáng阳biān边

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 客路青山外My road runs on beyond the green hills;我要走的路在青山的那一边，
  2. 行舟绿水前my boat moves ahead on the green water.船在碧绿的江水上向前行驶。
  3. 潮平两岸阔The tide is full, and the banks spread wide apart.潮水涨满，两岸之间显得更宽了。
  4. 风正一帆悬The wind is fair, and a single sail hangs high.顺风吹着，一片船帆高高挂起。
  5. 海日生残夜Before the night is done, the sun rises from the sea;夜还没过完，海上已经升起了太阳；
  6. 江春入旧年before the old year ends, spring comes to the river.旧年还没过完，江上已经有了春意。
  7. 乡书何处达How can my letter home reach my family?我的家信怎样才能送到家乡呢？
  8. 归雁洛阳边Let the geese flying north carry it to Luoyang.请北归的大雁带到洛阳那边去吧。

03

Background创作背景

Beigu Mountain stands north of today's Zhenjiang in Jiangsu, with the Yangtze River on three sides. In the title, cì means to moor a boat for the night. Wang Wan came from Luoyang and often traveled in the south. At the end of one winter he sailed past Beigu Mountain. The river scene made him think of home, and he wrote this poem. There is another version titled "Thoughts in Jiangnan", with somewhat different words. Textbooks use this one.

北固山在今天江苏镇江的北边，三面靠着长江。题目里的“次”是停船住下的意思。王湾是洛阳人，常在江南一带来往。一年冬末春初，他坐船经过北固山下，看到江上的景色，想起家乡，写了这首诗。这首诗还有另一个版本，题目叫《江南意》，文字有些不同。课本用的是现在这个版本。

04

The poet作者

Wang Wan was a Tang poet from Luoyang, in today's Henan. We do not know when he was born or died. Few of his poems survive, and this is the most famous. It is said that the chief minister Zhang Yue loved the lines about the sea sun and the river spring so much that he wrote them out himself and hung them in the hall of government as a model for scholars.

王湾，唐代诗人，洛阳（今属河南）人，生卒年不详。他留下来的诗不多，这一首最有名。据说当时的宰相张说非常喜欢“海日生残夜，江春入旧年”这两句，亲手写下来挂在政事堂里，给读书人做榜样。

05

The mood意境

The poet is on a boat. Around him are green hills, green water, a wide river and one sail full of wind. The scene is open and calm. The best part is lines five and six. The night is not over, yet the sun is already rising from the sea. The old year is not over, yet spring has already reached the river. New things arrive quietly before the old ones end, and the reader's heart lifts. But time moves so fast, and the poet is still far from home. So he thinks of writing a letter, and can only ask the geese flying north to take it. The lovelier the scene, the deeper his longing for home.

诗人在船上，眼前是青山、绿水、宽宽的江面和一片鼓满风的帆，画面又开阔又平静。最妙的是第五、六句：天还没亮完，太阳已经从海上升起；旧年还没过完，春天已经来到江上。新的东西在旧的东西还没结束时就悄悄来了，读来让人心里一亮。可是时间过得这样快，诗人还在外面漂泊，于是想到写信回家，只好托北飞的大雁带去。景色越美，乡愁越浓。
