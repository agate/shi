Qian Qiu Sui Yin: An Autumn Scene

 

bié别guǎn馆hán寒zhēn砧

gū孤chéng城huà画jiǎo角

yí一pài派qiū秋shēng声rù入liáo寥kuò廓

dōng东guī归yàn燕cóng从hǎi海shàng上qù去

The corpus gives only the tune name; the common text adds the subtitle An Autumn Scene. In the last line the corpus uses an older way of writing the final character; this page uses the modern form. Some editions write màn in line twelve with a different character of the same meaning.

语料库题目只有词牌名，通行本题作《千秋岁引·秋景》。最后一句语料库作“思量著”，“著”是“着”的旧写法，这里写作“着”。“漫留”有的本子作“谩留”，意思相同。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 别馆寒砧From the guest house comes the sound of cloth being beaten in the cold.旅舍里传来秋天捣衣的声音，
  2. 孤城画角From the lonely city wall a horn sounds.孤城上响起了画角声，
  3. 一派秋声入寥廓A whole sky of autumn sounds rises into the wide, empty air.一片秋声传向高远空阔的天空。
  4. 东归燕从海上去The swallows heading east fly off over the sea.东归的燕子从海上飞走了，
  5. 南来雁向沙头落The wild geese coming south land on the sandbank.南来的大雁向着沙滩落下。
  6. 楚台风The fresh wind on the King of Chu's terrace,楚王兰台上的清风，
  7. 庾楼月the bright moon over Yu Liang's tower,庾亮南楼上的明月，
  8. 宛如昨those lovely times seem like only yesterday.这些美好的时光好像就在昨天。
  9. 无奈被些名利缚But there is nothing I can do. I am tied up by fame and gain.没有办法，我被这些名和利捆住了；
  10. 无奈被他情担阁There is nothing I can do. I am held back by feelings too.没有办法，又被感情耽误了。
  11. 可惜风流总闲却What a pity that a free and joyful life is always left unused.可惜那潇洒自在的情趣，总是被白白放在一边。
  12. 当初漫留华表语Long ago I lightly promised to leave the world behind, like the crane on the pillar,当初我轻易说过要像丁令威那样远离尘世，
  13. 而今误我秦楼约and now I have missed my date with the one I love.如今却耽误了我和心上人的约会。
  14. 梦阑时Whenever a dream ends,每当梦醒的时候，
  15. 酒醒后whenever I sober up from wine,每当酒醒以后，
  16. 思量着I think it over and over.我总是想来想去。
02

Words字词注释

别馆bié guǎn
bié guǎn: a guest house where a traveler stays.别馆：客居的旅舍。
寒砧hán zhēn
hán zhēn: the sound of beating cloth in the cold. Zhēn is the stone on which people pounded cloth in autumn to make winter clothes.寒砧：秋天捣衣的声音。砧是捣衣用的石板，秋天人们在上面捶打衣料，准备冬衣。
画角huà jiǎo
huà jiǎo: a painted army horn, with a sad, lonely sound.画角：古代军中吹的号角，外面画着花纹，声音悲凉。
一派yí pài
yī pài: a whole stretch of.一派：一片。
寥廓liáo kuò
liáo kuò: the high, wide, empty sky.寥廓：高远空阔的天空。
沙头shā tóu
shā tóu: the edge of a sandbank.沙头：沙滩边。
楚台风chǔ tái fēng
chǔ tái fēng: the wind on the King of Chu's terrace. Long ago the poet Song Yu was with the King of Chu on a terrace when a fresh wind blew, and the king said how pleasant it was.楚台风：楚王兰台上的风。战国时宋玉陪楚襄王游兰台，一阵风吹来，楚王说“快哉此风”。
庾楼月yǔ lóu yuè
yǔ lóu yuè: the moon over Yu Liang's tower. In the Jin dynasty, Yu Liang climbed the south tower at Wuchang one autumn night to enjoy the moon with friends.庾楼月：庾亮南楼上的月亮。东晋时庾亮在武昌，曾在秋夜登南楼和朋友赏月谈笑。
宛如昨wǎn rú zuó
wǎn rú zuó: as if it were only yesterday.宛如昨：好像就在昨天。
无奈wú nài
wú nài: there is nothing to be done.无奈：没有办法。
名利缚míng lì fù
míng lì fù: tied up by fame and gain.名利缚：被名声和利益捆住了。
担阁dān gé
dān gé: held up, delayed.担阁：耽搁、耽误。
风流fēng liú
fēng liú: here, a free and easy life full of joy and charm.风流：这里指潇洒自在的生活和美好的情趣。
闲却xián què
xián què: left unused, wasted.闲却：白白地放在一边，没有用上。
漫màn
màn: lightly, without thinking it through.漫：随便、轻易地。
华表语huá biǎo yǔ
huá biǎo yǔ: the words on the stone pillar. Legend says Ding Lingwei became an immortal, turned into a crane, flew home, perched on a stone pillar and sighed at how the world had changed. Here it means the poet's old promise to leave the busy world and live quietly.华表语：传说丁令威学道成仙，变成仙鹤飞回家乡，停在城门的华表上说话，感叹人世变化。这里指当初想要远离尘世、归隐修道的话。华表，立在路边或城门前的石柱。
秦楼约qín lóu yuē
qín lóu yuē: a date with a loved one. Legend says Nongyu, daughter of a Qin duke, played the flute with Xiao Shi in a tower, and the two later flew away as immortals.秦楼约：和心爱的人的约会。传说秦穆公的女儿弄玉和萧史在秦楼上吹箫，后来一起成仙飞去。
梦阑mèng lán
mèng lán: when the dream ends. Lán means to come to an end.梦阑：梦醒了。阑，尽、完。
思量sī liang
sī liang: to think it over again and again.思量：想来想去。
03

How to read it读音

角jiǎo
Huà jiǎo is a painted army horn, so this character is read jiǎo.画角是古代军中吹的号角，“角”读 jiǎo。
担dān
Dān gé is the same word as dān ge today, meaning to hold up or delay. The first character is read dān.“担阁”就是“耽搁”，耽误的意思，“担”读 dān。
量liang
Sī liang means to think something over. In speech today the second syllable is light and toneless; it can also be read sī liáng.“思量”是想、反复考虑，现在口语读 sī liang，“量”读轻声；读书时也可以读 sī liáng。
着zhe
Today this character is read zhe with no tone. The poem rhymes on old clipped-tone words, and in old times this word was one of them; some readers say zhuó here.“着”现在读轻声 zhe。这首词押“落、廓、昨、缚、阁、却、约”等入声字的韵，古时这里读入声，有人朗读时读 zhuó。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric uses an autumn scene to show how tired the poet is of chasing fame and gain, and how much he longs to be free. Wang Anshi spent most of his life busy with government. In his later years he gave up his post as chief minister and retired to Jiangning, today's Nanjing. The lyric is usually thought to be a late work, but no one can say for sure which year it was written. Its images of the Chu terrace wind, Yu's tower moon, the words on the pillar and the date in the Qin tower all come from old stories.

这首词借秋天的景色，写对名利生活的厌倦和对自由的向往。王安石一生大部分时间忙于政事，晚年辞去宰相，退居江宁（今江苏南京）。一般认为这首词是他晚年的作品，但具体写于哪一年，现在说不准。词里的“楚台风”“庾楼月”“华表语”“秦楼约”都是古代的典故。

05

The poet作者

Wang Anshi (1021–1086), also called Jiefu or Banshan, came from Linchuan in Fuzhou, today's Jiangxi. He was a famous statesman and writer of the Northern Song, one of the Eight Great Prose Masters of Tang and Song. As chief minister he led the well-known reforms meant to make the country rich and strong, but many people opposed him. He did not write many lyrics, but several are fresh and deep. This one shows how tired a great man felt inside.

王安石（1021—1086），字介甫，号半山，抚州临川（今江西抚州）人，北宋著名的政治家和文学家，“唐宋八大家”之一。他当过宰相，主持了有名的“变法”，想让国家变得富强，但遭到很多人反对。他的词写得不多，却常有清新深沉的好作品，这首词就写出了一个大人物心里的疲惫。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza paints autumn with sounds and pictures. There is the pounding of cloth, the call of the horn, a whole sky of autumn sounds. The swallows leave and the geese arrive, one going and one coming, so we feel the season changing and time moving on. Then he remembers the wind on the Chu terrace and the moon over Yu's tower, the happy, carefree days that seem like yesterday. In the second stanza he says nothing can be done, twice in a row, and speaks his heart. Fame and gain tie him down. Feelings hold him back. A good life has gone to waste. He once promised to leave the busy world, yet now he has even missed his date with the one he loves. The last three short lines slow right down: when the dream ends, when the wine wears off, he keeps thinking. It is like someone waking in the night and lying there alone, thinking. His regret feels very real.

上片写秋景，全靠声音和画面：捣衣声、号角声，一片秋声飘向天空；燕子飞走，大雁落下，一去一来，让人感到季节在变，时间在走。接着想起楚台的风、庾楼的月，那些潇洒快乐的日子，好像就在昨天。下片连用两个“无奈”，一下子说出心里话：被名利捆着，被感情拖着，美好的生活都荒废了。当初说要远离尘世，如今却连和心上人的约会也误了。最后三个短句“梦阑时，酒醒后，思量着”，节奏慢下来，像一个人半夜醒来，一个人静静地想。这份后悔和无奈，读起来很真实。
