Tang poem · seven-character quatrain唐诗 · 七言绝句

jiāng将fù赴wú吴xīng兴dēng登lè乐yóu游yuán原yì一jué绝

dù杜mù牧Du Mu · Tang唐

Climbing Leyou Plateau Before Leaving for Wuxing

 

qīng清shí时yǒu有wèi味shì是wú无néng能

xián闲ài爱gū孤yún云jìng静ài爱sēng僧

yù欲bǎ把yì一huī麾jiāng江hǎi海qù去

lè乐yóu游yuán原shàng上wàng望zhāo昭líng陵

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 清时有味是无能In these peaceful times I find life pleasant, because I have no talent;太平年代我过得有滋味，是因为自己没有本事，
  2. 闲爱孤云静爱僧at leisure I love the lone cloud, in quiet I love the monks.闲着时爱看天上的孤云，安静时爱和僧人做伴。
  3. 欲把一麾江海去Soon I will carry a banner off to a post by the rivers and seas,我就要拿着一面旗子，到江海边的地方去做官了，
  4. 乐游原上望昭陵so I climb Leyou Plateau and gaze toward Zhaoling.临走前登上乐游原，远远望一望昭陵。
02

Words字词注释

清时qīng shí
qīng shí: a peaceful, well-run time.清时：太平、清明的时代。
有味yǒu wèi
yǒu wèi: to have flavor, to find life enjoyable.有味：有滋味，过得有意思。
无能wú néng
wú néng: having no talent. Here the poet says it with irony, mocking himself.无能：没有才能。这里是诗人说反话，带着自嘲。
孤云gū yún
gū yún: a lone cloud in the sky, a picture of carefree ease.孤云：天上一片单独的云，象征悠闲自在。
僧sēng
sēng: a Buddhist monk.僧：和尚。
一麾yì huī
yī huī: a single banner. Officials sent out to govern a region carried a banner, so "holding a banner" means going off to a post.一麾：一面旗子。古时出京当地方长官，会带着旗子，所以“把一麾”就是去外地做官。
江海jiāng hǎi
jiāng hǎi: rivers and seas, meaning the watery region around Huzhou.江海：指湖州一带靠近江湖的地方。
乐游原lè yóu yuán
lè yóu yuán: Leyou Plateau, high ground in the south of Chang'an, a favorite place for outings and views.乐游原：长安城南边的一块高地，是游玩、远望的好地方。
昭陵zhāo líng
zhāo líng: Zhaoling, the tomb of Emperor Taizong of Tang, Li Shimin, one of the dynasty's ablest rulers.昭陵：唐太宗李世民的陵墓。唐太宗是唐朝有作为的好皇帝。
03

How to read it读音

乐lè
In the place name Leyou Plateau, the character is read lè, as in "joy".乐游原：读 lè yóu yuán。“乐”在这里是快乐的“乐”。
04

Background创作背景

In 850, Du Mu held a post in the capital, Chang'an, but things were not going well for him. He asked to be sent away and was made governor of Huzhou, called Wuxing in old times, in today's Zhejiang. Before leaving, he climbed Leyou Plateau south of the city and wrote this poem. The title calls it "one quatrain".

唐宣宗大中四年（850年），杜牧在京城长安做官，日子过得不顺心，主动请求到外地去，被派去做湖州刺史。湖州古时叫吴兴，在今天的浙江。临走前，他登上长安城南的乐游原，写了这首诗。题目里的“一绝”，就是一首绝句。

05

The poet作者

Du Mu (803 to about 852) was a late Tang poet, named with Li Shangyin as the "Little Li and Du". His grandfather had been a chief minister, and Du Mu too dreamed of helping run the country. He loved to talk about warfare and affairs of state, but he was never given a big role. On the surface this poem is about leisure; underneath lies his disappointment.

杜牧（803—约852），晚唐诗人，和李商隐并称“小李杜”。他的祖父杜佑做过宰相，他自己也有治国的抱负，爱谈兵法和国家大事，可是一直没被重用。这首诗表面写悠闲，底下藏着他的失意。

06

The mood意境

The first line is irony. In peaceful times the able men are busy with great work, and only a "talentless" man like me has time to enjoy life. What he really means is: I have talent, but no one uses it. In the second line he turns to lone clouds and monks. It looks relaxed, but it is lonely. In the last two lines, about to leave the capital, he climbs high and looks toward Zhaoling. Emperor Taizong was famous for making good use of able men. Gazing at his tomb, the poet seems to think: if only I lived in his time. He never complains out loud. All his sadness is in that one long look.

第一句是反话。天下太平，有本事的人都在忙大事，只有我这个“无能”的人闲得有滋味。其实诗人是在说：我有才能，却没人用我。第二句写他只好去爱孤云、爱僧人，看起来很闲，心里却很寂寞。后两句他要离开京城了，偏偏登高去望昭陵。唐太宗最会用人才，望着他的陵墓，诗人心里在想：要是生在那个时代就好了。不说一句抱怨，失落都在这一“望”里。
