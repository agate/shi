Yu Jie Xing: South of the Street, Green Trees Full of Catkins

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 街南绿树春饶絮South of the street, the green trees in spring are full of catkins.街南的绿树在春天飘着很多柳絮，
  2. 雪满游春路Like snow, they cover the spring paths.像雪一样铺满了游春的路。
  3. 树头花艳杂娇云Bright blossoms on the treetops mingle with lovely clouds,树梢的花开得鲜艳，和彩云混在一起，
  4. 树底人家朱户and under the trees is a house with red gates.树下是朱红大门的人家。
  5. 北楼闲上I climb the north tower at leisure,我悠闲地登上北楼，
  6. 疏帘高卷roll the thin blinds up high,把稀疏的竹帘高高卷起，
  7. 直见街南树and see straight across to the trees south of the street.一眼就望见街南的树。
  8. 阑干倚尽犹慵去I lean on the railing for a long time, still not wanting to leave.我靠着栏杆站了很久，还是不想离开，
  9. 几度黄昏雨How many rainy dusks have passed like this?不知已经过了多少个下雨的黄昏。
  10. 晚春盘马踏青苔One late spring I rode my horse over the green moss,记得那年晚春，我骑着马踏过青苔，
  11. 曾傍绿阴深驻and stopped deep in the green shade.曾在绿树浓荫的深处停留。
  12. 落花犹在Now the fallen flowers are still here,如今落花还在，
  13. 香屏空掩the fragrant screen stands closed and empty,香屏风空空地掩着，
  14. 人面知何处but where is that face now?那个人却不知去了哪里。
02

Words字词注释

饶絮ráo xù
ráo xù: full of catkins. Ráo means plenty. Catkins are willow seeds with white fluff that float everywhere.饶絮：柳絮很多。饶，多。柳絮是柳树的种子，带着白绒毛，到处飘。
雪满xuě mǎn
xuě mǎn: the white catkins cover everything like snow.雪满：柳絮白白的，像雪一样铺满。
游春yóu chūn
yóu chūn: going out to enjoy spring.游春：春天出去游玩。
树头shù tóu
shù tóu: the treetops.树头：树梢。
杂娇云zá jiāo yún
zá jiāo yún: mixed with lovely clouds.杂娇云：和美丽的彩云混在一起。
朱户zhū hù
zhū hù: red-painted gates, the sign of a well-off family.朱户：朱红色的大门，指富贵人家。
闲上xián shàng
xián shàng: climbing up at leisure.闲上：悠闲地登上。
疏帘shū lián
shū lián: a loosely woven bamboo blind.疏帘：编得稀疏的竹帘。
直见zhí jiàn
zhí jiàn: seeing straight across to.直见：一眼就看到。
阑干lán gān
lán gān: a railing.阑干：栏杆。
倚尽yǐ jìn
yǐ jìn: leaning on it for a long, long time.倚尽：靠了很久很久。
慵去yōng qù
yōng qù: too listless to leave. Yōng means lazy, listless.慵去：懒得离开，不想走。慵，懒。
几度jǐ dù
jǐ dù: how many times.几度：几次，好多回。
盘马pán mǎ
pán mǎ: riding a horse round and about.盘马：骑着马来回转。
踏青苔tà qīng tái
tà qīng tái: hooves treading on green moss.踏青苔：马蹄踩着青苔。
傍bàng
bàng: close to, beside.傍：靠近。
绿阴lǜ yīn
lǜ yīn: the deep shade of green trees.绿阴：绿树的浓荫。
深驻shēn zhù
shēn zhù: stopping deep inside.深驻：在深处停留。
香屏xiāng píng
xiāng píng: a fine, fragrant screen; the room where she lived.香屏：华美的屏风，指那人住过的屋子。
空掩kōng yǎn
kōng yǎn: closed and empty.空掩：空空地关着。
人面rén miàn
rén miàn: that face, meaning the loved one. It borrows from the Tang poet Cui Hu: the face is gone, but the peach blossoms still smile in the spring wind.人面：那个人的脸，指心上人。借用唐代崔护“人面不知何处去，桃花依旧笑春风”。
03

Background创作背景

This ci is about returning to an old place and missing someone. The poet once rode his horse to a house south of a street and lingered there, where someone he loved lived. Later he comes back. The flowers are still there and so is the screen, but she is gone. The last line borrows the idea of the Tang poet Cui Hu's line, "where has that face gone?" The ci does not say when it was written or whom he missed, and we cannot tell now.

这首词写的是重回旧地、怀念旧人。作者曾经在一条街南的人家门前骑马停留，那里住着他喜欢的人。后来他再来，花还在，屏风还在，人却不见了。最后一句借用了唐代崔护“人面不知何处去”的诗意。具体写于何时、怀念的是谁，词里没有说，现在也无法确定。

04

The poet作者

Yan Jidao (about 1038 – about 1110), also called Xiaoshan, was the youngest son of Yan Shu, a chief minister and famous ci poet, so people called him Little Yan. His family later declined, and he held only small posts all his life. He was proud and aloof. His collection, Xiaoshan Ci, is full of memories and longing, in clear, lovely words and with deep feeling.

晏几道（约1038—约1110），字叔原，号小山，是宰相词人晏殊的小儿子，人称“小晏”。他家后来衰落，他本人一直只做小官，性格清高孤傲。他的词集叫《小山词》，多写回忆和相思，语言清丽，感情真挚。

05

The mood意境

The first stanza is a spring street. The green trees south of the street shed so many catkins that they cover the spring paths like snow. Blossoms glow on the treetops, mixed with bright clouds, and below the trees stands a house with red gates. He idly climbs the north tower, rolls up the blinds, and sees those trees across the street at once. The second stanza turns to memory. He leans on the railing for a long time and still does not want to go. He cannot tell how many rainy dusks have passed like this. He recalls riding his horse over green moss one late spring, and stopping deep in the shade. Now the fallen flowers are still there, and the fragrant screen stands closed and empty, but where is she? The scene is the same and the person is gone. Under the quiet words lies a deep sense of loss.

上片是春天的街景。街南的绿树飘着很多柳絮，像雪一样铺满了游春的路。树梢花开得艳，和彩云混在一起；树下是朱红大门的人家。他悠闲地登上北楼，卷起帘子，一眼就看见街南那些树。下片转向回忆：他靠着栏杆站了很久，还是不想走，不知道这样已经过了几个下雨的黄昏。他想起晚春时曾骑着马踏过青苔，在浓浓的树荫下停留。现在落花还在，香屏风空空地掩着，那个人却不知道去了哪里。景物依旧，人已不在，淡淡的句子里藏着很深的怅惘。
