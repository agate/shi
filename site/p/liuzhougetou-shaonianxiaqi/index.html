This follows the common text, as in Three Hundred Song Lyrics. The database text differs in six places, such as different characters for jīn háo zòng (proud and free), lián fēi kòng (riding side by side), jiǔ lú (wine shop), bù shū cóng (piles of paperwork), gōng cū yòng (used for odd jobs) and tiān jiāo zhǒng (the proud nomad chiefs). Most of these are copying slips or errors from converting traditional characters to simplified ones.

这里用的是《宋词三百首》等通行本的文字。资料库的本子有几处不同：“矜豪纵”作“衿豪踪”，“联飞鞚”作“聊飞鞚”，“酒垆”作“酒炉”，“簿书丛”作“薄书从”，“供粗用”作“共粗用”，“天骄种”作“天娇种”。这些多半是传抄或繁体转简体时的错字。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 少年侠气When I was young, I was full of a knight's spirit.我年轻时满身侠气，
  2. 交结五都雄I made friends with heroes from all the great cities.结交了各大城市的英雄豪杰。
  3. 肝胆洞We opened our hearts to each other.对朋友敞开真心，
  4. 毛发耸At any wrong, our hair stood on end with anger.遇到不平事，气得头发都竖起来。
  5. 立谈中In the time of a short talk,站着说几句话的工夫，
  6. 死生同we swore to live and die together.就约定同生共死，
  7. 一诺千金重A promise weighed more than a thousand pieces of gold.答应的一句话，比千金还重。
  8. 推翘勇People called us the bravest.大家推我们为最勇敢的人，
  9. 矜豪纵We were proud to be bold and free.我们也以豪放不羁为骄傲。
  10. 轻盖拥We rode light carriages with friends crowding round,坐着轻车，朋友们前呼后拥，
  11. 联飞鞚and galloped side by side on fast horses骑着骏马并排飞奔，
  12. 斗城东east of the capital.在京城的东郊游玩。
  13. 轰饮酒垆In wine shops we drank loudly together.在酒店里大伙儿热闹地痛饮，
  14. 春色浮寒瓮Good wine floated in the cool jars,清凉的酒坛里浮着美酒，
  15. 吸海垂虹and we drank like a rainbow drinking up the sea.我们喝起来像长虹吸干大海。
  16. 闲呼鹰嗾犬In free time we called our hawks and set our dogs running,闲时呼唤猎鹰，指使猎狗，
  17. 白羽摘雕弓took white-feathered arrows and carved bows,带上白羽箭，取下雕花弓，
  18. 狡穴俄空and soon the dens of the clever hares were empty.一会儿就把狡兔的窝打空了。
  19. 乐匆匆But those happy days passed too fast.可惜快乐的日子太匆匆。
  20. 似黄粱梦It was all like the yellow millet dream.一切像一场黄粱梦，

  24. 官冗从I became a minor officer with an idle post,做了一个闲散的小武官，

  28. 鹖弁如云众Officers like me are as many as the clouds,像我这样的武官多得像云，
  29. 供粗用used only for small jobs,只被派去干些杂事，

02

Words字词注释

侠气xiá qì
xiá qì: the spirit of a knight-errant, brave and loyal, ready to help others.侠气：讲义气、肯为别人出头的豪迈气概。
交结jiāo jié
jiāo jié: to make friends with.交结：结交，交朋友。
五都雄wǔ dū xióng
wǔ dū xióng: heroes of the great cities. Wǔ dū, the five capitals, means all the big cities.五都雄：各大城市里的英雄豪杰。五都，泛指当时的大城市。
肝胆洞gān dǎn dòng
gān dǎn dòng: an open heart. Liver and gall stand for one's true heart; dòng means open, see-through.肝胆洞：对朋友敞开心胸，真诚相待。肝胆，比喻真心；洞，透亮，敞开。
毛发耸máo fà sǒng
máo fà sǒng: hair standing on end with anger at injustice.毛发耸：遇到不平的事，气得头发都竖起来。
立谈中lì tán zhōng
lì tán zhōng: in the time of a short talk standing up, that is, very quickly.立谈中：站着说几句话的工夫，形容时间很短。
死生同sǐ shēng tóng
sǐ shēng tóng: promising to live and die together.死生同：约定同生共死。
一诺千金重yí nuò qiān jīn zhòng
yī nuò qiān jīn zhòng: one promise weighs more than a thousand pieces of gold, so it must be kept.一诺千金重：答应别人的一句话，比千斤黄金还重，一定做到。
推翘勇tuī qiáo yǒng
tuī qiáo yǒng: honored by all as the bravest.推翘勇：被大家推为最勇敢出众的人。
矜豪纵jīn háo zòng
jīn háo zòng: proud of being bold and free. Jīn means to take pride in.矜豪纵：以豪放、不受拘束为骄傲。矜，自夸。
轻盖拥qīng gài yōng
qīng gài yōng: riding light carriages, surrounded by friends. Gài, the canopy of a carriage, stands for the carriage.轻盖拥：坐着轻快的车子，朋友们前呼后拥。盖，车上的伞盖，代指车。
联飞鞚lián fēi kòng
lián fēi kòng: galloping side by side. Kòng is a bridle, standing for the horse.联飞鞚：骑着马并排飞奔。鞚，马笼头，代指马。
斗城dǒu chéng
dǒu chéng: the capital. Han dynasty Chang'an was shaped like the Big Dipper. Here it means the Northern Song capital, Bianjing.斗城：指京城。汉代长安城的形状像北斗星，所以叫斗城。这里借指北宋都城汴京。
轰饮hōng yǐn
hōng yǐn: drinking noisily together in a big crowd.轰饮：一大群人热热闹闹地痛快喝酒。
酒垆jiǔ lú
jiǔ lú: a wine shop. Lú is the earthen counter where wine jars stood.酒垆：酒店。垆，酒店里放酒坛子的土台子。
春色浮寒瓮chūn sè fú hán wèng
chūn sè fú hán wèng: good wine floats in the cool jar. Spring was an old name for wine.春色浮寒瓮：清凉的酒坛里浮着美酒。春色，指酒，古人常把酒叫“春”。
吸海垂虹xī hǎi chuí hóng
xī hǎi chuí hóng: drinking like a rainbow bending down to drink up the sea. People once thought rainbows drank water.吸海垂虹：喝酒像长虹垂下来吸干大海一样，形容酒量极大。古人以为彩虹会垂下来喝水。
嗾犬sǒu quǎn
sǒu quǎn: to urge the hunting dogs on.嗾犬：发出声音指使猎狗去追。
白羽bái yǔ
bái yǔ: arrows with white feathers.白羽：装着白色羽毛的箭。
摘雕弓zhāi diāo gōng
zhāi diāo gōng: taking down a carved bow.摘雕弓：取下刻着花纹的弓。
狡穴俄空jiǎo xué é kōng
jiǎo xué é kōng: soon the dens of the clever hares were empty, meaning a big catch. É means soon.狡穴俄空：狡猾的兔子的洞穴一会儿就空了，意思是打猎收获很多。俄，一会儿。
乐匆匆lè cōng cōng
lè cōng cōng: the happy days passed too quickly.乐匆匆：快乐的日子过得太匆忙。
黄粱梦huáng liáng mèng
huáng liáng mèng: the yellow millet dream. In a Tang story, a young man dreams a whole lifetime of glory, and wakes before the millet in the pot is cooked. Good times are short as a dream.黄粱梦：唐代传奇故事：卢生在枕上睡了一觉，梦里过了一辈子荣华富贵，醒来时锅里的黄米饭还没煮熟。比喻好日子像梦一样短。
辞丹凤cí dān fèng
cí dān fèng: leaving the capital. Dān fèng, the Red Phoenix, was a gate of Chang'an, so it stands for the capital.辞丹凤：离开京城。丹凤，指京城，唐代长安有丹凤门。
明月共míng yuè gòng
míng yuè gòng: only the bright moon for company.明月共：只有明月陪伴着。
漾孤篷yàng gū péng
yàng gū péng: a lone boat drifting. Péng is the boat's cover, standing for the boat.漾孤篷：一只小船在水上漂荡。篷，船篷，代指船。
官冗从guān rǒng cóng
guān rǒng cóng: serving as a minor attendant officer, a low and idle post.官冗从：做一个闲散的小武官。冗从，古代侍从官的名称，这里指地位低的小官。
怀倥偬huái kǒng zǒng
huái kǒng zǒng: always busy and troubled at heart.怀倥偬：心里总是匆忙、烦乱。
落尘笼luò chén lóng
luò chén lóng: fallen into the cage of the dusty world, no longer free.落尘笼：落进了世俗的笼子里，不自由。
簿书丛bù shū cóng
bù shū cóng: buried in piles of paperwork. Bù shū are government papers.簿书丛：整天埋在一堆堆公文里。簿书，官府的文书。
鹖弁hé biàn
hé biàn: an officer's cap with the feathers of the hé, a fierce bird. It stands for military officers.鹖弁：武官戴的帽子，上面插着鹖鸟的羽毛。这里指武官。
如云众rú yún zhòng
rú yún zhòng: as many as the clouds.如云众：像云一样多。
供粗用gōng cū yòng
gōng cū yòng: only used for small, rough jobs.供粗用：只被派去干些杂事。
忽奇功hū qí gōng
hū qí gōng: great deeds are ignored, with no chance to do them. Hū means to neglect.忽奇功：建立大功的事被忽视了，没有机会。忽，轻视、忽略。
笳鼓动jiā gǔ dòng
jiā gǔ dòng: the army's reed pipes and war drums sound, meaning war on the border. Jiā is a reed pipe.笳鼓动：军中的胡笳和战鼓响起来了，指边境起了战事。笳，一种吹奏乐器。
渔阳弄yú yáng nòng
yú yáng nòng: the drum tune Yuyang, loud and tragic. It also recalls the An Lushan Rebellion, which started at Yuyang, and hints at war on the border.渔阳弄：古代鼓曲《渔阳掺挝》，声音悲壮。这里也让人想到唐代安史之乱从渔阳起兵，暗指边境的战乱。
思悲翁sī bēi wēng
sī bēi wēng: 'Thinking of the Sad Old Man', a Han army song. Here he means himself, a sad man with a wish to serve and no chance.思悲翁：汉代军乐的曲名。这里借来说自己像一个悲伤的老人，空有报国的心。
不请长缨bù qǐng cháng yīng
bù qǐng cháng yīng: cannot ask for a long rope. In the Han dynasty, Zhong Jun asked the emperor for a long rope to tie up the king of Nanyue. Here the poet has no such chance.不请长缨：不能去请求一根长绳子。汉代终军向皇帝请求一根长绳，说要把南越王捆来。这里说自己没有这样的机会。缨，绳子。
天骄种tiān jiāo zhǒng
tiān jiāo zhǒng: the proud chiefs of the northern nomads. Here it hints at the Western Xia, which raided the border at that time.天骄种：指北方和西北强悍的游牧民族首领，这里暗指当时侵扰边境的西夏。
剑吼西风jiàn hǒu xī fēng
jiàn hǒu xī fēng: the sword roars in the west wind, as if angry too.剑吼西风：宝剑在秋风里发出吼声，好像也在愤怒。
恨hèn
hèn: regret, bitterness.恨：遗憾，怨恨。
登山临水dēng shān lín shuǐ
dēng shān lín shuǐ: climbing hills and standing by rivers, wandering with nothing to do.登山临水：登上高山，来到水边，指到处游荡。
七弦桐qī xián tóng
qī xián tóng: a seven-string zither, made of paulownia wood.七弦桐：七根弦的琴，用梧桐木做成。
手寄shǒu jì
shǒu jì: to put his feelings into the music his hands play.手寄：用手弹琴，把心事寄托在琴声里。
目送归鸿mù sòng guī hóng
mù sòng guī hóng: watching the wild geese fly home. It echoes a line by Ji Kang: 'My eyes follow the homing geese, my hands play the five strings.'目送归鸿：眼睛送着飞回去的大雁。出自嵇康的诗“目送归鸿，手挥五弦”。

How to read it读音

少shào
Read shào here: young.少：读 shào，年纪轻。
发fà
Read fà here: hair.发：读 fà，头发。
翘qiáo
Read qiáo here: outstanding, as in qiáo chǔ, the best of a group.翘：读 qiáo，出众、突出，如“翘楚”。
纵zòng
Read zòng: free and unrestrained.纵：读 zòng，放纵、不受拘束。
斗dǒu
Read dǒu, as in the Big Dipper. Not dòu (to fight).斗：读 dǒu，北斗星。斗城，指京城。不读 dòu。
从cóng
Usually read cóng today. In the old official title rǒng cóng, it had a falling tone, like zòng.从：现在一般读 cóng。“冗从”是古代官名，古音读去声，相当于 zòng。
倥kǒng
Today's dictionaries read kǒng zǒng: busy and rushed.倥偬：现在字典读 kǒng zǒng，意思是事情多、匆忙。
系xì
Read xì here: to tie up with a rope.系：读 xì，用绳子捆住。
04

Background创作背景

When He Zhu was young, he served as a guard officer in Bianjing, the Northern Song capital, and made many bold friends. Later he spent years as a low-ranking officer in the provinces, with no chance to show what he could do. The lyric is usually dated to the Yuanyou years of Emperor Zhezong, around 1088. He was then a local police officer in Hezhou, today's He County in Anhui. At that time the Western Xia often raided the northwest border, but the court mostly gave in and asked for peace. He Zhu could not serve his country, so he wrote this lyric.

贺铸年轻时在北宋都城汴京做侍卫武官，结交了许多豪侠朋友。后来他一直在外地做低级武官，没有机会施展本领。一般认为这首词写于宋哲宗元祐年间（约1088年），当时他在和州（今安徽和县）做管界巡检，管一个地方的治安。那时西北边境的西夏常常来侵扰，朝廷却多半忍让求和。贺铸报国无门，就写下了这首词。

05

The poet作者

He Zhu (1052–1125) was a Northern Song lyric poet. He was a distant relative of the empress of the first Song emperor. He was tall with a fierce face, and people nicknamed him 'Ghost-Head He'. He was bold and loved to talk about the affairs of the country, yet he held only small posts all his life. Some of his lyrics are soft and tender, like the one about 'rain when the plums turn yellow', which earned him the name 'Plum He'. Others are fiery like this one.

贺铸（1052—1125），字方回，北宋词人。他是宋太祖贺皇后的远房族孙，长得高大，相貌很凶，人称“贺鬼头”。他性格豪爽，喜欢谈论天下大事，却一生只做小官。他的词有的柔美，比如写“梅子黄时雨”的《青玉案》，所以又叫“贺梅子”；有的像这首一样慷慨激昂。

06

The mood意境

The two halves of this lyric are like two colors. The first half is bright red. The young men are loyal, and with one word they swear to live and die together. They gallop on horses, drink huge amounts of wine and hunt with hawks and dogs. Each line has only three characters, one after another, fast like hoofbeats. The second half turns grey. The joy ends like waking from the yellow millet dream. He becomes a small officer, trapped by paperwork. War breaks out on the border. He wants to fight, but gets no chance. Only his sword roars in the autumn wind. In the end he can only play his zither and watch the geese fly away. From hot to cold, from laughter to sighs, the lyric shows the anger and helplessness of a hero who is never used.

这首词前后两半像两种颜色。上半首是热烈的红色：少年们讲义气，一句话就约定同生共死；他们骑马飞奔，大碗喝酒，带着鹰犬打猎，痛快极了。每句只有三个字，一句接一句，像马蹄声一样又快又急。下半首却变成灰色：快乐像黄粱梦一样醒了，他做了个小官，整天被公文困住。边境打仗了，他想去杀敌，却没有机会，只有宝剑在秋风里吼叫。最后他只能弹琴，看着大雁飞走。从热到冷，从笑到叹，写出了一个英雄被埋没的愤怒和无奈。
