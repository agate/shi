01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 画楼帘幕卷新晴On a newly cleared day, the curtains of the painted tower are rolled up;雨后初晴，画楼上的帘幕卷了起来，
  2. 掩银屏The silver screen stands half closed,银色的屏风半掩着，
  3. 晓寒轻And the dawn holds a light chill.清晨还有一点轻轻的寒意。
  4. 坠粉飘香Petals fall and their fragrance drifts,花瓣纷纷落下，香气四处飘散，
  5. 日日唤愁生Calling up sorrow day after day.一天天地把愁绪唤了出来。
  6. 暗数十年湖上路I silently count ten years of paths along the lake;我心里默默数着这十年来湖边走过的路，
  7. 能几度How many times有几回，
  8. 著娉婷Was a lovely woman by my side?是有美人陪伴着的？
  9. 年华空自感飘零The years slip away, and alone I grieve my drifting life.年华流逝，我只能独自感叹身世飘零。
  10. 拥春酲Dazed with wine in springtime,在春天里喝得昏昏沉沉，
  11. 对谁醒Whom will I face when I wake?醒来又能面对谁呢？
  12. 天阔云闲The sky is wide, the clouds drift idly,天空辽阔，云朵悠闲，
  13. 无处觅箫声But nowhere can I find that flute's sound.却再也找不到那熟悉的箫声。
  14. 载酒买花年少事Carrying wine and buying flowers, those were the doings of youth;带着酒、买了花去游玩，那是年轻时的事，
  15. 浑不似None of it is the same now,现在完全不像了，
  16. 旧心情Nor is my heart what it used to be.再也不是从前的心情。
02

Words字词注释

画楼huà lóu
huà lóu: a painted tower.画楼：有彩绘装饰的楼。
卷新晴juǎn xīn qíng
juǎn xīn qíng: rolling up the curtains as the sky clears after rain.卷新晴：雨后刚刚放晴，把帘子卷起来。
银屏yín píng
yín píng: a screen trimmed with silver.银屏：镶着银饰的屏风。
晓寒xiǎo hán
xiǎo hán: the chill of early morning.晓寒：清晨的寒意。
坠粉飘香zhuì fěn piāo xiāng
zhuì fěn piāo xiāng: petals fall and their scent drifts about; spring flowers are fading.坠粉飘香：花瓣纷纷落下，香气四处飘散，指春花凋谢。
唤愁生huàn chóu shēng
huàn chóu shēng: calling sorrow up.唤愁生：把愁绪唤了出来。
暗数àn shǔ
àn shǔ: to count silently in the heart.暗数：心里默默地数。
湖上路hú shàng lù
hú shàng lù: the paths by the lake where he once went on outings. One view says the lake is West Lake in Hangzhou.湖上路：湖边游玩的路。一说指杭州西湖。
能几度néng jǐ dù
néng jǐ dù: how many times?能几度：能有几回。
娉婷pīng tíng
pīng tíng: graceful; here, a beautiful woman.娉婷：姿态美好的样子，这里指美丽的女子。
飘零piāo líng
piāo líng: drifting about like fallen leaves, with nothing to hold on to.飘零：像落叶一样到处漂泊，没有依靠。
春酲chūn chéng
chūn chéng: the dazed feeling after drinking in spring. Chéng means the muddled head after too much wine.春酲：春天里喝醉了酒，醉后头昏昏的样子。酲，酒醉后神志不清。
对谁醒duì shuí xǐng
duì shuí xǐng: when I sober up, whom will I face? There is no one to be with.对谁醒：醒过来又面对谁呢？意思是没有人可以相伴。
箫声xiāo shēng
xiāo shēng: the sound of a bamboo flute; here it hints at a woman of the past who played it.箫声：吹箫的声音，这里暗指从前那位会吹箫的女子。
载酒买花zài jiǔ mǎi huā
zài jiǔ mǎi huā: carrying wine and buying flowers for an outing; the carefree pleasures of youth.载酒买花：带着酒，买了花，去游玩。指年轻时风流快乐的生活。
浑不似hún bú sì
hún bù sì: not at all like. Hún means completely.浑不似：完全不像。浑，全，完全。
03

How to read it读音

数shǔ
Àn shǔ means to count silently in one's head. As a verb, this character is read shǔ.“暗数”是心里默默地数，“数”是动词，读 shǔ。
著zhuó
Here this character is read zhuó, meaning to be beside or keep company with.“著”这里读 zhuó，意思是附着、伴着。
醒xǐng
Today this character is read xǐng. In old times it could also be read in a level tone, rhyming with the other line endings.“醒”现在读 xǐng。古代它也有平声的读法，和“晴、屏、轻”等押韵。
少shào
Nián shào means one's young days, so this character is read shào.“年少”指年轻的时候，“少”读 shào。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is set in late spring. The poet looks back on ten years of outings, misses a woman who once kept him company, and sighs that his youth is gone. The lake is thought by some to be West Lake, as Lu Zugao served as an official in Lin'an, today's Hangzhou. Who the woman was and the exact year it was written are no longer known.

这首词写暮春时节，词人回想十年来的旧游，怀念一位曾经相伴的女子，感叹年华老去。“湖上路”一说指杭州西湖，卢祖皋曾在临安（今杭州）做官。词里的女子是谁，写作的具体年份，都已无法确定。

05

The poet作者

Lu Zugao (about 1174–1224), known by his pen name Pujiang, came from Yongjia, today's Wenzhou in Zhejiang. He passed the top civil exam under Emperor Ningzong of the Southern Song and later held posts at court. His lyrics are clear, graceful and gentle, often about spring sorrow and memories of the past. His collected lyrics are called the Pujiang Lyrics.

卢祖皋（约1174—1224），字申之，号蒲江，永嘉（今浙江温州）人。南宋宁宗时考中进士，后来在朝中做官。他的词清丽婉转，多写春愁和怀旧，有词集《蒲江词》。

06

The mood意境

The first stanza begins on a morning. The sky has cleared after rain, the curtains are rolled up, the screen is half closed, and there is a slight chill. Outside, petals fall and their scent drifts. It should be lovely, yet every day it stirs up sorrow. Why? Because he is counting the lakeside paths of the past ten years, and the days with a lovely woman beside him were really very few. The second stanza is about now. The years slip by, he drifts from place to place, and he drinks to forget, but when he wakes there is no one beside him. The sky is wide and the clouds idle, yet he can no longer hear that familiar flute. The last three lines are like a long sigh: the joy of youth, carrying wine and buying flowers, feels completely different now. The words are light and plain, but the sorrow falls into his heart petal by petal.

上片从一个清晨写起：雨后放晴，帘子卷起，屏风半掩，有一点寒意。窗外花瓣飘落，香气四散，本该是美好的景色，却天天勾起愁思。为什么？因为他在心里数着十年来湖边走过的路，有美人相伴的日子，其实没有几回。下片写现在：年华白白流逝，自己四处漂泊，只能借酒消愁，可醒来身边却没有人。天那么宽，云那么闲，再也听不到那熟悉的箫声。最后三句像一声长叹：年轻时载酒买花的快乐，现在想起来，心情已经完全不一样了。全词语言清淡，愁绪却像落花一样，一片一片飘满了心里。
