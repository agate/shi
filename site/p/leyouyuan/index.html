乐游原

李商隐Li Shangyin · Tang唐

Leyou Plateau

 

xiàng向wǎn晚yì意bú不shì适

qū驱chē车dēng登gǔ古yuán原

xī夕yáng阳wú无xiàn限hǎo好

zhǐ只shì是jìn近huáng黄hūn昏

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 向晚意不适Toward evening I felt uneasy,傍晚时分，我心里不太痛快，
  2. 驱车登古原so I drove my carriage up to the old plateau.就驾着车登上了古老的乐游原。
  3. 夕阳无限好The setting sun was beautiful beyond words;夕阳美得没法形容，
  4. 只是近黄昏it is only that dusk was drawing near.只可惜已经快到黄昏了。
02

Words字词注释

向晚xiàng wǎn
xiàng wǎn: toward evening.向晚：快到晚上的时候，傍晚。
意不适yì bú shì
yì bù shì: feeling uneasy. Shì means comfortable, happy.意不适：心里不痛快。适，舒服、愉快。
驱车qū chē
qū chē: to drive a carriage.驱车：赶着车。
古原gǔ yuán
gǔ yuán: the old plateau, meaning Leyou Plateau, high ground in the south of Chang'an. You could see the whole city from it, and Tang people loved to go there.古原：指乐游原，在长安城南边的一块高地，地势高，能看到全城，是唐代人游玩的好地方。
无限wú xiàn
wú xiàn hǎo: beautiful beyond words.无限好：好得没法说。
只是zhǐ shì
zhǐ shì: only, it is a pity that. Another reading says it means "just because".只是：只不过，可惜。一说是“正是”的意思。
近黄昏jìn huáng hūn
jìn huáng hūn: close to dusk. Dusk is the time after sunset before full dark.近黄昏：快到天黑了。黄昏，太阳落山后、天还没全黑的时候。
03

Background创作背景

Leyou Plateau was in the southeast of the Tang capital Chang'an. It was the highest place in the city, and people often climbed it for the view. In this poem Li Shangyin feels low one evening and goes up to watch the sunset. The year it was written is not clear. The poem also appears under the title "Climbing Leyou Plateau". Li Shangyin never got far in his career, and sadness often shows in his poems.

乐游原在唐代长安城的东南部，是城里最高的地方，人们常到这里登高游玩。这首诗写李商隐傍晚心情不好，登上乐游原看夕阳。具体写于哪一年不清楚。这首诗也有题作《登乐游原》的。李商隐一生不得志，常在诗里流露伤感。

04

The poet作者

Li Shangyin (about 813 to about 858) was a famous poet of the late Tang, often named together with Du Mu. All his life he was caught between two quarrelling groups of officials at court. Neither side trusted him, so he never rose high, and he was often unhappy. His poems are full of delicate feeling and are often hard to pin down.

李商隐（约813—约858），字义山，号玉谿生，晚唐著名诗人，和杜牧并称“小李杜”。他一生卷入朝廷里两派官员的争斗，两边都不讨好，官一直做得不大，心情常常郁闷。他的诗感情细腻，常常含蓄难懂。

05

The mood意境

The poet feels gloomy and wants some fresh air, so he drives up to the high plateau. Looking out, he sees the setting sun painting the world gold and red. It is beautiful. But at once he thinks: this lovely sight will soon be swallowed by the night. Joy and regret rise in him together. These lines are often taken to mean that beautiful things tend not to last. Some readers also hear the poet sighing over his own passing years, or over the fading power of the Tang dynasty. Another reading takes zhǐ shì to mean "precisely because": the sun is so lovely just because dusk is near. Whichever way you read them, these lines have stayed famous for over a thousand years.

诗人心里烦闷，想出去散散心，就驾车登上高高的乐游原。一眼望去，夕阳把天地染得金红，美极了。可是他马上想到：这么美的景色，马上就要被黑夜吞没了。美好和可惜一起涌上心头。这两句常被理解为：美好的东西往往很短暂，也有人从中读出诗人对自己年华老去、对唐朝国运衰落的感叹。还有一种理解认为“只是”是“正是”的意思：正因为接近黄昏，夕阳才这么美。不同的读法，都让这两句成了千古名句。
