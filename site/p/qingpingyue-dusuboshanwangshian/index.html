No. 97 · Song lyric · ci (song lyric) in two stanzas第 97 首 · 宋词 · 词（双调）

qīng清píng平yuè乐·dú独sù宿bó博shān山wáng王shì氏ān庵

xīn辛qì弃jí疾Xin Qiji · Song宋

Pure Serene Music: Staying Alone at the Wang Family Hut on Mount Bo

 

rào绕chuáng床jī饥shǔ鼠

biān蝙fú蝠fān翻dēng灯wǔ舞

wū屋shàng上sōng松fēng风chuī吹jí急yǔ雨

pò破zhǐ纸chuāng窗jiān间zì自yǔ语

píng平shēng生sài塞běi北jiāng江nán南

guī归lái来huá华fà发cāng苍yán颜

bù布bèi被qiū秋xiāo宵mèng梦jué觉

yǎn眼qián前wàn万lǐ里jiāng江shān山

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 绕床饥鼠Hungry rats run round the bed;饿坏了的老鼠绕着床跑，
  2. 蝙蝠翻灯舞bats tumble and dance around the lamp.蝙蝠围着灯上下翻飞。
  3. 屋上松风吹急雨On the roof the wind through the pines drives a sudden rain,屋顶上，松林里的风夹着急雨吹过，
  4. 破纸窗间自语and the torn window paper mutters to itself.窗上的破纸被吹得呼呼响，像在自言自语。
  5. 平生塞北江南All my life I have gone from the northern frontier to the south of the Yangtze;我这一生，走过北方边塞，也到过江南，
  6. 归来华发苍颜now I am back, my hair grey and my face old.如今回来，头发花白，脸也老了。
  7. 布被秋宵梦觉On an autumn night, under a coarse cloth quilt, I wake from a dream,秋夜里，盖着粗布被子，我从梦中醒来，
  8. 眼前万里江山and before my eyes still lie ten thousand li of rivers and hills.眼前仿佛还是那万里江山。
02

Words字词注释

绕床饥鼠rào chuáng jī shǔ
rào chuáng jī shǔ: hungry rats running round and round the bed.绕床饥鼠：饿极了的老鼠绕着床跑来跑去。
翻灯舞fān dēng wǔ
fān dēng wǔ: flitting and tumbling around the lamp.翻灯舞：围着灯上下翻飞。
松风sōng fēng
sōng fēng: wind blowing through the pines.松风：吹过松林的风。
急雨jí yǔ
jí yǔ: a sudden, heavy rain.急雨：又大又急的雨。
破纸窗间自语pò zhǐ chuāng jiān zì yǔ
pò zhǐ chuāng jiān zì yǔ: the torn paper on the window flaps in the wind, as if talking to itself.破纸窗间自语：窗上糊的纸破了，被风吹得呼呼响，像在自言自语。

píng shēng: all my life.平生：一生、这一辈子。

sài běi jiāng nán: the northern frontier and the lands south of the Yangtze. Xin Qiji fought the Jin in the north as a young man, then served as an official all over the south.塞北江南：北方边塞和长江以南。辛弃疾年轻时在北方抗金，后来在南方各地做官。
华发huá fà
huá fà: greying hair.华发：花白的头发。
苍颜cāng yán
cāng yán: an old, worn face.苍颜：苍老的面容。
布被bù bèi
bù bèi: a quilt of coarse cloth.布被：粗布做的被子。
秋宵qiū xiāo
qiū xiāo: an autumn night.秋宵：秋天的夜晚。

03

How to read it读音

塞sài
In sài běi (north of the frontier) this character means a frontier pass and is read sài.“塞北”的“塞”指边塞，读 sài。
华huá
In huá fà (greying hair) this character means streaked with white and is read huá.“华发”的“华”是花白的意思，读 huá。
觉jué
In mèng jué (waking from a dream) this character means to wake up and is read jué, not jiào.“梦觉”的“觉”是睡醒的意思，读 jué，不读 jiào。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is usually thought to have been written while Xin Qiji, removed from office, was living at Daihu Lake in Shangrao. Mount Bo is in today's Guangfeng, Jiangxi, not far from Shangrao, and he often went there. The 'Wang family hut' was a small thatched hut belonging to a family named Wang. One autumn night he stayed there alone in the shabby little hut, woke in the night, and wrote this lyric.

这首词一般认为是辛弃疾被罢官、闲居上饶带湖时写的。博山在今江西广丰境内，离上饶不远，他常去那里游玩。“王氏庵”是一户姓王的人家的小草屋。一个秋天的夜里，他一个人借住在这间破旧的小屋里，夜里醒来，写下了这首词。

05

The poet作者

Xin Qiji (1140–1207), known as Jiaxuan, was a great ci writer of the Southern Song. In his early twenties he rose up against the Jin in the north. With a few dozen men he rode into a Jin camp of tens of thousands and captured a traitor, then went south. He longed to win back the north, but he was never given real power and was removed from office many times. His lyrics often tie his own fate to the fate of his country.

辛弃疾（1140—1207），号稼轩，南宋大词人。他二十多岁就在北方起兵抗金，带着几十个人冲进几万人的金营抓回叛徒，后来到了南方。他一心想收复北方，却总是不被重用，多次被罢官。他的词常把个人的遭遇和国家的命运连在一起。

06

The mood意境

The first half shows night in a shabby hut. Rats run round the bed, bats flit around the lamp, wind and rain roar outside, and the torn window paper flaps and mutters. It is cold and desolate, the kind of place where most people could not sleep. The second half turns. He thinks of his whole life spent travelling north and south for his country, and how he has come back with white hair. The last two lines are the most moving. On an autumn night, under a coarse quilt, he wakes from a dream, and before his eyes lie ten thousand li of rivers and hills. He is old and lives in a broken hut, yet his heart is still set on winning back the land. The bleaker the first half, the stronger this wish shines.

上半首写破屋的夜：老鼠绕床跑，蝙蝠围灯飞，屋外风雨大作，窗纸被吹得呼呼响。又冷清又荒凉，一般人早就睡不着了。下半首一转：他想起自己一辈子南征北战，回来时已经满头白发。最后两句最动人：秋夜里盖着粗布被子，从梦中醒来，眼前还是那万里江山。人已经老了，住在破屋里，心里想的却还是收复国土。前面写得越荒凉，越显出这份心愿的执着。
