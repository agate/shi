yù御jiē街xíng行·qiū秋rì日huái怀jiù旧

fàn范zhòng仲yān淹Fan Zhongyan · Song宋

To the Tune Yu Jie Xing: Thinking of the Past on an Autumn Day

 

fēn纷fēn纷zhuì坠yè叶piāo飘xiāng香qì砌

yè夜jì寂jìng静

hán寒shēng声suì碎

zhēn真zhū珠lián帘juǎn卷yù玉lóu楼kōng空

tiān天dàn淡yín银hé河chuí垂dì地

nián年nián年jīn今yè夜

yuè月huá华rú如liàn练

cháng长shì是rén人qiān千lǐ里

chóu愁cháng肠yǐ已duàn断wú无yóu由zuì醉

jiǔ酒wèi未dào到

xiān先chéng成lèi泪

cán残dēng灯míng明miè灭zhěn枕tóu头qī欹

ān谙jìn尽gū孤mián眠zī滋wèi味

dōu都lái来cǐ此shì事

méi眉jiān间xīn心shàng上

wú无jì计xiāng相huí回bì避

The corpus text writes the word for falling leaves with a different character of the same meaning; the usual text uses the more common one, followed here. The corpus gives only the tune name; the title "Thinking of the Past on an Autumn Day" was added later by some anthologies.

原始文本第一句作“纷纷堕叶”，通行本多作“纷纷坠叶”，意思一样，这里用通行本。原始文本只有词牌名，“秋日怀旧”是后来一些选本加的题目。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 纷纷坠叶飘香砌Leaves fall in flurries onto the fragrant steps.纷纷飘落的树叶，飘落在洒满花香的台阶上。
  2. 夜寂静The night is silent,夜里静悄悄的，
  3. 寒声碎but for the small, cold sounds of the wind.只听见秋风吹叶的细碎寒声。
  4. 真珠帘卷玉楼空The pearl curtain is rolled up; the fine tower stands empty;珍珠帘子卷起来了，华美的楼上空空荡荡，
  5. 天淡银河垂地the sky is pale, and the Milky Way hangs down to the ground.天色清淡，银河低垂到地面。
  6. 年年今夜Every year on this night,每年的这一夜，
  7. 月华如练the moonlight is bright as white silk,月光都像白绸一样明亮，
  8. 长是人千里yet the one I miss is always a thousand miles away.可想念的人总是远在千里之外。
  9. 愁肠已断无由醉My heart is broken with sorrow, and I cannot even get drunk;愁得肝肠寸断，想喝醉都没有办法，
  10. 酒未到before the wine has touched my lips,酒还没喝下去，
  11. 先成泪it has turned into tears.就先变成了眼泪。
  12. 残灯明灭枕头欹The dying lamp flickers, my pillow tilts,残灯忽明忽暗，枕头斜靠着，
  13. 谙尽孤眠滋味and I have tasted all the flavors of sleeping alone.一个人睡的滋味，我已经尝够了。
  14. 都来此事Add it all up, this one sorrow:算来这件心事，
  15. 眉间心上if it is not on my brow, it is in my heart,不是在眉头，就是在心上，
  16. 无计相回避and there is no way to escape it.怎么也躲不开。
02

Words字词注释

纷纷fēn fēn
fēn fēn: one after another, many and scattered.纷纷：一片接一片，多而乱的样子。
坠叶zhuì yè
zhuì yè: falling leaves.坠叶：落下来的树叶。
香砌xiāng qì
xiāng qì: steps covered with fallen petals and their scent. Qì means stone steps.香砌：落满花瓣、带着香气的台阶。砌，台阶。
寒声碎hán shēng suì
hán shēng suì: the small, broken, chilly sounds of the autumn wind among falling leaves.寒声碎：秋风吹动落叶，发出细碎、带着寒意的声音。
真珠帘zhēn zhū lián
zhēn zhū lián: a curtain of strung pearls.真珠帘：用珍珠穿成的帘子。真珠，就是珍珠。
玉楼yù lóu
yù lóu: a fine, beautiful building.玉楼：华美的楼房。
天淡tiān dàn
tiān dàn: the sky is pale and clear.天淡：天色清淡。
银河垂地yín hé chuí dì
yín hé chuí dì: the Milky Way hangs low down to the earth.银河垂地：银河低低地垂向地面。
月华yuè huá
yuè huá: moonlight.月华：月光。
练liàn
liàn: white silk.练：白色的丝绸。
长是cháng shì
cháng shì: always is.长是：常常是，总是。
人千里rén qiān lǐ
rén qiān lǐ: the one I love is a thousand miles away.人千里：想念的人远在千里之外。
愁肠已断chóu cháng yǐ duàn
chóu cháng yǐ duàn: sorrow so great it breaks one's insides, meaning very deep grief.愁肠已断：愁得肠子都断了，形容极其悲伤。
无由wú yóu
wú yóu: there is no way to.无由：没有办法。
残灯cán dēng
cán dēng: a lamp about to burn out.残灯：快要燃尽的灯。
明灭míng miè
míng miè: flickering bright and dim.明灭：忽明忽暗。
欹qī
qī: slanting, tilted.欹：斜，倾斜。
谙尽ān jìn
ān jìn: to have tasted it all, to know it fully. Ān means to know well.谙尽：尝够了，完全体会到。谙，熟悉、知道。
孤眠gū mián
gū mián: sleeping alone.孤眠：一个人睡。
都来dōu lái
dōu lái: all told, when you add it up.都来：算来，总起来说。
无计wú jì
wú jì: no way at all.无计：没有办法。
相回避xiāng huí bì
xiāng huí bì: to escape it.相回避：躲开它。
03

How to read it读音

长cháng
It reads cháng and means always.长是：读 cháng，同“常”，意思是总是。
头tóu
In everyday speech the word for pillow is said with a light second syllable; when reading this lyric aloud, it is usually read tóu.枕头：现在口语读轻声 zhěntou，读这首词时一般把“头”读 tóu。
欹qī
It reads qī, meaning slanting.欹：读 qī，意思是斜。
都dōu
In dōu lái it reads dōu, meaning all told.都来：读 dōu，意思是算来、总起来。
相xiāng
It reads xiāng.相回避：读 xiāng。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about missing someone far away on an autumn night. We do not know exactly when or where it was written, and it does not say who is being missed; one view is that it is the poet's wife. Fan Zhongyan served for many years as an official in different places, and once led troops guarding the northwest border, so he was often far from home. This lyric is very gentle, quite unlike his frontier lyric "Yu Jia Ao".

这首词写的是秋夜里思念远方的人。具体写作时间和地点都不清楚，词里想念的是谁也没有明说，一说是想念远方的妻子。范仲淹做过很多年地方官，还在西北边境带兵守卫过，常常离家很远。这首词写得很柔，和他写边塞的《渔家傲》很不一样。

05

The poet作者

Fan Zhongyan (989–1052) was a famous statesman and writer of the Northern Song. He grew up very poor and became an official by studying hard. His essay "The Yueyang Tower" has a famous line: be the first to worry about the world's troubles and the last to enjoy its pleasures. Few of his lyrics survive, but this one about longing feels true and moving.

范仲淹（989—1052），北宋名臣，也是文学家。他小时候家里很穷，靠刻苦读书做了官。他写的《岳阳楼记》里有一句“先天下之忧而忧，后天下之乐而乐”，非常有名。他的词留下来的不多，但这首写相思的，读起来很真切动人。

06

The mood意境

The first half paints the scene. Leaves fall one by one onto the steps. The night is still, with only the small sounds of the wind. The tower is empty, the Milky Way hangs so low it seems to touch the ground, and the moonlight is white as silk. Every year this night is just as lovely, yet the one I miss is never here. The lovelier the scene, the lonelier the person. The second half turns to feelings. He wants wine to drown his sorrow, but before he drinks, the tears come first, which is sadder than crying when drunk. The lamp is dying, the pillow tilts, and he knows every taste of sleeping alone. The last lines are the most famous: this sorrow, if not on the brow, is in the heart, and there is no escape. Later the poet Li Qingzhao wrote a line much like it: just as it leaves the brow, it climbs into the heart.

上半首写景。树叶一片片落在台阶上，夜很静，只有细碎的风声。楼上空空的，银河低得好像垂到了地上，月光白得像绸子。每年这一夜月色都这么美，可想念的人总不在身边。景越美，人越孤单。下半首写心情。想借酒消愁，酒还没喝，眼泪先流下来了，比“喝醉了哭”还要伤心。灯快灭了，枕头斜着，一个人睡的滋味全尝遍了。最后一句最有名：这份心事，不在眉头，就在心上，怎么也躲不开。后来李清照写“才下眉头，却上心头”，就和这一句很像。
