mǎn满tíng庭fāng芳·xiǎo晓sè色yún云kāi开

qín秦guān观Qin Guan · Song宋

Man Ting Fang: Dawn Light Breaks Through the Clouds

 

xiǎo晓sè色yún云kāi开

chūn春suí随rén人yì意

zhòu骤yǔ雨cái才guò过hái还qíng晴

gǔ古tái台fāng芳xiè榭

fēi飞yàn燕cù蹴hóng红yīng英

wǔ舞kùn困yú榆qián钱zì自luò落

qiū秋qiān千wài外

lǜ绿shuǐ水qiáo桥píng平

dōng东fēng风lǐ里

zhū朱mén门yìng映liǔ柳

dī低àn按xiǎo小qín秦zhēng筝

duō多qíng情

xíng行lè乐chù处

zhū珠diàn钿cuì翠gài盖

yù玉pèi辔hóng红yīng缨

jiàn渐jiǔ酒kōng空jīn金kē榼

huā花kùn困péng蓬yíng瀛

dòu豆kòu蔻shāo梢tóu头jiù旧hèn恨

shí十nián年mèng梦

qū屈zhǐ指kān堪jīng惊

píng凭lán阑jiǔ久

shū疏yān烟dàn淡rì日

jì寂mò寞xià下wú芜chéng城

The corpus has an extra character in the line about playing the zheng. By the pattern of this tune the line should have five characters, and the common text gives it that way.

语料库作“低底按小秦筝”，多了一个“底”字。按这个词牌，这一句应是五个字，通行本作“低按小秦筝”。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 晓色云开At dawn the clouds part.清晨的天色里，云散开了，
  2. 春随人意Spring is just as we wished:春天正合人的心意，
  3. 骤雨才过还晴a sudden shower has just passed, and the sky is clear again.一阵急雨刚过，天又晴了。
  4. 古台芳榭Old terraces, pavilions among flowers,古老的楼台，花丛中的亭榭，
  5. 飞燕蹴红英swallows darting about kick down red petals.飞来飞去的燕子踢落了红色的花瓣。
  6. 舞困榆钱自落Elm seeds, tired of dancing in the wind, fall by themselves.榆钱在风里舞累了，自己落下来，
  7. 秋千外Beyond the swing,秋千那边，
  8. 绿水桥平the green water has risen level with the bridge.绿水涨得快和小桥一样平。
  9. 东风里In the east wind,东风里，
  10. 朱门映柳red gates shine among the willows,红漆大门映着柳树，
  11. 低按小秦筝and someone softly plays a little zheng.有人低低地弹着小秦筝。
  12. 多情I was so full of feeling.我是那样多情。
  13. 行乐处In those places of pleasure,在那些游乐的地方，
  14. 珠钿翠盖there were carriages with pearls and kingfisher canopies,有缀着珠宝、撑着翠盖的车，
  15. 玉辔红缨horses with jade bridles and red tassels.有玉做的马缰、红色的缨穗。
  16. 渐酒空金榼Slowly the golden flasks ran dry,渐渐地，金色酒器里的酒喝空了，
  17. 花困蓬瀛and in that fairyland of flowers we grew tired.花丛像仙山一样，人也玩累了。
  18. 豆蔻梢头旧恨The girl like a young cardamom bud, the old regret,想起那位少女，旧日的遗憾，
  19. 十年梦ten years gone like a dream:十年往事像一场梦，
  20. 屈指堪惊counting on my fingers, I am startled.屈指一算，让人吃惊。
  21. 凭阑久I lean on the railing a long time.我靠着栏杆站了很久，
  22. 疏烟淡日Thin mist, a pale sun,稀疏的烟雾，淡淡的阳光，
  23. 寂寞下芜城fall lonely over the city of Yangzhou.寂寞地落在扬州城上。
02

Words字词注释

晓色xiǎo sè
xiǎo sè: the color of the sky at dawn.晓色：清晨的天色。
春随人意chūn suí rén yì
chūn suí rén yì: the spring weather is just what people hoped for.春随人意：春天的天气正合人的心意。
骤雨zhòu yǔ
zhòu yǔ: a sudden, heavy shower.骤雨：来得又快又猛的雨。
芳榭fāng xiè
fāng xiè: a pavilion among flowers. Xiè is a pavilion on a terrace or by the water.芳榭：周围开满花的亭台。榭，建在高台或水边的亭子。
蹴cù
cù: to kick or tread on.蹴：踢、踩。
红英hóng yīng
hóng yīng: red petals.红英：红色的花瓣。
榆钱yú qián
yú qián: elm seeds, round and flat, like strings of little coins.榆钱：榆树的果实，圆圆扁扁的，像一串串小铜钱。
舞困wǔ kùn
wǔ kùn: tired of dancing in the wind.舞困：在风里飞舞累了。
桥平qiáo píng
qiáo píng: the spring water has risen almost level with the bridge.桥平：春水涨起来，快和桥面一样平了。
朱门zhū mén
zhū mén: red-painted gates, the homes of the rich.朱门：红漆大门，指有钱人家。
按àn
àn: to play an instrument.按：弹奏。
秦筝qín zhēng
qín zhēng: the zheng, a plucked string instrument said to come from the old Qin lands.秦筝：筝，一种弹拨的弦乐器，相传出自秦地，所以叫秦筝。
行乐处xíng lè chù
xíng lè chù: places of fun and pleasure.行乐处：游玩取乐的地方。
珠钿翠盖zhū diàn cuì gài
zhū diàn cuì gài: carriages trimmed with pearls, with canopies of kingfisher feathers. Grand carriages.珠钿翠盖：车子上装饰着珠宝，车顶有翠鸟羽毛做的车盖。指华丽的车子。
玉辔红缨yù pèi hóng yīng
yù pèi hóng yīng: jade bridles and red tassels. Finely decked horses.玉辔红缨：玉做的马缰绳，红色的缨穗。指装饰华美的马。
金榼jīn kē
jīn kē: a golden wine flask.金榼：金色的酒器。榼，盛酒的器具。
蓬瀛péng yíng
péng yíng: Penglai and Yingzhou, fairy islands in the sea. Here it means a lovely place of pleasure.蓬瀛：蓬莱和瀛洲，传说海上的仙山。这里比喻美丽的游乐之地。
豆蔻梢头dòu kòu shāo tóu
dòu kòu shāo tóu: the Tang poet Du Mu wrote of a young girl in Yangzhou as a cardamom bud at the tip of a branch in early spring. Later the image meant a girl of about thirteen.豆蔻梢头：唐代杜牧写扬州少女有“豆蔻梢头二月初”，后来用豆蔻比喻十三四岁的少女。
十年梦shí nián mèng
shí nián mèng: Du Mu wrote of waking from a ten-year dream of Yangzhou. Here it means the old days in Yangzhou feel like a dream.十年梦：杜牧有“十年一觉扬州梦”，这里借来说扬州的旧日往事像一场梦。
屈指堪惊qū zhǐ kān jīng
qū zhǐ kān jīng: counting on one's fingers, one is shocked how long it has been.屈指堪惊：弯着手指一算，让人吃惊。
凭阑píng lán
píng lán: leaning on the railing.凭阑：靠着栏杆。
疏烟淡日shū yān dàn rì
shū yān dàn rì: thin mist and a pale sun.疏烟淡日：稀稀的烟雾，淡淡的太阳。
芜城wú chéng
wú chéng: another name for Yangzhou. Bao Zhao of the Southern Dynasties wrote a famous piece about the ruined city of Yangzhou.芜城：扬州的别称。南朝鲍照写过《芜城赋》，写扬州的荒凉。
03

How to read it读音

还hái
Here it means again, so it is read hái. Some read it as 'returning to clear weather' and say huán.还，又、仍然，读 hái。也有人理解成“恢复晴天”，读 huán。
燕yàn
It means the swallow, read yàn.燕，燕子，读 yàn。
钿diàn
It means a flower-shaped ornament of gold, silver or jewels, read diàn.钿，用金银珠宝做成的花形装饰，读 diàn。
04

Background创作背景

This lyric is about spring in Yangzhou and the fun of days gone by. Qin Guan came from Gaoyou, close to Yangzhou, and he had spent time there. The lyric borrows famous lines Du Mu wrote in Yangzhou in the Tang. It is usually read as Qin Guan looking back on his Yangzhou days many years later. Sources differ on the exact year.

这首词写扬州的春天和旧日的游乐。秦观是高邮人，高邮离扬州很近，他曾在扬州游玩。词里借用了唐代杜牧在扬州写的名句，一般认为是秦观多年后回想扬州旧事而作。具体写作年份说法不一。

05

The poet作者

Qin Guan (1049 to 1100), courtesy name Shaoyou, was a Northern Song ci poet. He was a student of Su Shi and one of the Four Scholars of the Su school. His lyrics are gentle and delicate, often about parting and sorrow, and he is a model of the soft, graceful style. Later, caught in court fights, he was sent away again and again, and a light sadness runs through his lyrics.

秦观（1049—1100），字少游，北宋词人，是苏轼的学生，“苏门四学士”之一。他的词温柔细腻，多写离别和伤感，是婉约词的代表。他后来因为政治斗争被一贬再贬，词里常带着淡淡的愁。

06

The mood意境

The first half is a bright spring picture. Clouds part after rain, swallows kick down red petals, elm seeds drift, spring water rises to the bridge, and the sound of a zheng comes from red gates among the willows. It is light and lovely. Then two words, full of feeling, turn the lyric back to the past. Grand carriages and horses, empty wine flasks, flower gardens where everyone grew tired: the fun reached its height. But thinking of the girl of those days and the ten years since, it all feels like a dream, and counting the years is a shock. At the end the poet leans on the railing a long time and sees only thin mist and a pale sun falling lonely over Yangzhou. The livelier the start, the colder the end.

上半首是一幅明亮的春景：云开雨过，燕子踢落红花，榆钱飘下，春水涨到桥边，柳树掩映的红门里传出筝声。画面又轻又美。下半首“多情”两个字一转，回到当年：华丽的车马，喝空的酒器，玩累的花丛，热闹到了极点。可是一想到旧日的少女和十年的往事，就像一场梦，算一算让人吃惊。最后作者久久靠着栏杆，只看见淡烟淡日寂寞地落在扬州城上。前面越热闹，结尾越冷清。
