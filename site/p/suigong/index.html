suí隋gōng宫

lǐ李shāng商yǐn隐Li Shangyin · Tang唐

The Sui Palace

 

zǐ紫quán泉gōng宫diàn殿suǒ锁yān烟xiá霞

yù欲qǔ取wú芜chéng城zuò作dì帝jiā家

yù玉xǐ玺bù不yuán缘guī归rì日jiǎo角

jǐn锦fān帆yīng应shì是dào到tiān天yá涯

yú于jīn今fǔ腐cǎo草wú无yíng萤huǒ火

zhōng终gǔ古chuí垂yáng杨yǒu有mù暮yā鸦

dì地xià下ruò若féng逢chén陈hòu后zhǔ主

qǐ岂yí宜chóng重wèn问hòu后tíng庭huā花

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 紫泉宫殿锁烟霞The palaces of Chang'an stood locked in mist and cloud, empty,长安的宫殿被云雾锁住，空空地没人住，
  2. 欲取芜城作帝家while the emperor wanted to make Yangzhou his capital.隋炀帝还想把扬州当作新的都城。
  3. 玉玺不缘归日角If the jade seal had not passed to the man born to be emperor,如果不是皇位落到了唐高祖手里，
  4. 锦帆应是到天涯his brocade-sailed boats would probably have sailed to the ends of the earth.那锦帆龙船大概会一直游到天边去。
  5. 于今腐草无萤火Now no fireflies rise from the rotting grass,到现在，腐草里再也飞不出萤火虫，
  6. 终古垂杨有暮鸦and only crows at dusk perch forever in the weeping willows.只有河堤上的垂柳，永远落着黄昏的乌鸦。
  7. 地下若逢陈后主If he meets Chen Shubao under the earth,隋炀帝在地下如果遇见陈后主，
  8. 岂宜重问后庭花how could he dare ask again for "Flowers in the Rear Court"?难道还好意思再请他唱《后庭花》吗？
02

Words字词注释

紫泉zǐ quán
zǐ quán: the Purple Spring, really the Purple Deep, a river near Chang'an. Here it means Chang'an. The first Tang emperor was named Li Yuan, so the word for deep was changed to spring to avoid his name.紫泉：紫渊，长安附近的一条河，这里指长安。唐高祖叫李渊，为了避开皇帝的名字，改“渊”为“泉”。
锁烟霞suǒ yān xiá
suǒ yān xiá: locked in mist and clouds, meaning the palaces stood empty.锁烟霞：被云雾锁住，意思是宫殿空着没人住。
芜城wú chéng
wú chéng: the Ruined City, Guangling, today's Yangzhou in Jiangsu. The poet Bao Zhao wrote a famous piece about it, so the name stuck.芜城：广陵，就是今天江苏扬州。南朝鲍照写过《芜城赋》，后来就用芜城指扬州。
帝家dì jiā
dì jiā: the emperor's home, the capital.帝家：皇帝的家，就是都城。
玉玺yù xǐ
yù xǐ: the emperor's jade seal, a sign of the throne.玉玺：皇帝用的玉印，代表皇位。
不缘bù yuán
bù yuán: if it were not because.不缘：如果不是因为。
日角rì jiǎo
rì jiǎo: a bump in the middle of the forehead shaped like the sun. People in old times thought it marked a future emperor. Here it means Li Yuan, the first Tang emperor.日角：额头中间的骨头隆起像太阳，古人认为这是当皇帝的面相。这里指唐高祖李渊。
锦帆jǐn fān
jǐn fān: sails made of brocade. Emperor Yang of Sui toured Yangzhou in grand dragon boats with brocade sails.锦帆：用锦缎做的船帆。隋炀帝坐着装饰华丽的龙船游扬州，船帆都用锦缎做。
天涯tiān yá
tiān yá: the edge of the sky, the farthest places.天涯：天边，极远的地方。
于今yú jīn
yú jīn: until now.于今：到现在。
腐草无萤火fǔ cǎo wú yíng huǒ
fǔ cǎo wú yíng huǒ: people in old times believed fireflies grew out of rotting grass. Emperor Yang had huge numbers of fireflies gathered and set free at night to light up the hills on his outings. Now not even fireflies are left.腐草无萤火：古人以为萤火虫是腐烂的草变的。隋炀帝曾经让人收集了很多萤火虫，晚上游山时放出来照亮山谷。现在连萤火虫都没有了。
终古zhōng gǔ
zhōng gǔ: forever, all along.终古：长久，一直。
垂杨chuí yáng
chuí yáng: weeping willows. Emperor Yang dug the Grand Canal and planted willows all along its banks.垂杨：垂柳。隋炀帝开运河，在河堤上种了很多柳树。
暮鸦mù yā
mù yā: crows at dusk.暮鸦：黄昏时的乌鸦。
陈后主chén hòu zhǔ
chén hòu zhǔ: Chen Shubao, the last ruler of the Chen dynasty. He cared only for pleasure and was conquered by the Sui.陈后主：陈叔宝，南朝陈的最后一个皇帝，只顾玩乐，被隋朝灭掉了。
岂宜qǐ yí
qǐ yí: how could one dare.岂宜：怎么好意思，哪里应该。
后庭花hòu tíng huā
hòu tíng huā: "Flowers in the Rear Court", a song by Chen Shubao, later seen as the music of a lost kingdom. Legend says Emperor Yang once dreamed of meeting him and asked his favorite consort to dance to it.后庭花：《玉树后庭花》，陈后主作的歌曲，后人把它看作亡国之音。传说隋炀帝曾梦见陈后主，还请他的宠妃跳了这支舞。
03

How to read it读音

角jiǎo
In ri jiao, this character is read jiǎo.“日角”的“角”读 jiǎo。
应yīng
Here it means probably, and is read yīng.“应是”是应该是、大概是，读 yīng。
重chóng
Here it means to ask again, so it is read chóng, not zhòng.“重问”是再问一次，读 chóng，不读 zhòng。
04

Background创作背景

The Sui Palace means the travel palaces Emperor Yang of Sui built at Jiangdu, today's Yangzhou. Emperor Yang, Yang Guang, loved pleasure trips. Three times he sailed to Yangzhou in dragon boats, wasting the people's money and labor. In the end he was killed by his own men in Yangzhou, and the Sui fell soon after. Li Shangyin uses this history to mock rulers who care only for pleasure and not for their people, and to warn the Tang rulers of his own day. The year it was written cannot be fixed.

隋宫指隋炀帝在江都（今扬州）修建的行宫。隋炀帝杨广喜欢游玩，三次乘龙船下扬州，劳民伤财，最后在扬州被部下杀死，隋朝也很快灭亡。李商隐借这段历史，讽刺只顾享乐、不管百姓的皇帝，也是在提醒当时的唐朝统治者。这首诗写于哪一年，已经不能确定。

05

The poet作者

Li Shangyin (about 813 to about 858) was a poet of the late Tang. Besides his untitled poems, he wrote many poems on history, using old events to talk about his own times. He used many allusions, and often a single line hides a whole story. "The Sui Palace" is like that.

李商隐（约813—约858），字义山，晚唐诗人。除了无题诗，他还写了很多咏史诗，借古代的事情说当时的事。他用典故很多，常常一句里藏着一个故事，《隋宫》就是这样。

06

The mood意境

Every line is about Emperor Yang, yet not one line curses him. The mockery hides inside the stories. The poem begins by saying he left the palaces of Chang'an empty and insisted on going to Yangzhou. Then it says that if the Tang had not taken the throne, his boats might have sailed on to the ends of the earth, his fun never ending. The third couplet is the most famous. Back then he set fireflies loose to light the hills; now not a single firefly rises from the grass. The willows he planted along the canal are still there, but only crows sit in them at dusk. Noise and glitter have turned to silence. At the end the poet makes a joke. If, under the earth, he meets Chen Shubao, who also lost his kingdom to pleasure, would he dare ask him for that song again? The joke carries a cold warning.

这首诗句句写隋炀帝，却没有一句骂他，讽刺都藏在故事里。开头说他放着长安的宫殿不住，偏要去扬州；接着说，要不是唐朝得了天下，他的龙船恐怕要游到天边去，玩起来没完没了。第三联最有名：当年他放萤火虫照山谷，现在腐草里一只萤火虫也没有；当年河堤上种的柳树还在，却只有黄昏的乌鸦落在上面。热闹变成了冷清。最后诗人开了个玩笑：他在地下见到同样因为玩乐亡国的陈后主，还好意思再请人家唱《后庭花》吗？笑里带着冷冷的警告。
