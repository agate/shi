sòng送shàng上rén人

liú刘cháng长qīng卿Liu Changqing · Tang唐

Seeing Off a Monk

 

gū孤yún云jiāng将yě野hè鹤

qǐ岂xiàng向rén人jiān间zhù住

mò莫mǎi买wò沃zhōu洲shān山

shí时rén人yǐ已zhī知chù处

The poem's title is also given as "Seeing Off a Monk from Beyond the World". Standard editions such as Three Hundred Tang Poems call it "Seeing Off a Monk". The text of the poem is the same.

这首诗的题目一作《送方外上人》。《唐诗三百首》等通行本题作《送上人》。诗的正文相同。

01

Line by line逐句翻译

  1. 孤云将野鹤You are like a lone cloud, a wild crane;你就像一片孤云、一只野鹤，
  2. 岂向人间住how could you ever settle in the busy human world?怎么会在热闹的人世间住下来呢？
  3. 莫买沃洲山Do not go and buy Wozhou Mountain to live in retreat,不要去买沃洲山来隐居，
  4. 时人已知处for everyone already knows that place.那里早已是世人都知道的地方了。

03

Background创作背景

Shàng rén is a polite word for a monk, and fāng wài means beyond the ordinary world. Liu Changqing wrote this poem to see off a monk heading far away. It uses an old story: the Eastern Jin monk Zhi Dun once tried to buy a mountain to live in retreat, and the owner laughed that true hermits of old never needed to buy a mountain first. Liu uses the story to tease his friend kindly. When it was written and who the monk was are not known.

上人是对僧人的尊称，方外是指世俗之外。刘长卿送一位僧人去远方，写下这首诗。诗里用了一个典故：东晋高僧支遁想向别人买山来隐居，对方笑他说，没听说古代真正的隐士要花钱买山才去隐居的。刘长卿借这个故事，和朋友开了一个善意的玩笑。这首诗写于何时、送的是哪位僧人，都不清楚。

04

The poet作者

Liu Changqing, also called Wenfang, was a Tang poet. Sources differ on his dates; he lived in the eighth century. He was best at five-character poems and called himself the Great Wall of five-character verse, meaning his poems were as solid as the Great Wall. He was demoted twice in his career, often felt lonely and let down, and liked to spend time with monks and hermits.

刘长卿，字文房，唐代诗人，生卒年说法不一，大约生活在八世纪。他最擅长写五言诗，自称“五言长城”，意思是自己的五言诗像长城一样坚固。他做官时两次被贬，心里常有失意和孤独，喜欢和僧人、隐士来往。

05

The mood意境

The first line compares the monk to a lone cloud and a wild crane. A cloud drifts freely, a crane flies freely, and no one can hold them. Such a person would never live in a busy place. The last two lines suddenly turn into a friendly joke: don't go and buy Wozhou Mountain, it is too famous; everyone knows it, so how could it be quiet? The deeper meaning is that true retreat is not about a famous mountain but about a free heart. The poem holds a farewell, some praise, and a little humor, so it feels light yet full of flavor.

第一句把僧人比作孤云和野鹤。云自由地飘，鹤自由地飞，谁也管不住。这样的人，当然不会住在热闹的地方。后两句忽然一转，像朋友之间的玩笑：你可别去买沃洲山啊，那里太有名了，人人都知道，哪里还清静？言外之意是：真正的隐居不在于山有多出名，而在于心是否自由。诗里有送别，有赞美，也有一点幽默，读起来轻松又有味道。
